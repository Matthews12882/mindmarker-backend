# Rande Gerber

**Rande Gerber** (born April 27, 1962) is an American businessman. He founded Gerber Group and co-founded tequila brand [Casamigos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casamigos "Casamigos") with [George Clooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Clooney "George Clooney") and [Mike Meldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Meldman "Michael Meldman"), as well as nightlife companies Midnight Oil and Caliche Rum. His portfolio has included restaurants, bars, and lounges worldwide. He is married to [supermodel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermodel "Supermodel") [Cindy Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cindy_Crawford "Cindy Crawford") and is the father of models [Kaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaia_Gerber "Kaia Gerber") and the late [Presley Gerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presley_Gerber "Presley Gerber").

## Early life

Gerber is the son of Jordan Gerber and Ellen Peckman. His family is Jewish. Gerber grew up in [Hewlett, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett,_New_York "Hewlett, New York") and graduated from [Hewlett High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Hewlett_High_School "George W. Hewlett High School") in 1980. He earned a bachelor's degree in marketing from the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona "University of Arizona").

## Career

Gerber Group opened its first venue, The Whiskey, a bar at the [Paramount Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Hotel "Paramount Hotel") in the New York City theater district, in 1991.

In 2013, he partnered with George Clooney and Michael Meldman to launch [Casamigos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casamigos "Casamigos") Tequila. In June 2017, it was sold to [Diageo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diageo "Diageo") for $700 million, with an additional $300 million possible depending on the company's performance over the next ten years.

In January 2019, it was announced that Gerber and his partners, Meldman, [Jeff Shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Shell "Jeff Shell"), and [Jay Sures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Sures "Jay Sures") would purchase [Nate 'n Al of Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nate_%27n_Al_of_Beverly_Hills "Nate 'n Al of Beverly Hills") and keep it open.

In April 2024, Gerber and his partners expanded the Casamigos portfolio with the launch of "Casamigas Jalapeño Tequila," a spicy line extension featuring his wife, Cindy Crawford, on the label.

In July 2025, Gerber announced a partnership with the luxury sun care brand Hampton Sun. He joined the company to advise on its 2025 rebranding and product rollout after encountering the brand while vacationing in Miami.

In October 2025, reports emerged that Gerber, Meldman, and Clooney were developing a non-alcoholic beer brand planned for release in 2026 called [Crazy Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crazy_Mountain "Crazy Mountain").

## Personal life

Gerber married model [Cindy Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cindy_Crawford "Cindy Crawford") on May 29, 1998. They have two children, [Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presley_Gerber "Presley Gerber") (1999–2026) and [Kaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaia_Gerber "Kaia Gerber") (born 2001), both of whom started modeling careers while growing up. In August 2021, the couple sold their home in [Trousdale Estates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trousdale_Estates "Trousdale Estates"), [Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills "Beverly Hills"), for a reported $13.5 million.
