# Miley Cyrus

**Miley Ray Cyrus** ([/ˈmaɪli ˈsaɪrəs/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*MY-lee SY-rəs*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"), formerly **Hemsworth**; born **Destiny Hope Cyrus**; November 23, 1992) is an American singer, songwriter, and actress. An influential figure in [popular music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_music "Popular music"), Cyrus is known for her evolving artistry and image reinventions. She was an established [child actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_actress "Child actress") before developing a successful entertainment career as an adult. Cyrus emerged as a [teen idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_idol "Teen idol") with her portrayal of [Miley Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Stewart "Miley Stewart") in the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") television series *[Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana "Hannah Montana")* (2006–2011), growing a profitable franchise and achieving two number-one soundtracks on the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200").

Cyrus's music career outside of *Hannah Montana* began with her US number-one [pop rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_rock "Pop rock") albums *[Meet Miley Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana_2:_Meet_Miley_Cyrus "Hannah Montana 2: Meet Miley Cyrus")* (2007) and *[Breakout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakout_(Miley_Cyrus_album) "Breakout (Miley Cyrus album)")* (2008). The single "[Party in the U.S.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_in_the_U.S.A. "Party in the U.S.A.")", from her EP *[The Time of Our Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Time_of_Our_Lives_(EP) "The Time of Our Lives (EP)")* (2009), became one of the [best-selling singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_singles "List of best-selling singles"). She explored a more provocative image with the [dance-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance-pop "Dance-pop") album *[Can't Be Tamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Be_Tamed "Can't Be Tamed")* (2010) and [trap-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trap-pop "Trap-pop") album *[Bangerz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangerz "Bangerz")* (2013). The latter featured the single "[We Can't Stop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Can%27t_Stop "We Can't Stop")" and her first [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") number-one, "[Wrecking Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecking_Ball_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)")". She experimented with [psychedelic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_music "Psychedelic music"), [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"), and [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") genres on her next albums *[Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus_%26_Her_Dead_Petz "Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz")* (2015), *[Younger Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Younger_Now "Younger Now")* (2017), and *[Plastic Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic_Hearts "Plastic Hearts")* (2020). After signing with [Columbia Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Records "Columbia Records"), she released her eighth studio album *[Endless Summer Vacation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endless_Summer_Vacation "Endless Summer Vacation")* (2023) and the visual album *[Something Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Something_Beautiful_(Miley_Cyrus_album) "Something Beautiful (Miley Cyrus album)")* (2025); the former was led by her second US number-one single "[Flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flowers_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Flowers (Miley Cyrus song)")", which became the [best-selling single of 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_singles#By_year "List of best-selling singles") and won two [Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Awards "Grammy Awards"), including [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Record of the Year"). Cyrus transitioned to [Atlantic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Records "Atlantic Records") and released her tenth studio album, *[Bass Persuades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_Persuades "Bass Persuades")* (2026), which became her first album in over a decade to top the *Billboard* 200.

As an actress and television personality, Cyrus starred in the films *[Bolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(2008_film) "Bolt (2008 film)")* (2008), *[Hannah Montana: The Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana:_The_Movie "Hannah Montana: The Movie")* (2009), *[The Last Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Song_(2010_film) "The Last Song (2010 film)")* (2010), *[LOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LOL_(2012_film) "LOL (2012 film)")* (2012), and *[So Undercover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/So_Undercover "So Undercover")* (2013). On television, she was the subject of the documentary *[Miley: The Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley:_The_Movement "Miley: The Movement")* (2013), served as a coach on two seasons of *[The Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(American_TV_series) "The Voice (American TV series)")* (2016–2017), and starred in the miniseries *[Crisis in Six Scenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crisis_in_Six_Scenes "Crisis in Six Scenes")* (2017) and [an episode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel,_Jack_and_Ashley_Too "Rachel, Jack and Ashley Too") of *[Black Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Mirror "Black Mirror")* (2019). She also hosted the holiday special *[Miley's New Year's Eve Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley%27s_New_Year%27s_Eve_Party "Miley's New Year's Eve Party")* (2021–2022).

Cyrus has received [various accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Miley_Cyrus "List of awards and nominations received by Miley Cyrus"), including three Grammy Awards, one [Brit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award "Brit Award"), five [*Billboard* Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Music_Awards "Billboard Music Awards"), three [MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Awards "MTV Video Music Awards"), and eight *[Guinness World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Records "Guinness World Records")*. She has received a [star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_stars_on_the_Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "List of stars on the Hollywood Walk of Fame") on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame"), was named a [Disney Legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Legends "Disney Legends"), and ranked among the [greatest pop stars of the 21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard%27s_Greatest_Pop_Stars_of_the_21st_Century "Billboard's Greatest Pop Stars of the 21st Century") by *Billboard*. Cyrus is also the ninth-highest-certified female [digital singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-certified_music_artists_in_the_United_States#Top_50_certified_music_artists_(digital_singles) "List of highest-certified music artists in the United States") artist by the RIAA. She has featured in lists such as the [*Time* 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") (2008 and 2014), [*Forbes* 30 under 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_30_Under_30 "Forbes 30 Under 30") in the music category, and [*Forbes* Celebrity 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_Celebrity_100 "Forbes Celebrity 100") (2010 and 2015). In 2014, she founded the [Happy Hippie Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Hippie_Foundation "Happy Hippie Foundation"), focusing on the [LGBTQ community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_community "LGBTQ community") and [youth homelessness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_homelessness "Youth homelessness").

## Early life and career beginnings

Destiny Hope Cyrus was born on November 23, 1992, in [Franklin, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin,_Tennessee "Franklin, Tennessee"), to [Leticia "Tish" Jean Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tish_Cyrus "Tish Cyrus") (née Finley) and country singer [Billy Ray Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Ray_Cyrus "Billy Ray Cyrus"). She was born with [supraventricular tachycardia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supraventricular_tachycardia "Supraventricular tachycardia"), a condition causing an abnormal resting heart rate. Her birth name, Destiny Hope, expressed her parents' belief that she would accomplish great things. Her parents nicknamed her "Smiley", which they later shortened to "Miley", because she often smiled as an infant. In 2008, she legally changed her name to Miley Ray Cyrus; her middle name honors her grandfather, [Democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Democratic_Party "U.S. Democratic Party") politician [Ronald Ray Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Cyrus "Ron Cyrus"), who was from [Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky "Kentucky"). Cyrus's [godmother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godparent "Godparent") was singer-songwriter [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton "Dolly Parton").

Against the advice of her father's record company, Cyrus's parents secretly married on December 28, 1993, a year after her birth. They had two more children, son Braison and daughter [Noah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Cyrus "Noah Cyrus"). From a previous relationship, her mother has two other children, [Brandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandi_Cyrus "Brandi Cyrus") and [Trace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_Cyrus "Trace Cyrus"). Her father's first child, Christopher Cody, was born in April 1992 and grew up separately with his mother, waitress Kristin Luckey, in [South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina "South Carolina").

All of Cyrus's maternal siblings are established entertainers. Trace was a vocalist and guitarist for the electronic pop band [Metro Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Station_(band) "Metro Station (band)"). Noah is an actress and, along with Braison, models, sings, and is a songwriter. Brandi was formerly a musician for the [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock "Indie rock") band Frank + Derol and is a professional [DJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_jockey "Disk jockey"). The Cyrus farmhouse is located on 500 acres of land outside Nashville.

Cyrus attended Heritage Elementary School in [Williamson County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamson_County,_Tennessee "Williamson County, Tennessee") while she and her family lived in [Thompson's Station, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thompson%27s_Station,_Tennessee "Thompson's Station, Tennessee"). When she was cast in *Hannah Montana,* the family moved to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") and she attended Options for Youth Charter Schools studying with a private tutor on set. Raised as a Christian, she was baptized in a [Southern Baptist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Baptist "Southern Baptist") church before moving to [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles "Hollywood, Los Angeles") in 2005. She attended church regularly while growing up and wore a [purity ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purity_ring "Purity ring"). In 2001, when Cyrus was eight, she and her family moved to [Toronto, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto,_Ontario,_Canada "Toronto, Ontario, Canada"), while her father filmed the television series *[Doc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_(2001_TV_series) "Doc (2001 TV series)")*. After Billy Ray Cyrus took her to see a 2001 [Mirvish production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirvish_Productions "Mirvish Productions") of *[Mamma Mia!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamma_Mia!_(musical) "Mamma Mia! (musical)")* at the [Royal Alexandra Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Alexandra_Theatre "Royal Alexandra Theatre"), Miley Cyrus grabbed his arm and told him, "This is what I want to do, daddy. I want to be an actress." She began to take singing and acting lessons at the Armstrong Acting Studio in Toronto.

Cyrus's first acting role was as Kylie in her father's television series *Doc*. In 2003, she received credit under her birth name for her role as "Young Ruthie" in [Tim Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Burton "Tim Burton")'s *[Big Fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Fish "Big Fish")*. During this period, she auditioned with [Taylor Lautner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Lautner "Taylor Lautner") for the feature film *[The Adventures of Sharkboy and Lavagirl in 3-D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Sharkboy_and_Lavagirl_in_3-D "The Adventures of Sharkboy and Lavagirl in 3-D").* Although she was one of two finalists for the role, she chose to appear in *Hannah Montana* instead. Her mother took on the role of Miley's manager and worked to acquire a team to build her daughter's career. Cyrus signed with Mitchell Gossett, director of the youth division at Cunningham Escott Slevin Doherty. Gossett is often credited with "discovering" Cyrus and played a key role in her auditioning for *Hannah Montana*. She later signed with Jason Morey of Morey Management Group to handle her music career; Dolly Parton steered her to him. She hired her father's finance manager as part of her team.

## Career

### 2006–2009: *Hannah Montana* and early musical releases

Cyrus auditioned for the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") television series *[Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana "Hannah Montana")* when she was thirteen years old. She auditioned for the role of the best friend, [Lilly Truscott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilly_Truscott "Lilly Truscott"), but was called to audition for [the primary character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Stewart "Miley Stewart") instead seeing her comical performance. Despite being denied the part at first because she was "too small and too young" for the role, she was later cast as Miley Stewart because of her singing and goofy acting abilities. The series premiered in March 2006 to the largest audience for a Disney Channel program and quickly ranked among the highest-rated series on basic cable. The success of the series led to Cyrus' being labeled a "[teen idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_idol "Teen idol")". She toured with [the Cheetah Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cheetah_Girls_(group) "The Cheetah Girls (group)") as Hannah Montana in September 2006 and performed songs from the show's first season. [Walt Disney Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Records "Walt Disney Records") released a [soundtrack credited to Cyrus's character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana_(soundtrack) "Hannah Montana (soundtrack)") in October of that year. The record was both a critical and commercial success, topping the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") chart in the United States; it went on to sell over three million copies worldwide. With the release of the soundtrack, Cyrus became the first act within [the Walt Disney Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company "The Walt Disney Company") to have deals in television, film, consumer products, and music.

Cyrus signed a four-album deal with [Hollywood Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Records "Hollywood Records") to distribute music under her own persona. She released the two-disc album *[Hannah Montana 2: Meet Miley Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana_2:_Meet_Miley_Cyrus "Hannah Montana 2: Meet Miley Cyrus")* in June 2007. The first disc was credited as the second soundtrack by "Hannah Montana", while the second disc served as Cyrus's debut studio album. The album became her second to reach the top of the *Billboard* 200, and has sold over three million copies. Months after the release of the project, "[See You Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/See_You_Again_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "See You Again (Miley Cyrus song)")" (2007) was released as the lead single from the album. The song was a commercial success, and has sold over two million copies in the United States since its release. She collaborated with her father on the single "[Ready, Set, Don't Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready,_Set,_Don%27t_Go "Ready, Set, Don't Go")" (2007). Next Cyrus embarked on her highly successful [Best of Both Worlds Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_of_Both_Worlds_Tour_(Miley_Cyrus) "Best of Both Worlds Tour (Miley Cyrus)") (2007–08) to promote its release. [Ticketmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticketmaster "Ticketmaster") officials commented that "there [hadn't] been a demand of this level or intensity since [The Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles "The Beatles") or [Elvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley")." The tour's success led to the theatrical release of the 3D concert film *[Hannah Montana & Miley Cyrus: Best of Both Worlds Concert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana_%26_Miley_Cyrus:_Best_of_Both_Worlds_Concert "Hannah Montana & Miley Cyrus: Best of Both Worlds Concert")* (2008). While initially intended to be a limited release, the film's success led to a longer run.

Cyrus and friend [Mandy Jiroux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandy_Jiroux "Mandy Jiroux") began posting videos on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") in February 2008, referring to the clips as "The Miley and Mandy Show"; the videos garnered a large online following. In April 2008, several pictures of Cyrus in her underwear and swimsuit were leaked online by a teenager who hacked her [Gmail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gmail "Gmail") account. Further controversy erupted when it was reported that the then-15-year-old Cyrus had posed topless during a photo shoot by [Annie Leibovitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Leibovitz "Annie Leibovitz") for *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")*. *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")* subsequently clarified that although the shot left the impression that Cyrus was bare-breasted, she was wrapped in a bed sheet and was not topless.

Cyrus went on to release her second studio album, *[Breakout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakout_(Miley_Cyrus_album) "Breakout (Miley Cyrus album)")* (2008), in June of that year. The album earned the highest first-week sales of her career thus far and became her third to top the *Billboard* 200. Cyrus later starred with [John Travolta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Travolta "John Travolta") in the animated film *[Bolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(2008_film) "Bolt (2008 film)")* (2008), her debut as a film actress; she also co-wrote the song "[I Thought I Lost You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Thought_I_Lost_You "I Thought I Lost You")" (2008) for the film, which she sings as a duet with Travolta. The film was both a critical and commercial success and earned her a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award") nomination for [Best Original Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Original_Song "Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song").

In March 2009, Cyrus released "[The Climb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Climb_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "The Climb (Miley Cyrus song)")" (2009) as a single from the soundtrack to [Hannah Montana: The Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana:_The_Movie "Hannah Montana: The Movie"). It was met with a warm critical and commercial reaction, becoming a crossover hit in both pop and country music formats. The soundtrack, which features the single, went on to become Cyrus's fourth entry to top the *Billboard* 200; at age 16, she became the youngest artist in history to have four number-one albums on the chart. She released her [fourth soundtrack as Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana_3 "Hannah Montana 3") in July 2009, which debuted at number two on the *Billboard* 200. Cyrus later launched her first fashion line, Miley Cyrus and [Max Azria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Azria "Max Azria"), through [Walmart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walmart "Walmart"). It was promoted by the release of "[Party in the U.S.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_in_the_U.S.A. "Party in the U.S.A.")" (2009) and the [EP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") *[The Time of Our Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Time_of_Our_Lives_(EP) "The Time of Our Lives (EP)")* (2009). Cyrus said the record was "a transitioning album [...] really to introduce people to what I want my next record to sound like and with time I will be able to do that a little more". "Party in the U.S.A." became one of Cyrus's most successful singles to date and is considered to be one of her [signature songs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signature_song "Signature song"). She embarked on her first world tour, the [Wonder World Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_World_Tour_(Miley_Cyrus) "Wonder World Tour (Miley Cyrus)") (2009) which was a critical and commercial success. On December 7, 2009, Cyrus performed for [Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II "Elizabeth II") and other members of the [British royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_royal_family "British royal family") at the [Royal Variety Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Variety_Performance "Royal Variety Performance") in [Blackpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool "Blackpool"), [Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancashire "Lancashire"). *Billboard* [ranked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Miley_Cyrus "List of awards and nominations received by Miley Cyrus") her as the fourth best-selling female music artist of 2009.

### 2010–2012: New image with *Can't Be Tamed* and focus on acting

Hoping to foster a more mature image, Cyrus starred in the film *[The Last Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Song_(2010_film) "The Last Song (2010 film)")* (2010), based on the [Nicholas Sparks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Sparks "Nicholas Sparks") novel. It was met with negative critical reviews but was a box office hit. Cyrus further attempted to shift her image with the release of her third studio album, *[Can't Be Tamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Be_Tamed "Can't Be Tamed")* (2010). The album featured a more dance-oriented sound than her prior releases and stirred a considerable amount of controversy over its lyrical content and Cyrus's live performances. It sold 106,000 copies in its first week of release and became her first studio album not to top the *Billboard* 200 chart in the United States. Cyrus released her [final soundtrack as Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana_Forever_(soundtrack) "Hannah Montana Forever (soundtrack)") in October 2010; it was seen as a commercial failure due to its low position on the charts compared to her previous albums.

Cyrus was the subject of further controversy when a video posted online in December 2010 showed her, then aged eighteen, smoking [salvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvia_divinorum "Salvia divinorum") with a [bong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bong "Bong"). 2010 ended with her ranking at number thirteen on the [*Forbes* Celebrity 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_Celebrity_100 "Forbes Celebrity 100") list. She embarked on her worldwide [Gypsy Heart Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsy_Heart_Tour "Gypsy Heart Tour") in April 2011 which had no North American dates; she cited her various controversial moments as the reason, claiming that she only wanted to travel where she felt "the most love". Following the release of *Can't Be Tamed*, Cyrus officially parted ways with Hollywood Records. With her obligations to *Hannah Montana* fulfilled, Cyrus announced her plans to take a hiatus from music so she could focus on her acting career. She confirmed she would not be going to college.

Cyrus hosted the March 5, 2011, episode of *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* where she poked fun at her recent controversies. That November it was announced that Cyrus would be the voice of Mavis in the animated film *[Hotel Transylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Transylvania_(film) "Hotel Transylvania (film)")*; however by February 2012 she was dropped from the project and replaced with [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez "Selena Gomez"). At the time Cyrus said her reason for leaving the movie was wanting to work on her music; later it was revealed the real reason behind her exit was because she bought her then-boyfriend Liam Hemsworth a birthday cake shaped like a penis and licked it. She made an appearance on the [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV") television series *[Punk'd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk%27d "Punk'd")* with [Kelly Osbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Osbourne "Kelly Osbourne") and [Khloé Kardashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khlo%C3%A9_Kardashian "Khloé Kardashian"). Cyrus starred alongside [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore "Demi Moore") in the independent film *[LOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LOL_(2012_film) "LOL (2012 film)")* (2012). The film had a limited release; it was a critical and commercial failure. She starred in the comedy film *[So Undercover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/So_Undercover "So Undercover")* playing the role of an undercover FBI agent at a college sorority.

Cyrus released a string of live performances known as the *[Backyard Sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_Sessions "Backyard Sessions")* on YouTube during the spring and summer of 2012; the performances were of classic songs she personally liked. She collaborated with producers [Rock Mafia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_Mafia "Rock Mafia") on their song "Morning Sun" (2012), which was made available for free download online. She had previously appeared in the music video for their debut single, "[The Big Bang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_(song) "The Big Bang (song)")" (2010). Cyrus later provided guest vocals on "[Decisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decisions_(song) "Decisions (song)")" (2012) by [Borgore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgore "Borgore"). Both Cyrus and Hemsworth appeared in the song's music video. She went on to guest star as Missi in two episodes of the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") sitcom *[Two and a Half Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_and_a_Half_Men "Two and a Half Men")*. Cyrus drew significant media attention when she cut her traditionally long, brown hair in favor of a blonde, [pixie cut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixie_cut "Pixie cut"); she commented that she had "never felt more [herself] in [her] whole life" and that "it really changed [her] life."

### 2013–2015: *Bangerz* and *Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz*

In 2013, Cyrus hired [Larry Rudolph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Rudolph "Larry Rudolph") to be her manager, best known for previously representing [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears"). It was confirmed that Cyrus had signed with [RCA Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCA_Records "RCA Records") for her future releases. She worked with producers such as [Pharrell Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharrell_Williams "Pharrell Williams") and [Mike Will Made-It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Will_Made-It "Mike Will Made-It") on her fourth studio album, resulting in a hip hop-influenced sound. She collaborated with numerous hip hop artists releases and appeared on the [Snoop Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snoop_Dogg "Snoop Dogg") song "[Ashtrays and Heartbreaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtrays_and_Heartbreaks "Ashtrays and Heartbreaks")" (2013), released as the lead single from his twelfth studio album, *[Reincarnated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reincarnated_(album) "Reincarnated (album)")*. She collaborated with [will.i.am](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will.i.am "Will.i.am") on the song "[Fall Down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_Down_(will.i.am_song) "Fall Down (will.i.am song)")" (2013), released as a promotional single that same month. The song entered the *Billboard* Hot 100 at number fifty-eight, marking her first appearance on the chart since "[Can't Be Tamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Be_Tamed_(song) "Can't Be Tamed (song)")" (2010). She provided guest vocals on the [Lil Twist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Twist "Lil Twist") song "Twerk", which also featured vocals by [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber "Justin Bieber"). The song was unreleased for unknown reasons but leaked online. On May 23, 2013, it was confirmed that Cyrus would be featured on the [Mike Will Made It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Will_Made_It "Mike Will Made It") single "[23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/23_(Mike_Will_Made_It_song) "23 (Mike Will Made It song)")", with [Wiz Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiz_Khalifa "Wiz Khalifa") and [Juicy J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juicy_J "Juicy J"). The single went on to peak at number eleven on the Hot 100, and had sold over one million copies worldwide as of 2013.

Cyrus released her new single "[We Can't Stop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Can%27t_Stop "We Can't Stop")" on June 3. Touted as her comeback single, it became a worldwide commercial success, topping charts in territories such as the United Kingdom. The song's music video set the [Vevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vevo "Vevo") record for most views within twenty-four hours of release and became the first to reach 100 million views on the site. Cyrus performed with [Robin Thicke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Thicke "Robin Thicke") at the [2013 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2013 MTV Video Music Awards"), a performance that resulted in widespread media attention and public scrutiny. Her simulated sex acts with a foam finger were described as "disturbing" and the whole performance as "cringe-worthy". Cyrus released "[Wrecking Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecking_Ball_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)")" (2013) as the second single from *Bangerz* on the same day as the VMAs. The accompanying music video, which showed her swinging naked on a wrecking ball, was viewed over nineteen million times within 24 hours of its release, and drew criticism from some for allegedly [objectifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_objectification "Sexual objectification") Cyrus, including fellow singer [Sinéad O'Connor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin%C3%A9ad_O%27Connor "Sinéad O'Connor"), who said that "you will obscure your talent by allowing yourself to be pimped, whether it's the music business or yourself doing the pimping". Despite this, the single became Cyrus's first to top the Hot 100 in the US, and maintained the number-one spot for three weeks. It sold over two million copies.

On October 2, 2013, MTV aired the documentary *[Miley: The Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley:_The_Movement "Miley: The Movement")*, that chronicled the recording of her fourth studio album *[Bangerz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangerz "Bangerz")*, which was released on October 4. The album was a commercial success, debuting at number one on the *Billboard* 200 with first week sales of 270,000 copies. On October 5, Cyrus hosted *Saturday Night Live* for the second time. On November 5, Cyrus featured on rapper [Future](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_(rapper) "Future (rapper)")'s "[Real and True](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_and_True "Real and True")" with [Mr. Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Hudson "Mr. Hudson"); an accompanying music video premiered five days later on November 10, 2013. On December 9, 2013, she was declared Artist of the Year by MTV.

On January 29, 2014, she played an acoustic concert show on *[MTV Unplugged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Unplugged "MTV Unplugged")*, performing songs from *Bangerz* featuring a guest appearance by [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna "Madonna"). It became the highest-rated *MTV Unplugged* in the past decade, with over 1.7 million streams. Cyrus was also featured in the [Marc Jacobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Jacobs "Marc Jacobs") Spring 2014 campaign along with [Natalie Westling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Westling "Natalie Westling") and Esmerelda Seay Reynolds. She launched her controversial [Bangerz Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangerz_Tour "Bangerz Tour") (2014) that year, which was positively received by critics. Two months into her tour, Cyrus's [Alaskan Klee Kai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaskan_Klee_Kai "Alaskan Klee Kai") was found mauled to death at her home after fighting with a coyote. Two weeks later, Cyrus suffered an allergic reaction to the [antibiotic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibiotic "Antibiotic") [cephalexin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cephalexin "Cephalexin"), prescribed to treat a sinus infection, resulting in her hospitalization in [Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri "Kansas City, Missouri"). Though she rescheduled some of her US tour dates, she resumed the tour two weeks later, beginning with the European leg.

While collaborating with [the Flaming Lips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flaming_Lips "The Flaming Lips") on their remake of the Beatles' *[Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sgt._Pepper%27s_Lonely_Hearts_Club_Band "Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band")*, *[With a Little Help from My Fwends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/With_a_Little_Help_from_My_Fwends "With a Little Help from My Fwends")*, Cyrus began working with [Wayne Coyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_Coyne "Wayne Coyne") on her fifth studio album. She said that she was taking her time to focus on the music, and that the album would not be released until she felt it was ready. Coyne compared his collaborative material with Cyrus to the catalogs of [Pink Floyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Floyd "Pink Floyd") and [Portishead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portishead_(band) "Portishead (band)") and described their sound as being "a slightly wiser, sadder, more true version" of Cyrus's pop music output. Cyrus also worked on the films *[The Night Before](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Night_Before_(2015_film) "The Night Before (2015 film)")* (2015) and *[A Very Murray Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Very_Murray_Christmas "A Very Murray Christmas")* (2015) during this period; both roles were cameos.

Reports began to surface in 2015 that Cyrus was working on two albums simultaneously, one of which she hoped to release at no charge. This was confirmed by her manager who claimed she was willing to end her contract with RCA Records if they refused to let her release a free album. Cyrus was the host of the [2015 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2015 MTV Video Music Awards"), making her its first openly [pansexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pansexual "Pansexual") host, and gave a surprise performance of a new song "[Dooo It!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dooo_It! "Dooo It!")" (2015) during the show's finale. Immediately following the performance, Cyrus announced that her fifth studio album, *[Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus_%26_Her_Dead_Petz "Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz")* (2015), was available for free streaming on [SoundCloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoundCloud "SoundCloud"). The album was written and produced primarily by Cyrus, and has been called [experimental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_music "Experimental music") and [psychedelic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_music "Psychedelic music"), with elements of [psychedelic pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_pop "Psychedelic pop"), [psychedelic rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_rock "Psychedelic rock"), and [alternative pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_pop "Alternative pop").

### 2016–2017: *The Voice* and *Younger Now*

In 2015, following the release of her fifth studio album, Cyrus resumed working on her sixth studio effort. She was a key advisor during the [tenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(U.S._season_10) "The Voice (U.S. season 10)") of the reality singing competition *[The Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(American_TV_series) "The Voice (American TV series)")*. In March 2016, Cyrus had signed on as a coach for the [eleventh season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(U.S._season_11) "The Voice (U.S. season 11)") of *The Voice* as a replacement for [Gwen Stefani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwen_Stefani "Gwen Stefani"); Cyrus became the youngest coach to appear in any incarnation of the series. In September 2016, Cyrus co-starred in *[Crisis in Six Scenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crisis_in_Six_Scenes "Crisis in Six Scenes")*, a television series [Woody Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Allen "Woody Allen") created for [Amazon Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Studios "Amazon Studios"). She played a radical activist who causes chaos in a conservative 1960s household while hiding from the police. On September 17, 2016, she appeared on *[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")* and covered [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan")'s "Baby, I'm In the Mood for You". Cyrus also had an uncredited voice cameo as [Mainframe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainframe_(comics) "Mainframe (comics)") in the superhero film *[Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Galaxy_Vol._2 "Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2")*, released in May 2017.

On May 11, 2017, Cyrus released "[Malibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Malibu (Miley Cyrus song)")" as the lead single from her sixth album. The single debuted at No. 64 on the *Billboard* Hot 100 and peaked at No. 10 on the chart on its second week. On June 9, Cyrus released "[Inspired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspired_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Inspired (Miley Cyrus song)")" after performing the song at the [One Love Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Love_Manchester "One Love Manchester") benefit concert. It served as a promotional single from the album. On August 8, Cyrus announced that her sixth studio album would be titled *[Younger Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Younger_Now "Younger Now")* and would be released on September 29, 2017. The album's title track was released as the second single from the album on August 18 and debuted and peaked at No. 79 on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"). On August 27, Cyrus performed the track at the [2017 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2017 MTV Video Music Awards"). On September 15, she performed "Malibu", "[Younger Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Younger_Now_(song) "Younger Now (song)")", "[See You Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/See_You_Again_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "See You Again (Miley Cyrus song)")", "[Party in the U.S.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_in_the_U.S.A. "Party in the U.S.A.")" and a cover of the [Roberta Flack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Flack "Roberta Flack") hit "[The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_First_Time_Ever_I_Saw_Your_Face "The First Time Ever I Saw Your Face")" (written by [Ewan McColl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ewan_McColl "Ewan McColl")) for the [BBC Radio 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_1 "BBC Radio 1") [Live Lounge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Lounge "Live Lounge"). On October 2, as part of her one-week regular musical appearances on *[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")*, Cyrus sang her 2009 hit single "The Climb" for the first time since 2011 alongside a cover of "[No Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Freedom "No Freedom")" by [Dido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dido_(singer) "Dido (singer)") to honor the victims of the [Las Vegas shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Las_Vegas_shooting "2017 Las Vegas shooting"). The former song has since been performed at multiple charity events, protests, and marches, including at the [March For Our Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_for_Our_Lives "March for Our Lives") demonstrations in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C. "Washington, D.C.") That same year, Cyrus returned as a coach in the [thirteenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(U.S._season_13) "The Voice (U.S. season 13)") of *The Voice* after taking a one-season hiatus. On October 5, 2017, Cyrus confirmed that she would not be returning to *The Voice* for season fourteen. On October 30, 2017, Cyrus revealed she would not release any further singles from *Younger Now*, nor would she tour for it.

### 2018–2019: *She Is Coming* and *Black Mirror*

Before the release of *Younger Now* in September 2017, Cyrus expressed she was "already two songs deep on the next [album]". Producers attached to her seventh studio album included previous collaborator [Mike Will Made It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Will_Made_It "Mike Will Made It") and new collaborators [Mark Ronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ronson "Mark Ronson") and [Andrew Wyatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Wyatt "Andrew Wyatt"). Her first collaboration with Ronson, "[Nothing Breaks Like a Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing_Breaks_Like_a_Heart "Nothing Breaks Like a Heart")" from his 2019 album *[Late Night Feelings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Night_Feelings "Late Night Feelings")*, was released on November 29, 2018, to great commercial reception, especially in Europe, where it peaked at number two on the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart") as well as in [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland "Ireland") and topped the charts in several [Eastern European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Europe "Eastern Europe") countries including [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary "Hungary") or [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia "Croatia").

During the first quarter of 2019, Cyrus became quite notable for her cover songs. Having already taken part in [MusiCares Person of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MusiCares_Person_of_the_Year "MusiCares Person of the Year") in 2018 celebrating [Fleetwood Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleetwood_Mac "Fleetwood Mac"), she returned the year after to honor the career of her godmother [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton "Dolly Parton") by performing "[Islands in the Stream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islands_in_the_Stream_(song) "Islands in the Stream (song)")" alongside Canadian singer-songwriter [Shawn Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Mendes "Shawn Mendes"), with whom she also performed "[In My Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_My_Blood_(Shawn_Mendes_song) "In My Blood (Shawn Mendes song)")" a couple days later at the [61st Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/61st_Grammy_Awards "61st Grammy Awards"). Cyrus's other covers include her version of [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande "Ariana Grande")'s "[No Tears Left to Cry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Tears_Left_to_Cry "No Tears Left to Cry")" for [BBC Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio "BBC Radio")'s [Live Lounge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Lounge "Live Lounge"), her participation at the [Chris Cornell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Cornell "Chris Cornell") tribute concert I Am The Highway, where she sang "As Hope & Promise Fade" as well as her cover record of [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John "Elton John")'s "[Don't Let The Sun Go Down On Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Let_the_Sun_Go_Down_on_Me "Don't Let the Sun Go Down on Me")", included in the tribute album *[Revamp: Reimagining the Songs of Elton John & Bernie Taupin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revamp:_Reimagining_the_Songs_of_Elton_John_%26_Bernie_Taupin "Revamp: Reimagining the Songs of Elton John & Bernie Taupin").* Cyrus also honored John at the I'm Still Standing: A Grammy Salute to Elton John tribute concert in 2018, where she covered "[The Bitch Is Back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bitch_Is_Back "The Bitch Is Back")".

On May 31, 2019, Cyrus tweeted that her seventh studio album would be titled *She Is Miley Cyrus* and would comprise three six-song EPs, which would be released before the full-length album: *[She Is Coming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_Is_Coming "She Is Coming")* on May 31, *She Is Here* in the summer, and *She Is Everything* in the fall. *She Is Coming*, which also included vocal collaborations with [RuPaul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuPaul "RuPaul"), [Swae Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swae_Lee "Swae Lee"), [Mike Will Made It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Will_Made_It "Mike Will Made It") and [Ghostface Killah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghostface_Killah "Ghostface Killah"), debuted at number five on the US *Billboard* 200 with 36,000 album-equivalent units, while the lead single "[Mother's Daughter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother%27s_Daughter_(song) "Mother's Daughter (song)")" entered at number 54 on the US *Billboard* Hot 100. The [Wuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuki "Wuki") remix of "Mother's Daughter" received a nomination for [Best Remixed Recording](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Remixed_Recording,_Non-Classical "Grammy Award for Best Remixed Recording, Non-Classical") at the [62nd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/62nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards "62nd Annual Grammy Awards") while the original music video won two [MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Awards "MTV Video Music Awards"). Cyrus promoted the EP with a summer European tour that visited A-list festivals like [Glastonbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury_Festival "Glastonbury Festival") and [Primavera Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primavera_Sound "Primavera Sound").

Cyrus starred in "[Rachel, Jack and Ashley Too](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel,_Jack_and_Ashley_Too "Rachel, Jack and Ashley Too")", an episode of the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") sci-fi series *[Black Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Mirror "Black Mirror")*, which was filmed in South Africa in November 2018. It was released on Netflix on June 5, 2019. In the episode, she played fictional pop star Ashley O and voiced her [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") doll extension, Ashley Too. The plot was compared to [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears")'s [conservatorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatorship "Conservatorship") and the [Free Britney movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Britney "Free Britney"), which Cyrus has been an advocate for. The music video for the song "[On a Roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_a_Roll "On a Roll")" from the episode was released on June 13; the song itself and the B-side "Right Where I Belong" were released to digital platforms the next day.

On June 27, it was revealed that Cyrus had collaborated with [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande "Ariana Grande") and [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey "Lana Del Rey") on "[Don't Call Me Angel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Call_Me_Angel "Don't Call Me Angel")", the lead single of the soundtrack to the 2019 film *[Charlie's Angels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie%27s_Angels_(2019_film) "Charlie's Angels (2019 film)").* It was released on September 13, 2019. In August 2019, Cyrus released "[Slide Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slide_Away_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Slide Away (Miley Cyrus song)")", her first song since announcing her separation from then-husband Hemsworth. The song hinted at their breakup and contained lyrics such as "Move on, we're not 17, I'm not who I used to be". A music video was released in September 2019 that contained further references, including a [ten of hearts playing card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_52-card_deck "Standard 52-card deck") at the bottom of a pool to represent the end of her decade-long relationship with Hemsworth.

### 2020–2022: *Plastic Hearts* and television projects

On August 14, 2020, Cyrus released the lead single from her seventh studio album, "[Midnight Sky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Sky "Midnight Sky")" and confirmed the cancellation of the EPs *She Is Here* and *She Is Everything* due to major recent changes in her life that did not fit the essence of the project, including her divorce from Hemsworth, and the burning of the couple's house during the [Woolsey Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolsey_Fire "Woolsey Fire") in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"). "Midnight Sky" became her highest-charting solo single since "[Malibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Malibu (Miley Cyrus song)")" in 2017, peaking at number 14 on the US *Billboard* Hot 100. Internationally, in the United Kingdom the song has thus far peaked at number five on the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart"). The track was later [mashed up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashup_(music) "Mashup (music)") with [Stevie Nicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Nicks "Stevie Nicks")' "[Edge of Seventeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_of_Seventeen "Edge of Seventeen")".

In October, Cyrus had a third [Backyard Session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_Sessions "Backyard Sessions") on [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV") and announced via Instagram that her seventh studio album *[Plastic Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic_Hearts "Plastic Hearts")* would be released on November 27, 2020. It was previously intended to be called *She Is Miley Cyrus*, completing the EP series once finalized. The album was released to positive reviews from critics and performed well, debuting at number two on the Billboard 200, with 60,000 units, becoming her twelfth top ten entry on the chart. With that entry, Cyrus broke the record for attaining the most US [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") top-five albums in the 21st century by a female music artist. *Plastic Hearts* marked a step of Cyrus into [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") and [glam rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glam_rock "Glam rock") music and spawned two other singles: "[Prisoner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Prisoner (Miley Cyrus song)")" featuring English singer [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa "Dua Lipa") and "[Angels like You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_like_You "Angels like You")", which peaked at 8 and 66 respectively in the United Kingdom. The album also included vocal collaborations with [Billy Idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Idol "Billy Idol") and [Joan Jett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Jett "Joan Jett"). Due to popular demand and social media virality, Cyrus included the live covers of [Blondie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blondie_(band) "Blondie (band)")'s "[Heart of Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_Glass_(song) "Heart of Glass (song)")" and [The Cranberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cranberries "The Cranberries")' "[Zombie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie_(The_Cranberries_song) "Zombie (The Cranberries song)")".

Cyrus won a [2020 Webby Special Achievement Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Webby_Awards "2020 Webby Awards"). In February 2021, Cyrus performed at the first TikTok Tailgate show in [Tampa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa,_Florida "Tampa, Florida"), for 7,500 [vaccinated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_vaccine "COVID-19 vaccine") healthcare workers. It served as a pre-show before [Super Bowl LV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LV "Super Bowl LV"). It aired on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok") and [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS"). The performance was featured in the music video for "[Angels like You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_like_You "Angels like You")". In March 2021, Cyrus departed RCA and signed with [Columbia Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Records "Columbia Records"), a sister label of RCA under the [Sony Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Music_Entertainment "Sony Music Entertainment") umbrella. That same month Cyrus embraced her days as Hannah Montana and wrote an open letter to the character on social media for the show's 15th anniversary, despite all statements that her days as Montana gave Cyrus an [identity crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_crisis "Identity crisis"). Rumors about a possible revival of the show have been around ever since. ### 2023–2025: *Endless Summer Vacation*, *Something Beautiful*, and other projects

In late 2022, Cyrus and her longtime collaborator [Mike Will Made It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Will_Made_It "Mike Will Made It") teased new music to be released in 2023. Days later, during the second edition of *Miley's New Year's Eve Party*, the singer's next lead single, "[Flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flowers_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Flowers (Miley Cyrus song)")", was announced. It was released on January 13, 2023, and debuted at number one on the *Billboard* Hot 100, [Global 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Global_200 "Billboard Global 200"), and [Global Excl. US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Global_Excl._US "Billboard Global Excl. US") charts. With thirteen weeks each atop the Global 200 and Global Excl. US charts, it became the longest-running leader on the former chart, at the time. Topping the Hot 100 for eight non-consecutive weeks, it was Cyrus's second US number-one single—her first in a decade, since "[Wrecking Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecking_Ball_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)")" (2013)—and her longest-running chart-topper. "Flowers" became a global success, reaching number one in 37 countries, including Australia, Canada, France, Germany, and the UK. It was 2023's most-streamed and most-downloaded song across various platforms in numerous countries, and the most-consumed song on US radio. On Spotify, the single became the fastest track to surpass 100 million and 1 billion plays (7 and 112 days), at the time. Due to 57 weeks atop the *Billboard* [Adult Contemporary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_Contemporary_(chart) "Adult Contemporary (chart)") chart, it became the longest-running number one song on any *Billboard* [airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airplay "Airplay") chart in history. It also earned the most cumulative weeks atop all *Billboard* airplay charts (106 weeks). "Flowers" topped the year-end charts in various regions, and ranked as the second best-performing song of the year on the [year-end Hot 100 chart of 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Year-End_Hot_100_singles_of_2023 "Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2023"). According to the [International Federation of the Phonographic Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_the_Phonographic_Industry "International Federation of the Phonographic Industry") (IFPI), it was the [best-selling song in 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_singles#Best-selling_singles_by_year_worldwide "List of best-selling singles") globally. A demo version of the track was made available in March 2023. "Flowers" was certified seven-times platinum by the RIAA in March 2025.

Cyrus's eighth studio album, *[Endless Summer Vacation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endless_Summer_Vacation "Endless Summer Vacation")*, was released on March 10, 2023. Her first studio effort with Columbia Records, she described the record as "[her] love letter to LA", which reflects upon physical and mental growth she experienced during production. Cyrus decided to primarily focus on songcraft, before handling production, on the album—a pop and [dance-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance-pop "Dance-pop") record. It debuted at number three on the US *Billboard* 200, earning 119,000 album-equivalent units. "[River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "River (Miley Cyrus song)")", the second single off the record, was released on March 13, 2023, and reached number 32 on the US Hot 100. "[Jaded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaded_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Jaded (Miley Cyrus song)")", which peaked at number 56, became the third single in April 2023. *Endless Summer Vacation* was the 19th best-selling album globally in 2023, according to the IFPI.

A documentary concert special—as part of Cyrus's [Backyard Sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_Sessions "Backyard Sessions") series—titled *Endless Summer Vacation (Backyard Sessions)*, premiered on [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%2B "Disney+") on March 10, accompanying the album's release. Executive produced by Cyrus, it features her performing songs from the album, and her 2009 single "[The Climb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Climb_(song) "The Climb (song)")", with an appearance by [Rufus Wainwright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus_Wainwright "Rufus Wainwright"). In June, she voiced Van, a nihilistic female creature, in the second season of the Netflix [adult animated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_animated "Adult animated") sitcom *[Human Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Resources_(TV_series) "Human Resources (TV series)")*. An updated version of Cyrus's Disney+ special, titled *Endless Summer Vacation: Continued (Backyard Sessions)*, premiered on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") on August 24, 2023. The day after, Cyrus released the single "[Used to Be Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Used_to_Be_Young "Used to Be Young")", which was included in the digital reissue of *Endless Summer Vacation* and debuted at number eight in the US. On October 20, [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton "Dolly Parton") released a rock re-recording of Cyrus's "Wrecking Ball" featuring her as a guest vocalist, as the final single off her studio album *[Rockstar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockstar_(Dolly_Parton_album) "Rockstar (Dolly Parton album)")* (2023). *Billboard* ranked Cyrus as the ninth-best-selling musician of 2023.

At the [66th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "66th Annual Grammy Awards"), Cyrus received six nominations, including [Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Album of the Year") and [Best Pop Vocal Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Vocal_Album "Grammy Award for Best Pop Vocal Album") for *Endless Summer Vacation*, and won [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Record of the Year") and [Best Pop Solo Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Solo_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance") for "Flowers"—her first set of wins. She also performed "Flowers" at the ceremony. The track won [International Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award_for_International_Song "Brit Award for International Song") at the [2024 Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Awards_2024 "Brit Awards 2024"), marking Cyrus's first win. She appeared in a cameo role as Tiffany Plastercaster—inspired by [Cynthia Plaster Caster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynthia_Plaster_Caster "Cynthia Plaster Caster")—in the [Ethan Coen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Coen "Ethan Coen")-directed comedy road film *[Drive-Away Dolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive-Away_Dolls "Drive-Away Dolls")*, released in February 2024. In May 2024, as one of 16 acts featured on the [Talking Heads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talking_Heads "Talking Heads") tribute album *[Everyone's Getting Involved: A Tribute to Talking Heads' Stop Making Sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everyone%27s_Getting_Involved:_A_Tribute_to_Talking_Heads%27_Stop_Making_Sense "Everyone's Getting Involved: A Tribute to Talking Heads' Stop Making Sense")*, Cyrus provided a synth-driven dance-pop rendition of the band's "[Psycho Killer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psycho_Killer "Psycho Killer")". She co-wrote and recorded "Beautiful That Way" for the [soundtrack album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Showgirl_(soundtrack) "The Last Showgirl (soundtrack)") to the [Gia Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gia_Coppola "Gia Coppola")-directed *[The Last Showgirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Showgirl "The Last Showgirl")*, released as a promotional single on December 9. Later that month, *Billions Club Live with Miley Cyrus: A Concert Film*, documenting her June 2025 Paris concert for Spotify, was released on the platform. In December 2025, Cyrus released "[Dream as One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_as_One "Dream as One")" for [the soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash_(soundtrack) "Avatar: Fire and Ash (soundtrack)") to *[Avatar: Fire and Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash "Avatar: Fire and Ash")*; it was nominated for [Best Original Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Original_Song "Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song") at the [83rd Golden Globes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/83rd_Golden_Globes "83rd Golden Globes").

## Artistry

### Influences and musical style

Cyrus has mainly been described as a [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music") singer, although her later work has earned her the title of a "millennial ambassador" for [rock music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music"). Her music has also incorporated elements of many other different genres including [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"), [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop"), indie, and [psychedelic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_music "Psychedelic music"). Cyrus has cited [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley") as her biggest inspiration. She has also cited artists such as [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna "Madonna"), [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey "Lana Del Rey"), [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton "Dolly Parton"), [Timbaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbaland "Timbaland"), [Whitney Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Houston "Whitney Houston"), [Christina Aguilera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Aguilera "Christina Aguilera"), [Joan Jett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Jett "Joan Jett"), [Lil Kim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Kim "Lil Kim"), [Shania Twain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shania_Twain "Shania Twain"), [Hanson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanson_(band) "Hanson (band)"), [OneRepublic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OneRepublic "OneRepublic"), and [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears") as influences. Since the beginning of her music career, Cyrus has been described as being predominantly a pop artist. Her *Hannah Montana 2: Meet Miley Cyrus* debut studio effort was characterized as sounding similar to her releases as "Hannah Montana" featuring a [pop-rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop-rock "Pop-rock") and [bubblegum pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubblegum_pop "Bubblegum pop") sound. Cyrus hoped that the release of *Breakout* (2008) would help distance her from this sound; the record featured Cyrus experimenting with various genres. Cyrus co-wrote eight songs for the album and was quoted as saying: "I just hope this record showcases that, more than anything, I'm a [song]writer." The songs on her early releases feature lyrics on the topics of love and relationships.

Cyrus possesses a [mezzo-soprano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mezzo-soprano "Mezzo-soprano") [vocal range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_type "Voice type"), although her vocals were once described as [alto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alto "Alto") with a "Nashville [twang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guttural_vocals "Guttural vocals")" in both her spoken and singing voice. Her voice has a distinctive raspy sound to it, similar in vein to that of [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_(singer) "Pink (singer)") and [Amy Winehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Winehouse "Amy Winehouse"). On "Party in the U.S.A." (2009), her vocals feature [belter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belt_(music) "Belt (music)") [refrains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refrain "Refrain"), while those on the song "Obsessed" (2009) are described as "husky". Releases such as "The Climb" (2009) and "These Four Walls" (2008) feature elements of [country music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") and showcase Cyrus's "twangy vocals". Cyrus experimented with an [electropop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electropop "Electropop") sound on "[Fly on the Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_on_the_Wall_(song) "Fly on the Wall (song)")" (2008), a genre that she would explore further with the release of *Can't Be Tamed* (2010), her third studio album. It was initially intended to feature [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music") elements prior to its completion, and Cyrus claimed after its release that it could be her final pop album. The album's songs speak of Cyrus's desire to achieve freedom in both her personal and professional life. She began working on *Bangerz* (2013) during a musical hiatus, and described the record as having a "dirty south feel" prior to its release. Critics noted the use of [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop") and [synthpop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthpop "Synthpop") on the album. The album's songs are placed in chronological order telling the story of her failed relationship with Liam Hemsworth. Cyrus described *Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz* (2015) as "a little psychedelic, but still in that [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music") world". For her rock-influenced album, *Plastic Hearts*, Cyrus cited Britney Spears and [Metallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallica "Metallica") as major influences. Inspired by pop and dance-pop, *Endless Summer Vacation* (2023) "feels like a recap of her career's 15-plus years, with Cyrus breezing through genres with the ease of a well-seasoned tourist". Cyrus related its overall concept to her affection for Los Angeles.

### Live performances

Cyrus's live performances have been noted for combining theatrical pop staging with a vocal style influenced by rock and country music. Reviewing a 2010 performance at the [House of Blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Blues "House of Blues"), Ann Powers of the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* described Cyrus as a capable rock singer with considerable vocal power and a rough quality to her voice, and noted that the concert included covers of songs associated with [Joan Jett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Jett "Joan Jett"), including "I Love Rock 'n' Roll", "Cherry Bomb" and "Bad Reputation". During the [Bangerz Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangerz_Tour "Bangerz Tour") in 2014, [Alexis Petridis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexis_Petridis "Alexis Petridis") of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* contrasted the production's elaborate visual spectacle with an acoustic portion of the show, writing that performances of [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan")'s "You're Gonna Make Me Lonesome When You Go" and [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton "Dolly Parton")'s "[Jolene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jolene "Jolene")" highlighted the earthier qualities of Cyrus's voice and her background as a Nashville performer. A 2015 *Los Angeles Times* review of the [Milky Milky Milk Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milky_Milky_Milk_Tour "Milky Milky Milk Tour") similarly credited Cyrus's live performances with adding force and emotional intensity to material from *[Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus_%26_Her_Dead_Petz "Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz")*, particularly its ballads.

Cover songs have become a recurring feature of Cyrus's live repertoire. Her 2012 [Backyard Sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_Sessions "Backyard Sessions") included versions of "Lilac Wine", "Look What They've Done to My Song Ma" and Parton's "Jolene"; the latter continued to appear in her subsequent concert sets. Reviewing her first live album, *[Attention: Miley Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention:_Miley_Live "Attention: Miley Live")* (2022), Dani Blum of *[Pitchfork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitchfork_(website) "Pitchfork (website)")* attributed Cyrus's effectiveness as a cover performer to the strength and texture of her voice; seven of the album's twenty original-edition tracks contained complete or partial covers, including "Jolene", "[Nothing Compares 2 U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing_Compares_2_U "Nothing Compares 2 U")" and "[Bang Bang (My Baby Shot Me Down)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Bang_(My_Baby_Shot_Me_Down) "Bang Bang (My Baby Shot Me Down)")".

#### Pre-2023

Cyrus's performances have also attracted controversy and extensive media attention. Her performance of "[Party in the U.S.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_in_the_U.S.A. "Party in the U.S.A.")" at the [2009 Teen Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Teen_Choice_Awards "2009 Teen Choice Awards") prompted criticism over her outfit and the use of a pole as part of the choreography. She faced similar controversy after performing "[Can't Be Tamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Be_Tamed_(song) "Can't Be Tamed (song)")" on *[Britain's Got Talent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britain%27s_Got_Talent "Britain's Got Talent")* in 2010, during which she simulated a kiss with a female backup dancer. Cyrus defended the performance and rejected the resulting criticism.

Cyrus received widespread media and public scrutiny following her performance of "[We Can't Stop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Can%27t_Stop "We Can't Stop")" and "[Blurred Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blurred_Lines "Blurred Lines")" with [Robin Thicke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Thicke "Robin Thicke") at the [2013 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2013 MTV Video Music Awards"). Wearing a flesh-colored latex outfit, Cyrus used an oversized foam finger as a prop and [twerked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twerking "Twerking") against Thicke during the performance. The performance generated extensive media coverage; contemporary reviews characterized it as both a "bad acid trip" and a "trainwreck". The provocative imagery continued on the Bangerz Tour, where Cyrus entered the stage by sliding down a large tongue-shaped slide and incorporated elaborate costumes, props and sexually suggestive staging.

Alongside the attention surrounding her stage image, Cyrus increasingly incorporated rock standards and reinterpretations into her live performances. In January 2019, she performed [Temple of the Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_the_Dog "Temple of the Dog")'s "[Say Hello 2 Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Say_Hello_2_Heaven "Say Hello 2 Heaven")" at *I Am the Highway*, a tribute concert for [Chris Cornell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Cornell "Chris Cornell") at [The Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Forum "Kia Forum") in Los Angeles. Mikael Wood of the *Los Angeles Times* wrote that Cyrus's performance demonstrated her suitability for the tribute, highlighting the raspy and blues-influenced qualities of her vocals.

At the [2019 Glastonbury Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury_Festival_2019 "Glastonbury Festival 2019"), Cyrus performed a set that combined her own material with covers of [Amy Winehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Winehouse "Amy Winehouse")'s "[Back to Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_Black_(song) "Back to Black (song)")", [Led Zeppelin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Led_Zeppelin "Led Zeppelin")'s "[Black Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Dog_(Led_Zeppelin_song) "Black Dog (Led Zeppelin song)")", [Metallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallica "Metallica")'s "[Nothing Else Matters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing_Else_Matters "Nothing Else Matters")", Parton's "Jolene" and [Nine Inch Nails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Inch_Nails "Nine Inch Nails")' "[Head Like a Hole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_Like_a_Hole "Head Like a Hole")". She also brought [Billy Ray Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Ray_Cyrus "Billy Ray Cyrus") and [Lil Nas X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Nas_X "Lil Nas X") onstage for "[Old Town Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Town_Road "Old Town Road")". *[NME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NME "NME")* described the appearance as a rock-oriented reinvention of Cyrus's stage persona and praised her vocals and stage presence, identifying the "Old Town Road" collaboration as a notable moment of the festival.

Cyrus's cover performances received renewed attention in 2020. Her version of [Blondie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blondie_(band) "Blondie (band)")'s "[Heart of Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_Glass_(song) "Heart of Glass (song)")" at the [iHeartRadio Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHeartRadio_Music_Festival "IHeartRadio Music Festival") circulated widely online and was subsequently released to streaming services. For *[MTV Unplugged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Unplugged "MTV Unplugged") Presents Miley Cyrus Backyard Sessions*, she performed songs by artists including [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears"), [The Cardigans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cardigans "The Cardigans"), [Pearl Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_Jam "Pearl Jam"), [The Velvet Underground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Velvet_Underground "The Velvet Underground") and [Jackson Browne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Browne "Jackson Browne"). *Rolling Stone* observed that her series of televised and streamed covers had drawn significant online attention and praised her ability to reinterpret songs from different genres. At the [Save Our Stages](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Save_Our_Stages&action=edit&redlink=1 "Save Our Stages (page does not exist)") festival that October, she performed [The Cranberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cranberries "The Cranberries")' "[Zombie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie_(The_Cranberries_song) "Zombie (The Cranberries song)")" and [The Cure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cure "The Cure")'s "[Boys Don't Cry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boys_Don%27t_Cry_(The_Cure_song) "Boys Don't Cry (The Cure song)")".

In February 2021, Cyrus headlined the [Super Bowl LV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LV "Super Bowl LV") TikTok Tailgate, a pregame concert held for invited healthcare workers. The performance featured guest appearances by [Joan Jett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Jett "Joan Jett") and [Billy Idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Idol "Billy Idol") and combined Cyrus's songs with covers including "Heart of Glass", "Bad Reputation", "[White Wedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Wedding_(song) "White Wedding (song)")", "Head Like a Hole", "[Rebel Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Girl_(song) "Rebel Girl (song)")" and "Jolene". The prevalence of covers and rearranged versions of her own songs was subsequently documented on *Attention: Miley Live*, her first non-*Hannah Montana* live album.

#### 2023–present

In March 2023, Cyrus released *[Endless Summer Vacation (Backyard Sessions)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endless_Summer_Vacation_(Backyard_Sessions) "Endless Summer Vacation (Backyard Sessions)")*, a [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%2B "Disney+") concert special in which she performed eight songs from *[Endless Summer Vacation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endless_Summer_Vacation "Endless Summer Vacation")*, including "[Flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flowers_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Flowers (Miley Cyrus song)")", "[River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "River (Miley Cyrus song)")" and "[Jaded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaded_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Jaded (Miley Cyrus song)")", as well as "[The Climb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Climb_(song) "The Climb (song)")" from her earlier career. An expanded version aired on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") in August 2023 in conjunction with the release of "[Used to Be Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Used_to_Be_Young "Used to Be Young")".

Cyrus marked her 31st birthday in November 2023 with a private performance at the [Chateau Marmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chateau_Marmont "Chateau Marmont") in Los Angeles. The show included the first live performance of "Flowers", a cover of [Journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_(band) "Journey (band)")'s "[Faithfully](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faithfully_(song) "Faithfully (song)")", "Used to Be Young" and "[Jingle Bells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingle_Bells "Jingle Bells")". At the [66th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "66th Annual Grammy Awards") in February 2024, she gave the first televised performance of "Flowers". During the performance, Cyrus altered the lyrics to acknowledge having won her first Grammy Award earlier in the ceremony. *Rolling Stone* described the performance as a showcase for her gravelly vocals and noted its visual and vocal references to [Tina Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Turner "Tina Turner").

In July 2024, Cyrus co-hosted the Gucci Summer Celebration at Chateau Marmont and performed a slowed-down, jazz-influenced arrangement of "Flowers" during the private event.

During the promotion of *[Something Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Something_Beautiful_(Miley_Cyrus_album) "Something Beautiful (Miley Cyrus album)")* in 2025, Cyrus favored a series of smaller-scale appearances rather than a conventional tour. In May, she held an intimate TikTok listening event at Chateau Marmont for approximately 100 attendees, performing new songs including "[End of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_the_World_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "End of the World (Miley Cyrus song)")", "[Easy Lover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easy_Lover_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Easy Lover (Miley Cyrus song)")" and "[More to Lose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_to_Lose "More to Lose")", as well as "The Climb", which she had not performed publicly for several years. Cyrus also performed "More to Lose" on *[Jimmy Kimmel Live!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel_Live! "Jimmy Kimmel Live!")* on May 22, marking the song's live television debut with a stripped-down arrangement.

On May 30, Cyrus made an unannounced appearance at an album-release event at [3 Dollar Bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3_Dollar_Bill "3 Dollar Bill") in Brooklyn, where she performed "Easy Lover". On June 2, she performed "More to Lose", "Easy Lover" and "Flowers" during another surprise appearance at [Bemelmans Bar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bemelmans_Bar "Bemelmans Bar") inside [The Carlyle Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_Carlyle_Hotel&action=edit&redlink=1 "The Carlyle Hotel (page does not exist)") in New York City. *[Paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_(magazine) "Paper (magazine)")* described the performance and Cyrus's other small-venue appearances of the period as a shift toward more intimate live settings. On June 4, Cyrus held an album signing at [Rough Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Trade_(shops) "Rough Trade (shops)") in New York and surprised attendees with a live performance. She appeared on *[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")* later that day, and an interview with Cyrus on the *Every Single Album* podcast was released the following day.

## Public image

In the early years of her career, Cyrus had a generally wholesome image as a teen idol. Her fame increased dramatically in the wake of the *Vanity Fair* photo scandal in 2008, and it was reported that photographs of Cyrus could be sold to photo agencies for up to $2,000 per photo. In subsequent years, her image continued to shift dramatically from her teen idol status. In 2008, [Donny Osmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donny_Osmond "Donny Osmond") wrote of Cyrus's imminent transition to adulthood: "Miley will have to face adulthood... As she does, she'll want to change her image, and that change will be met with adversity." The release of her 2010 album *Can't Be Tamed* saw Cyrus officially attempting to distance herself from her teenage persona by releasing controversial music videos for her songs "Can't Be Tamed" and "Who Owns My Heart". Her behavior throughout 2013 and 2014 sparked a substantial amount of controversy, although her godmother Dolly Parton said "...the girl can write. The girl can sing. The girl is smart. And she doesn't have to be so drastic. But I will respect her choices. I did it my way, so why can't she do it her way?" Liel Leibovitz at *[Tablet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_(magazine) "Tablet (magazine)")* noted in 2013: "Talking to the website *Hunger*, the singer argued that those adults who deem her gyrations too sultry and her music too saccharine were simply too ancient—and Jewish—to get it. 'With magazines, with movies, it's always weird when things are targeted for young people yet they're driven by people that are like 40 years too old', Cyrus opined. And one group stands out in [her] mind as deserving of most of the blame: 'It can't be like this 70-year-old Jewish man that doesn't leave his desk all day, telling me what the clubs want to hear.'"

Cyrus was ranked number 17 on *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")*'s list of the most powerful celebrities in 2014; the magazine notes that "The last time she made our list was when she was still rolling in *Hannah Montana* money. Now the pop singer is all grown up and courting controversy at every turn." In August 2014, her life was documented in a comic book titled *Fame: Miley Cyrus*; it begins with her controversial 2013 MTV Video Music Awards performance and covers her Disney fame as well as exploring her childhood in Tennessee. The comic book was written by Michael L. Frizell, drawn by Juan Luis Rincón, and is available in both print and digital formats. In September 2010, Cyrus placed tenth on *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")*'s first-ever edition of its 21 Under 21 listicle; she was ranked twenty-first in 2011 and eighteenth in 2012. In 2013, *[Maxim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine) "Maxim (magazine)")* listed Cyrus as number one on their annual Hot 100 list. Cyrus was chosen by *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine as one of the finalists for [Person of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Person_of_the_Year "Time Person of the Year") in November 2013; she came in third place with 16.3% of the staff vote. In March 2014, [Skidmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skidmore_College "Skidmore College") in [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(state) "New York (state)") began to offer a special topics sociology course entitled "The Sociology of Miley Cyrus: Race, Class, Gender and Media" which was "using Miley as a lens through which to explore sociological thinking about identity, entertainment, media and fame". In 2015, Cyrus was listed as one of the nine runners-up for *[The Advocate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Advocate_(LGBT_magazine) "The Advocate (LGBT magazine)")*'s Person of the Year. In March 2024, to commemorate the 65th anniversary of [International Women's Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Women%27s_Day "International Women's Day"), Cyrus was one of a number of celebrities who had their likeness turned into [Bratz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bratz "Bratz") doll.

## Personal life

Cyrus resides in [Hidden Hills, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Hills,_California "Hidden Hills, California"), and also owns a $5.8 million home in her hometown of [Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin,_Tennessee "Franklin, Tennessee"). She was raised as a [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christians "Christians") and identified herself as such during her childhood and early adult life, but she included references to [Tibetan Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetan_Buddhism "Tibetan Buddhism") in her 2015 song "Milky Milky Milk" and is also influenced by [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu "Hindu") beliefs.

Since 2019, she has been a vocally [childfree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voluntary_childlessness "Voluntary childlessness") person.

### Sexuality and gender

Cyrus [came out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_out "Coming out") to her mother at age 14 and has said: "I never want to label myself! I am ready to love anyone that loves me for who I am! I am open." In June 2015, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine reported that she identified as [gender fluid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_fluid "Gender fluid"). She said she "doesn't relate to being boy or girl, and I don't have to have my partner relate to boy or girl", adding that she is "literally open to every single thing that is consenting and doesn't involve an animal and everyone is of age".

Cyrus is a supporter of the [LGBTQ community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_community "LGBTQ community"). Her 2010 song "My Heart Beats for Love" was written for one of her gay friends, and she has since said [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London") is her favorite place to perform due to its extensive gay scene. Cyrus has an [equals sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equals_sign "Equals sign") tattooed on her ring finger in support of same-sex marriage. After her 2018 marriage to Liam Hemsworth, Cyrus went on record to say she still identified as [queer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queer "Queer"). In 2014, she founded the [Happy Hippie Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Hippie_Foundation "Happy Hippie Foundation"), which works to "fight injustice facing homeless youth, LGBTQ youth and other vulnerable populations".

### Veganism

Cyrus became a [vegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegan "Vegan") and stopped eating animal products in 2014. In 2020, she said on *[The Joe Rogan Experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Joe_Rogan_Experience "The Joe Rogan Experience")* that she had to switch to a [pescatarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pescetarianism "Pescetarianism") diet after suffering from [omega-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega-3_fatty_acids "Omega-3 fatty acids") deficiency: "I've been a vegan for a very long time and I had to introduce fish and omegas into my life because my brain wasn't functioning properly." Cyrus said she cried when eating her first fish after her vegan diet, saying "I cried for the fish ... it really hurts me to eat fish."

### Cannabis use

Cyrus has been open about her recreational use of [cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_(drug) "Cannabis (drug)"). She told *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* in 2013 that it was "the best drug on earth" and called it, along with [MDMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA "MDMA"), a "happy drug". While accepting the Best Video Award at the [2013 MTV Europe Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_MTV_Europe_Music_Awards "2013 MTV Europe Music Awards"), Cyrus appeared to smoke a [joint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_(cannabis) "Joint (cannabis)") onstage; this was removed from the delayed broadcast of the show in the United States. In a 2014 interview with *[W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_(magazine) "W (magazine)")* magazine, Cyrus said "I love weed" and "I just love getting stoned." In a 2017 interview on *[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")*, she said she had quit cannabis before the press tour for *Younger Now* so she could be "super clear" when discussing the record. In May 2018, she told Jimmy Kimmel: "I also think it's the most magical, amazing... it's my first and true love. It's just not for me right now at this time in my life, but I'm sure there will be a day I will happily indulge." During a December 2018 interview with [Andy Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Cohen_(TV_personality) "Andy Cohen (TV personality)"), she credited her mother for reintroducing her to cannabis. In 2019, Cyrus sent "Nothing Breaks Like a Heart" collaborator [Mark Ronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ronson "Mark Ronson") a cannabis bouquet from [Lowell Herb Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_Herb_Co "Lowell Herb Co") as a tongue-in-cheek Valentine's Day gift. She invested in the company in August.

Before and shortly after vocal cord surgery in November 2019, Cyrus said she had abstained from cannabis and alcohol.

### Relationships

Cyrus has said that she dated singer-actor [Nick Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Jonas "Nick Jonas") from June 2006 to December 2007, claiming they were "in love" and began dating soon after they first met. Their relationship attracted considerable media attention. Cyrus was in a nine-month relationship with model [Justin Gaston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Gaston "Justin Gaston") from 2008 to 2009. In 2009, while filming *The Last Song*, she began an [on-again, off-again relationship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On-again,_off-again_relationship "On-again, off-again relationship") with her co-star [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth "Liam Hemsworth"). They were first engaged from May 2012 to September 2013. She has also dated actor [Patrick Schwarzenegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Schwarzenegger "Patrick Schwarzenegger") (2014–2015) and model [Stella Maxwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_Maxwell "Stella Maxwell") (2015).

Cyrus and Hemsworth rekindled their relationship in March 2016, and got engaged again that October. In November 2018, their home burned down in the [Woolsey Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolsey_Fire "Woolsey Fire") in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"). On December 23, Cyrus and Hemsworth married in a private ceremony at their home in [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee"). She said her marriage redefined "what it looks like for someone that's a queer person like me to be in a hetero relationship" while "still very sexually attracted to women". Cyrus said the ceremony was "kind of out of character for me" because they had "worn rings forever [and] definitely didn't need it in any way". She believed the loss of their home to be the catalyst for the wedding, saying "the timing felt right" and that "no one is promised the next day, or the next, so I try to be 'in the now' as much as possible". On August 10, 2019, Cyrus announced their separation; on August 21, Hemsworth filed for divorce, citing "irreconcilable differences". Their divorce was finalized on January 28, 2020.

After announcing her separation from Hemsworth, Cyrus dated Kaitlynn Carter from August to September 2019. In October 2019, Cyrus began dating Australian singer [Cody Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Simpson "Cody Simpson"), a longtime friend. In August 2020, Cyrus announced that she and Simpson had split up. Her announcement coincided with the release of her single "[Midnight Sky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Sky "Midnight Sky")", which was inspired by her breakups with Hemsworth, Carter, and Simpson. In 2021, Cyrus began dating American musician Maxx Morando, who worked as a producer on her 2023 album, *Endless Summer Vacation*. On December 2, 2025, it was reported that Cyrus and Morando are engaged.

## Philanthropy

Throughout her career Cyrus has sung on several charity singles such as: "[Just Stand Up!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_Stand_Up! "Just Stand Up!")", "[Send It On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Send_It_On_(Disney_song) "Send It On (Disney song)")", "[Everybody Hurts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everybody_Hurts_(Helping_Haiti_song) "Everybody Hurts (Helping Haiti song)")" and "[We Are the World 25 for Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Are_the_World_25_for_Haiti "We Are the World 25 for Haiti")". She is an avid supporter of the [City of Hope National Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Hope_National_Medical_Center "City of Hope National Medical Center") in California, having attended benefit concerts in 2008, 2009 and 2012. ### Happy Hippie Foundation

Cyrus is the founder of the [Happy Hippie Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Hippie_Foundation "Happy Hippie Foundation"), which works to "fight injustice facing homeless youth, LGBTQ youth, and other vulnerable populations". From 2014 to 2016 the foundation served nearly 1,500 homeless youth in Los Angeles, reached more than 25,000 LGBTQ youth and their families with resources about gender, and provided social services to transgender individuals, youth in conflict zones, and people affected by crises. Happy Hippie encourages Cyrus's fans to support causes including gender equality, LGBTQ rights and mental health through awareness campaigns and fundraising. Leading up to the 2020 presidential election, Happy Hippie encouraged its Instagram followers to seek out [VoteRiders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VoteRiders "VoteRiders") for assistance ensuring that gender identity would not affect their right to vote.

On June 15, 2015, Cyrus launched the campaign #InstaPride in collaboration with Instagram. The campaign features a series of portraits starring [transgender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transgender "Transgender") and gender-expansive people, which were posted to her Instagram feed with the hashtags "#HappyHippiePresents" and "#InstaPride". It stated that it was aimed at encouraging diversity and tolerance by showing these people in a positive light as examples for others who might be struggling to figure themselves out, and as a reference point for people who did not know personally anyone in that situation. Cyrus was behind the camera for the entire photoshoot, and interviewed her 14 subjects to share their personal stories. She said she wanted to bring attention to and celebrate people who would not normally find themselves being the stars of a photoshoot or portrayed on the cover of a magazine.

Following the loss of Miley and Hemsworth's Malibu home from the [Woolsey Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolsey_Fire "Woolsey Fire"), the community and they launched the Malibu Foundation for relief efforts following the [2018 California wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_California_wildfires "2018 California wildfires"), Miley's Happy Hippie Foundation donating $500,000 to the Malibu Foundation.

In 2024, Cyrus announced that the foundation would be renamed as Miley Cyrus Foundation.

## Legacy

Cyrus's early success as a [teen idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_idol "Teen idol") and the face of [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel")'s billion-dollar franchise *[Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana "Hannah Montana")* played an important role in shaping the 2000s teen pop culture, earning her the honorific nickname of "Teen Queen". Bickford stated *Hannah Montana* adopted a business model of combining celebrity acts with film, television, and popular music for a pre-adolescent audience. He called the series "genre-defining" and likened this model to [1990s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990s_in_music "1990s in music") [teen pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_pop "Teen pop") artists such as [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears") and [NSYNC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSYNC "NSYNC"), who were also marketed to children. Morgan Genevieve Blue of *Feminist Media Studies* stated the series' primary female characters, Miley and her alter ego Hannah, are positioned as [post-feminist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postfeminism "Postfeminism") subjects in a way their representation is confined to notions of femininity and consumerism. *[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times "The Times")* journalist Craig McLean named Cyrus the "world's biggest-ever teenage star".

During the Best of Both Worlds Tour, tickets were sold out in minutes and stadiums were completely filled making it the highest-grossing concert tour for a new act in 2007 and 2008. According to *Billboard* boxscore, the Best of Both Worlds Tour had a total attendance of approximately one million people and grossed over US$54 million, earning Cyrus the award for Breakthrough Act at the 2008 [Billboard Touring Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Touring_Awards "Billboard Touring Awards"). In 2012, *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* ranked Cyrus as one of the top 25 teen idol breakout moments of the rock era, which Andy Greene wrote: "Miley's rise was meteoric. Tickets to her 2007 Best of Both Worlds tour sold out faster than any tour in memory ... It seemed like she was poised to become a more stable version of Britney Spears – especially after singles 'The Climb' and 'Party In The USA'". Due to her popularity, [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney "Paul McCartney") compared their success to that of [the Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles "The Beatles") in an interview during his tour in 2011. In this regard, he commented: "I think when they have new sensations, like Miley Cyrus or Justin Bieber, teenagers identify with them, in the same way that the boys identified with The Beatles, [...] when you have thousands of teenagers feeling the same, they become elated because they have this love for something in common, whether it is The Beatles, Miley Cyrus, Justin Bieber, or whatever."

Over the years, Cyrus's song "Party in the U.S.A." gained popularity in American culture on holidays and historic events. The song has re-entered the charts every [Independence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_(United_States) "Independence Day (United States)") since its release. Following the [death of Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Osama_bin_Laden "Death of Osama bin Laden") on May 2, 2011, a resurgence in popularity of the music video occurred. The official [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") video was flooded with comments regarding the death of bin Laden and it was immediately deemed a celebratory anthem for the event. In 2013, an online petition on the [White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House "White House")'s "We the People" petitions website was urging then-president [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama") to change the U.S. national anthem from "[The Star-Spangled Banner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Star-Spangled_Banner "The Star-Spangled Banner")" to "Party in the U.S.A." Following the [2020 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election "2020 United States presidential election"), as major news outlets announced [Democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") nominee [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden") the winner of the presidential race, on November 7, 2020, supporters in New York City started singing "Party in the U.S.A." at [Times Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Square "Times Square").

Cyrus's album *[Bangerz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangerz "Bangerz")* (2013), along with its promotional events, is considered to be one of the most controversial moments in the 2010s wider popular culture and established Cyrus among the decade's most controversial figures. *[Glamour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glamour_(magazine) "Glamour (magazine)")* writer Mickey Woods likened the promotional "era" for the album to those of [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears")'s and [Christina Aguilera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Aguilera "Christina Aguilera")'s third and fourth studio albums *[Britney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_(album) "Britney (album)")* (2001) and *[Stripped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripped_(Christina_Aguilera_album) "Stripped (Christina Aguilera album)")* (2002), respectively, adding that Cyrus's record "will probably be retrospectively deemed iconic, maybe even classic". *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")* listed *Bangerz* as one of the greatest and most influential albums of the 2010s noting that "with this pivotal album release, Cyrus took control of her public persona, surprising less with her provocative antics than with her constant artistic evolution". The album was ranked number 230 on *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")*'s "250 Greatest Albums of the 21st Century". *Bangerz* is considered a [trendsetter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trendsetting "Trendsetting") in "weaving together urban and pop influences, what's most revered now is what it represented then" according to Lyndsey Havens. Patrick Ryan of *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today "USA Today")* commented that Cyrus's collaborations with [Mike Will Made It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Will_Made_It "Mike Will Made It") on the album contributed to his new-found prominence, stating that Mike Will Made It's position as an executive producer has helped him "[jump] to the forefront as an interesting character [...] in an era where a lot of producers have fallen behind the scenes again". *[Vice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_(magazine) "Vice (magazine)")* described Cyrus as "the most punk rock musician out there" and that she was "spinning circles around every single pop star who [was] trying to be edgy" at the time. [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV") named Cyrus their Best Artist of 2013, and James Montgomery of [MTV News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_News "MTV News") elaborated on the network's decision that Cyrus "[declared] her independence and [dominated] the pop-culture landscape", adding that "she schooled—and shocked—us all in 2013, and did so on her own terms." *Billboard* staff called Cyrus the "Most Talked About Pop Star" of 2013, and also recognized the controversial evolution of her career as the "Top Music Moment" of the year, elaborating that she was a "maelstrom that expanded and grazed nearly every aspect of pop culture in 2013". The publication also ranked "We Can't Stop" as the best song of 2013 for being "one of the bolder musical choices in recent memory", and as one of the songs that defined the 2010s decade. The song's music video and Cyrus's controversial 2013 VMAs performance with Robin Thicke were declared as the 27th greatest music video and one of the most "defining" pop culture moments of the 2010s. In 2025, *The Needle Drop*'s [Anthony Fantano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Fantano "Anthony Fantano") pointed Cyrus as "one of a few artists who's actually managed to keep her career interesting after riding the *Disney* *Channel* actress to pop star pipeline" stating that "in a lot of ways, you could actually say she set the tone for some of these newer faces who took a similar path and also got a bit edgier in their transition into pop stardom".

In 2015, Rebecca Nicholson from *The Guardian* published an article calling Cyrus the [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna "Madonna") of her generation, saying that "she's a *Disney* survivor with a fluid approach to gender identity. And, like the old three-chord punks, she gives really good quote". According to Nicholson, Cyrus takes "the 90s Madonna approach to public sexuality: it's deliberately provocative, and crucially, it is not being served up for male consumption." Likewise, she defends Cyrus's controversial rebellion, highlighting that behind the character there is a human, talented and strong person who manages to connect with the public, just like the "Queen of Pop". In November of the same year, *[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(website) "Vulture (website)")* ranked Cyrus number one on its "Disney and Nickelodeon Stars Gone Pop" listicle, writing that "no post-millennium child star [had] grown up as wildly, rapidly, or successfully as [Cyrus]" at the time. Appearing at number eight on the 2021 revision of the ranking, the publication named her as one of the few child stars with a successful music career as an adult, calling her "the archetype for Disney 2.0 stars" who "picked up the child-star trap of getting pigeonholed and set it on fire". *Billboard* cataloged the singer as one of the "Greatest of All Time *Billboard* 200 Artists", occupying position thirty-one; she was on the ninth rank among female artists. In 2017, the aforementioned magazine also published an article naming the singer a "Queer Superhero" for her philanthropic fight for the LGBTQ+ community. In 2019, *Billboard* ranked her 62nd on its "Greatest of All Time Artists" chart, and 55th on the [2010s decade-end chart of Top Artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Decade-End#2010s "Billboard Decade-End"), signifying the most successful acts of the decade.

Due to her continual artistic reinventions, sonic and stylistic evolution, and versatility, Cyrus has been nicknamed the "[Pop Chameleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorific_nicknames_in_popular_music#C "Honorific nicknames in popular music")" by media and various publications. She has also been considered a [pop icon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_icon "Pop icon") by several publications, with the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") calling her "the ultimate 21st century pop star". In 2023, *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* named Cyrus as one of its "Platinum Players" in music. *Billboard* included Cyrus in its "Greatest Pop Stars of 2023" listicle, naming her the "Comeback Artist of the Year". The magazine called her 2023 single "Flowers" the "biggest chart smash of her career" and noted that it "re-established [Cyrus] as one of pop's foremost hitmakers". In 2024, at age 31, Cyrus became the youngest recipient of the [Disney Legends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Legends "Disney Legends") award, for her outstanding contributions to the Walt Disney Company. That year, she was ranked number 15 on *Billboard*'s "[Greatest Pop Stars of the 21st Century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard%27s_Greatest_Pop_Stars_of_the_21st_Century "Billboard's Greatest Pop Stars of the 21st Century")"; the magazine wrote that Cyrus has "endured as one of the century's most significant pop stars—because no matter what style she's trying out, at the end of the day, she's always still just being Miley". In 2025, *Billboard* ranked her twenty-first on its "Top Artists of the 21st Century" list, and ninth on its "Top 100 Women Artists of the 21st Century" list. Cyrus was honored with the Innovator Award at the [2026 iHeartRadio Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_iHeartRadio_Music_Awards "2026 iHeartRadio Music Awards"). In May 2026, she received her own [star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_stars_on_the_Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "List of stars on the Hollywood Walk of Fame") in the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame") as a recognition of her success. Artists that have cited Cyrus or her work as inspiration or an influence include [Adéla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad%C3%A9la_(singer) "Adéla (singer)"), [Chappell Roan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chappell_Roan "Chappell Roan"), [JoJo Siwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JoJo_Siwa "JoJo Siwa"), [Lea Michele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lea_Michele "Lea Michele"), [Lil Nas X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Nas_X "Lil Nas X"), and [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan "Troye Sivan").

## Recognition

Throughout her career, Cyrus has received [several awards and nominations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Miley_Cyrus "List of awards and nominations received by Miley Cyrus"). In 2009, at only 16 years old, she received her first [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards") nomination for [Best Original Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Golden_Globe_Awards "66th Golden Globe Awards") with the song "[I Thought I Lost You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Thought_I_Lost_You "I Thought I Lost You")" featuring [John Travolta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Travolta "John Travolta") for the movie *[Bolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(2008_film) "Bolt (2008 film)")*, since then she has received two more nominations in the same category, in 2025 with the song "[Beautiful That Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_That_Way_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "Beautiful That Way (Miley Cyrus song)")" for the movie *[The Last Showgirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Showgirl "The Last Showgirl")* and in 2026 with the song "[Dream As One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_as_One "Dream as One")" for the movie *[Avatar: Fire and Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash "Avatar: Fire and Ash")*. She also won the [MTV Movie Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award "MTV Movie Award") for [Best Song From A Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_MTV_Movie_Awards "2009 MTV Movie Awards") with her song "[The Climb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Climb_(Miley_Cyrus_song) "The Climb (Miley Cyrus song)")" in 2009. She received 16 nominations at the [World Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Music_Awards "World Music Awards") in 2014 and 50 [Teen Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Choice_Award "Teen Choice Award") nominations from 2006 to 2014, making her the most nominated person in the history of the Teen Choice Awards. In 2024, she won her first two [Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Awards "Grammy Awards") at the [66th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "66th Annual Grammy Awards") and by 2026 she has accumulated 10 nominations.
