# Nathan Fielder

**Nathan Joseph Fielder** (born May 12, 1983) is a Canadian filmmaker, actor, and comedian, known for his [awkward persona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cringe_comedy "Cringe comedy") and for creating works that blur the line between reality and fiction.

As a teenager, Fielder was involved in his school's [improv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improvisational_theatre "Improvisational theatre") group, which featured fellow comedian [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen "Seth Rogen"). He majored in business at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria "University of Victoria") in [Victoria, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria,_British_Columbia "Victoria, British Columbia"), which influenced his later work. After attending a comedy course at [Humber College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humber_Polytechnic "Humber Polytechnic") in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto"), Fielder began his career as a correspondent for [CBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Television "CBC Television")'s *[This Hour Has 22 Minutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Hour_Has_22_Minutes "This Hour Has 22 Minutes")* for the segment series *Nathan on Your Side* from 2008 to 2009. He wrote and appeared on the American [sketch show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sketch_show "Sketch show") *[Important Things with Demetri Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Important_Things_with_Demetri_Martin "Important Things with Demetri Martin")* (2011).

In 2013, Fielder co-created and directed the [Comedy Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_Central "Comedy Central") parody reality show *[Nathan for You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_for_You "Nathan for You")*, in which he stars as a slightly more awkward version of himself, who offers advice to struggling businesses. The show ended in 2017 and was followed by the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") [docu-comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docu-comedy "Docu-comedy") *[The Rehearsal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rehearsal_(TV_series) "The Rehearsal (TV series)")* (2022–present). Fielder also executive produced the HBO docu-series *[How To with John Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_To_with_John_Wilson "How To with John Wilson")* (2020–2023). In 2023, he and [Benny Safdie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Safdie "Benny Safdie") created his first scripted show, [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network) "Showtime (TV network)")'s *[The Curse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Curse_(American_TV_series) "The Curse (American TV series)")*, in which he stars, alongside Safdie and [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone "Emma Stone"). Fielder and [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim "Lance Oppenheim") co-directed the critically acclaimed documentary *[You Can See Everything](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything "You Can See Everything")* (2026), which follows the disgraced entrepreneur [Elizabeth Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Holmes "Elizabeth Holmes").

He has also become recognized for his activism, including his Holocaust-awareness clothing brand [Summit Ice Apparel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Ice_Apparel "Summit Ice Apparel") and his advocacy for aviation safety. His accolades include an [Independent Spirit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Spirit_Awards "Independent Spirit Awards") and a [WGA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writers_Guild_of_America_Awards "Writers Guild of America Awards"), as well as three nominations for a [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards"). In 2023, he was featured on *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")*'s list of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100").

## Early life and education

Nathan Joseph Fielder was born on May 12, 1983, in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver "Vancouver"). His family is [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews "Jews"). His parents, Deb and Eric Fielder, are both [social workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_work "Social work"). He attended [Vancouver Talmud Torah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_Talmud_Torah "Vancouver Talmud Torah") and [Point Grey Secondary School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Grey_Secondary_School "Point Grey Secondary School") in Vancouver. He was a member of the latter's improv comedy group, which included comedian [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen "Seth Rogen").

He studied business at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria "University of Victoria"), where he graduated with a [Bachelor of Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Commerce "Bachelor of Commerce") degree in 2005. After university, he moved to Toronto and enrolled in Humber College's [Comedy Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humber_College_Comedy:_Writing_and_Performance "Humber College Comedy: Writing and Performance") in 2006. He worked briefly for a brokerage firm before quitting to start his comedy career.

## Career

### 2006–2012: Early work

After receiving the [Tim Sims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Sims "Tim Sims") Encouragement Fund Award in 2006, Fielder worked as a writer on *[Canadian Idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Idol "Canadian Idol")*, where he was noticed by [Michael Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Donovan_(producer) "Michael Donovan (producer)"), an executive producer for the CBC comedy series *[This Hour Has 22 Minutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Hour_Has_22_Minutes "This Hour Has 22 Minutes")*. Donovan hired Fielder as a field correspondent and developed his popular recurring segment, "Nathan on Your Side". In 2010, Fielder wrote and directed a number of sketches for Season 2 of *[Important Things with Demetri Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Important_Things_with_Demetri_Martin "Important Things with Demetri Martin")* on [Comedy Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_Central "Comedy Central"). He was also featured as a guest voice actor on the Season 2 finale of *[Bob's Burgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob%27s_Burgers "Bob's Burgers")*, "[Beefsquatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beefsquatch "Beefsquatch")", as well as the Season 6 episode, "The Land Ship". He played [Jon Benjamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Jon_Benjamin "H. Jon Benjamin")'s [boom operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boom_operator_(media) "Boom operator (media)") in the 2011 television series *[Jon Benjamin Has a Van](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Benjamin_Has_a_Van "Jon Benjamin Has a Van")*, and [Bob Woodward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Woodward "Bob Woodward") in the "Washington, D.C." episode of Comedy Central's *[Drunk History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drunk_History "Drunk History")*. Fielder's [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") channel, mainly comprising short sketches involving him and his friends, was active during this period.

### 2013–2017: *Nathan for You*

In 2013, Fielder co-created his own show on Comedy Central, *[Nathan for You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_for_You "Nathan for You")*. He wrote, directed, and starred in the show which is based on the "Nathan On Your Side" segments he did for *This Hour Has 22 Minutes*. The show's premise features Fielder, playing a persona loosely based on himself, providing advice for local small businesses. The show finished its fourth and last season in November 2017.

On the morning of February 7, 2014, a coffee shop called [Dumb Starbucks Coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumb_Starbucks_Coffee "Dumb Starbucks Coffee") opened in the [Los Feliz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Feliz "Los Feliz") neighborhood of Los Angeles. The shop said it was operating as a [parody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parody "Parody") of the coffee chain [Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks "Starbucks") and used Starbuck's [siren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siren_(mythology) "Siren (mythology)") logo in its signage, cups, and other materials. In the episode, Fielder says his understanding of parody law is "based off what I read on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia "Wikipedia")". The shop sold items standard to Starbucks locations, such as [Norah Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norah_Jones "Norah Jones") CDs and drinks, by using the term "Dumb" in the names, such as "Dumb Norah Jones Duets" and "Dumb Iced Vanilla Latte". Beverages and pastries were distributed free of charge. A few days later, Starbucks announced that it was not affiliated and that it was "evaluating [its] next steps".

The identity of the person behind the shop was not released initially, and various artists and comedians including [Banksy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banksy "Banksy") and [Tim & Eric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_%26_Eric "Tim & Eric") were theorized to be involved with the prank. (The latter's production company [Abso Lutely Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abso_Lutely_Productions "Abso Lutely Productions") had applied for a filming permit at the Dumb Starbucks Coffee location.) On February 10, 2014, the shop was closed by the [Los Angeles County Department of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Department_of_Public_Health "Los Angeles County Department of Public Health") for not having the permits required to operate a coffee shop. Shortly thereafter, Fielder announced he was behind the parody and the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* noted the prank's similarity to other skits performed on his show *Nathan for You*.

In 2015, Fielder guest-starred on the [Adult Swim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adult_Swim "Adult Swim") show *[Rick and Morty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_and_Morty "Rick and Morty")* and appeared in the film *[The Night Before](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Night_Before_(2015_film) "The Night Before (2015 film)").* In 2017 he co-starred in the biopic *[The Disaster Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Disaster_Artist_(film) "The Disaster Artist (film)")*.

### 2018–present: Career expansion

Fielder worked as a consulting producer, writer and co-director of the [Sacha Baron Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacha_Baron_Cohen "Sacha Baron Cohen") created political [mockumentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mockumentary "Mockumentary") series *[Who is America?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who_is_America%3F "Who is America?")* (2018). In 2019, it was announced that Fielder had signed an [overall deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overall_deal "Overall deal") with [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO"), under which he would serve as executive producer for the [John Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wilson_(filmmaker) "John Wilson (filmmaker)")-created documentary series *[How To with John Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_To_with_John_Wilson "How To with John Wilson")* and star, write, and direct in a separate comedy series. In 2021, HBO announced the name of another new comedy series, *[The Rehearsal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rehearsal_(TV_series) "The Rehearsal (TV series)")*, starring Fielder, who also served as writer, executive producer, and director. The first season of *The Rehearsal* was released in 2022 to critical acclaim and it was renewed by HBO for a second season. For the series, Fielder won the [Independent Spirit Award for Best New Non-Scripted or Documentary Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/38th_Independent_Spirit_Awards "38th Independent Spirit Awards").

In 2022, [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network) "Showtime (TV network)") picked up the comedy series *[The Curse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Curse_(American_TV_series) "The Curse (American TV series)")*, created and written by Fielder and filmmaker [Benny Safdie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Safdie "Benny Safdie"), and starring Fielder, Safdie, and [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone "Emma Stone"). The first three episodes premiered at the [2023 New York Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_New_York_Film_Festival "2023 New York Film Festival") in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan "Manhattan"). The rest of the episodes aired on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+") and were shown at [Lincoln Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_at_Lincoln_Center "Film at Lincoln Center"). Stone added Fielder to *Time*'s "100 Most Influential People in 2023". She called his ability to "move fluidly between directing, writing, acting, and producing, all while editing *The Rehearsal*" a "staggering feat". In 2025, Season 2 of *The Rehearsal* aired on HBO with a focus on aviation safety, receiving critical acclaim. The show's central idea of rehearsing and performing social interactions resonated with many autistic viewers, who viewed it as an analogy for [autistic masking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autistic_masking "Autistic masking"). After the overarching subject matter of aircraft pilots on *The Rehearsal*, Fielder became an [FAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Aviation_Administration "Federal Aviation Administration") certified [737](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737 "Boeing 737") [commercial pilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_certification_in_the_United_States#Commercial_pilot "Pilot certification in the United States") in February 2025. As of 2025, he works as a [ferry pilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferry_flying "Ferry flying") for Nomadic Aviation Group, a company that transports empty planes around the world.

On September 6, 2026, Fielder and [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim "Lance Oppenheim")'s [A24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A24 "A24") documentary *[You Can See Everything](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything "You Can See Everything")* premiered in a surprise screening at the [Telluride Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telluride_Film_Festival "Telluride Film Festival") in Telluride, Colorado. The film, which depicts [Theranos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theranos "Theranos") founder [Elizabeth Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Holmes "Elizabeth Holmes") in the weeks before she began serving her 11-year prison sentence for defrauding investors, will be released on October 16, 2026.

Fielder was married to a children's librarian and they divorced in 2014. He is incorrectly listed as a female on his [U.S. green card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_card "Green card").[*[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")*] As of 2022, he lives in [Silver Lake, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Lake,_Los_Angeles "Silver Lake, Los Angeles").

## Activism

### Holocaust awareness

As a student, Fielder attended a speech hosted by the Vancouver Holocaust Education Centre (VHEC) featuring a [Holocaust survivor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_survivor "Holocaust survivor"). The experience led to his longstanding relationship with the VHEC.

In 2015, Fielder founded the [not-for-profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonprofit_organization "Nonprofit organization") company [Summit Ice Apparel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Ice_Apparel "Summit Ice Apparel"). It produces softshell jackets and raises awareness of the [Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust "Holocaust"). It had close to $500,000 in sales in the first three months, with all profits going to VHEC. As of 2025, Summit Ice Apparel said it had earned millions in support of youth education surrounding "intolerance, racial bias, and genocide".

Fielder was inspired to start Summit Ice Apparel after learning that Taiga in Vancouver, an organization he previously supported, published a tribute to the Holocaust denier [Doug Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Collins_(journalist) "Doug Collins (journalist)"). In 2017, Fielder hosted a one-day [pop-up shop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop-up_shop "Pop-up shop") in Vancouver where customers could trade in their Taiga clothing for Summit Ice Apparel products.

The second episode of *Nathan For You*'s third season, which featured Summit Ice Apparel, was removed by Paramount+ worldwide as the German division of Paramount expressed [concerns of antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism_in_21st-century_Germany "Antisemitism in 21st-century Germany") in the wake of the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war"). Fielder expressed disappointment in the second episode of *The Rehearsal*'s second season, calling Summit Ice Apparel his "proudest achievement".

### Aviation safety

During a guest appearance on *[Jimmy Kimmel Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel_Live! "Jimmy Kimmel Live!")* in 2025, Fielder revealed that he had been following airplane crashes for "almost 20 years." That led him to notice a trend of pilot miscommunication in the cockpit and publicly express concern for the state of pilot training. In an interview with [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN"), he said, "I went through the training [as a 737 pilot]... someone shows you a [PowerPoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_PowerPoint "Microsoft PowerPoint") slide saying that if you are a copilot and the captain does something wrong, you need to speak up about it... they don't do anything that makes it stick emotionally."

In Season 2 of *The Rehearsal*, Fielder uses roleplay to train pilots on effective communication before and during flight. In response to the FAA's denial of the show's central claim that pilot communication is to blame for airline disasters, Fielder criticized them on CNN and said, "That's dumb. They're dumb."

Fielder has a working relationship with [John Goglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Goglia "John Goglia"), an American aviation safety consultant and former member of the [National Transportation Safety Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Transportation_Safety_Board "National Transportation Safety Board") (NTSB) who plays a significant role as Fielder's advisor on *The Rehearsal*. Goglia has accompanied Fielder on public interviews related to aviation safety.
