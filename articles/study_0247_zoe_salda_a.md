# Zoe Saldaña

**Zoë Yadira Saldaña-Perego** ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names "Birth name") **Saldaña Nazario**; born June 19, 1978), known professionally as **Zoe Saldaña**, is an American actress. The [second highest grossing lead actor in history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_actors "List of highest-grossing actors"), she is the recipient of [many accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Zoe_Salda%C3%B1a "List of awards and nominations received by Zoe Saldaña"), including an [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress"), a [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Supporting_Role "BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role"), two [Critics Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Awards "Critics' Choice Movie Awards"), a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture"), a [SAG Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Female_Actor_in_a_Supporting_Role "Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Supporting Role"), and a [Cannes Film Festival Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannes_Film_Festival_Award_for_Best_Actress "Cannes Film Festival Award for Best Actress"). *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine named her one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") in 2023 and 2026.

A trained dancer, Saldaña began her acting career in 1999 with a guest role in *[Law & Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order "Law & Order")*. Her first film role was in *[Center Stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_Stage_(2000_film) "Center Stage (2000 film)")* (2000) in which she played a ballet dancer. She received early recognition for her work opposite [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears") in the [road movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_movie "Road movie") *[Crossroads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossroads_(2002_film) "Crossroads (2002 film)")* (2002) and for her role in *[Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean:_The_Curse_of_the_Black_Pearl "Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl")* (2003). Saldaña's breakthrough came as [Nyota Uhura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyota_Uhura#Reboot_(Kelvin_timeline)_films "Nyota Uhura") in the [*Star Trek* reboot film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Star_Trek_films#Reboot_(Kelvin_Timeline)_films "List of Star Trek films") (2009–2016) and [Neytiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neytiri "Neytiri") in [James Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron "James Cameron")'s [*Avatar* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_(franchise) "Avatar (franchise)") (2009–present). She gained further recognition for portraying [Gamora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamora_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Gamora (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in five films of the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe"), beginning with *[Guardians of the Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Galaxy_(film) "Guardians of the Galaxy (film)")* (2014).

In addition to franchise work, Saldaña has also starred in films such as the comedy *[Death at a Funeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_at_a_Funeral_(2010_film) "Death at a Funeral (2010 film)")* (2010), the action thriller *[Colombiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombiana "Colombiana")* (2011), the crime drama *[Out of the Furnace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_the_Furnace "Out of the Furnace")* (2013), the animated comedy *[Missing Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missing_Link_(2019_film) "Missing Link (2019 film)")* (2019), and the science fiction film *[The Adam Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adam_Project "The Adam Project")* (2022). For her portrayal of a lawyer in the French musical crime film *[Emilia Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilia_P%C3%A9rez "Emilia Pérez")*, she received the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress"). On television, she starred in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") romantic drama miniseries *[From Scratch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/From_Scratch_(TV_series) "From Scratch (TV series)")* (2022) and the [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+") spy series *[Lioness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lioness_(American_TV_series) "Lioness (American TV series)")* (2023–present). As a singer, she has performed original songs for the *Avatar* film series.

## Early life

Zoë Yadira Saldaña Nazario was born on June 19, 1978, in [Passaic, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passaic,_New_Jersey "Passaic, New Jersey"), and raised in the [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") borough of [Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens "Queens"). Her parents are Aridio Saldaña, who was [Dominican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Americans "Dominican Americans"), and Asalia Nazario, who is half Dominican and half Puerto Rican. In an interview with *[Wired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired_(magazine) "Wired (magazine)")*, Saldaña has said that she is three-quarters Dominican and one-quarter Puerto Rican. She and her two sisters, Cisely and Mariel, were raised to be bilingual in English and Spanish; the latter was their first language at home. When she was nine, their father was killed in a car accident. Saldaña and her two sisters were sent to live with their late father's family in the [Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic "Dominican Republic"). Their mother remained in New York to earn enough money to pay for her daughters' Dominican private school.

Her widowed mother, Asalia, married Dagoberto Galán, who became the girls' stepfather. They consider him their father. While the whole family lived in the Dominican Republic for a time, after Saldaña's sophomore year in high school, the family returned to New York City to escape political unrest. She completed her early education at [Newtown High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtown_High_School_(Queens) "Newtown High School (Queens)") in Queens. The majority of her late childhood was spent in [Jackson Heights, Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Heights "Jackson Heights").

Saldaña discovered her love of dance while living in the Dominican Republic. She was enrolled in the ECOS Espacio de Danza Academy studying forms of dance, but describes ballet as her passion. She told *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")* that she quit ballet because she did not "have the feet", and had too much pride and ambition to just be in the [corps de ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_de_ballet "Corps de ballet").

In 1995, Saldaña performed with the Faces theater group in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn "Brooklyn"). During these years, she performed with the New York Youth Theater. Her appearance in their production of *[Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_and_the_Amazing_Technicolor_Dreamcoat "Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat")* led a [talent agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talent_agency "Talent agency") to recruit her. Her dance training and her acting experience helped her land her first film role, playing ballet student Eva Rodriguez in *[Center Stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_Stage_(2000_film) "Center Stage (2000 film)")* (2000).

## Career

### 1999–2008: Rise to prominence

Saldaña's first on-screen role was a 1999 guest appearance in an episode of *[Law & Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order "Law & Order")*. Her first film role was in *[Center Stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_Stage_(2000_film) "Center Stage (2000 film)")* (2000), directed by [Nicholas Hytner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hytner "Nicholas Hytner"), about dancers at the fictitious American Ballet Academy in New York City. She appeared in the [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears") vehicle *[Crossroads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossroads_(2002_film) "Crossroads (2002 film)")* (2002). The film earned negative reviews from critics, but was a box-office success. Saldaña starred in the comedy-drama *[Drumline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drumline_(film) "Drumline (film)")* (2002), alongside [Nick Cannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cannon "Nick Cannon"), earning mixed reviews.

In *[Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean:_The_Curse_of_the_Black_Pearl "Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl")* (2003), she played [Anamaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Pirates_of_the_Caribbean_characters#Anamaria "List of Pirates of the Caribbean characters"), a pirate joining Will Turner and Mr. Gibbs for a chance to confront Jack Sparrow for stealing her ship. She appeared in *[The Terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Terminal "The Terminal")* (2004) as Dolores Torres, an immigration officer and *Star Trek* fan, a role helping Saldaña during her portrayal in the *[Star Trek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_(2009_film) "Star Trek (2009 film)")* reboot (2009). In 2004, she had roles in *[Haven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haven_(film) "Haven (film)")* and *[Temptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temptation_(2004_film) "Temptation (2004 film)")*; both earned little-to-no box-office success.

In 2005, Saldaña appeared in *[Constellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constellation_(film) "Constellation (film)")*, *[Guess Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guess_Who_(film) "Guess Who (film)")* with [Ashton Kutcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashton_Kutcher "Ashton Kutcher"), and *[Dirty Deeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirty_Deeds_(2005_film) "Dirty Deeds (2005 film)")*. She starred in the romantic comedy-dramas *[Premium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premium_(film) "Premium (film)")* (2006) and *[After Sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After_Sex_(2007_film) "After Sex (2007 film)")* (2007). Saldaña starred in *[Blackout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackout_(2007_film) "Blackout (2007 film)")*, a television film set in New York City during the [Northeast Blackout of 2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Blackout_of_2003 "Northeast Blackout of 2003"). The film premiered at the 2007 Zurich Film Festival, and debuted on [BET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Entertainment_Television "Black Entertainment Television") in 2008. Saldaña had a supporting role as Angie Jones in the action thriller *[Vantage Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vantage_Point_(film) "Vantage Point (film)")* (2008).

### 2009–2019: Franchise roles

Saldaña appeared in two roles in 2009 that raised her profile considerably. She played [Nyota Uhura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyota_Uhura#Reboot_(Kelvin_timeline)_films "Nyota Uhura") in *[Star Trek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_(2009_film) "Star Trek (2009 film)")*. The film's director [J. J. Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Abrams "J. J. Abrams") asked Saldaña to play the role because he enjoyed her work. She met with [Nichelle Nichols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nichelle_Nichols "Nichelle Nichols") to understand the creation of Uhura's background and name of the character. Saldaña's mother was a *Star Trek* fan, and left her voice-mails during filming, advising on the role. Steven Spielberg had taught her the [Vulcan salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulcan_salute "Vulcan salute") five years earlier while he directed her in *The Terminal*. *Star Trek* (2009) was a box-office success earning $385.7 million.

Saldaña's second high-profile film in 2009 was *[Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_(2009_film) "Avatar (2009 film)")*, where she portrayed the indigenous hunter [Neytiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neytiri "Neytiri"). *Avatar* was well received by critics, with an approval rating of 81% on the review aggregator website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"). It grossed $2.7 billion worldwide to become the [highest-grossing film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films "List of highest-grossing films"), as well as specifically in the [United States and Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films_in_Canada_and_the_United_States "List of highest-grossing films in Canada and the United States"). It became the first film to gross more than $2 billion worldwide.

The film was nominated for ten [Saturn Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Award "Saturn Award"), and won all ten at the [36th Saturn Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Saturn_Awards "36th Saturn Awards") ceremony. Saldaña's [Saturn Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Award_for_Best_Actress "Saturn Award for Best Actress") win marked a rare occurrence for an all-[CG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics "Computer graphics") character.

In 2010, Saldaña performed in *[The Losers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Losers_(2010_film) "The Losers (2010 film)")* as Aisha al-Fadhil, a native [Bolivian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivians "Bolivians") woman. She loved the physicality of the ruthless assassin, citing her youth and athletic background for letting her really jump in and get thrown around and do her own kicks. Saldaña playfully recounted having to bulk up in order to carry the weapons while filming over eight hours a day. In 2010, she appeared in *[Takers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takers "Takers")*, *[Death at a Funeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_at_a_Funeral_(2010_film) "Death at a Funeral (2010 film)")*, and *[Burning Palms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_Palms_(film) "Burning Palms (film)")*. Her television ad for [Calvin Klein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Klein "Calvin Klein")'s "Envy" line debuted in 2010. In 2011, Saldaña starred in the romantic comedy *[The Heart Specialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heart_Specialist "The Heart Specialist")*, and portrayed assassin Cataleya Restrepo in the crime drama *[Colombiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombiana "Colombiana")*. Although the latter film earned negative reviews from critics, Saldaña's performance was praised.

In 2012, she appeared in the romantic drama *[The Words](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Words_(film) "The Words (film)")*, earning negative reviews from critics and little success at the box-office.

In 2013, Saldaña reprised her role as Uhura in *[Star Trek Into Darkness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_Into_Darkness "Star Trek Into Darkness")*, the sequel to the *Star Trek* reboot. Like the previous film, it was a box-office success, ending its North American theater run with a box office total of $228,778,661, placing it as the eleventh-highest-grossing film of 2013. It earned $467,365,246 worldwide, ranking it in 14th place for 2013, and making it the highest-grossing film of the franchise. Saldaña voiced her character in the 2013 release of the [*Star Trek* video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_(2013_video_game) "Star Trek (2013 video game)").

In 2014, Saldaña played [Gamora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamora_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Gamora (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in *[Guardians of the Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Galaxy_(film) "Guardians of the Galaxy (film)")*. Saldaña portrayed the character with make-up rather than [computer generated imagery (CGI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery") or [performance capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture "Motion capture"). The film became the third-highest-grossing film in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe"), behind *[The Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Avengers_(2012_film) "The Avengers (2012 film)")* and *[Iron Man 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_3 "Iron Man 3")*. It was the third-highest-grossing 2014 film (behind *[Transformers: Age of Extinction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers:_Age_of_Extinction "Transformers: Age of Extinction")* and *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies")*), and the highest-grossing superhero film of 2014.

The film earned positive reviews. Saldaña was nominated for numerous awards for her work in the film, including [Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Actress in an Action Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_an_Action_Movie "Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Actress in an Action Movie"), Favorite Action Movie Actress at the [People's Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/41st_People%27s_Choice_Awards "41st People's Choice Awards"), and Best On-Screen Transformation at the [MTV Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Movie_Awards "2015 MTV Movie Awards").

In May 2014, she performed in *[Rosemary's Baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosemary%27s_Baby_(miniseries) "Rosemary's Baby (miniseries)")*, a television mini-series adaptation of [Ira Levin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ira_Levin "Ira Levin")'s [horror novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosemary%27s_Baby_(novel) "Rosemary's Baby (novel)"). Saldaña also co-produced the four-hour two-part show. In 2014, Saldaña was recognized by *Elle* magazine during The Women in Hollywood Awards. Women were honored for achievements in film and the motion-picture industry, including acting, directing, and producing.

Saldaña starred in *[Nina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_(2016_film) "Nina (2016 film)")* (2016), an unauthorized biography about the jazz musician [Nina Simone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Simone "Nina Simone"). The film depicts the late singer's rise to fame and relationship with her manager Clifton Henderson. Simone's family criticized Saldaña's being cast in this role. In August 2020, Saldaña apologized for taking the role, saying "I'm so sorry. I know better today and I'm never going to do that again. She's one of our giants and someone else should step up. Somebody else should tell her story."

In 2016, she co-starred in the second sequel *[Star Trek Beyond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_Beyond "Star Trek Beyond")* released in July, and [Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck "Ben Affleck")'s crime drama *[Live by Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_by_Night_(film) "Live by Night (film)")* released in December.

Saldaña returned as Gamora in the *Guardians of the Galaxy* sequel, *[Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Galaxy_Vol._2 "Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2")*, released in May 2017. She reprised the role in *[Avengers: Infinity War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Infinity_War "Avengers: Infinity War")* (2018) and its sequel *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")* (2019), albeit as an alternate version of the character in the latter film. She returned in this role in *[Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Galaxy_Vol._3 "Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3")* (2023), her last film in the MCU as Gamora.

Also in 2017, Saldaña played Mrs. Mollé in *[I Kill Giants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Kill_Giants_(film) "I Kill Giants (film)")*, [Anders Walter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Walter "Anders Walter")'s adaptation of Joe Kelly's graphic novel *[I Kill Giants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Kill_Giants "I Kill Giants")*. Shooting commenced in Ireland in September 2016. Also that year, she appeared in the animated film *[My Little Pony: The Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Little_Pony:_The_Movie_(2017_film) "My Little Pony: The Movie (2017 film)")*, performing the voice of pirate parrot Captain Celaeno.

On May 3, 2018, she received a star at 6920 Hollywood Boulevard in the Motion Pictures section of the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame").

### 2020–present: Career expansion

In 2021, Saldaña starred in two Netflix productions, appearing as Rosita in the musical *[Vivo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivo_(film) "Vivo (film)")* and as the titular character in *[Maya and the Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_and_the_Three "Maya and the Three")*. She joined the all-star cast in [David O. Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_O._Russell "David O. Russell")'s *[Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_(2022_film) "Amsterdam (2022 film)")*. Saldaña reprised her role as Neytiri in *[Avatar: The Way of Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_The_Way_of_Water "Avatar: The Way of Water")* (2022). As part of her role, she performed vocals for the original song "The Songcord" on the [film's soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_The_Way_of_Water_(soundtrack) "Avatar: The Way of Water (soundtrack)"), penned by [Simon Franglen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Franglen "Simon Franglen"). She also began starring in the [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+") spy thriller television series *[Special Ops: Lioness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lioness_(American_TV_series) "Lioness (American TV series)")*, created by [Taylor Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Sheridan "Taylor Sheridan"), in 2023.

She played Rita Mora Castro, a high-powered junior criminal defense attorney in the controversial musical crime film *[Emilia Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilia_P%C3%A9rez "Emilia Pérez")* (2024), receiving critical acclaim and sharing the [Cannes Film Festival Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannes_Film_Festival_Award_for_Best_Actress "Cannes Film Festival Award for Best Actress") with her co-stars ([Karla Sofía Gascón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karla_Sof%C3%ADa_Gasc%C3%B3n "Karla Sofía Gascón"), [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez "Selena Gomez"), and [Adriana Paz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adriana_Paz "Adriana Paz")). Saldaña also won the [BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Supporting_Role "BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role"), the [Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture"), the [Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Female_Actor_in_a_Supporting_Role "Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Supporting Role"), and the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress"). She is the first [Dominican American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Americans "Dominican Americans") actress to win an Academy Award.

In addition to the *Avatar* sequels, Saldaña had a voice role in the [Pixar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixar "Pixar") science fiction adventure film *[Elio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elio_(film) "Elio (film)")* (2025). After the release of *[Avatar: Fire and Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash "Avatar: Fire and Ash")*, Saldaña became the highest grossing actress of all time.

## Personal life

In June 2010, Saldaña was engaged to her longtime boyfriend Keith Britton, an actor and the CEO of My Fashion Database. In November 2011, she and Britton announced they had ended their relationship after eleven years. Saldaña was in a relationship with actor [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper "Bradley Cooper") from December 2011 to January 2013.

In March 2013, Saldaña began dating Italian artist Marco Perego and they married three months later in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"). In July 2015, Saldaña revealed Perego adopted her surname upon marriage. Thereafter, Zoë became Zoë Saldaña-Perego and Marco became Marco Perego-Saldaña. They have three sons together. Saldaña has stated her children will be multi-lingual because she and her husband speak Spanish, Italian, and English around them.

In July 2016, during an interview with [Net-a-Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YOOX_Net-a-Porter_Group "YOOX Net-a-Porter Group")'s *The Edit*, Saldaña revealed she has [Hashimoto's thyroiditis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashimoto%27s_thyroiditis "Hashimoto's thyroiditis"), an autoimmune disease, along with her mother and sisters. To combat the effects of this disease, Saldaña said she and her husband adhere to a gluten- and dairy-free diet. Saldaña is a supporter of [FINCA International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FINCA_International "FINCA International"), a [microfinance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microfinance "Microfinance") organization. In 2017, she founded *BESE*, a digital media platform designed to "combat the lack of diversity in the mainstream media" with an interest on positive stories within the Latino community. In September 2020, Saldaña used her social media presence to participate in the [VoteRiders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VoteRiders "VoteRiders") #IDCheck Challenge to help spread the word about [voter ID requirements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voter_identification_laws_in_the_United_States "Voter identification laws in the United States") for [that year's presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election "2020 United States presidential election").

In October 2023, Saldaña signed the "No Hostage Left Behind" open letter in support of the desire for "Freedom for Israelis and Palestinians to live side by side in peace. Freedom from the brutal violence spread by Hamas. And most urgently, in this moment, freedom for the hostages".
