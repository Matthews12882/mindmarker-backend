# Dario Amodei

**Dario Amodei** (born 1983) is an American [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") (AI) researcher and entrepreneur. In 2021, he and his sister [Daniela Amodei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Amodei "Daniela Amodei") co-founded [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic "Anthropic"), the company behind the [large language model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model "Large language model") series [Claude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(language_model) "Claude (language model)"). Before that, he was the vice president of research at [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI "OpenAI"). At US$965 billion as of May 2026, Anthropic is one of the most valuable AI [pure play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pure_play "Pure play") companies, rivaled by OpenAI.

Amodei earned a doctorate in [biophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biophysics "Biophysics") from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University"). From 2014 to 2016, he worked on [machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning "Machine learning") at [Baidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baidu "Baidu") and [Google Brain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Brain "Google Brain"). At OpenAI, he worked on the development of [GPT-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-2 "GPT-2"), [GPT-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-3 "GPT-3"), and [reinforcement learning from human feedback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement_learning_from_human_feedback "Reinforcement learning from human feedback"). In 2021, Dario and his sister, Daniela, founded [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic "Anthropic") along with five other former OpenAI employees including [Jared Kaplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Kaplan "Jared Kaplan") and [Chris Olah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Olah "Chris Olah").

Amodei has led an [AI safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_safety "AI safety") culture at Anthropic, and has expressed a [probability of catastrophic harm from AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P(doom) "P(doom)") at 10–25%. Simultaneously, Amodei has advocated for democratic nations to [maintain an AI lead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence_arms_race "Artificial intelligence arms race") to [deter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deterrence_theory "Deterrence theory") authoritarian nations, [especially China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence_industry_in_China "Artificial intelligence industry in China"), via advanced [military AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_applications_of_artificial_intelligence "Military applications of artificial intelligence"), [semiconductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor "Semiconductor") [export controls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Export_control "Export control") and [distillation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_distillation "Knowledge distillation") safeguards. Since 2026, Amodei has also called for a slowdown of AI development, especially [recursive self-improvement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursive_self-improvement "Recursive self-improvement") by democratic states, while engaging China in [arms control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arms_control "Arms control")-analogous diplomacy. *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine listed Amodei as one of [the world's 100 most influential people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") in 2025 and 2026.

## Early life

Dario Amodei was born in 1983 in San Francisco, California. His sister, [Daniela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Amodei "Daniela Amodei"), was born four years later. His father, Riccardo Amodei, an Italian-American leather craftsman from [Massa Marittima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massa_Marittima "Massa Marittima"), [Tuscany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuscany "Tuscany"), died of [hepatitis C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepatitis_C "Hepatitis C") when Dario was 23 years old. His mother, Elena Engel, a [Jewish American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Jews "American Jews") born in Chicago, worked as a [project manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_manager "Project manager") for libraries.

Dario grew up in San Francisco and graduated from [Lowell High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_High_School_(San_Francisco) "Lowell High School (San Francisco)"). He was a member of the [USA Physics Olympiad team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Physics_Olympiad "United States Physics Olympiad") in 2000. Amodei began college at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech "Caltech"), where he worked with [Tom Tombrello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Tombrello "Tom Tombrello") as one of his Physics 11 students. He later transferred to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University"), where he received a bachelor's degree in physics. He was awarded the [Hertz Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertz_Foundation "Hertz Foundation") Fellowship in 2007 while earning his doctorate in [biophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biophysics "Biophysics") from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University "Princeton University"). He later received the Hertz Thesis Prize in 2011 for his doctoral research on [electrophysiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrophysiology "Electrophysiology") of [neural circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_circuit "Neural circuit"). He was a postdoctoral scholar at the [Stanford University School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University_School_of_Medicine "Stanford University School of Medicine").

## Career

From November 2014 until October 2015, he worked at [Baidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baidu "Baidu"). After that, he worked at [Google Brain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Brain "Google Brain"). In 2016, Amodei joined [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI "OpenAI"). He worked on the development of [GPT-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-2 "GPT-2"), [GPT-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-3 "GPT-3"), and [reinforcement learning from human feedback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement_learning_from_human_feedback "Reinforcement learning from human feedback").

In 2021, Dario and his sister, Daniela, founded [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic "Anthropic") along with five other former senior members of OpenAI including [Jared Kaplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Kaplan "Jared Kaplan") and [Chris Olah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Olah "Chris Olah"). The Amodei siblings were among those who left OpenAI due to directional differences.

In November 2023, the board of directors of OpenAI approached Amodei about replacing [Sam Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Altman "Sam Altman") and potentially merging the two startups. Amodei declined both offers.

*[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine listed Amodei as one of [the world's 100 most influential people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") in 2025, and again in 2026 alongside his sister Daniela. He also was named as one of the "Architects of AI" for *Time*'s [Person of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Person_of_the_Year "Time Person of the Year").

As of May 2026,[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dario_Amodei&action=edit) Anthropic has an estimated value of $965 billion, with *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* estimating Amodei's net worth to be $15.5 billion.

In September 2026, Amodei called for the technology industry to slow the pace of AI development and said Anthropic would allow independent evaluators permanent access to its models to assess whether the company was following its safety commitments. In Anthropic's IPO financials and prospectus release, it was disclosed that Amodei's compensation package in 2025 tallied $18 million, including stocks. Their IPO is valued at $2 trillion as of October 2026.

## Views

### Benefits of AI

In October 2024, Amodei published an essay titled "Machines of Loving Grace", in which he speculated about how AI could improve human welfare. In it, he writes, "I think that most people are underestimating just how radical the upside of AI could be, just as I think most people are underestimating how bad the risks could be." The essay described a vision of civilization where the risks of AI had been addressed and powerful AI was applied to raise the quality of life for everyone, suggesting that AI could contribute to enormous advances in biology, neuroscience, economic development, global peace, work, and meaning of lives. Amodei wrote separately that his father's death, a few years before the discovery of an effective Hepatitis C treatment ([sofosbuvir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofosbuvir "Sofosbuvir")), is one motivation for his support for AI-accelerated medical research.

In "Machines of Loving Grace", Amodei also stresses the importance, "that democracies have the upper hand on the world stage when powerful AI is created", and argues for an "entente" strategy where a coalition of democracies uses AI to achieve a decisive strategic and military advantage over their adversaries, while distributing the benefits to all cooperating democratic nations.

### Risks of AI

Amodei has led an [AI safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_safety "AI safety") culture at Anthropic, and has expressed a [probability of catastrophic harm from AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P(doom) "P(doom)") at 10–25%. At the same time, Amodei has advocated for democratic nations to [maintain an AI lead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence_arms_race "Artificial intelligence arms race") to [deter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deterrence_theory "Deterrence theory") authoritarian nations, especially China, via advanced [military AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_applications_of_artificial_intelligence "Military applications of artificial intelligence"), [semiconductor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor "Semiconductor") [export controls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Export_control "Export control") and [distillation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_distillation "Knowledge distillation") safeguards. Since 2026, Amodei has also called for a slowdown of AI development, especially [recursive self-improvement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursive_self-improvement "Recursive self-improvement") by democratic states, while engaging China in [arms control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arms_control "Arms control")-analogous diplomacy.

In January 2026, Amodei published a follow-up essay titled "The Adolescence of Technology", which focuses on the risks posed by powerful AI. In it, he discusses five major categories of AI risk: autonomous systems acting against human interests, misuse for mass destruction, misuse to seize political power, economic disruption, and unforeseen effects of rapid technological change.

The first category concerns the possibility that AI systems develop goals or behaviors [misaligned with human intentions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_alignment "AI alignment"). He notes that such behaviors have already been observed in testing at Anthropic, including AI models engaging in deception, blackmail, and scheming.

The second category involves misuse of AI for destruction by individuals or small groups, with Amodei expressing particular concern about [biological weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_weapon "Biological weapon"). He warns that AI could enable people without specialized training to create [weapons of mass destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapon_of_mass_destruction "Weapon of mass destruction").

The third category concerns misuse of AI by powerful actors to seize or maintain power. Amodei cautions that AI could enable authoritarian governments to conduct unprecedented [surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance "Surveillance"), deploy [autonomous weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_weapon "Autonomous weapon"), and engage in mass propaganda. He identifies the [Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Party "Chinese Communist Party") as the greatest threat in this regard, arguing that democracies must maintain AI leadership to prevent a "global totalitarian dictatorship".

The fourth category addresses economic disruption, including [mass labor displacement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_unemployment "Technological unemployment") and concentration of wealth. Amodei notes that AI could displace half of all entry-level white-collar jobs within one to five years, and warns of wealth concentration exceeding that of the [Gilded Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilded_Age "Gilded Age"), with personal fortunes potentially reaching into the trillions of dollars.

The fifth category encompasses indirect effects and unknown factors, including rapid advances in biology that could alter human lifespans or human intelligence, unhealthy changes to human life from AI interaction, and challenges to human purpose in a world where AI exceeds human capabilities across virtually all domains.

### Dispute with the Department of Defense

In February 2026, Amodei refused to remove a contractual ban on [Claude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(language_model) "Claude (language model)") usage for mass domestic surveillance or fully autonomous weapons, as requested by [Pete Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Hegseth "Pete Hegseth")'s [Department of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Defense "United States Department of Defense") (DoD). Anthropic was subsequently labeled a DoD "supply-chain risk" and the Trump administration ordered US agencies to stop using Claude. The Pentagon denied interest in such use cases but insisted on unrestricted access. The DoD's actions have been widely condemned as illegal retaliation against [protected speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_speech "Freedom of speech") with several organizations filing [amicus briefs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amicus_briefs "Amicus briefs") supporting Anthropic. On March 26, 2026, a federal judge issued a [temporary injunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injunction "Injunction") against the DoD, agreeing that their actions appeared to be "classic [First Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution "First Amendment to the United States Constitution") retaliation."

## Personal life

Amodei began dating Camilla Clark in 2014 and married her in 2022.
