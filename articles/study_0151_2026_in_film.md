# 2026 in film

**2026 in film** is an overview of events in the film industry scheduled to occur in 2026. [Best Picture Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture "Academy Award for Best Picture")-winners *[All Quiet on the Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Quiet_on_the_Western_Front_(1930_film) "All Quiet on the Western Front (1930 film)")* and *[Cimarron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cimarron_(1931_film) "Cimarron (1931 film)")* entered the [public domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_domain "Public domain") this year.

### Box office records

* *[Spider-Man: Brand New Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Brand_New_Day "Spider-Man: Brand New Day")* had the [biggest opening weekend ever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_openings_for_films#Biggest_openings_in_the_United_States_and_Canada "List of highest-grossing openings for films") in the United States and Canada with $360 million, outdoing *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")* (2019). It also represented the second film to debut with more than $300 million domestically.
  + The film had the [biggest opening day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_openings_for_films#Biggest_opening_day_in_the_United_States_and_Canada "List of highest-grossing openings for films") in the United States and Canada with $168 million, overtaking *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")* (2019).
  + It became the [highest-grossing film of all time domestically in the United States and Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films_in_the_United_States_by_year "List of highest-grossing films in the United States by year"), surpassing *[Star Wars: The Force Awakens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Force_Awakens "Star Wars: The Force Awakens")* (2015). [Lucasfilm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucasfilm "Lucasfilm") congratulated the film for beating their record.
  + It crossed the $1 billion worldwide milestone six days after release, becoming [the second-fastest film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fastest-grossing_films "List of fastest-grossing films") to reach that milestone behind *[Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")*.
  + It became the 64th film overall to gross $1 billion worldwide.
  + The film crossed the $2 billion milestone, surpassing the $1.9 billion gross of *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home "Spider-Man: No Way Home")*, becoming the highest-grossing [Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man "Spider-Man") film worldwide and the highest-grossing [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures "Sony Pictures") release worldwide. It reached the milestone in 19 days, becoming [the second-fastest film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fastest-grossing_films "List of fastest-grossing films") to reach that milestone behind *[Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")*.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]
  + *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* became the 8th film to cross the $2 billion mark, the third film to cross the $2.5 billion mark and the [third-highest grossing film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films "List of highest-grossing films").
  + In India, it had the biggest opening for a foreign film (surpassing *Avengers: Endgame*) and became the [highest-grossing foreign film ever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films_in_India "List of highest-grossing films in India") (surpassing *[Avatar: The Way of Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_The_Way_of_Water "Avatar: The Way of Water")*).
* *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")* became the highest grossing movie of all time in October, with the rerelease *Encore* making the film surpass *[Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_(2009_film) "Avatar (2009 film)")* for the second time. *Endgame* initially surpassed *Avatar* in July 2019, after which the latter retook the record in March 2021.
* *[The Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Odyssey_(2026_film) "The Odyssey (2026 film)")* became the [highest-grossing R-rated film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_R-rated_films "List of highest-grossing R-rated films") of all time, surpassing *[Deadpool & Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool_%26_Wolverine "Deadpool & Wolverine")* (2024).
  + It became the 65th film overall to cross $1 billion worldwide.
  + It became [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan "Christopher Nolan")'s highest-grossing film worldwide, surpassing *[The Dark Knight Rises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_Rises "The Dark Knight Rises")* (2012).
  + It had the highest-grossing opening weekend and the biggest second weekend for Nolan, surpassing *The Dark Knight Rises*.
  + The film set the record for the highest-grossing opening weekend at [BFI IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BFI_IMAX "BFI IMAX"), surpassing *[Star Wars: The Last Jedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Last_Jedi "Star Wars: The Last Jedi")* (2017).
  + It became the highest-grossing [IMAX release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_released_in_IMAX "List of films released in IMAX") of all time, surpassing *[Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_(2009_film) "Avatar (2009 film)")* (2009).
  + It became the first [R-rated film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_film_rating_system "Motion Picture Association film rating system") to cross $1.7 billion and became the [eleventh-highest grossing film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films#Highest-grossing_films "List of highest-grossing films").
  + It became Nolan's highest-grossing film domestically, surpassing *[The Dark Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight "The Dark Knight")* (2008).
  + It became the highest-grossing [Universal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Pictures "Universal Pictures") film ever, surpassing *[Jurassic World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World "Jurassic World")* (2015).
* The *[Toy Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_(franchise) "Toy Story (franchise)")* film series surpassed $4 billion with the release of *[Toy Story 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_5 "Toy Story 5").*
  + *Toy Story 5* became the highest grossing film of the *Toy Story* franchise, surpassing *[Toy Story 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_4 "Toy Story 4")* (2019).
  + It became the 17th [animated film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animated_film "Animated film") and 63rd overall to cross $1 billion and [tenth-highest grossing animated film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highest-grossing_animated_films "Highest-grossing animated films") of all time.
* [*Michael*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_(2026_film) "Michael (2026 film)") became the [highest-grossing biopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_biographical_films "List of highest-grossing biographical films") of all time, surpassing *[Oppenheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppenheimer_(film) "Oppenheimer (film)")* (2023).
  + It has the biggest opening weekend for a biopic with $217.4 million grossed worldwide. It also has the biggest opening weekend for a [musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_film "Musical film") biopic.
  + It became the highest-grossing [Lionsgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate_Films "Lionsgate Films") film ever, surpassing *[The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn Part 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_Breaking_Dawn_%E2%80%93_Part_2 "The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2")* (2012) and *[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire "The Hunger Games: Catching Fire")* (2013).
  + The film became the [highest-grossing musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_musical_films "List of highest-grossing musical films") biopic of all time, dethroning *[Bohemian Rhapsody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemian_Rhapsody_(film) "Bohemian Rhapsody (film)")* (2018).
  + The film surpassed *[The Passion of the Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Passion_of_the_Christ "The Passion of the Christ")* (2004) to become the highest-grossing biopic domestically in North America.
  + It became the first biopic and the 62nd overall to gross over $1 billion worldwide.
  + [Jaafar Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaafar_Jackson "Jaafar Jackson"), who made his acting debut in *Michael*, became the first lead actor to debut with over $1 billion.
* The *[Super Mario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_non-video_game_media_featuring_Mario "List of non-video game media featuring Mario")* film series surpassed $2 billion with the release of *[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Super_Mario_Galaxy_Movie "The Super Mario Galaxy Movie")*. It became the first [video game film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_film "Video game film") franchise to surpass $2 billion.
  + *Super Mario* became the first [animated film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animated_film "Animated film") franchise with two films opening with more than $350 million worldwide.
  + *The Super Mario Galaxy Movie* became the first film of 2026 and the 61st overall to cross $1 billion.
  + *Super Mario* became the first video game film franchise with two films crossing $1 billion.
* *[The Devil Wears Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil_Wears_Prada_(film) "The Devil Wears Prada (film)")* film series surpassed $1 billion with the release of *[The Devil Wears Prada 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil_Wears_Prada_2 "The Devil Wears Prada 2")*.
  + *The Devil Wears Prada 2* became the highest-grossing film in the franchise, surpassing its predecessor.
* *[Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary_(film) "Project Hail Mary (film)")* became [Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer "Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer")'s highest-grossing domestic release, surpassing *[Skyfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyfall "Skyfall")* (2012).
* *[Pegasus 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_3_(film) "Pegasus 3 (film)")* became the [highest-grossing pure sports film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_sports_films "List of highest-grossing sports films") of all time, surpassing *[F1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F1_(film) "F1 (film)")* (2025).
  + The *Pegasus* film series surpassed $1 billion with the release of *Pegasus 3*.
* *[Obsession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obsession_(2025_film) "Obsession (2025 film)")* became [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features "Focus Features")' highest grossing domestic release in its history, and is one of the most profitable in history having been produced on a $750,000 budget.
  + The film became the first film since *[E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.T._the_Extra-Terrestrial "E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial")* (1982) to see ticket sales increase in its second and third weekends respectively outside of the holiday season.
  + The film surpassed *[The Blair Witch Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blair_Witch_Project "The Blair Witch Project")* (1999) to become the highest-grossing [film festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_festival "Film festival") acquisition of all time.
  + It surpassed *[Sinners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinners_(2025_film) "Sinners (2025 film)")* (2025) to become the highest-grossing original Hollywood [live-action film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live-action_film "Live-action film") of the decade.
  + The film surpassed *[Enter the Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enter_the_Dragon "Enter the Dragon")* (1973) to become the highest-grossing [low-budget film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-budget_film "Low-budget film") with a production budget under $1 million.
* With the release of *[Minions & Monsters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minions_%26_Monsters "Minions & Monsters")*, the *[Despicable Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Despicable_Me "Despicable Me")* franchise became the first animated film franchise to surpass $6 billion.
* *[Backrooms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backrooms_(film) "Backrooms (film)")* became [A24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A24 "A24")'s biggest opening weekend in its history with $118 million globally.
  + *[Backrooms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backrooms_(film) "Backrooms (film)")* became A24's highest grossing film to date, overtaking *[Marty Supreme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marty_Supreme "Marty Supreme")* (2025).
  + Director [Kane Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kane_Parsons "Kane Parsons") was 20 years old at the time of the film's release, making him the youngest filmmaker to reach number one at the United States box office. The previous record holder, [Josh Trank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Trank "Josh Trank"), was 27 years old when his film *[Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicle_(film) "Chronicle (film)")* (2012) was released.
* The [*Moana* franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moana_(franchise) "Moana (franchise)") surpassed $2 billion with the release of the [live-action remake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moana_(2026_film) "Moana (2026 film)") of the [original *Moana* film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moana_(2016_film) "Moana (2016 film)").
* The [*Scary Movie* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scary_Movie_(film_series) "Scary Movie (film series)") surpassed $1 billion with the release of *[Scary Movie 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scary_Movie_(2026_film) "Scary Movie (2026 film)")*. It became the first parody film franchise to surpass $1 billion.
* The [*Scream* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_(franchise) "Scream (franchise)") became the sixth horror film franchise to surpass $1 billion with the release of *[Scream 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_7 "Scream 7")*.
  + *Scream 7* became the highest grossing film in the series, surpassing *[Scream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_(1996_film) "Scream (1996 film)")* (1996).

### Franchise records

* The *[Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe")* became [the first franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_franchises_and_film_series "List of highest-grossing franchises and film series") to surpass $33 billion, $34 billion and $35 billion with the release of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* as well as the rerelease *Avengers: Endgame Encore*.
* The [*Spider-Man* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_in_film "Spider-Man in film") became the second film franchise to surpass $12 and $13 billion with the release of *Spider Man: Brand New Day*.

### Other records

* With 718.6 million views in the first 24 hours and later crossing 1.1 billion, *[Spider-Man: Brand New Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Brand_New_Day "Spider-Man: Brand New Day")* (2026) became the most-viewed trailer of all time, surpassing *[Deadpool & Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool_%26_Wolverine "Deadpool & Wolverine")* (2024).
