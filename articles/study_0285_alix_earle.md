# Alix Earle

**Alix Ashley Earle** (born December 16, 2000) is an American [social media personality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influencer "Influencer") who rose to popularity posting "[Get Ready With Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_Ready_With_Me "Get Ready With Me")" videos on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok") in which she shares details of her personal life. She is known by online communities for promoting products in a way that rapidly increases sales, which has been dubbed by users as the "Alix Earle effect".

## Early life and education

Earle was born December 16, 2000, in [Monmouth County, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monmouth_County,_New_Jersey "Monmouth County, New Jersey"), to Thomas "TJ" Earle, a successful construction business owner in [Wall Township, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Township,_New_Jersey "Wall Township, New Jersey"), and Alisa Maniaci, who is of [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians "Italians") descent. Her parents met at [Lynn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynn_University "Lynn University") in [Boca Raton, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Raton,_Florida "Boca Raton, Florida"). In July 2008, the *[New York Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Post "New York Post")* reported that Earle's father was having an affair with [Ashley Alexandra Dupré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Alexandra_Dupr%C3%A9 "Ashley Alexandra Dupré"), an escort known for her involvement in the [Eliot Spitzer prostitution scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliot_Spitzer_prostitution_scandal "Eliot Spitzer prostitution scandal") that resulted in [Spitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliot_Spitzer "Eliot Spitzer") resigning as [Governor of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_New_York "Governor of New York"). Earle's parents divorced in 2013 and her father married Dupré in October of that year. Earle has a younger sister, Ashtin Earle, and three half-siblings from her father's second marriage.

Earle graduated from [Red Bank Catholic High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bank_Catholic_High_School "Red Bank Catholic High School") in 2019 and attended the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami "University of Miami"), where she graduated with a degree in [marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing "Marketing") in 2023.

## Career

### Social media

In February 2020, as a [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami "University of Miami") freshman, Earle began posting on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok"). Her first video depicted her and her friends showing off their outfits, which were made of trash bags.

In the summer of 2022, she was affected by acne stemming from large facial cysts. She initially avoided posting with the acne visible, but later decided to use her platform to help others struggling with the same issue. After her acne video, Earle began posting "[Get Ready With Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_Ready_With_Me "Get Ready With Me")" (GRWM) videos showcasing her makeup routine while discussing her daily life.

Upon graduation, Earle created the Alix Earle Scholarship for students at [Miami Herbert Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Herbert_Business_School "Miami Herbert Business School"). In July 2025, Earle was named in the inaugural TIME100 Creators list, which recognized 100 influential online creators from around the world.

### Podcast and other ventures

In September 2023, Earle announced a podcast, *Hot Mess with Alix Earle*, produced by The Unwell Network, which is led by [Alex Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Cooper "Alexandra Cooper"). Earle was named in the [*Forbes* 30 Under 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_30_Under_30 "Forbes 30 Under 30") list in 2023. In June 2024, Earle appeared in the music video for the single "Girls" by the Australian artist [The Kid Laroi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kid_Laroi "The Kid Laroi").

In 2025, Earle became an investor and brand partner for SipMargs, a company that sells canned cocktail margaritas. In May 2025, Earle was announced as a contestant on [season 34](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_34 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 34") of [*Dancing with the Stars*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series) "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series)"). Partnered with [Valentin Chmerkovskiy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Chmerkovskiy "Valentin Chmerkovskiy"), she reached the finale and ultimately placed second behind [Robert Irwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Irwin_(conservationist) "Robert Irwin (conservationist)"). In December 2025, Earle was announced as a strategic investor in Gorgie, a clean energy drink brand founded in 2022 by Michelle Cordeiro Grant. In March 2026, Earle announced her new skincare brand, Reale Actives after a months-long teaser campaign with [Easter egg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_egg_(media) "Easter egg (media)") clues across social media.

In September, 2026, Earle starred in the new Netflix series *Earle Meets World*, a reality show starring her family.

## Personal life

Earle dated [Tyler Wade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_Wade "Tyler Wade"), a professional baseball player for the [San Diego Padres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Padres "San Diego Padres"), for three months in late 2022. In November 2023, Earle confirmed her relationship with [Braxton Berrios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braxton_Berrios "Braxton Berrios"), a [wide receiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide_receiver "Wide receiver") for the [New York Giants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Giants "New York Giants"). Earle and Berrios split in December 2025.

In December 2022, Earle disclosed on video her use of the [SSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SSRI "SSRI") [Lexapro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexapro "Lexapro") to deal with anxiety issues she has had since high school. In October 2023, Earle spoke publicly about her struggles with an [eating disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eating_disorder "Eating disorder") and [body dysmorphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_dysmorphic_disorder "Body dysmorphic disorder") during high school.

In 2026, Earle claimed that the only book she may have read was [Dr. Seuss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Seuss "Dr. Seuss"), and other than that she has "never read a book", despite having gained a degree in marketing.
