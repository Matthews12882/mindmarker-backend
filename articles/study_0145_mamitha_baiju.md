# Mamitha Baiju

**Mamitha Baiju** (Malayalam pronunciation: [[/mɐmiːt̪ɐ bɐid͡ʒu/]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Malayalam "Help:IPA/Malayalam")) (born 22 June 2001) is an Indian actress who appears predominantly in [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam_cinema "Malayalam cinema") and [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_cinema "Tamil cinema") films. She has acted as a child artist in an uncredited role in the 2016 film *[Paavada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paavada "Paavada")*. She debuted with the 2017 film *[Sarvopari Palakkaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarvopari_Palakkaran "Sarvopari Palakkaran")*. She won the [Kerala Film Critics Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala_Film_Critics_Award "Kerala Film Critics Award") for Best Supporting Actress for her role in *[Kho-Kho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kho-Kho_(2021_film) "Kho-Kho (2021 film)")* (2021). Her role in *[Premalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premalu "Premalu")* (2024) became her breakout role and the film emerged as [one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_Malayalam_films "List of highest-grossing Malayalam films"). She went on to star in commercial successes such as *[Dude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dude_(2025_film) "Dude (2025 film)")* (2025), *[Jana Nayagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jana_Nayagan "Jana Nayagan")* (2026), *[Vishwanath & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishwanath_%26_Sons "Vishwanath & Sons")* (2026) and *[Bethlehem Kudumba Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem_Kudumba_Unit "Bethlehem Kudumba Unit")* (2026). The latter emerged as the [highest-grossing Malayalam film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_Malayalam_films "List of highest-grossing Malayalam films").

## Early life and education

Mamitha hails from [Kidangoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidangoor,_Kottayam "Kidangoor, Kottayam") in the [Kottayam district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kottayam_district "Kottayam district") of [Kerala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala "Kerala"). She is the daughter of Dr. Baiju Krishnan, a diabetologist, and Mini. She has an elder brother, Mithun.

Mamitha studied at Mary Mount Public School in Kattachira and at N.S.S. Higher Secondary School, Kidangoor. She was pursuing a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science "Bachelor of Science") in [Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology "Psychology") from the [Sacred Heart College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_College,_Thevara "Sacred Heart College, Thevara") in [Kochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kochi "Kochi") but later dropped out during her third year due to attendance shortage.

## Career

### Career beginnings and early work (2017 - 2023)

Mamitha has acted in an uncredited role, as a child artist in the Prithviraj starrer *[Paavada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paavada "Paavada")* in 2016. She starred in *[Sarvopari Palakkaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarvopari_Palakkaran "Sarvopari Palakkaran")* (2017), followed by small supporting roles in films like *[Honey Bee 2: Celebrations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey_Bee_2:_Celebrations "Honey Bee 2: Celebrations")*, [*Dakini*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakini_(film) "Dakini (film)"), *[Varathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varathan "Varathan")* and *[Vikruthi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikruthi "Vikruthi")*. She had her first prominent roles in 2021 in films like *[Operation Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Java "Operation Java")* and *[Kho Kho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kho-Kho_(2021_film) "Kho-Kho (2021 film)")*. Her performance in *[Kho Kho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kho-Kho_(2021_film) "Kho-Kho (2021 film)")* earned her [Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala_Film_Critics_Association_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress "Kerala Film Critics Association Award for Best Supporting Actress") at [Kerala Film Critics Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala_Film_Critics_Award "Kerala Film Critics Award"). In 2022, she played Sona in the film *[Super Sharanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Sharanya "Super Sharanya")* which became a commercial success. Sajin Shrijith of *[The New Indian Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Indian_Express "The New Indian Express")* wrote about her performance, "She has proved already to be an actor with much potential, and she could reach greater heights if she continues to pick sensible scripts."

### Career breakthrough and rising commercial success (2024 - present)

In 2024, she played her first leading role as Reenu in the film *[Premalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premalu "Premalu")* directed by [Girish A.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girish_A._D. "Girish A. D."), and it became her breakout role. The film opened to widespread critical acclaim and it currently ranks as one of the [highest-grossing Malayalam films of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_Malayalam_films "List of highest-grossing Malayalam films"). Aswin Devan of *[The New Indian Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Indian_Express "The New Indian Express")* wrote of her performance, "Despite the film featuring a pool of talented actors, Mamitha Baiju stands out as a charmer, effortlessly stealing the show."

Her Tamil debut film was *[Rebel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_(2024_film) "Rebel (2024 film)")* (2024) starring [G. V. Prakash Kumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._V._Prakash_Kumar "G. V. Prakash Kumar"). The same year she dubbed for [Krithi Shetty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krithi_Shetty "Krithi Shetty") for *[ARM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARM_(film) "ARM (film)")*. In 2025, she starred opposite [Pradeep Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pradeep_Ranganathan "Pradeep Ranganathan") and [R. Sarathkumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Sarathkumar "R. Sarathkumar") in the film *[Dude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dude_(2025_film) "Dude (2025 film)")* (2025). It opened to mixed reviews, earning more than ₹100 crores at the box-office and widespread acclaim for her portrayal. Roopa Radhakrishnan of *[The Times of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_of_India "The Times of India")* noted "Mamitha is terrific. The actress has dubbed for herself and it has added a sense of authenticity to her character."

Mamitha next starred with [Dhanush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhanush "Dhanush") in *[Kara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kara_(film) "Kara (film)")* (2026). A Gulte.com review noted "Mamitha Baiju is a surprise package. She got a very limited role in the film but she delivered a very good performance in whatever screentime she had." She then starred with [Vijay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijay_(actor) "Vijay (actor)") in *[Jana Nayagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jana_Nayagan "Jana Nayagan")* (2026). Aswin Bharadwaj of Lensmen Reviews praised her climax fight sequence, writing that she "cracked the superhero landing pose" and matched Vijay's screen presence.

Mamitha then starred in the [romantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romance_film "Romance film") [family drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_drama "Family drama") film *[Vishwanath & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishwanath_%26_Sons "Vishwanath & Sons")* opposite [Suriya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriya "Suriya") directed by [Venky Atluri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venky_Atluri "Venky Atluri"). The film became a commercial success and she received critical acclaim for her performance. Janani K noted of her performance, "Mamitha Baiju's Maddy could easily have been written off as a typical *loosu ponnu* (immature woman), borderline toxic. Credit to her for making the character loveable instead of annoying. She knows exactly when to let her bubbliness out and when to rein it in." She reunited with director [Girish A.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girish_A._D. "Girish A. D.") for a third time, starring in the rom-com *[Bethlehem Kudumba Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem_Kudumba_Unit "Bethlehem Kudumba Unit")* opposite [Nivin Pauly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nivin_Pauly "Nivin Pauly"). The film received widespread critical acclaim upon release and became the [highest-grossing Malayalam film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_Malayalam_films "List of highest-grossing Malayalam films"). Santanu Das of [Hindustan Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Times "Hindustan Times") wrote of her performance, "It is Mamitha Baiju who carries the film, her face lighting up the screen. Ashley is such a tricky part to play, given what she chooses to say and what she hides. It is a graceful, understated performance without which the film wouldn't work."

She will reunite with her *Premalu* co-star [Sangeeth Prathap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangeeth_Prathap "Sangeeth Prathap") to star in the Malayalam film Hello Dear. In 2026, Mamitha Baiju was announced as the lead actress in the inaugural production of Pradeep Ranganathan's production company, PR Show.

## In the media

At the Telugu success meet for [Premalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premalu "Premalu") in March 2024, [S. S. Rajamouli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Rajamouli "S. S. Rajamouli") praised Mamitha Baiju's performance, predicting that she would emerge as a heartthrob in South cinema. In a 2026 profile, The Hollywood Reporter India described Mamitha as a "pop-culture phenomenon," citing her "likeably awkward charm" on and off camera and her unrehearsed, natural public persona, "free from the trappings of PR-mandated stiffness."

*The Hollywood Reporter India* noted that reels featuring her routinely resurface on social media feeds long after a film's release, calling her a fixture of "the movie fandom consciousness" of the moment. This online popularity has coincided with her commercial rise. Following the successive box office success of [Jana Nayagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jana_Nayagan "Jana Nayagan"), [Vishwanath & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishwanath_%26_Sons "Vishwanath & Sons"), and [Bethlehem Kudumba Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem_Kudumba_Unit "Bethlehem Kudumba Unit") in 2026, she became only the third Indian actress, after [Deepika Padukone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepika_Padukone "Deepika Padukone") and [Alia Bhatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alia_Bhatt "Alia Bhatt"), to star in three films each grossing over ₹200 crore worldwide within a single calendar year.
