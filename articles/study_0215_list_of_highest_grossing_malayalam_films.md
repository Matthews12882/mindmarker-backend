# List of highest-grossing Malayalam films

[Malayalam cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam_cinema "Malayalam cinema") is part of [Indian cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_India "Cinema of India"), based in [Kerala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerala "Kerala") and focused on producing films in the [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam "Malayalam") language. This ranking lists the highest-grossing Malayalam films based on conservative global [box office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_office "Box office") estimates reported by various sources. The figures are not adjusted for inflation.
