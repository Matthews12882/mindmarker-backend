# XNXX

**XNXX** is a Czech–French pornographic [video sharing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_sharing "Video sharing") and viewing website. It was founded in 1997 in [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris "Paris"), with servers and offices in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal "Montreal"), [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo "Tokyo") and [Newark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newark,_New_Jersey "Newark, New Jersey").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] It is owned by [WGCZ Holding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WGCZ_Holding "WGCZ Holding"), the same company that runs [XVideos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XVideos "XVideos"). As of February 2026[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=XNXX&action=edit), it is the 44th-[most-visited website](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-visited_websites "List of most-visited websites") in the world and the fourth most-visited pornography website after [Pornhub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pornhub "Pornhub"), [xHamster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XHamster "XHamster"), and [XVideos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XVideos "XVideos").

## History

[WGCZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WGCZ_Holding "WGCZ Holding")'s ownership was first revealed in 2014 when WGCZ brought a [Uniform Domain-Name Dispute-Resolution Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_Domain-Name_Dispute-Resolution_Policy "Uniform Domain-Name Dispute-Resolution Policy") case against a similar domain in 2014.

In 2018, the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India "Government of India") blocked XNXX, among other porn websites, after a [Uttarakhand High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttarakhand_High_Court "Uttarakhand High Court") court order demanding the same in a rape case where the perpetrators stated they were motivated to do so after watching online pornography.

In January 2023, the *[Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times "Financial Times")* reported that WGCZ tube sites [XVideos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XVideos "XVideos") and XNXX received 6 billion visits a month.
