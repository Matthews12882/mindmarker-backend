# G. D. Naidu

**Gopalaswamy Doraiswamy Naidu** (23 March 1893 – 4 January 1974) was an Indian [innovator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innovator "Innovator"), [inventor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventor "Inventor"), [industrialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrialist "Industrialist"), and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator "Educator"). He redesigned and transformed imported technologies into practical and affordable innovations for India, and is widely regarded as a versatile genius. His contributions spanned [industrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering "Industrial engineering"), [electrical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering "Electrical engineering"), [mechanical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering "Mechanical engineering"), [agricultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrotechnology "Agrotechnology"), and [automobile engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_engineering "Automobile engineering"). Credited with several innovations, he manufactured India's first indigenous [electric motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_motor "Electric motor") and [petrol engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrol_engine "Petrol engine") car. He established several companies and educational institutions.

## Early life

Gopalaswamy Doraiswamy Naidu was born on 23 March 1893 in a [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language "Telugu language")-speaking [agrarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture "Agriculture") family at Kalangal in [Coimbatore district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbatore_district "Coimbatore district") of the erstwhile [Madras Presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Presidency "Madras Presidency"). His mother died when he was a few months old. He disliked school and dropped out in the third grade. He worked in his father's farm during the day, and taught himself in the evenings.

As a teenager, Naidu came across a [Rudge-Whitworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudge-Whitworth "Rudge-Whitworth") [motorcycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorcycle "Motorcycle") for the first time when a British official drove one to his village. This sparked his curiosity, and ignited a fascination with [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"). He later moved to [Coimbatore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbatore "Coimbatore"), where he worked several jobs, including [waiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waiter "Waiter"), [mechanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanic "Mechanic"), and [cotton mill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_mill "Cotton mill") worker. He saved about ₹400 (equivalent to ₹110,000 or US$1,200 in 2026) over three years to purchase the same motorcycle, which he had seen a few years back in his village. He then took apart and put back together to understand how it worked.

In 1915, Naidu joined a restaurant run by the British businessman [Robert Stanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Stanes "Robert Stanes"), where he learned [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language "English language"). He later worked in a [ginning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginning "Ginning") factory, and traveled to [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay "Bombay") to capitalize on the cotton textile industry boom; however, he lost his savings when prices subsequently dropped.

## Career

In 1920, Naidu offered to work as a mechanic for Stanes, who helped him secure a loan of ₹4,000 (equivalent to ₹790,000 or US$8,300 in 2026) to start his own transport business, and sold one of his buses to him in 1921. Naidu personally drove the bus for his transport service between [Palani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palani "Palani") and [Pollachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollachi "Pollachi"). He later established the transport company, United Motor Service, which operated a fleet of 280 buses by 1933, and 600 buses in the late 1930s.

Naidu founded New Electric Works in 1930. In the mid-1930s, he developed the "Rasant" [electric shaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_shaver "Electric shaver") by adapting the motor of a toy car. After patenting the device in Europe, he began large scale manufacturing by importing components from several European countries. The product achieved commercial success, selling in several European countries, and was featured in American magazine advertisements. Working along with D. Balasundaram, Naidu developed India's first indigenous electric motor in 1937, which led to establishment of Textool and [Lakshmi Machine Works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakshmi_Machine_Works "Lakshmi Machine Works"). Initially, they manufactured [castings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casting "Casting") and [windings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_coil "Electromagnetic coil"), while importing [bearings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical) "Bearing (mechanical)") and enamelled wire for the motors. However, due to the disruption of the supply chain due to the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War "Second World War"), they built machines to manufacture the wires and bearings locally.

During the Second World War, Naidu manufactured electronic components including [capacitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capacitor "Capacitor") and [resistors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistor "Resistor") in Coimbatore, which he supplied to the [British Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Army "British Army"). He later built several products such as [voting machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_machine "Voting machine"), [juice extractors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juice_extractor "Juice extractor"), coin-operated [phonographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph "Phonograph"), [electronic calculators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_calculator "Electronic calculator"), [lathes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lathe "Lathe"), [projectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projector "Projector"), [five-valve radios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_American_Five "All American Five"), [vending machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vending_machine "Vending machine"), [kerosene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerosene "Kerosene")-run fans, and a distance adjuster for film cameras. In 1952, Naidu built two-seater [petrol engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrol_engine "Petrol engine") car costing ₹2,000 (equivalent to ₹240,000 or US$2,400 in 2026). However, the government denied him license to produce these cars. He established several companies including Universal Radiators, Gopal Clock Industry, Coimbatore Diesel Products and Coimbatore Engineering, Coimbatore Armature Winding Works, and UMS Radio Company.

## Later life and death

In 1944, Naidu retired from active involvement with his companies. He had established a 40 acres (16 ha) farm in 1941, in which he researched and identified new varieties of [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton "Cotton"), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize "Maize"), and [papaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya "Papaya"). He was involved in various philanthropic activities, and established grants for research and welfare schemes. He founded the Industrial Labour Welfare Association to impart vocational training and donated land for the Indian Chamber of Commerce and Industry. He researched and devised methods to build low cost dwellings, that could be built within a day.

In 1945, Naidu established India's first [polytechnic college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_college "Polytechnic college") in Coimbatore, and served as its first principal. He worked with [C. S. Ratnasabhapathy Mudaliar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._S._Ratnasabhapathy_Mudaliar "C. S. Ratnasabhapathy Mudaliar") and [R. K. Shanmukham Chetty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._K._Shanmukham_Chetty "R. K. Shanmukham Chetty") to bring the waters of [Siruvani River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siruvani_River "Siruvani River") to Coimbatore. In 1950, he established the G D Science Museum in Coimbatore. He also organized temporary exhibitions of various machines and gadgets across various cities to demonstrate how they worked. It became permanent as the [G. D. Naidu Industrial Exhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._D._Naidu_Industrial_Exhibition "G. D. Naidu Industrial Exhibition") in Coimbatore in 1967.

Naidu was an avid [photographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photographer "Photographer"), and traveled to several countries. He traveled to the [funeral of King George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funeral_of_King_George_V "Funeral of King George V") in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"), and met [Adolf Hitler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Hitler "Adolf Hitler") in Germany. He died on 4 January 1974.

## Legacy

Naidu is widely considered a versatile genius, and known by the epithet, "[Edison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edison "Edison") of India".
[C. V. Raman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._V._Raman "C. V. Raman") said of Naidu: "A great educator, an entrepreneur in many fields of engineering and industry, a warm-hearted man filled with love for his fellows and a desire to help them in their troubles, Mr Naidu is truly a man in a million – perhaps this is an understatement!" He is credited as one of the "wealth creators" of [Coimbatore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbatore "Coimbatore").

Several institutions and places including the GD Matriculation Higher Secondary School, and the [G. D. Naidu Elevated Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._D._Naidu_Elevated_Expressway "G. D. Naidu Elevated Expressway"), are named after him. Makkal Sinthanai Peravai, a non-profit organization, awards the annual G. D. Naidu Award for Young Scientist in his honour. The [Gedee Car Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedee_Car_Museum "Gedee Car Museum") is a private vintage car museum founded by his son, G. D. Gopal, in 2015 as a commemoration of his father.

Ranjit Kumar and produced by the [Films Division of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Films_Division_of_India "Films Division of India"). It won the Best Science & Technology Film Award at the [66th National Film Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_National_Film_Awards "66th National Film Awards").
* *[G.D.N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.D.N "G.D.N")* (2026) is a biographical film directed by [Krishnakumar Ramakumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Star_Krishna "Five Star Krishna") with [R. Madhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Madhavan "R. Madhavan") playing the role of Naidu.
