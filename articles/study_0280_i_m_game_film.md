# I'm Game (film)

***I'm Game*** is a 2026 Indian [Malayalam-language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam-language "Malayalam-language") [fantasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_film "Fantasy film") [action film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_film "Action film") directed by [Nahas Hidhayath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahas_Hidhayath "Nahas Hidhayath"), who co-wrote the script with Sajeer Baba, Ismail Aboobacker and Bilal Moidu, and produced by [Dulquer Salmaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan "Dulquer Salmaan") and Jom Varghese under the banner of [Wayfarer Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan "Dulquer Salmaan"). The film stars Dulquer Salmaan, alongside [Antony Varghese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Varghese "Antony Varghese"), [Mysskin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysskin "Mysskin"), [Kayadu Lohar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayadu_Lohar "Kayadu Lohar"), [Samyuktha Viswanathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samyuktha_Viswanathan "Samyuktha Viswanathan"), [Kathir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathir_(actor) "Kathir (actor)"), [Vinay Forrt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinay_Forrt "Vinay Forrt") and Parth Tiwari.

Principal photography began in early 2025, and continued from 9 March 2025 to 16 April 2026, spanning over 150 locations across [South India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_India "South India"). The film's music was composed by [Jakes Bejoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakes_Bejoy "Jakes Bejoy") with cinematography by [Jimshi Khalid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimshi_Khalid "Jimshi Khalid") and [Jomon T. John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jomon_T._John "Jomon T. John"). It was edited by [Chaman Chakko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaman_Chakko "Chaman Chakko").

*I'm Game* was released in theatres on 3 September 2026, coinciding with the Janmashtami weekend and received mixed reviews from critics praising Dulquer's performance, technical aspects, action sequences and background score but criticized the screenplay, pacing and predictable narrative. The film was a moderate success.

## Plot

Dan John, a wealthy and lazy businessman living in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad "Hyderabad") has an intense gambling addiction which leads to his downfall in both business and life. The sheer chaos created by him during a sports betting session leads to a chase, which is followed by a road accident that causes Dan to lose his hand, later replaced with a transplant.

## Production

### Development

*I'm Game* was directed by [Nahas Hidhayath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahas_Hidhayath "Nahas Hidhayath"), who previously directed *[RDX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDX_(film) "RDX (film)")* (2023). The screenplay was jointly written by Sajeer Baba, Bilal Moidu and Ismail Aboobacker, with dialogues by Adarsh Sukumaran and Shabas Rasheed. The film was produced by [Dulquer Salmaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan "Dulquer Salmaan") and Jom Varghese under the banner of [Wayfarer Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayfarer_Films "Wayfarer Films"). The project was initially associated with the working title *DQ40*, referring to Dulquer Salmaan's 40th film as a lead actor.

The film was described during its production as a sports-based thriller. Its technical crew includes cinematographer [Jimshi Khalid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimshi_Khalid "Jimshi Khalid"), music composer [Jakes Bejoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakes_Bejoy "Jakes Bejoy"), editor [Chaman Chacko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaman_Chacko "Chaman Chacko") and production designer Ajayan Chalissery.

### Casting

[Dulquer Salmaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan "Dulquer Salmaan") was cast in the lead role. The cast also includes [Kayadu Lohar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayadu_Lohar "Kayadu Lohar"), [Antony Varghese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Varghese "Antony Varghese"), [Kathir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathir_(actor) "Kathir (actor)"), [Mysskin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysskin "Mysskin"), [Vinay Forrt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinay_Forrt "Vinay Forrt"), Parth Tiwari and [Samyuktha Viswanathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samyuktha_Viswanathan "Samyuktha Viswanathan"). Kayadu Lohar was announced as the female lead, while Mysskin joined the film in a prominent role, marking his Malayalam acting debut.

Antony Varghese, who had previously worked with Nahas Hidhayath in *RDX*, was also part of the principal cast. Mysskin described his character as an antagonist in the film.

### Filming

Principal photography commenced in 2025, with the production beginning in [Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram "Thiruvananthapuram"). Antony Varghese and Mysskin attended the film's pooja ceremony marking the beginning of production. Dulquer Salmaan was expected to join the production during a subsequent schedule.

The film was shot over approximately 11 months, covering 156 shooting days across more than 100 locations in [South India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_India "South India"). Mysskin completed his portions during the production, with the film subsequently moving through its remaining schedules.

Filming wrapped in April 2026, which was announced by Salmaan on his social media pages.

## Music

The film's songs and [background score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_score "Film score") are composed by [Jakes Bejoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakes_Bejoy "Jakes Bejoy"). The first single, "Mazhavillaye" sung by [Armaan Malik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armaan_Malik "Armaan Malik"), was released on 10 August 2026. The second single, "Loser" sung by Salmaan himself was released on 27 August 2026. The full soundtrack was released on 29 August 2026 in the audio launch event, held in [Kochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kochi "Kochi").

## Release

*I'm Game* was released Worldwide on 3 September 2026 in its original [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam "Malayalam") language, also it was dubbed in [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language "Telugu language"), [Kannada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kannada "Kannada"), [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language "Tamil language") and [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi "Hindi") languages in standard, [EPIQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qube_Cinema_Technologies "Qube Cinema Technologies") and P[XL] formats. It was originally planned to release on 20 August 2026 on the onam weekend, clashing with *[Khalifa: The Ruler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa:_The_Ruler "Khalifa: The Ruler")* and *[Bethlehem Kudumba Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem_Kudumba_Unit "Bethlehem Kudumba Unit")*, but was postponed due to unfinished post-production works.

### Distribution

[Annapurna Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annapurna_Studios "Annapurna Studios") acquired the theatrical distribution rights for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. [Pen Marudhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen_Studios "Pen Studios") acquired the theatrical distribution rights for North India, while *Lighter Buddha Films* acquired the theatrical distribution rights for Karnataka. *Friday Entertainment* acquired the theatrical distribution rights in France, *Magic Rays* in the United Kingdom, *Suraya Filem* in Malaysia, and *myKinoPlex* in Germany.

## Reception

### Box office

The film grossed ₹4.24 crore at the box office in Kerala on its opening day. The film grossed ₹10 crore net across in its first two days of release in India. The film's global box office reached ₹160 crore.

### Critical response

*I'm Game* received mixed reviews from critics. Tanisha Bhattacharya of [NDTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NDTV "NDTV") praising Dulquer's performance, technical aspects, action sequences and background score but criticized the screenplay, pacing and predictable narrative. S. R. Praveen of *[The Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hindu "The Hindu")* noted that while the film begins with a promising and engaging premise, it ultimately squanders its potential by succumbing to familiar narrative tropes and highlighted that despite its intriguing setup, the long runtime and unfulfilled plot threads regarding themes like greed and wealth cause the film to lose its focus.

Gopika Is of *[The Times of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_of_India "The Times of India")*, awarded the film rating of 2.5 out of 5 stars and noted that while the film attempts to blend action and suspense within a game-centric narrative, it ultimately suffers from a predictable storyline and an uneven pace. While the performance of the lead cast and certain technical elements such as the cinematography received mild appreciation, the reviewer felt the script lacked the depth and tension required to make the thriller engaging throughout its runtime. Anandu Suresh of *[The Indian Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Indian_Express "The Indian Express")* rated the film 2.5 out of 5 stars, describing it as an ambitious fantasy actioner that falls slightly short of its potential and highlighted Dulquer Salmaan's performance and the film's visual scale, while pointing out pacing issues and an uneven second half that relied too heavily on visual effects over narrative depth.

### Audience response

The film received generally positive initial responses from audiences and critics alike, with viewers highlighting Dulquer Salmaan's powerful performance as a strong return to form. Praise was directed toward the film's engaging storyline, high-energy pacing, and technical execution, particularly its action sequences and background score. While a few viewers noted minor pacing flaws in the narrative, the film was largely appreciated for its entertainment value and strong theatrical appeal.
