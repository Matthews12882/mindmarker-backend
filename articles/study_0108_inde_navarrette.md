# Inde Navarrette

**Danielle Fabiola "Inde" Navarrette** ([/ˈɪndi ˌnævəˈrɛti/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*IN-dee NAV-ə-RET-ee*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born March 3, 2001) is an American actress. She began acting in short films as a teenager, before landing roles in the drama series *[13 Reasons Why](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_Reasons_Why "13 Reasons Why")* (2020) and the superhero series *[Superman & Lois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_%26_Lois "Superman & Lois")* (2021–2024). Navarrette achieved global stardom for headlining the supernatural horror film *[Obsession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obsession_(2025_film) "Obsession (2025 film)")* (2025), winning several accolades including a [Critics' Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Super_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Horror_Movie "Critics' Choice Super Award for Best Actress in a Horror Movie"). She will also portray [Rogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_(Marvel_Comics) "Rogue (Marvel Comics)") in the untitled [*X-Men* film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films#Untitled_X-Men_film_(2028) "List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films") (2028) set in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe").

## Early life

Danielle Fabiola Navarrette was born on March 3, 2001, in [Tucson, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson,_Arizona "Tucson, Arizona"). Her father is of Mexican descent and served in the [United States Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Marine_Corps "United States Marine Corps"), and her mother is an Australian hairstylist. Because her father served in the [U.S. military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Armed_Forces "United States Armed Forces"), Navarrette's family relocated frequently, and by age fifteen, she had attended eleven schools. After her parents separated, Navarrette was raised primarily in [Visalia, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visalia,_California "Visalia, California"), briefly attending [Mt. Whitney High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mt._Whitney_High_School "Mt. Whitney High School") in Visalia before relocating to the [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") area and enrolling at [Redondo Union High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redondo_Union_High_School "Redondo Union High School").

Navarrette credits both sides of her family for sparking her interest in acting. Her paternal family introduced her to horror films at a young age, and her maternal family regularly watched the [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards"). Her mother gave her the nickname "Inde" because of her independent nature as a child. Navarrette has four siblings: an older brother, two younger brothers, and a younger sister.

## Career

Navarrette's first role came in the [Snapchat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snapchat "Snapchat") original sitcom *Denton's Death Date* (2019), where she starred as Veronica. In 2020, Navarrette was cast in season four of the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") series *[13 Reasons Why](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_Reasons_Why "13 Reasons Why")* as Estela de la Cruz. In 2021, Navarrette signed with [Creative Artists Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Artists_Agency "Creative Artists Agency") (CAA), and landed her first notable starring role later that year as Sarah Cortez in *[Superman & Lois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_%26_Lois "Superman & Lois")* (2021). In June 2023, it was announced that Navarrette and six of her co-stars were departing the show for the [fourth and final season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_%26_Lois_season_4 "Superman & Lois season 4") due to budget cuts. Navarrette returned to make a few minor appearances in season four. In 2025, Navarrette played Teresa Flores in the action film *[Trap House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trap_House_(film) "Trap House (film)")*.

Navarrette starred as Nikki Freeman in the horror film *[Obsession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obsession_(2025_film) "Obsession (2025 film)")*, which premiered at the [Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival "Toronto International Film Festival") on September 5, 2025 before a United States theatrical release by [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features "Focus Features") on May 15, 2026. Her performance received critical acclaim, with critics highlighting both her emotional vulnerability and physical intensity. Louis Peitzman of *[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(website) "Vulture (website)")* called it a stand-out performance for Navarrette, especially as her first major role in the horror genre. Katie Rife of *[RogerEbert.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RogerEbert.com "RogerEbert.com")* said her emotional scenes revealed the humanity behind Nikki's violent behaviour and made the character more tragic. Guy Lodge of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* praised Navarrette for "acing one of the more physically and emotionally taxing horror leads to come down the pike in a while", and his colleague Clayton Davis named her as a candidate for the [Academy Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress "Academy Award for Best Actress"). Focus Features proceeded to initiate an awards campaign for Navarrette in the fall of 2026. Bill Bria of *[/Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki//Film "/Film")* compared Nikki's impact to performances in classic horror films such as *[The Exorcist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exorcist "The Exorcist")* (1973) and *[Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(1976_film) "Carrie (1976 film)")* (1976). Following the success of *Obsession*, Navarrette said she appreciated the story remaining a standalone film, while expressing interest in continuing to work in the horror genre. She later added: "Everything has changed for me, I’m now in rooms I could never even imagine. I’m pitching my own projects and am hopefully can produce and direct my own stuff."

After the festival premiere of *Obsession*, Navarrette filmed a lead role in the horror film *Invertigo*. In the summer of 2026, she appeared in a music video for singer [Sombr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sombr "Sombr") as well as in a [Gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gap_Inc. "Gap Inc.") commercial alongside American musician [Malcolm Todd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malcolm_Todd "Malcolm Todd"). That same year, after meeting director [Jake Schreier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Schreier "Jake Schreier") and expressing interest in portraying [Mystique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystique_(character) "Mystique (character)"), it was announced that Navarrette was cast as [Rogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_(Marvel_Comics) "Rogue (Marvel Comics)") in an [untitled X-Men reboot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_in_film#Untitled_X-Men_film_(2028) "X-Men in film") set within the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") (MCU), which has a planned 2028 release. Additionally, she is signed on to star in *The Waffle House Index*, a science fiction thriller film to be produced and released by [Apple Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Studios "Apple Studios"); she is also an executive producer for the film. She is scheduled to host an episode of *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* on October 31, 2026.

## Personal life

Navarrette is [bisexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisexuality "Bisexuality"). She is a [video game enthusiast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamer "Gamer"), having formerly live-streamed games such as *[Call of Duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty "Call of Duty")* on [Twitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_(service) "Twitch (service)") and [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") under the username "Inde\_navarrette".
