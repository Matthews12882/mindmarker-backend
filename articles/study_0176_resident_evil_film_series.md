# Resident Evil (film series)

***Resident Evil*** is a [biopunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biopunk "Biopunk") [horror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horror_film "Horror film") film series mainly produced by the German [production company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_company "Production company") [Constantin Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantin_Film "Constantin Film"), co-produced and distributed by [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures "Sony Pictures"), and based on the [Japanese video game franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil "Resident Evil") by [Capcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capcom "Capcom"). The series includes [live-action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live-action "Live-action") films based on the franchise, but not [animated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animated "Animated") films.

Constantin Film bought the rights to adapt the series to live action in January 1997. In 2000, [Paul W. S. Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_W._S._Anderson "Paul W. S. Anderson") was announced as writer and director for *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2002_film) "Resident Evil (2002 film)")* (2002). Anderson continued as writer and producer for *[Resident Evil: Apocalypse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Apocalypse "Resident Evil: Apocalypse")* (2004) and *[Resident Evil: Extinction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Extinction "Resident Evil: Extinction")* (2007), and returned as the director for *[Resident Evil: Afterlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Afterlife "Resident Evil: Afterlife")* (2010), *[Resident Evil: Retribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Retribution "Resident Evil: Retribution")* (2012) and *[Resident Evil: The Final Chapter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_The_Final_Chapter "Resident Evil: The Final Chapter")* (2016). These first six films follow Alice ([Milla Jovovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milla_Jovovich "Milla Jovovich")), a character created for the films. Alice is a covert operative who battles the [Umbrella Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Resident_Evil_characters#Umbrella_Corporation "List of Resident Evil characters"), whose [bioweapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_warfare "Biological warfare") have triggered a [zombie apocalypse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie_apocalypse "Zombie apocalypse"). [Characters from the games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Resident_Evil_characters "List of Resident Evil characters") appear, including [Claire Redfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Redfield "Claire Redfield"), [Jill Valentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jill_Valentine "Jill Valentine"), [Ada Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Wong "Ada Wong"), [Carlos Oliveira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Resident_Evil_characters#Carlos_Oliveira "List of Resident Evil characters"), [Chris Redfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Redfield "Chris Redfield"), [Leon S. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_S._Kennedy "Leon S. Kennedy"), [Barry Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Burton "Barry Burton"), and the antagonists [Albert Wesker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Wesker "Albert Wesker") and [James Marcus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Resident_Evil_characters#James_Marcus "List of Resident Evil characters").

The series was [rebooted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reboot_(fiction) "Reboot (fiction)") with *[Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Welcome_to_Raccoon_City "Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City")* (2021), directed by [Johannes Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Roberts "Johannes Roberts"). It was a commercial and critical failure. A second reboot, *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2026_film) "Resident Evil (2026 film)")*, directed by [Zach Cregger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Cregger "Zach Cregger"), was released in 2026 to widespread critical acclaim and became the highest grossing *Resident Evil* film.

Though most of the films have received generally negative reviews from critics, the *Resident Evil* series has grossed over $1.2 billion. It was once the highest-grossing [film series based on a video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_based_on_video_games "List of films based on video games") and the [highest-grossing horror film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_horror_films "List of highest-grossing horror films"), and is the highest-grossing [zombie film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie_film "Zombie film") series. The series retains the record for the most live-action film adaptations of a video game.

### Original series

#### *Resident Evil* (2002)

In January 1997, [Constantin Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantin_Film "Constantin Film") acquired the live-action film rights to the *Resident Evil* franchise with [Alan B. McElroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_B._McElroy "Alan B. McElroy") possibly writing the script. [George A. Romero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_A._Romero "George A. Romero") was hired by [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures "Sony Pictures") and [Capcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capcom "Capcom") to direct and write *Resident Evil*. His script was later rejected and he was ultimately dropped. While Romero's script was supported by a number of people from Capcom and Constantin, it was rejected by Constantin producer [Bernd Eichinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Eichinger "Bernd Eichinger"), as well as Capcom producer [Yoshiki Okamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshiki_Okamoto "Yoshiki Okamoto"). A [documentary based on this adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_A._Romero%27s_Resident_Evil "George A. Romero's Resident Evil") was released on digital on January 7, 2025. In late 2000, [Paul W. S. Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_W._S._Anderson "Paul W. S. Anderson") was announced as director and writer. Anderson stated the film would not include any [tie-ins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tie-in "Tie-in") with the video game series as "under-performing movie tie-ins are too common and *Resident Evil*, of all games, deserved a good celluloid representation". In 2001, [Milla Jovovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milla_Jovovich "Milla Jovovich") was cast as Alice, an original character who serves as the main protagonist of the film. By May 2001, [Columbia TriStar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Motion_Picture_Group "Sony Pictures Motion Picture Group") was in final negotiations to acquire North American distribution rights to the film and budgeted it at $40 million.

In the film, the [Umbrella Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbrella_Corporation "Umbrella Corporation") operates a secret genetic research facility named the Hive. Located beneath [Raccoon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon_City "Raccoon City"), the Hive has been sealed by its [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI "AI"), the Red Queen, after the release of the T-virus into the facility. The Red Queen kills everyone in the facility to ensure that the T-virus does not reach the surface, but the T-virus reanimates them, transforming the humans and animals into aggressive [zombies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombie "Zombie") and mutants. Alice was once a security operative working for the corporation until her memory was wiped by the Red Queen's nerve gas. As her team investigates the Hive, a monstrous Umbrella test subject called a Licker pursues them onto a train. Alice survives along with teammate Matt Addison, but they are separated and taken by an Umbrella [hazmat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dangerous_goods "Dangerous goods") unit, where Matt begins to genetically transform. Alice awakens from a coma to a ravaged Raccoon City after a T-virus outbreak.

#### *Resident Evil: Apocalypse* (2004)

A [sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequel "Sequel") began production in 2003 after the success of the first film. Anderson returned as the writer but, due to his work on [*Alien vs. Predator*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_vs._Predator_(film) "Alien vs. Predator (film)"), [Alexander Witt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Witt "Alexander Witt") took over the director's role. The film released on September 10, 2004.

In the film, the viral outbreak reaches the surface and to contain the infection, Major Timothy Cain seals all exits to and from [Raccoon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon_City "Raccoon City"). Among the people trapped in the city are [Jill Valentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jill_Valentine "Jill Valentine") and her partner Peyton Wells. After Alice awakens from her coma, she rescues Jill and Peyton from a group of Licker monsters. They are contacted by Dr. Charles Ashford, who directs them to an evacuation point in exchange for the rescue of his daughter Angela, trapped elsewhere in the city. Weapon [Nemesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nemesis_(Resident_Evil) "Nemesis (Resident Evil)") attacks the team after Cain commands it to kill all [STARS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.T.A.R.S._(fictional_organization) "S.T.A.R.S. (fictional organization)") (Special Tactics And Rescue Service) members. The team rescues Angela and makes its way to the evacuation helicopter guided by Umbrella mercenary [Carlos Olivera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Oliveira_(Resident_Evil) "Carlos Oliveira (Resident Evil)"). After Alice's confrontation with Cain and Nemesis leaves them dead, she and the team fly away from Raccoon City as it is destroyed by a nuclear missile. The helicopter crashes in the Arklay Mountains, and Alice is found by an Umbrella search team and taken to a research station. When Alice awakens, she displays extraordinary powers, and escapes with help from Jill and Carlos. As they drive away, Dr. Alexander Isaacs, a top-ranking Umbrella employee, reveals that Alice's escape is part of Umbrella's plan.

#### *Resident Evil: Extinction* (2007)

In November 2005, [Screen Gems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Gems "Screen Gems") gained the rights for the third installment in the franchise, which was then subtitled *Extinction*. Anderson returned as a writer and filming took place in [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico "Mexico"). [Russell Mulcahy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Mulcahy "Russell Mulcahy") was the director. The film was released on September 21, 2007.

The world has been consumed by the T-virus and Umbrella Corporation develops clones of Alice to find one who displays her powers. Elsewhere, [Claire Redfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Redfield "Claire Redfield") leads a convoy of survivors across the Nevada desert, including Raccoon City survivors Carlos Olivera and L.J. Wayne. Alice roams the country alone, staying off the grid. After accidentally destroying her motorcycle, she joins Redfield's convoy, rescuing them from a mob of attacking crows using her [telekinetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic") powers. Alice and the convoy head to [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Valley "Las Vegas Valley") to gather supplies so that they can reach [Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaska "Alaska") to find "Arcadia", thought to be a safe haven. In Las Vegas, they are ambushed by a group of Umbrella Corporation's new super zombies created by Dr. Alexander Isaacs, which kills most of the convoy. L.J., who had been bitten earlier and hiding the progress of the infection, now completely transforms and bites Olivera. Dr. Isaacs himself is bitten and retreats to the Umbrella base where he injects himself with large doses of the anti-virus as a cure. He transforms into a Tyrant, a towering creature with regenerative and psionic powers. Alice, Claire and Carlos locate a helicopter in Umbrella base and plan to use it to extract the survivors to Alaska. Olivera makes his [last stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_stand "Last stand") by sacrificing himself and plowing his truck into a horde of zombies, giving the rest time to load the survivors. Claire and the survivors head towards Alaska, while Alice stays behind to confront Isaacs, and discovers her clones in the facility. After she defeats Isaacs, Alice contacts Umbrella and warns them that she is coming for them with an army of her clones.

#### *Resident Evil: Afterlife* (2010)

Before the third installment had been released, Constantin prepared to release a fourth entry. The film was set in Japan and filmed in [3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-D_film "3-D film") with the [Fusion Camera System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusion_Camera_System "Fusion Camera System") developed by [James Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron "James Cameron") and Vince Pace for the 2009 film *[Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_(2009_film) "Avatar (2009 film)")*. It was written and directed by Paul W. S. Anderson and released on September 10, 2010.

After the events of *Extinction*, Alice and her clones attack the Umbrella Headquarters in Tokyo. Wesker escapes and destroys the facility, killing the clones. He is confronted by Alice aboard his helicopter, and injects her with a serum that removes her super-human abilities. The helicopter crashes, and only Alice is seen emerging from the rubble. After six months of a failed aerial search for other survivors, Alice heads toward the supposed location of Arcadia. She lands on an airfield in Alaska, and finds Claire with an Umbrella controlling device attached to her chest and a wiped memory. Claire and Alice fly to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), where they stumble on a prison wherein they meet Luther West and [Chris Redfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Redfield "Chris Redfield"), Claire's brother. The survivors explain that Arcadia is really a tanker off the coast that has been broadcasting a looped message and is picking up survivors. During an attempted escape, Alice, Claire and Chris are the only ones to emerge and they reach the tanker only to find it abandoned. Claire's memory begins to return and she remembers Umbrella workers attacking her group of survivors and moving them to the tanker. They learn that the ship is an Umbrella research facility, which is conducting experiments on the survivors. The three meet Albert Wesker, who has been granted super-human abilities from the T-virus. After the fight, Alice transmits a message to survivors calling them to create a new and true safe haven. As the film closes, a fleet of Umbrella Corporation gunships led by Valentine prepares to lead an assault on the survivors.

#### *Resident Evil: Retribution* (2012)

Written and directed by Anderson, *Retribution* was planned to be [filmed back to back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_back_film_production "Back to back film production") with the sixth installment, but Anderson decided to focus on the fifth film. Filming took place in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto"), [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario "Ontario") from mid-October to December 23, 2011. [Colin Salmon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Salmon "Colin Salmon") who played One and [Michelle Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Rodriguez "Michelle Rodriguez") who played Rain Ocampo in the first film returned. [Oded Fehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oded_Fehr "Oded Fehr"), who portrayed [Carlos Olivera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Oliveira_(Resident_Evil) "Carlos Oliveira (Resident Evil)") in the second and third films, also returned. Three characters from the video games made their debut in the film series—[Johann Urb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Urb "Johann Urb") as [Leon S. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_S._Kennedy "Leon S. Kennedy"), [Kevin Durand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Durand "Kevin Durand") as [Barry Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Burton "Barry Burton"), and [Li Bingbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Bingbing "Li Bingbing") as [Ada Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Wong "Ada Wong"). It was released internationally on September 14, 2012.

Alice awakens in an Umbrella base where she is interrogated by Valentine. During a power failure orchestrated by Ada Wong, Alice escapes from her cell and encounters her. Wong explains that Wesker plans to aid Alice's escape and battle the base's supercomputer Red Queen, to save what's left of mankind. Wesker has organized a team of freelance operatives to infiltrate the base and help Alice and Ada escape, including Leon S. Kennedy, Barry Burton and Luther West. Along the way, Alice finds a clone of a girl named Becky. They also face clone versions of Carlos Olivera, One and Rain Ocampo, all under the direction of Valentine. After Alice meets with the rescue team, Valentine's soldiers catch up to them, resulting in a shootout that kills Burton, Olivera and One. Alice, West, Kennedy and Becky reach the surface and are met by a submarine, from which Valentine, the clone Rain, and a captured Ada emerge. Valentine battles Alice while Rain fights Kennedy and West. Alice manages to remove the scarab device from Valentine, returning her back to normal, and joins Kennedy in defeating Rain. Alice, Ada, Becky, Leon and Valentine travel to Wesker's base. Wesker injects Alice with the T-virus, returning her superhuman powers, then tells her that she is responsible for saving the remaining humans from extinction.

#### *Resident Evil: The Final Chapter* (2016)

Anderson wrote and directed the sixth and final film in the series, subtitled *The Final Chapter*, which was filmed in 2D and was post-converted to stereoscopic 3D. [Milla Jovovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milla_Jovovich "Milla Jovovich") reprised her role as Alice, with only [Shawn Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Roberts "Shawn Roberts"), [Iain Glen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iain_Glen "Iain Glen") and [Ali Larter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Larter "Ali Larter") returning from previous entries as Albert Wesker, Dr. Isaacs and Claire Redfield, respectively. Continuing from the last film, Anderson expressed his desire for the final film to "come full circle", bringing back characters, themes and the environment of the Hive from the first movie. Filming was set to begin in South Africa in August 2014 but was delayed to the summer of 2015 due to Jovovich's pregnancy. Principal photography commenced on September 18, 2015, in South Africa. It was released on December 23, 2016, in Tokyo and on January 27, 2017, in North America.

Three weeks after the events in *Retribution*, humanity is on its last legs after Alice is betrayed by Wesker in Washington, D.C. Alice encounters the Red Queen, who tells her that she must return to Raccoon City, where the Umbrella Corporation has developed an airborne anti-virus which will kill every organism infected by the T-virus. In a race against time, Alice is captured by Dr. Isaacs, who reveals his previous incarnation was a clone. Alice escapes and arrives in Raccoon City, where she joins forces with Claire Redfield. After holding off Dr. Isaacs' undead army, Alice, Claire and a team of survivors make their way towards the Hive. They encounter the Red Queen, who tells them the T-virus was created by James Marcus, the founder of the Umbrella Corporation to save his daughter, Alicia, from an ailment that caused her to age rapidly. James was betrayed by his business partner, Dr. Isaacs, and killed by Albert Wesker. Dr. Isaacs and the board of the Umbrella Corporation then intended to release the T-virus to cleanse the world, save the rich and powerful with cryogenics in the Hive, and later rebuild it as they desire. After confronting Wesker and the real Dr. Isaacs at the bottom of the Hive, Alice learns she is a clone of the still-living Alicia Marcus. Alice releases the anti-virus, eliminating all infected by the T-virus. She survives, and gains the memories of Alicia Marcus as a reward to reclaim the childhood she never had. The series ends with Alice riding a motorcycle, stating that her job is not finished yet and that it will take several years for the anti-virus to circle the globe.

### Reboot

#### *Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City* (2021)

In May 2017, Constantin chairman [Martin Moszkowicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Moszkowicz "Martin Moszkowicz") stated that a reboot of the series was in development. In the same month, it was announced that [James Wan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Wan "James Wan") would produce the reboot with a script by Greg Russo, with both later leaving the project. In December 2018, [Johannes Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Roberts "Johannes Roberts") was hired as writer and director. Roberts stated that his film will be separate from the previous films in the series, with the story being based on *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(1996_video_game) "Resident Evil (1996 video game)")* and *[Resident Evil 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_2 "Resident Evil 2")*. By October 2020, the principal cast was reported with [Kaya Scodelario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaya_Scodelario "Kaya Scodelario") cast as Claire Redfield, [Hannah John-Kamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_John-Kamen "Hannah John-Kamen") as Jill Valentine, [Robbie Amell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbie_Amell "Robbie Amell") as Chris Redfield, [Neal McDonough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neal_McDonough "Neal McDonough") as William Birkin, [Tom Hopper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hopper "Tom Hopper") as Albert Wesker, and [Avan Jogia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avan_Jogia "Avan Jogia") as Leon S. Kennedy. Other cast members, [Donal Logue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donal_Logue "Donal Logue"), [Chad Rook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Rook "Chad Rook"), [Lily Gao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_Gao "Lily Gao") and [Marina Mazepa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Mazepa "Marina Mazepa") were cast as Chief [Brian Irons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Resident_Evil_characters#Brian_Irons "List of Resident Evil characters"), [Richard Aiken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Resident_Evil_characters#Richard_Aiken "List of Resident Evil characters"), [Ada Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Wong "Ada Wong") and [Lisa Trevor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Trevor "Lisa Trevor"), respectively. Filming began on October 17, 2020, in [Sudbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Sudbury "Greater Sudbury"), [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario "Ontario"), Canada, with reshoots in May 2021. The film was released on November 24, 2021, by [Sony Pictures Releasing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Releasing "Sony Pictures Releasing") in the United States.

Claire Redfield uncovers secrets about the Umbrella Corporation's experiments and accidents and travels back to her hometown of Raccoon City to warn her brother Chris Redfield. Meanwhile, a zombie outbreak begins after residents are poisoned in the water supply. Chris, along with fellow STARS members Jill Valentine and Albert Wesker, travel to the Spencer Mansion to recover bravo team and find out what is happening in the town. Claire travels with rookie police officer Leon S. Kennedy to the Raccoon City Orphanage to flee the city before it is destroyed, where they encounter a Licker and Claire's old friend Lisa Trevor. Wesker betrays Jill and attempts to steal the virus produced by Dr. William Birkin, a scientist working for the Umbrella Corporation. On the verge of death, Birkin injects himself with the G-virus, saving his life and mutating him into the Tyrant. The survivors flee on an underground train with Birkin's daughter Sherry and destroy the tyrant with a rocket launcher. In a mid-credits scene, Albert Wesker is saved from death by Ada Wong and is given his trademark sunglasses.

### Second reboot

#### *Resident Evil* (2026)

In January 2025, it was reported that [Zach Cregger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Cregger "Zach Cregger") was writing and directing a new film reboot, co-produced by [Constantin Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantin_Film "Constantin Film") and [PlayStation Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_Productions "PlayStation Productions"). In March 2025, [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures "Sony Pictures") won a bidding war to secure the rights to the *Resident Evil* franchise, including its upcoming untitled project, with [Columbia Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Pictures "Columbia Pictures") serving as the film's new distributor. Sony has set a release date of September 18, 2026 for the project. In June 2025, it was reported that the project had registered to film in the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic "Czech Republic"). On August 11, 2025, [Austin Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Abrams "Austin Abrams") was confirmed to star in the film. Filming was scheduled to begin in Prague in November 2025. Cregger stated that he had never seen any of the previous *Resident Evil* films and that the upcoming movie will be more closely based on the video game series, particularly *[Resident Evil 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_2 "Resident Evil 2")*, *[Resident Evil 3: Nemesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_3:_Nemesis "Resident Evil 3: Nemesis")*, and *[Resident Evil 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_4 "Resident Evil 4")*. On September 17, 2025 [Paul Walter Hauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Walter_Hauser "Paul Walter Hauser") was cast in a role. The next month, [Zach Cherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Cherry "Zach Cherry"), [Kali Reis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kali_Reis "Kali Reis") and Johnno Wilson were cast in roles with Cherry as a scientist and Reis as an ex-military character that was originally written for a male actor.

The first six films, all written and produced by Anderson, received mixed or negative reviews from critics.[*[better source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability#Questionable_sources "Wikipedia:Verifiability")*] However, they have gained a cult following among a distinct subset of reviewers. Calum Marsh of the *[National Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Post "National Post")* admired the Anderson-directed entries, saying: "The first is classical, close-quarters horror... Part four, *Afterlife*, is a riot of ludicrous action and superhero brawn... but it's a mere [amuse bouche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amuse_bouche "Amuse bouche") next to the five-star spread of part five, *Retribution*—a glorious, practically experimental sci-fi action wonder." [Ignatiy Vishnevetsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignatiy_Vishnevetsky "Ignatiy Vishnevetsky") of *[The A.V. Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_A.V._Club "The A.V. Club")* called them "lean and lively and fun and totally unpretentious", and commended Anderson's style, "focused on organizing action and orientating the viewer".

## Novelizations

The [novelizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novelization "Novelization") for the first three *Resident Evil* films were written by [Keith R. A. DeCandido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_R._A._DeCandido "Keith R. A. DeCandido"). The novelization for the fifth film was written by [John Shirley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Shirley "John Shirley"), and the sixth by [Tim Waggoner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Waggoner "Tim Waggoner"). The fourth film did not receive a novelization.

The novel for the first film, titled *Resident Evil: Genesis*, was published over two years after that film's release, while the *Extinction* novel was released in late July 2007, two months before the film's release. There was also a Japanese novelization of the first film, unrelated to DeCandido's version, written by Osamu Makino. Makino also wrote two novels based on the game *[Resident Evil: The Umbrella Chronicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_The_Umbrella_Chronicles "Resident Evil: The Umbrella Chronicles")*.
