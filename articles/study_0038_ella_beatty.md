# Ella Beatty

**Ella Beatty** (born April 8, 2000) is an American actress. An alumna of the [Juilliard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juilliard_School "Juilliard School"), she is best known for her breakthrough role as the [titular character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzie_Borden "Lizzie Borden") in *[Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster:_The_Lizzie_Borden_Story "Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story")* (2026). She has also starred in the films *[If I Had Legs I'd Kick You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_I_Had_Legs_I%27d_Kick_You "If I Had Legs I'd Kick You")* (2025) and *[Act One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Act_One_(2026_film) "Act One (2026 film)")* (2026).

## Early life and education

Beatty was born on April 8, 2000, in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"), to actors [Annette Bening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annette_Bening "Annette Bening") and [Warren Beatty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Beatty "Warren Beatty"); she is the youngest of the pair's four children. In 2022, she graduated from the [Juilliard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juilliard_School "Juilliard School"), where she studied drama.

## Career

Beatty made her screen debut in 2024 with a recurring role as Kerry O'Shea, the surrogate daughter of [Truman Capote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truman_Capote "Truman Capote") in the [Ryan Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Murphy_(producer) "Ryan Murphy (producer)") anthology series *[Feud: Capote vs. The Swans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feud:_Capote_vs._The_Swans "Feud: Capote vs. The Swans")* for [FX on Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_on_Hulu "FX on Hulu"). That same year, she replaced [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning "Elle Fanning") as River Rayner in the [Branden Jacobs-Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branden_Jacobs-Jenkins "Branden Jacobs-Jenkins") play *[Appropriate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appropriate_(play) "Appropriate (play)")*, in her [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_theatre "Broadway theatre") debut. The following year she returned to theater playing a flirtatious maid, Regina Engstrand in the [Lincoln Center Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Center_Theater "Lincoln Center Theater") production of [Henrik Ibsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrik_Ibsen "Henrik Ibsen")'s *[Ghosts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts_(play) "Ghosts (play)")* (2025) at [Mitzi E. Newhouse Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitzi_E._Newhouse_Theater "Mitzi E. Newhouse Theater") [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway").

In 2025, Beatty made her film debut in the [A24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A24 "A24") drama film *[If I Had Legs I'd Kick You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_I_Had_Legs_I%27d_Kick_You "If I Had Legs I'd Kick You")*, directed by [Mary Bronstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Bronstein "Mary Bronstein"), which had its premiere at the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival"). The following year she played the lead role of [Lizzie Borden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzie_Borden "Lizzie Borden") in [Ryan Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Murphy_(producer) "Ryan Murphy (producer)")'s *[Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster:_The_Lizzie_Borden_Story "Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story")*.
