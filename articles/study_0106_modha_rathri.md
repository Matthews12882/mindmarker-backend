# Modha Rathri

***Modha Rathri*** (transl. First Night) is a 2026 Indian [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil-language "Tamil-language")-language [comedy drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_drama "Comedy drama") film written and directed by Raja Karuppasamy in his directorial debut and produced by [Mythri Movie Makers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mythri_Movie_Makers "Mythri Movie Makers"). The film stars [Rishikanth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishikanth "Rishikanth") (in his debut as lead actor), [Anishma Anilkumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anishma_Anilkumar "Anishma Anilkumar"), [Shelly Kishore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelly_Kishore "Shelly Kishore"), [Chetan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chetan_(actor) "Chetan (actor)") and [Bagavathi Perumal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagavathi_Perumal "Bagavathi Perumal") in lead roles.

The film was officially announced in May 2026 under the tentative title *MythriTamil03*, denoting Mythri's third Tamil production and the official title was announced in same month.The film has music and background score by Bharath Sankar, cinematography handled by Surendran Paranjothi and editing by Ashok Arjunan.

*Modha Rathri* was released in theatres on 21 August 2026 and received critical acclaim praising the cast performances, humour and screenplay. The film was a sleeper hit at the box office.

## Plot

In the morning of the wedding ceremony of Maruthanayagam and Muthuselvi, Maruthu's family eagerly awaits his arrival from Dubai. Muthu, on the other hand, is anxious after her boyfriend Nithish slept in and missed his chance to pick her up. Maruthu, is unaware of the wedding arrangements and refuses to cooperate to the wedding. But his brother-in-law convinces him to get married.

After the wedding ceremony, Muthu plans to elope after her family leaves and before the Modha rathri (first night) ceremony starts. However, as a change of plans, her family stays back and she nervously waits for a chance to escape. Maruthu catches her trying to escape and attempts to inform everyone. But he gives in and lets her go after she reveals her only way out is taking her own life, if he reveals the truth. He joins her later, unwilling to let her go in an unknown town at night.

Her boyfriend struggles to come to the town, having spent all his money. After realising, he is not ready for the responsibility, he asks Muthu to stay with her husband and leaves. Shaken and hopeless, Muthu wanders around until a cynical local temple priest and Maruthu find her in a hut.

After the priest unknowingly reveals Maruthu's painful past, He fully vents to them. Maruthu used to date Nirmala about 5 years ago, and their families were vehemently against it due to caste differences. She eventually took her own life. He leaves to Dubai, away from his painful past, but confides in them that his life is lonely. He says he wishes for a partner, with whom he can discuss life affairs and enjoy life's little pleasures.

The priest takes them back to their house. Meanwhile, their families had figured out the couple's past and create a ruckus. Muthu's chauvinistic uncle also throws a tantrum to take Muthu away after being disrespected by Maruthu's brother-in-law. Amidst the chaos, Muthu attains her breaking point, screams at her mother with frustration and walks away. Maruthu follows her, and he attentively listens to Muthu's vent. They finally have a hearty laugh and the film ends without a defining note to their future.

## Production

### Development

*Modha Rathri* was announced in May 2026 as the third Tamil-language production of [Mythri Movie Makers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mythri_Movie_Makers "Mythri Movie Makers"). The film marks the directorial debut of Raja Karuppasamy, who also wrote the screenplay. It was produced by Naveen Yerneni and Y. Ravi Shankar under the Mythri Movie Makers banner. The film was described as a [rural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural "Rural") [comedy drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_drama "Comedy drama") set around an unusual wedding, with unexpected incidents and lies unfolding over the course of a single night.

The film's first-look poster was unveiled by filmmaker [Lokesh Kanagaraj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokesh_Kanagaraj "Lokesh Kanagaraj") on 1 May 2026. The announcement revealed that the story takes place during a single night surrounding a wedding and revolves around the events that occur when several characters become caught up in the situation.

### Casting

[Rishikanth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishikanth "Rishikanth") and [Anishma Anilkumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anishma_Anilkumar "Anishma Anilkumar") were cast in the lead roles. The supporting cast includes [Chetan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chetan_(actor) "Chetan (actor)"), [A. Venkatesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Venkatesh "A. Venkatesh"), [Bagavathi Perumal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagavathi_Perumal "Bagavathi Perumal"), Abdool Lee, [Shelly Kishore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelly_Kishore "Shelly Kishore"), Sangeetha Balan, Banupriya, Sumithra Devi L, Varshini Karmegham, Karthikeyan DK, Velan, Kowshik Kabilan and Raja Karuppusamy.

Anishma Anilkumar was cast as Muthuselvi, while Rishikanth plays the male lead. The film marked Anishma's next major Tamil project following her appearances in *[Sirai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirai_(2025_film) "Sirai (2025 film)")* and *[Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_(2026_film) "Youth (2026 film)")*.

### Filming

Principal photography commenced following the film's announcement in May 2026. The shooting was completed by 1 July 2026, with the makers announcing the completion of filming after wrapping the production.

Surendran Paranjothi handled the cinematography, while Ashok Arjunan served as the editor. Bharath Sankar composed the music, with A. Balumahendra serving as the production designer and [Poornima Ramaswamy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poornima_Ramaswamy "Poornima Ramaswamy") as the costume designer.

## Release

### Theatrical

*Modha Rathri* was released in theatres on 21 August 2026, coinciding [Milad un-Nabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawlid "Mawlid") weekend. The film was distributed by [AGS Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AGS_Entertainment "AGS Entertainment") in [Tamil Nadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Nadu "Tamil Nadu").

### Home media

The post-theatrical digital streaming rights were acquired by [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix").The film started streaming on Netlfix from 18 September 2026 in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi languages.

## Reception

### Critical response

*Modha Rathri* received critical acclaim from critics.

Manivasagan Namasivayam of *[DT Next](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DT_Next "DT Next")* gave 4/5 stars and wrote, "Despite the minor flaws, Modha Rathiri succeeds as an engaging wedding comedy and a family entertainer that blends laughter with observations on marriage, relationships, and society. Almost every character gets a moment to contribute, making the film a lively laughter riot and an impressive debut for Raja Karuppusamy".

Akshay Kumar of *[The New Indian Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Indian_Express "The New Indian Express")* and *[Cinema Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_Express "Cinema Express")* gave 3.5/5 stars and wrote, "Modha Rathri, replete with love, laughs and entertaining characters, gives courtship a chance in a hurried arranged marriage, finding a tiny room for two people to get smitten".A critic from *Pentagan Media* gave 3.5/5 stars and wrote, "Barath Shankar’s upbeat background score elevates the quality of the comedy scenes that follow spontaneously through the length and breadth of the film.".

Abhinav Subramanian of *[The Times of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_of_India "The Times of India")* gave 2.5/5 stars and wrote, "Rishikanth is fine, an unhurried presence in a film full of flapping arms. Anishma is alright in a part that asks for sporadic outbursts and long glum stretches. Bagavathi Perumal gets the loudest assignment and knows exactly how irritating to make it; most of the laughs come at his expense. The rest are sufficient. Shot across a handful of locations, the film looks functional more than flattering". [Ananda Vikatan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ananda_Vikatan "Ananda Vikatan") rated the film 43 out of 100.
