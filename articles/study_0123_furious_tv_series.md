# Furious (TV series)

***Furious*** is an American [crime drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_drama "Crime drama") television series created by [Elizabeth Meriwether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Meriwether "Elizabeth Meriwether") and loosely inspired by the 1987 film *[Black Widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Widow_(1987_film) "Black Widow (1987 film)")* by [Ronald Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Bass "Ronald Bass"). Produced by [20th Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Television "20th Television") and [Searchlight Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searchlight_Television "Searchlight Television"), the series stars [Emmy Rossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Rossum "Emmy Rossum") as [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation "Federal Bureau of Investigation") agent Alice Black, alongside [Lola Petticrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Petticrew "Lola Petticrew") as a calculating [serial killer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serial_killer "Serial killer") Catherine Grace. The series also stars [Quincy Tyler Bernstine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Tyler_Bernstine "Quincy Tyler Bernstine") and [Scoot McNairy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scoot_McNairy "Scoot McNairy"). It premiered on [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") on July 27, 2026, and was renewed for a second season in August 2026.

The show was a commercial success and received widespread critical acclaim, with particular praise for Rossum and Petticrew's performances.

## Premise

*Furious* follows [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation "Federal Bureau of Investigation") agent Alice Black ([Emmy Rossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Rossum "Emmy Rossum")) as she investigates Catherine Grace ([Lola Petticrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Petticrew "Lola Petticrew")), a calculating female [serial killer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serial_killer "Serial killer") targeting powerful men connected to a web of abuse. Both Alice and Catherine walk their own paths toward justice, and as their lives start to intertwine, the line between right and wrong begins to blur.

## Production

### Development

In March 2025, [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") ordered a series from [Elizabeth Meriwether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Meriwether "Elizabeth Meriwether") starring [Emmy Rossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Rossum "Emmy Rossum") loosely inspired by the 1987 film *[Black Widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Widow_(1987_film) "Black Widow (1987 film)")*. Meriwether executive produces alongside Rossum, [Brian Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Kirk "Brian Kirk") who also directed the first two episodes, [Sam Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Hoffman "Sam Hoffman"), [Matt Olmstead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Olmstead "Matt Olmstead"), and [Ronald Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Bass "Ronald Bass") who wrote the 1987 film. In October 2025, it was announced that the series was officially given the title *Furious*.

In August 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

### Casting

In June 2025, [Lola Petticrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Petticrew "Lola Petticrew") and [Scoot McNairy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scoot_McNairy "Scoot McNairy") joined the series as series regulars. In July 2025, [Quincy Tyler Bernstine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Tyler_Bernstine "Quincy Tyler Bernstine") was cast as a series regular. [Jake Lacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Lacy "Jake Lacy") joined the cast in a recurring role as Marshall in October 2025.

## Release

*Furious* premiered on [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") on July 27, 2026, with the first three episodes, followed by one new episode weekly through August 31. Internationally, the series was made available for streaming on [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%2B "Disney+").

## Reception

### Viewership

[Nielsen Media Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_Media_Research "Nielsen Media Research"), which records streaming viewership on U.S. television screens, reported that *Furious* recorded 372 million minutes of watch time from July 27 to August 2, ranking as the tenth most-streamed original series that week. [Luminate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminate_(company) "Luminate (company)"), which measures streaming performance in the United States using viewership data, audience engagement metrics, and content reach across multiple platforms, announced that the series premiered with three episodes and subsequently ranked No. 5 in Luminate's weekly rankings during its first full week of release, recording 2.43 million U.S. views. *Furious* was reported to be the most-watched series on Disney+ and Hulu worldwide in August and the second most-watched new drama on Hulu in the United States year to date, with 4 billion cross-media impressions. Approximately five weeks after its debut, *Furious* had accumulated 43 million hours of viewing time, equivalent to an estimated 6.2 million views based on the series' runtime.

### Critical response

On [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), the first season holds an approval rating of 99% based on 68 reviews, with an average of rated reviews of 8.5/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "A tense thrill ride spearheaded by a blazing [Emmy Rossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Rossum "Emmy Rossum"), this striking crime drama serves its stars and audiences *Furious* justice." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 81 out of 100 based on 22 critic reviews, indicating universal acclaim.
