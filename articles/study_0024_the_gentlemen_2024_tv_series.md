# The Gentlemen (2024 TV series)

***The Gentlemen*** is a [black comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_comedy "Black comedy") and [crime drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_drama "Crime drama") television series created by [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie") for [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). It is a [spin-off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin-off_(media) "Spin-off (media)") of Ritchie's [2019 film of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2019_film) "The Gentlemen (2019 film)"), taking place in its [fictional universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fictional_universe "Fictional universe") and sharing [thematic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theme_(narrative) "Theme (narrative)") connections but following a [standalone story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standalone_sequel "Standalone sequel") with a new cast, characters and settings. The series stars [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James "Theo James") in the lead role and premiered on March 7, 2024. In August 2024, the series was renewed for a second season, which premiered on September 3, 2026. In August 2026, ahead of the second season premiere, the series was renewed for a third season.

## Cast and characters

### Main

* [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James "Theo James") as Edward "Eddie" Horniman, the 13th [Duke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke "Duke") of Halstead and a former British Army captain who served as a [United Nations peacekeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_peacekeeping "United Nations peacekeeping") officer as well as on combat deployments to [Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Afghanistan_(2001%E2%80%932021) "War in Afghanistan (2001–2021)").
* [Kaya Scodelario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaya_Scodelario "Kaya Scodelario") as Susan "Susie" Glass, the daughter of and representative for Bobby Glass' [criminal syndicate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_firms_(organised_crime) "British firms (organised crime)") while he is in prison. She maintains a large-scale [cannabis grow-op](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_cultivation "Cannabis cultivation") on the Halstead Manor and serves as Edward's advisor in professional criminal endeavors. Her [front occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_organization#Organized_crime "Front organization") is masquerading as an antiques dealer.
* [Daniel Ings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ings "Daniel Ings") as Lord Frederick "Freddy" Horniman, Eddie's unreliable, [cocaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocaine "Cocaine")-addicted older brother who is indebted to a [Scouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool "Liverpool") crime family.
* [Joely Richardson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joely_Richardson "Joely Richardson") as Sabrina Horniman, the [Dowager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dowager "Dowager") Duchess of Halstead and mother of Freddy, Eddie, and Charly. * [Giancarlo Esposito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giancarlo_Esposito "Giancarlo Esposito") as Stanley Johnston, a wine-loving American billionaire who seeks to buy Halstead Manor.

## Production

[Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie") entered development of a television series for him to write and direct inspired by his film *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2019_film) "The Gentlemen (2019 film)")* with Moonage Pictures and [Miramax Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miramax_Television "Miramax Television") in October 2020. Ritchie was also executive producing alongside the producers of the original film. Matthew Read helped Richie write the pilot script, also coming on board to executive produce at Moonage with Will Gould, and [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") ultimately ordered the series.

The cast, including [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James "Theo James"), [Kaya Scodelario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaya_Scodelario "Kaya Scodelario"), [Daniel Ings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ings "Daniel Ings"), [Joely Richardson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joely_Richardson "Joely Richardson"), [Giancarlo Esposito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giancarlo_Esposito "Giancarlo Esposito"), [Peter Serafinowicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Serafinowicz "Peter Serafinowicz"), and [Vinnie Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinnie_Jones "Vinnie Jones"), was announced the week before filming was set to begin, and [Alexis Rodney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexis_Rodney "Alexis Rodney") was added in the first week of filming. In June 2023, [Max Beesley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Beesley "Max Beesley") was added to the cast, and Hugh Warren was revealed as the series producer. [Chanel Cresswell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanel_Cresswell "Chanel Cresswell") joined the cast in a recurring role during production in December. [Ray Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Winstone "Ray Winstone"), [Jasmine Blackborow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasmine_Blackborow "Jasmine Blackborow"), and Michael Vu were also revealed to be part of the cast through a teaser in January 2024.

Production took place in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London") and [Badminton House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badminton_House "Badminton House"), running from November 2022 through June 2023. Filming took place at [Longcross Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longcross_Studios "Longcross Studios") and [West London Film Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_London_Film_Studios "West London Film Studios") with additional on-location shooting throughout the city, including at the [Princess Victoria pub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Victoria,_Shepherd%27s_Bush "Princess Victoria, Shepherd's Bush") and the [Tate Modern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_Modern "Tate Modern") as well as areas of [Hackney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackney,_London "Hackney, London") and [Islington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islington "Islington"). Stoke Poges in [Buckinghamshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckinghamshire "Buckinghamshire") is used for two locations with [Stoke Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke_Court "Stoke Court") the location of the prison where Bobby Glass is seen on the roof with his pigeons and Chucky's mansion is a property on West End Lane. The backdrop of [Printworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Printworks_(London) "Printworks (London)") London was also present during the first episode.

On August 14, 2024, the series was renewed for a second season. On August 24, 2026, the series was renewed for a third season.

## Release

The first season premiered on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") on March 7, 2024. The second season premiered on September 3, 2026.

## Reception

### Critical response

The review aggregator [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") reported a 75% approval rating with an average rating of 7.0/10, based on 74 critic reviews. The website's critics consensus reads, "Slick as a well-pressed suit and given watchable wattage by its cast, [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie")'s *The Gentlemen* doesn't seamlessly expand upon the original movie but has enough verve to be fun on its own terms." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") gave it a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean") score of 66 out of 100 based on 27 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".

Anita Singh of *[The Telegraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Telegraph "Daily Telegraph")* gave the series three stars out of five, describing it as "everything you want from a Guy Ritchie caper."
Lucy Mangan of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* rated it three out of five, and summarized it as "a lot of daft stories crammed together and spattered with blood for your entertainment", recommending it to viewers who are fans of Ritchie's films.
