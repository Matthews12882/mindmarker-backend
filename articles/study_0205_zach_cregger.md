# Zach Cregger

**Zachary Michael Cregger** (born March 1, 1981) is an American comedian, actor, and filmmaker. Cregger is a founding member of the comedy troupe *[The Whitest Kids U' Know](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Whitest_Kids_U%27_Know "The Whitest Kids U' Know")* and starred in the sitcoms *[Friends with Benefits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends_with_Benefits_(TV_series) "Friends with Benefits (TV series)")*, *[Guys with Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guys_with_Kids "Guys with Kids")*, and *[Wrecked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecked_(TV_series) "Wrecked (TV series)")*. He wrote and directed the horror films *[Barbarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbarian_(2022_film) "Barbarian (2022 film)")* (2022), *[Weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_(2025_film) "Weapons (2025 film)")* (2025), and [*Resident Evil*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2026_film) "Resident Evil (2026 film)") (2026), also producing the latter two.

## Early life and education

Zachary Michael Cregger was born on March 1, 1981, and raised in [Arlington, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Virginia "Arlington, Virginia"). He performed in various local comedy and music groups before moving to [Brooklyn, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn,_New_York "Brooklyn, New York") to attend the [School of Visual Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Visual_Arts "School of Visual Arts").

## Career

### Actor and comedian

Cregger is a founding member of the Whitest Kids U' Know comedy troupe, formed alongside [Trevor Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Moore_(comedian) "Trevor Moore (comedian)") and Sam Brown in 2000. While shooting an independent film, he met Darren Trumeter who subsequently joined the group. The Whitest Kids U' Know garnered success locally and online, leading to an invitation to the HBO [U.S. Comedy Arts Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Comedy_Arts_Festival "U.S. Comedy Arts Festival") in [Aspen, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspen,_Colorado "Aspen, Colorado") in 2006. The troupe won the award for Best [Sketch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sketch_comedy "Sketch comedy") Group and attracted the attention of several networks. Following the showcase, [Fuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuse_(TV_channel) "Fuse (TV channel)") ordered a sketch series from the group. *The Whitest Kids U' Know* aired its first season on the network in 2007 before moving to then-sister network [IFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFC_(American_TV_channel) "IFC (American TV channel)"). The series ran for five seasons before concluding in 2011.

Alongside Moore, he starred in and co-directed the films *[Miss March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_March "Miss March")* and *[The Civil War on Drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Civil_War_on_Drugs "The Civil War on Drugs")*. The latter received a limited theatrical release and aired as a recurring [serial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serial_(radio_and_television) "Serial (radio and television)") during the final season of *The Whitest Kids U' Know*. The troupe's second feature film, *Mars*, premiered at the [Tribeca Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribeca_Festival "Tribeca Festival") in [Lower Manhattan, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Manhattan "Lower Manhattan") on June 6, 2024. In 2008, Cregger made his first film appearance in Deb Hagan's directorial debut *[College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_(2008_film) "College (2008 film)")*. In 2011, he joined the cast of the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") sitcom *[Friends with Benefits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends_with_Benefits_(TV_series) "Friends with Benefits (TV series)")*. He then starred on the NBC sitcom *[Guys with Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guys_with_Kids "Guys with Kids")* from 2012 to 2013. From 2016 to 2018, he starred as Owen O'Connor on the [TBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TBS_(American_TV_channel) "TBS (American TV channel)") sitcom *[Wrecked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecked_(TV_series) "Wrecked (TV series)")*.

### Filmmaker

Cregger wrote and directed the 2022 horror film *[Barbarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbarian_(2022_film) "Barbarian (2022 film)")*, starring [Georgina Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Campbell "Georgina Campbell"), [Bill Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Skarsg%C3%A5rd "Bill Skarsgård"), and [Justin Long](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Long "Justin Long"). He wrote and directed the 2025 horror film *[Weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_(2025_film) "Weapons (2025 film)")*, receiving critical acclaim and several accolades including the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress") for [Amy Madigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Madigan "Amy Madigan"). He wrote and directed *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2026_film) "Resident Evil (2026 film)")* (2026), based on the video game franchise [of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil "Resident Evil") from [Capcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capcom "Capcom").

In December 2025, it was announced that he will serve as a producer on a feature film adaptation of *[Torso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torso_(Image_Comics) "Torso (Image Comics)")* for [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). At the [Warner Bros. Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Pictures "Warner Bros. Pictures") panel at [CinemaCon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_United "Cinema United") 2026, a new film directed by Cregger, the science fiction thriller *The Flood*, was announced. It is set for release on August 11, 2028, and will be co-produced with [Amblin Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amblin_Entertainment "Amblin Entertainment"). [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence "Jennifer Lawrence") was cast in the lead role, and it was the last film greenlit by [Pamela Abdy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Abdy "Pamela Abdy") and [Michael De Luca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_De_Luca "Michael De Luca") at Warner Bros. In the same panel, New Line announced a *Weapons* prequel titled *Gladys*, set for release on September 8, 2028, which is expected to be co-written with Zach Shields, but will not be directed by Cregger.

In 2026, Cregger was announced to be writing a film adaptation of [Trevor Henderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Henderson "Trevor Henderson")'s internet phenomenon [Siren Head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siren_Head "Siren Head"), with [Brian Duffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Duffield "Brian Duffield") set to direct.

## Personal life

In 2013, Cregger met [Sara Paxton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Paxton "Sara Paxton") in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas") on the set of *[Love & Air Sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_%26_Air_Sex "Love & Air Sex")* while they portrayed an on-screen couple. They were engaged in 2018 and married in October 2019.
