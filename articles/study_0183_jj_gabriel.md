# JJ Gabriel

**Joseph Junior Andreou O'Cearuill** (born 6 October 2010), commonly known as **JJ Gabriel**, is an English [footballer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football") who plays as a [forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_(association_football) "Forward (association football)") for [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League "Premier League") club [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C "Manchester United F.C")'s youth teams.

## Early life

Gabriel was born to [Joe O'Cearuill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_O%27Cearuill "Joe O'Cearuill") and a Greek-Cypriot mother. He grew up in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London") and developed his skills at top academies including [Chelsea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C._Development_Squad_and_Academy "Chelsea F.C. Development Squad and Academy"), [Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy "Arsenal F.C. Under-21s and Academy") and [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy "West Ham United F.C. Under-21s and Academy")'s. By age nine, Gabriel gained widespread attention after a viral [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") video earned him the nickname "Kid Messi". In March 2025, he signed a long-term sponsorship deal with [Nike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nike,_Inc. "Nike, Inc.").

## Club career

On 5 April 2025, 14-year-old Gabriel became youngest-ever player to play for [Manchester United's under-18 team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy "Manchester United F.C. Under-21s and Academy"), scoring twice on his debut in the [U18 Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U18_Premier_League "U18 Premier League") against [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy "Leeds United F.C. Under-21s and Academy"). Prior to the [2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Manchester_United_F.C._season "2025–26 Manchester United F.C. season"), Gabriel was given a shirt number 95 and was permitted to train with the first team of [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C. "Manchester United F.C."). In October, the fifteen-year-old player took part in first team training. On 9 December 2025, he scored in the [FA Youth Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_FA_Youth_Cup "2025–26 FA Youth Cup") match against [Peterborough United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterborough_United "Peterborough United") at [Old Trafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trafford "Old Trafford"). On 12 May 2026, Gabriel was named the U18 Premier League Player of the Season.

On 1 August 2026, he made his first-team debut in a pre-season friendly against [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid "Atlético Madrid") at [Nationalarenan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalarenan "Nationalarenan") in Stockholm, Sweden, coming on as a substitute for [Bryan Mbeumo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Mbeumo "Bryan Mbeumo") in the 82nd minute. He became the youngest Manchester United player to appear in an unofficial match at 15 years old. On 10 September, Gabriel scored a [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat-trick#Association_football "Hat-trick") in a [UEFA Youth League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Youth_League "UEFA Youth League") match against [Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabah_FK_(Azerbaijan) "Sabah FK (Azerbaijan)") which ended 5–0, becoming the youngest goalscorer for Manchester United in the competition.

Gabriel's camp announced his intention to leave United on 10 September, after he was not included in the squad to face [Sabah FK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabah_FK_(Azerbaijan) "Sabah FK (Azerbaijan)") in the [UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League "UEFA Champions League") that day; United were unable to play Gabriel as they had not registered him on their UEFA A list of players.

## International career

Gabriel made his [England U15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-15_football_team "England national under-15 football team") debut in February 2025.

In August 2025, Gabriel turned down an England U16 international call-up for a pair of friendly matches against Italy. His father confirmed that he would instead remain with Manchester United's U18 side. The decision was made because staying with his club was considered more beneficial for his development.

On 1 November 2025, Gabriel made his England U16 debut in a 3–2 loss against Turkey U16, starting the game.

Gabriel made his [England U17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-17_football_team "England national under-17 football team") debut on 25 March 2026 during a [2027 UEFA European Under-17 Championship qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_UEFA_European_Under-17_Championship_qualification "2027 UEFA European Under-17 Championship qualification") match against [Faroe Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faroe_Islands_national_under-17_football_team "Faroe Islands national under-17 football team") at [St George's Park National Football Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George%27s_Park_National_Football_Centre "St George's Park National Football Centre").

## Style of play

Gabriel can best play as a centre-forward or a winger, and his best skills are technique, dribbling, and ball control. He also likes to do tricks.

## Personal life

His father [Joe O'Cearuill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_O%27Cearuill "Joe O'Cearuill") is a former professional footballer and played for the [Republic of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland_national_football_team "Republic of Ireland national football team"). Gabriel is eligible to play for England, Ireland, Cyprus, and Trinidad and Tobago. He was born and raised in England, but is eligible to play for other countries because of his Irish paternal grandmother, Cypriot mother, and Trinidadian grandfather. Gabriel is reported to have changed his name from his birth name for commercial purposes.
