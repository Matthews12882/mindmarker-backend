# 2026 US Open – Women's singles

[Elena Rybakina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elena_Rybakina "Elena Rybakina") defeated two-time defending champion [Aryna Sabalenka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryna_Sabalenka "Aryna Sabalenka") in the final, 6–4, 5–7, 6–2 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [2026 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_US_Open_(tennis) "2026 US Open (tennis)"). It was her third [major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis)#Tournaments "Grand Slam (tennis)") title (and second of the season, having also won the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2026 Australian Open – Women's singles")) and 14th [WTA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_Tour "WTA Tour") title overall. Rybakina was the sixth women's singles player (since 1988) to win both [hardcourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardcourt "Hardcourt") majors in the same year, with Sabalenka and Rybakina also being the first pair of women's singles players to contest both hardcourt major finals in the same year since [Steffi Graf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steffi_Graf "Steffi Graf") and [Arantxa Sánchez Vicario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arantxa_S%C3%A1nchez_Vicario "Arantxa Sánchez Vicario") in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_WTA_Tour "1994 WTA Tour"). This was also the first [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Open_(tennis) "US Open (tennis)") final contested by the top two seeds since [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2013 US Open – Women's singles").

Rybakina attained the [world No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WTA_number_1_ranked_singles_tennis_players "List of WTA number 1 ranked singles tennis players") singles ranking for the first time by reaching the semifinals, becoming the 30th player to attain the top ranking and the first Kazakhstani player to achieve a world No. 1 ranking in any discipline. Sabalenka, [Jessica Pegula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Pegula "Jessica Pegula"), and [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff "Coco Gauff") were also in contention for the top ranking at the beginning of the tournament.

Aged 46, [Venus Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_Williams "Venus Williams") was the oldest woman to compete in the US Open singles main draw since [Renée Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9e_Richards "Renée Richards") in [1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "1981 US Open – Women's singles"), after receiving a wildcard into the main draw. Starting at 12:13 AM, the first-round match between Williams and [Sofia Kenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kenin "Sofia Kenin") set the record for the latest start in tournament history, surpassing the previous record (12:08 AM) set by the third-round match between Sabalenka and [Ekaterina Alexandrova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Alexandrova "Ekaterina Alexandrova") in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2024 US Open – Women's singles"). [Zheng Qinwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Qinwen "Zheng Qinwen") was the first qualifier to reach the quarterfinals since [Emma Raducanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Raducanu "Emma Raducanu") in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2021 US Open – Women's singles").

All of the top four seeds reached the semifinals for the first time at the US Open since [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "1975 US Open – Women's singles") and at any major since the [2009 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2009 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles").
It was also the first time at the US Open since [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "1998 US Open – Women's singles") that the top six seeds reached the quarterfinals.
26 out of the 32 seeds reached the third round, the joint-highest number of seeds at a major since the introduction of the 32-seed system in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2001 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles"), equaling the [2007 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2007 French Open – Women's singles").
