# Jenna Dewan

**Jenna Lee Dewan** ([/dəˈwɑːn/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English"); born December 3, 1980) is an American actress and dancer. She started her career as a [backup dancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup_dancer "Backup dancer") for [Janet Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Jackson "Janet Jackson"), and later worked with artists including [Christina Aguilera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Aguilera "Christina Aguilera"), [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_(singer) "Pink (singer)"), and [Missy Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missy_Elliott "Missy Elliott"). She is known for her role as Nora Clark in the 2006 film *[Step Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_Up_(film) "Step Up (film)")*. She has also starred on the short-lived [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") series *[The Playboy Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Playboy_Club "The Playboy Club")* (2011) and had a recurring role on the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") series *[American Horror Story: Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Asylum "American Horror Story: Asylum")* (2012). She portrayed [Freya Beauchamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freya_Beauchamp "Freya Beauchamp") on the [Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network) "Lifetime (TV network)") series *[Witches of East End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witches_of_East_End_(TV_series) "Witches of East End (TV series)")* (2013–14), [Lucy Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Lane "Lucy Lane") in [The CW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW "The CW") series *[Supergirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergirl_(TV_series) "Supergirl (TV series)")* (2015–16) and *[Superman & Lois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_%26_Lois "Superman & Lois")* (2022–23), and Joanna in *[Soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundtrack_(TV_series) "Soundtrack (TV series)")* (2019) on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). Dewan has hosted the reality television shows *[World of Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Dance_(TV_series) "World of Dance (TV series)")* (2017–18) and *[Flirty Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flirty_Dancing "Flirty Dancing")* (2019) and served as a judge on *[Come Dance with Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_Dance_with_Me_(TV_series) "Come Dance with Me (TV series)")* (2022). Since 2021, she has appeared in a supporting role as Bailey Nune on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company")'s *[The Rookie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rookie "The Rookie")*.

## Early life

Dewan was born December 3, 1980 in [Hartford, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_Connecticut "Hartford, Connecticut"), the daughter of Nancy Smith (née Bursch) and Darryll Dewan, who was a [running back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Running_back "Running back") on the 1972 [Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fighting_Irish "Fighting Irish") football team. Her father is of Syrian-Lebanese and Polish descent and her mother is of Cornish, German and English ancestry.

As a child, Dewan moved frequently; she mentioned in an interview that she lived in seven cities before reaching the seventh grade. While attending high school at [Notre Dame Preparatory School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre_Dame_Preparatory_School_(Towson,_Maryland) "Notre Dame Preparatory School (Towson, Maryland)") in [Towson, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towson,_Maryland "Towson, Maryland"), Dewan was a varsity cheerleader. She transferred to [Grapevine High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapevine_High_School "Grapevine High School") in [Grapevine, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapevine,_Texas "Grapevine, Texas"). She was also a varsity [cheerleader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheerleader "Cheerleader") there and was voted [prom queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prom_queen "Prom queen") during her senior year. She attended the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California "University of Southern California") and was a member of the California Gamma chapter of [Pi Beta Phi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_Beta_Phi "Pi Beta Phi").

## Career

### Dancing

Dewan made her first appearance in [Janet Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Jackson "Janet Jackson")'s "[Doesn't Really Matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doesn%27t_Really_Matter "Doesn't Really Matter")" video in 2000 and later in "[All for You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_for_You_(Janet_Jackson_song) "All for You (Janet Jackson song)")" and the [All for You Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_for_You_Tour "All for You Tour") in 2001. She credits working with Jackson for aiding her career, allowing her to later work with many other artists and giving her the experience necessary to co-star in the dance film *[Step Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_Up_(film) "Step Up (film)")*. In 2016, Dewan modeled as the face of dancewear manufacturer [Danskin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iconix_Brand_Group "Iconix Brand Group") in a campaign and photo shoot that captured and featured her athleticism as a dancer.

### Acting

In 2006, Dewan co-starred with [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum "Channing Tatum") in *[Step Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_Up_(film) "Step Up (film)")*, as well as starring in *[Take the Lead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_the_Lead "Take the Lead")* that same year.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

In August 2008, Dewan starred in the [Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network) "Lifetime (TV network)") made-for-television film *[Fab Five: The Texas Cheerleader Scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_Five:_The_Texas_Cheerleader_Scandal "Fab Five: The Texas Cheerleader Scandal")* which follows five rule-breaking teens. She portrayed the role of disciplinarian Coach Emma Carr. In November 2009, she appeared in the straight-to-DVD comedy *[American Virgin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Virgin_(2009_film) "American Virgin (2009 film)")* alongside [Rob Schneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Schneider "Rob Schneider").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

In March 2011, Dewan was cast in the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") pilot of *[The Playboy Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Playboy_Club "The Playboy Club")*. In May 2011, NBC picked up the series for a full season. She portrayed the role of Bunny Janie, a provocative Playboy bunny who works at the first Playboy Club in Chicago in 1961. The series premiered on September 19 to 5.2 million viewers with mixed reviews from critics. Due to low ratings, NBC cancelled the series after just three episodes in October 2011.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

In November 2011, Dewan starred in the film *The Jerk Theory*. This was first released in Germany in 2009. It was later released in the United States in November 2011.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

Dewan appeared in the romantic-comedy film *[10 Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Years_(2011_film) "10 Years (2011 film)")*, alongside her then-husband Channing Tatum, who also co-produced. The film was released on September 14, 2012. That same year, she appeared as Teresa Morrison in *[American Horror Story: Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Asylum "American Horror Story: Asylum")*, the second season of the horror television series.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

In 2013, Dewan began starring in the [Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network) "Lifetime (TV network)") series *[Witches of East End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witches_of_East_End "Witches of East End")* as [Freya Beauchamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freya_Beauchamp "Freya Beauchamp"), one of the lead characters. In late 2015, she began a recurring role in the CBS/CW series *[Supergirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergirl_(TV_series) "Supergirl (TV series)")* as [Lucy Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Lane "Lucy Lane"), and reprised the role in its spinoff *[Superman & Lois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_%26_Lois "Superman & Lois").* She also had a recurring role in FOX's [*The Resident*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Resident_(TV_series) "The Resident (TV series)"). Dewan's 2019 projects include *[The Wedding Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Year "The Wedding Year")*, *[Berlin, I Love You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin,_I_Love_You "Berlin, I Love You")* and *[Soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundtrack_(TV_series) "Soundtrack (TV series)")*.

She joined the cast of [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company")'s *[The Rookie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rookie "The Rookie")* as Bailey in its third season, and was promoted to series regular in its fourth season.

In 2018, Dewan makes an appearance on the television series *[Seatbelt Psychic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seatbelt_Psychic "Seatbelt Psychic")* and receives a spooky reading from the famous medium [Thomas John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_John_(medium) "Thomas John (medium)").

In 2022, Dewan starred and executive produced in the Lifetime film *Let's Get Physical* as part of the "Ripped from the Headlines" feature film. She portrays Sadie Smith who is loosely based on Zumba instructor Alexis Wright with the film inspired by Alexis' and her side prostitution gig.

### 33andOut Productions

Along with friends [Reid Carolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reid_Carolin "Reid Carolin"), Adam Martingano, Brett Rodriguez and [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum "Channing Tatum"), Dewan started a production company called 33andOut Productions. Their first production is a documentary called *Earth Made of Glass* that follows Rwandan President [Paul Kagame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Kagame "Paul Kagame") and genocide survivor Jean-Pierre Sagahutu. It premiered at the 2010 [Tribeca Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival "Tribeca Film Festival").

## Personal life

In 2006, Dewan started dating actor and dancer [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum "Channing Tatum") after meeting on the set of their film *[Step Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_Up_(film) "Step Up (film)")*. They married on July 11, 2009, in [Malibu, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu,_California "Malibu, California"). They have one daughter, named Everly Elizabeth Maiselle Tatum born on May 31, 2013. On April 2, 2018, the couple announced that they were separating. Six months later, Dewan filed for divorce from Tatum. Their divorce was finalized in November 2019.

In October 2018, it was confirmed that Dewan was in a relationship with actor [Steve Kazee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Kazee "Steve Kazee"). In February 2020, she and Kazee announced their engagement. Their first child, a son named Callum Michael Rebel Kazee was born on March 6, 2020. On January 17, 2024 it was confirmed via Dewan's Instagram that she and Kazee were expecting their second child. Their daughter, Rhiannon Lee Kathryn Kazee, was born on June 14, 2024.
