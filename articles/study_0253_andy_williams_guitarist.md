# Andy Williams (guitarist)

**Andrew Williams** (December 12, 1977 – September 5, 2026) was an American musician and [professional wrestler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestler "Professional wrestler"). He was best known as the [rhythm guitarist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_guitarist "Rhythm guitarist") of the [metalcore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalcore "Metalcore") band [Every Time I Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Every_Time_I_Die "Every Time I Die").

As a professional wrestler, he was best known for his time in [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling") (AEW) under the [ring name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_name "Ring name") **The Butcher**, where he worked as one-half of a [tag team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Butcher_and_the_Blade "The Butcher and the Blade") with [The Blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blade_(wrestler) "The Blade (wrestler)").

## Musical career

Williams had aspired to be a professional wrestler and trained at Renegade Wrestling Association in [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario "Ontario"), Canada for six months, until he sustained a knee injury. During the layoff, he learned to play the guitar. The first hardcore bands he played in were Sirhan and Soiled who have both released demos and appear on several compilations.

In 1998, Williams formed [Every Time I Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Every_Time_I_Die "Every Time I Die") alongside lead guitarist Jordan Buckley and drummer Michael Novak. He appeared on every release that the band did, and had not missed a live show, until January 2020, when he wrestled [Diamond Dallas Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Dallas_Page "Diamond Dallas Page") and [Dustin Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin_Rhodes "Dustin Rhodes") at [AEW Bash at the Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Bash_at_the_Beach "AEW Bash at the Beach"). From January to March, Every Time I Die had written and recorded their ninth album, and had been expected to tour the album in June, supporting [Parkway Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkway_Drive "Parkway Drive") and [Hatebreed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatebreed "Hatebreed"), but this was delayed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"). Since he could not tour with the band, he was able to continue working for [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling") (AEW), as they are based in [Jacksonville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville,_Florida "Jacksonville, Florida"), and [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida "Florida") deemed wrestling as essential business critical to the state's economy. On January 17, 2022, Every Time I Die announced they had broken up.

In 2023, Williams formed Atomic Rule, alongside bassist Travis Bennington, and the band released a three-song demo in September 2025, as well as a three-song [extended play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") in December 2025. Prior to his death, Williams spoke of having finished writing and recording new music.

## Professional wrestling career

### Training (2010–2015)

In the mid-2010s, Williams resumed his training with [Josh Barnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Barnett "Josh Barnett"), initially as a workout session. He continued working with Barnett, but only sporadically, as he did not live in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California "California"). He began attending Grapplers Anonymous, based in [Lackawanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lackawanna,_New_York "Lackawanna, New York"), and received further training from long-time friend and professional wrestler [Jesse Guilmette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blade_(wrestler) "The Blade (wrestler)").

### Independent circuit (2015–2026)

During the summer of 2015, he started working on the [independent circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_circuit "Independent circuit"), and made run-ins at Smash Wrestling in Toronto where he chokeslammed Tarik, and at [Progress Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progress_Wrestling "Progress Wrestling"), where he powerbombed [Jimmy Havoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Havoc "Jimmy Havoc") through a table. His first tag match was on an Interspecies Wrestling Halloween show, partnering Wes in a dark match, and defeating Los Dumbfucks. In February 2016, he returned to Smash Wrestling, and had another confrontation with Tarik, who laid him out in the ring. His first singles match was confirmed the following month after the show, but at Any Given Sunday 4, he was defeated by Tarik. During the match, he tore his [meniscus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meniscus_(anatomy) "Meniscus (anatomy)"), which he had surgery for, before going on tour with Every Time I Die ten days later.

In March 2017, Williams appeared at [Joey Janela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Janela "Joey Janela")'s [Spring Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Janela%27s_Spring_Break "Joey Janela's Spring Break"), successfully teaming with [Penelope Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penelope_Ford "Penelope Ford") in an intergender tag team match against Guilmette (under his Braxton Sutter ring name) and his wife [Allie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allie_(wrestler) "Allie (wrestler)"). Janela had suggested that Williams should wrestle either [Nikolai Volkoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Volkoff "Nikolai Volkoff") or [Glacier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glacier_(wrestler) "Glacier (wrestler)") at the event, but he declined, preferring the intergender matchup instead. In September, Williams and Guilmette began regularly teaming with each other, and in their first match at a Pro Wrestling Rampage event, they won the PWR Tag Team Championship, defeating the Upper Echelon (Colby Redd and [P. B. Smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._B._Smooth "P. B. Smooth")). However, this reign was short-lived, lasting 63 days. In their first defence of the title, Williams was absent and was replaced by JJ Rumham (as he was playing in [Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne "Newcastle upon Tyne") during the *[Low Teens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_Teens "Low Teens")* tour); the pairing of Guilmette and Rumham were defeated by the Homewreckers (Anthony Gaines and Jet Rebel). Known as [the Butcher and the Blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Butcher_and_the_Blade "The Butcher and the Blade"), Williams and Guilmette predominately wrestled in promotions across [the Northeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_United_States "Northeastern United States"), as well as in [Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa "Ottawa"), where Capital City Championship Combat (C4) is based.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

### All Elite Wrestling / Ring of Honor (2019–2026)

On November 27, 2019, Williams made his [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling") (AEW) debut as the Butcher, attacking [Cody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Rhodes "Cody Rhodes") alongside [the Blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blade_(wrestler) "The Blade (wrestler)") and [the Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allie_(wrestler) "Allie (wrestler)"). It was later revealed that the Butcher and the Blade had been hired by [MJF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_Jacob_Friedman "Maxwell Jacob Friedman"). The team subsequently feuded with [Jurassic Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_Express "Jurassic Express") before aligning with the [Lucha Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucha_Brothers "Lucha Brothers") and [Eddie Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Kingston "Eddie Kingston") in 2020. The alliance later dissolved, with the Butcher and the Blade siding with Kingston and feuding with [Death Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Triangle "Death Triangle").

Following [Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(2021) "Revolution (2021)") in 2021, the Butcher and the Blade ended their alliance with Kingston and joined [Matt Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Hardy "Matt Hardy"), [Private Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_Party_(professional_wrestling) "Private Party (professional wrestling)") and others in the Hardy Family Office. The Butcher won a battle royal for a [TNT Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_TNT_Championship "AEW TNT Championship") opportunity, but was defeated by [Darby Allin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darby_Allin "Darby Allin"). He later missed several months due to a thumb injury before returning at [All Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Out_(2021) "All Out (2021)"). The Butcher and the Blade subsequently became number-one contenders for the [AEW World Tag Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_World_Tag_Team_Championship "AEW World Tag Team Championship"), but lost to the Lucha Brothers. In November 2021, Williams suffered a torn biceps during a match against [Chaos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_(professional_wrestling) "Chaos (professional wrestling)"). Weeks later, [Every Time I Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Every_Time_I_Die "Every Time I Die") broke up, prompting Williams to pursue professional wrestling full-time.

Upon returning in January 2022, the Butcher became part of a storyline involving [Andrade El Idolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrade_El_Idolo "Andrade El Idolo"), who eventually became the group's leader after Hardy's departure. The Butcher and the Blade later feuded with [Darby Allin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darby_Allin "Darby Allin") and [the Hardys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hardys "The Hardys") before their alliance with Andrade ended at [Double or Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_or_Nothing_(2022) "Double or Nothing (2022)"). Later that year, they aligned with [Kip Sabian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kip_Sabian "Kip Sabian") and began a recurring feud with [Best Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Friends_(professional_wrestling) "Best Friends (professional wrestling)").

Throughout 2023, the Butcher remained involved in various battle royals and title opportunities, including a challenge for the [AEW International Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_International_Championship "AEW International Championship") against [Orange Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Cassidy "Orange Cassidy"), which he lost. Beginning in May 2023, Williams also began appearing regularly for [Ring of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Honor "Ring of Honor") (ROH), primarily in tag team and multi-man matches. In early 2024, the Blade was sidelined with a long-term injury, leading the Butcher to compete primarily in singles matches. He subsequently competed in singles matches against wrestlers including [Swerve Strickland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swerve_Strickland "Swerve Strickland"), [Dustin Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin_Rhodes "Dustin Rhodes") and [Claudio Castagnoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Castagnoli "Claudio Castagnoli"). In June 2026, it was reported that the Blade and the Butcher's AEW contracts had expired, ending their nearly seven-year tenure with the promotion.

## Personal life

On [Renee Paquette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renee_Paquette "Renee Paquette")'s podcast *The Sessions*, in 2022, Williams revealed he had [dyslexia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyslexia "Dyslexia").

## Death

On September 5, 2026, Williams collapsed following a match at an Enjoy Wrestling event in [Millvale, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millvale,_Pennsylvania "Millvale, Pennsylvania"). The remainder of the event was subsequently cancelled and he was rushed to [Allegheny General Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegheny_General_Hospital "Allegheny General Hospital"), where he was pronounced dead at the age of 48. On September 6, the Allegheny County Medical Examiner's Office confirmed Williams' death.

### Tributes and reactions

Following the announcement of his death, numerous wrestlers and musicians paid tribute to Williams on social media, including Williams' former [Every Time I Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Every_Time_I_Die "Every Time I Die") bandmates. Vocalist [Keith Buckley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Buckley "Keith Buckley") described Williams as someone who "...made people feel like they belonged". [My Chemical Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Chemical_Romance "My Chemical Romance") also dedicated their performance of "[Boy Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_Division_/_Tomorrow%27s_Money "Boy Division / Tomorrow's Money")" to Williams during their concert the following night.

On September 6, 2026, a day after his death, [Malakai Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakai_Black "Malakai Black") won the [Progress Men's World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progress_Men%27s_World_Championship "Progress Men's World Championship") and dedicated his victory to Williams. [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling"), the professional wrestling promotion where he worked from 2019 to 2026, honored him during their [Rebel Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Rebel_Heart_(2026) "AEW Rebel Heart (2026)") special episode of *[Dynamite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Dynamite "AEW Dynamite")* on September 9 and dedicated the September 12 edition of *[Collision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Collision "AEW Collision")* to his memory as [a memorial show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Andy_Williams_memorial_show "AEW Andy Williams memorial show"). On the September 8 tapings of [Ring of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Honor "Ring of Honor"), ROH and AEW owner [Tony Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Khan "Tony Khan") opened the show with a [ten-bell salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten-bell_salute "Ten-bell salute") to Williams. In the main event of the September 12 edition of *[AEW Collision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Collision "AEW Collision")*, fellow Buffalo native [Daniel Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Garcia_(wrestler) "Daniel Garcia (wrestler)") won a [battle royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_royal "Battle royal") to earn the right to choose any charity to donate $100,000 to in Williams' name. Garcia chose to donate the money to [MusiCares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MusiCares "MusiCares"). Ganon Jones Jr., who wrestled Williams during his last match, retired from professional wrestling after the incident.

A public memorial service was held on September 27, 2026, where it was announced that a memorial statue and bench would be built in Williams' hometown of [North Tonawanda, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Tonawanda,_New_York "North Tonawanda, New York") at Gratwick Park.
