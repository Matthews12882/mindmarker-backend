# Lady Gaga

**Stefani Joanne Angelina Germanotta** (born March 28, 1986), known professionally as **Lady Gaga**, is an American singer, songwriter, and actress. An influential figure in [popular music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_music "Popular music"), she is known for her image reinventions, flamboyant fashion, and versatility across the entertainment industry. With estimated sales of 124 million records, she is one of the [best-selling music artists of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_music_artists "List of best-selling music artists").

After signing with [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records "Interscope Records") in 2007, Gaga achieved global recognition with her debut album, *[The Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fame "The Fame")* (2008), and its reissue, *[The Fame Monster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fame_Monster "The Fame Monster")* (2009). The project yielded a string of successful singles, including "[Just Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_Dance_(song) "Just Dance (song)")", "[Poker Face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poker_Face_(song) "Poker Face (song)")", and "[Bad Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Romance "Bad Romance")", which made her one of the few artists with at least three Diamond-certified songs in the US. Her second studio album, *[Born This Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_(album) "Born This Way (album)")* (2011), explored [electronic rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_rock "Electronic rock") and [techno-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Techno-pop "Techno-pop") and sold [1.1 million copies in the US in its first week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_fastest-selling_albums "Lists of fastest-selling albums"). Its [title track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_(song) "Born This Way (song)") became the fastest-selling song on the [iTunes Store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITunes_Store "ITunes Store"), with over one million downloads in less than a week. Following her [electronic dance music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_dance_music "Electronic dance music")-influenced third album, *[Artpop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artpop "Artpop")* (2013), she pursued [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz") on the album *[Cheek to Cheek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheek_to_Cheek_(album) "Cheek to Cheek (album)")* (2014) with [Tony Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Bennett "Tony Bennett"), and delved into [soft rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_rock "Soft rock") on the album *[Joanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_(album) "Joanne (album)")* (2016).

Gaga also ventured into acting, gaining praise for her leading roles in the miniseries *[American Horror Story: Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Hotel "American Horror Story: Hotel")* (2015–2016) and the films *[A Star Is Born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Star_Is_Born_(2018_film) "A Star Is Born (2018 film)")* (2018) and *[House of Gucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Gucci "House of Gucci")* (2021). Her contributions to the [*A Star Is Born* soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Star_Is_Born_(2018_soundtrack) "A Star Is Born (2018 soundtrack)"), which spawned the chart-topping single "[Shallow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shallow_(Lady_Gaga_and_Bradley_Cooper_song) "Shallow (Lady Gaga and Bradley Cooper song)")", made her the first person to win an [Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards"), [BAFTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Film_Awards "British Academy Film Awards"), [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards"), and [Grammy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Awards "Grammy Awards") in one year. Gaga returned to her early sound with the pop-oriented albums *[Chromatica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatica "Chromatica")* (2020) and *[Mayhem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhem_(Lady_Gaga_album) "Mayhem (Lady Gaga album)")* (2025), which respectively included the number-one singles "[Rain on Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_on_Me_(Lady_Gaga_and_Ariana_Grande_song) "Rain on Me (Lady Gaga and Ariana Grande song)")" and "[Die with a Smile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_with_a_Smile "Die with a Smile")". She also continued to explore jazz with *[Love for Sale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_for_Sale_(Tony_Bennett_and_Lady_Gaga_album) "Love for Sale (Tony Bennett and Lady Gaga album)")* (2021), her second and final album with Bennett, and the soundtrack *[Harlequin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlequin_(Lady_Gaga_album) "Harlequin (Lady Gaga album)")* (2024).

Gaga has seven number-one albums on the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") and six number-one songs on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"). She is the first female artist with four singles that each sold [at least 10 million copies globally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_singles "List of best-selling singles") and holds the record for the [most-attended concert by a woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-attended_concerts "List of most-attended concerts"). The [highest-paid female musician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_list_of_the_world%27s_highest-paid_musicians#Female "Forbes list of the world's highest-paid musicians") in 2011, [she has received](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Lady_Gaga "List of awards and nominations received by Lady Gaga") 16 Grammy Awards, 22 [MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Awards "MTV Video Music Awards"), 2 Golden Globe Awards, a [Sports Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Emmy_Award "Sports Emmy Award"), and a recognition from the [Songwriters Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songwriters_Hall_of_Fame "Songwriters Hall of Fame"). Gaga's philanthropy and activism focus on mental health awareness and [LGBTQ rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_rights_by_country_or_territory "LGBTQ rights by country or territory"). Her business ventures include [vegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veganism "Veganism") cosmetics brand [Haus Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haus_Labs "Haus Labs") and the non-profit [Born This Way Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_Foundation "Born This Way Foundation"), which supports the wellness of young people.

## Life and career

### 1986–2004: Early life

Stefani Joanne Angelina Germanotta was born on March 28, 1986, at [Lenox Hill Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenox_Hill_Hospital "Lenox Hill Hospital") in the [Lenox Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenox_Hill "Lenox Hill") neighborhood of [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan "Manhattan") in New York City. Her parents are [Cynthia Louise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynthia_Germanotta "Cynthia Germanotta") ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names "Birth name") Bissett), a philanthropist and business executive, and Joseph Germanotta, an Internet entrepreneur; both have Italian ancestry. According to Gaga, her parents came from [lower-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_lower_class "American lower class") families and worked hard for everything. They raised Gaga and her younger sister, Natali, in an [upper-middle-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_middle_class_in_the_United_States "Upper middle class in the United States") Catholic family on the [Upper West Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_West_Side "Upper West Side"). From age 11, Gaga attended the [Convent of the Sacred Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convent_of_the_Sacred_Heart_(New_York_City) "Convent of the Sacred Heart (New York City)"), a private all-girls [Catholic school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_school "Catholic school") on the [Upper East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_East_Side "Upper East Side"). She has described her high-school self as "very dedicated, very studious, very disciplined" and somewhat insecure. She considered herself a [misfit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outcast_(person) "Outcast (person)") and was mocked for being provocative and eccentric.

At age four, Gaga began playing the piano when her mother insisted that she become "a cultured young woman". She took piano lessons and practiced throughout her childhood. The lessons taught her to learn music by ear, which she preferred over reading sheet music. Her parents encouraged her to pursue music and enrolled her in Creative Arts Camp. As a teenager, she played at [open mic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_mic "Open mic") nights. She played the lead roles of Adelaide in the play *[Guys and Dolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guys_and_Dolls "Guys and Dolls")* and Philia in the play *[A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to the Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Funny_Thing_Happened_on_the_Way_to_the_Forum "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to the Forum")* at [Regis High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regis_High_School_(New_York_City) "Regis High School (New York City)"). She also studied [method acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_acting "Method acting") at the [Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Strasberg_Theatre_and_Film_Institute "Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute") for 10 years. Her screen debut was a background appearance in the 2000 music video for the [AC/DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC/DC "AC/DC") song "[Stiff Upper Lip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stiff_Upper_Lip_(AC/DC_song) "Stiff Upper Lip (AC/DC song)")". Gaga unsuccessfully auditioned for New York theater productions; she appeared in a background role as a high-school student in a 2001 episode of *[The Sopranos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sopranos "The Sopranos")* titled "[The Telltale Moozadell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Telltale_Moozadell "The Telltale Moozadell")". Fan interest in that episode increased when a clip of Gaga's scene surfaced online in 2010.

In 2003, Gaga gained early admission to [Collaborative Arts Project 21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_Arts_Project_21 "Collaborative Arts Project 21"), a music school at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University "New York University")'s [Tisch School of the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tisch_School_of_the_Arts "Tisch School of the Arts"). There, she improved her songwriting skills by writing essays on art, religion, social issues, and politics, including a thesis on the pop artists [Spencer Tunick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Tunick "Spencer Tunick") and [Damien Hirst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Hirst "Damien Hirst"). In 2005, Gaga withdrew from school during the second semester of her second year to focus on her music career. That year, she also played a restaurant customer on [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV")'s *[Boiling Points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling_Points "Boiling Points")*, a prank reality television show.

In a 2014 interview, Gaga discussed being raped at age 19 by her producer, and undergoing emotional and physical therapy for the trauma. She later revealed that following the assault, her rapist [dropped her off pregnant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pregnancy_from_rape "Pregnancy from rape") near her parents' house because she was sick and vomiting. Gaga has [post-traumatic stress disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder "Post-traumatic stress disorder") which she attributes to the rape; she said the trauma has changed her as a person and will never leave her.

### 2005–2007: Career beginnings

In 2005, Gaga recorded two songs with rapper [Melle Mel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melle_Mel "Melle Mel") for an [audiobook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiobook "Audiobook") accompanying the Cricket Casey children's novel *The Portal in the Park*. She also formed a band called the SGBand with some friends from NYU. They played gigs around New York and became a fixture of the downtown [Lower East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_East_Side "Lower East Side") club scene. After the 2006 [Songwriters Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songwriters_Hall_of_Fame "Songwriters Hall of Fame") New Songwriters Showcase at [the Cutting Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cutting_Room "The Cutting Room") in June, talent scout Wendy Starland recommended Gaga to music producer [Rob Fusari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Fusari "Rob Fusari"). He collaborated with Gaga, helping her develop songs and compose new material. He said they began dating in May 2006, and claimed he was the first person to call her "Lady Gaga", which was derived from the [Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_(band) "Queen (band)") song "[Radio Ga Ga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Ga_Ga "Radio Ga Ga")". According to Fusari, the name was coined when he attempted to call her "Radio Ga Ga" in a text message, and his phone's [spell checker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spell_checker "Spell checker") autocorrected "Radio" to "Lady". Their relationship lasted until January 2007.

Fusari and Gaga established a company called Team Lovechild, LLC to promote her career. They recorded and produced [electropop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electropop "Electropop") tracks, sending them to music industry executives. Joshua Sarubin, the head of [Artists and Repertoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artists_and_repertoire "Artists and repertoire") at [Def Jam Recordings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Def_Jam_Recordings "Def Jam Recordings"), responded positively and, after approval from his boss [Antonio "L.A." Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Reid "L.A. Reid"), Gaga was signed to Def Jam in September 2006. She was dropped from the label three months later, a decision which Reid later said was the biggest mistake of his life. Following this, Gaga began performing at [neo-burlesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-burlesque "Neo-burlesque") shows, and said these represented freedom to her. During this time, she met the performance artist [Lady Starlight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Starlight "Lady Starlight"), who helped mold her onstage persona. The pair began performing at downtown club venues like the [Mercury Lounge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_Lounge "Mercury Lounge"), [the Bitter End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bitter_End "The Bitter End"), and the [Rockwood Music Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockwood_Music_Hall "Rockwood Music Hall"). Their live performance art piece, known as "Lady Gaga and the Starlight Revue" and billed as "The Ultimate Pop Burlesque Rockshow", was a tribute to 1970s variety acts. They also performed at the 2007 [Lollapalooza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza "Lollapalooza") music festival.

Having initially focused on [avant-garde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avant-garde "Avant-garde") electronic dance music, Gaga began to incorporate pop melodies and the [glam rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glam_rock "Glam rock") style of [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie "David Bowie") and Queen into her songs. While Gaga and Starlight were performing, Fusari continued to develop the songs he had created with her, sending them to the producer and record executive [Vincent Herbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Herbert "Vincent Herbert"). In November 2007, Herbert signed Gaga to his label Streamline Records, an [imprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_name "Trade name") of [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records "Interscope Records"), which had been established that month. Gaga later credited Herbert as the man who discovered her. Having served as an apprentice songwriter during an internship at [Famous Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Famous_Music "Famous Music"), Gaga struck a [music publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_publisher_(popular_music) "Music publisher (popular music)") deal with [Sony/ATV Music Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony/ATV_Music_Publishing "Sony/ATV Music Publishing"). As a result, she was hired to write songs for [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears"), [New Kids on the Block](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Kids_on_the_Block "New Kids on the Block"), [Fergie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fergie_(singer) "Fergie (singer)"), and [the Pussycat Dolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pussycat_Dolls "The Pussycat Dolls").

At Interscope, the singer [Akon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akon "Akon") was impressed with Gaga's singing ability when she sang a [reference vocal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scratch_vocal "Scratch vocal") for one of his tracks in studio. Akon convinced [Jimmy Iovine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Iovine "Jimmy Iovine"), chairman and CEO of [Interscope Geffen A&M Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Geffen_A%26M_Records "Interscope Geffen A&M Records") (a sister company of Def Jam), to form a joint deal by having Gaga also sign with his own label [KonLive Distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KonLive_Distribution "KonLive Distribution"), and described her as his "franchise player". According to Gaga, some radio stations found her early music too "racy", "dance-oriented", and "underground" for the mainstream market, to which she responded: "My name is Lady Gaga, I've been on the music scene for years, and I'm telling you, this is what's next."

### 2008–2010: Breakthrough with *The Fame* and *The Fame Monster*

By 2008, Gaga had relocated to Los Angeles to complete her debut album, *[The Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fame "The Fame")*, and to set up her own creative team called the Haus of Gaga, modeled on [Andy Warhol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Warhol "Andy Warhol")'s [The Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Factory "The Factory"). Gaga collaborated with the songwriter-producers [RedOne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RedOne "RedOne") and [Martin Kierszenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Kierszenbaum "Martin Kierszenbaum") on *The Fame*, which was released on August 19, 2008 by [Cherrytree Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherrytree_Records "Cherrytree Records"), an Interscope imprint established by Kierszenbaum. The album reached number one in Austria, Canada, Germany, Ireland, Switzerland and the UK, as well as the top five in Australia and the US. Its first two singles, "[Just Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_Dance_(song) "Just Dance (song)")" and "[Poker Face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poker_Face_(song) "Poker Face (song)")", reached number one in Australia, Canada, the UK and the US. "Poker Face" was also the [world's best-selling single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_singles#Best-selling_singles_by_year_worldwide "List of best-selling singles") of 2009, with 9.8 million copies sold, and spent a record 83 weeks on *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")* magazine's [Digital Songs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Songs "Digital Songs") chart. Three other singles, "[Eh, Eh (Nothing Else I Can Say)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eh,_Eh_(Nothing_Else_I_Can_Say) "Eh, Eh (Nothing Else I Can Say)")", "[LoveGame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoveGame "LoveGame")" and "[Paparazzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paparazzi_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Paparazzi (Lady Gaga song)")", were released from the album, with "Paparazzi" reaching number one in Germany. Remixed versions of the singles from *The Fame*, except "Eh, Eh (Nothing Else I Can Say)", were included on Gaga's [extended play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") (EP) *[Hitmixes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitmixes "Hitmixes")* in August 2009. At the [52nd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/52nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards "52nd Annual Grammy Awards"), *The Fame* and "Poker Face" won [Best Dance/Electronic Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Dance/Electronic_Album "Grammy Award for Best Dance/Electronic Album") and [Best Dance Recording](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Dance_Recording "Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording"), respectively. Gaga was the most awarded artist at the [2010 Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Brit_Awards "2010 Brit Awards"), winning in three categories.

Following her opening act on [the Pussycat Dolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pussycat_Dolls "The Pussycat Dolls")' 2009 [Doll Domination Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doll_Domination_Tour "Doll Domination Tour") in Europe and Oceania, Gaga headlined her worldwide [The Fame Ball Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fame_Ball_Tour "The Fame Ball Tour"), which ran from March to September 2009. While traveling the globe, she wrote eight songs for *[The Fame Monster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fame_Monster "The Fame Monster")*, a reissue of *The Fame*. The new songs were also released as a standalone EP on November 18, 2009. The first single, "[Bad Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Romance "Bad Romance")", was released one month earlier and was number one in Canada and the UK, and number two in Australia, New Zealand and the US. "[Telephone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_(song) "Telephone (song)")", with [Beyoncé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyonc%C3%A9 "Beyoncé"), followed as the second single from the EP and became Gaga's fourth UK number one. Its third single was "[Alejandro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_(song) "Alejandro (song)")", which reached number one in Finland; its music video was deemed [blasphemous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blasphemy "Blasphemy") by the [Catholic League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_League_(U.S.) "Catholic League (U.S.)"). Both "Telephone" and "Alejandro" reached the top five in the US. The video for "Bad Romance" became the [most watched video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-viewed_YouTube_videos "List of most-viewed YouTube videos") on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") in April 2010. That October, Gaga became the first person with more than one billion total YouTube views.

At the [2010 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2010 MTV Video Music Awards"), Gaga won eight awards from 13 nominations, including [Video of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_for_Video_of_the_Year "MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year") for "Bad Romance". She was the most nominated artist for a single year, and the first woman to receive two nominations for Video of the Year at the same ceremony. *The Fame Monster* won the [Grammy Award for Best Pop Vocal Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Vocal_Album "Grammy Award for Best Pop Vocal Album"), and "Bad Romance" won [Best Female Pop Vocal Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Female_Pop_Vocal_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Female Pop Vocal Performance") and [Best Short Form Music Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Short_Form_Music_Video "Grammy Award for Best Short Form Music Video") at the [53rd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/53rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards "53rd Annual Grammy Awards"). *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* featured "Bad Romance" and its music video on their respective lists of the "[500 Greatest Songs of All Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone%27s_500_Greatest_Songs_of_All_Time "Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Songs of All Time")" and "[100 Greatest Music Videos of All Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone%27s_100_Greatest_Music_Videos_of_All_Time "Rolling Stone's 100 Greatest Music Videos of All Time")" in 2021.

In 2009, Gaga spent a record 150 weeks on the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart") and became the most downloaded female act in a year in the US, with 11.1 million downloads, earning an entry in the *[Guinness Book of World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_Book_of_World_Records "Guinness Book of World Records")*. Worldwide, *The Fame* and *The Fame Monster* together have sold more than 15 million copies, and the latter was 2010's second best-selling album. Its success allowed Gaga to start her second worldwide concert tour, [The Monster Ball Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monster_Ball_Tour "The Monster Ball Tour"), and release *[The Remix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Remix_(Lady_Gaga_album) "The Remix (Lady Gaga album)")*, her final record with Cherrytree Records and [among the best-selling remix albums of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_remix_albums "List of best-selling remix albums"). The Monster Ball Tour ran from November 2009 to May 2011 and grossed $227.4 million, making it the highest-grossing concert tour for a debut headlining artist. Concerts performed at [Madison Square Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Square_Garden "Madison Square Garden") in New York City were filmed for an [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") television special, *[Lady Gaga Presents the Monster Ball Tour: At Madison Square Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga_Presents_the_Monster_Ball_Tour:_At_Madison_Square_Garden "Lady Gaga Presents the Monster Ball Tour: At Madison Square Garden")*.

During this era, Gaga ventured into business, collaborating with consumer electronics company [Monster Cable Products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Cable_Products "Monster Cable Products") to create in-ear, jewel-encrusted headphones called Heartbeats by Lady Gaga. She also partnered with [Polaroid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polaroid_Corporation "Polaroid Corporation") in January 2010 as their creative director and announced a suite of photo-capture products called Grey Label. Her collaboration with her past record producer and ex-boyfriend Rob Fusari led to a lawsuit against her production team, Mermaid Music LLC. At this time, Gaga was tested borderline positive for [lupus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lupus "Lupus") but claimed not to be affected by the symptoms and hoped to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

### 2011–2014: *Born This Way*, *Artpop*, and *Cheek to Cheek*

In February 2011, Gaga released "[Born This Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_(song) "Born This Way (song)")", the lead single from her [studio album of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_(album) "Born This Way (album)"). The song sold more than one million copies within five days, earning the *Guinness World Record* for the fastest selling single on [iTunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITunes "ITunes"). It debuted atop the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), becoming the 1,000th number-one single in the history of the charts. Its second single "[Judas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judas_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Judas (Lady Gaga song)")" followed two months later, and "[The Edge of Glory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Edge_of_Glory "The Edge of Glory")" served as its third single. Both reached the top 10 in the US and the UK. Her music video for "The Edge of Glory", unlike her previous work, portrays her dancing on a fire escape and walking on a lonely street, without intricate choreography and back-up dancers.

*Born This Way* was released on May 23, 2011, and debuted atop the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") with first-week sales of [1.1 million copies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_fastest-selling_albums "Lists of fastest-selling albums"). It sold eight million copies worldwide and received three Grammy nominations, including Gaga's third consecutive nomination for [Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Album of the Year"). *Rolling Stone* listed the record among "[The 500 Greatest Albums of All Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone%27s_500_Greatest_Albums_of_All_Time "Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Albums of All Time")" in 2020. *Born This Way*'s following singles were "[You and I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_and_I_(Lady_Gaga_song) "You and I (Lady Gaga song)")" and "[Marry the Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marry_the_Night "Marry the Night")", which reached numbers 6 and 29 in the US, respectively. Its track, "[Bloody Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloody_Mary_(song) "Bloody Mary (song)")", became a resurgent success and was released as a single in 2022. While filming the "You and I" music video, Gaga met and started dating actor [Taylor Kinney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Kinney "Taylor Kinney") in July 2011, who played her love interest. She also embarked on the [Born This Way Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_Ball "Born This Way Ball") tour in April 2012, which was scheduled to conclude the following March, but ended one month earlier when Gaga canceled the remaining dates due to a [labral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acetabular_labrum "Acetabular labrum") tear of her right hip that required surgery. While refunds for the cancellations were estimated to be worth $25 million, the tour grossed $183.9 million globally.

In 2011, Gaga also worked with [Tony Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Bennett "Tony Bennett") on a [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz") version of "[The Lady Is a Tramp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lady_Is_a_Tramp "The Lady Is a Tramp")", with [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John "Elton John") on "Hello Hello" for the animated feature film *[Gnomeo & Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnomeo_%26_Juliet "Gnomeo & Juliet")*, and with [The Lonely Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lonely_Island "The Lonely Island") and [Justin Timberlake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Timberlake "Justin Timberlake") on "[3-Way (The Golden Rule)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-Way_(The_Golden_Rule) "3-Way (The Golden Rule)")". On October 15, 2011, she performed an exclusive, one-off concert in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") for the "A Decade of Difference" event, celebrating the 65th birthday of former US President [Bill Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Clinton "Bill Clinton"). In November 2011, she was featured in a [Thanksgiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving_(United_States) "Thanksgiving (United States)") television special titled *[A Very Gaga Thanksgiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Very_Gaga_Thanksgiving "A Very Gaga Thanksgiving")*, which attracted 5.7 million American viewers and spawned the release of her fourth EP, *[A Very Gaga Holiday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Very_Gaga_Holiday "A Very Gaga Holiday")*. In 2012, Gaga guest-starred as an animated version of herself in an episode of *[The Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons "The Simpsons")* called "[Lisa Goes Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Goes_Gaga "Lisa Goes Gaga")", and released her first fragrance, [Lady Gaga Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga_Fame "Lady Gaga Fame"), followed by a second one, [Eau de Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eau_de_Gaga "Eau de Gaga"), in 2014.

Gaga began work on her third studio album, *[Artpop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artpop "Artpop")*, in early 2012, during the Born This Way Ball tour. She crafted the album to mirror "a night at the club". In August 2013, Gaga released the album's lead single "[Applause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applause_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Applause (Lady Gaga song)")", which reached number one in Hungary, number four in the US, and number five in the UK. A [lyric video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyric_video "Lyric video") for *Artpop* track "[Aura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aura_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Aura (Lady Gaga song)")" followed in October to accompany [Robert Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Rodriguez "Robert Rodriguez")'s *[Machete Kills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machete_Kills "Machete Kills")*, where Gaga plays an assassin named La Chameleon. The film received generally mixed reviews and earned less than half of its $33 million budget. The second *Artpop* single, "[Do What U Want](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_What_U_Want "Do What U Want")", featured singer [R. Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Kelly "R. Kelly") and was released later that month, topping the charts in Hungary and reaching number 13 in the US. *Artpop* was released on November 6, 2013, to mixed reviews. Helen Brown of *[The Daily Telegraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Telegraph "The Daily Telegraph")* criticized Gaga for making another album about her fame and doubted the record's originality, but found it "great for dancing". The album debuted atop the *Billboard* 200 chart, and sold more than 2.5 million copies worldwide as of July 2014. "[G.U.Y.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.U.Y. "G.U.Y.")" was released as the third single in March 2014 and peaked at number 76 on the US *Billboard* Hot 100.

Gaga hosted an episode of *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* in November 2013. After holding her second Thanksgiving Day television special on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company"), *[Lady Gaga and the Muppets Holiday Spectacular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga_and_the_Muppets_Holiday_Spectacular "Lady Gaga and the Muppets Holiday Spectacular")*, she performed a special rendition of "Do What U Want" with [Christina Aguilera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Aguilera "Christina Aguilera") on the [fifth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(U.S._season_5) "The Voice (U.S. season 5)") of the American reality talent show *[The Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(U.S._TV_series) "The Voice (U.S. TV series)")*. In March 2014, Gaga had a [seven-day concert residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga_Live_at_Roseland_Ballroom "Lady Gaga Live at Roseland Ballroom") commemorating the last performance at New York's [Roseland Ballroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roseland_Ballroom "Roseland Ballroom") before its closure. Two months later, she embarked on the [ArtRave: The Artpop Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArtRave:_The_Artpop_Ball "ArtRave: The Artpop Ball") tour, building on concepts from her [ArtRave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArtRave "ArtRave") promotional event. Earning $83 million, the tour included cities canceled from the Born This Way Ball tour itinerary. In the meantime, Gaga split from longtime manager [Troy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy_Carter_(music_industry) "Troy Carter (music industry)") over "creative differences", and by June 2014, she and new manager Bobby Campbell joined Artist Nation, the artist management division of [Live Nation Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Nation_Entertainment "Live Nation Entertainment"). She briefly appeared in Rodriguez's *[Sin City: A Dame to Kill For](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin_City:_A_Dame_to_Kill_For "Sin City: A Dame to Kill For")*, and was confirmed as [Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace "Versace")'s spring-summer 2014 ambassador with a campaign called "Lady Gaga for Versace".

In September 2014, Gaga released a collaborative jazz album with Tony Bennett titled *[Cheek to Cheek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheek_to_Cheek_(album) "Cheek to Cheek (album)")*. The inspiration behind the album came from her friendship with Bennett, and fascination with jazz music since her childhood. He stated that Gaga is "the most talented artist I have ever met". Before the album was released, it produced the singles "[Anything Goes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anything_Goes_(Cole_Porter_song)#Tony_Bennett_and_Lady_Gaga_version "Anything Goes (Cole Porter song)")" and "[I Can't Give You Anything but Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Can%27t_Give_You_Anything_but_Love,_Baby#Tony_Bennett_and_Lady_Gaga_version "I Can't Give You Anything but Love, Baby")". *Cheek to Cheek* received generally favorable reviews; *The Guardian*'s Caroline Sullivan praised Gaga's vocals and Howard Reich of the *[Chicago Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Tribune "Chicago Tribune")* wrote that "*Cheek to Cheek* serves up the real thing, start to finish". The record was Gaga's third consecutive number-one album on the *Billboard* 200, and won a [Grammy Award for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Traditional_Pop_Vocal_Album "Grammy Award for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Album"). The duo recorded the concert special *[Tony Bennett and Lady Gaga: Cheek to Cheek Live!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Bennett_and_Lady_Gaga:_Cheek_to_Cheek_Live! "Tony Bennett and Lady Gaga: Cheek to Cheek Live!")*, and embarked on the [Cheek to Cheek Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheek_to_Cheek_Tour "Cheek to Cheek Tour") from December 2014 to August 2015.

### 2015–2017: *American Horror Story*, *Joanne*, and Super Bowl performances

In February 2015, Gaga became engaged to Taylor Kinney. After the lukewarm response to *Artpop*, Gaga began to reinvent her image and style. According to *Billboard*, this shift started with the release of *Cheek to Cheek* and the attention she received for her performance at the [87th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/87th_Academy_Awards "87th Academy Awards"), where she sang a medley of songs from *[The Sound of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sound_of_Music_(film) "The Sound of Music (film)")* in a tribute to [Julie Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Andrews "Julie Andrews"). Considered one of her best performances by *Billboard*, it triggered more than 214,000 interactions per minute globally on Facebook. She and [Diane Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Warren "Diane Warren") co-wrote the song "[Til It Happens to You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Til_It_Happens_to_You "Til It Happens to You")" for the documentary *[The Hunting Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunting_Ground "The Hunting Ground")*, which earned them a nomination for the [Academy Award for Best Original Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Song "Academy Award for Best Original Song"). *Billboard* named Gaga their 2015 [Woman of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Women_in_Music "Billboard Women in Music").

Gaga had spent much of her early life wanting to be an actress, and achieved her goal when she starred in *[American Horror Story: Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Hotel "American Horror Story: Hotel")*. Running from October 2015 to January 2016, *Hotel* is the fifth season of the television [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology_series "Anthology series") horror series, *[American Horror Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story "American Horror Story")*, in which Gaga played a hotel owner named Elizabeth. At the [73rd Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/73rd_Golden_Globe_Awards "73rd Golden Globe Awards"), Gaga received the [Best Actress in a Miniseries or Television Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Miniseries or Television Film") award for her work on the season. She appeared in [Nick Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Knight_(photographer) "Nick Knight (photographer)")'s 2015 fashion film for [Tom Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Ford "Tom Ford")'s 2016 spring campaign and was guest editor for *[V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_(American_magazine) "V (American magazine)")* fashion magazine's 99th issue in January 2016, which featured 16 different covers. She received Editor of the Year award at the [Fashion Los Angeles Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Front_Row "Daily Front Row").

In February 2016, Gaga sang the [US national anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Star-Spangled_Banner "The Star-Spangled Banner") at [Super Bowl 50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_50 "Super Bowl 50"), partnered with [Intel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel "Intel") and [Nile Rodgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nile_Rodgers "Nile Rodgers") for a tribute performance to the late David Bowie at the [58th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/58th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "58th Annual Grammy Awards"), and sang "Til It Happens to You" at the [88th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/88th_Academy_Awards "88th Academy Awards"), where she was introduced by [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden") and was accompanied on-stage by 50 people who had suffered from sexual assault. Her engagement to Taylor Kinney ended in July; she later said her career had interfered with their relationship.

Gaga played a witch named [Scathach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sc%C3%A1thach "Scáthach") in *[American Horror Story: Roanoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Roanoke "American Horror Story: Roanoke")*, the series' sixth season, which ran from September to November 2016. Her role in the fifth season of the show ultimately influenced her future music, prompting her to feature "the art of darkness". In September 2016, she released her fifth album's lead single, "[Perfect Illusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Illusion "Perfect Illusion")", which topped the charts in France and reached number 15 in the US. The album, titled *[Joanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_(album) "Joanne (album)")*, was named after Gaga's late aunt, who was an inspiration for the music. It was released on October 21, 2016, and became Gaga's fourth number one album on the *Billboard* 200, making her the first woman to reach the US chart's summit four times in the 2010s. The album's second single, "[Million Reasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Million_Reasons "Million Reasons")", followed the next month and reached number four in the US. She later released a piano version of the album's [title track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Joanne (Lady Gaga song)") in 2018, which won a [Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Solo_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance"). To promote the album, Gaga embarked on the three-date [Dive Bar Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dive_Bar_Tour_(Lady_Gaga) "Dive Bar Tour (Lady Gaga)").

Gaga performed as the headlining act during the [Super Bowl LI halftime show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LI_halftime_show "Super Bowl LI halftime show") on February 5, 2017. Her performance featured a group of hundreds of lighted drones forming various shapes in the sky above Houston's [NRG Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRG_Stadium "NRG Stadium")—the first time robotic aircraft appeared in a Super Bowl program. It attracted 117.5 million viewers in the US, exceeding the game's 111.3 million viewers and making it the third most-watched Super Bowl halftime show at the time. The performance led to a surge of 410,000 song downloads in the US for Gaga and earned her an [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award "Emmy Award") nomination in the [Outstanding Special Class Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Special_Class_Program "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Special Class Program") category. [CBS Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Sports "CBS Sports") included her performance as the second best in the history of Super Bowl halftime shows. In April, Gaga headlined the [Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley_Music_and_Arts_Festival "Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival"). She also released a standalone single, "[The Cure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cure_(Lady_Gaga_song) "The Cure (Lady Gaga song)")", which reached the top 10 in Australia. Four months later, Gaga began the [Joanne World Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_World_Tour "Joanne World Tour"), which she announced after the Super Bowl LI halftime show. Gaga's creation of *Joanne* and preparation for her halftime show performance were featured in the documentary *[Gaga: Five Foot Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaga:_Five_Foot_Two "Gaga: Five Foot Two")*, which premiered on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") that September. Throughout the film, she was seen suffering from [chronic pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronic_pain "Chronic pain"), which was later revealed to be the effect of a long-term condition called [fibromyalgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibromyalgia "Fibromyalgia"). In February 2018, it prompted Gaga to cancel the last ten shows of the Joanne World Tour, which ultimately grossed $95 million from 842,000 tickets sold.

### 2018–2019: *A Star Is Born* and Las Vegas residency

In March 2018, Gaga released a cover of Elton John's "[Your Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Your_Song "Your Song")" for his tribute album *[Revamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revamp_%26_Restoration#Revamp "Revamp & Restoration")*. Later that year, she starred as the struggling singer Ally in [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper "Bradley Cooper")'s musical romantic drama *[A Star Is Born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Star_Is_Born_(2018_film) "A Star Is Born (2018 film)")*, a remake of the [1937 film of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Star_Is_Born_(1937_film) "A Star Is Born (1937 film)"). The film follows Ally's relationship with singer Jackson Maine (played by Cooper), which becomes strained after her career begins to overshadow his. Cooper had approached Gaga after seeing her perform at a cancer research fundraiser. An admirer of Cooper's work, Gaga agreed to the project due to its portrayal of addiction and depression. *A Star Is Born* premiered at the [2018 Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_Venice_International_Film_Festival "75th Venice International Film Festival"), and was released worldwide that October. On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), the critical consensus describes Gaga as "appealing" in her role. [Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw "Peter Bradshaw") of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* wrote that her ability to be "part ordinary person, part extraterrestrial celebrity empress functions at the highest level". [Stephanie Zacharek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Zacharek "Stephanie Zacharek") of *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* felt she gave a "knockout performance" and found her to be "charismatic" without her usual makeup, wigs and costumes. For the role, Gaga won the [National Board of Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Board_of_Review_Award_for_Best_Actress "National Board of Review Award for Best Actress") and [Critics' Choice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award_for_Best_Actress "Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Actress") awards for Best Actress, in addition to receiving nominations for the [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress "Academy Award for Best Actress"), [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Drama "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama"), [Screen Actors Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Female_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role") and [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Leading_Role "BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Leading Role") for Best Actress.

Gaga and Cooper co-wrote and produced most of the songs on the [soundtrack for *A Star Is Born*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Star_Is_Born_(2018_soundtrack) "A Star Is Born (2018 soundtrack)"), which she insisted they perform live in the film. Its lead single, "[Shallow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shallow_(Lady_Gaga_and_Bradley_Cooper_song) "Shallow (Lady Gaga and Bradley Cooper song)")", performed by the two, was released on September 27, 2018. It topped the charts in Australia, Austria, Ireland, New Zealand, Switzerland, the UK, and the US. The soundtrack contains 34 tracks, including 17 original songs, and received generally positive reviews. Mark Kennedy of *[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post "The Washington Post")* called it a "five-star marvel", while Ben Beaumont-Thomas of *The Guardian* thought it contained "instant classics" showcasing Gaga's "emotional might". Commercially, the soundtrack debuted at number one in the US, extending Gaga's record for the most *Billboard* 200 number ones in the 2010s among women. It also topped the charts in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Switzerland and the UK. As of June 2019, the soundtrack had sold over six million copies worldwide. The album earned Gaga a [BAFTA Award for Best Film Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Film_Music "BAFTA Award for Best Film Music") and four Grammy Awards: [Best Compilation Soundtrack for Visual Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Compilation_Soundtrack_for_Visual_Media "Grammy Award for Best Compilation Soundtrack for Visual Media") (2019), [Best Pop Duo/Group Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Duo/Group_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance") for "Shallow" (2019) and two awards for [Best Song Written for Visual Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Song_Written_for_Visual_Media "Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media"), for "Shallow" (2019) and "[I'll Never Love Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%27ll_Never_Love_Again "I'll Never Love Again")" (2020). "Shallow" also won her the Academy Award, [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Original_Song "Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song"), and [Critics' Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award_for_Best_Song "Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Song") for Best Original Song. Gaga gave live performances of the song at the [61st Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/61st_Annual_Grammy_Awards "61st Annual Grammy Awards") and the [91st Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/91st_Academy_Awards "91st Academy Awards").

In August 2018, Gaga signed a [concert residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concert_residency "Concert residency"), titled [Lady Gaga Enigma + Jazz & Piano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga_Enigma_%2B_Jazz_%26_Piano "Lady Gaga Enigma + Jazz & Piano"), to perform at the [MGM Park Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_Resort_and_Casino "Monte Carlo Resort and Casino") in [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas "Las Vegas"). The residency consisted of two types of shows: Enigma, which focused on theatricality and included Gaga's biggest hits, and Jazz & Piano, which involved tracks from the [Great American Songbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_American_Songbook "Great American Songbook") and stripped-down versions of Gaga's songs. The Enigma show opened in December 2018 and the Jazz & Piano show opened in January 2019. In October 2018, Gaga announced her engagement to the talent agent Christian Carino, whom she had met the previous year; they called off the engagement in February 2019. In September 2019, Gaga launched a [vegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegan "Vegan") makeup line, [Haus Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haus_Labs "Haus Labs"), exclusively on [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company) "Amazon (company)"). Haus Laboratories lipstick reached number one on Amazon's list of best-selling lipsticks.

### 2020–2023: *Chromatica*, *Love for Sale*, and *House of Gucci*

Gaga's sixth studio album, *[Chromatica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatica "Chromatica")*, was released on May 29, 2020, to positive reviews. It debuted at the top of the US charts, becoming her sixth consecutive number-one album in the country, and reached the top spot in more than a dozen other territories including Australia, Canada, France, Italy and the UK. *Chromatica*'s lead single, "[Stupid Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupid_Love_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Stupid Love (Lady Gaga song)")", was released on February 28, 2020, and charted at number five in the US and the UK. The second single, "[Rain on Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_on_Me_(Lady_Gaga_and_Ariana_Grande_song) "Rain on Me (Lady Gaga and Ariana Grande song)")", featuring [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande "Ariana Grande"), followed on May 22. It won the Best Pop Duo/Group Performance at the [63rd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards "63rd Annual Grammy Awards"), and debuted at number one in the US, making Gaga the third person to top the country's chart in the 2000s, 2010s and 2020s. At the [2020 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2020 MTV Video Music Awards"), Gaga won five awards, including the inaugural Tricon Award representing achievement in three or more fields of entertainment. In September 2020, Gaga appeared in the video campaign for [Valentino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentino_(fashion_house) "Valentino (fashion house)")'s Voce Viva fragrance, singing a stripped-down version of the *Chromatica* track "[Sine from Above](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine_from_Above "Sine from Above")".

After endorsing [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden") in the [2020 United States presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election "2020 United States presidential election"), Gaga sang the US national anthem during [his inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Joe_Biden "Inauguration of Joe Biden") as the country's 46th president on January 20, 2021. In February, Gaga's dog walker Ryan Fischer was hospitalized after being shot in Hollywood. ## Artistry

### Influences

Gaga grew up listening to artists such as [Michael Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jackson "Michael Jackson"), [the Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles "The Beatles"), Stevie Wonder, Queen, [Bruce Springsteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Springsteen "Bruce Springsteen"), [Pink Floyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Floyd "Pink Floyd"), [Led Zeppelin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Led_Zeppelin "Led Zeppelin"), [Whitney Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Houston "Whitney Houston"), Elton John, [Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_(musician) "Prince (musician)"), [En Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/En_Vogue "En Vogue"), [TLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TLC_(group) "TLC (group)"), Christina Aguilera, [Janet Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Jackson "Janet Jackson"), and [Blondie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blondie_(band) "Blondie (band)"), who have all influenced her music. Gaga's musical inspiration varies from [dance-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance-pop "Dance-pop") singers such as [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna "Madonna") and Michael Jackson to glam rock artists such as David Bowie and [Freddie Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_Mercury "Freddie Mercury"), as well as the theatrics of the [pop artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_art "Pop art") Andy Warhol and her own performance roots in musical theater. She has been compared to Madonna, who has said that she sees herself reflected in Gaga, while Gaga has expressed her desire to revolutionize pop music the way Madonna did. Gaga has also cited heavy metal bands as an influence, specifically [Iron Maiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Maiden "Iron Maiden"), [Black Sabbath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sabbath "Black Sabbath"), [Nine Inch Nails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Inch_Nails "Nine Inch Nails") and [Marilyn Manson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marilyn_Manson_(band) "Marilyn Manson (band)"). She has credited Beyoncé as a key inspiration to pursue a musical career. Her other inspirations include [Grace Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Jones "Grace Jones"), [Duke Ellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Ellington "Duke Ellington"), and [Carole King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carole_King "Carole King").

Gaga was inspired by her mother to be interested in fashion, which would later become a major influence and integrated with her music. Stylistically, Gaga has been compared to [Leigh Bowery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leigh_Bowery "Leigh Bowery"), [Isabella Blow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabella_Blow "Isabella Blow"), and [Cher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cher "Cher"); she once commented that as a child, she absorbed Cher's fashion sense and made it her own. Gaga became friends with British fashion designer [Alexander McQueen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_McQueen "Alexander McQueen") shortly before his suicide in 2010, and became known for wearing his designs, particularly his towering [armadillo shoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armadillo_shoe "Armadillo shoe"). She has called fashion designer [Donatella Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donatella_Versace "Donatella Versace") her [muse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse "Muse"); Versace referred to Gaga as "the fresh Donatella". Gaga has also been influenced by [Princess Diana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana,_Princess_of_Wales "Diana, Princess of Wales"), whom she has admired since childhood.

Gaga has called the Indian [alternative medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_medicine "Alternative medicine") advocate [Deepak Chopra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepak_Chopra "Deepak Chopra") a "true inspiration", and has also quoted Indian leader [Osho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajneesh "Rajneesh")'s book *Creativity* on Twitter. Gaga said she was influenced by Osho's work in valuing rebellion through creativity and equality.

### Musical style and themes

Critics have analyzed and scrutinized Gaga's musical and performance style, as she has experimented with new ideas and images throughout her career. She has called the continual reinvention "liberating", and has been drawn to it since childhood. Gaga has said that [synesthesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synesthesia "Synesthesia"), characterized by her seeing colors while making music, has played a key role in her creative process, explaining: "As I'm writing, it assembles in my brain, then through the recording it becomes a full piece of colour. Every song is a different shade." Gaga's music spans a variety of music genres, primarily [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music"), [dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance_music "Dance music"), [electronic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_music "Electronic music"), and [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz"). She has also branched out into [rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music"), [disco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco "Disco"), [Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_music "Americana music"), and [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"). Gaga's voice has been classified as a [contralto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contralto "Contralto"), with a [vocal range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocal_range "Vocal range") spanning from [B♭2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%E2%99%AD_(musical_note) "B♭ (musical note)") to [B5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B_(musical_note) "B (musical note)"). She has changed her vocal style regularly, and deemed *Born This Way* "much more vocally up to par with what I've always been capable of". In summing up her voice, *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")* wrote: "There's an immense emotional intelligence behind the way she uses her voice. Almost never does she overwhelm a song with her vocal ability, recognizing instead that artistry is to be found in nuance rather than lung power."

According to Evan Sawdey of *[PopMatters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PopMatters "PopMatters")*, Gaga managed "to get you moving and grooving at an almost effortless pace" with *The Fame*. Gaga has said that she believes "all good music can be played on a piano and still sound like a hit". [Simon Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Reynolds "Simon Reynolds") wrote in 2010: "Everything about Gaga came from [electroclash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroclash "Electroclash"), except the music, which wasn't particularly 1980s, just ruthlessly catchy [naughties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noughties "Noughties") pop glazed with [Auto-Tune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto-Tune "Auto-Tune") and undergirded with [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_R%26B "Contemporary R&B")-ish beats."

Gaga's songs have covered a wide variety of concepts; *The Fame* discusses the lust for stardom, while *The Fame Monster* expresses fame's dark side through [monster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paranoia "Paranoia") metaphors. *The Fame* is an electropop and dance-pop album that has influences of 1980s pop and 1990s [Europop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europop "Europop"), whereas *The Fame Monster* displays Gaga's taste for pastiche, drawing on "Seventies arena glam, perky [ABBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABBA "ABBA") [disco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco "Disco"), and sugary throwbacks like [Stacey Q](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stacey_Q "Stacey Q")". *Born This Way* has lyrics in English, French, German, and Spanish and features themes common to Gaga's controversial songwriting such as sex, love, religion, money, drugs, identity, liberation, sexuality, freedom, and individualism. The album explores new genres, such as [electronic rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_rock "Electronic rock") and [techno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Techno "Techno").

The themes in *Artpop* revolve around Gaga's personal views of fame, love, sex, [feminism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feminism "Feminism"), self-empowerment, overcoming addiction, and reactions to media scrutiny. *Billboard* described *Artpop* as "coherently channeling R&B, techno, disco and rock music". With *Cheek to Cheek*, Gaga pursued the jazz genre. *Joanne*, exploring the genres of country, funk, pop, dance, rock, electronic music and folk, was influenced by her personal life. The *A Star Is Born* soundtrack contains elements of [blues rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues_rock "Blues rock"), country and [bubblegum pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubblegum_pop "Bubblegum pop"). *Billboard* commented that its lyrics are about wanting change, its struggle, love, romance, and bonding, describing the music as "timeless, emotional, gritty and earnest. They sound like songs written by artists who, quite frankly, are supremely messed up but hit to the core of the listener."

On *Chromatica*, Gaga returned to her dance-pop roots, and discussed her struggles with mental health. *Love for Sale* and *Harlequin* expand on her venture into jazz music. The former consists of a tribute to [Cole Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Porter "Cole Porter"), while the latter was inspired by her role in *Joker: Folie à Deux*. With *Mayhem*, Gaga aimed to revisit "the pop music my earliest fans loved." Described by *Billboard* as a "chaotic blur of genres", it incorporates [synth-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synth-pop "Synth-pop"), with [industrial dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_dance "Industrial dance") influences, and elements of [electro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electro_(music) "Electro (music)"), disco, [industrial pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_pop "Industrial pop"), rock and [pop rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_rock "Pop rock"). Lyrically, it explores themes of love, chaos, fame, identity, and desire, using metaphors of transformation, duality, and excess.

### Videos and stage

Featuring constant costume changes and provocative visuals, Gaga's music videos are often described as short films. The video for "Telephone" earned Gaga the *Guinness World Record* for Most Product Placement in a Video. According to author Curtis Fogel, she explores [bondage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondage_(sexual) "Bondage (sexual)") and [sadomasochism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadomasochism "Sadomasochism") and highlights prevalent feminist themes. The main themes of her music videos are sex, violence, and power. She has called herself "a little bit of a feminist" and asserted that she is "sexually empowering women". *Billboard* ranked her sixth on its list of "The 100 Greatest Music Video Artists of All Time" in 2020, stating that "the name 'Lady Gaga' will forever be synonymous with culture-shifting music videos".

Regarded as "one of the greatest living musical performers" by *Rolling Stone*, Gaga has called herself a perfectionist when it comes to her elaborate shows. Her performances have been described as "highly entertaining and innovative"; the blood-spurting performance of "Paparazzi" at the [2009 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2009 MTV Video Music Awards") was described as "eye-popping" by MTV News and ranked among the best MTV VMA performances of all time by various publications. She continued the blood-soaked theme during The Monster Ball Tour, causing protests in England from family groups and fans in the aftermath of the [Cumbria shootings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbria_shootings "Cumbria shootings"), in which a taxi driver had killed 12 people, then himself. At the [2011 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2011 MTV Video Music Awards"), Gaga appeared in [drag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_(clothing) "Drag (clothing)") as her male [alter ego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alter_ego "Alter ego"), Jo Calderone, and delivered a lovesick monologue before performing her song "You and I". She stated that she created the character to explore "what I was looking for in men, and also what I was maybe lacking in myself". When asked about the persona in 2025, she said that Calderone was "no longer with us". As Gaga's choreographer and creative director, [Laurieann Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurieann_Gibson "Laurieann Gibson") provided material for her shows and videos for four years before she was replaced by her assistant [Richard Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Jackson_(choreographer) "Richard Jackson (choreographer)") in 2014.

In an October 2018 article for *Billboard*, Rebecca Schiller traced back Gaga's videography from "Just Dance" to the release of *A Star Is Born*. Schiller noted that following the *Artpop* era, Gaga's stripped-down approach to music was reflected in the clips for the singles from *Joanne*, taking the example of the music video of lead single "Perfect Illusion" where she eschewed "the elaborate outfits for shorts and a tee-shirt as she performed the song at a desert party". It continued with her performances in the film as well as her stage persona. Reviewing The Chromatica Ball in 2022, Chris Willman of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* wrote that Gaga "could have further played the authenticity card for all it's worth" after the release of *Joanne* and *A Star Is Born*, but instead "has determined to keep herself weird — or just weird enough to provide necessarily ballast to her more earnest inclinations".

## Public image

Public reception of Gaga's music, fashion sense, and persona is polarized. She is noted for her outlandish fashion sense, which has served as an important aspect of her character. During her early career, members of the media compared her fashion choices to those of Christina Aguilera. When she met briefly with then-president [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama") at a [Human Rights Campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_Campaign "Human Rights Campaign") fundraiser, he found the interaction "intimidating" as she was dressed in 16-inch (40 cm) heels, making her the tallest woman in the room. When interviewed by [Barbara Walters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Walters "Barbara Walters") for her annual [ABC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_News_(United_States) "ABC News (United States)") special *[10 Most Fascinating People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Walters%27_10_Most_Fascinating_People "Barbara Walters' 10 Most Fascinating People")* in 2009, she dismissed the claim that she is [intersex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersex "Intersex") as an [urban legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_legend "Urban legend"). Responding to a question on this issue, she expressed her fondness for [androgyny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Androgyny "Androgyny").

In 2011, 121 women gathered at the [Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/53rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards "53rd Annual Grammy Awards") dressed in costumes similar to those worn by Gaga, earning the 2011 *Guinness World Record* for Largest Gathering of Lady Gaga Impersonators. The [Global Language Monitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Language_Monitor "Global Language Monitor") named "Lady Gaga" as the Top Fashion Buzzword with her trademark "no pants" a close third. *Entertainment Weekly* put her outfits on its end of the decade "best-of" list, saying that she "brought performance art into the mainstream". *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* ranked her number one on their "Best Dressed Stars of 2021" list, writing that Gaga "strutted the streets in high-fashion designs, from a sculptural seersucker number to a black lace corseted gown—accessorizing each with elegant updos, sky-high heels and retro shades—like it was no sweat."

*Time* placed Gaga on their All-*Time* 100 Fashion Icons list, stating: "Lady Gaga is just as notorious for her outrageous style as she is for her pop hits", mentioning outfits "made from plastic bubbles, [Kermit the Frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermit_the_Frog "Kermit the Frog") dolls, and raw meat". Gaga wore a [dress made of raw beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat_dress_of_Lady_Gaga "Meat dress of Lady Gaga") to the 2010 MTV Video Music Awards, which was supplemented by boots, a purse, and a hat also made out of raw beef. Partly awarded in recognition of the dress, *[Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(magazine) "Vogue (magazine)")* named her one of the Best Dressed people of 2010 and *Time* named the dress the Fashion Statement of the year. It attracted the attention of worldwide media; the animal rights organization [People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_for_the_Ethical_Treatment_of_Animals "People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals") (PETA) found it offensive. The meat dress was displayed at the [National Museum of Women in the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_of_Women_in_the_Arts "National Museum of Women in the Arts") in 2012, and entered the [Rock and Roll Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_and_Roll_Hall_of_Fame "Rock and Roll Hall of Fame") in September 2015.

Gaga's fans call her "Mother Monster", and she often refers to them as "Little Monsters", a phrase she had tattooed on herself in dedication. In his article "Lady Gaga Pioneered Online Fandom Culture As We Know It" for *[Vice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_(magazine) "Vice (magazine)")*, Jake Hall wrote that Gaga inspired several subsequent fan-brandings, such as those of [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift"), [Rihanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rihanna "Rihanna") and [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber "Justin Bieber"). In July 2012, Gaga also co-founded the [social networking service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_networking_service "Social networking service") LittleMonsters.com, devoted to her fans. Scott Hardy, CEO of Polaroid, praised Gaga for inspiring fans and for her close interactions with them on social media.

### Censorship

In 2011, lyrics of Gaga's song "Born This Way", which reference homosexuality and LGBTQ subjects, were censored by Malaysian broadcaster [AMP Radio Networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMP_Radio_Networks "AMP Radio Networks") as a precaution against government restrictions against songs which might violate "good taste or decency or [are] offensive to public feeling". The same year, the [Ministry of Culture of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Culture_of_the_People%27s_Republic_of_China "Ministry of Culture of the People's Republic of China"), reportedly enforcing a directive designed to tackle "poor taste and vulgar content", banned the publication of six of Gaga's songs from the album *Born This Way* on all Chinese music websites.

In 2014, her album *Artpop* was released in China with conditions being placed on the album artwork, covering her almost naked body as well as changing the title of the song "[Sexxx Dreams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexxx_Dreams "Sexxx Dreams")" to "X Dreams". In 2016, her meeting with the [Dalai Lama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Dalai_Lama "14th Dalai Lama") resulted in the [Chinese government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_government "Chinese government") adding her to a list of hostile foreign forces, and Chinese websites and media organizations were ordered to stop distributing her songs. The [Publicity Department of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publicity_Department_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party "Publicity Department of the Chinese Communist Party") also issued an order for state-controlled media to condemn the meeting. In 2019, coverage of Gaga in the 91st Academy Awards was removed on some Chinese streaming platforms such as [Mango TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango_TV "Mango TV") and in 2021 her appearance was cut from *Friends: The Reunion*; both incidents received backlash from Chinese fans.

## Activism

### Philanthropy

After declining an invitation to appear on the single "[We Are the World 25 for Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Are_the_World_25_for_Haiti "We Are the World 25 for Haiti")", because of rehearsals for her tour, to benefit victims of the [2010 Haiti earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Haiti_earthquake "2010 Haiti earthquake"), Gaga donated the proceeds of her January 2010 [Radio City Music Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_City_Music_Hall "Radio City Music Hall") concert to the country's reconstruction relief fund. All profits from her online store that day were also donated, and Gaga announced that $500,000 was collected for the fund. Hours after the [2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami "2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami") hit Japan, Gaga tweeted a link to Japan Prayer Bracelets. All revenue from a bracelet she designed in conjunction with the company was donated to relief efforts; these raised $1.5 million. In June 2011, Gaga performed at [MTV Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Japan "MTV Japan")'s charity show in [Makuhari Messe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makuhari_Messe "Makuhari Messe"), which benefited the [Japanese Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Red_Cross "Japanese Red Cross").

In 2012, Gaga joined the campaign group [Artists Against Fracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artists_Against_Fracking "Artists Against Fracking"). That October, [Yoko Ono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoko_Ono "Yoko Ono") gave Gaga and four other activists the [LennonOno Grant for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LennonOno_Grant_for_Peace "LennonOno Grant for Peace") in [Reykjavík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reykjav%C3%ADk "Reykjavík"), Iceland. The following month, Gaga pledged to donate $1 million to the American Red Cross to help the victims of [Hurricane Sandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Sandy "Hurricane Sandy"). Gaga has also contributed to the fight against [HIV and AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS "HIV/AIDS"), focusing on educating young women about the risks of the disease. In collaboration with [Cyndi Lauper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyndi_Lauper "Cyndi Lauper"), Gaga joined forces with [MAC Cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make-up_Art_Cosmetics "Make-up Art Cosmetics") to launch a line of lipstick under their supplementary cosmetic line, Viva Glam. Sales have raised more than $202 million to fight HIV and AIDS.

In April 2016, Gaga joined Vice President Joe Biden at the [University of Nevada, Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nevada,_Las_Vegas "University of Nevada, Las Vegas") to support his [It's On Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Task_Force_to_Protect_Students_from_Sexual_Assault "White House Task Force to Protect Students from Sexual Assault") campaign as he traveled to colleges on behalf of the organization, which has seen 250,000 students from more than 530 colleges sign a pledge of solidarity and activism. Two months later, Gaga attended the [84th Annual US Conference of Mayors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Conference_of_Mayors "United States Conference of Mayors") in Indianapolis where together with the Dalai Lama she talked about the power of kindness and how to make the world a more compassionate place.

In April 2020, Gaga curated the televised benefit concert, *[One World: Together at Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Together_at_Home "Together at Home")*, a collaboration with [Global Citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Citizen_(organization) "Global Citizen (organization)") to benefit the [World Health Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Organization "World Health Organization")'s [COVID-19 Solidarity Response Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_Solidarity_Response_Fund "COVID-19 Solidarity Response Fund"). The special raised $127 million, which according to *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* "puts it on par with the other legendary fundraiser, [Live Aid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Aid "Live Aid"), as the highest grossing charity concert in history." In recognition of her activism for the [Black Lives Matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Lives_Matter "Black Lives Matter") movement during the 2020 [George Floyd protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Floyd_protests "George Floyd protests"), Gaga received the [King Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Center_for_Nonviolent_Social_Change "King Center for Nonviolent Social Change")'s Yolanda Denise King High Ground Award in January 2021. In her acceptance speech, she denounced racism and white supremacy and addressed her social responsibility as a high-profile artist and white woman.

### Born This Way Foundation

In 2012, Gaga launched the [Born This Way Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_This_Way_Foundation "Born This Way Foundation") (BTWF), a non-profit organization that focuses on youth empowerment. It has been named after her 2011 single and album. Media proprietor [Oprah Winfrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oprah_Winfrey "Oprah Winfrey"), writer Deepak Chopra, and US Secretary of Health and Human Services [Kathleen Sebelius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Sebelius "Kathleen Sebelius") spoke at the foundation's inauguration at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University "Harvard University"). The foundation's original funding included $1.2 million from Gaga, $500,000 from the MacArthur Foundation, and $850,000 from [Barneys New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barneys_New_York "Barneys New York"). In July 2012, the BTWF partnered with [Office Depot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_Depot "Office Depot") for a series of limited edition back-to-school products, with 25% of its sales going towards the foundation, for a guaranteed donation of $1 million. The foundation's initiatives have included the "Born Brave Bus" that followed Gaga on tour as a youth drop-in center as an initiative against bullying.

In October 2015, at the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence, Gaga joined 200 high school students, policy makers, and academic officials, including [Peter Salovey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Salovey "Peter Salovey"), to discuss ways to recognize and channel emotions for positive outcomes. In 2016, the foundation partnered with Intel, [Vox Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_Media "Vox Media"), and *[Recode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recode "Recode")* to fight online harassment. The sales revenue of the 99th issue of the *V* magazine, which featured Gaga and Kinney, was donated to the foundation. Gaga and Elton John released the clothing and accessories line Love Bravery at [Macy's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macy%27s "Macy's") in May 2016. 25% of each purchase benefited the BTWF and the [Elton John AIDS Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John_AIDS_Foundation "Elton John AIDS Foundation"). Gaga partnered with [Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks "Starbucks") for a week in June 2017 with the "Cups of Kindness" campaign, where the company donated 25 cents from some of the beverages sold to the foundation. She also appeared in a video by [Staples Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staples_Inc. "Staples Inc.") to raise funds for the foundation and DonorsChoose.org.

On the 2018 [World Kindness Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Kindness_Day "World Kindness Day"), Gaga partnered with the foundation to bring food and relief to a [Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Cross "Red Cross") shelter for people who have been forced to evacuate homes due to the [California wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_California_wildfires "2018 California wildfires"). The foundation also partnered with Starbucks and [SoulCycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoulCycle "SoulCycle") to thank California firefighters for their relief work during the crisis. Gaga had to previously evacuate her own home during the [Woolsey Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolsey_Fire "Woolsey Fire") which spread through parts of Malibu. In March 2019, she penned a letter to supporters of the BTWF, announcing the launch of a new pilot program for a teen mental health first aid project with the National Council for Behavioral Health. Gaga revealed her personal struggles with [mental health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health "Mental health") in her letter and how she was able to get support which saved her life: "I know what it means to have someone support me and understand what I'm going through, and every young person in the world should have someone to turn to when they're hurting. It saved my life, and it will save theirs."

In September 2020, Gaga released an anthology book, *Channel Kindness: Stories of Kindness and Community*, featuring fifty-one stories about kindness, bravery, and resilience from young people all over the world collected by the BTWF, and introduced by herself. She promoted it with a 21 days of kindness challenge on her social media, using the "BeKind21" hashtag. In 2021, Gaga collaborated with the Champagne house Dom Pérignon to benefit the BTWF by releasing a limited edition of Rosé Vintage 2005 bottles along with a sculpture designed by her. The 110 exclusive pieces were sold at private sales, and the profit of $570,000 was donated to the foundation. On the 2021 World Kindness Day, Gaga released a 30-minute special, titled *[The Power of Kindness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Power_of_Kindness "The Power of Kindness")*, as part of the foundation's Channel Kindness program, in which together with a mental health expert and a group of eleven young people, she explored the connection between kindness and mental health.

### LGBTQ advocacy

A [bisexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisexuality "Bisexuality") woman, Gaga has supported LGBTQ rights worldwide. She has attributed much of her early success as a mainstream artist to her [gay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homosexuality "Homosexuality") fans and is considered a gay icon. Early in her career, Gaga had difficulty getting [radio airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airplay "Airplay"), and stated: "The turning point for me was the gay community." She thanked FlyLife, a [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_culture_in_New_York_City "LGBTQ culture in New York City")-based [LGBTQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ "LGBTQ") marketing company which worked with her label Interscope at the time, in the liner notes of *The Fame*. One of her first televised performances was in May 2008 at the [NewNowNext Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NewNowNext_Awards "NewNowNext Awards"), an awards show aired by the LGBTQ television network [Logo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logo_TV "Logo TV").

Gaga spoke at the 2009 [National Equality March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Equality_March "National Equality March") in Washington, D.C., to support the LGBTQ rights movement. She attended the 2010 MTV Video Music Awards accompanied by four gay and lesbian former members of the [United States Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Armed_Forces "United States Armed Forces") who had been unable to serve [openly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_out "Coming out") under the US military's "[don't ask, don't tell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_ask,_don%27t_tell "Don't ask, don't tell")" policy, which banned open homosexuality in the military. Gaga urged her fans via YouTube to contact their senators in an effort to overturn the policy. In September 2010, she spoke at a [Servicemembers Legal Defense Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servicemembers_Legal_Defense_Network "Servicemembers Legal Defense Network")'s rally in [Portland, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Maine "Portland, Maine"). Following this event, *[The Advocate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Advocate_(LGBTQ_magazine) "The Advocate (LGBTQ magazine)")* named her a "fierce advocate" for gays and lesbians.

Gaga appeared at [Europride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europride "Europride"), an international event dedicated to LGBTQ pride, in Rome in June 2011. She criticized the poor state of [gay rights in many European countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_rights_in_Europe "LGBTQ rights in Europe") and described gay people as "revolutionaries of love". Later that year, she was referenced by teenager [Jamey Rodemeyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_of_Jamey_Rodemeyer "Suicide of Jamey Rodemeyer") in the hours prior to his death, with Rodemeyer having tweeted "@ladygaga bye mother monster, thank you for all you have done, paws up forever". Rodemeyer's suicide prompted Gaga to meet with then-President Barack Obama to address anti-gay bullying in American schools. She later [endorsed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Barack_Obama_2012_presidential_campaign_endorsements "List of Barack Obama 2012 presidential campaign endorsements") him during the [2012 US presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_United_States_presidential_election "2012 United States presidential election"). In 2011, Gaga was also ordained as a minister by the [Universal Life Church Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Life_Church_Monastery "Universal Life Church Monastery") allowing her to officiate the wedding of two female friends.

In June 2016, during a vigil held in Los Angeles for victims of the [attack at the gay nightclub Pulse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulse_nightclub_shooting "Pulse nightclub shooting") in Orlando, Gaga read aloud the names of the 49 people killed in the attack, and gave a speech. She also appeared in [Human Rights Campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_Campaign "Human Rights Campaign")'s tribute video to the victims of the attack. Following [Joan Jett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Jett "Joan Jett"), Gaga was the second of nearly 200 musicians and music industry executives to sign [*Billboard*'s Open Letter on Gun Violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard%27s_Open_Letter_on_Gun_Violence "Billboard's Open Letter on Gun Violence"), demanding that Congress enact [universal background checks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_background_check "Universal background check") on all gun buyers. She subsequently supported the 2018 [March for Our Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_for_Our_Lives "March for Our Lives") gun-control rally in Washington, D.C. Gaga opposed the [presidency of Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_presidency_of_Donald_Trump "First presidency of Donald Trump") and his [military transgender ban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transgender_military_ban_in_the_United_States "Transgender military ban in the United States"). She [supported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Hillary_Clinton_2016_presidential_campaign_celebrity_endorsements "List of Hillary Clinton 2016 presidential campaign celebrity endorsements") former Secretary of State [Hillary Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton "Hillary Clinton") for president in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_States_presidential_election "2016 United States presidential election"). In 2018, a leaked memo from Trump's office revealed that his administration wanted to change the legal definition of sex to exclude [transgender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transgender "Transgender") Americans. Gaga was one of the many celebrities to condemn him and spread the #WontBeErased campaign to her Twitter followers.

In January 2019, during one of her Enigma shows, she criticized Vice President [Mike Pence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Pence "Mike Pence") for his wife [Karen Pence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Pence "Karen Pence") working at an evangelical [Christian school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_school "Christian school") where LGBTQ people are turned away, calling him "the worst representation of what it means to be a Christian". Gaga also stated: "I am a Christian woman, and what I do know about Christianity is that we bear no prejudice, and everybody is welcome". Gaga made a congratulatory speech commemorating the 50th anniversary of the [Stonewall riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonewall_riots "Stonewall riots") and the [LGBTQ community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_community "LGBTQ community")'s accomplishments at [WorldPride NYC 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonewall_50_%E2%80%93_WorldPride_NYC_2019 "Stonewall 50 – WorldPride NYC 2019") outside the [Stonewall Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonewall_Inn "Stonewall Inn"), birthplace of the modern [gay rights movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_social_movements "LGBTQ social movements"). Following Trump's win in the [2024 US presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election "2024 United States presidential election"), in which Gaga endorsed [Kamala Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamala_Harris "Kamala Harris"), she voiced her support for the transgender community after his [rollback of their protections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Order_14168 "Executive Order 14168").

## Legacy

Gaga was named the "[Queen of Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorific_nicknames_in_popular_music#G "Honorific nicknames in popular music")" in a 2011 ranking by *Rolling Stone* based on record sales and social media metrics. In 2012, she ranked fourth in [VH1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VH1 "VH1")'s [Greatest Women in Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_(VH1_TV_series) "The Greatest (VH1 TV series)"), and became a feature of the temporary exhibition *The Elevated. From the Pharaoh to Lady Gaga*, which marked the 150th anniversary of the [National Museum in Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum,_Warsaw "National Museum, Warsaw").

Gaga has often been praised for using controversy to bring attention to various issues. According to Frankie Graddon of *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")*, Gaga—who wore a meat dress to highlight her distaste for the US military's "don't ask, don't tell" policy—influenced protest dressing on red carpet. *Billboard* named her the "[Greatest Pop Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard%27s_Greatest_Pop_Stars#By_year "Billboard's Greatest Pop Stars")" of 2009, asserting that "to say that her one-year rise from rookie to MVP was meteoric doesn't quite cut it, as she wasn't just successful, but game-changing—thanks to her voracious appetite for reinvention." Because of *The Fame*'s success—it was listed as one of the 100 Greatest Debut Albums of All-Time by *Rolling Stone* in 2013—Gaga has been credited as one of the musicians that popularized synth-pop in the late 2000s and early 2010s.

According to [Kelefa Sanneh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelefa_Sanneh "Kelefa Sanneh") of *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker "The New Yorker")*, "Lady Gaga blazed a trail for truculent pop stars by treating her own celebrity as an evolving art project." Including *Born This Way* as one of the 50 best female albums of all time, *Rolling Stone*'s [Rob Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Sheffield "Rob Sheffield") considers it "hard to remember a world where we didn't have Gaga, although we're pretty sure it was a lot more boring". In 2015, *Time* noted that Gaga had "practically invented the current era of pop music as spectacle". A decade later, Daniel D'Addario of *Variety* further observed the influence of Gaga's sense of spectacle on the next generation of artists, calling her "a pop star's pop star". He surmised that [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter "Sabrina Carpenter")'s use of self-parodying and ultra-glam [camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_(style) "Camp (style)") in her performances, [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish "Billie Eilish")'s incorporation of grandeur into her shows, and [Chappell Roan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chappell_Roan "Chappell Roan")'s "drag-inflected let's-put-on-a-show spirit" and "eagerness to use costumes and makeup to help tell her stories" all came from each watching the video for "Bad Romance" in their childhoods.

A 2017 journal published by *[Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology_of_Aesthetics,_Creativity,_and_the_Arts "Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts")* studying structural patterns in melodies of [earworm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earworm "Earworm") songs compiled lists of catchiest tracks from 3,000 participants, in which Gaga's "Bad Romance", "Alejandro", and "Poker Face" ranked number one, eight, and nine, respectively. In 2018, [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR") wrote she was the second most influential female artist of the 21st century, calling her "one of the first big artists of the 'Internet age'". *Billboard* also placed Gaga fifth on its 2024 "[Greatest Pop Stars of the 21st Century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard%27s_Greatest_Pop_Stars#21st_century "Billboard's Greatest Pop Stars")" list, and sixth on the 2025 "Top 100 Women Artists of the 21st Century" list. She and her work have influenced various artists including [Miley Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus "Miley Cyrus"), [Nicki Minaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicki_Minaj "Nicki Various higher education institutions have offered courses focusing on Gaga. Due to her influence on 21st century culture and her rise to global fame, sociologist Mathieu Deflem of the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina "University of South Carolina") launched a course titled "Lady Gaga and the Sociology of the Fame" in early 2011 with the objective of unraveling "some of the sociologically relevant dimensions of the fame of Lady Gaga". Focusing on her influence on gender and sexual identity, the [University of Virginia College of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia_College_of_Arts_and_Sciences "University of Virginia College of Arts and Sciences") introduced a writing course titled "GaGa for Gaga: Sex, Gender, and Identity" during its Fall 2010 semester, which explored "how identity is challenged by gender and sexuality and how Lady Gaga confronts this challenge". Courses exploring different aspects of Gaga's phenomenon, including her music and fame, have also been offered by [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University "Arizona State University"), [Tufts University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufts_University "Tufts University"), [St. Catherine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Catherine_University "St. Catherine University"), and the [University of Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Richmond "University of Richmond").

A new genus of [ferns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fern "Fern"), *[Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaga_(plant) "Gaga (plant)")*, and its species, *G. germanotta* and *G. monstraparva*, have been named in her honor. The name *monstraparva* alluded to Gaga's fans, known as Little Monsters, since their symbol is the outstretched "monster claw" hand, which resembles a tightly rolled young fern leaf prior to unfurling. Gaga also has an extinct mammal, *[Gagadon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gagadon "Gagadon") minimonstrum*, a parasitic wasp, *[Aleiodes gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleiodes_gaga "Aleiodes gaga")*, and a treehopper, *[Kaikaia gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaikaia_gaga "Kaikaia gaga")*, named for her.

In [Taichung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taichung "Taichung"), Taiwan, July 3 is designated as "Lady Gaga Day" marking the first day Gaga visited the country in 2011. In May 2021, to celebrate the tenth anniversary of *Born This Way* and its cultural impact, [West Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Hollywood,_California "West Hollywood, California") mayor, [Lindsey P. Horvath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_P._Horvath "Lindsey P. Horvath"), presented Gaga with a [key to the city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_to_the_city "Key to the city") and declared May 23 as "Born This Way Day". A street painting with the [Daniel Quasar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Quasar "Daniel Quasar")'s version of the [pride flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow_flag_(LGBTQ) "Rainbow flag (LGBTQ)") featuring the album's title was also unveiled on [Robertson Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robertson_Boulevard_(Los_Angeles) "Robertson Boulevard (Los Angeles)") as a tribute to the album, and how it has inspired the LGBTQ community over the years.

## Achievements

Gaga has won sixteen [Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Awards "Grammy Awards"), an [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award"), a [Sports Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Emmy_Award "Sports Emmy Award"), two [Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards"), a [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Film_Awards "British Academy Film Awards"), twenty two [MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Awards "MTV Video Music Awards") (including the inaugural Tricon Award), three [Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Awards "Brit Awards"), and eighteen *Guinness World Records*. She has been honored with the inaugural [Songwriters Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songwriters_Hall_of_Fame "Songwriters Hall of Fame") Contemporary Icon Award as a songwriter-artist who has attained an iconic status in pop culture. She also received the [National Arts Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Arts_Awards "National Arts Awards")' Young Artist Award, honoring individuals who have shown accomplishments and leadership early in their career, and the [Grammy Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Museum "Grammy Museum") Jane Ortner Artist Award, which recognizes artists who have demonstrated passion and dedication to education through the arts. The [Council of Fashion Designers of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Fashion_Designers_of_America "Council of Fashion Designers of America") recognized her with the Fashion Icon award. In 2019, Gaga became the first person to win an Academy, BAFTA, Golden Globe, and Grammy Award in one year for her contributions to the *A Star Is Born* soundtrack. In 2023, *Rolling Stone* included her among the [200 Greatest Singers of All Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone%27s_200_Greatest_Singers_of_All_Time "Rolling Stone's 200 Greatest Singers of All Time").

With estimated sales of 124 million records, Gaga is one of [the world's best-selling music artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_music_artists "List of best-selling music artists"). She is the first female artist to have four singles ("Just Dance", "Poker Face", "Bad Romance" and "Shallow") each sell at least 10 million copies globally. As of 2026, she has grossed more than $1.1 billion in revenue from concert tours and residencies, with attendance of 8.4 million, being the sixth woman to pass the billion total as reported to *Billboard* Boxscore, receiving the [*Pollstar* Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollstar "Pollstar") for Pop Touring Artist of the Decade (2010s). She is also among the [top digital singles artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-certified_music_artists_in_the_United_States "List of highest-certified music artists in the United States") in the US, with 87.5 million equivalent units certified according to [Recording Industry Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America "Recording Industry Association of America") (RIAA), was the first woman to receive the Digital Diamond Award certification from RIAA, one of the [few artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIAA_certification#Most_Diamond_2 "RIAA certification") with at least three Diamond certified songs ("Bad Romance", "Poker Face" and "Just Dance"), and the first artist to have two songs pass seven million downloads ("Poker Face" and "Just Dance").

Named *Billboard*'s [Artist of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Year-End_Top_Artist "Billboard Year-End Top Artist") in 2010, Gaga subsequently appeared on its "Greatest of All Time Artists" chart. She became the longest-reigning act of *Billboard*'s [Dance/Electronic Albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance/Electronic_Albums "Dance/Electronic Albums") chart with 211 weeks at number one, while *The Fame* spent a record 142 weeks atop the chart. As of 2020, six of her singles appeared on the [International Federation of the Phonographic Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_the_Phonographic_Industry "International Federation of the Phonographic Industry")'s annual Top 10 Global Singles chart ("Just Dance", "Poker Face", "Bad Romance", "Telephone", "Born This Way" and "Shallow"), setting the record for the most entries by any female artist.

According to *Guinness World Records*, Gaga was the most followed person on Twitter from 2011 to 2013, the most famous celebrity in 2013, and the most powerful popstar in 2014. She was included on *Forbes*' [Celebrity 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_Celebrity_100 "Forbes Celebrity 100") from 2010 to 2015 and 2018 to 2020, topping the list in 2011. Gaga also appeared on their list of the [World's Most Powerful Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_list_of_The_World%27s_100_Most_Powerful_Women "Forbes list of The World's 100 Most Powerful Women") from 2010 to 2014, and topped their list of the Most Powerful Musicians in 2013. She was named one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") by *Time* magazine in 2010 and 2019, and ranked second in its most influential people of the past ten years readers' poll in 2013.

As of October 2020, *Forbes* estimated Gaga's [net worth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_worth "Net worth") at $150 million. The magazine named her the [world's highest-paid female musician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_list_of_the_world%27s_highest-paid_musicians#Female "Forbes list of the world's highest-paid musicians") in 2011 with $90 million, and the fourth [top-earning female musician of the 2010s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_list_of_highest-earning_musicians#2010s "Forbes list of highest-earning musicians") with $500 million. In 2012, she placed fourth on *Billboard*'s list of the top moneymakers of 2011 with earnings of $25 million, which included sales from *Born This Way* and her Monster Ball Tour.
