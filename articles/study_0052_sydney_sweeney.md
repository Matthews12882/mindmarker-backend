# Sydney Sweeney

**Sydney Bernice Sweeney** (born September 12, 1997) is an American actress. She gained early recognition for her roles in the series *[Everything Sucks!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_Sucks! "Everything Sucks!")*, *[The Handmaid's Tale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handmaid%27s_Tale_(TV_series) "The Handmaid's Tale (TV series)")*, and *[Sharp Objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharp_Objects_(miniseries) "Sharp Objects (miniseries)")* in 2018. She received wider acclaim for her performances in the drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")* (2019–2026) and the [first season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_Lotus_season_1 "The White Lotus season 1") of the [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology "Anthology") series *[The White Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_Lotus "The White Lotus")* (2021), both of which earned her nominations for [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards").

In film, Sweeney garnered critical acclaim for her performances in the drama film *[Reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_(2023_film) "Reality (2023 film)")* (2023) and for her portrayal of [professional boxer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_boxer "Professional boxer") [Christy Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_Martin "Christy Martin") in the biopic *[Christy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_(2025_American_film) "Christy (2025 American film)")* (2025), and has also appeared in the box office hits *[Anyone but You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyone_but_You "Anyone but You")* (2023) and *[The Housemaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Housemaid_(2025_film) "The Housemaid (2025 film)")* (2025). Her other film credits include *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood")* (2019), *[Madame Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_Web_(film) "Madame Web (film)")* (2024), and *[Immaculate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_(2024_film) "Immaculate (2024 film)")* (2024).

Sweeney has appeared in various advertising campaigns, some sparking controversy.

## Early life

Sydney Bernice Sweeney was born on September 12, 1997, in [Spokane, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spokane,_Washington "Spokane, Washington"). Her mother is a former [criminal defense lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_defense_lawyer "Criminal defense lawyer") and her father works in the [hospitality industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitality_industry "Hospitality industry"). She has a brother, Trent, who joined the [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force "United States Air Force") in 2020. Sweeney was raised in the [Idaho panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_panhandle "Idaho panhandle") along the Washington border at a rural lakeside home that her family has inhabited for five generations. She has said her family is religious. Sweeney attended school at [Saint George's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_George%27s_School_(Spokane,_Washington) "Saint George's School (Spokane, Washington)") in Spokane. She said that she was active in numerous sports, and had a [wakeboarding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakeboarding "Wakeboarding") accident as a child in which the board sliced the area next to her eye, requiring 19 stitches.

Sweeney became interested in acting after auditioning for a role as an extra in an independent film shooting in the Spokane area. To convince her parents to allow her to pursue acting, she presented them with a five-year business plan. Sweeney began to audition and book commercial acting jobs in Seattle and [Portland, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon "Portland, Oregon"), where the family temporarily resided, until moving to Los Angeles at age 13. In grade school, roughly around the fourth grade, Sweeney, having developed a love of mathematics at a young age, was in a mathematics club called Math Is Cool, and the school's robotics team. She attended [Brighton Hall School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_Hall_School "Brighton Hall School") in [Burbank, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burbank,_California "Burbank, California"), and was [valedictorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valedictorian "Valedictorian") of her graduating class. In 2016, Sweeney briefly worked at [Universal Studios Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Studios_Hollywood "Universal Studios Hollywood"), but left after she was hired for an acting job.

## Career

### Early work (2009–2019)

As a child actress, Sweeney debuted on television in 2009 with a bit part in an episode of the series *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series) "Heroes (American TV series)")*. Her first film role was in the 2010 horror comedy *[ZMD: Zombies of Mass Destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZMD:_Zombies_of_Mass_Destruction_(film) "ZMD: Zombies of Mass Destruction (film)")*. In 2011, Sweeney appeared in the short film *Takeo*, and the police drama series *[Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_(2010_TV_series) "Chase (2010 TV series)")*, both roles earned her two nominations from the [Young Artist Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Artist_Award "Young Artist Award"). Also in 2011, she acted in [Michael Lembeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Lembeck "Michael Lembeck")'s made-for-television film *The Bling Ring*, based on the [burglary group of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bling_Ring "Bling Ring"). Subsequently, she had supporting roles in television shows such as *[90210](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/90210_(TV_series) "90210 (TV series)")*, *[Criminal Minds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Minds "Criminal Minds")*, *[Grey's Anatomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey%27s_Anatomy "Grey's Anatomy")* and *[Pretty Little Liars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretty_Little_Liars "Pretty Little Liars")*.

In 2018, she starred as Emaline Addario in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") series *[Everything Sucks!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_Sucks! "Everything Sucks!")*, which revolved around two groups of [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school_(North_America) "High school (North America)") students in Oregon in 1996. She appeared in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") miniseries *[Sharp Objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharp_Objects_(miniseries) "Sharp Objects (miniseries)")*, recurring as Alice, a roommate whom [Amy Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Adams "Amy Adams")'s character meets at a [psychiatric facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychiatric_facility "Psychiatric facility"). Her character originally had a smaller role, but the director kept bringing her in for more scenes. For the role, Sweeney studied stories of girls who experience [mental illness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_illness "Mental illness") and [self-harming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-harm "Self-harm"), and visited hospitals with patients who self-harmed. She filmed *Everything Sucks!* and *Sharp Objects* concurrently, the former during the week and the latter on weekends. Sweeney had a role in the 2018 film *[Under the Silver Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Silver_Lake "Under the Silver Lake")*. She had a recurring role in the second season of the dystopian drama series *[The Handmaid's Tale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handmaid%27s_Tale_(TV_series) "The Handmaid's Tale (TV series)")* as Eden Spencer, a pious and obedient girl from the totalitarian and theocratic Republic of Gilead. She starred as the heroine of the horror film *[Along Came the Devil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Along_Came_the_Devil "Along Came the Devil")*. In 2019, Sweeney appeared in the drama film *[Clementine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clementine_(2019_film) "Clementine (2019 film)")*, the coming-of-age film *[Big Time Adolescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Time_Adolescence "Big Time Adolescence")*, and [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino "Quentin Tarantino")'s *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood")*.

### Breakthrough (2019–2023)

In June 2019, Sweeney took the role of [Cassie Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassie_Howard "Cassie Howard"), a teenager with a reputation for promiscuity, in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)").* The show was a breakout hit, becoming the third most watched HBO series as of 2025. Her performance was praised, earning her a nomination for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Drama Series") in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "74th Primetime Emmy Awards"). *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* wrote that her performance "proves that Sweeney's one hell of a shapeshifter between her deft turns in this, *The Handmaid's Tale*, *Everything Sucks* and *Sharp Objects*." Her many nude scenes in the series received significant attention, and generated discussions about [nudity in film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nudity_in_film "Nudity in film") and the [male gaze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Male_gaze "Male gaze").

In 2020, Sweeney founded the production company Fifty-Fifty Films. The same year, she starred in the television film *[Nocturne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nocturne_(2020_film) "Nocturne (2020 film)")* and [Webtoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webtoon "Webtoon")'s live-action advertisement for the online comic *[Lore Olympus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lore_Olympus "Lore Olympus")*, appearing as Persephone. In 2021, Sweeney appeared in the first season of [Mike White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_White_(filmmaker) "Mike White (filmmaker)")'s anthology series *[The White Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_Lotus "The White Lotus")* as a sardonic college sophomore. For that performance, she was nominated for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actress_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie") in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "74th Primetime Emmy Awards"). She appeared on the [*Time* 100 Next list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100#Time_100_Next_list "Time 100") for 2022.

In 2023, Sweeney starred as [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Air_Force "U.S. Air Force") veteran [Reality Winner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_Winner "Reality Winner") in [Tina Satter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Satter "Tina Satter")'s thriller drama *[Reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_(2023_film) "Reality (2023 film)")*, which premiered in February at the [73rd Berlin Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/73rd_Berlin_Film_Festival "73rd Berlin Film Festival"). Steph Green of *[IndieWire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndieWire "IndieWire")* called the film "inventively mounted and extraordinarily tense" and Sweeney "the real deal". Jessica Kiang of *Variety* wrote that she played Winner "so convincingly that it's hard to remember her as the sardonic, pampered teen in *The White Lotus*, or the nice-girl-turned-nasty in *Euphoria*". Sweeney next had a role in the crime thriller *[Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_(2023_film) "Americana (2023 film)")*, which premiered at the [South by Southwest Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest_Festival "South by Southwest Festival") in March 2023.

Sweeney appeared in the video for [the Rolling Stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rolling_Stones "The Rolling Stones")' single "[Angry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angry_(The_Rolling_Stones_song) "Angry (The Rolling Stones song)")", from their 2023 album *[Hackney Diamonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackney_Diamonds "Hackney Diamonds")*. In response to criticism that the video objectified her, she defended it as "empowerment through embracing my body". At age 25, Sweeney was selected as one of [Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")'s 2023 [30 Under 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30_Under_30 "30 Under 30") in the celebrity category. Later that year, she and [Glen Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Powell "Glen Powell") starred in the romantic comedy *[Anyone but You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyone_but_You "Anyone but You")*, which premiered in December 2023. Sweeney was an executive producer for the film and was instrumental in hiring Powell and director [Will Gluck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Gluck "Will Gluck"). She also contributed to the script. The film became a [sleeper hit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeper_hit "Sleeper hit"), grossing over $220 million worldwide.

### Established actress (2024–present)

In 2024, Sweeney had a supporting role in the [superhero film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhero_film "Superhero film") *[Madame Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_Web_(film) "Madame Web (film)")*, set in [Sony's Spider-Man Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony%27s_Spider-Man_Universe "Sony's Spider-Man Universe") franchise. It was released in February and received negative reviews, [bombing at the box office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_office_bomb "Box office bomb"). Of the film's commercial and critical failure, Sweeney said, "I was just hired as an actress in it, so I was just along for the ride for whatever was going to happen." In March 2024, Sweeney hosted an episode of *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")*. After her appearance, the conservative Canadian newspaper *[National Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Post "National Post")* ran an article titled "Are Sydney Sweeney's breasts double-D harbingers of the death of [woke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woke "Woke")?" and right-wing political scientist [Richard Hanania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Hanania "Richard Hanania") tweeted "Wokeness is dead" alongside a clip of Sweeney centered on her bust. Sweeney called the obsession with her appearance "this weird relationship that people have with me that I have no control over".

Sweeney then produced and starred in the psychological horror film *[Immaculate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_(2024_film) "Immaculate (2024 film)")*. She originally auditioned for the project in 2014. Years later, she purchased the rights to the screenplay and hired frequent collaborator Michael Mohan to direct. Sweeney has said that she considered producing a way to create opportunities for herself. Later in 2024, she co-starred in *[Eden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eden_(2024_film) "Eden (2024 film)")*, directed by [Ron Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard "Ron Howard"). Reviewing the film for *[TheWrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TheWrap "TheWrap"),* critic Chase Hutchinson wrote that Sweeney "runs away with the whole thing ... it's her subtle looks and a growing agency that turns *Eden* into something more ... every moment with her at the forefront is *Eden* at its best."

In June 2025, Sweeney starred alongside [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore "Julianne Moore") in *[Echo Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_Valley_(film) "Echo Valley (film)").* She learned how to ride horses for the role. *[Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_(2023_film) "Americana (2023 film)")* was released in just over 1,000 theaters in August and was labeled a flop by the media, although *[Deadline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood "Deadline Hollywood")* predicted it would be profitable for an independent film.

*[Christy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_(2025_American_film) "Christy (2025 American film)")*, a biopic about boxer [Christy Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_Martin "Christy Martin"), had its world premiere at the [2025 Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Toronto_International_Film_Festival "2025 Toronto International Film Festival"). Sweeney starred as Martin and also produced the film. Commenting on the role, Sweeney said she was inspired by Martin's strength and perseverance. She said that playing a character "that has so many layers and depths, that's a dream as an actor ... I'm a very outdoorsy, athletic person, so to play a character that [required me] to transform myself was a dream". Critic [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman") wrote that Sweeney gave a "potent, true-note, game-changing knockout of a performance ... She fully expresses the soul of a movie star, which is this: She completely becomes the character, and in doing so becomes us." Though the film was well received critically, it was a box-office disappointment. In the aftermath, Sweeney said: "I am so deeply proud of this movie ... We don't always just make art for numbers, we make it for impact. And *Christy* has been the most impactful project of my life."

In December 2025, Sweeney co-starred opposite [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried "Amanda Seyfried") in *[The Housemaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Housemaid_(2025_film) "The Housemaid (2025 film)")*, an adaptation of the [bestselling novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Housemaid_(novel) "The Housemaid (novel)"). The film was successful at the box office, and a sequel is in development with Sweeney set to return in her role. In April 2026, she reprised her role of Cassie in the third season of [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO")'s psychological drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")*.

The company signed a first-look deal with [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures "Sony Pictures"), under which Sweeney is expected to develop and produce multiple projects.

## Other ventures

In January 2023, Sweeney became a [brand ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_ambassador "Brand ambassador") for [Armani Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armani "Armani") and [Laneige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laneige "Laneige"). Her other advertising deals include partnerships with [Miu Miu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miu_Miu "Miu Miu"), [Samsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung "Samsung"), [Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Motor_Company "Ford Motor Company"), [Baskin-Robbins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baskin-Robbins "Baskin-Robbins"), [Cotton On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_On_Group "Cotton On Group") and [Guess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guess_(company) "Guess (company)"). In May 2025, she received widespread attention on social media after partnering with personal care brand [Dr. Squatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Squatch "Dr. Squatch") to launch a limited-edition soap line containing her bathwater.

In January 2026, Sweeney draped dozens of bras over the [Hollywood sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_sign "Hollywood sign") to promote her [SYRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrn "Syrn") lingerie brand.

### American Eagle

In July 2025, Sweeney appeared in an advertising campaign for [American Eagle Outfitters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Eagle_Outfitters "American Eagle Outfitters") called "[Sydney Sweeney Has Great Jeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Eagle_Outfitters#Sydney_Sweeney_ad_campaign "American Eagle Outfitters")". The ad became a subject of controversy, which *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* called a "media firestorm". Some social media users, and eventually some academics, claimed that the pun on "jeans" and "genes", associated with a blonde-haired, blue-eyed white woman, promoted [eugenics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenics "Eugenics") and [white supremacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_supremacy "White supremacy"). *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")*, *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")* and *[Deadline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood "Deadline Hollywood")* described the controversy as being largely manufactured by conservatives, while *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic "The Atlantic")* and the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* found the reactions from both sides exaggerated. Supporters of the campaign decried [wokeness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wokeness "Wokeness") and [cancel culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancel_culture "Cancel culture"). The [Trump administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_presidency_of_Donald_Trump "Second presidency of Donald Trump"), and eventually [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump") himself, commented on the controversy. In August 2025, media analysts interviewed by [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN") said that Sweeney's public image had not suffered from the affair. American Eagle Outfitters later commended the results of the campaign. Sweeney initially said she was unaffected by the controversy and declined to comment further. In December 2025, she released a statement saying "Many have assigned motives and labels to me that just aren't true", and clarifying that she was "against hate and divisiveness".

Sweeney's second campaign for American Eagle Outfitters, called "Syd for Short", was unveiled in April 2026.

### Novig

In September 2026, Sweeney appeared nude in an advertisement for the [sports betting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_betting "Sports betting") website [Novig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novig "Novig"). The advertisement attracted criticism from several female athletes, including gymnast [Gracie Kramer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gracie_Kramer "Gracie Kramer"), sprinter [Amy Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Hunt "Amy Hunt"), and swimmer [Ariarne Titmus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariarne_Titmus "Ariarne Titmus"), for objectifying and setting back women in sports. Others supported the ad as an example of body positivity, while some in marketing described the controversy as likely intentional. Commentators described the campaign and others like it resulting in Sweeney becoming "associated with causing buzz for a brand trying to raise its profile", while some supporters described the scrutiny as a result of Sweeney's "association with the political right".

## Personal life

Sweeney started dating businessman Jonathan Davino in 2018, and they became engaged in 2022. They produced films together (including *[Anyone but You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyone_but_You "Anyone but You")* and *[Immaculate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_(2024_film) "Immaculate (2024 film)")*), and Sweeney considered Davino her "producing partner". The couple separated in early 2025. By June 2025, Sweeney was in a relationship with record executive [Scooter Braun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scooter_Braun "Scooter Braun").

Sweeney is a car enthusiast and has owned a restored [1969 Ford Bronco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Bronco#First_generation_(1966) "Ford Bronco") and a [1965 Ford Mustang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Mustang_(first_generation) "Ford Mustang (first generation)"). As part of her partnership with [Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Motor_Company "Ford Motor Company"), Sweeney designed a custom [2024 Mustang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Mustang_(seventh_generation) "Ford Mustang (seventh generation)").

In 2021, Sweeney purchased a 3,200-square-foot (300 m2) [Tudor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tudor_Revival_architecture "Tudor Revival architecture")-style home in Los Angeles for US$3 million. In 2023, she bought a US$9.9 million "fixer-upper mansion" in Los Angeles's [Bel Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel_Air,_Los_Angeles "Bel Air, Los Angeles") neighborhood, and in 2024 she bought a US$13.5 million home in the [Florida Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Keys "Florida Keys").
