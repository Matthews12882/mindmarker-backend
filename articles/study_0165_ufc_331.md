# UFC 331

***UFC 331: Van vs. Pantoja 2*** (branded as ***Crypto.com UFC 331*** for sponsorship reasons) was a [mixed martial arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_martial_arts "Mixed martial arts") event produced by the [Ultimate Fighting Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_Fighting_Championship "Ultimate Fighting Championship") that took place on September 19, 2026, at [Crypto.com Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crypto.com_Arena "Crypto.com Arena") in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), California, United States.

## Background

The event marked the promotion's sixth visit to Los Angeles and first since [UFC 227](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_227 "UFC 227") in August 2018. Initial reports had speculated that the event may have been held at [Intuit Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intuit_Dome "Intuit Dome") in [Inglewood, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inglewood,_California "Inglewood, California"), or be part of the promotion's [Noche UFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noche_UFC "Noche UFC") 4 celebration of [Mexican Independence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Independence_Day "Mexican Independence Day") in [Glendale, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale,_Arizona "Glendale, Arizona").

A [UFC Flyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Flyweight_Championship "UFC Flyweight Championship") rematch between current champion [Joshua Van](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Van "Joshua Van") and former champion [Alexandre Pantoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Pantoja "Alexandre Pantoja") headlined the event. They previously competed in December 2025 at [UFC 323](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_323 "UFC 323"), where Van won the belt via technical knockout 26 seconds into the first round as a result of an arm injury sustained by Pantoja.

A five-round lightweight bout between [Arman Tsarukyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arman_Tsarukyan "Arman Tsarukyan") and [Maurício Ruffy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maur%C3%ADcio_Ruffy "Maurício Ruffy") served as the co-main event. Tsarukyan was initially expected to face former [UFC Lightweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Lightweight_Championship "UFC Lightweight Championship") [Charles Oliveira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Oliveira "Charles Oliveira") also in a five-round lightweight bout, with early reports indicating that Oliveira would defend his symbolic "[BMF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UFC_champions#BMF_Title "List of UFC champions")" (*baddest [motherfucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motherfucker "Motherfucker")*) title, but the bout was later changed to a non-title contest. Oliveira and Tsarukyan previously met in April 2024 at [UFC 300](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_300 "UFC 300"), where Tsarukyan won via split decision. On August 5, the promotion announced that Tsarukyan would instead face Ruffy. The withdrawal came just days after the death of Oliveira's longtime teammate, UFC flyweight [Allan Nascimento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Nascimento "Allan Nascimento"), although no official reason for Oliveira's withdrawal was given.

A [UFC Women's Bantamweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Women%27s_Bantamweight_Championship "UFC Women's Bantamweight Championship") bout between the reigning champion [Kayla Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayla_Harrison "Kayla Harrison") (who is also a [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judo_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_78_kg "Judo at the 2012 Summer Olympics – Women's 78 kg") and [2016 Olympic gold medalist in judo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judo_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_78_kg "Judo at the 2016 Summer Olympics – Women's 78 kg"); and a two-time [PFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Fighters_League "Professional Fighters League") women's lightweight tournament champion) and former two-time champion [Amanda Nunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Nunes "Amanda Nunes"), who also held the [UFC Women's Featherweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Women%27s_Featherweight_Championship "UFC Women's Featherweight Championship"), was reportedly planned for the event. However, the bout is postponed for undisclosed reasons. The bout was originally scheduled for [UFC 324](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_324 "UFC 324") in January 2026. However, Harrison withdrew from the bout after being diagnosed with herniated discs in her neck, which required surgery.

A bantamweight bout between former [UFC Bantamweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Bantamweight_Championship "UFC Bantamweight Championship") challenger [Marlon Vera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlon_Vera "Marlon Vera") and [Charles Jourdain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Jourdain "Charles Jourdain") took place at the event. The bout was originally scheduled to take place at [UFC Fight Night: du Plessis vs. Usman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Fight_Night:_du_Plessis_vs._Usman "UFC Fight Night: du Plessis vs. Usman") in July, but was moved to this event for undisclosed reasons.

A heavyweight bout between former interim [UFC Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Heavyweight_Championship "UFC Heavyweight Championship") challenger [Curtis Blaydes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Blaydes "Curtis Blaydes") and former [LFA Heavyweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legacy_Fighting_Alliance#LFA_Heavyweight_Championship "Legacy Fighting Alliance") [Waldo Cortes-Acosta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waldo_Cortes-Acosta "Waldo Cortes-Acosta") was scheduled for the event. However, the bout actually took place one week prior at [UFC Fight Night: Silva vs. Delgado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Fight_Night:_Silva_vs._Delgado "UFC Fight Night: Silva vs. Delgado").

A lightweight rematch between former lightweight title challenger [Renato Moicano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renato_Moicano "Renato Moicano") and former [UFC Featherweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Featherweight_Championship "UFC Featherweight Championship") challenger [Brian Ortega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Ortega "Brian Ortega") was scheduled for the main card. However, days before the event, Ortega had to withdraw due to an eye injury, so Moicano was re-scheduled to headline [UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Fight_Night:_Moicano_vs._Nolan "UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Nolan") against [Tom Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Nolan_(fighter) "Tom Nolan (fighter)"). The pair previously met in a featherweight bout at [UFC 214](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_214 "UFC 214") in July 2017, where Ortega won via third‑round guillotine choke. They were previously expected to rematch at [UFC 326](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_326 "UFC 326") in March, but Ortega withdrew due to injury and Moicano was later moved to headline [UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Duncan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Fight_Night:_Moicano_vs._Duncan "UFC Fight Night: Moicano vs. Duncan") one month afterward.

## Bonus awards

In addition to the standard $100,000 Fight of the Night and Performance of the Night bonuses, UFC CEO [Dana White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana_White "Dana White") announced a one-time "Proper Chaos Bonus" of $50,000, tied to the season two premiere of the [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+") series [MobLand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MobLand "MobLand"), which aired the night before the event. The winner was determined at White's discretion.

The following fighters received $100,000 bonuses (during the post-fight press conference, White matched the "Proper Chaos Bonus" to $100,000). The other finishes received $25,000 additional bonuses.

* **Fight of the Night:** **Joshua Van vs. Alexandre Pantoja**
* **Performance of the Night:** **Arman Tsarukyan** and **Casey O'Neill**
* **"Proper Chaos Bonus": Sean Sharaf**
