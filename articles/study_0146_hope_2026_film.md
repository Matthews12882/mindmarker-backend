# Hope (2026 film)

***Hope*** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language "Korean language"): 호프) is a 2026 South Korean [epic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_film "Epic film") [science fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_film "Science fiction film") [monster film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_film "Monster film") written, directed, and co-produced by [Na Hong-jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Na_Hong-jin "Na Hong-jin"). Featuring an [ensemble cast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ensemble_cast "Ensemble cast") including [Hwang Jung-min](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_Jung-min "Hwang Jung-min"), [Zo In-sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zo_In-sung "Zo In-sung"), [Jung Ho-yeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jung_Ho-yeon "Jung Ho-yeon"), [Taylor Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Russell "Taylor Russell"), [Cameron Britton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Britton "Cameron Britton"), [Alicia Vikander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Vikander "Alicia Vikander"), and [Michael Fassbender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fassbender "Michael Fassbender"), the film centers on a village police chief and his rookie deputy who must defend his local community from a mysterious creature, while a local hunting party finds itself being hunted deep in the mountains.

*Hope* had its world premiere in the main competition of the [2026 Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cannes_Film_Festival "2026 Cannes Film Festival") on May 17, 2026, where it competed for the [Palme d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palme_d%27Or "Palme d'Or"). It was released theatrically by [Plus M Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plus_M_Entertainment "Plus M Entertainment") in South Korea on July 15. The film received generally positive reviews from critics.

## Plot

Ko Bum-seok is the police chief of Hope Harbor, a small village near the [Korean Demilitarized Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Demilitarized_Zone "Korean Demilitarized Zone") populated mostly by elderly residents. His second cousin, Ko Sung-ki, asks him to inspect a dead cow with gaping wounds. Sung-ki and his fellow hunters attributes it to a stray tiger, but soon find further dead cattle and the farm's owner also dead. Bum-seok returns to a Hope Harbor in distress as a large creature rampages across the village, massacring several villagers. With reinforcements tied up fighting wildfires elsewhere, he teams up with retired colonel Ahn Nak-yeon to track it down, but the pair mistakenly shoot and badly wound a local chef while searching a dilapidated building.

Continuing the search alone, Bum-seok learns from a local [militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militia "Militia") that the creature is in fact an intelligent, apparently indestructible [humanoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanoid "Humanoid"). After it wipes out the militia, rookie officer Im Sung-ae saves Bum-seok using a grenade launcher. Sung-ae manages to sever one of the creature's legs, and the two give chase by car. Seeing the creature crying, Bum-seok hesitates to finish it off; it is killed after being run over by a tanker.

A surgeon examines the body, noting that it is a [digitigrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digitigrade "Digitigrade") with similarities to human anatomy but also additional unknown organs. Bum-seok and Nak-yeon later encounter an eccentric hunter and would-be carpenter, Yang-bae, who claims to have killed a second creature and stored its corpse in his freezer. The body turns out to be a monster infant named Xinertai. Bum-seok realizes the adult creature had been searching for its offspring, and angrily blames Yang-bae for triggering the massacre. Meanwhile, while tending the wounded at a makeshift hospital, Sung-ae learns that there are several such creatures out there. She tracks down Bum-seok to warn him, and the two, along with Nak-yeon and Yang-bae, head out to look for Sung-ki.

Elsewhere, Sung-ki and his fellow hunters stumble upon a massive crashed [spaceship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") in the forest and realize the creatures are [aliens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraterrestrials_in_fiction "Extraterrestrials in fiction"). Inside, they find a nursery-like chamber but are ambushed by the alien Empress J'aur and her subordinates, Ma'veyyo and Ai'dovor. Everyone present is killed except Sung-ki, who faces off against Ma'veyyo and is nearly killed before Bum-seok's group arrives in time, allowing Sung-ki to escape on a horse belonging to a heavily armed group of local union members. Ma'veyyo pursues Sung-ki along the highway as Bum-seok and Sung-ae attack from their car. Sung-ki maneuvers himself onto the car and badly wounds Ma'veyyo using a grenade launcher. Celebrating as he hangs out the window, Sung-ki is accidentally swiped off the side of the vehicle.

Ma'veyyo rejoins J'aur, and together they watch as an allied spaceship belonging to Emperor Daiads falls from the sky and crashes into a nearby mountain. The resulting shockwave sends the police car off the road, forcing Bum-seok, Nak-yeon, Sung-ae and Yang-bae to press on by foot as they try to evade the aliens and reach Hope Harbor in time to raise the alarm. Meanwhile, J'aur and Ma'veyyo discuss their next move, where Ma'veyyo promises to find Xinertai and offers his own heart to revive him.

A mid-credits scene reveals that Sung-ki has survived and is seen limping along the highway.

## Production

### Development

Writer and director [Na Hong-jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Na_Hong-jin "Na Hong-jin") was reportedly originally collaborating on the project with [Alfonso Cuarón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Cuar%C3%B3n "Alfonso Cuarón"). It is Na Hong-jin's first since *[The Wailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wailing_(2016_film) "The Wailing (2016 film)")* in 2016. He has described how the film developed from an idea he had in 2017 or 2018 of a single image that came to him whilst eating in a restaurant. It is produced by Forged Films, with [Plus M Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plus_M_Entertainment "Plus M Entertainment") handling international sales. Cinematography is by [Hong Kyung-pyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kyung-pyo "Hong Kyung-pyo") who previously collaborated with Na on *The Wailing*. The budget for the film was reportedly higher than any previous Korean film.

### Casting

The main cast includes [Hwang Jung-min](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_Jung-min "Hwang Jung-min"), [Zo In-sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zo_In-sung "Zo In-sung"), [Hoyeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jung_Ho-yeon "Jung Ho-yeon"), as well as [Alicia Vikander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Vikander "Alicia Vikander") and [Michael Fassbender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fassbender "Michael Fassbender"), who do not speak Korean in the film. It marks the first on-screen collaboration for real-life married couple Vikander and Fassbender since the 2016 film *[The Light Between Oceans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Light_Between_Oceans_(film) "The Light Between Oceans (film)")*. In April 2023, [Taylor Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Russell "Taylor Russell") and [Cameron Britton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Britton "Cameron Britton") joined the main cast. The final cast includes [Eum Moon-suk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eum_Moon-suk "Eum Moon-suk"), Lee Sang-hee, and [Im Hyun-sik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Im_Hyun-sik "Im Hyun-sik") in supporting roles. Although [Um Tae-goo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Um_Tae-goo "Um Tae-goo") and [Lee Kyu-hyung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Kyu-hyung "Lee Kyu-hyung") were initially reported to be cast in May 2023, they do not appear in the final production and are absent from all official promotional materials and release credits.

### Filming

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") took place in 2023 in [Bukpyeong-myeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukpyeong-myeon "Bukpyeong-myeon"). Filming locations included Namchang in [Haenam County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haenam_County "Haenam County"). Filming also took place in Romania in the area around the [Retezat Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retezat_Mountains "Retezat Mountains"). A sequence shot with natural daylight involving a horse riding through a forest was filmed in [Retezat National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retezat_National_Park "Retezat National Park"). Vikander and Russell had completed their filming block by July 2024.

## Release

*Hope* had its world premiere at the main competition of the [79th Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/79th_Cannes_Film_Festival "79th Cannes Film Festival") on May 17, 2026, marking the first South Korean film to compete for the [Palme d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palme_d%27Or "Palme d'Or") since *[Decision to Leave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_to_Leave "Decision to Leave")* (2022). The selection makes it the fourth film by director [Na Hong-jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Na_Hong-jin "Na Hong-jin") to be screened at the festival, following *[The Chaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chaser_(2008_film) "The Chaser (2008 film)")* (2008) in Midnight Screenings, *[The Yellow Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Yellow_Sea_(film) "The Yellow Sea (film)")* (2011) in [Un Certain Regard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Un_Certain_Regard "Un Certain Regard") section, and *[The Wailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wailing_(2016_film) "The Wailing (2016 film)")* (2016) in the Out of Competition section.

*Hope* is slated for a July 15, 2026 release in South Korea. [Neon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neon_(company) "Neon (company)") scheduled its theatrical release in the United States for September 9, and also acquired distribution rights for other English-speaking territories. In May 2026, [Mubi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mubi_(streaming_service) "Mubi (streaming service)") acquired distribution rights in Latin America, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Italy, Spain and Turkey. Later that month, it was reported that *Hope* had pre-sold to 200 territories worldwide; among the buyers disclosed were [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features "Focus Features"), which bought rights for France, the Benelux and South Africa, as well as [Sony Pictures Worldwide Acquisitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Worldwide_Acquisitions "Sony Pictures Worldwide Acquisitions"), which acquired the film for Portugal, Scandinavia, Iceland, the Middle East and Israel.

The film had its North American Premiere in the Centerpiece Presentation section of the [25th New York Asian Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/25th_New_York_Asian_Film_Festival "25th New York Asian Film Festival") on 20 July 2026. It had its UK Premiere at the 79th [Edinburgh International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_International_Film_Festival "Edinburgh International Film Festival") on 16 August 2026.

## Reception

### Box office

On its opening weekend, *Hope* had 1.5 million admissions in South Korea, grossing $11.3 million at the box office, topping *[Minions & Monsters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minions_%26_Monsters "Minions & Monsters")* debut weekend ($2.1 million) and [*Moana*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moana_(2026_film) "Moana (2026 film)") second weekend ($1.2 million). By 2 August 2026, the film had grossed over $35 million at the local box office.

Upon its release in North America on September 9, 2026, the film grossed $1.1 million on its opening day, ranking second at the box office behind *[The Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Odyssey_(2026_film) "The Odyssey (2026 film)")*. It went on to gross $3.2 million in its opening weekend in North America, before falling to $1.0 million in its second weekend. In North America, it is the third-highest grossing Korean film of all time behind *[The King of Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King_of_Kings_(2025_film) "The King of Kings (2025 film)")* (2025) and *[Parasite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parasite_(2019_film) "Parasite (2019 film)")* (2019).

### Critical response

On [review aggregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregation "Review aggregation") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 79% of 174 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 7.1/10. The website’s consensus reads: "An unhinged sci-fi epic that boasts awe-inspiring production design and amusingly quaint visual effects, Na Hong-jin's *Hope* gets the adrenaline pumping whenever it focuses on the unraveling of its hapless human heroes." On [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), the film has a score of 67 out of 100, based on reviews from 37 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.

Nicholas Barber from [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") gave the movie four out of five stars.
