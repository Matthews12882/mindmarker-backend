# Emmy Rossum

**Emmanuelle Grey Rossum** (born September 12, 1986) is an American actress and singer. Her accolades include a [Critics' Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Awards "Critics' Choice Awards") and a [Saturn Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Awards "Saturn Awards"), in addition to nominations for a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globes "Golden Globes") and an [Independent Spirit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_Independent_Spirit_Awards "Film Independent Spirit Awards").

Born and raised in Manhattan's [Upper East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_East_Side "Upper East Side"), Rossum began professionally performing as a child with the [Metropolitan Opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Opera "Metropolitan Opera") before taking early film roles in *[Songcatcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songcatcher "Songcatcher")* (2000), *[Nola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nola_(film) "Nola (film)")* (2003), *[Mystic River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystic_River_(film) "Mystic River (film)")* (2003), and *[The Day After Tomorrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Day_After_Tomorrow "The Day After Tomorrow")* (2004). She received critical acclaim for portrayal of [Christine Daaé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christine_Daa%C3%A9 "Christine Daaé") in the film adaptation of the musical *[The Phantom of the Opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Phantom_of_the_Opera_(2004_film) "The Phantom of the Opera (2004 film)")* (2004) for which she was nominated for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy"). She has since acted in the films *[Poseidon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poseidon_(film) "Poseidon (film)")* (2006), *[Dragonball: Evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragonball_Evolution "Dragonball Evolution")* (2009), *[Inside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_(2011_film) "Inside (2011 film)")* (2011), *[Beautiful Creatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_Creatures_(2013_film) "Beautiful Creatures (2013 film)")* (2013), *[Comet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_(film) "Comet (film)")* (2014), *[You're Not You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You%27re_Not_You "You're Not You")* (2014), and *[Cold Pursuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Pursuit "Cold Pursuit")* (2019).

On television, Rossum is best known for her portrayal of [Fiona Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Shameless_(American_TV_series)_characters#Fiona_Gallagher "List of Shameless (American TV series) characters") in the [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network) "Showtime (TV network)") comedy-drama series *[Shameless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shameless_(American_TV_series) "Shameless (American TV series)")* (2011–2019). Since the mid-2010s, she has directed and produced television, including the [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_(streaming_service) "Peacock (streaming service)") series *[Angelyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelyne_(miniseries) "Angelyne (miniseries)")* (2022) in which she also starred. She has also taken roles in the [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") series *[The Crowded Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crowded_Room "The Crowded Room")* (2024) and the [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") series *[Furious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furious_(TV_series) "Furious (TV series)")* (2026).

On stage, she made her debut as [Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliet "Juliet") in a production of the [William Shakespeare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare "William Shakespeare") romance *[Romeo and Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeo_and_Juliet "Romeo and Juliet")* (2006) and has since acted [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") and on [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_(theatre) "Broadway (theatre)"). She has also released two albums *[Inside Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_Out_(Emmy_Rossum_album) "Inside Out (Emmy Rossum album)")* (2007) and *[Sentimental Journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentimental_Journey_(Emmy_Rossum_album) "Sentimental Journey (Emmy Rossum album)")* (2013).

## Early life and education

Emmanuelle Grey Rossum was born on September 12, 1986, in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"). She is the only child of Cheryl Rossum, a single mother who worked as a corporate photographer. Her parents separated while her mother was pregnant, and as of 2007, she had met her father only twice.

Rossum's mother is of [Russian Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Russia "History of the Jews in Russia") descent, and her father is a White Christian [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant "Protestant") of [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Americans "English Americans") and [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Americans "Dutch Americans") ancestry. Rossum identifies as Jewish and has stated that her mother instilled in her the "Jewish code of ethics and morals". She was named for her maternal great-grandfather, whose first name was Emanuel, using the feminine spelling.

Upon singing "[Happy Birthday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Birthday_to_You "Happy Birthday to You")" in all 12 keys, Rossum was welcomed to join the [Metropolitan Opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Opera "Metropolitan Opera") Children's Chorus by chorus director Elena Doria at age seven. Over the course of five years she sang onstage with the chorus and had the chance to perform with singers such as [Plácido Domingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pl%C3%A1cido_Domingo "Plácido Domingo") and [Luciano Pavarotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luciano_Pavarotti "Luciano Pavarotti"). For anywhere from $5 to $10 a night, Rossum sang in six languages in 20 operas, including *[La bohème](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_boh%C3%A8me "La bohème")*, *[Turandot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turandot "Turandot")*, a [Carnegie Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Hall "Carnegie Hall") presentation of *[La damnation de Faust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_damnation_de_Faust "La damnation de Faust")*, and *[A Midsummer Night's Dream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Midsummer_Night%27s_Dream_(opera) "A Midsummer Night's Dream (opera)")*. She also worked under the direction of [Franco Zeffirelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Zeffirelli "Franco Zeffirelli") in *[Carmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmen "Carmen")*.

An increasing interest in pursuing acting led to taking classes with Flo Salant Greenberg of [The New Actors Workshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Actors_Workshop "The New Actors Workshop") in New York City. She has also worked with acting coach Terry Knickerbocker.

Rossum attended the [Spence School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spence_School "Spence School"), a private school in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan "Manhattan"), before dropping out to pursue career opportunities. She received her high school diploma at 15 years old via online-extension courses offered by [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University")'s [Education Program for Gifted Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Program_for_Gifted_Youth "Education Program for Gifted Youth") (EPGY). She then enrolled at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University"), studying French, art history and philosophy.

## Career

### 1997–2008: Rise to prominence

Rossum's television debut was in August 1997, as the original [Abigail Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abigail_Williams_(As_the_World_Turns) "Abigail Williams (As the World Turns)") in the daytime soap opera *[As the World Turns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_the_World_Turns "As the World Turns")*. She also had a guest role as Caroline Beels in *[Snoops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snoops_(1999_TV_series) "Snoops (1999 TV series)")*. Rossum was nominated for a [Young Artist Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Artist_Award "Young Artist Award") in 1999 for Best Performance in a TV movie for her work in the Disney Channel Original Movie *[Genius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genius_(1999_film) "Genius (1999 film)")*. She then portrayed a young [Audrey Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn "Audrey Hepburn") in the [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") television film *[The Audrey Hepburn Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Audrey_Hepburn_Story "The Audrey Hepburn Story")* (2000).

Rossum made her film debut at 13, in 2000's *[Songcatcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songcatcher "Songcatcher")*, as Deladis Slocumb, an [Appalachian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia "Appalachia") orphan. Debuting at the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival"), the film won the Special Jury Award for Outstanding Ensemble Performance. For her role, Rossum received an [Independent Spirit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Spirit_Award "Independent Spirit Award") nomination for Best Debut Performance and also had the opportunity to sing a duet with [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton "Dolly Parton") on the *Songcatcher* soundtrack. *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)") magazine* named Rossum as "One of the Ten to Watch" in 2000. In *[Nola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nola_(film) "Nola (film)")* (2003), Rossum played the title character, an aspiring songwriter. In her first major studio film, [Clint Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Eastwood "Clint Eastwood")'s *[Mystic River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystic_River_(film) "Mystic River (film)")* (2003), Rossum starred as Katie Markum, the ill-fated daughter of small-business owner Jimmy Markum, played by [Sean Penn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Penn "Sean Penn").

Rossum had a breakthrough role as Laura Chapman in the sci-fi-disaster film *[The Day After Tomorrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Day_After_Tomorrow "The Day After Tomorrow")* (2004). She later returned to New York, where she was the last to audition, in full costume and make-up, for the role of [Christine Daaé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christine_Daa%C3%A9 "Christine Daaé") in *[The Phantom of the Opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Phantom_of_the_Opera_(2004_film) "The Phantom of the Opera (2004 film)")* (2004), the onscreen adaptation of composer [Andrew Lloyd Webber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Lloyd_Webber "Andrew Lloyd Webber")'s [musical of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Phantom_of_the_Opera_(1986_musical) "The Phantom of the Opera (1986 musical)"). After having nearly missed the audition due to a family engagement, Rossum was asked to audition in person for Lloyd Webber at his home in New York. For her performance, Rossum received a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award") nomination for Best Actress in a musical or comedy. She also received a [Critics' Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Awards "Critics' Choice Movie Awards") for Best Young Actress,[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] along with a [Saturn Award for Best Performance by a Younger Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Award_for_Best_Performance_by_a_Younger_Actor "Saturn Award for Best Performance by a Younger Actor").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

In 2006, Rossum appeared in *[Poseidon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poseidon_(film) "Poseidon (film)")*, [Wolfgang Petersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Petersen "Wolfgang Petersen")'s remake of the disaster film *[The Poseidon Adventure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Poseidon_Adventure_(1972_film) "The Poseidon Adventure (1972 film)")*. She played Jennifer Ramsey, the daughter of [Kurt Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Russell "Kurt Russell")'s character. Rossum described the character as being proactive and strong in all situations, rather than a [damsel in distress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damsel_in_distress "Damsel in distress"). Rossum also appeared as [Juliet Capulet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliet_Capulet "Juliet Capulet") in a 2006 [Williamstown Theatre Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamstown_Theatre_Festival "Williamstown Theatre Festival") production of [William Shakespeare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare "William Shakespeare")'s *[Romeo and Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeo_and_Juliet "Romeo and Juliet")*. In early 2009, Rossum appeared in the poorly received *[Dragonball Evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragonball_Evolution "Dragonball Evolution")*. Her next big screen venture was the indie *[Dare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dare_(film) "Dare (film)")*, which was an official selection of the 2009 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival"). In November 2009, Rossum appeared in Broadway's 24 Hour Plays in which actors, writers, and directors collaborate to produce, and perform six [one-act plays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-act_play "One-act play") within 24 hours to benefit the Urban Arts Partnership. Rossum appeared in [Warren Leight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Leight "Warren Leight")'s "Daily Bread", directed by Lucie Tiberghien.

### 2009–2019: *Shameless* and other roles

> Yes, why not? Sure. I mean, if the story is there, then it should absolutely be told.

— Rossum when asked if she would be interested in reprising her role of Fiona in a *Shameless* revival.

In December 2009, Rossum joined the cast of the [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network) "Showtime (TV network)") [dramedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dramedy "Dramedy") series *[Shameless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shameless_(American_TV_series) "Shameless (American TV series)")*, based on the British series of [the same title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shameless_(British_TV_series) "Shameless (British TV series)"). The series co-stars [William H. Macy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_H._Macy "William H. Macy"). Rossum plays the oldest sister of a large, motherless family, functioning as a guardian/surrogate mother to her five younger siblings. The series has received consistent acclaim, with Rossum's performance being universally praised. She made her directorial debut with episode four of season seven, "I Am a Storm." In December 2016, Rossum was in a contract dispute with the show's producers over her desire for a salary equal to that of co-star Macy and back pay for the differential over seven seasons, which was resolved later that month. Following this news, *Shameless* was renewed for its eighth season beginning production in 2017. In August 2018, Rossum announced her departure from *Shameless* after nine seasons.

In mid-2011, Rossum starred in [D. J. Caruso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._J._Caruso "D. J. Caruso")'s [social film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_film "Social film"), *[Inside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_(2011_film) "Inside (2011 film)")*; the online film aired in several segments, incorporating multiple social media platforms including Facebook, YouTube, and Twitter. The following year, Rossum played Ridley, a siren Caster in *[Beautiful Creatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_Creatures_(2013_film) "Beautiful Creatures (2013 film)")* (2013), based on the [young adult fantasy novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_adult_novel "Young adult novel") of the [same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_Creatures_(novel) "Beautiful Creatures (novel)"). Although the film was released to mixed reviews, Rossum's performance was praised by critics and fans.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] She also starred in the drama *[You're Not You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You%27re_Not_You "You're Not You")* as Bec, an inexperienced caregiver who looks after and befriends Kate ([Hilary Swank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_Swank "Hilary Swank")), who suffers from the degenerative disease [ALS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALS "ALS"). By June 2013, Rossum was cast as the female lead opposite [Justin Long](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Long "Justin Long") in the independent romantic comedy *[Comet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_(film) "Comet (film)")*, which was directed by [Sam Esmail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Esmail "Sam Esmail"), to whom she later became engaged. Afterward she appeared in the film *[Before I Disappear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_I_Disappear "Before I Disappear")* (2014), based on the [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award")-winning short film *[Curfew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curfew_(2012_film) "Curfew (2012 film)")*.

In 2019, it was announced that Rossum would star in *[Angelyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelyne_(miniseries) "Angelyne (miniseries)")*, a drama series co-produced by her husband and longtime collaborator [Sam Esmail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Esmail "Sam Esmail"), for [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_(streaming_service) "Peacock (streaming service)"), the new streaming service by [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC"). The [limited series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_series_(television) "Limited series (television)") was her first lead role after *Shameless* and was based on *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* senior writer Gary Baum's investigative feature on the LA billboard model [Angelyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelyne "Angelyne").

### 2020–present

In 2023, she acted in the [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") limited series *[The Crowded Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crowded_Room "The Crowded Room")* acting opposite [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland "Tom Holland") and [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried "Amanda Seyfried"). In 2024, Rossum starred alongside [Zoë Winters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zo%C3%AB_Winters "Zoë Winters") in an [Off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") staging of Amy Berryman's play *[Walden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walden_(play) "Walden (play)")*, directed by [Whitney White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_White_(director) "Whitney White (director)"). The play was the final production by [Second Stage Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Stage_Theater "Second Stage Theater") in the Tony Kiser Theater. In 2026, she starred as an [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI "FBI") agent investigating a serial killer in the [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") crime drama series *[Furious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furious_(TV_series) "Furious (TV series)")* which she also serves as an executive producer.

## Music career

After her role in *The Phantom of the Opera*, Rossum was offered several deals to record classical music albums, but refused, opting to create an album of contemporary, more mainstream music. She explained it by feeling frustrated because of the lack of "emotional honesty" in music played in radios. Regarding the sound and style of her music, she said, "It's pop music, but not [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears") [bubblegum pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubblegum_pop "Bubblegum pop"). I want it to have a [David Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Gray_(British_musician) "David Gray (British musician)") or [Annie Lennox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Lennox "Annie Lennox") feel. I've been spending up to 12 hours a day in the studio." Rossum cites [Sarah McLachlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_McLachlan "Sarah McLachlan"), [Whitney Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Houston "Whitney Houston"), [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion "Celine Dion") and [Faith Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faith_Hill "Faith Hill") as some of her influences.
Rossum's album *[Inside Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_Out_(Emmy_Rossum_album) "Inside Out (Emmy Rossum album)")* was produced by Stuart Brawley. It was released on October 23, 2007, and peaked at 199 in the U.S. [*Billboard* charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts"). For the promotion of the record, [Geffen Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geffen_Records "Geffen Records") featured the song, "Slow Me Down," as part of the second volume of [Hollywood Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Records "Hollywood Records")' *Girl Next* compilation album, which was released on July 10, 2007. Later that year, she was chosen as [Yahoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo "Yahoo")'s "Who's Next" artist of the month and a "One to Watch" by [MSN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSN "MSN"). In December 2007, Rossum released three Christmas songs on the EP *Carol of the Bells.* It was also this year that Rossum sang the national anthem at the Save Mart 350 NASCAR NEXTEL Cup Series race at Infineon Raceway and performed at the first ever [Perez Hilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perez_Hilton "Perez Hilton") Presents event at the [El Rey Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Rey_Theatre_(Los_Angeles) "El Rey Theatre (Los Angeles)") in Hollywood. On October 27, 2007, Rossum again sang the national anthem at the [New Jersey Devils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_Devils "New Jersey Devils")'s first home game of the [2007–08 NHL season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_NHL_season "2007–08 NHL season"), which was also the first game the team played in the newly constructed [Prudential Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prudential_Center "Prudential Center"). She also performed at the Hollywood Christmas Celebration at the Grove in Los Angeles and the Lighting of the Great Tree in Atlanta, Georgia.

She joined [Counting Crows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counting_Crows "Counting Crows"), [Augustana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustana_(band) "Augustana (band)"), and [Michael Franti & Spearhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Franti "Michael Franti") as a "special guest" for select performances of the "Traveling Circus and Medicine Show" tour in early 2009. In 2010, Rossum sang a song called "Cruel One" on singer [Alex Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Band "Alex Band")'s debut solo album *[We've All Been There](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We%27ve_All_Been_There "We've All Been There")*. On the track she sings with Band, and [Chantal Kreviazuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chantal_Kreviazuk "Chantal Kreviazuk"). Rossum released the [Warner Bros. Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Records "Warner Bros. Records") album *[Sentimental Journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentimental_Journey_(Emmy_Rossum_album) "Sentimental Journey (Emmy Rossum album)")* on January 29, 2013. Unlike *Inside Out* on which Rossum wrote the songs herself, the album is a collection of covers of songs from the 1920s to 1960s. *Sentimental Journey* peaked at 92 on the U.S. *Billboard* charts, and number one in Jazz Albums. Rossum describes herself as a [lyric soprano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyric_soprano "Lyric soprano"), while saying her voice is still developing. She continues to train vocally at ZajacStudio, a studio run by soprano Joann C. Zajac.

## Charity work and advocacy

Rossum was a [YouthAIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouthAIDS "YouthAIDS") ambassador. In 2008, she was signed as the spokesperson for Pinkitude, an apparel line inspired by the [Pink Panther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Panther_(character) "Pink Panther (character)") character, to help raise breast cancer awareness. In 2010, she appeared in public service announcements for the [Natural Resources Defense Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Resources_Defense_Council "Natural Resources Defense Council") (NRDC) Action Fund. She also works with [Global Green USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Green_USA "Global Green USA") to raise money for environmental protection and awareness of ecological issues. In 2015, Rossum campaigned with the [Best Friends Animal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Friends_Animal_Society "Best Friends Animal Society") to encourage animal adoption.

On May 26, 2009, Rossum attended a march in [West Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Hollywood "West Hollywood") California protesting the California Supreme Court's ruling to uphold [Proposition 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proposition_8 "Proposition 8").

In February 2024, Rossum was one of 400 celebrities who signed an open letter by [Creative Community for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Community_for_Peace "Creative Community for Peace") denouncing attempts to remove [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel "Israel") from Eurovision 2024 amid the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war").

## Personal life

Rossum married Justin Siegel in February 2008, though neither made any public comment about their marital status. Nineteen months later, in September 2009, he filed for divorce, citing irreconcilable differences. The divorce was finalized on December 28, 2010.

Rossum dated musician [Adam Duritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Duritz "Adam Duritz") from 2009 to 2010.

In 2013, Rossum began dating writer/director [Sam Esmail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Esmail "Sam Esmail"). They got engaged two years later, in August 2015. They married on May 29, 2017, at the [Central Synagogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Synagogue_(Manhattan) "Central Synagogue (Manhattan)") in New York City. The couple have two children: a daughter, born May 2021, and a son, born April 2023.

Rossum has [celiac disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celiac_disease "Celiac disease") and predominantly hyperactive [ADHD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ADHD "ADHD") and has been on medication for the latter since childhood.

The designer [Vera Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_Wang "Vera Wang") was married to Rossum's cousin, investor and real-estate developer [Arthur P. Becker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_P._Becker "Arthur P. Becker") from 1989 to 2012.

Rossum has described herself as "[a spiritual person, but not especially religious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritual_but_not_religious "Spiritual but not religious")."
