# Stuart Fails to Save the Universe

***Stuart Fails to Save the Universe*** is an American [science fiction comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_comedy "Science fiction comedy") television series created and written by [Chuck Lorre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Lorre "Chuck Lorre"), [Zak Penn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zak_Penn "Zak Penn"), and [Bill Prady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Prady "Bill Prady"). The series is the second direct [spin-off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin-off_(media) "Spin-off (media)") of the [sitcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitcom "Sitcom") *[The Big Bang Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory "The Big Bang Theory")*, the overall fourth television series in the [franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory#Franchise "The Big Bang Theory"), and stars [Kevin Sussman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Sussman "Kevin Sussman"), [Lauren Lapkus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Lapkus "Lauren Lapkus"), [Brian Posehn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Posehn "Brian Posehn"), and [John Ross Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Ross_Bowie "John Ross Bowie"). After accidentally damaging a device that [Sheldon Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon_Cooper "Sheldon Cooper"), [Leonard Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Hofstadter "Leonard Hofstadter") and [Howard Wolowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Wolowitz "Howard Wolowitz") created, [Stuart Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_Bloom "Stuart Bloom") is tasked with averting a resulting "multiverse armageddon" while encountering versions of himself and his friends ([Denise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denise_(The_Big_Bang_Theory) "Denise (The Big Bang Theory)"), [Bert Kibbler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bert_Kibbler "Bert Kibbler"), and [Barry Kripke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Kripke "Barry Kripke")) in different universes. The series is directed by [Kyle Newacheck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_Newacheck "Kyle Newacheck"), [Jonathan Frakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Frakes "Jonathan Frakes"), and [Robert Duncan McNeill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Duncan_McNeill "Robert Duncan McNeill").

*Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* premiered on [HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") on July 23, 2026. In August 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

## Production

### Development

On April 12, 2023, it was announced that an untitled *[The Big Bang Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory "The Big Bang Theory")* spin-off was in early development at [Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max"), created by *The Big Bang Theory* co-creator and executive producer, [Chuck Lorre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Lorre "Chuck Lorre"). The spin-off series was formally announced at the 2023 [Warner Bros. Discovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Discovery "Warner Bros. Discovery") [upfront](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upfront_(advertising) "Upfront (advertising)") presentation by [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") and Max CEO and chairman, Casey Bloys. Bloys did not mention any plot details or who the series would focus on from *The Big Bang Theory* series, but it was reported that the series would have a new cast, with former *The Big Bang Theory* cast members to appear in guest roles. Actor and director [Jonathan Frakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Frakes "Jonathan Frakes") is involved in the show. [Robert Duncan McNeill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Duncan_McNeill "Robert Duncan McNeill")
directs episodes.

In October 2024, it was reported that the spin-off would focus on [Stuart Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Big_Bang_Theory_franchise_characters#Stuart_Bloom "List of The Big Bang Theory franchise characters"), the owner of the comic book store, Comic Center, who appeared as a recurring supporting character in *The Big Bang Theory*. In March 2025, it was announced that the spin-off would be titled *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe*. In July 2025, [HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") officially gave *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* a series order. In February 2026, Lorre described the series as a "science fiction action-adventure comedy", and said of its creation, "I just wanted to do something that challenged me, that I was uncomfortable and in unfamiliar territory and the special effects stuff, the computer graphics, all those things. [...] Most of my career has been two people sit on a couch and talk."

In August 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Television "Warner Bros. Television") for the spin-off. In February 2025, it was announced that [John Ross Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Ross_Bowie "John Ross Bowie") would be reprising his role as [Barry Kripke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Kripke "Barry Kripke"), with Bowie also signing a talent holding deal with Warner Bros. ### Filming

The series began filming in September 2025. Filming on the first season concluded in February 2026.

## Release

The series premiered on [HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") on July 23, 2026, with a new episode debuting on a weekly basis.

## Reception

### Critical response

On the review aggregator website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), the series holds an approval rating of 78%, based on 43 reviews, with an average of rated reviews of 6.8/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "*Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* manages to brighten up its franchise through a genre-bending all-things-go sitcom that has the comedic chops to satisfy." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 62 out of 100 based on 19 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.

### Title sequence

*Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* received attention for its [title sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_sequence "Title sequence"), which begins by [breaking the fourth wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_the_fourth_wall "Breaking the fourth wall") and asking the viewer to not use the "Skip Titles" function. The visuals of the title sequence change with each episode, and each episode features a new [arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrangement "Arrangement") of the [theme song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theme_song "Theme song") by composer [Danny Elfman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Elfman "Danny Elfman"). Series creator Chuck Lorre stated that the title sequence was created this way to encourage viewers to watch it before every episode, due to his distaste for modern streaming services allowing viewers to skip intro sequences.

Writing for *[Kotaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotaku "Kotaku")*, Kenneth Shepard notes that the title sequence's visuals appeared to be possibly [AI-generated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI-generated "AI-generated"). The show's creators have not said whether the title sequence is AI or not.
