# Tom Pelphrey

**Thomas Joseph Pelphrey** (born July 28, 1982) is an American actor.
He is known for playing the roles of Jonathan Randall on the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") television series *[Guiding Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiding_Light "Guiding Light")* (for which he won two [Daytime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Awards "Daytime Emmy Awards")), Mick Dante on *[As the World Turns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_the_World_Turns "As the World Turns")*, Kurt Bunker on *[Banshee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banshee_(TV_series) "Banshee (TV series)")*, Ward Meachum on *[Iron Fist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Fist_(TV_series) "Iron Fist (TV series)")*, Ben Davis on *[Ozark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_(TV_series) "Ozark (TV series)")*, Perry Abbott on *[Outer Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Range "Outer Range")*, Don Crowder on *[Love & Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_%26_Death_(miniseries) "Love & Death (miniseries)")*, Robbie Prendergrast on *[Task](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_(TV_series) "Task (TV series)")*, [Joe Mankiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Mankiewicz "Joe Mankiewicz") in [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher "David Fincher")'s film *[Mank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mank "Mank")*, and [Jason Derek Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Derek_Brown "Jason Derek Brown") in the [true crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_crime "True crime") film *[American Murderer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Murderer "American Murderer")*. For *Ozark*, he received an Emmy nomination for [Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series"). He received his second Primetime Emmy nomination and won for [Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series") in 2026 for his role in *Task*.

## Early life and education

Pelphrey was born Thomas Joseph Pelphrey on July 28, 1982, in [Howell Township, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howell_Township,_New_Jersey "Howell Township, New Jersey"), to Laurie (née Demgard) and Richard Pelphrey (1950–2007). He has one brother named Robert. He graduated from [Howell High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howell_High_School_(New_Jersey) "Howell High School (New Jersey)") in 2000, and from [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University "Rutgers University") ([Mason Gross School of the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_Gross_School_of_the_Arts "Mason Gross School of the Arts")) in 2004 with a [Bachelor of Fine Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Fine_Arts "Bachelor of Fine Arts") degree.

## Career

### *Guiding Light*

Pelphrey's first notable role was on the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") [soap opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap_opera "Soap opera") *[Guiding Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiding_Light "Guiding Light")* as Jonathan Randall, the son of [Reva Shayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reva_Shayne "Reva Shayne") and her late former husband, Richard Winslow. He joined the cast in October 2003 and received critical acclaim for his portrayal of anti-hero Jonathan.

He was nominated for a [Daytime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Award "Daytime Emmy Award") for "Outstanding Younger Actor" in 2005 and won the award the following year. He received a third consecutive nomination in the same category in 2007 but lost to [Bryton McClure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryton_McClure "Bryton McClure"). Pelphrey won his second [Daytime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Award "Daytime Emmy Award") for "Outstanding Younger Actor" in 2008.

### *As the World Turns*

Pelphrey played Mick Dante on *[As the World Turns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_the_World_Turns "As the World Turns")* from October 2009 to February 2010 when his character was sent to prison for his misdeeds. The role earned Pelphrey an Emmy pre-nomination for Best Supporting Actor.

### Other television and film roles

On March 30, 2007, he appeared in an episode of *[Numb3rs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numbers_(TV_series) "Numbers (TV series)")*, "Pandora's Box". On October 17, 2008, he appeared in an episode of "Ghost Whisperer", and the following season in an episode of *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit")*. In 2015 Pelphrey landed the recurring role of Kurt Bunker, a former neo-Nazi trying to reintegrate into society, on [Cinemax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinemax "Cinemax")'s action drama *[Banshee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banshee_(TV_series) "Banshee (TV series)")*. To prepare for this role he started lifting weights and did research by reading books on the neo-Nazi movement in America. In 2017, he appeared in *[Iron Fist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Fist_(TV_series) "Iron Fist (TV series)")* as [Ward Meachum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_Meachum "Ward Meachum") and portrayed Ben Davis on the third season of the Netflix crime drama *[Ozark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_(TV_series) "Ozark (TV series)")* which was released on March 27, 2020. His portrayal in the latter received critical acclaim and the lack of an Emmy nomination was perceived as a snub by fans and critics. Nonetheless, Pelphrey received nominations for [Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Supporting Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Supporting Actor in a Drama Series"), [Satellite Award for Best Supporting Actor – Series, Miniseries or Television Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_%E2%80%93_Series,_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Satellite Award for Best Supporting Actor – Series, Miniseries or Television Film") and [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by an Ensemble in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_an_Ensemble_in_a_Drama_Series "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by an Ensemble in a Drama Series") (shared with the cast). Pelphrey portrayed [Joe Mankiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Mankiewicz "Joe Mankiewicz") in [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher "David Fincher")'s 2020 film, *[Mank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mank "Mank")* and [Jason Derek Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Derek_Brown "Jason Derek Brown") in the 2022 [true crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_crime "True crime") film, *[American Murderer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Murderer "American Murderer")*. In the final season of *Ozark* (2022), Pelphrey reprised the role of Ben Davis as a guest appearance, for which he received an Emmy nomination for [Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series").

In 2022, he starred on the [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") series *[Outer Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Range "Outer Range")* as Perry Abbott.

In 2023, he starred on the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") miniseries *[Love & Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_%26_Death_(miniseries) "Love & Death (miniseries)")* as Don Crowder. Pelphrey won Best Actor at the Bucharest Film Awards and the Manchester International Film Festival for his performance in the 2023 film, *Jill*. He was nominated and won an Emmy for the 2026 [Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Supporting Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Supporting Actor in a Drama Series") for his performance in *[Task](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_(TV_series) "Task (TV series)")*.

### Theater

From September 6, 2007, to September 30, 2007, Pelphrey performed in Kevin Mandel's *A New Television Arrives Finally* as the title character, at Theatre 54, in New York. Pelphrey starred in *In God's Hat* at the Peter Jay Sharp Theater, in New York City, through August 7, 2010. In 2012, he appeared in the Broadway cast of the musical drama *[End of the Rainbow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_the_Rainbow "End of the Rainbow")* as Mickey Deans; in 2015, he returned to Broadway in a revival of *[Fool for Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fool_for_Love_(play) "Fool for Love (play)")*, playing Martin.

## Personal life

In 2022, Pelphrey began dating actress [Kaley Cuoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaley_Cuoco "Kaley Cuoco"). The two made their first public appearance as a couple at a [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame") ceremony in May 2022. Pelphrey and Cuoco announced on Instagram in October 2022 that they were expecting their first child together. Cuoco gave birth to their daughter on March 30, 2023. They announced their engagement on August 14, 2024. The couple announced in July 2026 that they are expecting their second child.

Pelphrey is a fan of the [New York Giants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Giants "New York Giants").
