# Woody Harrelson

**Woodrow Tracy Harrelson** (born July 23, 1961) is an American actor. He first became known for his role as charmingly naive bartender [Woody Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Boyd "Woody Boyd") on the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") sitcom *[Cheers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheers "Cheers")* (1985–1993), for which he won a [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Comedy Series") from five nominations. Harrelson has received three [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award") nominations: [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor") for *[The People vs. Larry Flynt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_People_vs._Larry_Flynt "The People vs. Larry Flynt")* (1996), and [Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") for *[The Messenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Messenger_(2009_film) "The Messenger (2009 film)")* (2009) and *[Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing,_Missouri "Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri")* (2017).

Other notable films include *[White Men Can't Jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Men_Can%27t_Jump "White Men Can't Jump")* (1992), *[Natural Born Killers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Born_Killers "Natural Born Killers")* (1994), *[The Thin Red Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thin_Red_Line_(1998_film) "The Thin Red Line (1998 film)")* (1998), *[No Country for Old Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Country_for_Old_Men "No Country for Old Men")* (2007), *[Seven Pounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Pounds "Seven Pounds")* (2008), *[Zombieland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland "Zombieland")* (2009), *[2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_(film) "2012 (film)")* (2009), *[Seven Psychopaths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Psychopaths "Seven Psychopaths")* (2012), *[The Edge of Seventeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Edge_of_Seventeen "The Edge of Seventeen")* (2016), *[War for the Planet of the Apes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_for_the_Planet_of_the_Apes "War for the Planet of the Apes")* (2017), *[Venom: Let There Be Carnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venom:_Let_There_Be_Carnage "Venom: Let There Be Carnage")* (2021), *[Triangle of Sadness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_of_Sadness "Triangle of Sadness")* (2022), and *[Suncoast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suncoast_(film) "Suncoast (film)")* (2024). He also played [Haymitch Abernathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Hunger_Games_characters "List of The Hunger Games characters") in [*The Hunger Games* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film_series) "The Hunger Games (film series)") (2012–2015), Chase McKinney / Merritt McKinney in the [*Now You See Me* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_You_See_Me_(film_series) "Now You See Me (film series)") (2013–), and [Tobias Beckett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobias_Beckett "Tobias Beckett") in *[Solo: A Star Wars Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo:_A_Star_Wars_Story "Solo: A Star Wars Story")* (2018).

Harrelson received further [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award") nominations for his portrayal of [Steve Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Schmidt "Steve Schmidt") in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") film *[Game Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Change_(film) "Game Change (film)")* (2012) and detective Marty Hart in the HBO crime anthology series *[True Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective "True Detective")* (2014). He also portrayed [E. Howard Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._Howard_Hunt "E. Howard Hunt") in the HBO political limited series *[White House Plumbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Plumbers_(miniseries) "White House Plumbers (miniseries)")* (2023) and a fictionalized version of himself in the [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_(streaming_service) "Apple TV (streaming service)") comedy series *[Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_(2026_TV_series) "Brothers (2026 TV series)")* (2026).

## Early life and education

Woodrow Tracy Harrelson was born on July 23, 1961, in [Midland, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland,_Texas "Midland, Texas"), to secretary Diane Lou and convicted [contract killer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitman "Hitman") [Charles Voyde Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Harrelson "Charles Harrelson"). He was raised in a [Presbyterian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian "Presbyterian") household alongside his two brothers, Jordan and [Brett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Harrelson "Brett Harrelson"), the latter of whom also became an actor. Their father received a life sentence for the 1979 killing of federal judge [John H. Wood Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_H._Wood_Jr. "John H. Wood Jr.") Harrelson has stated he had little contact with his father during childhood. Charles died in the [United States Penitentiary, Administrative Maximum Facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ADX_Florence "ADX Florence") on March 15, 2007.

Harrelson's family was poor and relied on his mother's wages. He attended [The Briarwood School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Briarwood_School_(Houston,_Texas) "The Briarwood School (Houston, Texas)") in Houston, Texas. In 1973, he moved to his mother's native city of [Lebanon, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon,_Ohio "Lebanon, Ohio"), where he attended [Lebanon High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon_High_School_(Ohio) "Lebanon High School (Ohio)"), from which he graduated in 1979.

Harrelson attended [Hanover College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover_College "Hanover College") in [Hanover, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover,_Indiana "Hanover, Indiana"), where he studied theater and English. While there, he was a member of the [Sigma Chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Chi "Sigma Chi") fraternity and became friends with future vice president [Mike Pence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Pence "Mike Pence"). He graduated in 1983.

## Career

### 1985–1993: *Cheers* and early film roles

Harrelson is widely known for his work on the NBC sitcom *[Cheers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheers "Cheers")*. He played bartender [Woody Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Boyd "Woody Boyd"), joining the cast in 1985 in season four, spending the final eight seasons (1985–1993) on the show. For this role, Harrelson was nominated for five [Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award "Emmy Award"), winning once in 1989.

While still working on *Cheers*, Harrelson began his film career. His first movie was *[Wildcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildcats_(film) "Wildcats (film)")*, a 1986 football comedy with [Goldie Hawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldie_Hawn "Goldie Hawn"). He followed his performance in *Wildcats* with the 1990 romantic comedy *[Cool Blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cool_Blue "Cool Blue")*, alongside [Hank Azaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Azaria "Hank Azaria"). He reunited with [Wesley Snipes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Snipes "Wesley Snipes") (who also had debuted in *Wildcats*) in the box-office hit *[White Men Can't Jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Men_Can%27t_Jump "White Men Can't Jump")* (1992) and the action movie *[Money Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_Train "Money Train")* (1995). In 1993, Harrelson starred opposite [Robert Redford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Redford "Robert Redford") and [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore "Demi Moore") in the drama *[Indecent Proposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indecent_Proposal "Indecent Proposal")*, which was a box office success, earning a worldwide total of over $265,000,000. He then played Mickey Knox in [Oliver Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Stone "Oliver Stone")'s *[Natural Born Killers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Born_Killers "Natural Born Killers")* and Dr. Michael Raynolds in the [Michael Cimino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Cimino "Michael Cimino") film *[The Sunchaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunchaser "The Sunchaser")*.

### 1994–2011: Established actor

In 2003, he co-starred as Galaxia/Gary in the comedy film *[Anger Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anger_Management_(film) "Anger Management (film)")*. He appeared in the action film *[After the Sunset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After_the_Sunset "After the Sunset")* and the Spike Lee film *[She Hate Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_Hate_Me "She Hate Me")*. In 2005, Harrelson was in *[The Big White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_White "The Big White")* and *[North Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Country_(film) "North Country (film)")*. Also in 2005 he appeared as Kelly Ryan, husband of a contest-obsessed woman in the film *[The Prize Winner of Defiance, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prize_Winner_of_Defiance,_Ohio "The Prize Winner of Defiance, Ohio")*. In the winter of 2005–2006 Harrelson returned to London's West End, starring in Tennessee Williams' *Night of the Iguana* at the [Lyric Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyric_Theatre_(London) "Lyric Theatre (London)"). Harrelson directed *Bullet for Adolf* (a play he wrote with Frankie Hyman) at the esteemed [Hart House Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hart_House_Theatre "Hart House Theatre") in Toronto, Ontario, which ran from April 21 to May 7, 2011. *Bullet for Adolf* opened Off-Broadway (New World Stages) with previews beginning July 19, 2012, and closed on September 30, 2012, canceling its announced extension through October 21. The play was panned by New York critics. Harrelson made two films in 2006, the animated film version of *[Free Jimmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Jimmy "Free Jimmy")* and also *[A Scanner Darkly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Scanner_Darkly_(film) "A Scanner Darkly (film)")* the latter being written and directed by [Richard Linklater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Linklater "Richard Linklater").

In June 6, 2010, Harrelson took part playing in [Soccer Aid 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_Aid#2010:_Series_3 "Soccer Aid") for [UNICEF UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF_UK "UNICEF UK") at [Old Trafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trafford "Old Trafford") in Manchester. The match was broadcast live on UK's [ITV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITV_(TV_network) "ITV (TV network)") television. After being brought on as a substitute for [Gordon Ramsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Ramsay "Gordon Ramsay"), Harrelson took the final penalty in the [penalty shootout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shootout_(association_football) "Penalty shootout (association football)"), following a 2–2 draw after 91.2 minutes.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Despite being initially unaware of exactly from where his kick had to be taken, Harrelson scored to win the game for "The Rest of the World" team, beating England for the first time since the tournament began. When later interviewed, he claimed that he "didn't even remember the moment of scoring." In 2011, he starred as Tommy in the movie *[Friends with Benefits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends_with_Benefits_(film) "Friends with Benefits (film)")*. Harrelson narrated the 2011 film *ETHOS*, which explores the idea of a self-destructing modern society, governed by unequal power and failed democratic ideals. Harrelson also took part in [Soccer Aid 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_Aid#2012:_Series_4 "Soccer Aid") on May 27, 2012. The match ended 3–1 in favor of England.

### 2012–present: Career expansion and acclaim

He played [Haymitch Abernathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy "Haymitch Abernathy") in 2012's *[The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film) "The Hunger Games (film)")*, and reprised the role in [all three subsequent films in the series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film_series) "The Hunger Games (film series)") which ended in 2015. In 2012, he had a leading role in the acclaimed [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") television film *[Game Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Change_(film) "Game Change (film)")* as republican strategist [Steve Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Schmidt "Steve Schmidt") working for the 2008 Presidential campaign for [John McCain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCain "John McCain") and [Sarah Palin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Palin "Sarah Palin"). Harrelson acted alongside [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore "Julianne Moore"), [Sarah Paulson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Paulson "Sarah Paulson"), and [Ed Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Harris "Ed Harris"). The film was directed by [Jay Roach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Roach "Jay Roach") and written by [Danny Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Strong "Danny Strong"). For his performance he was nominated for numerous accolades including the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie"), the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Miniseries or Television Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Miniseries or Television Film"), and the [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Miniseries or Television Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Miniseries_or_Television_Movie "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Miniseries or Television Movie"). On February 3, 2012, he participated in an "Ask Me Anything" session on the website [Reddit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddit "Reddit"). The AMA turned into a PR disaster when Harrelson failed to make meaningful responses to any questions and soon specifically refused to respond to anything not directly related to the then-upcoming worldwide release of the crime drama *[Rampart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rampart_(film) "Rampart (film)")* (2011), in which he starred and received a nomination for the [Independent Spirit Award for Best Male Lead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Spirit_Award_for_Best_Male_Lead "Independent Spirit Award for Best Male Lead").

Harrelson returned to television in 2014, starring along with [Matthew McConaughey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_McConaughey "Matthew McConaughey") in the first season of the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") crime series *[True Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective "True Detective")*, where he played Marty Hart, a Louisiana cop investigating murders that took place over a timespan of 17 years. He and McConaughey received nominations for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series") losing to [Bryan Cranston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Cranston "Bryan Cranston") for the final season of *[Breaking Bad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad "Breaking Bad")*. In 2015, Woody Harrelson and daughter Zoe starred in a 7-minute short film for [U2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U2 "U2")'s 'Song for Someone.' In 2016, Harrelson announced that he would direct, write, produce, and star in a film, *[Lost in London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_in_London "Lost in London")*, which was shot as a single [take](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take "Take") and premiered live on January 19, 2017. Harrelson played police chief Bill Willoughby in the black comedy crime film *[Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing,_Missouri "Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri")*, released in 2017, for which he received nominations for an [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") and a [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Supporting_Role "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Supporting Role"). In 2017, he played the antagonist The Colonel in the science fiction film *[War for the Planet of the Apes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_for_the_Planet_of_the_Apes "War for the Planet of the Apes")*. Also that year, he starred in comedic drama film *[The Glass Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Glass_Castle_(2017_film) "The Glass Castle (2017 film)"),* an adaptation of [Jeannette Walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeannette_Walls "Jeannette Walls")'s memoir.

In 2018, Harrelson played [Tobias Beckett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobias_Beckett "Tobias Beckett"), a criminal and [Han Solo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo")'s mentor in [Lucasfilm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucasfilm "Lucasfilm")'s *[Solo: A Star Wars Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo:_A_Star_Wars_Story "Solo: A Star Wars Story").* In 2018, Harrelson appeared in a cameo at the end of the film *[Venom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venom_(2018_film) "Venom (2018 film)")*, portraying [Cletus Kasady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cletus_Kasady "Cletus Kasady"), and he reprised the role as the main antagonist, also voicing [Carnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnage_(character) "Carnage (character)") in the 2021 sequel *[Venom: Let There Be Carnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venom:_Let_There_Be_Carnage "Venom: Let There Be Carnage").* In 2019, he starred with Kevin Costner in *[The Highwaymen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Highwaymen_(film) "The Highwaymen (film)")*. In November 2019, he starred in [Roland Emmerich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_Emmerich "Roland Emmerich")'s blockbuster movie *[Midway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midway_(2019_film) "Midway (2019 film)")*, playing Admiral Chester Nimitz. The same year he reprised his role of Tallahassee in *[Zombieland 2: Double Tap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland_2:_Double_Tap "Zombieland 2: Double Tap")* (2019). Starting in 2019, Harrelson made numerous appearances portraying [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden") on *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")*, before being replaced by [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey "Jim Carrey").

In March 2021, he is set to portray [Felix Kersten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Kersten "Felix Kersten") in *The Man with the Miraculous Hands*, the feature film adaptation of [Joseph Kessel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kessel "Joseph Kessel")'s 1960 novel of the same title. Harrelson starred in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") political limited series *[White House Plumbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Plumbers_(miniseries) "White House Plumbers (miniseries)")* (2023) where he portrayed intelligence officer [E. Howard Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._Howard_Hunt "E. Howard Hunt"). The series is focused on the [Watergate scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watergate_scandal "Watergate scandal") and investigation. Harrelson also executive produced the series. Also in 2023, he starred in *[Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champions_(2023_film) "Champions (2023 film)")*, as a disgraced coach coerced to head up a Special Olympics basketball team, featuring many actors with special needs.

## Personal life

In 1985, Harrelson married Nancy Simon (daughter of playwright [Neil Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Simon "Neil Simon")) in [Tijuana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tijuana "Tijuana"), Mexico. The union was not intended to be serious, and the two had planned to divorce the following day, but the storefront marriage/divorce parlor was closed when they returned to it and they remained married for another ten months.

In 2008, he married Laura Louie, a co-founder of the [organic food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organic_food "Organic food") delivery service Yoganics. They met in 1987 when she worked as his personal assistant. They reside in [Maui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maui "Maui"), Hawaii, and have three daughters (born in 1993, 1996, and 2006).

Harrelson was awarded an honorary [Doctor of Humane Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Humane_Letters "Doctor of Humane Letters") (DHL) from [Hanover College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover_College "Hanover College") in 2014.

Harrelson is a fan of chess. In November 2018, he attended the first game of the [World Chess Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Chess_Championship_2018 "World Chess Championship 2018") in London, played between Norwegian champion [Magnus Carlsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnus_Carlsen "Magnus Carlsen") and American contender [Fabiano Caruana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabiano_Caruana "Fabiano Caruana"). He made the ceremonial first move for the game. He had also played the ceremonial first move for the previous [World Chess Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Chess_Championship_2016 "World Chess Championship 2016"), held in New York City in 2016.

In 1999 in Prague, Woody Harrelson, playing White, employed the [Parham Attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parham_Attack "Parham Attack"), named after Bernard Parham, to draw World Chess Champion [Garry Kasparov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garry_Kasparov "Garry Kasparov"). However, Harrelson was aided by several chess Grandmasters who were in Prague to spectate the chess match between GM [Alexei Shirov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Shirov "Alexei Shirov") and GM [Judit Polgár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judit_Polg%C3%A1r "Judit Polgár").

In 2020, Harrelson was seen practicing [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu "Brazilian jiu-jitsu") while filming, having received the [first stripe on his white belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu_ranking_system "Brazilian jiu-jitsu ranking system").

Harrelson is a fan of the [Cincinnati Bengals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati_Bengals "Cincinnati Bengals").

In 2023, Harrelson's longtime friend and *True Detective* co-star/co-executive producer [Matthew McConaughey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_McConaughey "Matthew McConaughey") stated that he and Harrelson could potentially be half-brothers. McConaughey's mother claimed to have been intimate with Harrelson's father, Charles Harrelson, around the time of McConaughey's conception.

### Lifestyle and views

Harrelson follows a [raw vegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_veganism "Raw veganism") diet. He does not eat meat, dairy, sugar or flour. In *[Zombieland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland "Zombieland")*, in which he plays a character with an affinity for [Twinkies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twinkies "Twinkies"), the Twinkies were replaced with vegan faux-Twinkies made from [cornmeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornmeal "Cornmeal"). He appeared on a postage stamp (as a PhotoStamp) in 2011 as one of [PETA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_for_the_Ethical_Treatment_of_Animals "People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals")'s 20 famous vegetarians, and he was named PETA's Sexiest Vegetarian in 2012 (along with [Jessica Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Chastain "Jessica Chastain")).

Harrelson was a religious [Presbyterian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterian "Presbyterian") as a child, and studied theology during college. Harrelson told *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy "Playboy")* in October 2009, "I was getting into theology and studying the roots of the Bible, but then I started to discover the man-made nature of it. I started seeing things that made me ask, 'Is God really speaking through this instrument?' My eyes opened to the reality of the Bible being just a document to control people." However, he later described himself as having "a strong spiritual life" and said he believed in God.

In April 2020, Harrelson made a post on Instagram promoting [the conspiracy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G_Corona "5G Corona") that claims a link between [5G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G "5G") networks and [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19 "COVID-19"), which was later deleted. In May 2022, Harrelson said he "doesn't believe in the [germ theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germ_theory "Germ theory")" and found the use of [face masks as a preventative measure against COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_masks_during_the_COVID-19_pandemic "Face masks during the COVID-19 pandemic") to be "absurd", adding, "I'm sick of like, you're wearing a mask, and you think it contains your breath—but if it did you'd die, you'd be breathing in your own [carbon monoxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_monoxide "Carbon monoxide") [*[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic "Sic")*]."

### Legal issues

On June 1, 1996, Harrelson was arrested in [Lee County, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_County,_Kentucky "Lee County, Kentucky"), after symbolically planting four [hemp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemp "Hemp") seeds to challenge the state law that did not distinguish between industrial hemp and marijuana. Harrelson had arrived in the county with his attorney, former Kentucky governor [Louie B. Nunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louie_B._Nunn "Louie B. Nunn"), an agent and a camera crew from [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN"). While at a local hotel, Harrelson phoned the county sheriff, Junior Kilburn, to advise him of his intentions. Kilburn and Deputy Sheriff Danny Towsend arrived at the location where Harrelson informed them he would be. With the cameras rolling, Harrelson planted the hemp seeds into the ground. Once planted, Kilburn placed Harrelson under arrest for cultivating [marijuana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis "Cannabis") and booked him into the county jail. He was released on $200 bail the same day. He later signed autographs and posed for photos with deputies. He was acquitted of those charges with the help of Nunn after just 25 minutes. The events were featured in the 2010 Michael P. Henning documentary film *[Hempsters: Plant the Seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hempsters:_Plant_the_Seed "Hempsters: Plant the Seed")*.

In 2002, Harrelson was arrested in London after an incident in a taxi that ended in a police chase. Harrelson was taken to a London police station and later released on bail. The case was later dismissed after Harrelson paid the taxi driver involved in the incident £550 ($844). This became the inspiration for his 2017 live-streamed film *[Lost in London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_in_London "Lost in London")*.

In 2008, *[TMZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMZ_(website) "TMZ (website)")* photographer Josh Levine filed a lawsuit against Harrelson for an alleged attack outside a Hollywood nightclub in 2006. A video of the incident appeared to show Harrelson grabbing a camera and clashing with the photographer. Los Angeles prosecutors declined to press charges against the actor, but Levine filed a suit that summer asking for $2.5 million in damages. The case was dismissed in April 2010.

## Other ventures

### Business

On May 13, 2022, Harrelson opened The Woods WeHo—a cannabis [dispensary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dispensary "Dispensary") off [Santa Monica Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica_Boulevard "Santa Monica Boulevard") in Los Angeles, California.

In September 2023, Harrelson announced the launch of his co-founded The Holistic Spirits Company, a plant-based spirits brand based in Utah.

### Activism

Harrelson is an enthusiast and supporter of the legalization of [marijuana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_(drug) "Cannabis (drug)") and [hemp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemp "Hemp"). He was a guest on [Ziggy Marley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziggy_Marley "Ziggy Marley")'s track "Wild and Free", a song advocating the growing of cannabis. Since 2003, Harrelson has served as a member on [NORML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORML "NORML")'s advisory board.

Harrelson has attended environmental events such as the PICNIC'07 festival that was held in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam "Amsterdam") in September 2007. PICNIC describes its annual festival as "three intensive days [when] we mix creativity, science, technology, media, and business to explore new solutions in the spirit of co-creation". He once scaled the [Golden Gate Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Gate_Bridge "Golden Gate Bridge") with members of North Coast [Earth First!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth_First! "Earth First!") group to unfurl a banner that read, "Hurwitz, aren't ancient redwoods more precious than gold?" in protest of Maxxam Inc. CEO [Charles Hurwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Hurwitz "Charles Hurwitz"), who once stated, "He who has the gold, makes the rules."

Harrelson once traveled to the west coast in the U.S. on a bike and a domino caravan with a hemp oil-fueled [biodiesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiesel "Biodiesel") bus with the Spitfire Agency (the subject of the independent documentary *[Go Further](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go_Further "Go Further")*) and narrated the 1999 documentary [*Grass*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grass_(1999_film) "Grass (1999 film)"). He briefly owned an [oxygen bar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_bar "Oxygen bar") in [West Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Hollywood,_California "West Hollywood, California") called "O2".[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

Harrelson has spoken publicly against the [2003 invasion of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_invasion_of_Iraq "2003 invasion of Iraq") as well as previously protesting against the [First Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War "Gulf War"), both at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA "UCLA") as well as during a college concert tour in Iowa and Nebraska in 1991 under the auspices of "Woody Harrelson Educational Tours". In October 2009, he was given an honorary degree by [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University "York University") for his contributions in the fields of environmental education, sustainability, and activism.

In June 2010, Harrelson took part in [Soccer Aid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_Aid "Soccer Aid") at [Old Trafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trafford "Old Trafford") in Manchester to raise money for [UNICEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF "UNICEF"). Harrelson played the last 15 minutes and scored the winning goal in the penalty shootout following a 2–2 draw during normal time. He played in the UNICEF game 2012, playing the last 10 minutes of the game for the Rest of the World team, losing 3–1 to England.

### Politics

Harrelson describes himself as an anarchist. In a conversation with [Howard Zinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Zinn "Howard Zinn"), Harrelson stated that he considers Zinn to be a personal hero of his. In 2002, Harrelson wrote an article in the British newspaper *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* condemning President [George W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush "George W. Bush")'s preparation for a [US invasion of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_War "Iraq War") as a "racist and imperialist war". He also stated that he was against the U.S.'s previous [war in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War "Gulf War") and President [Bill Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Clinton "Bill Clinton")'s [sanctions against Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctions_against_Iraq "Sanctions against Iraq"). In 2013, Harrelson condemned President [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama") for failing to withdraw U.S. troops from Afghanistan, negatively comparing him to [Richard Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon "Richard Nixon").

In 2017, Harrelson strongly criticized President [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump") in an interview with the [Associated Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_Press "Associated Press"), where he is quoted stating "We have a guy running this country who has unearthed a lot of bigotry and a lot of racism, and it seems to be more virulent than ever. So, yeah, it's strange how we just, it seems like we're going backward".

In 2023, he appeared to endorse [Robert F. Kennedy Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy_Jr "Robert F. Kennedy Jr"). In May 2024, Kennedy's [campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy_Jr._2024_presidential_campaign "Robert F. Kennedy Jr. 2024 presidential campaign") released a short documentary on the life and career of the politician, narrated by Harrelson.

## Filmography and awards

With a career portfolio of filmography and other performances spanning a variety of genres, Harrelson is known for his roles both in front of and behind the camera on several projects. His acting credits have earned him global recognition, having garnered him nominations for seven [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards"), three [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards"), a [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award "BAFTA Award"), four [Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards"), and seven [Screen Actors Guild Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards "Screen Actors Guild Awards"). His Oscar nominations include a [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor") nod for his role as [Larry Flynt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Flynt "Larry Flynt") in the 1996 [biographical drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biographical_film "Biographical film") *[The People vs. Larry Flynt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_People_vs._Larry_Flynt "The People vs. Larry Flynt")*, and [Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") for his portrayals of a [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War "Gulf War") veteran and a [police chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_police "Chief of police") in 2009's *[The Messenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Messenger_(2009_film) "The Messenger (2009 film)")* and 2017's *[Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing,_Missouri "Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri")*, respectively.

On television, his breakthrough role as bartender [Woody Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Boyd "Woody Boyd") earned him five [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards") nominations in the category of [Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Comedy Series"), with one win. Harrelson later returned to the small screen with roles in the comedy series *[Frasier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frasier "Frasier")* and the 2012 television film *[Game Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Change_(film) "Game Change (film)")*, for which he received two more Emmy nominations: for [Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series") and [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Miniseries or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie"), respectively.

Aside from his numerous successes in the industry, other credits include *[North Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Country_(film) "North Country (film)")* (2005), *[No Country for Old Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Country_for_Old_Men "No Country for Old Men")* (2007), *[2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_(film) "2012 (film)")*, *[Zombieland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland "Zombieland")* (both 2009), *[Now You See Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_You_See_Me_(film) "Now You See Me (film)")* (2013) and [its 2016 sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_You_See_Me_2 "Now You See Me 2"), [*The Hunger Games* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film_series) "The Hunger Games (film series)") (2012–2015), *[The Edge of Seventeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Edge_of_Seventeen "The Edge of Seventeen")*, *[LBJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LBJ_(2016_film) "LBJ (2016 film)")* (both 2016), *[The Glass Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Glass_Castle_(2017_film) "The Glass Castle (2017 film)")* (2017) and *[Kate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_(film) "Kate (film)")* (2021), Harrelson has also starred in critically panned films, with his role in 1993's *[Indecent Proposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indecent_Proposal "Indecent Proposal")* winning him the [Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Award_for_Worst_Supporting_Actor "Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Supporting Actor") in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Golden_Raspberry_Awards "14th Golden Raspberry Awards").

Harrelson was nominated for the 2023 [Offie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Offies "The Offies") for Best Lead Performer - *Ulster American*.
