# Presley Gerber

**Presley Walker Gerber** (July 2, 1999 – September 20, 2026) was an American model. He was the elder child of [Cindy Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cindy_Crawford "Cindy Crawford") and [Rande Gerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rande_Gerber "Rande Gerber") and the brother of model and actress [Kaia Gerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaia_Gerber "Kaia Gerber"). He began modeling as a teenager and later appeared in runway shows and advertising campaigns for several major fashion brands. He also spoke publicly about his experiences with mental health and addiction.

## Early life and education

Presley Walker Gerber was born in a [home birth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_birth "Home birth") on July 2, 1999 in [Brentwood, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentwood,_Los_Angeles "Brentwood, Los Angeles"), California, U.S., the first child and only son of supermodel [Cindy Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cindy_Crawford "Cindy Crawford") and businessman [Rande Gerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rande_Gerber "Rande Gerber"). He had a younger sister, [Kaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaia_Gerber "Kaia Gerber"). He graduated from [Malibu High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_High_School "Malibu High School") in 2017.

Gerber's parents said the name Presley came from a dinner with record producer [David Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Foster "David Foster"), who was then married to [Linda Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Thompson "Linda Thompson"), formerly [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley")'s girlfriend. They recalled Foster asking, "Can you imagine being me, having to follow Presley?" and that the name stuck.

## Career

### Modeling career

Gerber began his modeling career at age 15 after signing with [IMG Models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMG_Models "IMG Models"), and made his runway debut for [Moschino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moschino "Moschino") at age 16. He walked for [Burberry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burberry "Burberry"), [Dolce & Gabbana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolce_%26_Gabbana "Dolce & Gabbana"), [Bottega Veneta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottega_Veneta "Bottega Veneta"), [Tommy Hilfiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Hilfiger_(company) "Tommy Hilfiger (company)") and [Ralph Lauren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Lauren_Corporation "Ralph Lauren Corporation"), and appeared in a [Super Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl "Super Bowl") advertisement for [Pepsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepsi "Pepsi") with his mother in 2018. He also worked on campaigns for [Calvin Klein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Klein_(fashion_house) "Calvin Klein (fashion house)"), [Celine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_(brand) "Celine (brand)") and [Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_SA "Omega SA").

In 2017, he won the Reader's Choice Breakout Star men's category in Models.com's Model of the Year awards. He had previously been nominated for the Celebrity as Model men's category in 2016, and was subsequently nominated for the Model of the Year men's category in 2018.

### Acting

In 2017, Gerber had a role in the music video for [Madison Beer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Beer "Madison Beer")'s song "[Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_(Madison_Beer_song) "Dead (Madison Beer song)")". In 2024, he appeared as himself in the eighth episode of the reality‑sports show *The Wager*, where Gerber and his father were challenged by [Jason Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Kennedy_(TV_personality) "Jason Kennedy (TV personality)") and Wes Burrows.

## Personal life and death

In January 2019, Gerber was arrested and charged with [driving under the influence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driving_under_the_influence "Driving under the influence") (DUI). He received three years' probation and was required to complete a DUI program and two days of [community service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_service "Community service").

In 2020, he tattooed his face with the word "misunderstood" under his eye, which led to viral controversy. Gerber compared the negative comments to [transphobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transphobia "Transphobia") in a later post. According to *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")*, he had removed it by August 2021.

In 2023, Gerber discussed his experiences with mental health on the Studio 22 [podcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast "Podcast"). He said he had dealt with depression and related issues, and added that "whether I help one person or a hundred people get out of that place ... that's all I need to do." On the podcast, he also mentioned considering a mental health‑focused clothing line. The same year, Gerber began an Instagram series called "Mental Health Mondays", where he discussed his experiences with depression and addiction. On the series, he said he first saw a psychiatrist at around 13 and began taking [Xanax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xanax "Xanax") at 16, which initially helped before he started to worry about its long‑term effects. He said he knew what dependence and addiction were but did not realise he was moving in that direction. He described being treated with several medications over the years, including [Prozac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prozac "Prozac") at 14 for anxiety and panic attacks. Gerber said he first went to rehab in his late teens, where he was given [trazodone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trazodone "Trazodone"), an antidepressant that he said left him feeling numb. He was prescribed other drugs during multiple rehab stays, the first at 18 or 19, and mentioned using [ketamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketamine "Ketamine") and [kratom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kratom "Kratom") to manage withdrawal symptoms. He also recalled taking [Valium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valium "Valium"), [Adderall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adderall "Adderall") and [buprenorphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buprenorphine "Buprenorphine") before becoming dissatisfied with medication and traditional rehab programs.

In March 2026, he posted a video showing him preparing for a "pre‑[ibogaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibogaine "Ibogaine") flood dose" in [Cancun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canc%C3%BAn "Cancún"), Mexico. In June 2026, Gerber took part in a mental-health event in [Malibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu,_California "Malibu, California"). He spoke about his experiences with mental health, including a turning point at age 23 and the pressures of growing up in the public eye, and said he felt more free after being open about what he had gone through.

Gerber died at Resolutions Living, a rehabilitation facility in [Santa Monica, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California "Santa Monica, California"), one day after he arrived, on September 20, 2026, at age 27.
