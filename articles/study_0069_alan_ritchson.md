# Alan Ritchson

**Alan Michael Ritchson** (born November 28, 1982) is an American actor. He made his acting debut as [Arthur Curry / Aquaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaman "Aquaman") on [The CW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW "The CW") superhero series *[Smallville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallville "Smallville")* (2005–10), where he appeared as a guest star between the [fifth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallville_season_5 "Smallville season 5") and [tenth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallville_season_10 "Smallville season 10") seasons. He subsequently had a starring role in the [Spike TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_(TV_network) "Spike (TV network)") sitcom *[Blue Mountain State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_State "Blue Mountain State")* (2010–2011), a role he reprised in the [2016 film sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_State:_The_Rise_of_Thadland "Blue Mountain State: The Rise of Thadland"). He also headlined the [SyFy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SyFy "SyFy") action series *[Blood Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Drive_(TV_series) "Blood Drive (TV series)")* (2017), and returned to superhero television as [Hank Hall / Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Hall "Hank Hall") on the [DC Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Universe_(streaming_service) "DC Universe (streaming service)")/[HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") series *[Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titans_(2018_TV_series) "Titans (2018 TV series)")* from 2018 to 2021.

He gained wider recognition for portraying the [title character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Reacher "Jack Reacher") in the [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") action thriller series *[Reacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reacher_(TV_series) "Reacher (TV series)"),* beginning in 2022. His latest film appearance is *[War Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_Machine_(2026_film) "War Machine (2026 film)")* (2026), where he plays an [Army Ranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Rangers "United States Army Rangers") candidate fighting an extraterrestrial killing machine.

Outside television, Ritchson played [Raphael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael_(Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles) "Raphael (Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles)") in the 2014 *[Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles_(2014_film) "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2014 film)")* reboot and its [2016 sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles:_Out_of_the_Shadows "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Out of the Shadows"), along with appearances in *[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire "The Hunger Games: Catching Fire")* (2013), *[Lazer Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazer_Team "Lazer Team")* (2015), and *[Fast X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_X "Fast X")* (2023). He made his directorial debut and co-starred in the action-comedy *[Dark Web: Cicada 3301](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Web:_Cicada_3301 "Dark Web: Cicada 3301")* (2021).

## Early life and education

Ritchson was born on November 28, 1982, in [Grand Forks, North Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Forks,_North_Dakota "Grand Forks, North Dakota"). He is the son of Vickie Ritchson, a high school teacher, and David Ritchson, a retired [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Air_Force "U.S. Air Force") [chief master sergeant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_master_sergeant "Chief master sergeant"). He has one older brother and one younger brother, and was raised Catholic. The family moved around for his father's work. When he was two, his family moved from [Guam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guam "Guam") to [Rantoul, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rantoul,_Illinois "Rantoul, Illinois"). When he was 10, Ritchson's father was transferred to [Eglin Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eglin_Air_Force_Base "Eglin Air Force Base") and the family lived in nearby [Niceville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niceville,_Florida "Niceville, Florida"). At [Niceville High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niceville_High_School "Niceville High School"), Ritchson became interested in performing; he performed in school musicals and regarded [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey "Jim Carrey") as his idol. In 1999, while still in high school, he began taking college-level music theater and dance classes at Okaloosa Walton Community College, now [Northwest Florida State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Florida_State_College "Northwest Florida State College") in Niceville. He graduated from Niceville High in 2001.

After graduating from high school, he received a full scholarship to study music and theater at Okaloosa Walton, where he was a member of the Fine Arts Division's Soundsations and Madrigal Singers. However, Ritchson dropped out after two years "because [he] didn't feel fulfilled".

He worked at a gas station for a short time before signing with [Next Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Management "Next Management") in [Miami Beach, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Beach,_Florida "Miami Beach, Florida"), to work as a model for a few years.

## Career

### Modeling

During his modeling career, Ritchson was an underwear and runway model. He also worked with [Bruce Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Weber_(photographer) "Bruce Weber (photographer)"), a photographer for the [Abercrombie & Fitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abercrombie_%26_Fitch "Abercrombie & Fitch") catalogue.

### Television

Ritchson gained celebrity status in 2004 when he appeared on *[American Idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Idol "American Idol")* as one of the top 87 contestants in the [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Idol_season_3 "American Idol season 3") before being cut during the Hollywood round. His appearance on the show was noted for his striptease in one episode in which he charmed judge [Paula Abdul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paula_Abdul "Paula Abdul") during his audition. He decided to stay in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") and pursue an acting career.

His television acting credits include a recurring guest star role on the television series *[Smallville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallville "Smallville")* as superhero [Arthur Curry / Aquaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaman "Aquaman"). His role in Smallville marked the first time Aquaman was portrayed by an actor in an officially licensed live-action production. His future *Smallville* co-star, [Justin Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Hartley "Justin Hartley"), later portrayed Aquaman in an [unaired pilot of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaman_(2006_TV_pilot) "Aquaman (2006 TV pilot)") before joining the cast of *Smallville* as another character. Ritchson reprised his role as Aquaman in the final season.

Ritchson had a starring role as an army officer in the [Hallmark Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallmark_Channel "Hallmark Channel") movie *[Though None Go with Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Though_None_Go_with_Me "Though None Go with Me")* (2006) alongside [Cheryl Ladd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl_Ladd "Cheryl Ladd"), and the role of Lucian Manet in the [Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network) "Lifetime (TV network)") film *Nora Roberts' Midnight Bayou* (2009). In 2009, he appeared on a third-season episode of [Starz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starz_(TV_channel) "Starz (TV channel)")'s *[Head Case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_Case "Head Case")* in which he played a stripper. Ritchson also appeared on *[CSI: Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Miami "CSI: Miami")*, in which he played a dead victim in episode 19 of season 8. In 2011, he appeared in an episode of season 3 of *[90210](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/90210_(TV_series) "90210 (TV series)")* as a love interest for main character [Teddy Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddy_Montgomery "Teddy Montgomery") ([Trevor Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Donovan "Trevor Donovan")).

In 2010, he started playing the main role of Thad Castle, the captain of a college football team, in [Spike TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_(TV_network) "Spike (TV network)")'s *[Blue Mountain State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_State "Blue Mountain State")*. He stayed on the show until it was cancelled after its third season in February 2012. He also appeared in several television shows, including *[Hawaii Five-0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii_Five-0_(2010_TV_series) "Hawaii Five-0 (2010 TV series)")*. In 2015, Ritchson became a regular on the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") variety show *[I Can Do That](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Can_Do_That_(American_TV_series) "I Can Do That (American TV series)")* (based on the [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis "Israelis") variety show) alongside [Nicole Scherzinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Scherzinger "Nicole Scherzinger"), [Ciara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciara "Ciara"), [Joe Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Jonas "Joe Jonas"), [Cheryl Burke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl_Burke "Cheryl Burke") and [Jeff Dye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Dye "Jeff Dye"), and hosted by [Marlon Wayans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlon_Wayans "Marlon Wayans"). In 2016, he appeared in "[Nosedive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nosedive_(Black_Mirror) "Nosedive (Black Mirror)")", an episode of the [anthology series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology_series "Anthology series") *[Black Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Mirror "Black Mirror")*.

In 2017, he played the main character in the [Syfy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syfy "Syfy") series *[Blood Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_Drive_(TV_series) "Blood Drive (TV series)")*. In 2018, Ritchson played the recurring role of [Hank Hall / Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Hall "Hank Hall") in the [DC Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Universe_(streaming_service) "DC Universe (streaming service)") series *[Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titans_(2018_TV_series) "Titans (2018 TV series)")*. His initial deal was for just two episodes but he was promoted to a series regular for the [second season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titans_season_2 "Titans season 2"). Producers decided to reduce the number of characters and Ritchson was written out of the series.

In 2020, it was announced that Ritchson would play the role of [Jack Reacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Reacher "Jack Reacher") in an [eponymous series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reacher_(TV_series) "Reacher (TV series)") on [Amazon Prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime "Amazon Prime").

### Films

His movie credits include a role in the 2006 film *[The Butcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Butcher_(2006_film) "The Butcher (2006 film)")*, as well as a minor role in 2009's *[Fired Up!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fired_Up! "Fired Up!")* In 2007, director [Robert Zemeckis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Zemeckis "Robert Zemeckis") used Ritchson for his facial image, physique, and in movement for actor [Ray Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Winstone "Ray Winstone") of the [title character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf_(hero) "Beowulf (hero)") of *[Beowulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf_(2007_film) "Beowulf (2007 film)")*.

Ritchson portrayed Gloss, a tribute in the 75th Hunger Games, in *[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire "The Hunger Games: Catching Fire")* (2013). Ritchson also portrayed [Raphael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael_(Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles) "Raphael (Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles)") in *[Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles_(2014_film) "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2014 film)")*, a reboot to [the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles_(film_series) "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (film series)") after the four previous films. Ritchson would later criticize producers of the new *TMNT* live-action films, claiming they mistreated him and other actors portraying the turtles during and after filming. He returned to the role of Thad Castle in the *[Blue Mountain State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_State "Blue Mountain State")* movie, *[Blue Mountain State: The Rise of Thadland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_State:_The_Rise_of_Thadland "Blue Mountain State: The Rise of Thadland")*. Ritchson co-starred in [Rooster Teeth Production's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rooster_Teeth "Rooster Teeth") first feature-length movie—the science fiction comedy *[Lazer Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazer_Team "Lazer Team")* in January 2016. He also played a medium role in *[The Wedding Ringer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Ringer "The Wedding Ringer")*.

In 2018, Ritchson was announced as the director, co-writer, and producer of *[Dark Web: Cicada 3301](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Web:_Cicada_3301 "Dark Web: Cicada 3301")*, a comedy-thriller film inspired by the [eponymous organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cicada_3301 "Cicada 3301"). He also co-starred in it as an [NSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Agency "National Security Agency") agent. The film is the first original project of Phreaker Films, a film fund run by Ritchson. The rights for *Dark Web: Cicada 3301* were acquired by [Lionsgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate_Studios "Lionsgate Studios") in 2020, who released the film digitally on March 12, 2021. In 2022, he joined the cast of *[Fast X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_X "Fast X")*.

In 2024, Ritchson appeared in [Jon Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Gunn "Jon Gunn")'s drama movie *[Ordinary Angels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_Angels_(film) "Ordinary Angels (film)")*, written by [Meg Tilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Tilly "Meg Tilly") and [Kelly Fremon Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Fremon_Craig "Kelly Fremon Craig"), as well as in [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie")'s *[The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ministry_of_Ungentlemanly_Warfare "The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare")*, an action comedy film. In 2025 he starred in the buddy action comedy film *[Playdate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playdate_(film) "Playdate (film)")*.

Ritchson appears in a [web series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_series "Web series") titled *Enormous Friends* with his *Blue Mountain State* co-star, [Rob Ramsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Ramsay "Rob Ramsay").

## Personal life

Ritchson met his wife, Catherine, while he was taking a dance class at a college and they married in M‭ay 2006. They lived with their three sons in [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida "Florida") for years. In 2023, the couple decided to sell their home and live on the road, allowing Ritchson to spend more time with his family. The family stayed in [Airbnbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbnb "Airbnb") and hotels; Catherine, a financial analyst, homeschooled their sons.[*[clarification needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Please_clarify "Wikipedia:Please clarify")*] In September 2026, Ritchson announced he and Catherine had separated.

Ritchson has been diagnosed with [bipolar disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_disorder "Bipolar disorder") and has spoken openly about his struggles with the condition.

In March 2026, Ritchson was involved in a physical altercation with neighbor Ronnie Taylor in [Brentwood, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentwood,_Tennessee "Brentwood, Tennessee"); while Ritchson rode his motorcycle through the neighborhood, Taylor stepped in front of Ritchson, blocking his path and chastising Ritchson for this being a danger to children playing in the area. Taylor then initiated a physical confrontation and Ritchson acted in self-defense. The police were called, but Ritchson declined to pursue charges.

### Religious and political views

Ritchson is a practicing [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christians "Christians"). He frequently discusses the significance of his faith, and has expressed a desire to support the development of [Christian films in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_film_industry "Christian film industry"). He started the [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") channel InstaChurch, in January 2022, where he discusses his convictions. Though raised Catholic, he does not consider himself as such, stating "I can't for one second support the [Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church "Catholic Church") while there are still cardinals, bishops and priests being passed around with known pedophilic tendencies."

On April 3, 2024, *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* published an interview of Ritchson in which he says that he had once been sexually assaulted by a "very famous photographer" and for this reason, he left his modeling career. He described the modeling industry as "legalized sex trafficking" for photographers. In the same interview, Ritchson also criticized Christian supporters of [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump"), saying: "Trump is a [rapist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_sexual_misconduct_allegations "Donald Trump sexual misconduct allegations") and a [con man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prosecution_of_Donald_Trump_in_New_York "Prosecution of Donald Trump in New York"), and yet the entire Christian church seems to treat him like he's their poster child and it's unreal. I don't understand it."

In a February 2025 interview with *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")*, Ritchson indicated that former congressman [Matt Gaetz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Gaetz "Matt Gaetz") had been a classmate of his. Ritchson called Gaetz an "adversary" and "not a good dude". In the same interview, he expressed admiration for US senator [Bernie Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Sanders "Bernie Sanders"), calling him a "hero".

Ritchson has expressed support for [psychedelic therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_therapy "Psychedelic therapy"), and credits [MDMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA "MDMA") with treating his [suicidal ideation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicidal_ideation "Suicidal ideation").
