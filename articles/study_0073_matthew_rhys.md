# Matthew Rhys

**Matthew Rhys** ([/riːs/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-Rhys.ogg "File:En-us-Rhys.ogg") [*REESS*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born 8 November 1974) is a Welsh actor. Known for portraying [morally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morality "Morality") complex protagonists, he has received various accolades including four [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards"), becoming the first and only actor to win in all three Lead Actor categories in Emmys history. He has also been nominated for two [Actor Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Awards "Actor Awards"), two [BAFTAs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTAs "BAFTAs"), and four [Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards").

Rhys gained recognition for playing [Kevin Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Walker_(Brothers_%26_Sisters) "Kevin Walker (Brothers & Sisters)") in the family drama series *[Brothers & Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_%26_Sisters_(2006_TV_series) "Brothers & Sisters (2006 TV series)")* (2006–2011) and [Philip Jennings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Jennings_(The_Americans) "Philip Jennings (The Americans)") in the spy drama series *[The Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Americans "The Americans")* (2013–2018); he earned the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series") in 2018 for his work on *The Americans*. He earned Emmy nominations for his roles in the comedy series *[Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_(TV_series) "Girls (TV series)")* (2017) and the mystery series *[Perry Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Mason_(2020_TV_series) "Perry Mason (2020 TV series)")* (2020–2023). At the [2026 Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/78th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "78th Primetime Emmy Awards"), he was a three-time winner: [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie") for the crime thriller *[The Beast in Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beast_in_Me_(TV_series) "The Beast in Me (TV series)")* (2025), and [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series") and [Outstanding Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series") (as an executive producer) for the comedy-horror series *[Widow's Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widow%27s_Bay "Widow's Bay")* (since 2026).

In film, Rhys appeared as Demetrius in *[Titus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titus_(film) "Titus (film)")* (1999), Doc Fairweather in *[Deathwatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deathwatch_(2002_film) "Deathwatch (2002 film)")* (2002), [Dylan Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Thomas "Dylan Thomas") in *[The Edge of Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Edge_of_Love "The Edge of Love")* (2008), [Daniel Ellsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ellsberg "Daniel Ellsberg") in *[The Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Post_(film) "The Post (film)")* (2017), and Lloyd Vogel in *[A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Beautiful_Day_in_the_Neighborhood "A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood")* (2019). He has also voiced several characters in animated series, including Aloysius in *[Infinity Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_Train "Infinity Train")* (2019), [Emperor Belos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Belos "Emperor Belos") in *[The Owl House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Owl_House "The Owl House")* (2020–2023), Riley Greene in *[Gremlins: Secrets of the Mogwai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gremlins:_Secrets_of_the_Mogwai_(TV_series) "Gremlins: Secrets of the Mogwai (TV series)")* (2023), and Gammon Felda / the Florid Sword in *[The Wingfeather Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wingfeather_Saga "The Wingfeather Saga")* (2024–present).

## Early life and education

Matthew Rhys was born in [Cardiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff "Cardiff"), Wales, on 8 November 1974. He grew up in Cardiff with his older sister and attended [Welsh-medium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_medium_education "Welsh medium education") schools, Ysgol Gynradd Gymraeg Melin Gruffydd (in [Whitchurch, Cardiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitchurch,_Cardiff "Whitchurch, Cardiff")) and [Ysgol Gyfun Gymraeg Glantaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ysgol_Gyfun_Gymraeg_Glantaf "Ysgol Gyfun Gymraeg Glantaf") (in [Llandaff North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llandaff_North "Llandaff North"), Cardiff). In 1993, he was awarded the [Patricia Rothermere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Rothermere "Patricia Rothermere") Scholarship.

At age 17, after playing [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley") in a school musical, he applied to and was accepted at the [Royal Academy of Dramatic Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Dramatic_Art "Royal Academy of Dramatic Art") (RADA) in London. During Rhys's time at RADA, he appeared in the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") police series *[Backup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup_(TV_series) "Backup (TV series)")* and in *[House of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_America "House of America")*. He returned to Cardiff to act in his own language in the Welsh film *Bydd yn Wrol* (*Be Brave*), for which he won Best Actor at the [BAFTA Cymru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Cymru "BAFTA Cymru") (Welsh BAFTAs).

## Career

### 1996–2012: Early roles and *Brothers & Sisters*

In January 1998, Rhys went to New Zealand to star in *Greenstone*, a colonial costume drama for television. He then landed a role in *[Titus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titus_(film) "Titus (film)")*, Julie Taymor's adaptation of *[Titus Andronicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titus_Andronicus "Titus Andronicus")*, starring [Anthony Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Hopkins "Anthony Hopkins") and [Jessica Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lange "Jessica Lange"). Next he played Ray in [Peter Hewitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hewitt_(director) "Peter Hewitt (director)")'s comedy film *[Whatever Happened to Harold Smith?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whatever_Happened_to_Harold_Smith%3F "Whatever Happened to Harold Smith?")* After returning to Wales, he did two consecutive films with [Jonathan Pryce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pryce "Jonathan Pryce"): *[The Testimony of Taliesin Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Testimony_of_Taliesin_Jones "The Testimony of Taliesin Jones")*, a film about a dysfunctional single-parent family in which he played the elder son, and [Sara Sugarman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Sugarman "Sara Sugarman")'s comedy *[Very Annie Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Very_Annie_Mary "Very Annie Mary")*, in which he played the role of Nob. Rhys would later reunite with *Very Annie Mary* star [Rachel Griffiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Griffiths "Rachel Griffiths") on *[Brothers & Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_%26_Sisters_(2006_TV_series) "Brothers & Sisters (2006 TV series)")*.

In 2000, Rhys played the lead role in *[Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis_(British_TV_series) "Metropolis (British TV series)")*, a drama series for [Granada TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada_TV "Granada TV") about the lives of six twenty-somethings living in London. Next he starred in *Peaches*, the film of the play written and directed by [Nick Grosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Grosso "Nick Grosso"). Rhys starred as Benjamin in the 2000 world premiere of the stage adaptation of *[The Graduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Graduate "The Graduate")*, alongside [Kathleen Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Turner "Kathleen Turner") at the [Gielgud Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gielgud_Theatre "Gielgud Theatre") in London's [West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_of_London "West End of London"). Rhys travelled to Ireland to star in the 18th-century swashbuckling adventure *[The Abduction Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Abduction_Club "The Abduction Club")*. He played the lead role of Darren Daniels in *Tabloid* and then returned to New Zealand to shoot the epic drama *[The Lost World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_World_(2001_film) "The Lost World (2001 film)")* for the BBC. His other film credits include the independent horror film *[Deathwatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deathwatch_(2002_film) "Deathwatch (2002 film)")* in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague "Prague") and *[Fakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fakers "Fakers")*, a comic crime caper.

In 2003, he played Justin Price in the [final episode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbo_(season_10)#ep69 "Columbo (season 10)") of the long-running television series *[Columbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbo "Columbo")*. He appeared in the independent feature *[Love and Other Disasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_and_Other_Disasters "Love and Other Disasters")*, in *[Virgin Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Territory "Virgin Territory")*, and as poet [Dylan Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Thomas "Dylan Thomas") in the love quadrangle biographical film *[The Edge of Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Edge_of_Love "The Edge of Love")*. He moved to the United States, to [Santa Monica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica "Santa Monica") after being cast in [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company")'s show *[Brothers & Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_%26_Sisters_(2006_TV_series) "Brothers & Sisters (2006 TV series)")* as gay lawyer [Kevin Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Walker_(Brothers_%26_Sisters) "Kevin Walker (Brothers & Sisters)"). The show had a five-season run, coming to an end in 2011.

In January 2012, Rhys appeared in a [BBC Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Two "BBC Two") two-part drama adaptation of [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens "Charles Dickens")'s last, unfinished novel, *[The Mystery of Edwin Drood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mystery_of_Edwin_Drood "The Mystery of Edwin Drood")*. The [Public Broadcasting Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Broadcasting_Service "Public Broadcasting Service") (PBS) aired it in the United States as one feature-length episode on 15 April 2012. In 2012, Rhys reprised Sir [Alec Guinness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Guinness "Alec Guinness")'s 1959 double role of John Barratt / Jacques DeGué in a new adaptation of [Daphne du Maurier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daphne_du_Maurier "Daphne du Maurier")'s *[The Scapegoat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Scapegoat_(Du_Maurier_novel) "The Scapegoat (Du Maurier novel)")*. That same year, Rhys was cast as Jimmy in the [Roundabout Theatre Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundabout_Theatre_Company "Roundabout Theatre Company")'s [Off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") revival of [John Osborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Osborne "John Osborne")'s play, *[Look Back in Anger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_Back_in_Anger "Look Back in Anger")*, at the [Laura Pels Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Pels_Theatre "Laura Pels Theatre") in the Harold and Miriam Steinberg Center for Theatre. The production played a limited engagement through 8 April 2012.

### 2013–2019: *The Americans* and acclaim

Rhys starred opposite his future partner [Keri Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keri_Russell "Keri Russell") in the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") series *[The Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Americans "The Americans")*, a 1980s [Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War "Cold War") spy drama about Russian [KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB "KGB") [sleeper agents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illegals_Program "Illegals Program"). The show debuted in January 2013 and aired its sixth and final season in 2018. Rhys received a [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award") for his acting in the sixth season. In 2013, Rhys starred in the television adaptation of the [P. D. James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._D._James "P. D. James") novel *[Death Comes to Pemberley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Comes_to_Pemberley_(TV_series) "Death Comes to Pemberley (TV series)")* as [Jane Austen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Austen "Jane Austen") hero [Fitzwilliam Darcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitzwilliam_Darcy "Fitzwilliam Darcy"). He appeared as [Daniel Ellsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ellsberg "Daniel Ellsberg") in the [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg") film *[The Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Post_(film) "The Post (film)")* in 2017. The same year, he made a guest appearance on the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") series *[Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_(TV_series) "Girls (TV series)")*, for which he received a Primetime Emmy Award nomination.

In 2019, he starred in [Marielle Heller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marielle_Heller "Marielle Heller")'s *[A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Beautiful_Day_in_the_Neighborhood "A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood").* Rhys's character was based on the journalist [Tom Junod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Junod "Tom Junod"), who skeptically profiled [Fred Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Rogers "Fred Rogers") for *[Esquire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_(magazine) "Esquire (magazine)")* in the 1990s*.* To prepare for the role, Rhys worked with Junod to explore how he would approach the interview. Critics praised the performance, with [Ian Freer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Freer "Ian Freer") writing for *[Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_(magazine) "Empire (magazine)")* that Rhys acted "with a burning-eyed intensity, perfectly render[ing] a man who can't quite make a leap to forgiveness".

### 2020–present: Television expansion

From 2020 to 2023, Rhys starred in the HBO series *[Perry Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Mason_(2020_TV_series) "Perry Mason (2020 TV series)")*. In 2025, Rhys starred as one of the lead characters, Nile Jarvis, a morally complex character, in Netflix's eight-part miniseries *[The Beast in Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beast_in_Me_(TV_series) "The Beast in Me (TV series)")*. From 16 to 28 November 2025, he returned to the Welsh stage to play [Richard Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burton "Richard Burton") in the one-man play *Playing Burton*, celebrating the 100th anniversary of Burton's birth and raising funds for the inaugural season of [Michael Sheen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Sheen "Michael Sheen")'s new [Welsh National Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_National_Theatre "Welsh National Theatre"). He received positive reviews from critics for his performance as Burton.

In 2026, Rhys voiced the character of Dinosaurus in the fourth season of *[Invincible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invincible_(TV_series) "Invincible (TV series)")* and began starring in the [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_(streaming_service) "Apple TV (streaming service)") comedy-horror series *[Widow's Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widow%27s_Bay "Widow's Bay").* At the [78th Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/78th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "78th Primetime Emmy Awards"), Rhys won [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie") for *The Beast in Me* and [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series") for *Widow's Bay*, making him the first actor to win two lead acting Emmys in one night, and the first actor to win Emmys as a lead in the comedy, drama, and limited or anthology series or movie categories.

In May 2026, it was reported that he will serve as an executive producer, as well as appear onscreen as [Harold Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Evans "Harold Evans"), for the BBC series *Dragon Slayers*, which is expected to start filming by September 2026 in Manchester.

Rhys will voice the character [Han Solo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo") in *[Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_IV_-_A_New_Hope "Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope")*, which is to be dubbed into Welsh for the first time as the film prepares to celebrate its 50th anniversary in 2027.

## Business ventures

### Patagonia productions

Rhys set up his own production company, Patagonia, in 2011. It has two projects in development. One of them involves an adaptation of a book titled *[Operation Julie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Julie "Operation Julie")*, written by Lyn Ebenezer, about the biggest [LSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LSD "LSD") busts in Wales's history; Rhys bought the [film rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_rights "Film rights") in December 2010. In 2023, Rhys spoke on a podcast about how he has been working on a potential film about [Owain Glyndŵr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owain_Glynd%C5%B5r "Owain Glyndŵr")'s life and [his rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glynd%C5%B5r_rebellion "Glyndŵr rebellion"), begun in 1400, against the Kingdom of England.

### Pubs

With the help of fellow Welsh actor [Rhys Ifans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhys_Ifans "Rhys Ifans"), Rhys supported fundraising for the Vale of Aeron pub in [Ystrad Aeron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ystrad_Aeron "Ystrad Aeron"). Rhys also invested in another pub in [Pennal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennal "Pennal"), a town in [Gwynedd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwynedd "Gwynedd"), North Wales, where his father grew up and Rhys would spend summers and Easters visiting. In 2019, the [Glan Yr Afon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glan-yr-afon,_Gwynedd "Glan-yr-afon, Gwynedd") (also Riverside) was put on the market without any buyers, but through a campaign supported by Rhys, there was a shares offering for the public which raised £450,000 to save the pub. The local community completed the purchase in December 2022.

### Charter yacht

In [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"), Rhys owns and rents out a [charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yacht_charter "Yacht charter") boat named *Rarebit*. The [Wheeler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilar_(boat) "Pilar (boat)") boat was constructed circa 1934; Rhys bought it from [eBay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBay "EBay") in 2017. He rebuilt the boat himself with the advice of [shipwrights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipbuilding "Shipbuilding").

## Personal life

Rhys has been in a relationship with his *[The Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Americans "The Americans")* co-star [Keri Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keri_Russell "Keri Russell") since 2014, after being romantically linked in late 2013. They had their first child, a son, in 2016. In interviews conducted in 2021, they referred to each other as husband and wife, though Rhys stated in an interview in March 2025 that they "literally haven't got round to marriage yet". The family lives together in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn "Brooklyn") along with Russell's two older children from a previous relationship.

He is a supporter of [Plaid Cymru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaid_Cymru "Plaid Cymru") and has spoken out in favour of [Welsh independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_independence "Welsh independence"). Rhys stated in November 2018 that he spoke to his son "only in [Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_language "Welsh language")", adding that he wants his son to understand the language.

On 15 July 2008, [Aberystwyth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberystwyth_University "Aberystwyth University") honoured Rhys as a fellow. On 8 August 2008, he was honoured at the [Welsh National Eisteddfod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_National_Eisteddfod "Welsh National Eisteddfod") by being accepted as a member to the [druidic order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druidic_order "Druidic order") of the [Gorsedd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorsedd "Gorsedd") of the Bards for his contributions to the [Welsh language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_language "Welsh language") and Wales. His bardic name in the Gorsedd is Matthew Tâf. In August 2009, Rhys took to the stage with the [National Youth Orchestra of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Youth_Orchestra_of_Wales "National Youth Orchestra of Wales") as part of the National Eisteddfod.
