# Sitee

***Sitee*** is a 1993 Maldivian [drama film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_(film_and_television) "Drama (film and television)") written and directed by [Ahmed Nimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Nimal "Ahmed Nimal"). Produced by Aslam Rasheed under Slam Studio in association with Kid Productions, the film stars Nimal and Fathimath Rameeza in pivotal roles.

The film is based on true incidents that occurred to a friend of [Ahmed Nimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Nimal "Ahmed Nimal"). Filming was completed in one month while shooting for the songs was extended for another month. The song "Mulhi Jaan Hithaa" is shot in five different islands from five atolls.

## Plot

Hameed ([Ahmed Nimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Nimal "Ahmed Nimal")), a fisherman, relocates to [Malé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mal%C3%A9 "Malé") hunting for jobs when his poor mother, Naseema ([Arifa Ibrahim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arifa_Ibrahim "Arifa Ibrahim")), dies. He is taken in as a servant by a reputed family where he starts an affair with their only child, Shadhiya (Fathimath Rameeza). When their affair is exposed to her father, he expels Hameed from their house where he is then accompanied by [Chilhiya Moosa Manik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilhiya_Moosa_Manik "Chilhiya Moosa Manik"), witnessing his honesty in a prior meeting and treats Hameed as his own child.
