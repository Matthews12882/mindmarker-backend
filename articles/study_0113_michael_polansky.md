# Michael Polansky

**Michael Woods Polansky** (born September 26, 1983) is an American entrepreneur, [venture capitalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital "Venture capital"), music producer, and songwriter. He is engaged to [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga "Lady Gaga") and has co-written various songs with her.

## Early life and education

Polansky was born in [Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota "Minnesota") on September 26, 1983 to Steven Polansky and Ellen Woods. He studied [applied mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematics "Applied mathematics") and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science "Computer science") at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University "Harvard University") and graduated in 2006.

## Career

Polansky is founder or co-founder of the companies Avos Capital Management, Hawktail, and Outer Biosciences. He is a board member of [Parker Institute for Cancer Immunotherapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Institute_for_Cancer_Immunotherapy "Parker Institute for Cancer Immunotherapy") and Lady Gaga's cosmetics brand [Haus Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haus_Labs "Haus Labs"). Polansky is the executive director of the Parker Foundation, which he co-founded with [Sean Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Parker "Sean Parker") in 2015. He has also been the chief operating officer of [Airtime.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airtime.com "Airtime.com"), which was founded by Parker and [Shawn Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Fanning "Shawn Fanning") in 2011. Polansky has been involved in other projects by Parker.

Polansky was a co-executive producer of Lady Gaga's 2024 soundtrack album *[Harlequin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlequin_(Lady_Gaga_album) "Harlequin (Lady Gaga album)")* and co-wrote and reworked the traditional songs "[Get Happy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_Happy_(song) "Get Happy (song)")", "[Good Morning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Morning_(1939_song) "Good Morning (1939 song)")", "[If My Friends Could See Me Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_My_Friends_Could_See_Me_Now "If My Friends Could See Me Now")", and "[Oh, When the Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_the_Saints_Go_Marching_In "When the Saints Go Marching In")". He also has writing and production credits on Lady Gaga's 2025 studio album *[Mayhem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhem_(Lady_Gaga_album) "Mayhem (Lady Gaga album)")*, including "The Beast", "Blade of Grass", "[Disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease_(Lady_Gaga_song) "Disease (Lady Gaga song)")", "Don't Call Tonight", "[How Bad Do U Want Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_Bad_Do_U_Want_Me "How Bad Do U Want Me")", "[LoveDrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoveDrug "LoveDrug")", and "[Vanish into You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanish_into_You "Vanish into You")". He and Lady Gaga also co-wrote the original song "All I Need Is Time". In addition, he co-produced [the Mayhem Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mayhem_Ball "The Mayhem Ball"), Gaga's 2025–2026 world tour, which was designed as a theatrical production divided into acts, and the music video for her 2026 single "[Runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway_(song) "Runway (song)")".

## Personal life

Polansky is based in San Francisco, as of 2024. As of 2025, he is engaged to [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga "Lady Gaga"). The couple started dating in 2020. When he asked how he should propose to her, she suggested using a blade of grass, which inspired her song of the same name on *Mayhem*.
