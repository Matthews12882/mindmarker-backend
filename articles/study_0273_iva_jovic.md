# Iva Jovic

**Iva Jovic** (born 6 December 2007) is an American professional [tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis "Tennis") player. She has a career-high singles ranking of No. 11 by the [WTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_Tennis_Association "Women's Tennis Association"), achieved on 12 October 2026, and a career-high doubles ranking of world No. 64, achieved on 24 August 2026. She has won two WTA Tour singles titles, both at the [Guadalajara Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadalajara_Open "Guadalajara Open"), in 2025 and 2026. Her best [Grand Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis) "Grand Slam (tennis)") singles result is reaching the quarterfinals of the [2026 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2026 Australian Open – Women's singles").

As a junior, Jovic reached a career-high [ITF junior combined ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_tennis "Junior tennis") of world No. 2 in 2024. She won two junior Grand Slam doubles titles, at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_doubles "2024 Australian Open – Girls' doubles") and [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_doubles "2024 Wimbledon Championships – Girls' doubles"), partnering compatriot [Tyra Caterina Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyra_Caterina_Grant "Tyra Caterina Grant").

## Early life

Iva Jovic was born on 6 December 2007 in [Torrance, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torrance,_California "Torrance, California"), to Bojan and Jelena Jovic, who emigrated to the United States in the early 2000s. Her father is from [Leskovac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leskovac "Leskovac"), Serbia, while her mother was born in [Split](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split,_Croatia "Split, Croatia"), Croatia. Both parents are pharmacists. She has an older sister, Mia, who played college tennis at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles "University of California, Los Angeles") (UCLA). Jovic grew up speaking [Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language "Serbian language") at home and regularly visited relatives in Serbia.

Jovic began playing tennis at the age of five, initially practicing with her older sister on the tennis courts of their apartment complex in Torrance. She also participated in other sports during childhood, including soccer, gymnastics and swimming. At the age of eleven, she began training under Peter Smith at the Jack Kramer Club in [Rolling Hills Estates, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Hills_Estates,_California "Rolling Hills Estates, California"). During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic") in 2020, she began focusing more seriously on tennis, increasing her training at the [United States Tennis Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Tennis_Association "United States Tennis Association")'s training facility in [Carson, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson,_California "Carson, California").

## Career

### Juniors

In December 2021, Jovic won the girls' under-14 singles title at the Junior Orange Bowl.

In November 2022, Jovic was part of the United States team that won the [Junior Billie Jean King Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_Davis_Cup_and_Junior_Billie_Jean_King_Cup "Junior Davis Cup and Junior Billie Jean King Cup"), alongside [Clervie Ngounoue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clervie_Ngounoue "Clervie Ngounoue") and [Valerie Glozman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerie_Glozman "Valerie Glozman"). The following month, she partnered [Tyra Caterina Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyra_Caterina_Grant "Tyra Caterina Grant") to win the girls' doubles title at the [Orange Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Bowl_(tennis) "Orange Bowl (tennis)") in [Plantation, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantation,_Florida "Plantation, Florida").

In November 2023, Jovic helped the United States defend its Junior Billie Jean King Cup title, this time alongside Grant and [Alanis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alanis_Hamilton "Alanis Hamilton"). The team won the tournament without dropping a set, with Jovic clinching the final against the Czech Republic by defeating [Laura Samson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Samson "Laura Samson"). In December, Jovic and Grant successfully defended their Orange Bowl doubles title, becoming the first pair in 30 years to win the event in consecutive years.

In 2024, Jovic and Grant won the girls' doubles title at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_doubles "2024 Australian Open – Girls' doubles") without dropping a set. They subsequently reached the final of the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_doubles "2024 French Open – Girls' doubles"), before winning their second junior Grand Slam doubles title at the [Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_doubles "2024 Wimbledon Championships – Girls' doubles").

In singles, Jovic reached the semifinals of the junior events at both [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_singles "2024 Wimbledon Championships – Girls' singles") and the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls%27_singles "2024 US Open – Girls' singles"). In August, she won the singles and doubles titles at the USTA Girls' 18s National Championships, earning a wildcard into the women's singles main draw at the US Open. She finished the year ranked world No. 2 in the [ITF junior combined rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_tennis "Junior tennis").

### 2022–23: Pro debut, first ITF Circuit title

In June 2022, Jovic made her professional debut as a [wildcard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_card_(sports) "Wild card (sports)") at the 15k SoCal Pro Circuit Jack Kramer Club event in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), where she reached the final. In October 2023, she won her first title at the 25k Ascension Project Women's Open in [Redding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redding,_California "Redding, California").

### 2024: Major debut and first win

She made her debut at the [2024 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_(tennis) "2024 US Open (tennis)"), after receiving a wildcard for the main-draw in singles and doubles, after a victory in the [USTA Girls 18s National Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USTA_Girls_18s_National_Championships "USTA Girls 18s National Championships"). She was the youngest player in the tournament. Jovic recorded her first major and [WTA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_Tour "WTA Tour") win over [Magda Linette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magda_Linette "Magda Linette"), becoming the youngest American to win a women's main-draw match at the US Open since 2000. She lost in the second round to 29th seed [Ekaterina Alexandrova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Alexandrova "Ekaterina Alexandrova") in three sets.

### 2025: WTA Tour title, top 50

Jovic entered the main draw at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Australian_Open "2025 Australian Open") for the first time, after winning the [USTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Tennis_Association "United States Tennis Association")'s Wild Card Challenge, and defeated [Nuria Párrizas Díaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuria_P%C3%A1rrizas_D%C3%ADaz "Nuria Párrizas Díaz") in the first round. She lost her next match to sixth seed [Elena Rybakina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elena_Rybakina "Elena Rybakina").

At [Indian Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2025 BNP Paribas Open – Women's singles"), Jovic reached the second round on her [WTA 1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_1000_tournaments "WTA 1000 tournaments") debut as a wildcard entrant, defeating [Julia Grabher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Grabher "Julia Grabher") in three sets and recording her first WTA 1000-level win. In the second round, she lost to sixth seed [Jasmine Paolini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasmine_Paolini "Jasmine Paolini"), in three sets.

Jovic gained a wildcard entry into the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open "2025 French Open") by winning the USTA's Roland Garros Wild Card Challenge. She defeated [Renata Zarazúa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renata_Zaraz%C3%BAa "Renata Zarazúa") in the first round, before losing to 12th seed Elena Rybakina for the second successive major.

In June, she won her first [WTA 125](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_125_tournaments "WTA 125 tournaments") title at the [Ilkley Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ilkley_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2025 Ilkley Open – Women's singles") defeating [Rebecca Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Marino "Rebecca Marino") in the final. Following this success, Jovic achieved another milestone by making her top 100 debut on June 16, 2025. Later that month she qualified to make her main-draw debut at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wimbledon_Championships "2025 Wimbledon Championships"), where she lost to [Suzan Lamens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzan_Lamens "Suzan Lamens") in the first round.

In August, Jovic entered the main draw at the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cincinnati_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2025 Cincinnati Open – Women's singles") and defeated fellow lucky loser [Solana Sierra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solana_Sierra "Solana Sierra") and 20th seed [Linda Nosková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Noskov%C3%A1 "Linda Nosková") to reach the third round at a WTA Tour event for the first time in her career. Her run was ended by [Barbora Krejčíková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbora_Krej%C4%8D%C3%ADkov%C3%A1 "Barbora Krejčíková"). Later that month at the [Tennis in Cleveland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Tennis_in_the_Land_%E2%80%93_Singles "2025 Tennis in the Land – Singles"), she overcame fourth seed [Anastasia Potapova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Potapova "Anastasia Potapova"), before losing to [Ann Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Li_(tennis) "Ann Li (tennis)"). At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_(tennis) "2025 US Open (tennis)"), Jovic defeated [Aliaksandra Sasnovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliaksandra_Sasnovich "Aliaksandra Sasnovich") in the first round, but lost her next match to Jasmine Paolini.

The following month at the [Guadalajara Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Guadalajara_Open_Akron "2025 Guadalajara Open Akron"), Jovic won her first WTA Tour title, saving a match point in the quarterfinal match against Andorran player [Victoria Jiménez Kasintseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Jim%C3%A9nez_Kasintseva "Victoria Jiménez Kasintseva"), and defeating Colombian player [Emiliana Arango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emiliana_Arango "Emiliana Arango") in the final. As a result, she climbed in the rankings to world No. 36 on September 15, 2025.

### 2026: Guadalajara title, major quarters, top 20

Jovic reached the final in [Hobart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hobart_International "2026 Hobart International"), where she lost to qualifier [Elisabetta Cocciaretto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elisabetta_Cocciaretto "Elisabetta Cocciaretto").

At the age of 18 years and a month old, Jovic reached a major third round for the first time at the [2026 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open "2026 Australian Open") defeating [Katie Volynets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Volynets "Katie Volynets") and [Priscilla Hon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priscilla_Hon "Priscilla Hon"). Next, she defeated world No. 8, Jasmine Paolini, to earn her first top-10 win. In the fourth round, she defeated [Yulia Putintseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yulia_Putintseva "Yulia Putintseva") in 53 minutes, losing only one game to reach her first Grand Slam tournament quarterfinal. She became the youngest American to reach the Australian Open women's singles quarterfinals since [Venus Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_Williams "Venus Williams") in 1998. Despite her loss in the quarterfinals to world No. 1, [Aryna Sabalenka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryna_Sabalenka "Aryna Sabalenka"), Jovic reached the top 20 on February 2, 2026.

At the [2026 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_Open "2026 French Open"), Jovic was the 17th seed and defeated [Alexandra Eala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Eala "Alexandra Eala") and [Emma Navarro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Navarro "Emma Navarro") in straight sets, before falling to [Naomi Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Osaka "Naomi Osaka") in three sets. At the [2026 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2026 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles"), she reached the fourth round, losing to compatriot [Jessica Pegula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Pegula "Jessica Pegula") in three sets. At the [2026 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles "2026 US Open – Women's singles"), she defeated [Magdalena Fręch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_Fr%C4%99ch "Magdalena Fręch"), [Francesca Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Jones_(tennis) "Francesca Jones (tennis)"), and Alexandra Eala to reach the fourth round, where she was beaten by compatriot [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff "Coco Gauff") in straight sets. She became the youngest American woman since Venus Williams in 1998 to reach the fourth round in three different majors in the same season. Later that month, she retained her title at the  [2026 Guadalajara Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Guadalajara_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles "2026 Guadalajara Open – Singles") with a straight sets win over compatriot [Peyton Stearns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peyton_Stearns "Peyton Stearns") in the final, and moved to a career-high world ranking of number 13.
