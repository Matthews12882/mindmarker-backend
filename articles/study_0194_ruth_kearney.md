# Ruth Kearney

**Ruth Delia Kearney** is an Irish actress known for her roles as Jess Parker in *[Primeval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primeval_(TV_series) "Primeval (TV series)")*, Daisy in *[The Following](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Following "The Following")*, and London in *[Flaked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaked "Flaked")*.

## Early life

Kearney was born in London, England, to Irish parents before relocating to [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin "Dublin"), Ireland, at five years old. She grew up in [Monkstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monkstown,_Dublin "Monkstown, Dublin"). She attended [St. Andrew's College, Booterstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Andrew%27s_College,_Dublin "St. Andrew's College, Dublin"), and then studied Drama and [Classics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classics "Classics") at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin "Trinity College Dublin"), graduating with honours, before training at the [Bristol Old Vic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Old_Vic "Bristol Old Vic") Theatre School, graduating in 2009. Her career began in theatre, with performances in productions such as *Man of Mode* alongside [Antonia Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonia_Thomas "Antonia Thomas") and [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James "Theo James"), *On the Razzle*, *Three Sisters*, *Oh! What a lovely War!* and *Othello*.

## Career

Kearney is known for her leading role as Jess Parker in the science fiction-[drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_film "Drama film") *[Primeval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primeval_(TV_series) "Primeval (TV series)")*, Kearney joined the cast in 2011 and appeared in both Series 4 and Series 5 of the show

In 2014, she guest-starred in the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") [political thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_thriller "Political thriller") *[Tyrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrant_(TV_series) "Tyrant (TV series)")*, playing the role of Katharina.

In 2015 Kearney played the recurring role of Daisy Locke in the [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Following_(season_3) "The Following (season 3)") of the [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting "Fox Broadcasting") American television [psychological thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_thriller "Psychological thriller") series *[The Following](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Following "The Following")*.

She stars as the female lead London in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") original comedy series *[Flaked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaked "Flaked")* alongside [Will Arnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Arnett "Will Arnett"). Kearney portrays an attractive newcomer to [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice,_Los_Angeles "Venice, Los Angeles") who gets caught in a love triangle with the characters portrayed by [Will Arnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Arnett "Will Arnett") and [David Sullivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Sullivan_(actor) "David Sullivan (actor)"). The role is Kearney's first major comedy television series. The series premiered on 11 March 2016 with all eight episodes released simultaneously.

Flaked returned for a second reduced season in 2017. Ruth Kearney also guest starred in the first season of [ePix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPix "EPix")'s *[Get Shorty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_Shorty_(TV_series) "Get Shorty (TV series)")* as Becca Morgan; her first episode aired in September 2017.

## Personal life

Since 2018 Kearney has been married to English actor [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James "Theo James"). They met at the [Bristol Old Vic Theatre School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Old_Vic_Theatre_School "Bristol Old Vic Theatre School"). The couple have two children.
