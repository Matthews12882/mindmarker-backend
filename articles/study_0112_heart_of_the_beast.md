# Heart of the Beast

***Heart of the Beast*** is a 2026 American [survival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survival_film "Survival film") [thriller film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_film "Thriller film") directed by [David Ayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ayer "David Ayer") and written by Cameron Alexander. The film stars [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt "Brad Pitt") as a retired [Special Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Special_Forces "United States Army Special Forces") veteran navigating a grueling 58-mile (93-kilometer) trek to safety with his traumatized combat [German Shepherd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Shepherd "German Shepherd") after their seaplane crashes in the Alaskan wilderness.

*Heart of the Beast* premiered at the [FirstBank Amphitheater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FirstBank_Amphitheater "FirstBank Amphitheater") in [Franklin, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin,_Tennessee "Franklin, Tennessee") on September 16, 2026, and released nationwide in the United States on September 25, 2026, by [Paramount Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Pictures "Paramount Pictures"). The film received generally positive reviews from critics.

## Plot

Retired [Special Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Special_Forces "United States Army Special Forces") [Command Sergeant Major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_Sergeant_Major "Command Sergeant Major") James Belmont and his [combat dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dogs_in_warfare "Dogs in warfare") Odin, both suffering from [PTSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PTSD "PTSD") and nightmares, land their [Cessna Skywagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_185_Skywagon "Cessna 185 Skywagon") on a remote [Alaskan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaska "Alaska") lake and camp overnight. While exploring the surrounding forest, James cautiously encounters Watkins, an outgoing stranger who sets up camp nearby. They talk late into the night. The next morning, Watkins departs, leaving James a book in exchange for a beer he had taken from the camp overnight. He highlights a particular passage in the book.

James and Odin depart by plane, but James suffers a heart attack mid-flight and crashes into a body of water. Odin is thrown out of the plane during the crash. James finds him nearby, weak but alive, then returns to the sunken wreckage to salvage his gear. Once Odin recovers enough to travel, the pair begin hiking toward civilization, approximately 58 miles (93 kilometers) away by James's calculations.

During their trek, they encounter a grizzly bear, which Odin confronts until it retreats. Later, they cross a precarious wooden bridge spanning a valley. James slips and swings Odin to safety before falling into the rapids below, where his arm becomes trapped between rocks and breaks. Odin finds him in the river and drags him ashore, then helps him reset the broken bone.

The pair later spot a pack of grey wolves attacking a moose calf and cautiously retreat. That night, however, the wolves ambush them. James fights back with a spear and knife while Odin defends himself against the attackers. James eventually kills all but one wolf, which escapes, but his stomach suffers a bite. The following day, they wade through a swamp, where James becomes trapped beneath submerged branches but manages to free himself by diving underwater.

As his injuries and exhaustion take their toll, James repeatedly stumbles before reaching the top of a hill. Unable to continue, he rests and writes a letter which he zips into in a pouch on Odin's backpack. He points toward a nearby road, instructing Odin to run to it. After watching the [northern lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora_borealis "Aurora borealis") overhead, James dies from the mortal wounds.

Odin leaves James and reaches the road, where a young woman named Amber finds him. She reads the letter James wrote, documenting their ordeal and asking whoever finds the Odin to care for him and also leaving all his money to whomever takes the dog in. Odin enters Amber's truck, and they drive away.

## Production

In March 2024, [David Ayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ayer "David Ayer") signed on as director with a script by Cameron Alexander that was featured on the 2017 [Black List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_List_(survey) "The Black List (survey)") of the year's 'most liked' unproduced screenplays; Alexander served as an executive producer. [Damien Chazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Chazelle "Damien Chazelle") and [Olivia Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Hamilton_(actress) "Olivia Hamilton (actress)") produced the film through their label Wild Chickens Productions, with [Paramount Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Pictures "Paramount Pictures") distributing as part of a [first look deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_look_deal "First look deal") between the companies, and Ayer and Chris Long serve as producers through Cedar Park Entertainment. In January 2025, [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt "Brad Pitt") was announced to be starring in the project, also producing under his [Plan B Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plan_B_Entertainment "Plan B Entertainment") banner. [Temple Hill Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_Hill_Entertainment "Temple Hill Entertainment") also joined the project, with Ayer now producing under Crave Films. In February, [J. K. Simmons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._K._Simmons "J. K. Simmons") joined the cast, with [Anna Lambe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Lambe "Anna Lambe") joining a month later.

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") began on March 3, 2025, in [Queenstown, New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queenstown,_New_Zealand "Queenstown, New Zealand"), and [wrapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrap_(filmmaking) "Wrap (filmmaking)") on May 15.

Jared Michael Fry was hired to compose the score for the film, marking his third collaboration with Ayer following *[The Beekeeper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beekeeper_(2024_film) "The Beekeeper (2024 film)")* (2024) and *[A Working Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Working_Man "A Working Man")* (2025).

The watch Pitt wears in the movie is a [Rolex Submariner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolex_Submariner "Rolex Submariner"), but the interesting fact is that the strap was spotted by director David Ayer on the wrist of a friend. As a result, the creator of the strap, Matt Jackson of UK company NDC Straps was approached to supply four pieces for the production. The strap was an old model and no longer made, so Jackson had to find four examples in old stock and send them to the producers.

## Release

*Heart of the Beast* premiered at the [FirstBank Amphitheater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FirstBank_Amphitheater "FirstBank Amphitheater") in Franklin, Tennessee on September 16, 2026. It premiered in a non-competition slot of the [74th San Sebastián International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_San_Sebasti%C3%A1n_International_Film_Festival "74th San Sebastián International Film Festival") on September 22, 2026. Advanced screening occurred on September 19, 2026 in New Zealand before its release on September 24, 2026. It released in the United States by Paramount Pictures on September 25, 2026. Its first trailer was shown at [CinemaCon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaCon "CinemaCon") in April 2026.

## Reception

### Box office

As of October 14, 2026[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Heart_of_the_Beast&action=edit), *Heart of the Beast* has grossed $53 million in the United States and Canada, and $82 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $135 million.

In the United States and Canada, *Heart of the Beast* was released alongside *[Primetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_(film) "Primetime (film)")*, *[Forgotten Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forgotten_Island "Forgotten Island")*, and the re-release of *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")*, and was projected to gross $17–20 million in its opening weekend. The film made $7.6 million on its first day, and went on to debut to $20 million, finishing in third place behind *Endgame* and *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2026_film) "Resident Evil (2026 film)")*. In its sophomore weekend the film made $10.9 million, remaining in third place, and then $7.4 million in its third weekend, finishing in fourth.

### Critical response

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 85% of 181 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 7.0/10. The website's consensus reads: "A rigorously entertaining adventure story told through a spartan script and memorable set pieces, *Heart of the Beast* pays rousing tribute to man's best friend." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean"), assigned the film a score of 66 out of 100, based on 32 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews. Audiences polled by [CinemaScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaScore "CinemaScore") gave the film an average grade of "A–" on an A+ to F scale, while 73% of filmgoers surveyed by [PostTrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PostTrak "PostTrak") said they would definitely recommend it.
