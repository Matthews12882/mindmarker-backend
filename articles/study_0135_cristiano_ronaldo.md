# Cristiano Ronaldo

**Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro** (born 5 February 1985) is a Portuguese professional [footballer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football") who plays as a [forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_(association_football) "Forward (association football)") for and [captains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_(association_football) "Captain (association football)") both the [Saudi Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Pro_League "Saudi Pro League") club [Al-Nassr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nassr_FC "Al-Nassr FC") and the [Portugal national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_national_football_team "Portugal national football team"). Nicknamed **CR7**, he is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time. He has won [numerous individual accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_career_achievements_by_Cristiano_Ronaldo#Individual_honours "List of career achievements by Cristiano Ronaldo") throughout [his career](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Career_of_Cristiano_Ronaldo "Career of Cristiano Ronaldo"), including five [Ballons d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or "Ballon d'Or"), a record three [UEFA Men's Player of the Year Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Men%27s_Player_of_the_Year_Award "UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award"), and four [European Golden Shoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Golden_Shoe "European Golden Shoe"). He was named the [world's best player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_FIFA_Men%27s_Player#FIFA_Player_of_the_Year_(including_predecessors) "The Best FIFA Men's Player") five times by [FIFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA "FIFA").

Ronaldo is one of the most decorated players in the history of professional football. His [35 trophies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_career_achievements_by_Cristiano_Ronaldo#Collective_awards "List of career achievements by Cristiano Ronaldo") include five [UEFA Champions Leagues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League "UEFA Champions League"), two [UEFA Nations Leagues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Nations_League "UEFA Nations League") and the [UEFA European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_European_Championship "UEFA European Championship"). He is the only player to have scored 100 goals with four different clubs and to finish as the top scorer in four different domestic leagues. He has made [over 1,300 professional career appearances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_men%27s_footballers_with_1,000_or_more_official_appearances "List of men's footballers with 1,000 or more official appearances"), the most by an [outfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_association_football_terms#Outfield_player "Glossary of association football terms") player, and has scored [over 980 official senior career goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_footballers_with_500_or_more_goals "List of footballers with 500 or more goals") for club and country, making him the top goalscorer in the history of the sport.

Ronaldo began his career with [Sporting CP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP "Sporting CP") before signing with [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C. "Manchester United F.C.") in 2003. He helped the club win three consecutive [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League "Premier League") titles, the [2007–08 Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_UEFA_Champions_League "2007–08 UEFA Champions League"), and the [2008 FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup "FIFA Club World Cup"). For his performances in [2007–08](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_Manchester_United_F.C._season "2007–08 Manchester United F.C. season"), Ronaldo was awarded his first [Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Ballon_d%27Or "2008 Ballon d'Or") at age 23. In 2009, he joined [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_CF "Real Madrid CF") in a deal worth €94 million (£80 million), which was the [most expensive transfer in history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_association_football_transfers "List of most expensive association football transfers") at the time. He helped Madrid win four Champions Leagues and two [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga "La Liga") titles, and became the club's [all-time top goalscorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Real_Madrid_CF_records_and_statistics#Goalscorers "List of Real Madrid CF records and statistics"). He joined [Juventus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juventus_FC "Juventus FC") in 2018 and helped them win two consecutive [Serie A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serie_A "Serie A") titles and the [Coppa Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppa_Italia "Coppa Italia"). He returned briefly to Manchester United before joining Al-Nassr in 2023. He was the Saudi Pro League's top scorer in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Saudi_Pro_League "2023–24 Saudi Pro League") and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Saudi_Pro_League "2024–25 Saudi Pro League"), and led Al-Nassr to the league title in [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Saudi_Pro_League "2025–26 Saudi Pro League").

Ronaldo made his debut for [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_national_football_team "Portugal national football team") in 2003 at the age of 18. He helped them reach [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2004_final "UEFA Euro 2004 final") of [Euro 2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2004 "UEFA Euro 2004"), and was named [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Portugal_national_football_team_captains "List of Portugal national football team captains") ahead of [Euro 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2008 "UEFA Euro 2008"). He led Portugal to victory at [Euro 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016 "UEFA Euro 2016") and at the [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Nations_League "UEFA Nations League") in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_UEFA_Nations_League "2018–19 UEFA Nations League") and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Nations_League "2024–25 UEFA Nations League"). Ronaldo holds the records for most [international appearances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_men%27s_footballers_with_100_or_more_international_caps "List of men's footballers with 100 or more international caps") (234), most [men's international goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_men%27s_footballers_with_50_or_more_international_goals "List of men's footballers with 50 or more international goals") (146), and most international victories (141). He was named in the [UEFA Ultimate Team of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Team_of_the_Year#UEFA_Ultimate_Team_of_the_Year "UEFA Team of the Year") in 2015, the [All-time UEFA Euro XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_European_Championship_awards#All-time_Euro_XI "UEFA European Championship awards") in 2016, and the [Ballon d'Or Dream Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or_Dream_Team "Ballon d'Or Dream Team") in 2020. In recognition of his record-breaking goalscoring success, he received special FIFA awards for [Outstanding Career Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_FIFA_Football_Awards#FIFA_Special_Award_for_an_Outstanding_Career_Achievement "The Best FIFA Football Awards") in 2021.

Ronaldo was ranked by *[Sportico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penske_Media_Corporation#Sportico "Penske Media Corporation")* as the third highest-paid athlete of all time in April 2026, and appeared on *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")*' *[The World's Billionaires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World%27s_Billionaires "The World's Billionaires")* list the same year, with a net worth of $1.2 billion. *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* included him on its list of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") in 2014. Ronaldo is the most popular athlete on social media, with over 1 billion followers across [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook "Facebook"), [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter"), [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube"), and [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram").

## Early life

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on 5 February 1985 in the [São Pedro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Pedro_(Funchal) "São Pedro (Funchal)") parish of [Funchal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funchal "Funchal"), the capital of the Portuguese island of [Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira "Madeira"). He grew up in the nearby parish of [Santo António](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Ant%C3%B3nio_(Funchal) "Santo António (Funchal)"). His mother, Maria Dolores dos Santos Viveiros Aveiro, worked as a cook in the [hospitality industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitality_industry "Hospitality industry") and as a [cleaning woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleaning_woman "Cleaning woman"). His father, José Dinis Aveiro, was a municipal gardener at the *[Junta de Freguesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junta_de_Freguesia "Junta de Freguesia")* of Santo António and a part-time [kit man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equipment_manager "Equipment manager") for the football club [Andorinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CF_Andorinha "CF Andorinha"). Ronaldo's father had served in the [Portuguese Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Army "Portuguese Army") during the [Portuguese Colonial War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Colonial_War "Portuguese Colonial War") and saw combat in [Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angola "Angola"); he was traumatized by his experiences, which led to a lifelong struggle with alcoholism. Ronaldo has Cape Verdean ancestry through his great-grandmother, Isabel da Piedade, who was born on the island of [São Vicente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Vicente,_Cape_Verde "São Vicente, Cape Verde"), in what was then [Portuguese Cape Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Cape_Verde "Portuguese Cape Verde").

Ronaldo has an older brother, Hugo, and two older sisters, Elma and [Liliana "Kátia" Cátia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%A1tia_Aveiro "Kátia Aveiro"). His father was an admirer of United States president [Ronald Reagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Reagan "Ronald Reagan"), and named Ronaldo after him. Ronaldo's mother revealed that she had wanted to [abort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abortion_in_Portugal "Abortion in Portugal") him due to poverty, his father's [alcoholism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoholism "Alcoholism"), and having too many children already, but her doctor refused to perform the procedure. Ronaldo grew up in an impoverished [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_Portugal "Catholic Church in Portugal") home, sharing a room with all his siblings, and continues to identify as a Catholic in his adulthood. He idolised the Brazilian footballers [Ronaldinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldinho "Ronaldinho") and [Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldo_(Brazilian_footballer) "Ronaldo (Brazilian footballer)") during his youth.

As a child, Ronaldo played for Andorinha from 1992 to 1995, and later spent two years with [Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.D._Nacional "C.D. Nacional"). In 1997, at age 12, he went on a three-day trial with [Sporting CP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP "Sporting CP"), which signed him for a fee of £1,500. He moved from Madeira to [Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon "Lisbon") to join [Sporting CP's youth system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP_Youth_Academy "Sporting CP Youth Academy"), and attended school at Escola EB2 de Telheiras, located in the city's Telheiras area. While popular with other students, he struggled with his school responsibilities. He was expelled after throwing a chair at his teacher, who he said had "disrespected" him. A year later, he was diagnosed with [tachycardia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachycardia "Tachycardia"), a condition that threatened to end his football career. He underwent [cardiac ablation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catheter_ablation "Catheter ablation"), a type of heart surgery in which a laser is used to [cauterise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauterise "Cauterise") multiple [cardiac pathways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_conduction_system_of_the_heart "Electrical conduction system of the heart") into one, which altered his resting [heart rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_rate "Heart rate"). He was discharged from the hospital hours after the procedure and resumed training a few days later.

By age 14, Ronaldo believed he had the ability to play football semi-professionally. He made an agreement with his mother and his Sporting CP tutor, [Leonel Pontes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonel_Pontes "Leonel Pontes"), to cease his education and focus entirely on football. He did not complete schooling beyond the 6th grade. In 2021, Ronaldo's mother stated in an interview that her son would have been a [bricklayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bricklayer "Bricklayer") if he had not become a professional football player.

## Club career

### Sporting CP

After impressing Sporting while playing for their [youth teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP_Youth_Academy "Sporting CP Youth Academy"), Ronaldo was promoted to the [first team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP "Sporting CP") by manager [László Bölöni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_B%C3%B6l%C3%B6ni "László Bölöni"). On 14 August 2002, at age 17, Ronaldo played his first official match for the first team, in a [UEFA Champions League qualifying round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002%E2%80%9303_UEFA_Champions_League_qualifying_rounds "2002–03 UEFA Champions League qualifying rounds") against [Inter Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_Milan "Inter Milan"). His [Primeira Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primeira_Liga "Primeira Liga") debut occurred a month later against [Braga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.C._Braga "S.C. Braga"), and on 7 October he scored two goals against [Moreirense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreirense_F.C. "Moreirense F.C.") in Sporting's 3–0 win. After Sporting defeated [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C. "Manchester United F.C.") 3–1 in a [friendly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exhibition_game "Exhibition game") on 6 August 2003, United manager [Alex Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Ferguson "Alex Ferguson") was determined to acquire Ronaldo, describing him as "one of the most exciting young players" he had ever seen.

### Manchester United

#### 2003–2007: Development and breakthrough

On 12 August 2003, less than a week after the match that impressed Ferguson, Manchester United signed Ronaldo for £12 million, an England record for a teenager. He was the first Portuguese player to sign for the club. Although he requested the number 28, his number at Sporting, he received the [squad number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_number_(association_football) "Squad number (association football)") 7 shirt, which had previously been worn by such United players as [George Best](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Best "George Best"), [Eric Cantona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Cantona "Eric Cantona") and [David Beckham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Beckham "David Beckham"). Wearing the number 7 became an extra source of motivation for Ronaldo. He would later describe Ferguson as his "father in sport" and "one of the most important and influential factors" in his career.

Ronaldo made his debut for United as a substitute in a 4–0 win over [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers_F.C. "Bolton Wanderers F.C.") in the Premier League on 16 August 2003. His performance earned praise from Best, who hailed it as "undoubtedly the most exciting debut" he had ever seen. Ronaldo scored his first goal for the club from a free kick in a 3–0 win over [Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth_F.C. "Portsmouth F.C.") on 1 November. On 15 May 2004, he scored the opening goal in a victory against Aston Villa, and received the first [red card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_card_(association_football) "Red card (association football)") of his career. He finished his first season in England with a trophy, scoring the opening goal in United's 3–0 win over [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C. "Millwall F.C.") in the [2004 FA Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_FA_Cup_Final "2004 FA Cup Final").

At the start of the [2004–05 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_FA_Premier_League "2004–05 FA Premier League"), Ronaldo provided a goal and an assist against Aston Villa and scored twice against [Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C. "Arsenal F.C."). He won his second trophy in English football, the [Football League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005%E2%80%9306_Football_League_Cup "2005–06 Football League Cup"), after scoring the third goal in United's 4–0 final win over [Wigan Athletic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigan_Athletic_F.C. "Wigan Athletic F.C."). During his third season in England, Ronaldo was involved in several incidents. He clashed with his teammate [Ruud van Nistelrooy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruud_van_Nistelrooy "Ruud van Nistelrooy"), who took offence at Ronaldo's [showboating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showboating "Showboating") style of play. During the [2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup "2006 FIFA World Cup"), Ronaldo was involved in an incident which resulted in his club teammate [Wayne Rooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_Rooney "Wayne Rooney") being sent off. Afterwards, Ronaldo publicly asked for a transfer, saying that United had not supported him during the incident. United denied his request. Although his World Cup altercation with Rooney resulted in Ronaldo being booed throughout the [2006–07 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_FA_Premier_League "2006–07 FA Premier League"), he scored 20 league goals for the first time as United won the [Premier League title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_football_champions "List of English football champions").

#### 2007–2009: Collective and individual success and Ballon d'Or

For the [2006–07 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_Manchester_United_F.C._season "2006–07 Manchester United F.C. season"), Ronaldo won the [Player's Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFA_Players%27_Player_of_the_Year "PFA Players' Player of the Year"), [Fans' Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFA_Fans%27_Player_of_the_Year "PFA Fans' Player of the Year") and [Young Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFA_Young_Player_of_the_Year "PFA Young Player of the Year") awards from the [Professional Footballers' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Footballers%27_Association "Professional Footballers' Association"), and the [Football Writers' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Writers%27_Association "Football Writers' Association")'s [Footballer of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FWA_Footballer_of_the_Year "FWA Footballer of the Year") award. He was named runner-up to [Kaká](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kak%C3%A1 "Kaká") for the [2007 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Ballon_d%27Or "2007 Ballon d'Or"), and finished third for the [2007 FIFA World Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_FIFA_World_Player_of_the_Year "2007 FIFA World Player of the Year") award, behind Kaká and [Lionel Messi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi "Lionel Messi").

Ronaldo scored his first [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat-trick_(association_football) "Hat-trick (association football)") for United in a 6–0 win against [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C. "Newcastle United F.C.") on 12 January 2008. His 31 league goals in the season earned him the [Premier League Golden Boot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League_Golden_Boot "Premier League Golden Boot"), and he became the first [winger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winger_(association_football) "Winger (association football)") to win the [European Golden Shoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Golden_Shoe "European Golden Shoe"). For the second consecutive season, he received the PFA Players' Player of the Year award and the FWA Footballer of the Year award. On 21 May, United defeated [Chelsea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_FC "Chelsea FC") in the Champions League [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_UEFA_Champions_League_Final "2008 UEFA Champions League Final") in a penalty shoot-out after a 1–1 draw, with Ronaldo scoring his team's only goal. As the [Champions League top scorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Cup_and_UEFA_Champions_League_top_scorers "List of European Cup and UEFA Champions League top scorers"), Ronaldo was named the [UEFA Club Footballer of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Club_Footballer_of_the_Year "UEFA Club Footballer of the Year"). Ronaldo became United's first [Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Ballon_d%27Or "2008 Ballon d'Or") winner since George Best in 1968, and the first [Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League "Premier League") player to be named the [FIFA World Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Player_of_the_Year "FIFA World Player of the Year").

Shortly after, United alleged that [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_CF "Real Madrid CF") was pursuing Ronaldo, and filed a tampering complaint with [FIFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA "FIFA"). The governing body declined to take action against Madrid, and Ronaldo remained at United for another year. In the [2009 Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Champions_League "2009 Champions League"), his match-winning goal against [Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Porto "FC Porto") earned him the inaugural [FIFA Puskás Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Pusk%C3%A1s_Award "FIFA Puskás Award"), which recognises the best goal of the year.

### Real Madrid

#### 2009–2015: World record transfer and consecutive Ballon d'Or wins

In 2009, Ronaldo transferred to [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid "Real Madrid") for £80 million, a world record transfer fee at the time. He was unveiled in front of 80,000 fans at the [Santiago Bernabéu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Bernab%C3%A9u_Stadium "Santiago Bernabéu Stadium"), and said that playing for Real Madrid had been his childhood dream. He made his [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga "La Liga") debut against [Deportivo La Coruña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deportivo_de_La_Coru%C3%B1a "Deportivo de La Coruña") on 29 August, scoring once in a 3–2 win. He became the first Madrid player to scored in each of his first four league games. He scored his first two Champions League goals for the club in September, in the first group match against [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Z%C3%BCrich "FC Zürich"). In October, he suffered an ankle injury while on international duty, which kept him sidelined for seven weeks. He finished the season with 33 goals across all competitions.

Following [Raúl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%BAl_(footballer) "Raúl (footballer)")'s departure from the club, Ronaldo was given the No. 7 shirt for the 2010–11 season. He scored 53 goals during the season and earned his first trophy in Spain as Madrid won the [Copa del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_del_Rey "Copa del Rey"). He became the first player to score 40 goals in a La Liga season. In addition to the [Pichichi Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichichi_Trophy "Pichichi Trophy"), Ronaldo won the European Golden Shoe for a second time, becoming the first player to win the award in different leagues.

During the [2011–12 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011%E2%80%9312_Real_Madrid_CF_season "2011–12 Real Madrid CF season"), Ronaldo scored 60 goals across all competitions and led Madrid to their first [league title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Spanish_football_champions "List of Spanish football champions") in four years with a record 100 points. He finished runner-up for the [2011 FIFA Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_FIFA_Ballon_d%27Or "2011 FIFA Ballon d'Or") behind [Lionel Messi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi "Lionel Messi"). He scored his 100th league goal for Madrid in a 5–1 win over [Real Sociedad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Sociedad "Real Sociedad") on 24 March 2012, breaking the previous club record held by [Ferenc Puskás](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferenc_Pusk%C3%A1s "Ferenc Puskás"). In the [2012–13 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_Real_Madrid_CF_season "2012–13 Real Madrid CF season"), Ronaldo scored his first [hat-trick in the Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UEFA_Champions_League_hat-tricks "List of UEFA Champions League hat-tricks") in a 4–1 win over [Ajax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Ajax "AFC Ajax"). Ronaldo finished second in voting for the [2012 FIFA Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_FIFA_Ballon_d%27Or "2012 FIFA Ballon d'Or"), again behind Messi. On 6 January, he captained Madrid for the first time in an official match, scoring twice in a 4–3 win over Sociedad.

During the [2013–14 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_Real_Madrid_CF_season "2013–14 Real Madrid CF season"), Madrid's attacking trio of Ronaldo, [Gareth Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Bale "Gareth Bale") and [Karim Benzema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Benzema "Karim Benzema") was popularly dubbed "BBC" (an acronym of Bale, Benzema and Cristiano). Ronaldo scored 69 goals in 2013, winning the [2013 FIFA Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_FIFA_Ballon_d%27Or "2013 FIFA Ballon d'Or"). In 2014, he helped Madrid win their tenth [UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League "UEFA Champions League") title, scoring once in their 4–1 final win over [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid "Atlético Madrid"). As the competition's top goalscorer for the third time, with a record 17 goals, he was named the [UEFA Best Player in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Best_Player_in_Europe "UEFA Best Player in Europe"). He scored 31 goals in 30 league games during the season, which earned him the [European Golden Shoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Golden_Shoe "European Golden Shoe") (shared with [Luis Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Su%C3%A1rez_(Uruguayan_footballer) "Luis Suárez (Uruguayan footballer)")) and the [Pichichi Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichichi_Trophy "Pichichi Trophy"). On 4 May, he scored a back-heeled volley in the closing moments of a match against [Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_CF "Valencia CF"), which was voted the goal of the season by the [Liga Nacional de Fútbol Profesional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_Nacional_de_F%C3%BAtbol_Profesional "Liga Nacional de Fútbol Profesional") (LFP), earning him the [Best Player in La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LFP_Awards "LFP Awards") award.

During the [2014–15 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014%E2%80%9315_Real_Madrid_CF_season "2014–15 Real Madrid CF season"), Ronaldo set a new personal best of 61 goals. After winning the [2014 FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_Club_World_Cup "2014 FIFA Club World Cup"), Ronaldo received the [2014 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_Ballon_d%27Or "2014 FIFA Ballon d'Or"). He finished as top scorer in the Champions League for a third consecutive season, with 10 goals (tied with Messi and [Neymar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neymar "Neymar")). On 5 April, he scored five goals in a game for the first time in his career, including an eight-minute hat-trick, in a 9–1 win over [Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada_CF "Granada CF"). His 300th goal for Madrid followed three days later in a 2–0 win against [Rayo Vallecano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayo_Vallecano "Rayo Vallecano"). He finished the season with 48 league goals, winning a second consecutive Pichichi Trophy and the European Golden Shoe for a record fourth time.

#### 2015–2018: All-time Champions League and Real Madrid top scorer and fifth Ballon d'Or

Ronaldo became Real Madrid's [all-time top scorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Real_Madrid_CF_records_and_statistics#Goalscorers "List of Real Madrid CF records and statistics") on 12 September 2015 against [Espanyol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCD_Espanyol "RCD Espanyol"), his 230 goals surpassing the total of the previous record holder, [Raúl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%BAl_Gonz%C3%A1lez "Raúl González"). He also became the [all-time top scorer in the Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Cup_and_UEFA_Champions_League_top_scorers "List of European Cup and UEFA Champions League top scorers") with a hat-trick in the first group match against [Shakhtar Donetsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Shakhtar_Donetsk "FC Shakhtar Donetsk") in the [2015–16 competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_UEFA_Champions_League "2015–16 UEFA Champions League"). Two goals against Malmö FF in a 2–0 win on 30 September saw him reach 500 career goals for club and country. In the [Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_UEFA_Champions_League_final "2016 UEFA Champions League final"), Ronaldo scored the winning goal in the [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football) "Penalty shoot-out (association football)") against [city rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid_derby "Madrid derby") Atlético Madrid, winning his third Champions League title. He won the [2016 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Ballon_d%27Or "2016 Ballon d'Or") and the inaugural [Best FIFA Men's Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_FIFA_Football_Awards_2016 "The Best FIFA Football Awards 2016").

In the [2016–17 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_UEFA_Champions_League "2016–17 UEFA Champions League") quarter-finals against [Bayern Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Bayern_Munich "FC Bayern Munich") in April 2017, Ronaldo scored both goals in a 2–1 win which saw him become the first player to reach 100 goals in UEFA club competition. On 17 May, he scored twice against [Celta de Vigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RC_Celta_de_Vigo "RC Celta de Vigo") to surpass [Jimmy Greaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Greaves "Jimmy Greaves") as the all-time top scorer in the top five European leagues. He finished the season with 42 goals in all competitions and helped Madrid win the [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_La_Liga "2016–17 La Liga") title. In the [2017 Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_UEFA_Champions_League_Final "2017 UEFA Champions League Final"), Ronaldo scored two goals in a 4–1 victory over [Juventus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juventus_FC "Juventus FC") to bring him to 12 goals for the season, making him the competition's top goalscorer for the fifth straight season.

On 23 October, his performances throughout 2017 saw him awarded [The Best FIFA Men's Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_FIFA_Football_Awards_2017#The_Best_FIFA_Men's_Player "The Best FIFA Football Awards 2017") award for the second consecutive year. A day later, Ronaldo won the [2017 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Ballon_d%27Or "2017 Ballon d'Or"), receiving his fifth-time award on the [Eiffel Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower "Eiffel Tower") in Paris. On 3 April 2018, Ronaldo scored the first two goals in a 3–0 away win against Juventus in the [quarter-finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_UEFA_Champions_League_knockout_phase#Juventus_v_Real_Madrid "2017–18 UEFA Champions League knockout phase") of the [2017–18 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_UEFA_Champions_League "2017–18 UEFA Champions League"), with his second goal being an acrobatic [bicycle kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_kick "Bicycle kick"). Described as a "[PlayStation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation "PlayStation") goal" by Juventus defender [Andrea Barzagli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Barzagli "Andrea Barzagli"), with Ronaldo's foot approximately 7 ft 7 in (2.31 m) off the ground, it garnered him a standing ovation from the opposing fans in the stadium as well as a plethora of plaudits from peers, pundits and coaches.

In the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_UEFA_Champions_League_Final "2018 UEFA Champions League Final") on 26 May, Madrid defeated Liverpool 3–1, winning Ronaldo his fifth Champions League title, the first player to do so. He finished as the top scorer of the tournament for the sixth consecutive season with 15 goals. After the final, Ronaldo referred to his time with Madrid in the past tense, sparking speculation that he could leave the club.

### Juventus

#### 2018–2020: Consecutive Serie A titles

Ronaldo joined [Juventus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juventus "Juventus") in 2018 for €100 million, the transfer was the highest ever for a player over 30 years old and the highest paid by an Italian club. Upon signing, Ronaldo cited his need for a new challenge as his rationale for departing Madrid, but later attributed the transfer to the lack of support he felt was shown by club president [Florentino Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florentino_P%C3%A9rez "Florentino Pérez"). On 18 August, Ronaldo made his debut in a 3–2 away win against [Chievo Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.C._ChievoVerona "A.C. ChievoVerona"). On 19 September, in his first [Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_UEFA_Champions_League "2018–19 UEFA Champions League") match for Juve, he was sent off against Valencia, his first red card in 154 Champions League appearances. In the reverse (home) leg against Valencia, Cristiano won 100 Champions League matches, becoming the first ever player to do so.

Ronaldo won his first trophy with the club on 16 January 2019, the [2018 Supercoppa Italiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Supercoppa_Italiana "2018 Supercoppa Italiana"), after he scored the only goal from a header against [AC Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_Milan "AC Milan"). On 10 February, Ronaldo scored in a 3–0 win over Sassuolo, the ninth consecutive away game in which he had scored in the league, equalling [Giuseppe Signori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Signori "Giuseppe Signori")'s single season Serie A record of [most consecutive away games with at least one goal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_records_in_Italy#Most_consecutive_Serie_A_away_appearances_with_at_least_one_goal_scored_in_a_single_season "Football records in Italy"). On 12 March, Ronaldo scored a hat-trick in a 3–0 home win against Atlético in the second leg of the Champions League round of 16, helping Juventus overcome a two-goal deficit to reach the quarter-finals. On 20 April, Ronaldo played in the *[scudetto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Italian_football_champions "List of Italian football champions")* clinching game against [Fiorentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACF_Fiorentina "ACF Fiorentina"), as Juventus won their eighth successive title after a 2–1 home win, thereby becoming the first player to win league titles in [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League "Premier League"), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga "La Liga") and [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serie_A "Serie A"). With 21 goals and eight assists, Ronaldo won the [league award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serie_A_Awards "Serie A Awards") for Most Valuable Player.

On 1 October, he reached several milestones in Juventus's 3–0 [Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_UEFA_Champions_League "2019–20 UEFA Champions League") group stage win over [Bayer Leverkusen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayer_04_Leverkusen "Bayer 04 Leverkusen") including breaking [Iker Casillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iker_Casillas "Iker Casillas")' record for most Champions League wins of all time. On 18 December, Ronaldo leapt to a height of 8 ft 5 in (2.57 m), higher than the crossbar, to head the winning goal in a 2–1 away win against [Sampdoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Sampdoria "UC Sampdoria"). He scored his first Serie A hat-trick on 6 January 2020, in a 4–0 home win against [Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagliari_Calcio "Cagliari Calcio") and became only the second player to score hat-tricks in the Premier League, La Liga and Serie A. On 22 February, Ronaldo scored for a [record-equalling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_records_and_statistics_in_Italy#Most_consecutive_Serie_A_appearances_with_at_least_one_goal_scored_in_a_single_season "Football records and statistics in Italy") 11th consecutive league game, alongside [Gabriel Batistuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Batistuta "Gabriel Batistuta") and [Fabio Quagliarella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Quagliarella "Fabio Quagliarella"), in what was his 1,000th senior professional game, a 2–1 away win against [SPAL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.P.A.L. "S.P.A.L."). On 22 June, he scored a penalty in a 2–0 away win over [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna_FC_1909 "Bologna FC 1909"), overtaking [Rui Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rui_Costa "Rui Costa") to become the highest scoring Portuguese player in Serie A history. On 20 July, Ronaldo scored twice in a 2–1 home win over [Lazio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Lazio "SS Lazio"); his first goal was his 50th in Serie A. He became the first player in history to reach 50 goals in the Premier League, La Liga and Serie A, and becoming the second player after [Edin Džeko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edin_D%C5%BEeko "Edin Džeko") to score 50 goals in three of [Europe's top five major leagues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Five_(association_football) "Big Five (association football)"). Moreover, he became the oldest player, at the age of 35 years and 166 days, to score over 30 goals in one of the five top European leagues since [Ronnie Rooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronnie_Rooke "Ronnie Rooke") with Arsenal in 1948. On 26 July, Ronaldo scored the opening goal in a 2–0 home win over Sampdoria as Juventus were crowned Serie A champions for a ninth consecutive time. On 7 August, Ronaldo scored two goals in a 2–1 home win against [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_Lyonnais "Olympique Lyonnais") in the second leg of the Champions League round of 16, which saw him finish the season with 37 goals in all competitions; the tally allowed him to break Borel's club record of 36 goals in a single season.

#### 2020–2021: 100 Juve goals, *Capocannoniere*, and departure

Ronaldo played his 100th match in all competitions for Juventus on 13 December, scoring two penalties in a 3–1 away win over [Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genoa_CFC "Genoa CFC") in the league to bring his goal tally to 79. On 2 March 2021, he scored a goal in a 3–0 win over [Spezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spezia_Calcio "Spezia Calcio") in his 600th league match, to become the first player to score at least 20 goals in 12 consecutive seasons in the top five leagues of Europe. On 12 May, Ronaldo scored a goal in a 3–1 away win over Sassuolo to reach his 100th goal for Juventus in all competitions on his 131st appearance, becoming the fastest Juventus player to achieve the feat. With Juventus's victory in the [2021 Coppa Italia Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Coppa_Italia_Final "2021 Coppa Italia Final") on 19 May, Ronaldo became the first player in history to win every major domestic trophy in England, Spain and Italy. Ronaldo ended the season with 29 league goals, winning the *[Capocannoniere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capocannoniere "Capocannoniere")* award for highest goalscorer and becoming the first footballer to finish as top scorer in the English, Spanish and Italian leagues.

The start of the following season came amid reports Ronaldo would depart the club before the closure of the transfer window, despite Ronaldo and his agent [Jorge Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Mendes "Jorge Mendes") reaching a verbal agreement with [Manchester City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_City_F.C. "Manchester City F.C.") over personal terms, but the club pulled out of the deal, and later it was confirmed that City's [rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_derby "Manchester derby") Manchester United, Ronaldo's former club, were in advanced talks to sign him, while former manager [Alex Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Ferguson "Alex Ferguson") and several ex-teammates had been in contact to persuade him to re-sign for United.

### Return to Manchester United

#### 2021–2023: Champions League appearances record and 100 Premier League goals

On 27 August 2021, Manchester United announced they had reached an agreement with Juventus to re-sign Ronaldo, subject to agreement of personal terms, visa and medical. Ronaldo was given the number 7 shirt after [Edinson Cavani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinson_Cavani "Edinson Cavani") agreed to switch to 21. The first 24 hours of Ronaldo's shirt sales was reported to have broken the all-time record following a transfer, overtaking Messi after his move to [Paris Saint-Germain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Saint-Germain_F.C. "Paris Saint-Germain F.C.").

On 11 September, Ronaldo made his second debut at Old Trafford, scoring the opening two goals in a 4–1 league victory against [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C. "Newcastle United F.C."). On 29 September, he scored a last-minute winner in United's 2–1 victory at home to [Villarreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF "Villarreal CF") in the [Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_UEFA_Champions_League_group_stage#Group_F "2021–22 UEFA Champions League group stage"), and overtook [Iker Casillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iker_Casillas "Iker Casillas") as the player with the most appearances in the competition. Ronaldo proved to be crucial in the next Champions League fixtures, scoring various last-minute goals to help United qualify for the [round of 16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_UEFA_Champions_League_knockout_phase#Round_of_16 "2021–22 UEFA Champions League knockout phase") as group winners. On 2 December, Ronaldo scored two goals in a 3–2 home league win against [Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C. "Arsenal F.C."), which saw him surpass [800 career goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_footballers_with_500_or_more_goals "List of footballers with 500 or more goals"). Struggles ensued, with a fractured relationship with his teammates and interim manager, continuing for two months, until he scored in United's 2–0 win at home versus [Brighton & Hove Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_%26_Hove_Albion_F.C. "Brighton & Hove Albion F.C.") on 15 February 2022, his first in the new year. He finished the season with 24 goals in all competitions being named in the Premier League Team of the Year and the winner of United's [Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Matt_Busby_Player_of_the_Year "Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year") award, but United finished in a disappointing sixth place and qualified for the [UEFA Europa League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Europa_League "UEFA Europa League"); as a result, Ronaldo went trophyless for the first time since 2010.

After growing dissatisfaction with the direction of United on and off the field, Ronaldo desired to leave to join a club competing in the Champions League, but a move failed to materialise, with various European clubs refusing a transfer, due to his age, overall cost of a transfer and high wage demands. Shortly after, he fell out with manager [Erik ten Hag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_ten_Hag "Erik ten Hag") who used him as a substitute, leading United to terminate his contract on 22 November, following an interview with [Piers Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piers_Morgan "Piers Morgan"), where Ronaldo said that he felt "betrayed" by Ten Hag and criticised the management of the club.

### Al-Nassr

#### 2023–present: "Revolution" in Asian Football and Saudi Pro League champion

On 30 December 2022, Saudi Arabian club [Al-Nassr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nassr_FC "Al-Nassr FC") reached an agreement for Ronaldo to join the club, signing a contract until 2025. Ronaldo received [the highest football salary ever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_sports_contracts "List of largest sports contracts"), at €200 million per year, including a guaranteed football salary of €90 million, with commercial and sponsorship deals bringing his total annual salary to €200 million. He made his debut for Al-Nassr on 22 January 2023, as club captain, playing the full 90 minutes of a 1–0 win over [Al-Ettifaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ettifaq_FC "Al-Ettifaq FC"), and scored his first goal in a 2–2 draw against [Al-Fateh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Fateh_SC "Al Fateh SC") by converting a last-minute penalty. On 9 February, Ronaldo scored all four goals in a 4–0 win over [Al-Wehda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Wehda_FC "Al Wehda FC"), his first goal of the match being his 500th career league goal. According to the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC"), Ronaldo's transfer to Al-Nassr led a "revolution" in Asian football, with many players from other leagues, particularly those in Europe, transferring to Saudi Pro League clubs for the [2023–24 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Saudi_Pro_League "2023–24 Saudi Pro League").

In the final of the [Arab Club Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Club_Champions_Cup "Arab Club Champions Cup") on 12 August, Ronaldo scored both goals as they defeated rivals [Al-Hilal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hilal_SFC "Al Hilal SFC") 2–1 after extra time. Ronaldo scored six goals in the competition. At the close of the year, Ronaldo had scored 54 goals in all competitions for Al-Nassr and Portugal, making him the outright top scorer in 2023, reaching the same goalscoring record as in 2016. On 27 May 2024, in Al-Nassr's home fixture against [Al-Ittihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ittihad_Club_(Jeddah) "Al-Ittihad Club (Jeddah)"), Ronaldo scored his 34th and 35th league goals of the campaign, surpassing [Abderrazak Hamdallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abderrazak_Hamdallah "Abderrazak Hamdallah")'s record for the most goals scored in a single Saudi Pro League season. He also became the first footballer to finish as top scorer in four different leagues: the English, Spanish, Italian and Saudi leagues. On 31 May, in a 5–4 penalty shoot-out defeat to Al-Hilal in the [2024 King Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_King_Cup_final "2024 King Cup final") following a 1–1 draw after extra time (in which he scored his side's second spot kick), he equalled [Rogério Ceni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rog%C3%A9rio_Ceni "Rogério Ceni")'s record for most top-level matches by a male professional footballer (1,225).

On 30 January 2025, he became the first player to reach 700 club-level wins, while also scoring a goal in a 2–1 away victory against [Al Raed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Raed_FC "Al Raed FC"). On 28 February, Ronaldo played his 100th match in all competitions for Al-Nassr, a 2–1 away loss to Al-Orobah. On 26 May, he scored his 800th career club goal in a 3–2 away loss to [Al Fateh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Fateh_SC "Al Fateh SC") on the final day of the season. After the match, he hinted at his departure, but ended up extending his contract with Al Nassr until 2027. Despite that, he finished the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Saudi_Pro_League "2024–25 Saudi Pro League") with 25 league goals, becoming the league's top scorer for a second consecutive time.

On 23 August, Ronaldo scored his 100th goal for Al-Nassr with a penalty, becoming the first player in history to reach the milestone of 100 goals for four different clubs. In early February 2026, Ronaldo reportedly refused to play for Al‑Nassr in protest against Saudi Arabia's [Public Investment Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Investment_Fund "Public Investment Fund") (PIF), citing concerns over the club's transfer strategy and competitive standing, despite being fully fit. He returned to action, on 14 February, scoring a goal in a 2–0 away win over Al Fateh. On 21 February, he scored twice in 4–0 victory over Al-Hazm, his first goal made him the first player in football history to score 500 career goals after turning 30 years old. On 21 May, he scored twice in a 4–1 victory over [Damac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damac_FC "Damac FC"), securing his club's the [Saudi Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Saudi_Pro_League "2025–26 Saudi Pro League") title in the final match, marking Ronaldo's first league title win in five years and Al-Nassr's first league title since the 2018–19 season.

## International career

At the age of 18, Ronaldo made his debut for Portugal as a substitute against [Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_national_football_team "Kazakhstan national football team") on 20 August 2003. At [UEFA Euro 2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2004 "UEFA Euro 2004"), he scored his first international goal in a 2–1 group stage loss to [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece_national_football_team "Greece national football team") in his eighth international appearance. Despite Portugal losing to Greece again in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2004_final "UEFA Euro 2004 final"), Ronaldo made the [team of the tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_European_Championship_awards#Team_of_the_Tournament "UEFA European Championship awards"), with two assists and two goals. In the [2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup "2006 FIFA World Cup"), at the age of 21 years and 132 days, Ronaldo became the youngest ever goalscorer for Portugal at a World Cup finals. Portugal reached the semi-finals, where Portugal lost to [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_national_football_team "France national football team"), with Ronaldo being booed during their defeat, due to an incident that occurred in the quarter-finals against [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_football_team "England national football team"). FIFA's Technical Study Group overlooked him for the tournament's Best Young Player award and handed it to [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_national_football_team "Germany national football team")'s [Lukas Podolski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Podolski "Lukas Podolski"), citing his behaviour as a factor in the decision.

On 6 February 2007, Cristiano [captained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Portugal_national_football_team_captains "List of Portugal national football team captains") Portugal for the first time in a friendly against [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_national_football_team "Brazil national football team"). He wore the number 7 shirt ahead of [UEFA Euro 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2008 "UEFA Euro 2008"). Despite scoring eight goals in [qualifications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2008_qualification "UEFA Euro 2008 qualification"), the second-highest tally, he scored just one goal in the finals, the second goal of Portugal's 3–1 win in the group stage game against the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic_national_football_team "Czech Republic national football team"). Portugal were eliminated in the quarter-finals after a 3–2 loss to [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_national_football_team "Germany national football team").

Ronaldo failed to score in [2010 World Cup qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_(UEFA) "2010 FIFA World Cup qualification (UEFA)"). He scored only one goal in the [2010 World Cup finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIFA_World_Cup "2010 FIFA World Cup"); despite this, he was named man of the match in all three group stage matches, against [Ivory Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_Coast_national_football_team "Ivory Coast national football team"), [North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea_national_football_team "North Korea national football team") and Brazil. His only goal of the tournament came in their 7–0 demolishing of North Korea, which was his first international goal in 16 months.

Two years later, in [UEFA Euro 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2012 "UEFA Euro 2012"), Ronaldo was joint top scorer with three goals, enough for him to be included in the team of the tournament. During [qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_(UEFA) "2014 FIFA World Cup qualification (UEFA)") for the [2014 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup "2014 FIFA World Cup"), Ronaldo scored eight goals. Despite this, Portugal did not qualify directly; Ronaldo scored all four goals against [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men%27s_national_football_team "Sweden men's national football team") in the play-off to qualify. At the tournament in Brazil, Ronaldo assisted a last-minute 2–2 equaliser against the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men%27s_national_soccer_team "United States men's national soccer team"), and scored an 80th-minute winner in a 2–1 win over [Ghana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_national_football_team "Ghana national football team").

In 2016, Ronaldo led Portugal to their first-ever trophy at the [UEFA Euro 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016 "UEFA Euro 2016"), although he was subbed off in the 25th minute in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016_final "UEFA Euro 2016 final") against hosts France. He received the [Silver Boot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016#Awards "UEFA Euro 2016") as the second-highest goalscorer, which gave him his [fourth Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Ballon_d%27Or "2016 Ballon d'Or") later that year. With the win, Portugal qualified and made its only appearance in the [FIFA Confederations Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Confederations_Cup "FIFA Confederations Cup") held in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_FIFA_Confederations_Cup "2017 FIFA Confederations Cup"), where they finished in third place, with Ronaldo being named man of the match in all three of Portugal's group stage matches.

In the [2018 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup "2018 FIFA World Cup"), Ronaldo became the oldest player to score a [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_hat-tricks "List of FIFA World Cup hat-tricks") in a World Cup match, in a 3–3 draw against [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_football_team "Spain national football team"). The following match, Ronaldo scored the only goal in a 1–0 win against [Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco_national_football_team "Morocco national football team"), breaking Puskás' record as the highest European goalscorer of all time, with 85 international goals. For his performances in the tournament, Ronaldo was named in the [World Cup Dream Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup#Dream_Team "2018 FIFA World Cup").

Ronaldo led Portugal to victory in the inaugural [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_UEFA_Nations_League "2018–19 UEFA Nations League") in 2019, receiving the [top scorer award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UEFA_Nations_League_Finals#Awards "2019 UEFA Nations League Finals") in [the finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UEFA_Nations_League_Finals "2019 UEFA Nations League Finals"), and received the [Golden Boot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020#Awards "UEFA Euro 2020") as top scorer of [Euro 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020 "UEFA Euro 2020"). Ronaldo also became the first player to score at five European Championships. On 23 June 2021, Ronaldo scored two goals in the Euro 2020 match against France, breaking the men's goalscoring record previously held by [Ali Daei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Daei "Ali Daei").

On 9 October, he scored the opening goal in a 3–0 friendly win over [Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_national_football_team "Qatar national football team") at the [Estádio Algarve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est%C3%A1dio_Algarve "Estádio Algarve"); with his 181st international appearance, he also overtook [Sergio Ramos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Ramos "Sergio Ramos")'s [record for the most international caps received by a European player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_men%27s_footballers_with_100_or_more_international_caps "List of men's footballers with 100 or more international caps"). In the following match against [Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_national_football_team "Luxembourg national football team") on 12 October, also played at the Estádio Algarve, Ronaldo scored a hat-trick in a 5–0 win for Portugal, thus becoming the first player to score 10 [hat-tricks in men's international football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_footballers_who_achieved_hat-trick_records "List of footballers who achieved hat-trick records").

In the [2022 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup "2022 FIFA World Cup"), on 24 November, Ronaldo became the first male player to score at five World Cups, with a penalty against Ghana. Following a dispute with manager [Fernando Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Santos_(footballer,_born_1954) "Fernando Santos (footballer, born 1954)"), after their last group game against [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea_national_football_team "South Korea national football team"), he was dropped from the starting line-up for Portugal's last 16 match, marking the first time since [Euro 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro_2008 "Euro 2008") that he had not started a game for Portugal in a major international tournament, and the first time Portugal had started a knockout game without Ronaldo in the starting line-up at an international tournament since [Euro 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2000 "UEFA Euro 2000").

On 23 March 2023, Ronaldo scored two goals against [Liechtenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liechtenstein_national_football_team "Liechtenstein national football team") as he earned his 197th overall cap to become the most capped male footballer of all time. On 20 June, Ronaldo made his 200th appearance for his national team, scoring the only goal of an away win over Iceland, in the [Euro qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2024_qualifying "UEFA Euro 2024 qualifying"), as he became the first player in the history of men's international football to make 200 appearances for his country. On 16 October, Portugal secured first place in their qualifying group, following a 5–0 away victory over [Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina_national_football_team "Bosnia and Herzegovina national football team"), with Ronaldo scoring twice.

With his start in Portugal's [UEFA Euro 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2024 "UEFA Euro 2024") opener against the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic_national_football_team "Czech Republic national football team"), Ronaldo became the first player to feature in six European Championships, having previously been the first player to appear in five. Later that year, on 5 September, he scored his 900th career goal in a 2–1 victory over [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia_national_football_team "Croatia national football team") in the [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Nations_League "2024–25 UEFA Nations League"). On 15 November, Ronaldo broke the record of most international victories with 132, overtaking Sergio Ramos's record, following a 5–1 victory against Poland in the UEFA Nations League, where he scored twice. On 8 June 2025, Ronaldo won his second Nations League title, helping Portugal defeat Spain in the final, scoring eight goals in nine games in the tournament.

On 14 October, Ronaldo scored twice in a 2–2 draw against [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_national_football_team "Hungary national football team") in the [2026 World Cup qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification "2026 FIFA World Cup qualification"), reaching a record 41 goals in World Cup qualifying. Ronaldo was sent off for elbowing defender [Dara O'Shea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_O%27Shea "Dara O'Shea") in the back during Portugal's 2–0 loss to [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland_national_football_team "Republic of Ireland national football team") in the World Cup qualifiers in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin "Dublin") on 14 November. FIFA issued Ronaldo a three-month ban and one year of probation for his conduct, but cancelled the ban on 5 December, ensuring his availability for the World Cup.

On 19 May 2026, Ronaldo was selected in the 26-man Portugal squad for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup "2026 FIFA World Cup"), his joint-record sixth participation in the tournament. In Portugal's second group match on 23 June, he scored twice in a 5–0 victory against [Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan_national_football_team "Uzbekistan national football team"), becoming the first player to score in six FIFA World Cup tournaments and taking his World Cup tally to 10 goals, surpassing Portugal's previous record of nine goals set by [Eusébio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eus%C3%A9bio "Eusébio"). On 2 July, he scored his first World Cup knock-out stage goal from a penalty in a 2–1 over Croatia in the round of 32. Four days later, Portugal were eliminated in the round of 16 following a 1–0 defeat to Spain, with [Mikel Merino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikel_Merino "Mikel Merino") scoring the match-winning goal in stoppage time. Overall, commentators thought Ronaldo's performance at the 2026 World Cup was ineffective and lacked impact.

Following the 2026 FIFA World Cup, Ronaldo considered international retirement but was persuaded to continue by Portuguese Football Federation (FPF) president [Pedro Proença](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Proen%C3%A7a "Pedro Proença"). Tensions with manager [Jorge Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Jesus "Jorge Jesus") emerged after Ronaldo was benched throughout a victory over [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway_national_football_team "Norway national football team"), prompting him to leave Portugal's training camp on 30 September. Jesus subsequently described the dispute as a misunderstanding, but Ronaldo accused him of breaking two promises in a statement on 6 October, while apologising and expressing his willingness to return. On 10 October, the FPF provisionally suspended Ronaldo pending disciplinary proceedings, although Proença supported his eventual return.

## Player profile

### Style of play

A versatile [attacker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_(association_football) "Forward (association football)"), Ronaldo is capable of playing on either wing as well as through the centre of the pitch, and, while ostensibly right-footed, is very strong with both feet. Tactically, Ronaldo has undergone several evolutions throughout his career. While at Sporting and during his first season at Manchester United, he was typically deployed as a traditional [winger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midfielder#Winger "Midfielder") on the right side of midfield, where he regularly looked to deliver [crosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_(football) "Cross (football)") into the penalty area. In this position, he was able to use his pace and acceleration, agility and technical skills to take on opponents in one-on-one situations. Ronaldo became noted for his [dribbling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dribbling#Association_football "Dribbling") and flair, often displaying an array of [tricks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football_tactics_and_skills "Association football tactics and skills") and [feints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dummy_(football) "Dummy (football)"), such as the [step overs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_over "Step over") and so-called 'chops' that became his trademark; he has also been known to use the [flip-flap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip_flap_(association_football) "Flip flap (association football)").

His strength and jumping ability, combined with his elevation, heading accuracy and height of 1.87 m (6 ft 1+1⁄2 in), give him an edge in winning aerial duels. These attributes allow him to function as a target-man and make him an aerial goal threat in the penalty area; consequently, many of his goals have been headers. Ronaldo holds the record for the highest recorded jump in football history, measuring 2.93 metres (9 ft 7 in), which he achieved during a match against Manchester United while playing for Real Madrid in the round of 16 of the UEFA Champions League in 2013. Allied with his increased stamina and work-rate, his goalscoring ability improved drastically on the left wing where he was given the positional freedom to move into the centre to finish attacks. He has also increasingly played a [creative role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playmaker "Playmaker") for his team, often dropping deep to pick up the ball, participate in the build-up of plays and create chances for his teammates, courtesy of his vision and passing ability.

In his final seasons at United, Ronaldo played an even more attacking and central role, functioning both as a [striker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_(association_football)#Striker "Forward (association football)") and as a [supporting forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_(association_football)#Second_striker "Forward (association football)"), or even as an [attacking midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midfielder#Attacking_midfielder "Midfielder") on occasion. He developed into a prolific goalscorer, capable of finishing well both inside the penalty area and from distance with an accurate and powerful shot, courtesy of his striking ability. An accurate [penalty kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_(association_football) "Penalty kick (association football)") taker, he also became a set piece specialist, renowned for his powerful, [bending](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curl_(association_football) "Curl (association football)") [free kicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_free_kick "Direct free kick"). When taking free kicks, Ronaldo is known for using the *[knuckleball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knuckleball#Association_football "Knuckleball")* technique, which was developed by [Juninho Pernambucano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juninho_Pernambucano "Juninho Pernambucano"). He also adopts a trademark stance before striking the ball, which involves him standing with his legs far apart. Regarding Ronaldo's unique style of taking free kicks, former United assistant manager [Mike Phelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Phelan "Mike Phelan") commented: "People used to put the ball down, walk away, run up and hit it. He brought in a more dynamic showmanship. He places the ball down, the concentration level is high, he takes his certain amount of steps back so that his standing foot is in the perfect place to hit the ball in the sweet spot. He is the ultimate showman. He has that slight arrogance. When he pulls those shorts up and shows his thighs, he is saying 'All eyes on me' and this is going in. He understands the marketing side of it. The way he struts up and places it; the world is watching him."

At Real Madrid, Ronaldo continued to play a more offensive role, while his creative and defensive duties became more limited, although not entirely diminished. Initially deployed as a [centre forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_(association_football)#Centre-forward "Forward (association football)") by managers [Manuel Pellegrini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Pellegrini "Manuel Pellegrini") and [José Mourinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mourinho "José Mourinho"), he was later moved back onto the left wing, though in a free tactical role; this position allowed him to drift into the centre at will to get onto the end of crosses and score, or draw out defenders with his movement off the ball and leave space for teammates to exploit. Madrid's counter-attacking style of play also allowed him to become a more efficient and consistent player, as evidenced by his record-breaking goalscoring feats. While he mainly drew praise in the media for his prolific goalscoring, Ronaldo also demonstrated his ability as an effective creator in this role. This unique role has been described by pundits as that of a "false", "attacking", or "goalscoring winger", as Ronaldo effectively almost functioned as a striker at times with his central runs into the penalty area, despite actually playing on the left flank. From 2013 onwards, under manager [Carlo Ancelotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Ancelotti "Carlo Ancelotti"), he effectively adapted his style to the physical effects of ageing with increasingly reduced off-the-ball movement and general involvement, completing fewer dribbles and passes per game, and instead focusing on short-distance creating and goalscoring. Since 2017, Ronaldo adapted his style of play yet again to become more of a free-roaming centre forward under manager [Zinedine Zidane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinedine_Zidane "Zinedine Zidane"), a role in which he continued to excel and maintain a prolific goalscoring record; in this position, he earned praise in the media for his intelligent movement both on and off the ball, positional sense, link-up play and finishing, as well as his ability to lose or anticipate his markers, find space in the box and score from few touches or opportunities.

In his first season at Juventus, Ronaldo continued to play in a variety of different attacking roles under manager [Massimiliano Allegri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massimiliano_Allegri "Massimiliano Allegri"), depending on whom he was partnered with. While he had occupied an increasingly offensive role in his final years at Real Madrid, at times he functioned in a free role at Juventus, either as a lone striker or in his trademark role on the left wing, in a 4–2–3–1 or 4–3–3 formation, in which he often switched positions with [Mario Mandžukić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Mand%C5%BEuki%C4%87 "Mario Mandžukić"). In this role, he was also given licence to drop deep or even out wide onto the right flank to receive the ball, and be more involved in the build-up of plays; as such, aside from scoring goals himself, he began to take on opponents and create chances for other players with greater frequency than he had in his final seasons with Real Madrid. Off the ball, he was also capable of creating space for teammates with his movement and attacking runs into the box, or finishing off chances with his head or feet by getting onto the end of his teammates' crosses. On occasion he also played in an attacking partnership alongside Mandžukić in a 4–3–1–2, 4–4–2, or 3–5–2 formation. He continued to play a similar role in his second season with the club under manager [Maurizio Sarri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurizio_Sarri "Maurizio Sarri").

### Reception and image

Ronaldo is widely regarded as one of the two best players of his generation, alongside [Lionel Messi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi "Lionel Messi"). At age 23, Ronaldo won his first [Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or "Ballon d'Or") in [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Ballon_d%27Or "2008 Ballon d'Or") by a record-high vote count, and over the following decade he has often featured in debates and rankings about who is the greatest player in history. He is acclaimed for his prolific and consistent goal-scoring, especially in important and high-pressure situations. In an article for *[Bleacher Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleacher_Report "Bleacher Report")*, Ronaldo placed first in a ranking of the 50 most influential people in sports culture in 2018.

Ronaldo is noted for his work ethic, elite body conditioning and dedication to improvement on the training pitch, as well as being regarded as a natural leader. On his longevity and "extraordinary commitment to physical preparation", Adam Bate of [Sky Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Sports "Sky Sports") said: "Dedication is a huge part of staying at the top and Ronaldo's focus is perhaps unparalleled within the game." Former Brazil player [Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldo_(Brazilian_footballer) "Ronaldo (Brazilian footballer)") praised Cristiano's approach to training, arguing that "there are so few players who take care of their body like he does. I trained because I had to, he does it because he loves it." His drive and determination to succeed are fuelled by a desire to be talked about alongside other greats such as [Pelé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pel%C3%A9 "Pelé") and [Diego Maradona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Maradona "Diego Maradona") once retiring. He is credited, along with coach [José Mourinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mourinho "José Mourinho"), with helping to change the fortunes of Portuguese football in the 2010s and 2020s. At times, he has been criticised for [simulating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diving_(association_football) "Diving (association football)") when tackled. He was also occasionally criticised early in his career by manager Alex Ferguson, teammates and the media for being a selfish or overly flamboyant player. [Jonathan Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Wilson_(writer) "Jonathan Wilson (writer)") of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* opined that Ronaldo had made Juventus, who he joined aged 33 in 2018, weaker, due to "his relative immobility" in his mid-30s, even if his personal goal-scoring output remained high. Former Manchester United manager [Alex Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Ferguson "Alex Ferguson") has described Ronaldo as a "complete player".

During his career, Ronaldo has also been described as having an "arrogant image" on the pitch, with Ronaldo stating that he had become a "victim" because of how he was portrayed in the media. He is often seen moaning, gesticulating and scowling while trying to inspire his team to victory, with Ronaldo insisting that his competitive nature should not be mistaken for arrogance. His managers, teammates and various journalists have said that this reputation has caused an unfair image of him.

### Goal celebrations

Ronaldo has adopted several [goal celebrations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_celebration "Goal celebration") throughout his career, including one particular celebration which gained widespread coverage in the media, when he squatted and stared directly into a camera on the sidelines of the pitch with his hand on his chin. After scoring a goal, he usually celebrates with a "storming jump" and "turn", before "landing in spread-eagled fashion" into his "signature power stance", while usually simultaneously exclaiming "Sí" (Spanish and Italian for "yes"). This trademark celebration has been dubbed the "Siu" or "siuuu" in the media.

Ronaldo first performed the "Siu" celebration on 7 August 2013, during the [2013 International Champions Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_International_Champions_Cup "2013 International Champions Cup") between Real Madrid and [Chelsea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_F.C. "Chelsea F.C."). During an interview after the match, Ronaldo explained he scored the goal and "it just felt natural" and "didn't know where it came from". The phrase "Siu" is derived from Portuguese *sim*, meaning "yes". This was confirmed by Ronaldo in an interview in 2023, almost a decade since he first performed it. Ronaldo explained that the phrase "Siuuu" simply means yes, but "meaning it very strongly".

### Rivalry with Lionel Messi

Both Ronaldo and Lionel Messi have scored in multiple [UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League "UEFA Champions League") finals, have regularly broken the 50-goal barrier in a single season, and are the two [leading goal scorers in history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_footballers_with_500_or_more_goals "List of footballers with 500 or more goals"). Sports journalists and pundits regularly weigh the individual merits of both players in an attempt to argue who they believe is the best player in modern football or in the history of the game. It has been compared to several [sports rivalries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sports_rivalries "List of sports rivalries"), among them the [Muhammad Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali "Muhammad Ali")–[Joe Frazier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Frazier "Joe Frazier") rivalry in boxing, the [Borg–McEnroe rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borg%E2%80%93McEnroe_rivalry "Borg–McEnroe rivalry") in tennis and the [Senna–Prost rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senna%E2%80%93Prost_rivalry "Senna–Prost rivalry") from [Formula One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One "Formula One") motor racing. Some commentators choose to analyse the differing physiques and playing styles of the two. Part of the debate revolves around the contrasting personalities of the two players, as Ronaldo is sometimes depicted as an arrogant and theatrical showoff, while Messi is portrayed as a shy, humble character.

In a 2012 interview, Ronaldo commented on the rivalry, saying: "I think we push each other sometimes in the competition, this is why the competition is so high." [Alex Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Ferguson "Alex Ferguson"), Ronaldo's manager during his time at Manchester United, opined: "I don't think the rivalry against each other bothers them. I think they have their own personal pride in terms of wanting to be the best." Messi himself denied any rivalry, saying that it was "only the media, the press, who wants us to be at loggerheads but I've never fought with Cristiano." Responding to the claims that he and Messi do not get on well on a personal level, Ronaldo commented: "We don't have a relationship outside the world of football, just as we don't with a lot of other players." Ronaldo added that in years to come he hopes they can laugh about it together, stating: "We have to look on this rivalry with a positive spirit, because it's a good thing." Representing archrivals Barcelona and Real Madrid, the two players faced each other at least twice every season in the world's biggest club game, *[El Clásico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico "El Clásico")*, which is among the world's most viewed annual sporting events.

In a debate at [Oxford Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Union "Oxford Union") in October 2013, when asked whether FIFA president [Sepp Blatter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sepp_Blatter "Sepp Blatter") preferred Messi or Ronaldo, Blatter paid tribute to the work ethic of the Argentine before taking a swipe at Ronaldo, claiming "one of them has more expenses for the hairdresser than the other." Real Madrid demanded and promptly received a full apology. In response to Blatter's "commander" on the pitch comment, Ronaldo issued his own riposte with a mock-salute celebration after scoring a penalty against Sevilla. In August 2019, Ronaldo and Messi were interviewed while sat next to each other prior to the announcement of the [UEFA Men's Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Men%27s_Player_of_the_Year_Award "UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award"), with Ronaldo stating: "I pushed him and he pushed me as well. So it's good to be part of the history of football."

In September 2023, Ronaldo declared that his rivalry with Messi was over and "gone", after 36 official fixtures and 15 years of "sharing the stage".

## In popular culture

### Popularity and online following

Ronaldo was named in the 2014 [*Time* 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100"), *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")*'s annual list of the most influential people in the world. [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN "ESPN") named him the world's most famous athlete in 2016, 2017, 2018 and 2019. Demand for a replica Ronaldo shirt has been high throughout his career. In 2008, Ronaldo's number 7 Manchester United shirt was the best-selling Premier League sports product. In 2015, Ronaldo's number 7 Real Madrid shirt was the second best-selling worldwide, after Messi's number 10 Barcelona shirt. In 2018, within 24 hours of his number 7 Juventus shirt being released, over 520,000 had been sold, with $62.4 million generated in one day.

Ronaldo has established a strong online presence. The most popular sportsperson on social media, he counted over 500 million total followers across [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook "Facebook"), [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter") and [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") by February 2021, making him the first person to pass half a billion followers. The [most-followed person](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-followed_Facebook_pages "List of most-followed Facebook pages") on Facebook (171 million), [most-followed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-followed_Instagram_accounts "List of most-followed Instagram accounts") on Instagram (650 million) and [most-followed sportsperson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-followed_Twitter_accounts "List of most-followed Twitter accounts") on Twitter (115 million), his sponsors earned $936 million in media value across his accounts between June 2016 and June 2017.

After announcing the creation of his [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") channel, UR · Cristiano on 21 August 2024, it became the fastest to reach the 1 million subscribers milestone, doing so in 90 minutes. The channel also became the fastest to reach 5, 10, 20, 30 and 50 million subscribers. It also became the most subscribed channel in Portugal, surpassing the Portuguese [Nick Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Jr. "Nick Jr.") As of June 2026, the channel has 80 million subscribers.

### Wealth and sponsorships

In April 2026, [Sportico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penske_Media_Corporation#Sportico "Penske Media Corporation") ranked Ronaldo as the third highest-paid athlete in history, behind [Michael Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jordan "Michael Jordan") and [Tiger Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Woods "Tiger Woods"), with his income estimated at $2.52 billion. He has also been included in *Sportico'*s every annual ranking of the highest-paid athletes: he was named the third highest-paid athlete of 2021 ($120 million) and 2022 ($115 million) and the highest-paid athlete of 2023 ($275 million), 2024 ($260 million) and 2025 ($260 million). With earnings of €720 million (£615 million) from 2010 to 2019, Ronaldo was ranked second in *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* list of the decade's highest-paid athletes, behind boxer [Floyd Mayweather Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floyd_Mayweather_Jr. "Floyd Mayweather Jr.") *Forbes* ranked Ronaldo as the highest-paid athlete in the world in 2016 ($88 million), 2017 ($93 million) and from 2023 to 2025 ($136 million, $260 million and $275 million respectively), and he has entered [every top 10 since the ranking's inception in 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_list_of_the_world%27s_highest-paid_athletes "Forbes list of the world's highest-paid athletes"). He is the first footballer and only the third sportsman to earn $1 billion in their career.

Ronaldo is one of the world's most marketable sportsmen: *[SportsPro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SportsPro "SportsPro")* rated him the fifth most marketable athlete in 2012 and eighth most marketable athlete in 2013. Sports market research company Repucom named Ronaldo the most marketable and most recognised football player in the world in May 2014. Since his reputation grew at Manchester United, Ronaldo has signed many sponsorship deals for consumer products, including sportswear, football boots; since November 2012, Ronaldo has worn the Nike Mercurial Vapor personalised CR7 edition, soft drinks, clothing, automotive lubricants, financial services, electronics, and video games. Ronaldo featured as the cover star of [*FIFA* video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_(video_game_series) "FIFA (video game series)") *[FIFA 18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_18 "FIFA 18")* and was heavily involved in the game's promotion. His "Sii" [goal celebration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_celebration "Goal celebration") features in the *FIFA* series, accompanied with his own voiceover. He was also the face of *[Pro Evolution Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Evolution_Soccer "Pro Evolution Soccer")*, appearing on the covers of the [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Evolution_Soccer_2008 "Pro Evolution Soccer 2008"), [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Evolution_Soccer_2012 "Pro Evolution Soccer 2012") and [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Evolution_Soccer_2013 "Pro Evolution Soccer 2013") editions of the game.

### Philanthropy

Ronaldo has made contributions to various charitable causes throughout his career. Television footage of the [2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Indian_Ocean_earthquake_and_tsunami "2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami") showed an eight-year-old boy survivor named [Martunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martunis "Martunis") wearing a Portuguese football shirt who was stranded for 19 days after his family was killed. Following this, Ronaldo visited [Aceh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceh "Aceh"), Indonesia, to raise funds for rehabilitation and reconstruction. After accepting undisclosed damages from a libel case against *[The Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sun_(United_Kingdom) "The Sun (United Kingdom)")* newspaper in 2008, Ronaldo donated the damages to a charity in [Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira "Madeira").

In 2009, Ronaldo donated £100,000 to the hospital that saved his mother's life in [Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira "Madeira") following her battle with cancer, so that they could build a cancer centre on the island. In support of the victims of the [2010 Madeira flood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Madeira_floods_and_mudslides "2010 Madeira floods and mudslides"), Ronaldo pledged to play in a charity match in Madeira between Primeira Liga club Porto and players from Madeiran-based clubs [Marítimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.S._Mar%C3%ADtimo "C.S. Marítimo") and Nacional.

In 2012, Ronaldo and his agent paid for specialist treatment for a nine-year-old Canarian boy with apparently terminal cancer. In December 2012, Ronaldo joined FIFA's "11 for Health" programme to raise awareness among kids of how to steer clear of conditions including drug addiction, HIV, malaria, and obesity. In January 2013, Ronaldo became [Save the Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Save_the_Children "Save the Children")'s new Global Artist Ambassador, in which he hopes to help fight child hunger and obesity. In March 2013, Ronaldo agreed to be the ambassador for the [Mangrove Care Forum Bali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangrove_Care_Forum_Bali "Mangrove Care Forum Bali") in Indonesia, an organisation aiming to raise awareness of [mangrove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangrove "Mangrove") conservation.

Ronaldo was named the world's most charitable sportsperson in 2015 after donating £5 million to the relief effort after the [earthquake in Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2015_Nepal_earthquake "April 2015 Nepal earthquake") which killed over 8,000 people. In June 2016, Ronaldo donated the entirety of his €600,000 Champions League bonus after Real Madrid won the competition. In August of the same year, Ronaldo launched CR7Selfie, a [selfie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selfie "Selfie") app for charity to help Save the Children that lets participants take a selfie with him in one of several different outfits and poses.

### Eponyms and honours

In 2007, C.D. Nacional renamed its youth campus Cristiano Ronaldo Campus Futebol (Cristiano Ronaldo Football Campus). In December 2013, Ronaldo opened a museum, [Museu CR7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museu_CR7 "Museu CR7"), in his hometown of Funchal, Madeira, to house trophies and memorabilia; the museum is an official sponsor of the local football team [União da Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uni%C3%A3o_da_Madeira "União da Madeira"). At a ceremony held at the [Belém Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%A9m_Palace "Belém Palace") in January 2014, President of Portugal [Aníbal Cavaco Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An%C3%ADbal_Cavaco_Silva "Aníbal Cavaco Silva") raised Ronaldo to the rank of Grand Officer of the [Order of Prince Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Prince_Henry "Order of Prince Henry") "to distinguish an athlete of world renown who has been a symbol of Portugal globally, contributing to the international projection of the country and setting an example of tenacity for future generations." In June 2015, astronomers led by David Sobral from Lisbon and [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden "Leiden") discovered a galaxy which they named [Cosmos Redshift 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmos_Redshift_7 "Cosmos Redshift 7") (CR7) in tribute to Ronaldo.

On 23 July 2016, following Portugal's triumph at Euro 2016, Madeira Airport in Funchal was renamed as [Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo_International_Airport "Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport"). The name change was subject to much debate locally by some politicians and citizens, who even started a petition against the move, an action criticised by President of Madeira [Miguel Albuquerque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Albuquerque "Miguel Albuquerque"). On 21 September 2020, the Sporting CP's [football academy in Alcochete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Cristiano_Ronaldo "Academia Cristiano Ronaldo"), until then called *Academia Sporting*, was renamed *Academia Cristiano Ronaldo*. In 2023, the Lisbon City Council approved awarding him, at the proposal of its president, [Carlos Moedas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Moedas "Carlos Moedas"), the Medal of Honor of the City, because he is "a great Lisboner, in the sense of the passion he has for the city".

### Public art

In June 2010, during the build-up to the World Cup, Ronaldo became the fourth footballer, after [Steven Gerrard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Gerrard "Steven Gerrard"), Pelé, and David Beckham, to be represented as a waxwork at [Madame Tussauds London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_Tussauds_London "Madame Tussauds London"). Another waxwork of him was presented at the Madrid Wax Museum in December 2013. A bronze statue of Ronaldo, designed by artist Ricardo Madeira Veloso, was unveiled in Funchal in December 2014.

The unveiling of the rebranded Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport took place on 29 March 2017, which included a bust of his head being presented. The bust and the name change were controversial, with the lack of the bust's likeness to Ronaldo being ridiculed by comedians, including *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")*, A year later, sports website [Bleacher Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleacher_Report "Bleacher Report") commissioned sculptor [Emanuel Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emanuel_Santos "Emanuel Santos") to create another bust; however, this bust was never used and a new one was made by a Spanish sculptor, shown to the public on 15 June 2018.

### Business ventures

Ronaldo opened a fashion boutique under the name *CR7* (his initials and shirt number) on the island of Madeira in 2006 and opened a second in Lisbon in 2008. In partnership with Scandinavian manufacturer JBS Textile Group and the New York fashion designer [Richard Chai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Chai "Richard Chai"), Ronaldo co-designed a range of underwear and sock line, released in November 2013. He expanded his *CR7* fashion brand by launching a line of premium shirts and shoes in July 2014.

In September 2015, Ronaldo released his own fragrance, "Legacy", in a partnership with Eden Parfums. Since 26 October 2023, the new owners of [Medialivre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medialivre "Medialivre") (*[Correio da Manhã](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correio_da_Manh%C3%A3_(Portugal) "Correio da Manhã (Portugal)")*, *[Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_(Portuguese_newspaper) "Record (Portuguese newspaper)")* and *[Jornal de Negócios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jornal_de_Neg%C3%B3cios "Jornal de Negócios")* publisher) are a group of investors that include Cristiano Ronaldo through Portuguese company Expressão Livre. ## Personal life

### Family, children, and relationships

Ronaldo's father, José, died of an alcoholism-related liver condition in 2005 at age 52, when Ronaldo was 20. Ronaldo's mother, Dolores Aveiro, became a popular personality in Portugal after Ronaldo achieved fame. She has appeared in commercials for the brands [Maggi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggi "Maggi"), MultiOpticas and [Pingo Doce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingo_Doce "Pingo Doce").

Ronaldo has five children. His first child, a son named Cristiano Ronaldo Jr., was born in 2010 in the United States. Ronaldo Jr. is a youth footballer who has played for Portugal's under-15 team. Ronaldo has full custody of Ronaldo Jr. and has not publicly revealed the identity of his mother due to an agreement with her. Ronaldo is also a father to twins, born in 2017 in the U.S. via [surrogacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrogacy "Surrogacy").

Ronaldo was in a relationship with the English actress [Gemma Atkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Atkinson "Gemma Atkinson") for four months in 2007, then dated the Russian model [Irina Shayk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Shayk "Irina Shayk") from 2010 to 2015. He began a relationship with [Georgina Rodríguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Rodr%C3%ADguez "Georgina Rodríguez") in 2016 after meeting her while she was working as a sales assistant at a [Gucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gucci "Gucci") store in Madrid. Rodríguez gave birth to a daughter on 12 November 2017. The couple were expecting twins in 2022; the male twin died during childbirth while the female twin survived. Ronaldo and Rodríguez were married on 11 August 2026 in a civil ceremony in [Cascais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cascais "Cascais"), Portugal.

### Health

Ronaldo has said that he [does not drink alcohol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teetotalism "Teetotalism"); he received [libel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defamation "Defamation") damages over a *[Daily Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Mirror "Daily Mirror")* article that reported him drinking heavily in a nightclub while recovering from an injury in 2008.

### Legal issues

Ronaldo and another man were investigated by the British [Crown Prosecution Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prosecution_Service "Crown Prosecution Service") after a 2005 rape allegation was brought forward by two women. Within days, the women withdrew their allegation, and [Scotland Yard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland_Yard "Scotland Yard") later issued a statement declaring there was not enough evidence for a prosecution.

In 2017, it was reported that Ronaldo was being investigated by the [Las Vegas Police Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Metropolitan_Police_Department "Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department") for an allegation by a woman that he had raped her in 2009. Documents confirmed by Ronaldo's lawyers state that he paid a woman US$375,000 in a [non-disclosure settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-disclosure_agreement "Non-disclosure agreement") in 2010. Ronaldo and his lawyers issued a statement denying all accusations, describing them as an "intentional defamation campaign" relying on altered or fabricated documents, a claim which *[Der Spiegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Der_Spiegel_(website) "Der Spiegel (website)")* rejected. In 2019, [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas "Las Vegas") prosecutors said they would not charge Ronaldo based on the rape allegations, stating that the allegations could not be proven beyond a [reasonable doubt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reasonable_doubt "Reasonable doubt").

In 2018, the same woman filed a [civil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_law_(common_law) "Civil law (common law)") [lawsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawsuit "Lawsuit") in [Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada "Nevada") accusing Ronaldo of rape. *[The Daily Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Mirror "The Daily Mirror")*, citing court documents, reported in 2021 that she sought £56 million in [damages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damages "Damages") from Ronaldo. That year, federal judge Daniel Albregts recommended that the lawsuit be dismissed, stating that the woman's lawyer, Leslie Stovall, "acted in bad faith by asking for, receiving, and using [Football Leaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Leaks "Football Leaks") documents to prosecute" the case, despite the documents containing "privileged communications" between Ronaldo and his lawyers. Additionally, Albregts stated that no evidence was found of Ronaldo's lawyers having "intimidated [the woman] or impeded law enforcement" during the 2010 settlement with her. In 2022, the woman's rape lawsuit was [dismissed with prejudice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dismissed_with_prejudice "Dismissed with prejudice") in the [United States District Court for the District of Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_District_of_Nevada "United States District Court for the District of Nevada"), as district judge [Jennifer A. Dorsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_A._Dorsey "Jennifer A. Dorsey") ruled that Stovall's repeated use of "cyber-hacked [attorney–client privileged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney%E2%80%93client_privilege "Attorney–client privilege") documents" were actions representing "abuses and flagrant circumvention of the proper litigation process".

In 2007, Ronaldo pled guilty to driving 60 mph in a 40 mph zone in Manchester in 2006. He was fined £1,000 and given 6 [penalty points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_points "Penalty points") on his licence for speeding. In 2017, he was charged with fraudulently evading almost €15 million in tax in Spain between 2011 and 2014, a claim he denied at the time. In 2018, he was given a two-year suspended prison sentence and fined €18.8 million for tax evasion, which was later reduced to €16.8 million after he reached a deal with Spanish authorities. The sentence was served under [probation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probation "Probation"), without any prison time, on the condition that he would not re-offend.

Without endorsing any political agenda, Ronaldo said that a potential meeting with Trump would revolve around Ronaldo's desire for [world peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_peace "World peace"). On 18 November, Ronaldo and his [fiancée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fianc%C3%A9e "Fiancée") [Georgina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Rodr%C3%ADguez "Georgina Rodríguez") met Trump at the [White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House "White House").
