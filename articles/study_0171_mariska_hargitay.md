# Mariska Hargitay

**Mariska Magdolna Hargitay** (born January 23, 1964) is an American actress. She is best known for playing [Olivia Benson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Benson "Olivia Benson") on [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC")'s *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit")* since 1999, the longest-running character of all time in an American primetime drama. [Her accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Mariska_Hargitay "List of awards and nominations received by Mariska Hargitay") include three [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards") and a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globes "Golden Globes").

After attending [Marymount High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marymount_High_School "Marymount High School") in Los Angeles, she enrolled in the [UCLA School of Theater, Film and Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_School_of_Theater,_Film_and_Television "UCLA School of Theater, Film and Television"), leaving before completing her degree to pursue acting. Her credits outside *Law & Order: Special Victims Unit* include the series *[Falcon Crest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon_Crest "Falcon Crest")* and *[In the Heat of the Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Heat_of_the_Night_(TV_series) "In the Heat of the Night (TV series)")* (both 1988), *[Tequila and Bonetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tequila_and_Bonetti "Tequila and Bonetti")* (1992), *[Can't Hurry Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Hurry_Love "Can't Hurry Love")* (1995–1996), and *[ER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ER_(TV_series) "ER (TV series)")* (1997–1998). In 2013, she received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame").

Outside acting, Hargitay co-produced the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") documentary *[I Am Evidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Am_Evidence "I Am Evidence")* (2017), winning a [News and Documentary Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_and_Documentary_Emmy_Awards "News and Documentary Emmy Awards") for the project. She launched the production company Mighty Entertainment in 2025, under which she directed the documentary *[My Mom Jayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Mom_Jayne "My Mom Jayne")* (2025). She founded the Joyful Heart Foundation, which provides support to people who have been [sexually abused](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_abuse "Sexual abuse"). She is a certified rape counselor and has engaged in initiatives to support [domestic violence shelters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_shelter "Women's shelter") and raise awareness about untested [rape kits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_kit "Rape kit").

## Early life

Mariska Magdolna Hargitay was born at [Providence Saint John's Health Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_John%27s_Health_Center "Saint John's Health Center") in [Santa Monica, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California "Santa Monica, California"), to actress [Jayne Mansfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayne_Mansfield "Jayne Mansfield"). She was conceived and was born during Mansfield's marriage to [Mickey Hargitay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Hargitay "Mickey Hargitay") and grew up believing he was her biological father. Hargitay did not learn of her true paternity until she was in her twenties; her biological father is [Nelson Sardelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Sardelli "Nelson Sardelli"), Brazilian-born singer, actor, and comedian of Italian descent. Her first and middle names are Hungarian and translate to [Mary Magdalene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Magdalene "Mary Magdalene") (Mariska is a Hungarian [diminutive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminutive "Diminutive") of Mary, meaning "Little Mary"). Hargitay was named after her grandmother Mary. She was referred to as "Maria" after her mother's marriage to [Matt Cimber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Cimber "Matt Cimber"), who was [Italian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian-Americans "Italian-Americans"). She was raised [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic "Catholic"). Mansfield had four other children, including [Jayne Marie Mansfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayne_Marie_Mansfield "Jayne Marie Mansfield"). Hargitay also has three half-sisters from her biological father.

On June 29, 1967, Hargitay, two of her brothers, Mansfield, Sam Brody (Mansfield's then-boyfriend), and driver Ronald Harrison were in an automobile crash on a stretch of [U.S. Route 90](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_90 "U.S. Route 90") between [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans") and [Bay St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_St._Louis "Bay St. Louis"), [Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi "Mississippi"). The accident ripped off the top of the car, instantly killing Mansfield, Brody, and Harrison. Asleep in the back of the vehicle, three-and-a-half-year-old Hargitay and her brothers escaped with minor injuries. The accident left Hargitay with a zig-zag scar on her head.

After Mansfield's death, Hargitay and her brothers were raised by Mickey Hargitay and his third wife, Ellen Siano. Hargitay has said that the early loss of her mother left "a hole in my life that won't ever be filled. I will never get over it. I will always be a girl who lost her mom."

Hargitay began attending the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles "University of California, Los Angeles") (UCLA) in 1982 as a theater arts major. That same year, Hargitay was crowned Miss Beverly Hills USA. By the time she was a freshman in college, Hargitay had an agent and several small roles to her credit. She attended [UCLA School of Theater Film and Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_School_of_Theater_Film_and_Television "UCLA School of Theater Film and Television") where she was a member of [Kappa Kappa Gamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kappa_Kappa_Gamma "Kappa Kappa Gamma"). She left college before completing her degree.

Hargitay attended [Groundlings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundlings "Groundlings") Theatre and School in Los Angeles.

## Career

### Early work and roles

After Hargitay was crowned Miss [Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills "Beverly Hills") USA in 1982, she competed in the [Miss California USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_California_USA "Miss California USA") pageant in 1983. She was the fourth runner-up. In 1984, Hargitay appeared in [Ronnie Milsap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronnie_Milsap "Ronnie Milsap")'s music video for "[She Loves My Car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_Loves_My_Car "She Loves My Car")", the first [country music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") video to appear on [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV"). A year later, she had a small role in the horror film *[Ghoulies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghoulies "Ghoulies")*.

Hargitay briefly replaced [Gabrielle Fitzpatrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabrielle_Fitzpatrick "Gabrielle Fitzpatrick") as Dulcea in *[Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: The Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mighty_Morphin_Power_Rangers:_The_Movie "Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: The Movie")*, although her scenes were cut from the film when Fitzpatrick recovered from her surgery and returned to the film. In 1988, she had a recurring role as Carly Fixx in the soap opera *[Falcon Crest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon_Crest "Falcon Crest")*. Hargitay played Jill Banner in the Japanese-American co-production *[Strawberry Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strawberry_Road_(1991_film) "Strawberry Road (1991 film)")* in 1991. She portrayed police officer Angela Garcia in the 1992 series *[Tequila and Bonetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tequila_and_Bonetti "Tequila and Bonetti")*. Hargitay appeared in the two-part [fourth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Seinfeld_episodes#Season_4:_1992–1993 "List of Seinfeld episodes") [finale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pilot,_Part_1 "The Pilot, Part 1") of *[Seinfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinfeld "Seinfeld"),* where her character read for the role of [Elaine Benes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaine_Benes "Elaine Benes") in ["The Pilot"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pilot_(Seinfeld) "The Pilot (Seinfeld)"). (She had been considered for the character of Elaine Benes on *Seinfeld* itself before the show began.) Two years later, Hargitay portrayed Didi Edelstein in the 1995 sitcom *[Can't Hurry Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Hurry_Love "Can't Hurry Love")*. In 1997, Hargitay played detective Nina Echeverria on the drama series *[Prince Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Street_(TV_series) "Prince Street (TV series)")*; she also had a recurring role as inept desk clerk Cynthia Hooper during the [fourth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ER_episodes#Season_4_1997-1998 "List of ER episodes") of *[ER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ER_(TV_series) "ER (TV series)")*.

Hargitay said in 1986 that she had never thought about acting on television until a role on the one-hour adventure drama series *[Downtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_(1986_TV_series) "Downtown (1986 TV series)")* was offered to her. In fact, she experienced difficulties in her efforts to begin a career as a Hollywood actor and endured frequent comparisons to her mother.

### *Law & Order: Special Victims Unit*

Hargitay is best known for her portrayal of Olivia Benson on *Law & Order: SVU*. Benson is the longest-running character on the longest-running American primetime drama in history.

Casting for the lead characters of [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") [police procedural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_procedural "Police procedural") television drama series *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit")* occurred in the spring of 1999. [Dick Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Wolf "Dick Wolf"), along with officials from NBC and Studios USA, was at the final auditions for the two leads at [Rockefeller Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_Center "Rockefeller Center"). There were six finalists. For the female lead (Detective [Olivia Benson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Benson "Olivia Benson")), [Samantha Mathis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Mathis "Samantha Mathis"), [Reiko Aylesworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reiko_Aylesworth "Reiko Aylesworth"), and Hargitay were being considered. For the male lead (Detective [Elliot Stabler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot_Stabler "Elliot Stabler")), the finalists were [Tim Matheson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Matheson "Tim Matheson"), [John Slattery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Slattery "John Slattery"), and [Christopher Meloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Meloni "Christopher Meloni"). Meloni and Hargitay had auditioned in the final round together. After the actors left, there was a moment of dead silence, after which Wolf blurted out, "Oh well. There's no doubt who we should choose – Hargitay and Meloni." The duo, who Wolf believed had the perfect chemistry from the first time he saw them together, were his first choice. [Garth Ancier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garth_Ancier "Garth Ancier"), then head of NBC Entertainment, agreed, and the rest of the panel assembled voiced their assent.

Hargitay trained as a rape crisis advocate to prepare for the role of Benson. She has portrayed Benson since 1999. Hargitay has won an [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy "Emmy") and a [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe "Golden Globe") for her portrayal of Benson. She received [UCLA's TFT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_School_of_Theater,_Film_and_Television "UCLA School of Theater, Film and Television") Distinguished Alumni Award in 2011 and was honored at the school's June commencement ceremony.

During the last months of her pregnancy in 2006, Hargitay took maternity leave from *SVU*. She was temporarily replaced by [Connie Nielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connie_Nielsen "Connie Nielsen"), who portrayed Stabler's temporary partner [Dani Beck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Beck "Dani Beck").

In late December 2008, Hargitay suffered a partially [collapsed lung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapsed_lung "Collapsed lung") after taking a fall during a stunt on the set of *SVU*. She underwent surgery in January and returned to work shortly afterward.

In May 2009, after the show's [tenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit_(season_10) "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit (season 10)"), Hargitay and Meloni's contracts expired when they were reportedly making $375,000–$385,000 per episode. During negotiations in April for a new contract, the duo attempted to receive a percentage of the show's profits as other high-profile *Law and Order* actors had done in the past. It was rumored that NBC threatened to replace Hargitay and Meloni if they persisted in their demands. Eventually, their contracts were renewed for two more years.

As of August 2012, Hargitay was earning approximately $400,000–$500,000 per episode of *Law & Order: Special Victims Unit*. In 2013, Hargitay was awarded the 2,511th star of the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame"). Her star was placed next to the star of her mother, which is located at 6328 Hollywood Boulevard. In 2013 and 2014, she was ranked by *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* as the second-highest-paid television actress, after only [Sofía Vergara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sof%C3%ADa_Vergara "Sofía Vergara") of *[Modern Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Family "Modern Family")*. In the following years, Hargitay continued to be one of the highest-paid television actresses in the world, making well over $500,000 per episode.

In July 2021, Hargitay suffered a broken ankle after taking a fall in the rain while leaving the screening of *[Black Widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Widow_(2021_film) "Black Widow (2021 film)")*. Her injury caused minor script changes and was written into the season 23 premiere of *SVU*. In 2025, *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* named Hargitay as the 11th highest-paid actor of 2024, the second highest-paid actress of 2024 (after [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman "Nicole Kidman")), and the highest-paid actor on television by a wide margin; she earned an estimated $750,000 per episode between acting, producing, and syndication profits from *SVU*.

### Producing and Broadway

In January 2007, Hargitay and her older son appeared in a *[Got Milk?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Got_Milk%3F "Got Milk?")* advertisement. At the [2015 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2015 MTV Video Music Awards"), Hargitay won the "Video of the Year" Award, shared with [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift") and all of the celebrities that appeared in the music video for Swift's song "[Bad Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Blood_(Taylor_Swift_song) "Bad Blood (Taylor Swift song)")".

Hargitay has produced a documentary, *[I Am Evidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Am_Evidence "I Am Evidence")*. Released by [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") in 2018, the documentary discusses the thousands of untested rape kits in the United States. Hargitay called the reality of untested rape kits "the clearest and most shocking demonstration of how we regard these crimes in our country." The film received Best Documentary at the [40th News and Documentary Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/40th_News_and_Documentary_Emmy_Awards "40th News and Documentary Emmy Awards"), winning Hargitay her second Emmy and first as a producer.

In 2025, Hargitay announced the launch of Mighty Entertainment, a production company. Hargitay's feature film directorial debut, *[My Mom Jayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Mom_Jayne "My Mom Jayne")*, was released in the United States by Mighty Entertainment in June 2025. The documentary film delved into the life and death of her mother, Jayne Mansfield. That same year, she served as an executive producer on *[Nuns vs. The Vatican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuns_vs._The_Vatican "Nuns vs. The Vatican")*, directed by [Lorena Luciano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorena_Luciano "Lorena Luciano"). In 2026, it was announced that Hargitay would make her [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_theatre "Broadway theatre") debut playing the central character in *[Every Brilliant Thing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Every_Brilliant_Thing "Every Brilliant Thing")*, replacing [Daniel Radcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Radcliffe "Daniel Radcliffe").

## Activism

Hargitay is a certified rape counselor, as reported in 2004. As a rape survivor, she has called for an end to the stigma surrounding sexual assault. In a 2024 essay for *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")*, she wrote, "Tell someone you've survived cancer, and you're celebrated. I want the same response for sexual assault survivors. I want no shame with the victim."

On September 27, 2011, Hargitay donated $100,000 to her alma mater, the [UCLA School of Theater Film and Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_School_of_Theater_Film_and_Television "UCLA School of Theater Film and Television") for scholarship. In 2012, Hargitay campaigned for the reauthorization of the [Violence Against Women Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violence_Against_Women_Act "Violence Against Women Act") (VAWA). In 2025, Hargitay was included on the inaugural [*Time* 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") Philanthropy list for her work.

### Joyful Heart Foundation

Hargitay is the founder and former president of the Joyful Heart Foundation, an organization established in 2004 to provide support to survivors of [sexual assault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_assault "Sexual assault"), [domestic violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_violence "Domestic violence"), [child abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_abuse "Child abuse"), [elder abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elder_abuse "Elder abuse") and [human trafficking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_trafficking "Human trafficking").

In November 2009, Hargitay and the Joyful Heart Foundation built healing and wellness kits for women who suffered [domestic violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_violence "Domestic violence") and were living in the Los Angeles County's domestic violence shelters. They created enough kits to give one to each of about 600 women. In California, the domestic violence services budget was in a major crisis, and shelters were forced to turn women away. Hargitay and her foundation donated money to the cause.

As of November 2010, the Joyful Heart Foundation had sent over 5,000 women and children on therapeutic programs in New York, Los Angeles, and Hawaii, which combine [yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoga "Yoga"), [meditation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meditation "Meditation"), [massage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massage "Massage"), [journaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_therapy "Journal therapy"), and [swimming with dolphins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_with_dolphins "Swimming with dolphins"). According to Hargitay, the Foundation had raised $20 million as of April 2011. Reference to the Joyful Heart Foundation was worked into episodes of *Special Victims Unit*, via a necklace containing two pendants representing the Foundation that Hargitay's character began wearing in the show's [13th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit_(season_13) "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit (season 13)"). The Foundation works with several brands to create products supporting the cause, including Me&Ro, [Michael Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Stars "Michael Stars"), and AZIAM's Wife Lover Tanks.

## Personal life

Hargitay speaks five languages: English, French, [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language "Hungarian language"), Spanish, and Italian. In a 2010 interview with *[Good Housekeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Housekeeping "Good Housekeeping")*, Hargitay stated that she considers herself a Christian. Hargitay is a fan of the [New York Knicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Knicks "New York Knicks") and is regularly seen courtside; Knicks point guard [Jalen Brunson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalen_Brunson "Jalen Brunson") has said she is his favorite celebrity to see at games.

In December 2008, Hargitay suffered a partially [collapsed lung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapsed_lung "Collapsed lung") after falling on the set of *SVU*. She underwent surgery in January 2009 and was hospitalized on March 3, 2009 after suffering from chest pains related to the injury. In 2024, Hargitay publicly disclosed that she is a rape survivor.

### Family

Hargitay did not know her true paternity until she was in her twenties, when she was shown a picture of her biological father, [Nelson Sardelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Sardelli "Nelson Sardelli"). She confronted her father, [Mickey Hargitay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Hargitay "Mickey Hargitay"), who reacted with anger, and she never mentioned Sardelli to him again. After Mickey Hargitay's death, she eventually met Sardelli at one of his performances in [Atlantic City, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_City,_New_Jersey "Atlantic City, New Jersey"). She has gradually formed a relationship with Sardelli, although she still considers herself Hargitay's daughter. She did not publicly reveal her true paternity until 2025.

On August 28, 2004, in [Santa Barbara, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California "Santa Barbara, California"), Hargitay married [Peter Hermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hermann_(actor) "Peter Hermann (actor)"), an actor she met on the set of *Law & Order: SVU*. Hermann plays the recurring role of defense attorney Trevor Langan on *Law & Order: SVU*.

On June 28, 2006, Hargitay gave birth to a son by an emergency [caesarean section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesarean_section "Caesarean section"). In April 2011, Hargitay and her husband adopted a baby girl. In October 2011, they adopted a son.
