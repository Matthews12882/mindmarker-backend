# Vicky Krieps

**Vicky Krieps** (born 4 October 1983) is a Luxembourgish and German actress. She has appeared in a number of American, Luxembourgish, French, and German productions. Her [breakout role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakout_role "Breakout role") was in the London-based period film *[Phantom Thread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_Thread "Phantom Thread")* (2017), directed by [Paul Thomas Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Thomas_Anderson "Paul Thomas Anderson").

Her early films include *[Hanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanna_(film) "Hanna (film)")* (2011), *[Two Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Lives_(film) "Two Lives (film)")* (2012) and *[A Most Wanted Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Most_Wanted_Man_(film) "A Most Wanted Man (film)")* (2014), and she next starred in *[The Girl in the Spider's Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_in_the_Spider%27s_Web_(film) "The Girl in the Spider's Web (film)")* (2018), *[The Last Vermeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Vermeer "The Last Vermeer")* (2019), *[Old](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_(film) "Old (film)")* (2021), and *[The Survivor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Survivor_(2021_film) "The Survivor (2021 film)")* (2021). She earned acclaim for her performances in *[Bergman Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergman_Island_(2021_film) "Bergman Island (2021 film)")* (2021), *[Hold Me Tight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hold_Me_Tight_(2021_film) "Hold Me Tight (2021 film)")* (2021), and *[Corsage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corsage_(film) "Corsage (film)")* (2022). Her portrayal of [Empress Elisabeth of Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empress_Elisabeth_of_Austria "Empress Elisabeth of Austria") in *Corsage* earned her the [European Film Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Film_Award_for_Best_Actress "European Film Award for Best Actress").

## Early life and education

Krieps was born on 4 October 1983 in [Hesperange, Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hesperange "Hesperange"), the daughter of a [Luxembourgish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourgers "Luxembourgers") father, who managed a film distribution company, and a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans "Germans") mother. Her father, Bob Krieps, is the director general of the Ministry of Culture of Luxembourg, the former director of Sacem Luxembourg, and the former president of the Luxembourg Film Fund. Several of her films have been funded by the Luxembourg Film Fund, which is supervised by the Ministry of Culture, which then decides which films will be funded by state subsidies. Her paternal grandfather, [Robert Krieps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Krieps "Robert Krieps"), was a politician, war-time member of the [Luxembourg Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_Resistance "Luxembourg Resistance"), and Luxembourg's Minister of Justice, National Education and Culture in the 1970s and 1980s.

Krieps had her first acting experiences at the [Lycée de Garçons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyc%C3%A9e_de_Gar%C3%A7ons_de_Luxembourg "Lycée de Garçons de Luxembourg") secondary school in [Luxembourg City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_City "Luxembourg City"), receiving training at the [Conservatoire de Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatoire_de_Luxembourg "Conservatoire de Luxembourg").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Rather than enrolling in one of the drama schools, she participated in a social project at the primary school of a [South African township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Township_(South_Africa) "Township (South Africa)") near [Knysna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knysna "Knysna"). This confirmed her resolve to study acting, with the goal of performing on theatre stages.

Krieps attended [Zurich University of the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich_University_of_the_Arts "Zurich University of the Arts"), while gathering acting experience at [Schauspielhaus Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schauspielhaus_Z%C3%BCrich "Schauspielhaus Zürich").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

## Career

Krieps had numerous roles in Luxembourgish films and television series, before starring in foreign productions, such as *[Hanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanna_(film) "Hanna (film)")* (2011), *[Rommel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rommel_(film) "Rommel (film)")* (2012), *[Before the Winter Chill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_the_Winter_Chill "Before the Winter Chill")* (2013) and *[Elly Beinhorn: Solo Flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Elly_Beinhorn:_Solo_Flight&action=edit&redlink=1 "Elly Beinhorn: Solo Flight (page does not exist)") [[de](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elly_Beinhorn_%E2%80%93_Alleinflug "de:Elly Beinhorn – Alleinflug")]*, a [biopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biopic "Biopic") about the German aviation pioneer [Elly Beinhorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elly_Beinhorn "Elly Beinhorn"). She also had supporting roles in *[Anonymous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anonymous_(2011_film) "Anonymous (2011 film)")* (2011), and *[A Most Wanted Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Most_Wanted_Man_(film) "A Most Wanted Man (film)")* (2014).

She had her career breakthrough starring opposite [Daniel Day-Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Day-Lewis "Daniel Day-Lewis") in the period film *[Phantom Thread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_Thread "Phantom Thread")* (2017). The film revolves around the romantic relationship between an older man, a fashion designer, and a younger woman. Receiving critical acclaim for her role, Dan Jolin of *[Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_(magazine) "Empire (magazine)")* stated that Krieps "can hold her own opposite a titan like Day-Lewis", while David Edelstein of *[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(website) "Vulture (website)")* wrote that she is "bewitchingly lucent, her face just masklike enough to make our sudden awareness of all her dark thoughts a shock." The film received six [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award") nominations including for [Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture "Academy Award for Best Picture").

Krieps played the magazine publisher in *[The Girl in the Spider's Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_in_the_Spider%27s_Web_(film) "The Girl in the Spider's Web (film)")* (2018), and Minna Holberg in the drama film *[The Last Vermeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Vermeer "The Last Vermeer")* (2019). She portrayed the morally compromised Alsatian interpreter in the 2020 television series *[Das Boot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Das_Boot_(TV_series) "Das Boot (TV series)")*, and subsequently in the second series. In 2021, Krieps starred in [M. Night Shyamalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Night_Shyamalan "M. Night Shyamalan")'s horror film *[Old](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_(film) "Old (film)")*, and in the drama film *[Bergman Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergman_Island_(2021_film) "Bergman Island (2021 film)")* (2021).
That same year, she starred in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") film *[The Survivor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Survivor_(2021_film) "The Survivor (2021 film)")* and the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") film *[Beckett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckett_(film) "Beckett (film)")*. She starred in [Mathieu Amalric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathieu_Amalric "Mathieu Amalric")'s drama film *[Hold Me Tight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hold_Me_Tight_(2021_film) "Hold Me Tight (2021 film)")* (2021) which premiered at the [74th Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Cannes_Film_Festival "74th Cannes Film Festival"). Susannah Gruder of *[IndieWire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndieWire "IndieWire")* praised her performance writing, "Krieps, who delivers a disarmingly vulnerable, yet understated performance that keeps the film grounded. Her wispy elegance is tempered by a clumsy charm as she lovingly strokes her daughter’s hair, or floats through the world in her newly-solo life".

The following year, she played [Empress Elisabeth of Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empress_Elisabeth_of_Austria "Empress Elisabeth of Austria") in the historical drama *[Corsage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corsage_(film) "Corsage (film)")* which she also served as an executive producer. The film premiered at the [75th Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Cannes_Film_Festival "2022 Cannes Film Festival") in the Un Certain Regard section. She won the [Un Certain Regard Best Performance Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Un_Certain_Regard "Un Certain Regard"). Jessica Kiang of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* praised the casting describing it as "the best showcase for [Krieps]' defiantly peculiar charisma since *Phantom Thread*. For her performance she received the [European Film Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Film_Award_for_Best_Actress "European Film Award for Best Actress"). She continued to act in the acclaimed drama films *[More Than Ever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_Than_Ever_(film) "More Than Ever (film)")* (2022) and *[Ingeborg Bachmann – Journey into the Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingeborg_Bachmann_%E2%80%93_Journey_into_the_Desert "Ingeborg Bachmann – Journey into the Desert")* (2023). She played [Anne of Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_of_Austria "Anne of Austria") in the action adventure film *[The Three Musketeers: D'Artagnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Three_Musketeers:_D%27Artagnan "The Three Musketeers: D'Artagnan")* (2023).

Since 2023, Krieps has been a member of the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences "Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences").

Also in 2023, Krieps starred in the [western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_(genre) "Western (genre)") drama *[The Dead Don't Hurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dead_Don%27t_Hurt "The Dead Don't Hurt")* directed by [Viggo Mortensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viggo_Mortensen "Viggo Mortensen"). The film premiered at the [2023 Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Toronto_International_Film_Festival "2023 Toronto International Film Festival"). Krieps is described as being the true central figure of the [revisionist Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revisionist_Western "Revisionist Western"). Frank Schek of *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* wrote, "Krieps delivers yet another astonishing turn in a career that already seems destined for greatness. Her Vivienne — warm yet steely, courageous yet vulnerable, fierce yet loving — is a complex, fascinating character who’s compelling every moment she’s onscreen."

## Other activities

On the occasion of a partnership with eyewear brand [Mykita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mykita "Mykita") in 2025, [Rimowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimowa "Rimowa") released a campaign featuring Krieps and [Udo Kier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udo_Kier "Udo Kier"). Photographed by Jack Davison and choreographed by Lenio Kaklea, she was later featured in [Bottega Veneta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottega_Veneta "Bottega Veneta")'s 2025 advertising campaign celebrating the 50th anniversary of its signature Intrecciato leather.

## Personal life

Krieps moved to [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin "Berlin"), Germany, in her early 20s. She has [Luxembourgish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourgish_nationality_law "Luxembourgish nationality law") and German nationality. As of 2023[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Vicky_Krieps&action=edit), she resided in Berlin with the two children she had with her former partner, the German actor [Jonas Laux](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Jonas_Laux&action=edit&redlink=1 "Jonas Laux (page does not exist)") [[de](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonas_Laux "de:Jonas Laux")].

In May 2025, Krieps married Greek film production assistant Lazaros Gounaridis at a private ceremony in Gounaridis's hometown in [Chalkidiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalkidiki "Chalkidiki"), Greece. They met in 2023 while working together on the set of *[Hot Milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Milk_(film) "Hot Milk (film)")* (2025).

In April 2021, Krieps was part of the controversial campaign *#allesdichtmachen* ("close everything down"), which featured 50 German-speaking actors making fun of Germany's protective measures against [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19 "COVID-19"). In her video, Krieps says, among other things, that acting helped her to deal with her fear of people. The campaign, released when Germany had more than 80,000 deaths related to COVID-19, was highly criticised as being "tasteless" and "cringe-worthy", but it also received support from members of the [far-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far-right_politics "Far-right politics") and COVID-deniers.

When asked about the [#MeToo movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MeToo_movement "MeToo movement") in 2018, Krieps said: "Maybe it's very European, but I always see both sides. I feel very sorry for the people who have been harassed, but I also feel very sorry for the people who have lived a life where they have been harassing people." When Krieps was questioned on [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") about the alleged abuser in the cast of her film *[Corsage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corsage_(film) "Corsage (film)")* who was kept in the film after director [Marie Kreutzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Kreutzer "Marie Kreutzer") was informed about him, she said: "So, a feminist film made by two women should be discarded because of the misconduct of a male colleague?... Who exactly is being harmed by this?" Krieps was also an [executive producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_producer "Executive producer") on *Corsage*. The case became public in Austria in June 2022, when Austrian director [Katharina Mückstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharina_M%C3%BCckstein "Katharina Mückstein") shared an [Instagram story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram_story "Instagram story") that generated a lot of media attention and sparked a new wave of the #MeToo Movement in Austria. On 13 January 2023, another one of Krieps' co-stars from *Corsage*, [Florian Teichtmeister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Teichtmeister "Florian Teichtmeister") (who played her character's husband in the film), was charged with [possession of child pornography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Possession_of_child_pornography "Possession of child pornography"), and later that year he [pleaded guilty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleaded_guilty "Pleaded guilty") and was sentenced to a two-year [suspended prison sentence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspended_prison_sentence "Suspended prison sentence"). In an interview with *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* on 19 February 2023, Krieps talked about [Teichtmeister's case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Teichtmeister_child_pornography_scandal "Florian Teichtmeister child pornography scandal") for the first time, saying, "It's the problem of a man who needs probably treatment and help and also, it needs to be judged by the law. To me, I don't even see in what way this could be controversial to the movie. It was a shock to know that someone I was so close to was apparently someone I didn't know. This was more shocking, and shaking me in a way thinking about humans and society and how we think we know each other, but we don't. In that way I thought about it a lot but I never understood what it has to do with the movie. He's not playing [Sisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empress_Elisabeth_of_Austria "Empress Elisabeth of Austria") and the movie is not about [manliness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manliness "Manliness"). In my eyes it is not really connected."
