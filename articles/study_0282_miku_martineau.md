# Miku Martineau

**Miku Patricia Martineau** (born September 13, 2004) is a Canadian actress, voiceover artist, and singer. She gained prominence for her role as Ani in the film *[Kate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_(film) "Kate (film)")* (2021) and has since appeared in various film and television projects, including *[Honor Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_Society_(film) "Honor Society (film)")* (2022), *[Star Trek: Section 31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_Section_31 "Star Trek: Section 31")* (2025), and the Netflix series *[Bet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bet_(TV_series) "Bet (TV series)")* (2025) and *[Crew Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crew_Girl "Crew Girl")* (2026).

## Early life and education

Martineau was born in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto"), Canada, to Peter Martineau and Kumiko Martineau, who is of Japanese roots. She pursued formal training at the [Randolph College for the Performing Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randolph_College_for_the_Performing_Arts "Randolph College for the Performing Arts") in Toronto, where she studied acting, singing, and dancing.

## Career

In 2022, Martineau appeared as Christine in the teen comedy *[Honor Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_Society_(film) "Honor Society (film)")*. By 2025, she transitioned to science fiction, playing a young [Philippa Georgiou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippa_Georgiou "Philippa Georgiou") in *Star Trek: Section 31*, a [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+") film that premiered January 24, 2025, exploring the backstory of [Michelle Yeoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Yeoh "Michelle Yeoh")’s character.
