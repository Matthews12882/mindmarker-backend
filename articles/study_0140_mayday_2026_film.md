# Mayday (2026 film)

***Mayday*** is a 2026 American [action comedy film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_comedy_film "Action comedy film") written and directed by [John Francis Daley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Francis_Daley "John Francis Daley") and [Jonathan Goldstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Goldstein_(filmmaker) "Jonathan Goldstein (filmmaker)"), and produced by [Apple Original Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Original_Films "Apple Original Films"), [Skydance Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skydance_Media "Skydance Media"), and [Maximum Effort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_Effort "Maximum Effort"). It stars [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds "Ryan Reynolds"), [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh "Kenneth Branagh"), [Marcin Dorociński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Doroci%C5%84ski "Marcin Dorociński"), [Maria Bakalova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Bakalova "Maria Bakalova"), and [David Morse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Morse "David Morse").

The film was released on [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_(streaming_service) "Apple TV (streaming service)") on September 4, 2026, and received positive reviews from critics.

## Plot

In 1987, US Navy Lieutenant Troy "Assassin" Kelly rescues his wingman who is unable to land on an [aircraft carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_carrier "Aircraft carrier"). After they safely land, he is asked to fly the [SR-71 Blackbird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SR-71_Blackbird "SR-71 Blackbird") with US Air Force pilot [Major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_(United_States) "Major (United States)") Sanders for a covert reconnaissance mission over Russia. They are intercepted by a MiG and Sanders is killed; Troy maneuvers to collide with the MiG and ejects. After he lands, he is rescued by Nikolai "Nick" Ustinov, a former [KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB "KGB") officer and a fan of American culture. Nick regrets his time in the KGB and hopes to help Troy and one day live in the US. Troy attempts to flee, and is rescued by Nick, who hides him from search efforts from the KGB, led by Alexander Volkov.

Troy agrees to help Nick get asylum to the US if Ustinov helps him to escape Russia. Nick takes Troy on a train, but they are identified by Russian military and are forced to jump off the train. They flee to a [dacha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dacha "Dacha") and call the [US Embassy in Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_the_United_States,_Moscow "Embassy of the United States, Moscow") and are told to meet there. As they flee to [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow "Moscow"), Volkov sees a record of their call. Once Troy and Nick arrive in Moscow, they visit Nick's estranged daughter Anna, but are forced from her apartment when it is attacked by Volkov's agents. At a safe house owned by Anna, she confides to Troy that she is a lesbian, which is illegal in Russia and a reason why she grew apart from her father. Nick tells Troy that he already knew, which was one of the reasons he left the KGB. Troy convinces Nick to tell Anna. Nick and Anna reconcile, with Anna leaving Ustinov a photo of her with her girlfriend and a message asking him to call her once he reaches the US.

The next morning, Troy calls to arrange his rendezvous with the embassy, but they reveal that they will not provide asylum to Nick. Despite this, Troy agrees to meet at the Embassy; unknown to Troy, Nick hears this. Feeling betrayed, Nick hands Troy to Volkov, who interrogates them both; he attempts to force them to sign a confession, with Troy being shown a memo that the US Navy knowingly exposed its shipyard workers to [asbestos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asbestos "Asbestos") despite its [carcinogenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcinogenic "Carcinogenic") effects, which affected Kelly's father, but Troy refuses to sign. Nick agrees to sign but grabs a pen and stabs Volkov, and he and Troy flee and steal a mobile nuclear launcher. They reconcile when Troy tells Nick why his call sign is "Assassin" - it is a joke because he fell into an open deck hatch "ass first twice", and has nothing to do with the role of an actual assassin. They flee to the airport and steal a plane (Type [ATR 42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATR_42 "ATR 42")), which is boarded by Volkov as they take off. Troy kills Volkov by throwing him off the plane. Troy realizes they cannot make it to friendly territory in [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland "Finland") in their now-damaged plane, but they land on the [USS *Carl Vinson*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Carl_Vinson "USS Carl Vinson") which is on manoeuvres, after Troy negotiates immunity for Nick.

After the mission, Troy testifies to Congress about the Naval memo regarding asbestos and is placed on indefinite leave from the Navy. He and Nick eat at [McDonalds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonalds "McDonalds") and talk about taking a trip across the country in Nick's RV.

## Production

In June 2023, it was announced that an original [action adventure film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_adventure_film "Action adventure film") titled *Mayday* was in development for [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+"), with [Jonathan Goldstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Goldstein_(filmmaker) "Jonathan Goldstein (filmmaker)") and [John Francis Daley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Francis_Daley "John Francis Daley") writing and directing as well as [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds "Ryan Reynolds") (who would also produce through [Maximum Effort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_Effort "Maximum Effort")) and [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh "Kenneth Branagh") in the lead roles. In April 2024, [Maria Bakalova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Bakalova "Maria Bakalova"), [Marcin Dorociński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Doroci%C5%84ski "Marcin Dorociński"), [Lovell Adams-Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovell_Adams-Gray "Lovell Adams-Gray") and [Clark Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Johnson "Clark Johnson") were added to the cast. [David Morse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Morse "David Morse") also joined the cast of the film.

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") began in [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal "Montreal") on March 4, 2024, and [wrapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrap_(filmmaking) "Wrap (filmmaking)") on May 8, under the [working title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_title "Working title") *Blackbird*. The final stage of filming was in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest "Budapest").

## Release

*Mayday* was released on [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_(streaming_service) "Apple TV (streaming service)") on September 4, 2026.

## Reception

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 83% of 105 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 6.3/10. The website's consensus reads: "A spirited adventure that gets a lot of mileage from clever quips and Kenneth Branagh's extremely amusing performance, *Mayday* thaws Cold War tensions with good-natured aplomb." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean"), assigned the film a score of 60 out of 100, based on 24 critics, indicating "mixed or average" reviews.
