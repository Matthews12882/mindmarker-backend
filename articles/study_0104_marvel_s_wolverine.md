# Marvel's Wolverine

***Marvel's Wolverine*** is a 2026 [action-adventure game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action-adventure_game "Action-adventure game") developed by [Insomniac Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insomniac_Games "Insomniac Games") and published by [Sony Interactive Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Interactive_Entertainment "Sony Interactive Entertainment"). Based on the [Marvel Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Comics "Marvel Comics") character [Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_(character) "Wolverine (character)"), it is inspired by the long-running comic book mythology and various [adaptations in other media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_in_other_media "Wolverine in other media"). *Marvel's Wolverine* is a standalone entry in the *[Marvel's Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel%27s_Spider-Man "Marvel's Spider-Man")* series, telling an original, self-contained story that [shares continuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shared_universe "Shared universe") with Insomniac's other Marvel games. The narrative follows Wolverine, an amnesiac [mutant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutant_(Marvel_Comics) "Mutant (Marvel Comics)") who rejoins his former unit [Team X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_X_(comics) "Team X (comics)") to investigate the abduction of displaced mutants by industrialist [Bolivar Trask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivar_Trask "Bolivar Trask") and the [Reavers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reavers_(comics) "Reavers (comics)") militia, while searching for answers regarding his own past.

Insomniac Games, Sony Interactive Entertainment, and [Marvel Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Games "Marvel Games") entered discussions about developing further games centered on Marvel characters beyond [Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man "Spider-Man") during the development of *[Marvel's Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel%27s_Spider-Man_(video_game) "Marvel's Spider-Man (video game)")* (2018), with Insomniac expressing interest in adapting Wolverine. *Marvel's Wolverine* was announced in September 2021, alongside the involvement of creative director Brian Horton and game director Cameron Christian, as well as writers Walt Williams and Mary Kenney.

*Marvel's Wolverine* was among the major subjects of a targeted [ransomware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ransomware "Ransomware") attack on Insomniac Games in December 2023, in which various development assets showcasing gameplay and story elements briefly became publicly accessible. Horton and Christian were replaced by Marcus Smith and Mike Daly in their respective roles by October 2024. The game was released worldwide for the [PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5 "PlayStation 5") on September 15, 2026. It received generally favorable reviews from critics, with praise for its graphics, cinematics, voice acting, and the spectacle of its combat while being criticized for its lack of depth, repetitiveness, and weak narrative.

## Characters and setting

*Marvel's Wolverine* features an ensemble cast drawn from the comic book mythology of the character, the wider *[X-Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncanny_X-Men "Uncanny X-Men")* mythos, and various [adaptations in other media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_in_other_media "Wolverine in other media"). The game follows [James "Logan" Howlett / Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_(character) "Wolverine (character)") ([Liam McIntyre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_McIntyre "Liam McIntyre")), a centuries-old [mutant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutant_(Marvel_Comics) "Mutant (Marvel Comics)") with retractable claws, heightened animalistic instincts and a [healing factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Healing_factor "Healing factor"), who is grafted with an indestructible [adamantium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adamantium "Adamantium") alloy in his skeleton following intense experimentation.

The game is set three years after Wolverine's departure from the mutant strike force [Team X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_X_(comics) "Team X (comics)"), as he rejoins the group to investigate the abduction of mutants by billionaire industrialist [Bolivar Trask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivar_Trask "Bolivar Trask") ([Jason Spisak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Spisak "Jason Spisak")) and his cybernetically-enhanced private militia, the [Reavers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reavers_(comics) "Reavers (comics)"). Other characters that appear in the game include [Jean Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Grey "Jean Grey") (Krizia Bajos), [Dr. Nathaniel Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mister_Sinister "Mister Sinister") ([Troy Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy_Baker "Troy Baker")), [Mystique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystique_(character) "Mystique (character)") (Nicole Pacent), [Omega Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_Red "Omega Red") (Raphael Corkhill), [Sabretooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabretooth_(character) "Sabretooth (character)") (Brett Gipson), [Lady Deathstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Deathstrike "Lady Deathstrike") (Jolene Kim), [Leech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leech_(character) "Leech (character)") (Noga Wind), [Sunfire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunfire_(character) "Sunfire (character)") (Jonathan Tanigaki), [Tyger Tiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyger_Tiger "Tyger Tiger") ([Kelly Hu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Hu "Kelly Hu")), the [Sentinels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentinel_(comics) "Sentinel (comics)") and the [Hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hand_(comics) "The Hand (comics)").

## Plot

A feral [Logan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_(character) "Wolverine (character)") is discovered by mutant [geneticist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneticist "Geneticist") [Nathaniel Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mister_Sinister "Mister Sinister"). Over the next 27 years, Logan works for [Team X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_X_(comics) "Team X (comics)"), a team created by Essex to protect mutants, with Logan adopting the codename "Wolverine" before quitting. Three years later, Logan reunites with his former teammates [Sabretooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabretooth_(character) "Sabretooth (character)") and [Mystique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystique_(character) "Mystique (character)") to rescue Essex from a facility owned by [Bolivar Trask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivar_Trask "Bolivar Trask"), a scientist plotting to wipe out mutantkind. The team rescues Essex and defeats Trask's henchman [Omega Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_Red "Omega Red").

At the Essex Institute in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada "Canada"), Team X meets with [Walter Langkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasquatch_(comics) "Sasquatch (comics)") to discuss the threat Trask poses. Logan travels to [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver "Vancouver") to evacuate a [group of mutants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morlocks_(comics) "Morlocks (comics)") under attack by the [Reavers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reavers_(comics) "Reavers (comics)"), Trask's private militia. One of the mutants, [Jean Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Grey "Jean Grey"), shows up to help Logan. The mutants's leader [Callisto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callisto_(comics) "Callisto (comics)") is wounded by the Reaver's leader [Skullbuster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skullbuster "Skullbuster"). While Jean treats Callisto, Logan saves the other mutants before Trask can experiment on them. The rescued mutants take refuge with Essex, and Jean agrees to help Team X.

After the Reavers destroy the Institute, Team X relocates the surviving refugees to [Madripoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madripoor "Madripoor"), where they are sheltered by Mystique's human wife [Tyger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyger_Tiger "Tyger Tiger"). Trask unveils a prototype [Sentinel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentinel_(comics) "Sentinel (comics)"), a robot designed to hunt and kill mutants. The team defeats it, and Trask is seized and tortured by Essex; Logan mercy kills Trask, angering Essex and Sabretooth. The same night, a ninja cult known as [the Hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hand_(comics) "The Hand (comics)") abducts Walter.

Logan and Jean learn that Walter was working with the Hand and their leader [Lady Deathstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Deathstrike "Lady Deathstrike") to create a Helix Engine, a machine that can activate dormant mutant genes, but is fatal to those without the gene. The machine turns Walter into a feral ape-like creature, after which he is transported back to Madripoor. Deathstrike attempts to use the Helix Engine to heal herself, but it kills her instead.

Logan and Jean attempt to destroy the Engine, but Essex and Sabretooth arrive and steal it. Essex reveals to Logan that his powers were obtained through experiments on his wife, who was a telepathic mutant, and grafted her mutant genes onto himself. Essex deploys a giant Sentinel augmented with a Helix Engine, with the intention of forcibly evolving mutants across Madripoor. Logan confronts but is apparently killed by the Sentinel. However, Logan's consciousness survives, allowing him to recover several of his repressed memories. He learns that his real name is James Logan Howlett, and he and Jean once lived together on a farm when Essex approached them to join Team X. Logan refused to join, causing Essex to attempt mind-control. This inadvertently awakened the "Beast" within Logan, who addresses itself as [Apocalypse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocalypse_(character) "Apocalypse (character)") and Logan as [Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsemen_of_Apocalypse "Horsemen of Apocalypse"). Logan's berserker rage was triggered, causing him to accidentally kill Jean when she tried to calm him. Essex forced Logan to repeatedly relive the memory of Jean's death, causing his mind to break down and him to turn feral, before recruiting him into Team X as Wolverine. Jean managed to reincarnate through her [Phoenix Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Force "Phoenix Force") powers, but without memories of her past life.

Logan revives himself, defeats Sabretooth, and reunites with Jean. The two encounter Walter, whose body and mind have been further corrupted; Jean is unable to heal Walter, and Logan kills him at his urging. The pair discover that Essex has experimented on the kidnapped mutants, grafting their mutant genes and a piece of the Helix Engine onto himself to become the ultimate mutant. Essex reveals his plan to deploy Sentinels to various major world cities, intending to awaken mutants across the planet.

Logan and Jean pursue Essex to [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo "Tokyo") and destroy Essex's Sentinel, which in turn disables the other Sentinels. Essex is fatally wounded by Logan, but manages to trigger Logan's feral state and bring him under Apocalypse's control again. Jean purges Logan's mind, removing Apocalypse's influence at the cost of his memories. Logan sets off to rediscover his past and find a new purpose, while Jean stays with Callisto, who intends to rebuild Madripoor as a haven for mutants. In a secret ending set 75 years prior to the game's events, it is revealed that Jean had known Logan throughout her previous lives. In a post-credits scene, Sabretooth wanderes into Apocalypse's domain, and he replaces Logan's as one of Apocalypse's horsemen.

## Development

During the development of *[Marvel's Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel%27s_Spider-Man_(video_game) "Marvel's Spider-Man (video game)")* (2018), developer [Insomniac Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insomniac_Games "Insomniac Games"), [Marvel Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Games "Marvel Games") and publisher [Sony Interactive Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Interactive_Entertainment "Sony Interactive Entertainment") discussed prospects for future games based on [Marvel Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Comics "Marvel Comics") properties beyond [Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man "Spider-Man"), with the team at Insomniac continually suggesting their desire to work on a game featuring [Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_(character) "Wolverine (character)"). The development team were drawn to the character through the similar moral compass he shares with Spider-Man, notably the fact that "both heroes feel deeply compelled to defend people who are less able to do so". Insomniac eventually elected to [pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_(filmmaking) "Pitch (filmmaking)") this idea to both Marvel and Sony as their next licensed property following their successful collaboration with the two parties on developing *Marvel's Spider-Man*.

*Marvel's Wolverine* was announced alongside *[Marvel's Spider-Man 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel%27s_Spider-Man_2 "Marvel's Spider-Man 2")* (2023) by Insomniac Games, during the PlayStation Showcase event in September 2021. The developers confirmed shortly thereafter that the standalone game shares [continuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_(fiction) "Continuity (fiction)") with the *Marvel's Spider-Man* games. Brian Horton and Cameron Christian were announced as the game's creative and game directors, respectively, reprising their roles from *[Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel%27s_Spider-Man:_Miles_Morales "Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales")* (2020), with Horton describing the game as "full size, mature tone" in contrast to *Miles Morales*. Mary Kenney served as the game's writer with *[Spec Ops: The Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spec_Ops:_The_Line "Spec Ops: The Line")* (2012) writer Walt Williams. [Motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture "Motion capture") preparation had begun by April 2022, when lead animator Mike Yosh posted a photo of a motion capture soundstage on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter"). In June 2023, *[Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratchet_%26_Clank:_Rift_Apart "Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart")* (2021) co-writer Nick Folkman revealed on Twitter that he had been simultaneously involved as a writer on both *Marvel's Spider-Man 2* and *Marvel's Wolverine*, and that he would be completely focused on the latter now that his work on the former game had been completed. *Marvel's Spider-Man 2* creative director Bryan Intihar reconfirmed the game's shared continuity to the studio's *Spider-Man* games in October 2023, saying "they're all [Earth-1048](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-1048 "Earth-1048")".

[Sweet Baby Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_Baby_Inc. "Sweet Baby Inc.") announced in 2021 they will be working as a story consultation. In September 2024, Insomniac Games's core technology director Mike Fitzgerald confirmed that *Marvel's Wolverine* supports enhancements made by the [PlayStation 5 Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5_Pro "PlayStation 5 Pro") as they were implemented into their existing games for the console. Over the summer, Brian Horton left his role as creative director on the game to join [The Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Initiative_(company) "The Initiative (company)") at [Xbox Game Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_Game_Studios "Xbox Game Studios"), briefly assuming the role of creative director on the [*Perfect Dark*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Dark_(cancelled_video_game) "Perfect Dark (cancelled video game)") reboot prior to its cancellation and the studio's simultaneous closure in July 2025. Marcus Smith took over Horton's role, while Mike Daly replaced Christian as game director. The duo previously collaborated on *Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart* (2021). The game's cover art, which was revealed during a September 2025 [State of Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Play_(video_program) "State of Play (video program)"), was designed in collaboration with comic artist [Jock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jock_(cartoonist) "Jock (cartoonist)").

### 2023 Rhysida hack

In December 2023, Insomniac Games was targeted in a [ransomware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ransomware "Ransomware") attack that compromised various development documents and personal employee information. Among the surfaced materials, early in-game images and concept art featuring other characters were leaked online. The hackers, [Rhysida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhysida_(hacker_group) "Rhysida (hacker group)"), threatened to publish all procured images and resources obtained from the hack within seven days and they held an auction for the data with a starting price at 50 [bitcoin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitcoin "Bitcoin") (then-equivalent to US$2 million). Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) stated that they intended to investigate but did not anticipate the incident to affect other divisions of SIE or Sony as a whole. After Rhysida's deadline, company information, including employee data, model dumps and [pre-production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-production "Pre-production") slates for various games in development, were published online. These materials included various production assets related to *Marvel's Wolverine*, such as vertical gameplay slices, animation and model tests, [concept art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_art "Concept art"), finalized plot and character details, as well as personal information concerning in-house staff and contract workers involved in the game's development, including the unintended reveal of the voice cast.

In the hours following the leak, early playable builds were similarly circulated online and made accessible by dataminers. SIE responded by issuing [DMCA takedown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_Copyright_Infringement_Liability_Limitation_Act "Online Copyright Infringement Liability Limitation Act") notices through [internet service providers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_service_providers "Internet service providers") against those who attempted to download the files. Insomniac Games issued a statement addressing the hack three days later, stating that the events took an "extremely distressing" emotional toll on the development teams involved in their upcoming projects, but that the data breach would not impact their development plans, including production on *Marvel's Wolverine*. They thanked their fans and the wider gaming community for "the outpouring of compassion and unwavering support", affirming to share more details regarding the game when the timing was right. The events of the hack and its consequences for the developer were met with widespread sympathy throughout the video game industry.

## Release

On September 24, 2025, during the [State of Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Play_(video_program) "State of Play (video program)"), Sony announced that *Marvel's Wolverine* is set for release on [PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5 "PlayStation 5") in late 2026. On February 24, 2026, Insomniac confirmed the release date as September 15, 2026. *Marvel's Wolverine* was nominated for Most Anticipated Game at [the Game Awards 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2025 "The Game Awards 2025"). As part of a cross-promotion with *[Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_T%C5%8Dkon:_Fighting_Souls "Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls")*, the default Wolverine costume from *Marvel Tōkon* appears in *Marvel's Wolverine*, and vice-versa. Social media users reported that the version of the game released in Middle East regions had LGBTQ-based content censored.

*Marvel's Wolverine* received "generally favorable" reviews, according to [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"). [OpenCritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenCritic "OpenCritic") determined that 66% of critics recommended the game. The game's critical reception was worse compared to Insomniac's games from the decade prior.

*[Eurogamer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurogamer "Eurogamer")*'s Chris Tapsell said "Wolverine's characterisation is a bright spot in this otherwise underwhelming, old-school romp that reminds us how far action games have come."

Matt Miller's concluded his review on *[Game Informer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Informer "Game Informer")* with "Insomniac's latest action opus is beautiful to look at and listen to, and it plays like a dream. Whether you're a fan of the wily mutant lead or you simply gravitate to taut, tightly animated combat like what you'd find in *God of War* or *Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order*, *Wolverine* is an intense and unrelenting ride. It may not hit a lot of notes, but like Logan himself, it nails the vitals where it counts."

Tamoor Hussain from *[GameSpot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GameSpot "GameSpot")* praised the game for its solid combat, appealing visual style with cinematic flair and compelling story with good character performances while criticizing it for the gameplay not doing much beyond the basics and lack of weight, flow and challenge for the combat.

"*Marvel's Wolverine* is a surprisingly average game let down by basic combat, a messy story, and the whelming feeling it could have all been a lot better. It's an enjoyable enough experience but one that lacks the depth, nuance and variety you'd expect." was Leon Hurley's consensus on *[GamesRadar+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GamesRadar%2B "GamesRadar+")*.

The verdict for Simon Cardy on *[IGN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IGN "IGN")* was "*Marvel's Wolverine*'s slick and satisfying combat wears thin well before its conclusion, so much so that even its strong character work can't quite save it from being anything more than okay."

A strong linear and focused experience, fast-paced and gory combat, brutal takedowns and team combos, nice variety of locations, excellent vocal performances and outstanding visuals at times were the pros while relatively uninteresting narrative, some badly presented story elements and restrictive invisible walls were the cons on Liam Croft's review on *[Push Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Push_Square "Push Square")*.

Donovan Erskine on *[Shacknews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shacknews "Shacknews")*'s pros were satisfying melee-based combat, cinematic setpieces, impressive story performances, modular difficulty settings and snappy loading and scene transitions while his cons were story doesn't reach its potential, gameplay gets repetitive and not much compelling content outside of combat.

Keith Stuart from *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* said "Logan is well realised by Liam McIntyre and some exquisite character art, but there are lapses in narrative and emotional arcs and some of the combat feels like a chore."

*[Video Games Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_Games_Chronicle "Video Games Chronicle")*'s Jordan Middler summed up the review by saying "*Marvel's Wolverine* is fine, but it fails to capture the spirit of the legendary character in the same way Insomniac did with Spider-Man. It's a game out of time, full of dated quest design and combat that excels in spectacle but not much else. Best-in-class visuals pull up the overall package, but this isn't the definitive Wolverine game I'd hoped for."

### Sales and revenue

For the week ending September 20, *Marvel's Wolverine* debuted at number one on the sales chart in Switzerland and the physical sales chart in the United Kingdom. In Japan, *Marvel's Wolverine* debuted at number five on the physical sales chart for that week, selling 20,039 copies. The *Marvel's Wolverine* soundtrack debuted at number 19 on the [UK Official Classical Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Charts "Official Charts").

Market research and data analysis firm Alinea Analytics reported that *Marvel’s Wolverine* sold approximately 1.9 million copies during its first three days of release. Total sales during the period generated more than $130 million in revenue. According to market research firm Ampere Analysis, *Marvel’s Wolverine* was the second best-selling PlayStation 5 game in September, selling approximately 2.16 million units since its release on September 15. Of the approximately 2.16 million units sold, 35% were physical copies, accounting for roughly 756,000 sales. In Europe, physical copies represented a larger share, making up 51% of total sales.

### Awards

*Marvel's Wolverine* was nominated in the Most Anticipated Game category at [The Game Awards 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2025 "The Game Awards 2025"). Liam McIntyre was nominated for Best Lead Performer, while Troy Baker was nominated for Best Supporting Performer at the 2026 [Golden Joystick Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Joystick_Awards "Golden Joystick Awards").

## Future

The ransomware attack on Insomniac Games in December 2023 revealed that the studio was producing several Marvel video games, all in various stages of development. *Marvel's Wolverine* is planned as the first in a trilogy focused on the [X-Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men "X-Men"), similar to the *Marvel's Spider-Man* series. The hack confirmed a licensing deal between Insomniac Games, Sony Interactive Entertainment, and Marvel. Under this agreement, Insomniac would work with Marvel to produce [*X-Men* video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_featuring_the_X-Men "List of video games featuring the X-Men"). The deal includes restrictions: while *X-Men* characters can appear in other Marvel games, Marvel cannot announce any new *X-Men* games from other publishers for consoles, PC, or [cloud streaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_gaming "Cloud gaming") during the agreement. Other first-party platforms, like [Xbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox "Xbox") and [Nintendo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo "Nintendo"), cannot use *X-Men*-affiliated characters or elements as competitive advantages against PlayStation consoles for multiplatform Marvel games.
