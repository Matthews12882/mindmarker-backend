# Zendaya

**Zendaya Maree Stoermer Coleman** (born September 1, 1996) is an American actress and singer. Known for her work in television, [blockbusters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockbuster_(entertainment) "Blockbuster (entertainment)"), and advertising, her films as a [leading actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_actress "Leading actress") have grossed over $9.8 billion worldwide. [Her accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Zendaya "List of awards and nominations received by Zendaya") include two [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards") and a [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globes "Golden Globes"). She has been featured on Forbes' 30 under 30 list, and in 2022, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* named her one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100").

Born in [Oakland, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California "Oakland, California"), Zendaya began her career as a child model and dancer before gaining prominence for her roles on the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") sitcoms *[Shake It Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Up_(American_TV_series) "Shake It Up (American TV series)")* (2010–2013) and *[K.C. Undercover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K.C._Undercover "K.C. Undercover")* (2015–2018), the latter of which she also produced. For her performance as the drug-addicted teenager [Rue Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rue_Bennett "Rue Bennett") in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")* (2019–2026), Zendaya was nominated for three [Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series") and won two, becoming the youngest performer to win the award.

Zendaya made her feature film debut as [Michelle Jones-Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MJ_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "MJ (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") film *[Spider-Man: Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Homecoming "Spider-Man: Homecoming")* (2017). She reprised the role in the film's sequels *[Far From Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Far_From_Home "Spider-Man: Far From Home")* (2019), *[No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home "Spider-Man: No Way Home")* (2021), and *[Brand New Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Brand_New_Day "Spider-Man: Brand New Day")* (2026). She also starred in the musical film *[The Greatest Showman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_Showman "The Greatest Showman")* (2017), [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve "Denis Villeneuve")'s [*Dune* trilogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_(franchise)#Films "Dune (franchise)") (2021–2026), and *[The Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Odyssey_(2026_film) "The Odyssey (2026 film)")* (2026), and has earned praise for her performances in the romance films *[Malcolm & Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malcolm_%26_Marie "Malcolm & Marie")* (2021), *[Challengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challengers_(film) "Challengers (film)")* (2024) and *[The Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Drama_(film) "The Drama (film)")* (2026).

In 2011, Zendaya released the singles "[Swag It Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swag_It_Out "Swag It Out")" and "[Watch Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watch_Me_(Bella_Thorne_and_Zendaya_song) "Watch Me (Bella Thorne and Zendaya song)")", the latter a collaboration with [Bella Thorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Thorne "Bella Thorne"). Signed to [Hollywood Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Records "Hollywood Records"), she released the studio album [*Zendaya*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zendaya_(album) "Zendaya (album)") in 2013. Its lead single, "[Replay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replay_(Zendaya_song) "Replay (Zendaya song)")", peaked in the top 40 of the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") and earned multi-platinum certifications. Her duet with [Zac Efron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Efron "Zac Efron"), "[Rewrite the Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rewrite_the_Stars "Rewrite the Stars")", from [*The Greatest Showman* soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_Showman_(soundtrack) "The Greatest Showman (soundtrack)") (2017), charted in the top 20 and achieved multi-platinum status in various countries. Zendaya also wrote and performed several songs for *Euphoria*, co-performing "All for Us" and "I'm Tired" with Labrinth whom she co-wrote the songs with during [Coachella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella "Coachella") in 2023.

Zendaya's first book was *Between U and Me: How to Rock Your Tween Years with Style and Confidence* (2013), and she is known for her longtime partnership with stylist [Law Roach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_Roach "Law Roach"). In November 2021, she became the youngest person to ever win the CFDA Fashion Icon award at the [CFDA Fashion Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFDA_Fashion_Awards "CFDA Fashion Awards"). She has been the brand ambassador for [Tommy Hilfiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Hilfiger_(company) "Tommy Hilfiger (company)"), a spokesmodel for [Lancôme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanc%C3%B4me "Lancôme"), [Bulgari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgari "Bulgari"), [Valentino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentino_(fashion_house) "Valentino (fashion house)"), and [Louis Vuitton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Vuitton "Louis Vuitton"), and the global brand ambassador for [Glaceau Smartwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaceau_Smartwater "Glaceau Smartwater"). Zendaya has been the face of footwear company [On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_(company) "On (company)") since 2024, and became an ambassador for [Rolex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolex "Rolex") in 2025 and for [Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prada "Prada")'s perfume Paradoxe in 2026.

## Early life

Zendaya was born in [Oakland, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California "Oakland, California"). Her name derives from the [Shona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shona_language "Shona language") name *[Tendai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tendayi "Tendayi")* (meaning 'to give thanks'). Both of her parents were working as teachers at the time of her birth. Her father, Kazembe Ajamu Coleman, is a black American with Nigerian ancestry. Her mother, Claire Stoermer, has German and Scottish ancestry. Zendaya has six older half-siblings from her father's previous relationships: Latonja, Austin, Julien, Kaylee, Katianna and AnnaBella. Zendaya's parents divorced in 2016.

Zendaya attended [Fruitvale Elementary School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Oakland,_California_elementary_schools "List of Oakland, California elementary schools") in [Oakland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_Unified_School_District "Oakland Unified School District"), where her mother taught. She was described as energetic and outgoing at home but reserved and shy at school. At the end of her first school year, her parents had her repeat kindergarten to help build her confidence. Because of her father's passion for basketball, Zendaya's parents enrolled her in a local basketball team. After losing interest in the sport, she switched to soccer and track, but the child lost interest in those sports, too. At age six, Zendaya and two of her friends, the only Black children at the school, convinced the principal to let them perform a play at school for [Black History Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_History_Month "Black History Month"). From then, Glamour magazine describes she "was hooked."

## Career

### 2004–2009: Beginnings

At age eight, Zendaya joined Future Shock Oakland, a [hip-hop dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop_dance "Hip-hop dance") group with which she performed for three years. She also spent two years dancing [hula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hula "Hula") with the Academy of Hawaiian Arts. After deciding to pursue acting, Zendaya assisted her mother, who spent the summer working as a house manager at the [California Shakespeare Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Shakespeare_Theater "California Shakespeare Theater"). There, she assisted patrons with their seats and sold fundraising tickets, but her primary interest was in the performances. Her first acting role was as a [silkworm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silkworm "Silkworm") in her school's production of *[James and the Giant Peach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_and_the_Giant_Peach_(musical) "James and the Giant Peach (musical)")*. At age eleven, she began attending [Oakland School for the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_School_for_the_Arts "Oakland School for the Arts") and was cast in several roles in local theaters. Zendaya portrayed Little Ti Moune in *[Once on This Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_on_This_Island "Once on This Island")* at the [Berkeley Playhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley_Playhouse "Berkeley Playhouse"). She played Joe—a role originally written for a male performer—at [TheaterWorks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TheatreWorks_(Silicon_Valley) "TheatreWorks (Silicon Valley)")' Palo Alto production of *[Caroline, or Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline,_or_Change "Caroline, or Change")*.

By the sixth grade, Zendaya had played [Lady Anne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Neville "Anne Neville") in *[Richard III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_III_(play) "Richard III (play)")* and [Celia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celia_(As_You_Like_It) "Celia (As You Like It)") in *[As You Like It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_You_Like_It "As You Like It")* and had participated in a production of *[Twelfth Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelfth_Night "Twelfth Night")*. When she was in seventh grade, she and her father moved to a small apartment in [downtown Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Los_Angeles "Downtown Los Angeles"), while her mother remained in Oakland. She later reflected that her father was highly attuned to her ambitions, and she "missed a lot of fun things" while pursuing her dreams. In 2015, Zendaya graduated from [Oak Park High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Park_High_School_(California) "Oak Park High School (California)") of [Ventura County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_County "Ventura County").

### 2009–2016: Disney Channel

Zendaya began her career working as a fashion model for [Macy's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macy%27s "Macy's"), [Old Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Navy "Old Navy"), and department store [Mervyn's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mervyn%27s "Mervyn's"), and appeared as a backup dancer in a [Sears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sears "Sears") commercial with [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") star [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez "Selena Gomez"). In 2009, she was a featured performer in the video for the children's group [Kidz Bop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidz_Bop "Kidz Bop") cover of [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry "Katy Perry")'s song "[Hot n Cold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_n_Cold "Hot n Cold")". The same year, Zendaya was among 200 girls who auditioned for the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") sitcom *[Shake It Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Up_(American_TV_series) "Shake It Up (American TV series)")*, which follows two teenage friends who get their big break on the local dance show *Shake It Up Chicago*. She originally auditioned for the role of CeCe Jones, which ultimately went to her co-star [Bella Thorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Thorne "Bella Thorne"); Zendaya was cast as Rocky Blue. Disney Channel senior vice president Judy Taylor said Zendaya had a "great presence" and was "completely engaging".

The [first season of *Shake It Up*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Up_season_1 "Shake It Up season 1") premiered on November 7, 2010, was watched by 6.2 million viewers, and was the Disney Channel's second highest-rated premiere ever. In 2011, Zendaya released "[Swag It Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swag_It_Out "Swag It Out")", a promotional independent single, and starred in the book trailer for "From Bad To Cursed" by Katie Alender. The same year, Zendaya released the single "[Watch Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watch_Me_(Bella_Thorne_%26_Zendaya_song) "Watch Me (Bella Thorne & Zendaya song)")" with Thorne, which peaked at number 86 on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") chart. It was also in 2011 that [Target](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Target_Corporation "Target Corporation") stores launched a clothing line inspired by the outfits worn by Zendaya and other *Shake It Up* cast members. The [second season of *Shake It Up*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Up_season_2 "Shake It Up season 2") premiered in September 2011. Zendaya made her film debut as an aspiring magazine editor in *[Frenemies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frenemies_(film) "Frenemies (film)")* (2012), a [Disney Channel Original Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel_Original_Movie "Disney Channel Original Movie"). In February 2012, Zendaya's "[Something to Dance For](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Something_to_Dance_For "Something to Dance For")" was released as a promotional single for the [soundtrack of the second season of *Shake It Up*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Up:_Live_2_Dance "Shake It Up: Live 2 Dance").

In October 2012, *Shake It Up*'s [third and final season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Up_season_3 "Shake It Up season 3") premiered. That year, Zendaya signed with [Hollywood Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Records "Hollywood Records"). She was later announced as one of the celebrity contestants on the [16th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_16 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 16") of [*Dancing with the Stars*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series) "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series)"). At age sixteen, she became the youngest contestant in the show's history at the time. She and her dance partner, the professional dancer [Valentin Chmerkovskiy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Chmerkovskiy "Valentin Chmerkovskiy"), finished as runners-up to [Kellie Pickler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kellie_Pickler "Kellie Pickler") and [Derek Hough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_Hough "Derek Hough"). Zendaya released her debut studio album, *[Zendaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zendaya_(album) "Zendaya (album)")*, on September 17, 2013. The [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music") and [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_R%26B "Contemporary R&B") album explores love and heartbreak, and peaked at number 51 on the US [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200"). It was preceded by its lead single, "[Replay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replay_(Zendaya_song) "Replay (Zendaya song)")", which peaked at number 40 on the *Billboard* Hot 100. She auditioned several times for [*Descendants*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descendants_(2015_film) "Descendants (2015 film)") but did not get a role.

In 2014, Zendaya starred in the Disney Channel film *[Zapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapped_(2014_film) "Zapped (2014 film)")* as a high school student whose cell phone gains the power to control the boys around her. She was then cast as the lead in a Disney Channel pilot, *Super Awesome Katy*. She was unhappy with the name of her character and the name of the series, and lobbied for them to be changed. Ultimately, the character's name was changed from Katy Cooper to K.C. Cooper, and the show was retitled *[K.C. Undercover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K.C._Undercover "K.C. Undercover")*. Disney made the 16-year-old Zendaya a producer on the show after she insisted on it. The studio also agreed to Zendaya's demands that the series depict a [family of color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Person_of_color "Person of color"), and that K.C. not be talented at acting, dancing or singing, or be otherwise artistically inclined, to show audiences that there are "other things that a girl can be." *K.C. Undercover* premiered in January 2015. In 2016 in the middle of its second season, the series had 160 million total viewers across all of Disney's platforms. [Common Sense Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Sense_Media "Common Sense Media") rated the series 3 out of 5, saying, "Disney megastar Zendaya rescues tepid spy-comedy."

In 2014, Zendaya appeared as a guest judge on an episode of *[Project Runway: Under the Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Runway:_Under_the_Gunn "Project Runway: Under the Gunn")* in which contestants were challenged to design an outfit for her to wear at an upcoming concert performance. In March 2015, musician [Timbaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbaland "Timbaland") confirmed that he was collaborating with Zendaya on her second album, after her move from Disney's label to [Republic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Records "Republic Records"). The following year, she released the single "[Something New](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Something_New_(Zendaya_song) "Something New (Zendaya song)")" featuring [Chris Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Brown "Chris Brown"), through Hollywood Records and Republic Records. In December 2016, Zendaya appeared as a guest judge in the season finale of *[Project Runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Runway "Project Runway")*'s [15th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Runway_season_15 "Project Runway season 15").

### 2017–2021: Mature roles and breakthrough

In 2017, Zendaya appeared in the music video for [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars "Bruno Mars")'s song "[Versace on the Floor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace_on_the_Floor "Versace on the Floor")" and made her feature film debut in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") film *[Spider-Man: Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Homecoming "Spider-Man: Homecoming")* as [Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MJ_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "MJ (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"). She wore no makeup and regular clothes to her [screen test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_test "Screen test"); producers [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige "Kevin Feige") and [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal "Amy Pascal") did not recognize her despite her fame. Zendaya described Michelle as awkward, intellectual, and "very dry". She found it refreshing to play a character who was "weird" and "different", and believed that many young people—and especially young women—could relate to Michelle. Zendaya added her own embellishments to the character, including carrying around a mug of "strange" herbal tea.

*Spider-Man:* *Homecoming* grossed over $880 million worldwide, making it the [sixth-highest-grossing film of 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_in_film#Highest-grossing_films "2017 in film"). Critics at *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* and *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* praised Zendaya as a "scene stealer", while [Stephanie Zacharek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Zacharek "Stephanie Zacharek") of [*Time*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)") lauded her "delightfully sullen insouciance". Zendaya then starred in the musical film *[The Greatest Showman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_Showman "The Greatest Showman")* (2017), portraying a trapeze artist who falls in love with a white man ([Zac Efron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Efron "Zac Efron")) during a time when interracial relationships were socially taboo. [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman") of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* praised her on-screen chemistry with Efron, while David Rooney of *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* said she brought "touching sensitivity" to her role. Zendaya appeared on three songs from the [film's soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_Showman_(soundtrack) "The Greatest Showman (soundtrack)"), including "[Rewrite the Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rewrite_the_Stars "Rewrite the Stars")" and "[The Greatest Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_Show "The Greatest Show")", which reached numbers 70 and 88 respectively on *Billboard* Hot 100.

In September 2018, Zendaya voiced the [yeti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeti "Yeti") Meechee in the [Warner Animation Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Pictures_Animation "Warner Bros. Pictures Animation") film *[Smallfoot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallfoot "Smallfoot")*, which received positive reviews from critics. In 2019, she began starring in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")*, an adaptation of the Israeli show [*Euphoria*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(Israeli_TV_series) "Euphoria (Israeli TV series)"). She plays [Rue Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rue_Bennett "Rue Bennett"), a 17-year-old drug addict who also narrates the series. Rebecca Nicholson of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* said Zendaya defied expectations about her acting abilities by giving a "truly astonishing, mesmerising performance" as the "self-destructive, self-loathing Rue". [Doreen St. Félix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doreen_St._F%C3%A9lix "Doreen St. Félix") of *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker "The New Yorker")* wrote that "it becomes difficult, and then absolutely silly, to recall the pink outlines of her early career on the Disney Channel, so grandly does she inhabit this dark new role".

The same year, she reprised her role as MJ in *[Spider-Man: Far From Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Far_From_Home "Spider-Man: Far From Home")*. Critic [Christy Lemire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_Lemire "Christy Lemire") called her performance "darkly alluring" and praised her "humorous, deadpan charm", while [Todd McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_McCarthy "Todd McCarthy") of *The Hollywood Reporter* described her as "appealing" and funny. The film grossed over $1.1 billion worldwide, ranking as the [fourth highest-grossing release of 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_in_film#Highest-grossing_films "2019 in film"). In 2020, Zendaya won the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series") for her performance in *Euphoria*, becoming the youngest recipient of the award at 24 years old. In 2021, she starred in and produced [Sam Levinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Levinson "Sam Levinson")'s *[Malcolm & Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malcolm_%26_Marie "Malcolm & Marie")*, filmed during the early months of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic") under strict safety protocols and with a minimal crew. The film received mixed reviews, with [Richard Brody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Brody "Richard Brody") of *The New Yorker* calling Zendaya "the movie's only redeeming quality".

Zendaya next voiced [Lola Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Bunny "Lola Bunny") in *[Space Jam: A New Legacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Jam:_A_New_Legacy "Space Jam: A New Legacy")* (2021). She accepted the role after [Ryan Coogler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Coogler "Ryan Coogler") approached her, citing both her admiration for him and her fondness for the [original 1996 film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Jam "Space Jam"). She also drew inspiration from her family's love of basketball for her performance. The same year, she portrayed [Chani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chani_(character) "Chani (character)") in [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve "Denis Villeneuve")'s science fiction epic *[Dune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_(2021_film) "Dune (2021 film)")*, the first installment of a two-part adaptation of the [1965 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_(novel) "Dune (novel)"). [Glenn Kenny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Kenny "Glenn Kenny") of [RogerEbert.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RogerEbert.com "RogerEbert.com") called her performance "better than apt". Zendaya then reprised her role as MJ in *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home "Spider-Man: No Way Home")*; critic Brian Tallerico of [RogerEbert.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RogerEbert.com "RogerEbert.com") praised her chemistry with [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland "Tom Holland") and her handling of MJ's "emotional final beats".

### 2022–present: Established actress

Zendaya started 2022 with the release of *Euphoria*'s [second season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_season_2 "Euphoria season 2"). Having previously collaborated with [Labrinth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labrinth "Labrinth") on the single "[All for Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_for_Us "All for Us")" for the [first season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_season_1 "Euphoria season 1"), she co-wrote two more songs, "[I'm Tired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%27m_Tired "I'm Tired")" and "[Elliot's Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot%27s_Song "Elliot's Song")", also singing on "I'm Tired". For her acting performance, she earned four Emmy nominations at the [74th Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "74th Primetime Emmy Awards"). She became the youngest person in Emmy history to be nominated twice for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series, and her nomination as an executive producer for [Outstanding Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series") made her the youngest woman ever nominated in the category. By winning the lead actress award, she also became the youngest person to win two Emmys. Zendaya next appeared in [Elvis Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Mitchell "Elvis Mitchell")'s documentary *[Is That Black Enough for You?!?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Is_That_Black_Enough_for_You%3F!%3F "Is That Black Enough for You?!?")* (2022).

At the [80th Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/80th_Golden_Globe_Awards "80th Golden Globe Awards") in January 2023, Zendaya won the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Drama "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Drama") for her performance in *Euphoria*, and received a [Screen Actors Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award "Screen Actors Guild Award") nomination the next month. During the second weekend of [Coachella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella "Coachella") in April 2023, she joined Labrinth for a surprise performance of "All for Us" and "I'm Tired", which was her first live performance in eight years. She was also featured on "The Feels", the opening track of Labrinth's third studio album *[Ends & Begins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ends_%26_Begins "Ends & Begins")*. Zendaya next appeared in the documentary *[Invisible Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invisible_Beauty "Invisible Beauty")* (2023) which focuses on the life and work of American model and activist [Bethann Hardison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethann_Hardison "Bethann Hardison").

In 2024, Zendaya reprised her role as Chani in *[Dune: Part Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune:_Part_Two "Dune: Part Two")*. During filming in the Jordan desert, she experienced [heatstroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heatstroke "Heatstroke") because she did not drink enough water for fear of having change out of costume to go to the bathroom. Alison Willmore of [*Vulture*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(website) "Vulture (website)") praised her "fierce, open-hearted performance", while Austen Goslin of [*Polygon*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygon_(website) "Polygon (website)") lauded her "economical" acting, deeming it a career best. She next starred in and produced *[Challengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challengers_(film) "Challengers (film)")* (2024), directed by [Luca Guadagnino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Guadagnino "Luca Guadagnino"). In the film, she plays a former tennis prodigy who suffered a career-ending injury and became a coach. She characterized the role as an opportunity to abandon her youthful image and portray a character closer to her own age. Hans Simran of the *[New Statesman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Statesman "New Statesman")* called the film "a brilliant showcase for Zendaya, whose on-screen magnetism has rarely been channelled so effectively". For her performance, Zendaya was nominated for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy") at the [82nd Golden Globes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/82nd_Golden_Globes "82nd Golden Globes").

In 2026, Zendaya reprised her role as Rue in the [third and final season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_season_3 "Euphoria season 3") of *Euphoria* and starred opposite [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson "Robert Pattinson") in the romantic drama [*The Drama*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Drama_(film) "The Drama (film)"). She also appeared as the goddess [Athena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athena "Athena") in [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan "Christopher Nolan")'s [*The Odyssey*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Odyssey_(2026_film) "The Odyssey (2026 film)") and as [MJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MJ_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "MJ (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in *[Spider-Man: Brand New Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Brand_New_Day "Spider-Man: Brand New Day")*. After finding MJ to be too "normal" in an early draft of the *Brand New Day* screenplay, Zendaya worked with the writers to give MJ more quirks. The total combined gross of *The Drama*, *The Odyssey* and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* surpassed $4 billion worldwide, making Zendaya the highest-grossing actor of 2026.

#### Upcoming

Zendaya reprises her role as Chani in *[Dune: Part Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune:_Part_Three "Dune: Part Three")*, releasing in December 2026. She will voice [Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek_(character) "Shrek (character)") and [Fiona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Fiona "Princess Fiona")'s daughter Felicia in *[Shrek 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek_5 "Shrek 5")*, scheduled for release in 2027.

## Fashion and media image

Zendaya has been described as a "style star" by the fashion-industry trade journal *[Women's Wear Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_Wear_Daily "Women's Wear Daily")*. She is recognized for her use of method dressing, a promotional fashion practice in which an actor incorporates elements of a film's themes, characters, or aesthetics into their red carpet and press appearances. She began the practice following her work on *The Greatest Showman*. Zendaya is known for her longtime partnership with stylist [Law Roach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_Roach "Law Roach").

Zendaya has promoted the brands [Beats Electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beats_Electronics "Beats Electronics"), [Material Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna_fashion_brands#Material_Girl "Madonna fashion brands"), [CoverGirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoverGirl "CoverGirl"), and [Chi Hair Care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farouk_Shami "Farouk Shami"), and has modeled for the designers [Michael Kors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kors "Michael Kors") and [Dolce & Gabbana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolce_%26_Gabbana "Dolce & Gabbana"). In 2013, she published her first book, *Between U and Me: How to Rock Your Tween Years with Style and Confidence*, which she wrote to "help girls through the tougher parts of the tween years". In 2015, she launched her first shoe collection, Daya, and the following year she launched a clothing line, Daya by Zendaya.

In 2016, Zendaya was featured on *Forbes*' [30 under 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_30_Under_30 "Forbes 30 Under 30") list. She was included on *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")*'s [annual list of the 100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") in 2022; *Dune* director Denis Villeneuve called her "a cultural icon in the making. The same year, *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* ranked Zendaya on its list of the 500 most influential figures in global media. She was ranked as one of the best-dressed women in 2018 by fashion website [Net-a-Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YOOX_Net-a-Porter_Group "YOOX Net-a-Porter Group"). For the September 2020 *[InStyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InStyle "InStyle")* issue, she and her stylist Law Roach chose to use all Black designers, artists, and creatives. In October 2020, she won the Visionary Award at the [CNMI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chamber_of_Italian_Fashion "National Chamber of Italian Fashion") Green Carpet Fashion Awards for "promoting diversity and inclusion in fashion and film". In November 2021, she became the youngest person to ever win the [CFDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Fashion_Designers_of_America "Council of Fashion Designers of America") Fashion Icon award at the [CFDA Fashion Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFDA_Fashion_Awards "CFDA Fashion Awards").

In October 2018, Zendaya became a [brand ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_ambassador "Brand ambassador") for [Tommy Hilfiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Hilfiger_(company) "Tommy Hilfiger (company)") and co-designed the Tommy x Zendaya capsule collections, drawing inspiration from the 1970s and its "strong, iconic women". Her [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Fashion_Week "Paris Fashion Week") and [New York Fashion Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Fashion_Week "New York Fashion Week") shows were acclaimed for their celebration of diversity and inclusivity, featuring women of color, plus-size models, and models up to seventy years old. Zendaya became a spokesmodel for [Lancôme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanc%C3%B4me "Lancôme") in 2019, followed by [Bulgari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgari "Bulgari") and [Valentino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentino_(fashion_house) "Valentino (fashion house)") in 2020. In 2022, she was named the global brand ambassador for [Glaceau Smartwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaceau_Smartwater "Glaceau Smartwater"), and in 2023, she became an ambassador for [Louis Vuitton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Vuitton "Louis Vuitton"). Zendaya has been the face of footwear company [On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_(company) "On (company)") since 2024, and she became an ambassador for [Rolex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolex "Rolex") in 2025.

In 2025, an internet meme emerged revolving around a big [Emporio Armani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emporio_Armani "Emporio Armani") hat Zendaya wore to the Teen Vogue Young Hollywood Party in 2014. While it had already been a prominent subject of discussion, it led to the emergence of a subculture on TikTok based on the "Zendaya Hat Theory", a collection of absurdist memes based on the hat. A notable joke of the Zendaya Hat Theory was that Zendaya's husband Tom Holland lived under the hat. In August 2026, Zendaya became the Global Ambassador for [Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prada "Prada") Paradoxe.

## Philanthropy and activism

Zendaya has lent her support to several charities and causes. In 2012, Zendaya performed at a benefit event for [Operation Smile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Smile "Operation Smile") and supported [Hurricane Sandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Sandy "Hurricane Sandy") relief efforts as an ambassador for the organization [Convoy of Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convoy_of_Hope "Convoy of Hope"). In 2014, she recorded a [cover version](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_version "Cover version") of [John Legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Legend "John Legend")'s song "[All of Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_of_Me_(John_Legend_song) "All of Me (John Legend song)")", with a portion of the proceeds going to Convoy of Hope. In 2016, she celebrated her 20th birthday with a campaign to raise $50,000 to support the organization's Women's Empowerment Initiative. She was a spokesperson for [UNICEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNICEF "UNICEF")'s [Trick-or-Treat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trick-or-Treat_for_UNICEF "Trick-or-Treat for UNICEF") campaign in 2014. In July 2015, she visited South Africa with [UNAIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_United_Nations_Programme_on_HIV/AIDS "Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS"), the United Nations program dedicated to preventing and creating access to treatment for [HIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV "HIV") and [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS "HIV/AIDS"). She also held a fundraiser with [Crowdrise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowdrise "Crowdrise"), with proceeds going to nonprofit, community-based, Ikageng charity in [Soweto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soweto "Soweto") for a family of AIDS orphans. When *Malcolm & Marie*, filmed during the [pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"), was sold in September 2020, a portion of the proceeds was shared with [Feeding America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feeding_America "Feeding America"). In October 2023, she voiced support for [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine "Palestine").

Zendaya supports campaigns to raise awareness about underserved communities and underprivileged schools and to financially support schools. In 2017, she became a spokesperson for the Verizon Foundation's #WeNeedMore initiative, which seeks to bring technology and learning opportunities to children. In 2018, she partnered with [Google.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google.org "Google.org") to fund a computer science program at an Oakland school.

Zendaya considers herself an [intersectional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersectionality "Intersectionality") feminist. She took part in the [George Floyd protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Floyd_protests "George Floyd protests") in June 2020 and temporarily lent her Instagram account to [Patrisse Cullors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrisse_Cullors "Patrisse Cullors") to share [anti-racism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-racism "Anti-racism") resources and media. She has been an advocate for voting; in October 2016, she participated in the "Vote Your Future" initiative, and in September 2020, she and her nonpartisan "When We All Vote" organization encouraged fans to check their voter registration ahead of elections. In October 2013, Zendaya took part in [P&G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procter_%26_Gamble "Procter & Gamble")'s anti-bullying campaign Mean Stinks, co-hosting a nationwide live-streamed assembly with nearly 500 schools. In September 2017, she and her *[Spider-Man: Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Homecoming "Spider-Man: Homecoming")* co-stars were featured in a [PSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_service_announcement "Public service announcement") for the awareness campaign [Stomp Out Bullying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Bullying_Day "Anti-Bullying Day").

## Personal life

In November 2021, Zendaya and her *Spider-Man* co-star [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland "Tom Holland") confirmed they were in a relationship. Zendaya felt the [media attention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parasocial_interaction "Parasocial interaction") their relationship received was "weird and confusing and invasive". The couple moved into a £3 million home in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London") in 2023 and became engaged in December 2024. During the [32nd Actor Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/32nd_Actor_Awards "32nd Actor Awards") in March 2026, Zendaya's longtime stylist [Law Roach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_Roach "Law Roach") announced that Zendaya and Holland had secretly wed. Holland confirmed their marriage during an interview with [*Esquire* UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_(UK_Edition) "Esquire (UK Edition)") that June. The couple held a private wedding celebration two months later in [Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey "Surrey"). Zendaya also owns a house in Los Angeles and a condominium in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn "Brooklyn").

In 2024 she told *Vogue* magazine that she had a complicated relationship to being child star and wished that she went to school. She is a [vegetarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetarianism "Vegetarianism") due to her love for animals.

## Awards and recognition

In [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/72nd_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "72nd Primetime Emmy Awards"), at age 24, Zendaya became the youngest recipient of the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series"), winning for her role as [Rue Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rue_Bennett "Rue Bennett") in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO") drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")*. After winning the award again at the [2022 Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "74th Primetime Emmy Awards"), she became the youngest two-time acting winner. Zendaya received the [CinemaCon Star of the Year Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaCon "CinemaCon") in 2023.
