# Macklemore

**Benjamin Hammond Haggerty** (born June 19, 1983), better known by his [stage name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stage_name "Stage name") **Macklemore** ([/ˈmækləmɔːr/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*MAK-lə-mor*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); formerly **Professor Macklemore**), is an American rapper. A native of [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle "Seattle"), Washington, he started his career in 2000 as an independent artist releasing *Open Your Eyes* (2000), *[The Language of My World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Language_of_My_World "The Language of My World")* (2005), and *The Unplanned Mixtape* (2009). He rose to international success collaborating with producer [Ryan Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Lewis "Ryan Lewis") as the duo [Macklemore & Ryan Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macklemore_%26_Ryan_Lewis "Macklemore & Ryan Lewis") (2009–2016).

Macklemore & Ryan Lewis's 2012 single "[Thrift Shop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrift_Shop "Thrift Shop")" (featuring [Wanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanz "Wanz")) became the first self-released song to top the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") in 11 years. Their second single, "[Can't Hold Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Hold_Us "Can't Hold Us")" (featuring [Ray Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Dalton "Ray Dalton")), also peaked on chart, making Macklemore and Lewis the first duo in the chart's history to have their first two singles both reach the peak position. Their debut studio album *[The Heist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heist_(album) "The Heist (album)")* (2012) peaked at number 2 on the [U.S. *Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Billboard_200 "U.S. Billboard 200"). The duo entered into a slightly unusual three-month, one-album contract with [Warner Bros.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._records "Warner Bros. records")'s independent [distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_distribution "Record distribution") arm [ADA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_Distribution_Alliance "Alternative Distribution Alliance"), which allowed them to retain control over their [intellectual property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intellectual_property "Intellectual property") and maintain their "artistic integrity". At the [56th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/56th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "56th Annual Grammy Awards"), the duo won [Best New Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_New_Artist "Best New Artist"), [Best Rap Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Rap_Album "Grammy Award for Best Rap Album") (*The Heist*), [Best Rap Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Rap_Song "Grammy Award for Best Rap Song") ("[Same Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same_Love "Same Love")") and [Best Rap Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Rap_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Rap Performance") ("Thrift Shop"). The duo's second album, *[This Unruly Mess I've Made](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Unruly_Mess_I%27ve_Made "This Unruly Mess I've Made")* (2016), followed thereafter.

In June 2017, Macklemore released the single "[Glorious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorious_(Macklemore_song) "Glorious (Macklemore song)")" (featuring [Skylar Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skylar_Grey "Skylar Grey")), which marked his return to the music industry as well as his first major single produced without Lewis since his mainstream breakthrough. Macklemore released his second solo studio album, *[Gemini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(Macklemore_album) "Gemini (Macklemore album)")*, in September of that year, peaking at number 2 on the *Billboard* 200. On March 3, 2023, he released his third album *[Ben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_(Macklemore_album) "Ben (Macklemore album)")*, which peaked at number 18.

## Early life and influences

Benjamin Hammond Haggerty was born on June 19, 1983, in Seattle, Washington, one of two sons born to Bill Haggerty and Julie Schott. He was raised with his brother Tim in Seattle's [Capitol Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill,_Seattle "Capitol Hill, Seattle") neighborhood. He has [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_people "Irish people") heritage and was raised [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church "Catholic Church"). Haggerty was six years old when [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop") first came into his life by way of [Digital Underground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Underground "Digital Underground"). At age fifteen, he began to write lyrics.

At the time he started to sing, Haggerty listened to "a lot of [East Coast underground hip hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Coast_hip_hop "East Coast hip hop")", listing [Hieroglyphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hieroglyphics_(group) "Hieroglyphics (group)"), [Freestyle Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freestyle_Fellowship "Freestyle Fellowship"), [Aceyalone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceyalone "Aceyalone"), [Living Legends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_Legends_(group) "Living Legends (group)"), [Wu-Tang Clan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu-Tang_Clan "Wu-Tang Clan"), [Mobb Deep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobb_Deep "Mobb Deep"), [Nas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nas "Nas"), and [Talib Kweli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talib_Kweli "Talib Kweli") as his major influences. Haggerty attended [Garfield High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garfield_High_School_(Seattle,_Washington) "Garfield High School (Seattle, Washington)") and [Nathan Hale High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Hale_High_School_(Washington) "Nathan Hale High School (Washington)"). At Hale, he developed the stage name "Professor Macklemore" for an art project involving a made-up superhero, and at Garfield, he started a hip-hop group named Elevated Elements with other students. The group released an album, *Progress*, in 2000.

Haggerty enrolled at the [College of Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Santa_Fe "College of Santa Fe") for a year, later saying in an interview that "[i]t was a very pivotal time in a lot of ways. [Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_New_Mexico "Santa Fe, New Mexico") was the place where I got good at rapping". After not getting into the music program, he moved back to Seattle. Haggerty later enrolled at [Evergreen State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evergreen_State_College "Evergreen State College") in [Olympia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympia,_Washington "Olympia, Washington") and completed his bachelor's degree in 2009. While at Evergreen, he was interested in reaching a younger generation through his music. He became part of a program focusing on education and cultural identity called "Gateways for Incarcerated Youth", where he facilitated music workshops.

## Career

### 2000–2008: *The Language of My World*

In 2000, Haggerty recorded a mixtape titled *Open Your Eyes* under the name Professor Macklemore, which he distributed himself and released on October 21, 2000. Haggerty dropped "Professor" from his name and began working on his first official full-length album, called *[The Language of My World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Language_of_My_World "The Language of My World")*. The album was released on January 1, 2005, with its lead single, "Love Song", being announced the same day. "Love Song" featured singer Evan Roman, and was produced by Budo, who would later go on to produce several more tracks for Macklemore.

In 2006, Haggerty first met his future collaborator [Ryan Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Lewis "Ryan Lewis"). Lewis, who would go on to release two albums with Haggerty as [Macklemore & Ryan Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macklemore_%26_Ryan_Lewis "Macklemore & Ryan Lewis"), spent a few years working on Macklemore's promotion as a photographer. The two musicians would soon become good friends but would not formalize their collaborative efforts until 2009. In the meantime, Macklemore kept busy as a solo artist, appearing on [The Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Physics_(music_group) "The Physics (music group)")'s song "Good" in 2009, as well as performing at the Seattle major arts and music festival [Bumbershoot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumbershoot "Bumbershoot") in 2006, 2009, and 2011.

Macklemore's second mixtape, *The Unplanned Mixtape,* was released on September 7, 2009, reaching No. 7 on the [iTunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITunes "ITunes") Hip Hop chart. The [mixtape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixtape "Mixtape") was accompanied by the singles "The Town" and "And We Danced", the latter of which featured singer Ziggy Stardust. "The Town" was later remixed by Sabzi of the [Blue Scholars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Scholars "Blue Scholars").

### 2009–2017: Career with Ryan Lewis

In 2009, Macklemore and [Ryan Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Lewis "Ryan Lewis") formalized their collaboration as the duo [Macklemore & Ryan Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macklemore_%26_Ryan_Lewis "Macklemore & Ryan Lewis"). They released the EP *The VS. EP*. They also released "Irish Celebration" in December 2009 in anticipation of the release of *The Vs. EP*. In March 2010, the duo released "Stay At Home Dad", a track that "didn't quite make" *Vs.* In October 2010, they created the *VS. Redux* EP. Macklemore used his experience with substance abuse to create the mixtape's song "Otherside", which samples the [Red Hot Chili Peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Hot_Chili_Peppers "Red Hot Chili Peppers") song of the [same title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otherside "Otherside"). On April 8, 2011, Macklemore and Ryan Lewis performed the song at the 2011 [Mariners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariners "Mariners") Opening Day in front of a sold-out crowd of nearly 48,000 attendees. "[Wings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wings_(Macklemore_and_Ryan_Lewis_song) "Wings (Macklemore and Ryan Lewis song)")" was released on January 21, 2011, followed by "[Can't Hold Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can%27t_Hold_Us "Can't Hold Us")" (featuring [Ray Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Dalton "Ray Dalton")) on August 16, 2011. In February 2011, Macklemore and Lewis kicked off a multi-city tour in Seattle, which included three sold-out shows at the [Showbox at the Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showbox_at_the_Market "Showbox at the Market") music venue. That same year, the rapper appeared at many U.S. music festivals, including [Bumbershoot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumbershoot "Bumbershoot"), [Outside Lands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outside_Lands_Music_and_Arts_Festival "Outside Lands Music and Arts Festival"), [Lollapalooza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza "Lollapalooza"), [Rock the Bells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_the_Bells "Rock the Bells"), [Soundset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundset_Music_Festival "Soundset Music Festival"), [Sasquatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasquatch!_Music_Festival "Sasquatch! Music Festival"), and [Bonnaroo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnaroo "Bonnaroo").

Their album *[The Heist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heist_(album) "The Heist (album)")* was released in October 2012 and debuted on the U.S. [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") at number 2 of the week dated October 27, 2012, selling over 78,000 copies. "[Same Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same_Love "Same Love")" was released on July 18, 2012, and later "[White Walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Walls "White Walls")". In January 2013, [Music Choice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_Choice "Music Choice") featured Macklemore in the brand new series "Primed", which focuses on emerging artists. In May 2013, Haggerty was featured on [Clinton Sparks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Sparks "Clinton Sparks")'s single "Gold Rush", along with [2 Chainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_Chainz "2 Chainz") and D.A. The Heist World Tour began in August 2012 to promote *The Heist*.

In a private concert at the [Experience Music Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_Pop_Culture "Museum of Pop Culture") museum in Seattle on May 16, 2014, Macklemore wore a costume featuring a wig, beard, and prosthetic [hooked nose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooked_nose "Hooked nose"), which some said resembled a [Jewish caricature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereotypes_of_Jews "Stereotypes of Jews"), and accused him of [antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism "Antisemitism"). Guests at the museum event had been asked to dress as their "favorite music icon or video character" — many who saw photos of him said that Macklemore's costume did not portray such an icon. *[The Stranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stranger_(newspaper) "The Stranger (newspaper)")* wrote that the nose Macklemore seemingly wore may have been a "Sheik/[Fagin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fagin "Fagin")" nose. By May 20, the rapper had apologized and stated that his intention was to disguise himself, not to impersonate any 'type' of person. The [Anti-Defamation League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Defamation_League "Anti-Defamation League") accepted Macklemore's apology, saying that they "take him at his word that he did not have any ill-intent" and believed that the issue was 'a tempest in a teapot' "over an unfortunate choice of wardrobe." [B'nai B'rith International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%27nai_B%27rith_International "B'nai B'rith International") and [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen "Seth Rogen") expressed doubt over the explanation provided by Macklemore.

In January 2015, Macklemore announced via Twitter that his third studio album would be released sometime in the second half of that year. Despite this, the album was not released until February 26, 2016. On August 5, 2015, Macklemore released a song for free download titled "Growing Up (Sloane's Song)", which features [Ed Sheeran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Sheeran "Ed Sheeran"). On August 27, 2015, he released a new song called "[Downtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_(Macklemore_%26_Ryan_Lewis_song) "Downtown (Macklemore & Ryan Lewis song)")" which features [Foxy Shazam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxy_Shazam "Foxy Shazam") vocalist Eric Nally, [Kool Moe Dee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kool_Moe_Dee "Kool Moe Dee"), [Melle Mel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melle_Mel "Melle Mel"), and [Grandmaster Caz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grandmaster_Caz "Grandmaster Caz"), which he performed at the [2015 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2015 MTV Video Music Awards") on August 30. On his first tour in two years, An Evening with Macklemore and Ryan Lewis, Macklemore announced that his new album was finished and ready for release.

On January 15, 2016, Macklemore released a teaser video on his [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") channel revealing the name of his third studio album, *[This Unruly Mess I've Made](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Unruly_Mess_I%27ve_Made "This Unruly Mess I've Made")*, and announcing that it was scheduled to be released on February 26, 2016. On January 22, 2016, the duo released "[White Privilege II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Privilege_II "White Privilege II")", the second single on *This Unruly Mess I've Made*. On February 26, 2016, Macklemore and Ryan Lewis released the album *This Unruly Mess I've Made*. Also in 2016, Macklemore released two solo singles, "[Drug Dealer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_Dealer_(song) "Drug Dealer (song)")" and "Wednesday Morning". "Drug Dealer" features Macklemore rapping about his previous addictions and is featured in a documentary that includes clips of Macklemore discussing drug abuse with President [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama"). "Wednesday Morning" was released after the [2016 U.S. Election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_States_presidential_election "2016 United States presidential election") and features Macklemore rapping about the political future of the country. The tracks were produced by [Budo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budo_(musician) "Budo (musician)"). On June 15, 2017, Macklemore announced via his official [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") that the duo were on hiatus.

### 2017–present: *Gemini* & *Ben*

On June 15, 2017, Macklemore released "[Glorious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorious_(Macklemore_song) "Glorious (Macklemore song)")", featuring American singer [Skylar Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skylar_Grey "Skylar Grey"), as the lead single from Macklemore's second solo studio album *[Gemini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(Macklemore_album) "Gemini (Macklemore album)")*. On July 26, 2017, Macklemore released "[Marmalade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marmalade_(Macklemore_song) "Marmalade (Macklemore song)")", featuring American rapper [Lil Yachty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Yachty "Lil Yachty"), as the second single from Macklemore's upcoming album. "[Good Old Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Old_Days_(Macklemore_song) "Good Old Days (Macklemore song)")", a collaboration with [Kesha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kesha "Kesha"), was released as a single on October 9, 2017. Macklemore released *Gemini* on September 22. The album was produced by longtime collaborator Budo.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] On October 1, Macklemore performed "Same Love" in a set at the opening of [the Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_NRL_Grand_Final "2017 NRL Grand Final") of the [National Rugby League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Rugby_League "National Rugby League") in [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney "Sydney"), Australia — something that was considered by some to be controversial in the middle of a [national survey on same sex marriage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Marriage_Law_Postal_Survey "Australian Marriage Law Postal Survey"). Macklemore stated that it was one of his best performances due to the circumstances and thanked the fans in Sydney for the reception that he received throughout. On December 11, 2017, Macklemore announced an upcoming co-headlining tour with Kesha titled [The Adventures of Kesha and Macklemore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Kesha_and_Macklemore "The Adventures of Kesha and Macklemore"). This was Macklemore's fifth headlining tour, promoting his album *Gemini* and Kesha's sixth headlining tour, promoting her third solo album *[Rainbow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow_(Kesha_album) "Rainbow (Kesha album)")* (2017). The tour began in Phoenix on June 6, 2018, and concluded in Tampa on August 5, 2018.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

On October 29, 2021, Macklemore released a new single titled "Next Year", featuring American singer [Windser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windser "Windser"). Macklemore also worked with Ryan Lewis on the single, marking their first collaboration in three years. On July 22, 2022, Macklemore released a new single, "[Chant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chant_(song) "Chant (song)")", with Australian musician [Tones and I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tones_and_I "Tones and I"). On August 19, 2022, Macklemore released the single, "[Maniac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maniac_(Macklemore_song) "Maniac (Macklemore song)")", featuring American musician Windser. The third single, "[Faithful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faithful_(Macklemore_song) "Faithful (Macklemore song)")", featuring [NLE Choppa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NLE_Choppa "NLE Choppa"), was released on October 28, 2022. The fourth single, "[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(Macklemore_song) "Heroes (Macklemore song)")", featuring [DJ Premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ_Premier "DJ Premier"), was released on January 20, 2023. Macklemore's third solo studio album, *[Ben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_(Macklemore_album) "Ben (Macklemore album)")*, was released on March 3, 2023.

In May 2024, he released "[Hind's Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hind%27s_Hall "Hind's Hall")", a [protest song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protest_song "Protest song") in support of the [2024 pro-Palestinian protests on university campuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_pro-Palestinian_protests_on_university_campuses "2024 pro-Palestinian protests on university campuses"), with all proceeds donated towards [UNRWA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNRWA "UNRWA"). In September 2026, Macklemore joined Ed Sheeran's [Loop Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_Tour "Loop Tour") as an opening act. On September 4, he performed "Hind's Hall" and delivered a two-minute speech calling for a free Palestine. His comments attracted backlash from pro-Israel groups and, on September 14, he was [removed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macklemore%E2%80%93Ed_Sheeran_tour_controversy "Macklemore–Ed Sheeran tour controversy") from the tour after [concert venue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concert_venue "Concert venue") owners organized a boycott against him. Following his removal from the tour, he announced the "Free Palestine Tour", with proceeds donated to Palestinian charities.

## Personal life

### Family

Macklemore became engaged to his girlfriend of seven years, Tricia Davis, on January 21, 2013. On January 3, 2015, he announced on Twitter that he and Davis were expecting their first child that May. After the release of "Growing Up (Sloane's Song)", which featured English singer-songwriter [Ed Sheeran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Sheeran "Ed Sheeran"), the couple announced that their daughter was born in May. They married on June 27, 2015.

In September 2017, the couple announced that they were expecting their second child. On April 3, 2018, Macklemore announced during a performance at the [3Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3Arena_(Dublin) "3Arena (Dublin)") that Davis had given birth to their second daughter. In 2021, they had their third child. Earlier in 2017, Macklemore and his grandmother, Helen Schott, celebrated her [100th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenarian "Centenarian") birthday, with the two traveling around [Modesto, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modesto,_California "Modesto, California") for the music video of his song "[Glorious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorious_(Macklemore_song) "Glorious (Macklemore song)")."

### Health

In August 2008, Macklemore admitted himself into [drug rehabilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_rehabilitation "Drug rehabilitation") for [drug addiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_addiction "Drug addiction") and [alcoholism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoholism "Alcoholism"), and celebrated three years of sobriety before a brief relapse in 2011, which he describes in his song "Starting Over". He said in a 2012 documentary that he spent most of his twenties trying to combat his addictions and destructive way of life:

> I want to be someone who is respected and not just in terms of my music. I want to be respected in terms of the way that I treat people... Music is my creative outlet in terms of expressing what is important to me; what has importance, what has a value. And I want to be respected for that.

On May 14, 2016, Macklemore appeared in U.S. President [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama")'s weekly address to talk about the dangers of [addiction to opioids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opioid_use_disorder "Opioid use disorder") and prescription [painkillers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analgesic "Analgesic"). Macklemore talked about his own experiences with abuse of painkillers, stating "When you're going through it, it's hard to imagine anything being worse than addiction. But the shame and stigma associated with the disease keeps too many people from seeking the help they actually need. Addiction isn't a personal choice or a personal failure."

On September 29, 2018, Macklemore headlined a concert titled Recovery Fest in [Pawtucket, Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawtucket,_Rhode_Island "Pawtucket, Rhode Island"), held as a drug- and alcohol-free event to support charities that worked in combating opioid addiction. In the summer of 2020, during and in part due to the social isolation that took place during the [COVID-19 lockdowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_lockdowns "COVID-19 lockdowns"), Macklemore relapsed and suffered an overdose. He was hospitalized and given treatment; he eventually recovered from the episode. This experience, as well as the ongoing challenge of overcoming addiction while also raising children, served as an inspiration for much of the lyrical content that makes up his third solo studio album, *Ben.*

## Activism and political views

Macklemore voiced his support of [LGBT rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_rights "LGBT rights") and [same-sex marriage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage "Same-sex marriage") in the song "[Same Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same_Love "Same Love")" released in 2012, which also condemns [homophobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homophobia "Homophobia") in mainstream hip-hop, society, and mass media. Macklemore is also an outspoken critic of [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump"). In July 2016, he was featured on "Pt. 2" remix of [YG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YG_(rapper) "YG (rapper)")'s "[FDT (Fuck Donald Trump)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FDT_(song) "FDT (song)")" alongside [G-Eazy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-Eazy "G-Eazy"). In January 2021, after the [presidential inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Joe_Biden "Inauguration of Joe Biden") of [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden"), he released "Trump's Over Freestyle". Macklemore attended the [2017 Women's March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Women%27s_March "2017 Women's March") in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C. "Washington, D.C.") On May 16, 2019, Macklemore received the [Stevie Ray Vaughan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Ray_Vaughan "Stevie Ray Vaughan") Award from [MusiCares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MusiCares "MusiCares"), in recognition of his support of MusiCares and the addiction recovery process.

In August 2024, Macklemore cancelled his show scheduled to take place in October that year in [Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai "Dubai"), citing the United Arab Emirates' involvement "in the [ongoing genocide and humanitarian crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanitarian_impact_of_the_Sudanese_civil_war_(2023%E2%80%93present) "Humanitarian impact of the Sudanese civil war (2023–present)")" in Sudan. He said he would not perform in the country until it "stops arming and funding the [RSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_Support_Forces "Rapid Support Forces")". He also mentioned having no judgement of the artists continuing to perform in the Emirates.

### Israeli–Palestinian conflict

Macklemore is a supporter of [Palestinian liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_nationalism "Palestinian nationalism"). On October 30, 2023, during the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war"), he signed the [Artists4Ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artists4Ceasefire "Artists4Ceasefire") letter calling for a ceasefire and an end to the [Israeli blockade of the Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_blockade_of_the_Gaza_Strip_(2023%E2%80%93present) "Israeli blockade of the Gaza Strip (2023–present)"). In November 2023, while giving a speech at a pro-Palestinian rally in Washington, D.C., he criticised [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel "Israel") for committing [genocide against Palestinians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_genocide "Gaza genocide"). In December 2023, he read out a poem that called for a "[Free Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/From_the_river_to_the_sea "From the river to the sea")" and criticized the [United States' support for Israel in the Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_support_for_Israel_in_the_Gaza_war "United States support for Israel in the Gaza war").

In May 2024, he released "[Hind's Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hind%27s_Hall "Hind's Hall")", a [protest song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protest_song "Protest song") in support of the 2024 [Gaza war protests at universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war_protests_at_universities "Gaza war protests at universities"), with all proceeds donated towards [UNRWA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNRWA "UNRWA").

At a pro-Palestinian rally in September 2024, Macklemore exclaimed "fuck America", causing widespread controversy, including being dropped from the Neon City Festival in Las Vegas. The [Seattle Mariners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Mariners "Seattle Mariners"), [Seattle Kraken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Kraken "Seattle Kraken"), and [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC "Seattle Sounders FC") all condemned his statements and stated that they would review any further involvement from Macklemore. He later apologized for the incident, saying, "My thoughts and feelings are not always expressed perfectly or politely," and that at times he "slips up and gets caught in a moment."

Macklemore joined with [Watermelon Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermelon_Pictures "Watermelon Pictures") and [BreakThrough News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BreakThrough_News "BreakThrough News") to produce *[The Encampments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Encampments "The Encampments")*, a documentary film about the [Gaza Solidarity Encampment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Solidarity_Encampment_(Columbia_University) "Gaza Solidarity Encampment (Columbia University)") at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University"). The film features interviews with prominent protestors, including [Mahmoud Khalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmoud_Khalil "Mahmoud Khalil"). *The Encampments* premiered at [CPH:DOX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPH:DOX "CPH:DOX") on March 25, 2025.

#### Ed Sheeran tour controversy

While [opening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opening_act "Opening act") for [Ed Sheeran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Sheeran "Ed Sheeran")'s [Loop Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_Tour "Loop Tour") in [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey "New Jersey") at the [MetLife Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MetLife_Stadium "MetLife Stadium") in September 2026, Macklemore declared "Free Palestine," telling the audience "I believe that every human being walking on this earth deserves exactly the same freedom." He also condemned [United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Immigration_and_Customs_Enforcement "United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement") (ICE) during his speech to the audience. The [Israeli-American Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli-American_Council "Israeli-American Council"), a pro-Israel nonprofit organization, initiated a petition directed at Sheeran to remove Macklemore from the remainder of the tour. At the second MetLife Stadium concert, Macklemore restated his support of Palestinians, also saying that his proclamation and criticism of Israel is "in no way a criticism" of Jewish people, and "My message is for peace, love, for all human beings to be treated with dignity, respect, and equality." On September 14, 2026, Sheeran dropped Macklemore from the tour as a result of an ultimatum from several venue owners, including [Robert Kraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Kraft "Robert Kraft"). After he was dropped, Macklemore posted a statement on Instagram, saying: "Before I get into it, I want to say something that should be obvious. In this moment I think it matters. I am not a victim. Ed Sheeran is not a victim. [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_(singer) "Pink (singer)") is not a victim. We have careers, money, opportunities, safety and audiences around the world. Whatever consequences any of us face for what we say, we get to go home to our families. The victims are the Palestinian people."

[Aaron Rowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Rowe_(singer) "Aaron Rowe (singer)") and [Lukas Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Graham "Lukas Graham") had been scheduled to replace Macklemore on Sheeran's remaining U.S. dates, while [Beoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beoga "Beoga") had been performing with Sheeran during his sets, and [Finneas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finneas_O%27Connell "Finneas O'Connell") was scheduled to support Sheeran on dates in South America. All of them subsequently dropped out of the tour in solidarity with Macklemore and in support of and solidarity with Palestinians.

In September 2026, following the controversy, Macklemore announced he would embark on a "Free Palestine" tour with proceeds donated to organizations assisting Palestinians. He said, "People are asking artists to show up, educate themselves and be in community. To use the stage for more than entertainment. To push back when the call for a Free Palestine is met with attempts to silence it."

### Interests

Macklemore is a fan of the [Seattle Seahawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Seahawks "Seattle Seahawks"). He created a [12th man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_man_(football) "12th man (football)") promotional video and performed at the [2014 NFC Championship Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_NFC_Championship_Game "2014 NFC Championship Game"). He remains a fan of the now-[relocated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_SuperSonics_relocation_to_Oklahoma_City "Seattle SuperSonics relocation to Oklahoma City") [Seattle SuperSonics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_SuperSonics "Seattle SuperSonics"). In August 2019, Macklemore joined the ownership group of [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC "Seattle Sounders FC"), the local [Major League Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Soccer "Major League Soccer") club. He is a fan of the [Seattle Kraken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Kraken "Seattle Kraken") of the [National Hockey League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hockey_League "National Hockey League"); on April 18, 2022, the Kraken announced that Macklemore had joined its ownership group. Macklemore has an eclectic taste in art, including outlandish garments sporting sequins, fringe and feathers, a [kitschy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitsch "Kitsch") velvet painting of a [bald eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bald_eagle "Bald eagle"), an oil painting of [Drake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drake_(musician) "Drake (musician)") dancing and a [Dan Lacey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Lacey "Dan Lacey") painting of a nude [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber "Justin Bieber").
