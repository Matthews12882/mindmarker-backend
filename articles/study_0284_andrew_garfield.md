# Andrew Garfield

**Andrew Russell Garfield** (born 20 August 1983) is an English and American actor. He is known for his work across a variety of genres, from biographical dramas to action films, spanning both [independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film "Independent film") and [blockbuster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blockbuster_(entertainment) "Blockbuster (entertainment)") productions. [His accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Andrew_Garfield "List of awards and nominations received by Andrew Garfield") include a [Tony Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award "Tony Award"), two [Critics' Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Awards "Critics' Choice Awards"), a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award") and a [British Academy Television Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Awards "British Academy Television Awards"), in addition to nominations for two [Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards "Academy Awards"), three [British Academy Film Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Film_Awards "British Academy Film Awards") and a [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award"). In 2022, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine named him one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100").

Garfield began his career on [West End stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre "West End theatre") and British television. His [breakout roles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_role "Breakthrough role") came as a juvenile convict in the television film *[Boy A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_A_(film) "Boy A (film)")* (2007), followed by [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher "David Fincher")'s biographical drama *[The Social Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Social_Network "The Social Network")* (2010) and as a dim young man caught in a love triangle in [Mark Romanek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Romanek "Mark Romanek")'s dystopian romance *[Never Let Me Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Never_Let_Me_Go_(2010_film) "Never Let Me Go (2010 film)")* (2010). Garfield achieved wider recognition for starring as [the title character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_(The_Amazing_Spider-Man_film_series) "Peter Parker (The Amazing Spider-Man film series)") in [Marc Webb's *Spider-Man* films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_in_film#Marc_Webb_films "Spider-Man in film") (2012–2014) and the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") film *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home "Spider-Man: No Way Home")* (2021).

Garfield received Academy Award nominations for [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor") for his performances as [Desmond Doss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Doss "Desmond Doss") in the war film *[Hacksaw Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacksaw_Ridge "Hacksaw Ridge")* (2016) and [Jonathan Larson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Larson "Jonathan Larson") in the musical drama *[Tick, Tick... Boom!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tick,_Tick..._Boom!_(film) "Tick, Tick... Boom! (film)")* (2021). His other film credits include *[Silence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silence_(2016_film) "Silence (2016 film)")* (2016), *[Under the Silver Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Silver_Lake "Under the Silver Lake")* (2018), *[The Eyes of Tammy Faye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Eyes_of_Tammy_Faye_(2021_film) "The Eyes of Tammy Faye (2021 film)")* (2021) and *[We Live in Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Live_in_Time "We Live in Time")* (2024). On television, he starred in the crime drama miniseries *[Under the Banner of Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Banner_of_Heaven_(miniseries) "Under the Banner of Heaven (miniseries)")* (2022), which earned him a nomination for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie").

On stage, Garfield made his [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_theatre "Broadway theatre") debut as [Biff Loman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biff_Loman "Biff Loman") in the revival of *[Death of a Salesman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_a_Salesman "Death of a Salesman")* (2012), which earned him a nomination for the [Tony Award for Best Featured Actor in a Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Featured_Actor_in_a_Play "Tony Award for Best Featured Actor in a Play"). For his performance as Prior Walter in the [National Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_National_Theatre "Royal National Theatre") revival of *[Angels in America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_in_America "Angels in America")* on Broadway, he received a nomination for the [Laurence Olivier Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actor "Laurence Olivier Award for Best Actor") and later won the [Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Play "Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play").

## Early life and education

Andrew Russell Garfield was born on 20 August 1983 in Los Angeles, California. His mother, Lynn (née Hillman), was from [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex "Essex"), England, and his father, Richard Garfield, is from California. Richard's parents were also English. Garfield's parents moved the family from the United States to England when he was three years old, and he was brought up in [Epsom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epsom "Epsom"), Surrey.

He is Jewish on his father's side, and describes himself as a "Jewish artist". His paternal grandparents were from Jewish immigrant families who moved to London from Poland, Russia, and Romania, and the family surname was originally "Garfinkel". In April 2025, Garfield participated in the British TV show *[Who Do You Think You Are?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who_Do_You_Think_You_Are%3F_(British_TV_series) "Who Do You Think You Are? (British TV series)")* He uncovered his Jewish roots in [Kielce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kielce "Kielce"), Poland, and also found that a great-grandaunt, Ruchla Garfinkiel, married the first cousin of [Władysław Szpilman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W%C5%82adys%C5%82aw_Szpilman "Władysław Szpilman"), the subject of the 2002 film *[The Pianist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pianist_(2002_film) "The Pianist (2002 film)")*.

Garfield's parents ran a small interior-design business. His mother was also a teaching assistant at a nursery school, and his father became head coach of the [Guildford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guildford "Guildford") City Swimming Club. He has an older brother who is a [National Health Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Service_(England) "National Health Service (England)") (NHS) doctor at [Royal Brompton Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Brompton_Hospital "Royal Brompton Hospital"). Garfield was a gymnast and a swimmer during his early years. He originally intended to study business, but became interested in acting at age sixteen when a friend convinced him to take theatre studies at [A-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-level "A-level"), as they were one pupil short of being able to run the class. Garfield was educated at [Priory Preparatory School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priory_Preparatory_School "Priory Preparatory School") in [Banstead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banstead "Banstead") and later [City of London Freemen's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_London_Freemen%27s_School "City of London Freemen's School") in [Ashtead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtead "Ashtead"), before training at the [Central School of Speech and Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_School_of_Speech_and_Drama "Central School of Speech and Drama"), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London "University of London"). His first job was at [Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks "Starbucks"), being moved between three separate establishments in [Golders Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golders_Green "Golders Green") and [Hendon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendon "Hendon").

## Career

### Early work and breakthrough (2004–2011)

Garfield began taking acting classes in Guildford, Surrey, when he was nine, and appeared in a youth theatre production of *[Bugsy Malone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugsy_Malone "Bugsy Malone")*. He also joined a small youth theatre workshop group in Epsom and took theatre studies at A-level before studying for a further three years at a UK conservatoire, the Central School of Speech and Drama. Upon graduating in 2004, he began working primarily in stage acting. In 2004, he won a *[Manchester Evening News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Evening_News "Manchester Evening News")* Theatre Award for Best Newcomer for his performance in *Kes* at [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester "Manchester")'s [Royal Exchange Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Exchange_Theatre "Royal Exchange Theatre") (where he also played [Romeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeo "Romeo") the year after), and won the Outstanding Newcomer Award at the 2006 [*Evening Standard* Theatre Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evening_Standard_Theatre_Awards#Outstanding_Newcomer "Evening Standard Theatre Awards"). Garfield made his British television debut in 2005 appearing in the [Channel 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_4 "Channel 4") teen drama *[Sugar Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_Rush_(UK_TV_series) "Sugar Rush (UK TV series)")*. In 2007, he garnered public attention when he appeared in the [series three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Who_(series_3) "Doctor Who (series 3)") of the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC")'s *[Doctor Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Who "Doctor Who")*, in the episodes "[Daleks in Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daleks_in_Manhattan "Daleks in Manhattan")" and "[Evolution of the Daleks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution_of_the_Daleks "Evolution of the Daleks")". Garfield commented that it was "an honour" to be a part of *Doctor Who*. In October 2007, he was named one of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")*'s "10 Actors to Watch". He made his American film debut in November 2007, playing an American university student in the ensemble drama *[Lions for Lambs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lions_for_Lambs "Lions for Lambs")*, with co-stars [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise "Tom Cruise"), [Meryl Streep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meryl_Streep "Meryl Streep"), and [Robert Redford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Redford "Robert Redford"). "I'm just lucky to be there working on the same project as them, although I don't really expect to be recognized later by audiences", Garfield told *Variety* in 2007. In his review for *[The Boston Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boston_Globe "The Boston Globe")*, Wesley Morris considered Garfield's work "a willing punching bag for the movie's jabs and low blows".

In the Channel 4 drama *[Boy A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_A_(film) "Boy A (film)")*, released in November 2007, he portrayed a notorious killer trying to find new life after prison. The role garnered him the 2008 BAFTA Award for Best Actor. Amy Biancolli of the *[Houston Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Chronicle "Houston Chronicle")* wrote, "there is no doubt about the intelligence and sensitivity" of Garfield's portrayal. [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis "Minneapolis") *[Star Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Tribune "Star Tribune")*'s Christy DeSmith echoed Biancolli's sentiment, citing his "detailed expressions" as an example. Writing in *[The Seattle Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Seattle_Times "The Seattle Times")*, John Hartl noted that Garfield demonstrated range in the role, and concluded: "Garfield always manages to capture his passion". [Joe Morgenstern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Morgenstern "Joe Morgenstern"), the critic for *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal "The Wall Street Journal")*, dubbed Garfield's performance "phenomenal", assessing that he "makes room for the many and various pieces of Jack's personality". In 2008, he had a minor role in the film *[The Other Boleyn Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Other_Boleyn_Girl_(2008_film) "The Other Boleyn Girl (2008 film)")*, and was named one of the [Shooting Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shooting_Stars_Award "Shooting Stars Award") at the [Berlin International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_International_Film_Festival "Berlin International Film Festival"). In 2009, Garfield held supporting roles in the [Terry Gilliam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Gilliam "Terry Gilliam") film *[The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Imaginarium_of_Doctor_Parnassus "The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus")* and the *[Red Riding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Riding "Red Riding")* television trilogy. [Kenneth Turan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Turan "Kenneth Turan") of the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* thought that Garfield gave a stand out performance in the latter.

In 2010, Garfield co-starred opposite [Carey Mulligan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carey_Mulligan "Carey Mulligan") and [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley "Keira Knightley") in [Mark Romanek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Romanek "Mark Romanek")'s [dystopian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utopian_and_dystopian_fiction "Utopian and dystopian fiction") [science-fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_film "Science fiction film") drama *[Never Let Me Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Never_Let_Me_Go_(2010_film) "Never Let Me Go (2010 film)")*, an adaptation of [Kazuo Ishiguro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazuo_Ishiguro "Kazuo Ishiguro")'s 2005 [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Never_Let_Me_Go_(novel) "Never Let Me Go (novel)"). He said of his character, Tommy D., "There's a sense of anxiety that runs through these kids, especially Tommy, because he's so sensory and feeling and animalistic, that's my perspective of him." Garfield was attracted to the film based on the existential questions the story expresses. He said the experience of being a part of *Never Let Me Go* was "just a dream to come true". He further remarked that the scenes in which his character—unable to contain his frustration—erupts with a wail, were "intense" for him. "I think those screams are inside all of us, I just got a chance to let mine out". For his portrayal of a well-meaning, but dim young man caught in a love triangle, he won the 2010 [Saturn Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Saturn Award for Best Supporting Actor"). Writing for *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")*, [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman") praised the performances of the lead cast, reflecting that "these three all act with a spooky, haunted innocence that gets under your skin." In comparison to Mulligan and Knightley, Scott Bowles, writing for *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today "USA Today")*, deemed Garfield "the real find" of *Never Let Me Go*.

The same year, Garfield co-starred opposite [Jesse Eisenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Eisenberg "Jesse Eisenberg") in *The Social Network*, a drama based on the founders of Facebook. On his character, Garfield remarked, "No one knows who [Eduardo Saverin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Saverin "Eduardo Saverin") is, and I don't either. Of course, the fact he's a real-life human being, breathing on this Earth somewhere, creates a whole new dimension to my approach because you feel a greater sense of responsibility". Initially, the film's director, [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher "David Fincher"), had met Garfield under the auspices of him playing [Mark Zuckerberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg "Mark Zuckerberg"), having been referred to him by Mark Romanek. However, Fincher did not like Garfield for the part as he found Garfield's "incredible emotional access to his kind of core humanity" better tailored for the role of Saverin. Garfield's performance was very well received; he earned wider recognition and numerous nominations, including [BAFTA nominations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA "BAFTA") for Best Actor in a Supporting Role and [Rising Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rising_Star_Award,_BAFTA "Rising Star Award, BAFTA"), as well as a [Golden Globe nomination for Best Performance in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture"). [Mark Kermode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Kermode "Mark Kermode") of the BBC expressed his surprise that Garfield had been overlooked for an Academy Award nomination, opining that "everyone knows he's one of the very best things about *The Social Network*." Writing in *The Wall Street Journal*, Joe Morgenstern thought the role was portrayed with "great subtlety and rueful charm". *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* said Garfield delivered "a vulnerability that raises the emotional stakes in a movie", and proclaimed: "Keep your eyes on Garfield – he's shatteringly good, the soul of a film that might otherwise be without one."

### *The Amazing Spider-Man* and worldwide recognition (2012–2016)

Garfield was cast as [Spider-Man/Peter Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_(The_Amazing_Spider-Man_film_series) "Peter Parker (The Amazing Spider-Man film series)"), opposite [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone "Emma Stone") as his love interest [Gwen Stacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwen_Stacy "Gwen Stacy"), in [Marc Webb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Webb "Marc Webb")'s *[The Amazing Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Amazing_Spider-Man_(2012_film) "The Amazing Spider-Man (2012 film)")* (2012), a [reboot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reboot_(fiction) "Reboot (fiction)") of the [*Spider-Man* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_in_film "Spider-Man in film"). Garfield saw his casting as a "massive challenge in many ways", having to make the character "authentic" and "live and breathe in a new way". He described Peter as someone he could relate to and stated that the character had been an important influence on him since he was a child. For the role, he studied movements of athletes and spiders, and tried to incorporate them, and practiced [yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoga "Yoga") and [pilates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilates "Pilates"). *The Amazing Spider-Man* earned a worldwide total of $752,216,557, and Garfield's performance was generally well received. *The Guardian*'s [Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw "Peter Bradshaw") labelled his portrayal as the "definitive Spider-Man" and Tom Charity of [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN") commended his "combination of fresh-faced innocence, nervous agitation and wry humor".

In March 2012, Garfield made his Broadway theatre debut as Biff Loman in the revival of *Death of a Salesman*. According to *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")*'s David Rooney, Garfield had successfully "exposed the raw ache of Biff's solitude". Garfield was nominated for a Tony Award for Best Featured Actor in a Play for his performance. Two years later, Garfield hosted an episode of *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* and appeared in a music video for the song "[We Exist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Exist "We Exist")" by [Arcade Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_Fire "Arcade Fire"), playing a trans woman. Also in 2014, he co-produced and starred in the 2014 independent drama *[99 Homes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/99_Homes "99 Homes")* and reprised the titular role in *[The Amazing Spider-Man 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Amazing_Spider-Man_2 "The Amazing Spider-Man 2")*. Following a deal between Sony and [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios "Marvel Studios") to integrate the Spider-Man character into the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe"), sequels to the latter film were scrapped, and the role was taken on by [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland "Tom Holland") in a reboot. Arachnologists [Yuri M. Marusik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_M._Marusik "Yuri M. Marusik") and [Alireza Zamani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alireza_Zamani "Alireza Zamani") honored Garfield's portrayal of the role by naming a new species of [crevice weaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crevice_weaver "Crevice weaver") spider, *[Pritha garfieldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pritha_garfieldi "Pritha garfieldi")*, after him.

Following a year-long absence from the screen, Garfield had starring roles in two films of 2016, [Martin Scorsese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Scorsese "Martin Scorsese")'s drama *[Silence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silence_(2016_film) "Silence (2016 film)")* and [Mel Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Gibson "Mel Gibson")'s war film *[Hacksaw Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacksaw_Ridge "Hacksaw Ridge")*. In the former, based on [Shūsaku Endō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%ABsaku_End%C5%8D "Shūsaku Endō")'s 1966 [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silence_(End%C5%8D_novel) "Silence (Endō novel)"), Garfield played Sebastião Rodrigues, a Portuguese Jesuit priest in the seventeenth century who travels to Japan to spread his faith. Garfield spent a year with [James Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Martin_(priest,_born_1960) "James Martin (priest, born 1960)") studying to be a Jesuit priest and went on a silent retreat in Wales. The film's arduous principal photography took place in Taiwan, and Garfield lost 40 pounds (18 kg) to achieve his character's physicality. Kate Taylor of *[The Globe and Mail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Globe_and_Mail "The Globe and Mail")* disliked the film and wrote that Garfield "is sweetly resolute and gently anguished as the missionary Rodrigues but any hope that the actor might elucidate the psychology of philosophical certitude or the pain of religious doubt proves vain". At the box office, it earned less than half of its $50 million budget. *[Hacksaw Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacksaw_Ridge "Hacksaw Ridge")*, however, was a commercial success, earning over $175.3 million worldwide. In it, Garfield portrayed [Desmond Doss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Doss "Desmond Doss"), a combat medic during World War II, who was the first [conscientious objector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscientious_objector "Conscientious objector") in American history to be awarded the [Medal of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_Honor "Medal of Honor"). Writing for *USA Today*, Brian Truitt labelled the film as "brutally intense and elegantly crafted"; he believed that the central role allowed Garfield to bring depth to his career and commended him for portraying Doss with both "simple sweetness" and "steadfast mettle". He received a nomination for the [Academy Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor") for *Hacksaw Ridge*.

### Established career (2017–present)

Garfield played the role of Prior Walter in [Tony Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Kushner "Tony Kushner")'s two-part play *[Angels in America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_in_America "Angels in America")* at the Lyttelton Theatre in the [National Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_National_Theatre "Royal National Theatre"), London from April to August 2017, and the performance was broadcast live to cinemas around the world in summer 2017 through the [National Theatre Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Theatre_Live "National Theatre Live") series. It was directed by [Marianne Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marianne_Elliott "Marianne Elliott") and co-starred [Nathan Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Lane "Nathan Lane"), [James McArdle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_McArdle "James McArdle"), [Russell Tovey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Tovey "Russell Tovey"), and [Denise Gough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denise_Gough "Denise Gough"). Paul T Davis of The British Theatre Guide wrote that Garfield was "transformative and unrecognisable in places, completely inhabiting camp, laconic, frightened and totally loveable Prior Walter". He was nominated for the [Laurence Olivier Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actor "Laurence Olivier Award for Best Actor").

Garfield's sole film release of 2017 was the biopic *[Breathe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breathe_(2017_film) "Breathe (2017 film)")*, in which he portrayed [Robin Cavendish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Cavendish "Robin Cavendish"), a young man paralysed by [polio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polio "Polio"). In preparation, he interacted with individuals who had polio and collaborated closely with Cavendish's wife and son. Stephen Dalton of *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* wrote that despite an exceptional story, the film had glossed over the complexities in Cavendish's life, and thought that Garfield was "hampered by a role that restricts him to little more than nodding and grinning". In March 2018, Garfield reprised the role of Prior when the *Angels in America* production transferred to Broadway for an eighteen-week limited engagement at the [Neil Simon Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Simon_Theatre "Neil Simon Theatre"), alongside a majority of the London cast. Reviewing the production for *[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post "The Washington Post")*, [Peter Marks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Marks_(journalist) "Peter Marks (journalist)") remarked that "nothing [Garfield's] done prepares you for the star-powered dexterity of his Prior" and considered his performance to be the "persuasive moral core of the piece". He won the [Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Play "Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play") for his performance.

The [2018 Cannes Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Cannes_Film_Festival "2018 Cannes Film Festival") marked the premiere of Garfield's next film, the [David Robert Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Robert_Mitchell "David Robert Mitchell")-directed neo-noir *[Under the Silver Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Silver_Lake "Under the Silver Lake")*. In it, he played Sam, an unemployed and wayward young man who sets out on a journey to find his neighbour who has mysteriously disappeared. Writing for *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")*, Richard Lawson found Garfield to be "great in the role, doing nimble, subtle bits of physical comedy and teasing out the creepy, menacing side of Sam". Garfield starred in [Gia Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gia_Coppola "Gia Coppola")'s drama *[Mainstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainstream_(film) "Mainstream (film)")*, alongside [Maya Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Hawke "Maya Hawke") and [Jason Schwartzman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Schwartzman "Jason Schwartzman"), which had its world premiere at the [2020 Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Venice_Film_Festival "2020 Venice Film Festival").

In 2021, Garfield starred in *[The Eyes of Tammy Faye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Eyes_of_Tammy_Faye_(2021_film) "The Eyes of Tammy Faye (2021 film)")* opposite [Jessica Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Chastain "Jessica Chastain"), a drama about the televangelists [Tammy Faye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammy_Faye "Tammy Faye") and [Jim Bakker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Bakker "Jim Bakker"), which had its world premiere at the [2021 Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Toronto_International_Film_Festival "2021 Toronto International Film Festival"). That same year, Garfield portrayed composer [Jonathan Larson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Larson "Jonathan Larson") in [Lin-Manuel Miranda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lin-Manuel_Miranda "Lin-Manuel Miranda")'s [film adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tick,_Tick..._Boom!_(film) "Tick, Tick... Boom! (film)") of *[Tick, Tick... Boom!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tick,_Tick..._Boom! "Tick, Tick... Boom!")*. Miranda had first seen Garfield performing on stage in *Angels in America*. Garfield, who had not professionally sung before, underwent vocal training in preparation for the role. The film was released on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). For his performance, Garfield received the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy"), and a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Actor. Despite issuing repeated public denials to the contrary, Garfield reprised his role as Spider-Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe film *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home "Spider-Man: No Way Home")*, starring alongside his Spider-Man successor Tom Holland and predecessor [Tobey Maguire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobey_Maguire "Tobey Maguire"). Garfield described his experience working on the film as "joyful", and said that it gave him "closure" with his version of the Spider-Man character. He also said that he would be open to reprising the role in future if it felt right.

In 2022, Garfield was included on *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine's [annual list of 100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100"). That same year, he starred in [Dustin Lance Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin_Lance_Black "Dustin Lance Black")'s miniseries *[Under the Banner of Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Banner_of_Heaven_(TV_series) "Under the Banner of Heaven (TV series)")*, an adaptation of [Jon Krakauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Krakauer "Jon Krakauer")'s [book of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Banner_of_Heaven "Under the Banner of Heaven"). Reviewing the miniseries, *[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(website) "Vulture (website)")*'s Kathryn VanArendonk highlighted Garfield's "almost flagrantly tender portrayal" of Jeb Pyre, a [Mormon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mormon "Mormon") detective. His performance earned him a [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award") nomination for [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie"). In 2024, Garfield starred opposite [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh "Florence Pugh") in [John Crowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Crowley_(director) "John Crowley (director)")'s romance *[We Live in Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Live_in_Time "We Live in Time")*. The next year he starred with [Julia Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Roberts "Julia Roberts") in [Luca Guadagnino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Guadagnino "Luca Guadagnino")'s thriller *[After the Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After_the_Hunt "After the Hunt")*. In 2026, he starred in *[The Magic Faraway Tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Magic_Faraway_Tree_(film) "The Magic Faraway Tree (film)")*, a family film based on [Enid Blyton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enid_Blyton "Enid Blyton")'s [book series of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Faraway_Tree "The Faraway Tree"). In May 2025, he was cast to play Roy Horn of [Siegfried & Roy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegfried_%26_Roy "Siegfried & Roy") in the [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") miniseries *[Wild Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Things_(upcoming_TV_series) "Wild Things (upcoming TV series)")*. He will reunite with Guadagnino for *[Artificial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_(2026_film) "Artificial (2026 film)")*, portraying [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI "OpenAI") CEO [Sam Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Altman "Sam Altman").

## Philanthropy

In 2011, Garfield became the ambassador of sport for the Worldwide Orphans Foundation. He has traveled to countries like [Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haiti "Haiti") and [Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopia "Ethiopia") to work directly with the charity's programs, which focus on medical, developmental, and sports-based integration. He has participated in mental health initiatives, such as the ⁠Child Mind Institute's campaign, which provides resources and support for families. During the peak of the [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19 "COVID-19") pandemic. Garfield has publicly supported and raised awareness for SurvivorsUK, an organization dedicated to providing support for male victims and survivors of rape or sexual abuse.

Garfield has publicly expressed support for [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine "Palestine") and the lives of Palestinians, urging people to focus on those suffering in the [Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Strip "Gaza Strip"). In a conversation on the crisis, he stated "Maybe the lives of Palestinians in Gaza right now is where our hearts and energy should be directed". In October 2023, Garfield was a signatory in an open letter by [Artists4Ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artists4Ceasefire "Artists4Ceasefire") to President [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden"), calling for a ceasefire in the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war"). Garfield has often criticised the [capitalist system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalism "Capitalism") and [consumerism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumerism "Consumerism").

## Media image and reception

Having studied at the [RCSSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Central_School_of_Speech_and_Drama "Royal Central School of Speech and Drama"), Garfield builds his performances on script analysis and a technical understanding of the character. Garfield is considered one of the most versatile and emotionally committed actors of his generation. He is celebrated for his intense dedication to method acting.

*[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* included him in its "Men of the Year" list in both 2024 In 2023, *GQ* and *Glamour* named him the "Best Dressed Man" of 2010, with the former praising his sophisticated, retro-inspired tailoring and daring color choices. In 2013, Forbes featured Garfield in the "Top Grossing Actors" list, citing his $752 million. In 2022, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* named him one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100").

## Personal life

Garfield has referred to himself as an "agnostic [pantheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantheist "Pantheist")", although he identifies as Jewish. Having completed the *[Spiritual Exercises of Ignatius of Loyola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritual_Exercises_of_Ignatius_of_Loyola "Spiritual Exercises of Ignatius of Loyola")* in preparation for playing a [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit "Jesuit") in *Silence*, he said that "what was really easy was falling in love with this person, was falling in love with [Jesus Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Christ "Jesus Christ"). That was the most surprising thing."

Garfield holds [dual citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_citizenship "Dual citizenship") in the United States and the United Kingdom. In 2009, he told the *[Sunday Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunday_Herald "Sunday Herald")* that he felt "equally at home" in both countries and enjoyed "a varied cultural existence". When asked again in 2019, he stated, "I identify more as Jewish than anything... I have a love-hate relationship with both countries and used to be very proud to have both passports. Today, I'm slightly less proud." Garfield's primary place of residence is in [North London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_London "North London") near [Hampstead Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampstead_Heath "Hampstead Heath"). He told [Shaun Keaveny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaun_Keaveny "Shaun Keaveny") on a podcast in 2021 that he considers England home as that is where his family and friends are. He is a fan of basketball, having attended [New York Knicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Knicks "New York Knicks") games at [Madison Square Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Square_Garden "Madison Square Garden").

Garfield customarily gives interviews about his work but does not publicly discuss details of his private life. In 2011, Garfield began dating his *[The Amazing Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Amazing_Spider-Man_(film) "The Amazing Spider-Man (film)")* co-star [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone "Emma Stone") sometime during production of the film. In 2015, they were rumoured to have broken up although no formal statement was released. When asked about his sexuality, Garfield identified himself as heterosexual, but has stated, "I have an openness to any impulses that may arise within me at any time." Since early 2025, Garfield has been in a relationship with actress [Monica Barbaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Barbaro "Monica Barbaro").

Garfield's mother, Lynn, died of [pancreatic cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pancreatic_cancer "Pancreatic cancer") while he was filming *[The Eyes of Tammy Faye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Eyes_of_Tammy_Faye_(2021_film) "The Eyes of Tammy Faye (2021 film)")* and shortly before *[Tick, Tick... Boom!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tick,_Tick..._Boom!_(film) "Tick, Tick... Boom! (film)")* began production. He was able to fly home to be with her. In 2021 he spoke about his [grief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grief "Grief") on *[The Late Show with Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Show_with_Stephen_Colbert "The Late Show with Stephen Colbert")*. He discussed it again in 2024 in an interview with [Anderson Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anderson_Cooper "Anderson Cooper") on [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN"), as well as with [Elmo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmo "Elmo") in an episode of *[Sesame Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame_Street "Sesame Street")*.
