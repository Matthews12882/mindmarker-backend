# Neatsville, Kentucky

**Neatsville** is an [unincorporated community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unincorporated_area "Unincorporated area") in [Adair County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adair_County,_Kentucky "Adair County, Kentucky"), located in the [U.S. state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._state "U.S. state") of [Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky "Kentucky"). It is located at the junction of [Kentucky Route 206](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Route_206 "Kentucky Route 206") and [Kentucky Route 76](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Route_76 "Kentucky Route 76"). Its elevation is 705 feet (215 m). For unknown reasons, the town's name was spelled as **Neetsville** from 1876 until 1886, when the town's post office closed. In its early history from around the 1810s to 1900, Neatsville progressively grew to become a well-established, incorporated town. It has been relocated twice through the years, once due to flooding c. 1900 – c. 1902, which decimated the town, and once in the 1960s when the Green River was impounded to make way for the [Green River Reservoir State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_River_Lake_State_Park "Green River Lake State Park").

## Geography

Located in the north-east of Adair County, nearby communities are [Eunice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eunice,_Kentucky "Eunice, Kentucky") in the south, [Pellyton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pellyton,_Kentucky "Pellyton, Kentucky") in the north-east and [Dunbar Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dunbar_Hill,_kentucky&action=edit&redlink=1 "Dunbar Hill, kentucky (page does not exist)") in the north west.

## History

Various sources and accounts have referred to Neatsville as a [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village "Village"), a [postal village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_village "Postal village"), a [hamlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_(place) "Hamlet (place)"), or as a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town "Town") at throughout its history.

The Neat family settled the community in the early 1800s, with Rudolf Neat the first to acquire land there. As it expanded, the community grew to encompass several stores, a hotel, a doctor's office, mills, a sawmill, distilleries, a saloon, a salt works, a [cooper shop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_(profession) "Cooper (profession)"), a [carding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carding "Carding") machine, and a [Masonic Lodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freemasonry "Freemasonry").

It was incorporated as a town on February 23, 1847. Its post office was established on March 13, 1844, and closed in 1886. In 1848 the town's population was estimated to be around 50, and in 1876 the town's population was estimated at 60. The Masonic Lodge was relocated to [Pellyton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pellyton,_Kentucky "Pellyton, Kentucky") in 1917.

### Relocations

Between 1901 and 1902, a major flood devastated the town, forcing its relocation from the north bank to the south bank of the [Green River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_River_(Kentucky) "Green River (Kentucky)"). At that time, the former location was abandoned. Erosion had occurred in the foundations of the buildings in the former location due to the flooding. A 1916 local account of the town's former site after the flooding characterized it as "nearly obliterated" and "in ruins".

Neatsville was later relocated to its present location sometime in the 1960s, when the Green River was [impounded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dam "Dam") to enable the creation of the [Green River Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_River_Lake "Green River Lake").
