# Christian Bale

**Christian Charles Philip Bale** (born 30 January 1974) is an English actor. Known for his versatility and physical transformations for his roles, he has been a [leading man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_man "Leading man") in films of several genres. [His accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Christian_Bale "List of awards and nominations received by Christian Bale") include an [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award") and two [Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards"), in addition to nominations for four [BAFTAs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTAs "BAFTAs"). He was one of the [highest-paid actors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-paid_film_actors "List of highest-paid film actors") in 2014, and *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")* later named him one of the greatest actors of the 21st century.

Bale had his [breakthrough role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_role "Breakthrough role") at age 13 in [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg")'s 1987 war film *[Empire of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_the_Sun_(film) "Empire of the Sun (film)")*. After more than a decade of leading and supporting roles in films, he gained wider recognition for his portrayals of the serial killer [Patrick Bateman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Bateman "Patrick Bateman") in the black comedy *[American Psycho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psycho_(film) "American Psycho (film)")* (2000) and the title role in the thriller *[The Machinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Machinist "The Machinist")* (2004). He played the superhero [Batman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Wayne_(Dark_Knight_trilogy) "Bruce Wayne (Dark Knight trilogy)") in [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan "Christopher Nolan")'s [*The Dark Knight* trilogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_in_film#The_Dark_Knight_trilogy "Batman in film") (2005–2012), one of the highest-grossing film franchises.

Outside his work as Batman, Bale had starring roles in a range of films. For his portrayal of the boxer [Dicky Eklund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dicky_Eklund "Dicky Eklund") in [David O. Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_O._Russell "David O. Russell")'s biographical film *[The Fighter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fighter "The Fighter")* (2010), he won the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor"). Further Academy Award nominations came for his work in Russell's black comedy *[American Hustle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Hustle "American Hustle")* (2013) and [Adam McKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_McKay "Adam McKay")'s biographical satires *[The Big Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Short_(film) "The Big Short (film)")* (2015) and *[Vice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_(2018_film) "Vice (2018 film)")* (2018). For portraying the American vice president [Dick Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Cheney "Dick Cheney") in the latter, he won a second Golden Globe. Bale has since played [Ken Miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Miles "Ken Miles") in the sports drama *[Ford v Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_v_Ferrari "Ford v Ferrari")* (2019) and [Gorr the God Butcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorr_the_God_Butcher "Gorr the God Butcher") in the superhero film *[Thor: Love and Thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thor:_Love_and_Thunder "Thor: Love and Thunder")* (2022).

## Early life and education

Christian Charles Philip Bale was born on 30 January 1974 in [Haverfordwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverfordwest "Haverfordwest"), Pembrokeshire, Wales, to English parents—Jenny James, a circus performer, and [David Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bale "David Bale"), an entrepreneur and activist. Bale has remarked, "I was born in Wales but I'm not Welsh—I'm English". He has two elder sisters, Sharon and Louise, and a half-sister from his father's first marriage, Erin. One of his grandfathers was a comedian while the other was a stand-in for [John Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wayne "John Wayne"). Bale and his family left Wales when he was two years old, and after living in Portugal and [Oxfordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxfordshire "Oxfordshire"), England, they settled in [Bournemouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bournemouth "Bournemouth"), England. As well as saying that the family had lived in 15 towns by the time he was 15, Bale described the frequent relocation as being driven by "necessity rather than choice" and acknowledged that it had a major influence on his career selection.

He attended [Bournemouth School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bournemouth_School "Bournemouth School") until the age of 16. Bale's parents divorced in 1991, and at age 17, he moved with his sister Louise and their father to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), California, in the United States.

Bale trained in [ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet "Ballet") as a child. His first acting role came at eight years old in a commercial for the fabric softener [Lenor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenor "Lenor"). He also appeared in a *[Pac-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac-Man "Pac-Man")* cereal commercial. After his sister was cast in a [West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre "West End theatre") musical, Bale considered taking up acting professionally. He said later he did not find acting appealing but pursued it at the request of those around him because he had no reason not to do so. After participating in school plays, Bale performed opposite [Rowan Atkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowan_Atkinson "Rowan Atkinson") in the play *[The Nerd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nerd_(play) "The Nerd (play)")* in the West End in 1984. He did not undergo any formal acting training.

## Career

### Early roles and breakthrough (1986–1999)

After deciding to become an actor at age ten, Bale secured a minor role in the 1986 television film *[Anastasia: The Mystery of Anna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia:_The_Mystery_of_Anna "Anastasia: The Mystery of Anna")*. Its star, [Amy Irving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Irving "Amy Irving"), who was married to the director [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg"), subsequently recommended Bale for Spielberg's 1987 film *[Empire of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_the_Sun_(film) "Empire of the Sun (film)")*. At age 13, Bale was chosen from over 4,000 actors to portray a British boy in a [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War "Second World War") Japanese internment camp. For the film, he spoke with an upper-class cadence without the help of a dialogue coach. The role propelled Bale to fame, and his work earned him acclaim and the inaugural Best Performance by a Juvenile Actor Award from the [National Board of Review of Motion Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Board_of_Review_of_Motion_Pictures "National Board of Review of Motion Pictures"). Earlier in the same year, he starred in the fantasy film *[Mio in the Land of Faraway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mio_in_the_Land_of_Faraway "Mio in the Land of Faraway")*, based on the novel *[Mio, My Son](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mio,_My_Son "Mio, My Son")* by [Astrid Lindgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrid_Lindgren "Astrid Lindgren"). The fame from *Empire of the Sun* led to Bale being bullied in school and finding the pressures of working as an actor unbearable. He grew distrustful of the acting profession because of media attention but said that he felt obligated at a young age to continue to act for financial reasons. Around this time, the actor and filmmaker [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh "Kenneth Branagh") persuaded Bale to appear in his film *[Henry V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_V_(1989_film) "Henry V (1989 film)")* in 1989, which drew him back into acting. The following year, Bale played [Jim Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Hawkins_(character) "Jim Hawkins (character)") opposite [Charlton Heston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlton_Heston "Charlton Heston") as [Long John Silver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_John_Silver "Long John Silver") in *[Treasure Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_Island_(1990_film) "Treasure Island (1990 film)")*, a television film adaptation of [Robert Louis Stevenson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Louis_Stevenson "Robert Louis Stevenson")'s [1883 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_Island "Treasure Island").

Bale starred in the 1992 [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney "Disney") musical film *[Newsies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsies "Newsies")*, which was unsuccessful both at the box office and with critics. Rebecca Milzoff of *[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(magazine) "New York (magazine)")* revisited the film in 2012 and found the cracks in Bale's voice during his performance of the song "Santa Fe" charming and apt even though he was not a great singer. In 1993 he appeared in *[Swing Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swing_Kids_(1993_film) "Swing Kids (1993 film)")*, a film about teenagers who secretly listen to forbidden [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz") during the rise of [Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany "Nazi Germany"). In [Gillian Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillian_Armstrong "Gillian Armstrong")'s 1994 film *[Little Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Women_(1994_film) "Little Women (1994 film)")*, Bale played [Theodore "Laurie" Laurence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_%22Laurie%22_Laurence "Theodore \"Laurie\" Laurence") following a recommendation from [Winona Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winona_Ryder "Winona Ryder"), who starred as [Jo March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo_March "Jo March"). The film achieved critical and commercial success. Of Bale's performance, Ryder said he captured the complex nature of the role. He next voiced Thomas, a young compatriot of Captain [John Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Smith_(explorer) "John Smith (explorer)"), in the 1995 Disney animated film *[Pocahontas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocahontas_(1995_film) "Pocahontas (1995 film)")*, which attracted a mixed critical reception. Bale played a small part in the 1996 film *[The Portrait of a Lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Portrait_of_a_Lady_(film) "The Portrait of a Lady (film)")*, based on the [Henry James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_James "Henry James") [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Portrait_of_a_Lady "The Portrait of a Lady"), and appeared in the 1998 musical film *[Velvet Goldmine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velvet_Goldmine "Velvet Goldmine")*, set in the 1970s during the [glam rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glam_rock "Glam rock") era. In 1999 he was part of an [ensemble cast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ensemble_cast "Ensemble cast"), which included [Kevin Kline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Kline "Kevin Kline") and [Michelle Pfeiffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Pfeiffer "Michelle Pfeiffer"), portraying [Demetrius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demetrius_(A_Midsummer_Night%27s_Dream) "Demetrius (A Midsummer Night's Dream)") in *[A Midsummer Night's Dream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Midsummer_Night%27s_Dream_(1999_film) "A Midsummer Night's Dream (1999 film)")*, a film adaptation of [the play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Midsummer_Night%27s_Dream "A Midsummer Night's Dream") by [William Shakespeare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare "William Shakespeare").

### Rise to prominence and commercial decline (2000–2004)

Bale played [Patrick Bateman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Bateman "Patrick Bateman"), an investment banker and serial killer, in *[American Psycho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psycho_(film) "American Psycho (film)")*, a film adaptation of [Bret Easton Ellis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bret_Easton_Ellis "Bret Easton Ellis")'s [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psycho "American Psycho"), directed by [Mary Harron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Harron "Mary Harron"). While Harron had chosen Bale for the part, the film's production and distribution company, [Lionsgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate_Films "Lionsgate Films"), originally disagreed and hired [Leonardo DiCaprio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_DiCaprio "Leonardo DiCaprio") to play Bateman with [Oliver Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Stone "Oliver Stone") to direct. Bale and Harron were brought back after DiCaprio and Stone had left the project. Bale exercised and tanned himself for months to achieve Bateman's chiseled physique and had his teeth capped to assimilate to the character's narcissistic nature. *American Psycho* premiered at the 2000 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival"). Harron said the film critic [Roger Ebert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert "Roger Ebert") named it the most hated film at the event. Of Bale's work, Ebert wrote he "is heroic in the way he allows the character to leap joyfully into despicability; there is no instinct for self-preservation here, and that is one mark of a good actor." The film was released in April 2000, becoming a commercial and critical success and later developing a [cult following](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cult_following "Cult following"); the role established Bale as a [leading man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_man "Leading man").

In the four years that followed *American Psycho*, Bale's career experienced critical and commercial failure. He next played a villainous real estate heir in [John Singleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Singleton "John Singleton")'s action film *[Shaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaft_(2000_film) "Shaft (2000 film)")* and appeared in [John Madden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Madden_(director) "John Madden (director)")'s [film adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_Corelli%27s_Mandolin_(film) "Captain Corelli's Mandolin (film)") of the [Louis de Bernières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_de_Berni%C3%A8res "Louis de Bernières") novel *[Captain Corelli's Mandolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_Corelli%27s_Mandolin "Captain Corelli's Mandolin")* as Mandras, a Greek fisherman who vies with [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage "Nicolas Cage")'s title character for the affections of Pelagia, played by [Penélope Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen%C3%A9lope_Cruz "Penélope Cruz"). Bale said he found it refreshing to play Mandras, who is emotionally humane, after working on *American Psycho* and *Shaft*. In 2002, he appeared in three films: *[Laurel Canyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurel_Canyon_(film) "Laurel Canyon (film)")*, *[Reign of Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reign_of_Fire_(film) "Reign of Fire (film)")* and *[Equilibrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilibrium_(film) "Equilibrium (film)")*. Reviewing *Laurel Canyon* for *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")*, [Lisa Schwarzbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Schwarzbaum "Lisa Schwarzbaum") called Bale's performance "fussy". After having reservations about joining the [post-apocalyptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-apocalyptic "Post-apocalyptic") *Reign of Fire*, which involved [computer-generated imagery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery"), Bale professed his enjoyment of making films that could go awry and cited director [Rob Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Bowman_(filmmaker) "Rob Bowman (filmmaker)") as a reason for his involvement. In *Equilibrium*, he plays a police officer in a futuristic society and performs [gun kata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_kata "Gun kata"), a fictional martial art that incorporates gunfighting. *[IGN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IGN "IGN")*'s Jeff Otto characterised *Reign of Fire* as "poorly received" and *Equilibrium* as "highly underrated", while *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")*'s Stephen Applebaum described the two films along with *Shaft* and *Captain Corelli's Mandolin* as "mediocre fare".

Bale starred as the insomnia-ridden, emotionally dysfunctional title character in the psychological thriller *[The Machinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Machinist "The Machinist")*. To prepare for the role, he initially only smoked cigarettes and drank whiskey. His diet later expanded to include black coffee, an apple and a can of tuna per day. Bale lost 63 pounds (29 kg), weighing 121 pounds (55 kg) to play the character, who was written in the script as "a walking skeleton". His weight loss prompted comparisons with [Robert De Niro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_De_Niro "Robert De Niro")'s weight gain in preparation to play [Jake LaMotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_LaMotta "Jake LaMotta") in the 1980 film *[Raging Bull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raging_Bull "Raging Bull")*. Describing his transformation as mentally calming, Bale claimed he had stopped working for a while because he did not come upon scripts that piqued his interest and that the film's script drew him to lose weight for the part. *The Machinist* was released in October 2004; it performed poorly at the box office. Roger Moore of the *[Orlando Sentinel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Sentinel "Orlando Sentinel")* regarded it as one of the best films of the year, and [Todd McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_McCarthy "Todd McCarthy") of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* wrote that Bale's "haunted, aggressive and finally wrenching performance" gave it a "strong anchor".

### Batman and dramatic roles (2005–2008)

Bale portrayed [Bruce Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Wayne_(Dark_Knight_trilogy) "Bruce Wayne (Dark Knight trilogy)") and his superhero alias, [Batman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman "Batman"), in [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan "Christopher Nolan")'s *[Batman Begins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Begins "Batman Begins")*, a [reboot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reboot_(continuity) "Reboot (continuity)") of the [Batman film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_film_series "Batman film series"). Nolan cast Bale, who was still fairly unknown at the time, because Bale had "exactly the balance of darkness and light" Nolan sought. For the part, Bale regained the weight he lost for *The Machinist* and built muscle, weighing 220 pounds (100 kg). He trained in weapons, [Wing Chun Kung Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wing_Chun_Kung_Fu "Wing Chun Kung Fu") and the [Keysi Fighting Method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keysi_Fighting_Method "Keysi Fighting Method"). Acknowledging the story's peculiar circumstances involving a character "who thinks he can run around in a [batsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batsuit "Batsuit") in the middle of the night", Bale said he and Nolan had deliberately approached it with "as realistic a motivation as possible", referencing Wayne's parents' murder. Bale voiced Wayne and Batman differently. He employed gravelly tone qualities for Batman, which Nolan believed reinforced the character's visual appearance. *Batman Begins* was released in the US in June 2005. Tim Grierson and [Will Leitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Leitch "Will Leitch") of *Vulture* complimented Bale's "sensitive, intelligent portrayal of a spoiled, wayward Bruce who finally grows up (and fights crime)." The performance earned Bale the [MTV Movie Award for Best Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award_for_Best_Hero "MTV Movie Award for Best Hero").

In the same year, Bale voiced the titular [Howl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wizard_Howl "Wizard Howl"), a wizard, in the English-language [dub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubbing_(filmmaking) "Dubbing (filmmaking)") of [Hayao Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayao_Miyazaki "Hayao Miyazaki")'s *[Howl's Moving Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howl%27s_Moving_Castle_(film) "Howl's Moving Castle (film)")*, a Japanese animated film adaptation of [Diana Wynne Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_Wynne_Jones "Diana Wynne Jones")'s [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howl%27s_Moving_Castle_(novel) "Howl's Moving Castle (novel)"). He committed himself to voice the role after watching Miyazaki's animated film *[Spirited Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirited_Away "Spirited Away")*. Later that year, he starred as a US war veteran who deals with [post-traumatic stress disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder "Post-traumatic stress disorder") in the [David Ayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ayer "David Ayer")-helmed crime drama *[Harsh Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harsh_Times_(film) "Harsh Times (film)")*, which premiered at the [Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival "Toronto International Film Festival"). He portrayed the colonist [John Rolfe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Rolfe "John Rolfe") in *[The New World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_World_(2005_film) "The New World (2005 film)")*, a [historical drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_drama "Historical drama") film inspired by the stories of [Pocahontas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocahontas "Pocahontas"), directed by [Terrence Malick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrence_Malick "Terrence Malick"). The film was released in December 2005. The following year saw the premiere of *[Rescue Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rescue_Dawn "Rescue Dawn")*, by the German filmmaker [Werner Herzog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Herzog "Werner Herzog"), in which Bale portrayed the American fighter pilot [Dieter Dengler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieter_Dengler "Dieter Dengler"), who fights for his life after being shot down while on a mission during the [Vietnam War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War "Vietnam War"). After the two worked together, Herzog stated that he had considered Bale to be among his generation's greatest talents long before he played Batman. *[The Austin Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Austin_Chronicle "The Austin Chronicle")*'s Marjorie Baumgarten viewed Bale's work as a continuance of his "masterful command of yet another American personality type."

For the 2006 film *[The Prestige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prestige "The Prestige")*, Bale reunited with *Batman Begins* director Nolan, who said that Bale was cast after offering himself for the part. It is based on [the 1995 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prestige_(novel) "The Prestige (novel)") by [Christopher Priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Priest_(novelist) "Christopher Priest (novelist)") about a rivalry between two [Victorian era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_era "Victorian era") magicians, whom Bale and [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman "Hugh Jackman") play in the film. While it attracted acclaim from critics, the film performed more modestly during its run in theatres, earning $110 million against a $40 million budget. In his review for *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")*, [A. O. Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._O._Scott "A. O. Scott") highlighted Bale's "fierce inwardness" and called his performance "something to savor". Bale next starred in the 2007 drama films *[I'm Not There](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%27m_Not_There "I'm Not There")*, portraying two incarnations of singer-songwriter [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan"), and in *[3:10 to Yuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3:10_to_Yuma_(2007_film) "3:10 to Yuma (2007 film)")*, playing a justice-seeking [cattleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattleman "Cattleman"). He characterised his Dylan incarnations as "two men on a real quest for truth" and attributed his interest in *3:10 to Yuma* to his affinity for films where he gets to "just be dirty and crawling in the mud". Bale reprised the role of Batman in Nolan's *Batman Begins* sequel *[The Dark Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight "The Dark Knight")*, with [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger "Heath Ledger") joining the cast as the [Joker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joker_(The_Dark_Knight) "Joker (The Dark Knight)"). The film received widespread acclaim and became the fourth to gross more than $1 billion worldwide upon its July 2008 release. He did many of his own stunts, including one that involved standing on the roof of the [Sears Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sears_Tower "Sears Tower") in Chicago. *The Dark Knight* has been regarded by critics as the best [superhero film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhero_film "Superhero film").

### The *Dark Knight* trilogy completion and acclaim (2009–2012)

In February 2008, [Warner Bros.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Pictures "Warner Bros. Pictures") announced that Bale would star as the rebellion leader [John Connor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Connor "John Connor") in the post-apocalyptic action film *[Terminator Salvation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminator_Salvation "Terminator Salvation")*, directed by [McG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McG "McG"), who cited Bale as "the most credible actor of his generation". In February 2009 an audio recording of a tirade on the film's set in July 2008 involving Bale was released. It captured him directing profanities towards and threatening to attack the film's cinematographer [Shane Hurlbut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hurlbut "Shane Hurlbut"), who walked onto the set during the filming of a scene acted by Bale and [Bryce Dallas Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryce_Dallas_Howard "Bryce Dallas Howard"), and culminated in Bale threatening to quit the film if Hurlbut was not fired. Several colleagues in the film industry defended Bale, attributing the incident to his dedication to acting. Bale publicly apologised in February 2009, calling the outburst "inexcusable" and his behaviour "way out of order" and claiming he had made amends with Hurlbut. *Terminator Salvation* was released in May 2009 to tepid reviews. [Claudia Puig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudia_Puig "Claudia Puig") of *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today "USA Today")* considered Bale's work to be "surprisingly one-dimensional", while *[The Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Age "The Age")*'s Jake Wilson wrote he gave one of his least compelling performances. Bale later admitted he knew during production that the film would not revitalise the [*Terminator* franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminator_(franchise) "Terminator (franchise)") as he had wished. He asserted he would not work with McG again.

Bale portrayed the [Federal Bureau of Investigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation "Federal Bureau of Investigation") agent [Melvin Purvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Purvis "Melvin Purvis") opposite [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp "Johnny Depp") as the gangster [John Dillinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dillinger "John Dillinger") in [Michael Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Mann "Michael Mann")'s crime drama *[Public Enemies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Enemies_(2009_film) "Public Enemies (2009 film)")*. Released in July 2009, it earned critical praise and had a commercially successful theatrical run. Dan Zak of *[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post "The Washington Post")* was unsatisfied with the casting of Bale and Depp, believing their characters' rivalry lacked electricity, while *[The New Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic")*'s Christopher Orr found Bale's "characteristically closed off" performance "nonetheless effective". The following year, Bale starred in the role of [Dicky Eklund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dicky_Eklund "Dicky Eklund"), a professional boxer whose career has ended due to his drug addiction, in [David O. Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_O._Russell "David O. Russell")'s drama film *[The Fighter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fighter "The Fighter")*. It chronicles the relationship between Eklund and his brother and boxing trainee, [Micky Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micky_Ward "Micky Ward"), played by [Mark Wahlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Wahlberg "Mark Wahlberg"). To balance Eklund's tragic condition, Bale incorporated humour in his characterisation. The portrayal, for which he lost 30 pounds (14 kg), was acclaimed, the *[San Francisco Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Chronicle "San Francisco Chronicle")*'s [Mick LaSalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_LaSalle "Mick LaSalle") describing it as "shrewdly observed, physically precise and psychologically acute". Bale won the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") and the [Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture") for his performance. In 2011 he starred in [Zhang Yimou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yimou "Zhang Yimou")'s historical drama film *[The Flowers of War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flowers_of_War "The Flowers of War")*, which was the highest-grossing Chinese film of the year. Critics described it as "nationalistic", "anti-Japanese" and "too long, too melodramatic, too lightweight".

Bale played Batman again under Nolan's direction in the sequel *[The Dark Knight Rises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_Rises "The Dark Knight Rises")*, released in July 2012. He described Batman in the film as a remorseful recluse in poor mental and physical health, who has surrendered following the events of *The Dark Knight*. Following [a mass shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Aurora_theater_shooting "2012 Aurora theater shooting") at a midnight showing of the film in [Aurora, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora,_Colorado "Aurora, Colorado"), US, Bale and his wife visited survivors, doctors and first responders at [the Medical Center of Aurora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Medical_Center_of_Aurora "The Medical Center of Aurora") as well as a memorial to victims. *The Dark Knight Rises* was the 11th film to gross more than $1 billion worldwide, surpassing *The Dark Knight*. Nolan's Batman film series, dubbed the [*Dark Knight* trilogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_Trilogy "The Dark Knight Trilogy"), is one of the highest-grossing film franchises. It is also regarded as one of the best comic book film franchises. Bale's performance in the three films garnered universal acclaim, with *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")*, *[The Indian Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Indian_Express "The Indian Express")*, [MovieWeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MovieWeb "MovieWeb"), *[NME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NME "NME")* and a poll conducted by the *[Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times "Radio Times")* ranking it as the best portrayal of Batman on film. Bale later revealed his dissatisfaction with his work throughout the trilogy, saying he "didn't quite nail" his part and that he "didn't quite manage" what he had hoped he would as Batman.

### Continued critical success (2013–2019)

In 2013 Bale played a steel mill worker in [Scott Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Cooper_(director) "Scott Cooper (director)")'s thriller *[Out of the Furnace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_the_Furnace "Out of the Furnace")*. Cooper rewrote the film's script with Bale in mind before the two even met and would not proceed with the project without the actor's involvement. Critics commended the film and deemed it an excellent beginning of the next phase in Bale's career after playing Batman, with Kristopher Tapley of *Variety* noting his work in the film was his best. That same year, he starred in *[American Hustle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Hustle "American Hustle")*, which reunited him with David O. Russell after their work on *The Fighter*. To play the con artist Irving Rosenfeld, Bale studied footage of interviews with the real-world con artist [Mel Weinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Weinberg "Mel Weinberg"), who served as inspiration for the character. He gained 43 pounds (20 kg), shaved part of his head and adopted a slouched posture, which reduced his height by 3 inches (7.6 cm) and caused him to suffer a [herniated disc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herniated_disc "Herniated disc"). Russell indicated that Robert De Niro, who appeared in an uncredited role, did not recognise Bale when they were first introduced. Writing for the *[New York Daily News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Daily_News "New York Daily News")*, Joe Neumaier found Bale's performance to be "sad, funny and riveting". He was nominated for an [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award") and a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award") for his work.

Bale portrayed [Moses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses "Moses") in [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott "Ridley Scott")'s epic film *[Exodus: Gods and Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exodus:_Gods_and_Kings "Exodus: Gods and Kings")*. Released in December 2014, the film faced accusations of [whitewashing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitewashing_in_film "Whitewashing in film") for the casting of Caucasian actors in [Middle Eastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_groups_in_the_Middle_East "Ethnic groups in the Middle East") roles. Scott justified casting decisions citing financing needs, Bale stating that Scott had been forthright in getting the film made. Its critical response varied between negative and mixed, and the *[St. Louis Post-Dispatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis_Post-Dispatch "St. Louis Post-Dispatch")*'s Joe Williams called Bale's performance in the film the most apathetic of his career. Bale appeared in Terrence Malick's drama *[Knight of Cups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_of_Cups_(film) "Knight of Cups (film)")*, which *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic "The Atlantic")* critic David Sims dubbed a "noble failure". During its premiere at the [65th Berlin International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/65th_Berlin_International_Film_Festival "65th Berlin International Film Festival") in February 2015, he said he filmed the project without having learned any dialogue and that Malick had only given him a character description. Later that year, he starred as [Michael Burry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Burry "Michael Burry"), an antisocial hedge fund manager, in [Adam McKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_McKay "Adam McKay")'s *[The Big Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Short_(film) "The Big Short (film)")*, a biographical comedy-drama film about the [2008 financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_financial_crisis "2008 financial crisis"). *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal "The Wall Street Journal")*'s [Joe Morgenstern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Morgenstern "Joe Morgenstern") found his portrayal "scarily hilarious—or in one-liners and quick takes, deftly edited". The role earned Bale Academy Award and Golden Globe Award nominations for Best Supporting Actor.

In the 2016 historical drama *[The Promise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Promise_(2016_American_film) "The Promise (2016 American film)")*, set during the [Armenian genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_genocide "Armenian genocide"), he played an American journalist who becomes involved in a [love triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_triangle "Love triangle") with a woman, played by [Charlotte Le Bon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Le_Bon "Charlotte Le Bon"), and an Armenian medical student, played by [Oscar Isaac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Isaac "Oscar Isaac"). Critics disapproved of the film, which accrued a $102 million loss. Reviewing the film for *The New York Times*, Jeannette Catsoulis wrote that Bale appeared "muffled and indistinct". About the film, Bale says, "It's got to be a larger conversation because it has to be relevant today… often the violators get away with impunity. But it's such an important part of modern history, so tragic, so barbaric." In Cooper's 2017 film *[Hostiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostiles_(film) "Hostiles (film)")*, Bale starred as a [US Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Army "US Army") officer escorting a gravely ill [Cheyenne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheyenne "Cheyenne") war chief and his family back to their home in [Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montana "Montana"). He calls the film "a western with brutal, modern-day resonance" and his character "a bigoted and hate-filled man". Bale learned the [Cheyenne language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheyenne_language "Cheyenne language") while working on the film. *[Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_(film_magazine) "Empire (film magazine)")* critic Dan Jolin considered his performance striking and one of the strongest of his career. In 2018, Bale voiced [Bagheera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagheera "Bagheera") in the adventure film *[Mowgli: Legend of the Jungle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mowgli:_Legend_of_the_Jungle "Mowgli: Legend of the Jungle")*. *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")*'s David Fear wrote that his voice work and that of [Andy Serkis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Serkis "Andy Serkis"), who directed the film, "bring the soul as well as sound and fury".

For the 2018 biographical comedy drama *[Vice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_(2018_film) "Vice (2018 film)")*, written and directed by Adam McKay, Bale underwent a major body transformation once again, as he gained over 40 pounds (18 kg) and shaved his head to portray US Vice President [Dick Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Cheney "Dick Cheney"). He described Cheney, who is reckoned the most influential and loathed vice president in US history, as "quiet and secretive". The film reunited Bale with [Amy Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Adams "Amy Adams"), with whom he had co-starred in *The Fighter* and *American Hustle*. It received positive reviews, and *The Guardian*'s [Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw "Peter Bradshaw") commended Bale's "terrifically and in fact rather scarily plausible" Cheney impersonation. Lauded by critics, the performance won Bale the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Motion Picture Musical or Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture_Musical_or_Comedy "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Motion Picture Musical or Comedy") and garnered him an Academy Award nomination. During his acceptance speech at the [76th Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_Golden_Globe_Awards "76th Golden Globe Awards"), Bale thanked [Satan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satan "Satan") for inspiring his Cheney portrayal, which elicited a response from Cheney's daughter and [US Representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Representative "US Representative") [Liz Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Cheney "Liz Cheney"), who stated that Bale ruined his opportunity to play "a real superhero".

Bale portrayed the sports car racing driver [Ken Miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Miles "Ken Miles") in the 2019 sports drama *[Ford v Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_v_Ferrari "Ford v Ferrari")*, for which he lost 70 pounds (32 kg) after playing Cheney. Directed by [James Mangold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Mangold "James Mangold"), the film follows Miles and the automotive designer [Carroll Shelby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carroll_Shelby "Carroll Shelby"), played by [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon "Matt Damon"), in events surrounding the [1966 24 Hours of Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans "1966 24 Hours of Le Mans") race. The role earned Bale a fifth Golden Globe Award nomination. While promoting the film, he said he would no longer go through weight fluctuations for roles.

### Limited work (2020–present)

Bale played [Gorr the God Butcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorr_the_God_Butcher "Gorr the God Butcher"), a villain, in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") superhero film *[Thor: Love and Thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thor:_Love_and_Thunder "Thor: Love and Thunder")*, which was released in July 2022. He cited a character in the music video for the [Aphex Twin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aphex_Twin "Aphex Twin") song "[Come to Daddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_to_Daddy_(song) "Come to Daddy (song)")" as an inspiration for his characterization of Gorr. Bale's portrayal drew praise from critics, who deemed it "grounded and non-campy". He produced and appeared in David O. Russell's period film *[Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_(2022_film) "Amsterdam (2022 film)")* and Scott Cooper's thriller *[The Pale Blue Eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pale_Blue_Eye "The Pale Blue Eye")*, reuniting with both directors for the third time. *Amsterdam* was released in October 2022, receiving dire reviews and failing at the box office. *The Pale Blue Eye*, adapted from [the 2006 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pale_Blue_Eye_(novel) "The Pale Blue Eye (novel)") by [Louis Bayard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Bayard "Louis Bayard"), was released in December 2022, receiving mixed reviews from critics. With his involvement in the 2023 Japanese animated film *[The Boy and the Heron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boy_and_the_Heron "The Boy and the Heron")*, Bale voiced a character in an English-language dub of a film by Hayao Miyazaki for the second time.

Bale played [Frankenstein's monster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankenstein%27s_monster "Frankenstein's monster") in [Maggie Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Gyllenhaal "Maggie Gyllenhaal")'s fantasy period film *[The Bride!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bride! "The Bride!")*, which was released on 6 March 2026. He will also portray the football coach and the [Las Vegas Raiders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Raiders "Las Vegas Raiders") owner [Al Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Davis "Al Davis") in the [John Madden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Madden "John Madden") biopic *[Madden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madden_(film) "Madden (film)")*, working with Russell for the fourth time.

## Artistry and public image

Bale is known for his exhaustive dedication to the weight fluctuations that his parts demand as well as "the intensity with which he completely inhabits his roles", with *The Washington Post*'s [Ann Hornaday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Hornaday "Ann Hornaday") rating him among the most physically gifted actors of his generation. Max O'Connell of *[RogerEbert.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RogerEbert.com "RogerEbert.com")* deemed Bale's commitment to altering his physical appearance "an anchoring facet to a depiction of obsession" in his performances, while the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")*'s Hugh Hart likened the urgency that drives Bale's acting style to [method acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_acting "Method acting"), adding that it "convincingly animates even his most extreme physical transformations." Bale has said that he does not practise method acting and that he does not use a particular technique. He named [Rowan Atkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowan_Atkinson "Rowan Atkinson") as his template as an actor and added that he was mesmerised by him when they worked together. He also studied the work of [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman "Gary Oldman"), crediting him as the reason for his pursuit of acting.

Bale has been recognised for his versatility; Martha Ross of *[The Mercury News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mercury_News "The Mercury News")* named him one of his generation's most versatile actors. Known to be very private about his personal life, Bale has said that his objective was to embody characters without showing any aspect of himself. He explained that "letting people know who you are" does not help create different characters, viewing anonymity as "what's giving you the ability to play those characters". During interviews to promote films in which he puts on an accent, Bale would continue speaking in the given accent. Bale has also been noted for portraying roles with an American accent, with *The Atlantic*'s Joe Reid listing him among those who "work least in their native accents"; in real life, Bale speaks in an "emphatic, non-posh" English accent.

Bale is often described as a [sex symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_symbol "Sex symbol"). He was ranked at number eight on *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* magazine's list of the [highest-paid actors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-paid_film_actors "List of highest-paid film actors") of 2014, earning $35 million. *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")* named Bale one of the greatest film actors of the 21st century in 2025.

## Personal life

Bale has lived in Los Angeles since the 1990s and holds both UK and US citizenships. He married Sandra "Sibi" Blažić, an American former model of [Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbs "Serbs") descent, in Las Vegas on 29 January 2000. They have a daughter and a son. In 2000, Bale's father married feminist [Gloria Steinem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Steinem "Gloria Steinem"); he died in 2003 of [brain lymphoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_central_nervous_system_lymphoma "Primary central nervous system lymphoma").

On 22 July 2008, Bale was arrested in London after his mother and his sister Sharon reported him to the police for an alleged assault at a hotel. He was released on [bail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bail "Bail"). Bale denied the allegations and later called the incident "a deeply personal matter". On 14 August, the [Crown Prosecution Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prosecution_Service "Crown Prosecution Service") declared they would take no further action against him because of "insufficient evidence to afford a realistic prospect of conviction".

Bale became a vegetarian at the age of seven, but in 2009 he stated that he was "in and out of the vegetarianism now". He had stopped eating [red meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_meat "Red meat") after reading the children's book *[Charlotte's Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte%27s_Web "Charlotte's Web")*. An animal rights activist, he supports the organisations [Greenpeace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenpeace "Greenpeace"), the [World Wide Fund for Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wide_Fund_for_Nature "World Wide Fund for Nature"), the [Doris Day Animal League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doris_Day_Animal_League "Doris Day Animal League"), the [Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dian_Fossey_Gorilla_Fund_International "Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund International") and [Redwings Horse Sanctuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redwings_Horse_Sanctuary "Redwings Horse Sanctuary"). While promoting *The Flowers of War* in December 2011, Bale, with a crew from the television network [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN"), attempted to visit [Chen Guangcheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Guangcheng "Chen Guangcheng"), a confined blind [barefoot lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barefoot_lawyer "Barefoot lawyer"), in a village in eastern China. He was forced to retreat after scuffling with guards at a checkpoint. Bale finally met Chen at a dinner held by the nonprofit [Human Rights First](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_First "Human Rights First") the following year, during which he presented Chen with an award. Bale voiced Chen's story in [Amnesty International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amnesty_International "Amnesty International")'s podcast, *In Their Own Words*.

Bale supports [recognition of the Armenian genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_genocide_recognition "Armenian genocide recognition").

Along with [Eric Esrailian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Esrailian "Eric Esrailian") and Tim McCormick, Bale co-founded California Together, an organization aiming to construct a $22 million village in [Palmdale, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmdale,_California "Palmdale, California") to help siblings in [foster care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_care "Foster care") remain together.

## Acting credits and accolades

According to the [review aggregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregation "Review aggregation") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), which assigns films scores based on the number of positive critical reviews they receive, some of Bale's highest-scoring films are *The Dark Knight* (2008), *Ford v Ferrari* (2019), *American Hustle* (2013), *Little Women* (1994), *The Fighter* (2010), *Rescue Dawn* (2007), *3:10 to Yuma* (2007), *The Big Short* (2015), *Howl's Moving Castle* (2005) and *The Dark Knight Rises* (2012); the first, third and last of which are also listed by the data website [The Numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Numbers_(website) "The Numbers (website)") as his highest-grossing films, alongside *Terminator Salvation* (2009), *Batman Begins* (2005), *Pocahontas* (1995), *Thor: Love and Thunder* (2022) and *Exodus: Gods and Kings* (2014).

Bale has garnered four Academy Award nominations, including two in the [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor") category for his work in *American Hustle* and *Vice* (2018) as well as two in the [Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") category for his work in *The Fighter* and *The Big Short*; he won one for *The Fighter*. He has earned two Golden Globe Awards, including Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture for his role in *The Fighter* and Best Actor – Motion Picture Musical or Comedy for his role in *Vice*, and received two nominations for Best Actor – Motion Picture Musical or Comedy for his performances in *American Hustle* and *The Big Short* and a nomination for [Best Actor – Motion Picture Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture_Drama "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Motion Picture Drama") for his performance in *Ford v Ferrari*. Bale has also been nominated for eight [Screen Actors Guild Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards "Screen Actors Guild Awards"), winning in the [Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Supporting_Role "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Supporting Role") category for *The Fighter* and the [Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Cast_in_a_Motion_Picture "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture") category as part of the *American Hustle* cast.
