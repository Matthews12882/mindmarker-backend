# Adéla (singer)

**Adèla Jergová** (born 27 November 2003) is a Slovak singer, songwriter and dancer. She spent her teenage years training in ballet dance before expanding to music and competing in the 2023 talent competition television show *[Dream Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_Academy "Dream Academy")*. After signing to [Capitol Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Records "Capitol Records"), she released her debut [extended play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") (EP), *[The Provocateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Provocateur "The Provocateur")* (2025).

Adéla's first studio album, *[Prima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prima_(album) "Prima (album)")* (2026), debuted at number four on the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") chart. She is the first Slovak artist in history to appear on the on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") with the single "[Ain't in LA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain%27t_in_LA "Ain't in LA")" and the top-ten hit "[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman_(song) "Nicole Kidman (song)")".

## Early life

Adéla Jergová was born on 27 November 2003 in [Bratislava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bratislava "Bratislava"), Slovakia. She began taking [ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet "Ballet") lessons in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow "Moscow"), Russia, starting when she was three years old. She has stated that watching the American teen sitcom *[Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana "Hannah Montana")* as a young child inspired her to become a singer. She taught herself English, starting at eight years old, by watching American television series and interviews with American singers; she was fluent by age nine. Adéla has described being online as a child as having "[Americanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americanization "Americanization")" her and introducing her to more liberal ideas, which made her feel alienated from Slovak society. As a child, she became interested in vocals and production. She moved to [Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna "Vienna"), Austria, to join the [Vienna State Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_State_Ballet "Vienna State Ballet") as a teenager and then to [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"), England, to join the [English National Ballet School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_National_Ballet_School "English National Ballet School") the following year.

## Career

### 2022–2023: Career beginnings and *Dream Academy*

In 2022, Adéla moved to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), United States, to pursue a music career. She studied music at [Santa Monica College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica_College "Santa Monica College"). In 2023, she participated in the [Hybe Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybe_Corporation "Hybe Corporation") and [Geffen Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geffen_Records "Geffen Records")' reality competition series *[Dream Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_Academy "Dream Academy")* alongside 19 other contestants. During the first challenge, she performed a group dance to [Blackpink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpink "Blackpink")'s "[Pink Venom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Venom "Pink Venom")" and was one of the first to be eliminated after receiving the fewest fan votes. She later described the competition as "the worst year of my life". The winners of the series went on to form the girl group [Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katseye "Katseye"). Adéla also appeared in *[Pop Star Academy: Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Star_Academy:_Katseye "Pop Star Academy: Katseye")*, [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix")'s 2024 behind-the-scenes [docuseries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docuseries "Docuseries") covering the events of *Dream Academy*.

### 2024–present: *The Provocateur* and *Prima*

In September 2024, she made her debut with the release of "Homewrecked", which *[Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_(magazine) "Out (magazine)")* named one of the best singles for the week of its release. Adéla's second single "Superscar" was released in October 2024. In 2024, she attended college in Los Angeles for music. Adéla's third single, "Machine Girl", was released in February 2025 and co-produced by Canadian musician [Grimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grimes "Grimes"), who reached out to Adéla after hearing "Homewrecked" on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok"). Its music video co-starred actress [Sofia Wylie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Wylie "Sofia Wylie") and featured a cameo from Grimes. In May 2025, Adéla announced that she had signed with [Capitol Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Records "Capitol Records"). Her next single, "Death by Devotion", was produced by [Dylan Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Brady "Dylan Brady") and Zhone and released in July 2025.

*[The Provocateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Provocateur "The Provocateur")*, her debut [extended play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") (EP), was released on 22 August 2025. Alongside the EP, she released the single "Sex on the Beat" with a music video featuring a cameo from [Christina Aguilera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Aguilera "Christina Aguilera"). The music video was followed in September 2025 by a choreography-only video. She performed on her first tour, The ProvocaTour, in October 2025.

During April and May 2026, she performed as the opening act for [Demi Lovato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Lovato "Demi Lovato")'s [It's Not That Deep Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_Not_That_Deep_Tour "It's Not That Deep Tour"). She released the single "[KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB_(song) "KGB (song)")" the same month. The day after her final performance for the [It's Not That Deep Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_Not_That_Deep_Tour "It's Not That Deep Tour"), Adéla announced her headlining tour, the Red Bottoms tour, which began in late August 2026. Following the release of the single "[Ain't in LA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain%27t_in_LA "Ain't in LA")" in July 2026, she announced the release of her debut album, *[Prima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prima_(album) "Prima (album)")*, which was released on 4 September 2026. In August 2026, Adéla performed at [Lollapalooza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza "Lollapalooza") in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago"), United States. Her set gained praise from several outlets. In September, she was nominated for the SOZA Award 2025 for the most successful young author, her first recognition from the music industry in Slovakia.

Throughout the summer of 2026, both of her singles "Ain't in LA" and "[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman_(song) "Nicole Kidman (song)")" went viral online, becoming her first and second entries on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") chart; with these singles, she also became the first Slovak artist in history to appear on the chart. "Ain't in LA" peaked in the top 40 while "Nicole Kidman" reached the top 10.

## Artistry

Adéla has a [soprano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soprano "Soprano") vocal range, and her music is classified as [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music"). She has described her lyrics, which often tackle her sexual expression, as "exaggerated" and "over-the-top" versions of the events of her life. She has cited [Beyoncé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyonc%C3%A9 "Beyoncé") and [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga "Lady Gaga") as her biggest musical influences, calling herself "the biggest BeyHive you've ever fucking met", and listed [Mariah Carey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariah_Carey "Mariah Carey"), [Patti LaBelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patti_LaBelle "Patti LaBelle"), [Whitney Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Houston "Whitney Houston"), [Céline Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9line_Dion "Céline Dion"), and [Aretha Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aretha_Franklin "Aretha Franklin") as early inspirations for her vocals and [Imogen Heap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imogen_Heap "Imogen Heap"), [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande "Ariana Grande"), and Beyoncé as influential for her use of harmonies. She has also cited [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears "Britney Spears"), [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna "Madonna"), [Janet Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Jackson "Janet Jackson"), and [Miley Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus "Miley Cyrus") as foundational pop inspirations. She has cited "[Rock with U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_with_U "Rock with U")" by Jackson, "[Bad Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Romance "Bad Romance")" by Lady Gaga, and *[Lemonade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemonade_(2016_film) "Lemonade (2016 film)")* by Beyoncé as influences on her music videos.
