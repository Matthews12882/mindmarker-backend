# Slow Horses

***Slow Horses*** is a British [spy thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spy_fiction "Spy fiction") television series based on the *[Slough House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slough_House_(novel_series) "Slough House (novel series)")* novels by [Mick Herron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Herron "Mick Herron"), and adapted for television by the English comedian and writer [Will Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Smith_(comedian) "Will Smith (comedian)"). It follows Slough House, an [MI5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MI5 "MI5") unit where disgraced or failed agents are consigned, under the supervision of Jackson Lamb ([Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman "Gary Oldman")). It also stars [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden "Jack Lowden"), [Kristin Scott Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristin_Scott_Thomas "Kristin Scott Thomas"), [Jonathan Pryce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pryce "Jonathan Pryce"), [Hugo Weaving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Weaving "Hugo Weaving"), [Saskia Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskia_Reeves "Saskia Reeves"), [Rosalind Eleazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Eleazar "Rosalind Eleazar"), [Christopher Chung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Chung_(actor) "Christopher Chung (actor)"), [Aimee-Ffion Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aimee-Ffion_Edwards "Aimee-Ffion Edwards"), [Joanna Scanlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_Scanlan "Joanna Scanlan"), [Tom Brooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Brooke "Tom Brooke"), [Lucian Msamati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucian_Msamati "Lucian Msamati"), and [Olivia Cooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Cooke "Olivia Cooke").

The series premiered on [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") on 1 April 2022 to highly positive reviews. The first five series have adapted the novels *Slow Horses*, *Dead Lions*, *Real Tigers*, *Spook Street*, and *London Rules*, respectively. *Slow Horses* has been renewed for a seventh and eighth series, based on the novels *Bad Actors* (series 7) and *Clown Town* (series 8). The sixth series, based on the novels *Joe Country* and *Slough House*, premiered on 16 September 2026. All six series have received critical acclaim, with praise for the series' direction, performances, script, tonal balance, and consistency.

## Premise

Slough House is a [banishment room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banishment_room "Banishment room") for [MI5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MI5 "MI5") service rejects who have seriously failed a task but not badly enough to get sacked. Those consigned there are known as "slow horses", a play on the name of the place itself, Slough House, and an expression for people who are slow at thinking and action. Those assigned there are expected to endure dull, routine tasks, along with verbal abuse from their boss, Jackson Lamb, who anticipates that they will leave out of boredom or frustration. Life in Slough House is defined by drudgery, yet the Slow Horses somehow get involved in investigating cases that endanger Britain.

## Cast and characters

### Main

* [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman "Gary Oldman") as Jackson Lamb, the head of Slough House who is unkempt, flatulent and rude with a sour wit, all disguising a tactical sharpness and firm loyalty to his agents.
* [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden "Jack Lowden") as River Cartwright, an up-and-coming MI5 agent abruptly shunted to Slough House after a very public training-exercise blunder.
* [Kristin Scott Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristin_Scott_Thomas "Kristin Scott Thomas") as Diana Taverner, the ambitious Deputy Director General of MI5 and head of operations, designated "Second Desk" until the end of series 5 when promoted to "First Desk", Director General of MI5.
* [Jonathan Pryce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pryce "Jonathan Pryce") as David Cartwright (series 4–6; special guest series 1–3), River's grandfather, and a former senior MI5 official with a legendary reputation, often known as "The Old Bastard".
* [Hugo Weaving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Weaving "Hugo Weaving") as Frank Harkness (series 4 and 6), a former [CIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA "CIA") operative turned ruthless mercenary and the biological father of River Cartwright.
* [Saskia Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskia_Reeves "Saskia Reeves") as Catherine Standish (series 5–present; co-starring series 1–4), Slough House's loyal office administrator, and a recovering alcoholic traumatised by the death of her former boss, Charles Partner.
* [Rosalind Eleazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Eleazar "Rosalind Eleazar") as Louisa Guy (series 5–present; co-starring series 1–4), assigned to Slough House after a tail operation went badly wrong.
* [Christopher Chung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Chung_(actor) "Christopher Chung (actor)") as Roddy Ho (series 5–present; co-starring series 1–4), an arrogant and obnoxious computer nerd and former [hacktivist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacktivist "Hacktivist") with undeniable technical skills.
* [Aimee-Ffion Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aimee-Ffion_Edwards "Aimee-Ffion Edwards") as Shirley Dander (series 5–present; co-starring series 2–4), a physically capable agent undone by drug and anger issues.
* [Joanna Scanlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_Scanlan "Joanna Scanlan") as Moira Tregorian (series 6; co-starring series 4), one of the Queens of the Database (record keepers), banished to Slough House by Claude Whelan. She becomes Catherine's temporary replacement.
* [Tom Brooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Brooke "Tom Brooke") as J.K. Coe (series 6; co-starring series 4–5), the newest "Slow Horse" and former operative from Psych Eval. He is reclusive and has [PTSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PTSD "PTSD").
* [Lucian Msamati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucian_Msamati "Lucian Msamati") as Oliver Nash (series 6), the chairman of the Limitations Committee overseeing MI5's activities.
* [Olivia Cooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Cooke "Olivia Cooke") as Sidonie "Sid" Baker (series 6; co-starring series 1), a competent, likable MI5 agent inexplicably assigned to Slough House.

### Series 1 (2022)

*Slow Horses* is based on [the novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses_(novel) "Slow Horses (novel)") by [Mick Herron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Herron "Mick Herron"), which is part of the author's *Slough House* series. It tells the story of a team of British intelligence agents who have all committed career-ending mistakes, and subsequently work in a dumping ground department of MI5 called Slough House.

## Production

### Development

The series was given a straight to series order by [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") in October 2019, with English writer and comedian [Will Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Smith_(comedian) "Will Smith (comedian)") serving as showrunner. Each of the first five series has adapted the corresponding novel in the *Slough House* set. In October 2024, the show was renewed for a sixth series, which jointly adapts the sixth and seventh books, *Joe Country* and *Slough House*. The fifth series premiered on 24 September 2025, followed by new episodes on a weekly basis until the season finale on 29 October 2025. In July 2025, ahead of the fifth series premiere, Apple TV+ renewed for a seventh series which is to be based on *Bad Actors*. The sixth series premiered on 16 September 2026, with new episodes being released on a weekly basis until the finale on 21 October 2026. The day before the sixth series premiered, Apple TV renewed the show for an eighth series, based on the novel *Clown Town*.

By 2025, the series began receiving praise for its production schedule, which allows it to release seasons yearly despite being a streaming series. *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal "The Wall Street Journal")* released an op-ed regarding the show's production schedule and strategy, which includes filming seasons back-to-back, and having multiple phases of production in motion at once.

In July 2025, it was announced that Smith would depart as showrunner after the fifth series, with [Gaby Chiappe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaby_Chiappe "Gaby Chiappe") taking over as head writer for the sixth series, and Ben Vanstone for the seventh and eighth. Smith described his departure as a "practical decision" in the face of difficulties managing scriptwriting for two series at a time, per the show's established schedule of simultaneously shooting one series while writing and pre-producing the next.

### Casting

In October 2019, along with the series order, [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman "Gary Oldman") was announced to star. The cast was rounded out in December 2020 with the additions of [Olivia Cooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Cooke "Olivia Cooke"), [Jonathan Pryce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Pryce "Jonathan Pryce"), [Kristin Scott Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristin_Scott_Thomas "Kristin Scott Thomas") and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden "Jack Lowden").

### Filming

Since its premiere, production of *Slow Horses* has followed a schedule of shooting two series back to back, with the next series already having completed filming by the time the current one is released. Commentators have noted how the speed and consistency of *Slow Horses*' release schedule stands in contrast to the increasingly long, multi-year gaps between seasons of [prestige TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prestige_TV "Prestige TV") shows, such as [*Severance*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severance_(TV_series) "Severance (TV series)") and *[Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things "Stranger Things")*.

Filming of the first series began on 30 November 2020 in the UK, and continued into February 2021. In July 2021, filming continued in [Stroud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroud "Stroud"), Gloucestershire. It was originally intended to film earlier in 2020 but was delayed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"). By March 2023, filming for the third series had been completed and filming for the fourth was about to begin. In June 2025, Oldman announced that filming for the sixth series had already been completed prior to the release of the fifth, and that filming for the seventh was expected to begin by October of that year.

Each series thus far has consisted of six episodes helmed by a single director: [James Hawes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Hawes "James Hawes") directed the first series, Jeremy Lovering directed the second, [Saul Metzstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul_Metzstein "Saul Metzstein") directed the third and fifth, [Adam Randall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Randall_(director) "Adam Randall (director)") directed the fourth, sixth, and eighth, and Robert McKillop directed the seventh.

The sixth series received a preview screening in the Primetime programme at the [2026 Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Toronto_International_Film_Festival "2026 Toronto International Film Festival") prior to its Apple TV release.

All six series of *Slow Horses* have received critical acclaim.

For the first series, [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") lists a 95% [approval rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approval_rating "Approval rating") based on 60 reviews with an average rating of 7.5/10. The website's critical consensus reads, "*Slow Horses* refreshes the espionage genre by letting its band of snoops be bumbling, with [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman "Gary Oldman") giving a masterclass in frumpy authority." On [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), the first series has a [weighted average score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean") of 78 out of 100 based on 22 critics.

The second series received a 100% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes based on 25 reviews with an average rating of 8.5/10. The website's critical consensus says, "*Slow Horses* says nay to the sophomore jinx with a second series that might be even better than its supremely addictive predecessor." On Metacritic, series two has a weighted average score of 84 out of 100 based on 10 critics. Oldman was nominated for a [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe "Golden Globe") and [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award "BAFTA Award") for his portrayal of Jackson Lamb.

The third series received a 98% approval rating from Rotten Tomatoes based on 43 reviews with an average rating of 8.5/10. The website's critical consensus reads, "*Slow Horses*' shabby charms reach a full gallop in this superb third season, yielding what might just be Slough House's most compelling operation yet." On Metacritic, series three has a weighted average score of 85 out of 100 based on 21 critics. The third series received nine [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award") nominations, including [Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series") for Gary Oldman, [Supporting Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series") for [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden "Jack Lowden") and [Outstanding Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series").

The fourth series received a 100% approval rating from Rotten Tomatoes based on reviews from 44 critics with an average rating of 8.6/10. The website's critical consensus says, "The stakes get more personal than ever before in *Slow Horses*' superb fourth season, proving that this spy series is saddled up for the long haul with no signs of fatigue." On Metacritic, series four has a weighted average score of 85 out of 100 based on 19 critics. Oldman was nominated for his second [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe "Golden Globe"), second [Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award "Emmy Award"), second [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award "BAFTA Award"), and first [Screen Actors Guild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild "Screen Actors Guild") Award for his performance.

The fifth series has received a 96% approval rating from Rotten Tomatoes based on reviews from 43 critics with an average rating of 8.05/10. The website's critical consensus says, "*Slow Horses* loosens the reins in a more lighthearted season that doesn't quite measure up to the series' high bar, but it still excels as one of the most compulsively watchable offerings on television." On Metacritic, series five has a weighted average score of 79 out of 100 based on 17 critics. Oldman was nominated for his third [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe "Golden Globe"), third [Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award "Emmy Award") and second [Screen Actors Guild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild "Screen Actors Guild") Award for his performance, whilst Lowden received a second [Emmy nomination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series").

The sixth series has received a 100% approval rating from Rotten Tomatoes based on reviews from 36 critics with an average rating of 8.45/10. On Metacritic, series six has a weighted average score of 85 out of 100 based on 12 critics.
