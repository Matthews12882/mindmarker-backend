# Matthew McConaughey

**Matthew David McConaughey** ([/məˈkɒnəheɪ/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*mə-KON-ə-hay*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born November 4, 1969) is an American actor. He achieved his breakthrough with a supporting performance in the coming-of-age comedy *[Dazed and Confused](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dazed_and_Confused_(film) "Dazed and Confused (film)")* (1993). After a number of supporting roles, his first success as a leading man came in the legal drama *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(1996_film) "A Time to Kill (1996 film)")* (1996). His career progressed with lead roles in the science fiction film *[Contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_(1997_American_film) "Contact (1997 American film)")* (1997), the historical drama *[Amistad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amistad_(film) "Amistad (film)")* (1997), the war film *[U-571](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-571_(film) "U-571 (film)")* (2000), and the epic science fiction film *[Interstellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film) "Interstellar (film)")* (2014).

In the 2000s, McConaughey became known for starring in romantic comedies, including *[The Wedding Planner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Planner "The Wedding Planner")* (2001), *[How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Lose_a_Guy_in_10_Days "How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days")* (2003), *[Failure to Launch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Failure_to_Launch "Failure to Launch")* (2006), *[Fool's Gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fool%27s_Gold_(2008_film) "Fool's Gold (2008 film)")* (2008), and *[Ghosts of Girlfriends Past](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts_of_Girlfriends_Past "Ghosts of Girlfriends Past")* (2009), establishing him as a [sex symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_symbol "Sex symbol"). In 2011, after a two-year hiatus from film acting, McConaughey began to appear in more dramatic roles, beginning with the legal drama *[The Lincoln Lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lincoln_Lawyer_(film) "The Lincoln Lawyer (film)")*. In 2012, he gained wider praise for his roles as a stripper in *[Magic Mike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Mike "Magic Mike")* and a fugitive in *[Mud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mud_(2012_film) "Mud (2012 film)")*.

McConaughey's portrayal of [Ron Woodroof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Woodroof "Ron Woodroof"), a cowboy diagnosed with [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS "HIV/AIDS"), in the biopic *[Dallas Buyers Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Buyers_Club "Dallas Buyers Club")* (2013) earned him widespread critical acclaim and numerous accolades, including the [Academy Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor"). He followed it with a supporting role in *[The Wolf of Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wolf_of_Wall_Street_(2013_film) "The Wolf of Wall Street (2013 film)")* (2013), and a starring role as [Rust Cohle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust_Cohle "Rust Cohle") in the [first season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective_(season_1) "True Detective (season 1)") of [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO")'s crime anthology series *[True Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective "True Detective")* (2014), for which he was nominated for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series"). His subsequent film roles include starring in *[Interstellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film) "Interstellar (film)")* (2014) and *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2019_film) "The Gentlemen (2019 film)")* (2019), as well as voice work in *[Kubo and the Two Strings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kubo_and_the_Two_Strings "Kubo and the Two Strings")* (2016), *[Sing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing_(2016_American_film) "Sing (2016 American film)")* (2016), and *[Sing 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing_2 "Sing 2")* (2021).

## Early life and education

Matthew David McConaughey was born on November 4, 1969, in [Uvalde, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uvalde,_Texas "Uvalde, Texas"). He has Irish heritage, particularly from the [County Cavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Cavan "County Cavan")/[County Monaghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Monaghan "County Monaghan") area. His mother, Mary Kathleen ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names "Birth name") McCabe), a published author and a former kindergarten teacher, was from [Trenton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trenton,_New_Jersey "Trenton, New Jersey"). His father, James Donald McConaughey, also had Irish roots. He was born in [Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi "Mississippi") in 1929 and raised in [Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana "Louisiana") where he ran an oil pipe supply business; he played for the [Kentucky Wildcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Wildcats_football "Kentucky Wildcats football") and the [Houston Cougars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Cougars_football "Houston Cougars football") college football teams. Jim was selected by the [Green Bay Packers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Bay_Packers "Green Bay Packers") of the [National Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League "National Football League") (NFL) in the 27th round of the [1953 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953_NFL_draft "1953 NFL draft"). He was released before the season began and never played an official league game in the NFL. McConaughey's parents married each other three times, having divorced each other twice. He has two older brothers, Michael and Patrick (who was adopted). Michael, nicknamed "Rooster", is a millionaire who starred in the [CNBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNBC "CNBC") docu-series *[West Texas Investors Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Texas_Investors_Club "West Texas Investors Club")*. The family were [Methodists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodism "Methodism"). He is a relative of [Confederate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_Army "Confederate States Army") brigadier general [Dandridge McRae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandridge_McRae "Dandridge McRae").

McConaughey moved to [Longview, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longview,_Texas "Longview, Texas") in 1980 and later attended [Longview High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longview_High_School "Longview High School"). In 1988, he went to Australia thinking he would be attending a high school in [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney "Sydney"). Instead he lived in [Warnervale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warnervale "Warnervale"), New South Wales for a year while he went to [Gorokan High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorokan_High_School "Gorokan High School") (he was a [Rotary Youth Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotary_Youth_Exchange "Rotary Youth Exchange") student) and worked as an assistant for an attorney and as a bank teller for [ANZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_and_New_Zealand_Banking_Group "Australia and New Zealand Banking Group"). During this time in Australia and for the next 10 years he seriously considered becoming a monk. A friend of his who was a monk told McConaughey that he was not here to be a monk, that he was meant to be "a communicator" and "a storyteller".

McConaughey attended the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin "University of Texas at Austin") (UT-Austin), where he joined the [Delta Tau Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Tau_Delta "Delta Tau Delta") fraternity. He began in the fall of 1989 and graduated in the spring of 1993 with a Bachelor of Science in Radio-Television-Film. His original plan changed as he had wanted to attend [Southern Methodist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Methodist_University "Southern Methodist University") until one of his brothers told him that private-school tuition would have been a burden on the family's finances. He had planned to attend law school after graduation from college but discovered he did not have any interest in becoming a lawyer.

## Career

### Early 1990s–2000: Career beginnings

In the early 1990s, McConaughey began working in television commercials. In 1992, he was cast as the boyfriend in the music video for "[Walkaway Joe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walkaway_Joe "Walkaway Joe")", a song by [Trisha Yearwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trisha_Yearwood "Trisha Yearwood") featuring [Don Henley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Henley "Don Henley"). Also that year, he acted in an episode of *[Unsolved Mysteries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unsolved_Mysteries "Unsolved Mysteries")*.

[Bob Balaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Balaban "Bob Balaban")'s [*My Boyfriend's Back*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Boyfriend%27s_Back_(1993_film) "My Boyfriend's Back (1993 film)") premiered on August 6, 1993, where McConaughey made his first big screen appearance as ''Guy 2''. On September 24, [Richard Linklater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Linklater "Richard Linklater")'s [*Dazed and Confused*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dazed_and_Confused_(film) "Dazed and Confused (film)") premiered. McConaughey played Wooderson in a large ensemble cast of actors who would later become stars. He was not originally cast in the film, as the role of Wooderson was originally small and meant to be cast locally for budget purposes. At the time of casting, he was a film student at the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin "University of Texas at Austin") in [Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas") and went out with his girlfriend to the Hyatt hotel bar. He approached casting director [Don Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Phillips_(casting_director) "Don Phillips (casting director)"). Phillips recalls, "The bartender says to him, 'See that guy down there? That's Don Phillips. He cast Sean Penn in *Fast Times*.' And Matthew goes, 'I'm gonna go down and talk to this guy.'" Phillips also recalls that Linklater didn't like McConaughey at first "because he was too handsome". During production, another character named Pickford was meant to be a larger role. Due to the behavior of the actor playing Pickford with other cast members, his screen time was cut in favor of McConaughey's character, Wooderson. Linklater recalled "There was another actor who was kind of the opposite [of McConaughey]. He wasn't really getting along with everybody. I could tell the actors weren't responding to him." Much of the Wooderson role was improvised or written on the spot. *Dazed and Confused* was released on September 24, 1993, in 183 theaters, grossing $918,127 on its opening weekend. It went on to make $7.9 million in North America. The film received positive reviews from critics. The film generally gets favorable reviews. On review aggregator [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), it has a 92% approval rating. The website's critical consensus reads: "Featuring an excellent ensemble cast, a precise feel for the 1970s, and a killer soundtrack, *Dazed and Confused* is a funny, affectionate, and clear-eyed look at high school life." In her review for *[The Austin Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Austin_Chronicle "The Austin Chronicle")*, Marjorie Baumgarten gave particular praise to Matthew McConaughey's performance: "He is a character we're all too familiar with in the movies, but McConaughey nails this guy without a hint of condescension or whimsy, claiming this character for all time as his own".

In 1994, McConaughey acted in *[Angels in the Outfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_in_the_Outfield_(1994_film) "Angels in the Outfield (1994 film)")*, *[The Return of the Texas Chainsaw Massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Texas_Chainsaw_Massacre "The Return of the Texas Chainsaw Massacre")*, and [Daniel Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Johnston "Daniel Johnston")'s music video "Life in Vain". McConaughey acted in [Herbert Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Ross "Herbert Ross")' *[Boys on the Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boys_on_the_Side "Boys on the Side")*, which premiered on February 3, 1995. That year he also acted in a crime thriller, [Brian Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Cox_(director) "Brian Cox (director)")'s *[Scorpion Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scorpion_Spring "Scorpion Spring")*. [John Sayles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Sayles "John Sayles")' [*Lone Star*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_Star_(1996_film) "Lone Star (1996 film)") (1996) is a neo-Western mystery film set in a small town in [South Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Texas "South Texas"). McConaughey is in an ensemble cast that features [Chris Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Cooper "Chris Cooper"), [Kris Kristofferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Kristofferson "Kris Kristofferson"), and [Elizabeth Peña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Pe%C3%B1a "Elizabeth Peña"). McConaughey played the lawyer Jake Brigance in [Joel Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Schumacher "Joel Schumacher")'s *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(1996_film) "A Time to Kill (1996 film)")* which premiered July 24. The film is based on the [John Grisham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Grisham "John Grisham") courtroom crime novel of the [same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(Grisham_novel) "A Time to Kill (Grisham novel)"). In an ensemble piece McConaughey, [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock "Sandra Bullock"), [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson "Samuel L. Jackson"), and [Kevin Spacey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Spacey "Kevin Spacey") share the top billing. On [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") the film has an approval rating of 67%. The critics' consensus reads: "Overlong and superficial, *A Time to Kill* nonetheless succeeds on the strength of its skillful craftsmanship and top-notch performances". In the U.S. it reached number one during its first two weeks and grossed over $108 million domestically, and an additional $43,500,000 was made internationally. At the [MTV Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_%26_TV_Awards "MTV Movie & TV Awards"), McConaughey won Best Breakthrough Performance. *[Larger Than Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larger_than_Life_(film) "Larger than Life (film)")* is a road comedy film starring [Bill Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Murray "Bill Murray") and directed by [Howard Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Franklin "Howard Franklin"); McConaughey played a supporting role. Also that year he acted in [*Glory Daze*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_Daze_(film) "Glory Daze (film)"). McConaughey starred in the science fiction film *[Contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_(1997_American_film) "Contact (1997 American film)")* (1997), directed by [Robert Zemeckis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Zemeckis "Robert Zemeckis"), an adaptation of [Carl Sagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan "Carl Sagan")'s 1985 [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_(novel) "Contact (novel)"); Sagan and his wife [Ann Druyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Druyan "Ann Druyan") wrote the story outline for the film. In the film [Jodie Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Foster "Jodie Foster") portrays the film's protagonist. Also that year, McConaughey starred as then-lawyer [Roger Sherman Baldwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Sherman_Baldwin "Roger Sherman Baldwin") in [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg")'s *[Amistad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amistad_(film) "Amistad (film)")*.

*[The Newton Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Newton_Boys "The Newton Boys")*, co-written and directed by Richard Linklater, was released in 1998. It is based on the true story of the [Newton Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton_Gang "Newton Gang"), a family of bank robbers from [Uvalde, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uvalde,_Texas "Uvalde, Texas"). In 1999, McConaughey acted in *[EDtv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EDtv "EDtv").* Directed by [Ron Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard "Ron Howard"), it was an adaptation of the Quebecois film *[Louis 19, King of the Airwaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_19,_King_of_the_Airwaves "Louis 19, King of the Airwaves") (Louis 19, le roi des ondes)* (1994), The film was a box office bomb, grossing only $35.2 million from an $80 million production budget. In 2000, he starred in *[U-571](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-571_(film) "U-571 (film)")*, a submarine film directed by [Jonathan Mostow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Mostow "Jonathan Mostow").

### 2001–2011: Romantic comedies and professional expansion

By the early 2000s, he was being cast in romantic comedies including *[The Wedding Planner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Planner "The Wedding Planner")* and *[How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Lose_a_Guy_in_10_Days "How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days");* both were successful at the box office. These and others, such as *[Fool's Gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fool%27s_Gold_(2008_film) "Fool's Gold (2008 film)")* (2008), and *[Ghosts of Girlfriends Past](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts_of_Girlfriends_Past "Ghosts of Girlfriends Past")* (2009), established him as a [sex symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_symbol "Sex symbol").

He appeared as a firefighter in a low-budget film, *[Tiptoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiptoes "Tiptoes")* with [Kate Beckinsale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Beckinsale "Kate Beckinsale"), in *[Two for the Money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_for_the_Money_(2005_film) "Two for the Money (2005 film)")* as a protégé to a gambling mogul, [Al Pacino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Pacino "Al Pacino"), and in *[Frailty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frailty_(2001_film) "Frailty (2001 film)")* with [Bill Paxton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Paxton "Bill Paxton") who was also the director. McConaughey acted in the 2005 feature film *[Sahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara_(2005_film) "Sahara (2005 film)")*; [Steve Zahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Zahn "Steve Zahn") and [Penélope Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen%C3%A9lope_Cruz "Penélope Cruz") co-starred. Prior to the release of the film, he promoted it by sailing down the [Amazon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_River "Amazon River") and trekking to [Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mali "Mali"). That same year, McConaughey was named [*People*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)") magazine's "Sexiest Man Alive" for 2005. In 2006 he co-starred with [Sarah Jessica Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Jessica_Parker "Sarah Jessica Parker") in the romantic comedy *[Failure to Launch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Failure_to_Launch "Failure to Launch")* and as Marshall head football coach Jack Lengyel in *[We Are Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Are_Marshall "We Are Marshall")*. McConaughey also provided voice work in an ad campaign for the [Peace Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Corps "Peace Corps") in late 2006. He replaced [Owen Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Wilson "Owen Wilson") in [Ben Stiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Stiller "Ben Stiller")'s *[Tropic Thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropic_Thunder "Tropic Thunder")* after Wilson's suicide attempt. In 2008 McConaughey became the new spokesman for the national radio campaign, ["Beef: It's What's for Dinner"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cattlemen%27s_Beef_Association "National Cattlemen's Beef Association"), replacing [Sam Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Elliott "Sam Elliott").

McConaughey recognized that his "lifestyle, living on the beach, running with my shirt off, doing romantic comedies" had caused him to be typecast for certain roles, and he sought dramatic work with other themes. This shift in his choice of roles has been known as the "McConaissance" between 2011 and 2014. He said:

> I got to feeling like, for a few years, I was doing something that I liked to do with romantic and action comedies. But believe me, I noticed there were other things that were not coming in. And if they were coming in, it was in an independent form with a much smaller paycheck, and nobody really wanting to get behind them ... But I knew I could say no to the things I'd been doing. In saying no to those things, I knew work was going to dry up for awhile ... Year and a half, still nothing. At two years, all of a sudden, in my opinion, I became a new good idea for some good directors.

### 2011–2014: Established actor

In 2012, McConaughey starred alongside [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum "Channing Tatum") in *[Magic Mike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Mike "Magic Mike")*, based on Tatum's early life; it was directed by [Steven Soderbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Soderbergh "Steven Soderbergh"). Also in 2012 came *[Mud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mud_(2012_film) "Mud (2012 film)")*, which gained him praise for his role as a fugitive. He returned to his East Texas roots, working again with director [Richard Linklater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Linklater "Richard Linklater") on *[Bernie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_(2011_film) "Bernie (2011 film)"),* playing district attorney Danny Buck Davidson. In June 2012, McConaughey was invited to join the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences "Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences").

In 2013 he portrayed [Ron Woodroof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Woodroof "Ron Woodroof") in the biographical drama *[Dallas Buyers Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Buyers_Club "Dallas Buyers Club").* The role of a rodeo rider who discovers he has [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS "HIV/AIDS") and struggles to get treatment required him to lose nearly 50 lb (22 kg). The film earned McConaughey numerous accolades, including the [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Leading Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role "Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Leading Role"), the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture_Drama "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Motion Picture Drama"), and the [Academy Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor "Academy Award for Best Actor"). His co-star [Jared Leto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Leto "Jared Leto") won the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") for his role as Rayon, making *Dallas Buyers Club* the first film since *[Mystic River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystic_River_(film) "Mystic River (film)")* (2003) to win both awards. He was featured in [Martin Scorsese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Scorsese "Martin Scorsese")'s *[The Wolf of Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wolf_of_Wall_Street_(2013_film) "The Wolf of Wall Street (2013 film)")* as Mark Hanna, an early boss of [Jordan Belfort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Belfort "Jordan Belfort"). During this time, McConaughey recorded a public service announcement in Austin, Texas for the [LBJ Presidential Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LBJ_Presidential_Library "LBJ Presidential Library").

In April 2014, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine included McConaughey in its annual [Time 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") as one of the "Most Influential People in the World". In August 2014, the [Lincoln Motor Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Motor_Company "Lincoln Motor Company") signed a multi-year collaboration with McConaughey for an ad campaign. The commercials, directed by [Nicolas Winding Refn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Winding_Refn "Nicolas Winding Refn") (*[Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive_(2011_film) "Drive (2011 film)")*), featured McConaughey as a storyteller driving a Lincoln's [MKC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_MKC "Lincoln MKC") crossover. Shortly after the commercials debuted in September 2014, they were parodied by [Ellen DeGeneres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_DeGeneres "Ellen DeGeneres"), [Conan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_O%27Brien "Conan O'Brien"), [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey "Jim Carrey"), *[South Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Park "South Park"),* and *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")*. Overall sales for Lincoln increased by 25 percent one month after the ads debuted. The series of commercials starring McConaughey continued for several more years; during this period he also endorsed the [MKZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_MKZ "Lincoln MKZ") sedan, [MKX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_MKX "Lincoln MKX") and [Nautilus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Nautilus "Lincoln Nautilus") crossovers and [Aviator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Aviator "Lincoln Aviator") SUV.

In 2014, McConaughey received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame"); it is located on 6931 Hollywood Boulevard. Also in the same year, he shared star billing with [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson "Woody Harrelson") in [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO "HBO")'s crime drama anthology series *[True Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective "True Detective").* For his role as [Rust Cohle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust_Cohle "Rust Cohle"), he won the [Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actor in a Drama Series"). He was also nominated for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series"), which he lost to [Bryan Cranston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Cranston "Bryan Cranston") and the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Miniseries or Television Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Miniseries or Television Film"). With his first Oscar win and the critical acclaim received for *True Detective*, "McConaughey seems to be tapping into something essential, remaining himself while stretching, getting older while staying the same age." Critic Rachel Syme dubbed his recognition and performances while taking on more complex, dramatic roles as "The McConaissance". The same year, McConaughey received critical acclaim for playing Cooper, a widowed father and astronaut in [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan "Christopher Nolan")'s epic science fiction film *[Interstellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film) "Interstellar (film)")* (2014).

### 2015–2019: Career fluctuations

After finishing [Gus Van Sant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gus_Van_Sant "Gus Van Sant")'s 2015 film *[The Sea of Trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sea_of_Trees "The Sea of Trees")* with [Ken Watanabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Watanabe "Ken Watanabe"), in 2016, McConaughey starred in two films, *[Free State of Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_of_Jones_(film) "Free State of Jones (film)")* and *[Gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_(2016_film) "Gold (2016 film)")*, and voiced leading characters in two animated films, *[Kubo and the Two Strings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kubo_and_the_Two_Strings "Kubo and the Two Strings")* and *[Sing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing_(2016_American_film) "Sing (2016 American film)")*. In 2016, McConaughey was hired as creative director and celebrity spokesman for [Wild Turkey's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Turkey_(bourbon) "Wild Turkey (bourbon)") latest campaign, to bring in more women and more international customers.

McConaughey starred as [Walter Padick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randall_Flagg "Randall Flagg") in the 2017 [Stephen King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_King "Stephen King") adaptation *[The Dark Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Tower_(2017_film) "The Dark Tower (2017 film)")*, which received negative reactions from most critics. In 2018, he starred in the true life gangster drama *[White Boy Rick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Boy_Rick "White Boy Rick")*, which gained mixed reviews. In 2019, he headlined the erotic thriller *[Serenity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serenity_(2019_film) "Serenity (2019 film)")*, that also starred [Diane Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Lane "Diane Lane") and [Anne Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Hathaway "Anne Hathaway"). The film was panned by both critics and audiences after its release on January 25. McConaughey next had the starring role in [Harmony Korine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmony_Korine "Harmony Korine")'s *[The Beach Bum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beach_Bum "The Beach Bum")*, a comedy also featuring [Zac Efron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Efron "Zac Efron") and [Jonah Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonah_Hill "Jonah Hill"). The film was released on March 29, 2019. In late 2019 McConaughey appeared in the [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie") film *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2019_film) "The Gentlemen (2019 film)"),* playing fictional cannabis baron Mickey Pearson.

### 2020–present: Limited work

In 2020 McConaughey published a memoir, *[Greenlights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenlights_(book) "Greenlights (book)")*. On February 6, 2023, it was announced that he voiced an animated version of [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley") on the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") animated series *[Agent Elvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_Elvis "Agent Elvis")*, released on March 17, 2023. McConaughey returned to feature acting in 2025, starring in the crime film *[The Rivals of Amziah King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rivals_of_Amziah_King "The Rivals of Amziah King")*, which premiered at the [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest "South by Southwest") festival in March to critical acclaim. He is also starring in [Paul Greengrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Greengrass "Paul Greengrass")' survival thriller *[The Lost Bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_Bus "The Lost Bus")* as a bus driver navigating the [2018 Camp Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Fire_(2018) "Camp Fire (2018)"); the film was released on [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") in October 2025. That same year, McConaughey was granted voice and likeness trademarks with the [United States Patent and Trademark Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Patent_and_Trademark_Office "United States Patent and Trademark Office"). His lawyer, [Kevin Yorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Yorn "Kevin Yorn"), stated that the goal was to "protect someone's stealing somebody's voice" with the misuse of artificial intelligence.

## Personal life

McConaughey met [Camila Alves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camila_Alves "Camila Alves") in 2006. He and Alves became engaged on December 25, 2011, and were married in a private Catholic ceremony on June 9, 2012, in [Austin,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas") where they reside. Together, they have three children. Reflecting on having kids, in 2026, he said: "When you have children, that’s the literal form of becoming immortal. They live on, and how much of you can they carry forward." A Christian, McConaughey often speaks publicly about his faith. He attends a non-denominational church. He has stated that he has privately received personal criticism and judgment for his beliefs from some members of [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles "Hollywood, Los Angeles"):

> I have had – and I won't throw any people under the bus – but I have had moments where I was on stage receiving an award in front of my peers in Hollywood, and there were people in the crowd that I have prayed with before dinners many times, and when I thanked God, I saw some of those people go to clap, but then notice that, "bad thing on my resume" and then sit back on their hands.

In 2019, he became a minority owner of [Austin FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_FC "Austin FC"), a team in [Major League Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Soccer "Major League Soccer") which began play in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Major_League_Soccer_season "2021 Major League Soccer season"), with McConaughey as the team's "minister of culture". McConaughey has been a longtime fan of the [Washington Commanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Commanders "Washington Commanders") football team.

In 2023, McConaughey stated that he and longtime friend and *True Detective* co-star/co-executive producer [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson "Woody Harrelson") could be half-brothers. His mother claimed to have been intimate with Harrelson's father, [Charles Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Harrelson "Charles Harrelson"), around the time of McConaughey's conception. In September 2023, [Madame Tussauds New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madame_Tussauds_New_York "Madame Tussauds New York") unveiled a new wax figure of Matthew McConaughey, inspired by his 2021 appearance on *[The Tonight Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")*. The event, featured during McConaughey's visit to *[The View](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_View_(talk_show) "The View (talk show)")*, promoted his children's book, *Just Because*. In September 2025, he published another book, *[Poems & Prayers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poems_%26_Prayers "Poems & Prayers")*. McConaughey and his wife became the godparents of [Princess Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Cruises "Princess Cruises") ship [Star Princess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Princess_(2024) "Star Princess (2024)") in the christening ceremony in [Fort Lauderdale, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Lauderdale,_Florida "Fort Lauderdale, Florida") on November 7, 2025.

## Politics

In a November 2020 appearance on *[The Late Show with Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Show_with_Stephen_Colbert "The Late Show with Stephen Colbert")*, McConaughey denied he was interested in running for governor. *[The Texas Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Texas_Tribune "The Texas Tribune")* reported on McConaughey's lack of involvement in politics, saying that he had not voted in a primary race since "at least" 2012 and had never donated to a political campaign at the state or federal level up through 2021. He had voted in the [2018 Texas elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Texas_elections "2018 Texas elections") and the [2020 United States elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_elections "2020 United States elections").

In March 2021, McConaughey confirmed that he was considering running in the [2022 Texas gubernatorial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Texas_gubernatorial_election "2022 Texas gubernatorial election"). In an October 2021 [Twitter Spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter_Spaces "Twitter Spaces") interview with [NPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NPR "NPR"), McConaughey was asked if he was going to run for [governor of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Texas "Governor of Texas"). He replied, "I am not – until I am." When asked questions about political issues, such as voting rights and abortion, McConaughey opted to remain "purposely vague", and he did not disclose his political party. Just over two weeks before the Texas primary candidate filing deadline, McConaughey released a video on his official Twitter profile stating that he would not be competing for the office.

In June 2022, McConaughey joined the White House press briefing and advocated for "commonsense gun laws" during a 20-minute speech, in which he spoke about the [Uvalde school shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robb_Elementary_School_shooting "Robb Elementary School shooting"), which occurred in his hometown of Uvalde. He said, "We need responsible gun ownership. We need background checks. We need to raise the minimum age to purchase an AR-15 rifle to 21. We need a waiting period for those rifles. We need red flag laws and consequences for those who abuse them." [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN "CNN") described the speech as "impassioned and at-times emotional". In July 2024, McConaughey spoke at the annual [National Governors Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Governors_Association "National Governors Association") meeting. He reiterated his interest in running for political office and said he has been "on a learning tour".

## Philanthropy

McConaughey started the "just keep livin foundation", which is "dedicated to helping teenage kids lead active lives and make healthy choices to become great men and women". On February 25, 2016, McConaughey received the Creative Conscience award from [unite4:humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unite4:humanity "Unite4:humanity") for his work with his foundation. In 2019, McConaughey officially became a professor of practice for the Department of Radio-Television-Film at the [Moody College of Communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moody_College_of_Communication "Moody College of Communication") at his *alma mater*, UT-Austin; he had served as a visiting instructor since 2015. The first two sessions were about the filming of the movie *[Free State of Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_of_Jones_(film) "Free State of Jones (film)")*.
