# Chad Gilbert

**Chad Everett Gilbert** (March 9, 1981 – September 20, 2026) was an American guitarist, record producer and songwriter. He was a founding member of the [punk rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk_rock "Punk rock") band [New Found Glory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Found_Glory "New Found Glory"), for whom he played lead guitar and sang backing vocals. He was also the lead vocalist for the band's now-defunct side-project [International Superheroes of Hardcore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Superheroes_of_Hardcore "International Superheroes of Hardcore"). Additionally, he was the vocalist for the [hardcore punk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardcore_punk "Hardcore punk") band [Shai Hulud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shai_Hulud_(band) "Shai Hulud (band)") from 1995 to 1998 and 2012 to 2013.

Gilbert produced several records, notably [H2O](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H2O_(American_Band) "H2O (American Band)")'s *[Nothing to Prove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing_to_Prove_(H2O_album) "Nothing to Prove (H2O album)")* and [A Day to Remember](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Day_to_Remember "A Day to Remember")'s albums *[Homesick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homesick_(A_Day_to_Remember_album) "Homesick (A Day to Remember album)")*, *[What Separates Me from You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_Separates_Me_from_You "What Separates Me from You")* and *[Common Courtesy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Courtesy_(album) "Common Courtesy (album)")*. In 2010, he announced he would release solo material online, free of charge, and released several demos and 7" vinyl records under the name *What's Eating Gilbert*.

## Early life

Chad Everett Gilbert was born in [Coral Springs, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral_Springs,_Florida "Coral Springs, Florida"), on March 9, 1981. He attended [J. P. Taravella High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._P._Taravella_High_School "J. P. Taravella High School"), but left after the 11th grade when [New Found Glory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Found_Glory "New Found Glory") signed with the California-based record label [Drive-Thru Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive-Thru_Records "Drive-Thru Records").

## Personal life

Gilbert married [Sherri DuPree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherri_DuPree "Sherri DuPree") of the rock band [Eisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisley "Eisley") in February 2007, and they divorced later that year. He married [Paramore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramore "Paramore")'s lead vocalist and keyboardist [Hayley Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayley_Williams "Hayley Williams") in February 2016, and they divorced in 2017 after infidelity on Gilbert's part. Gilbert married Lisa Cimorelli, member of the all-sisters singing group [Cimorelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cimorelli "Cimorelli"), on October 3, 2020. They had a daughter born in 2021.

### Health and death

On January 26, 2010, Gilbert reported that "suspicious cells" had been found in his [thyroid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyroid "Thyroid") and that he would have half of it surgically removed. Four days later, he posted on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(social_network) "X (social network)") that the surgery had been a success and no cancer had been found.

On December 5, 2021, Gilbert was hospitalized after his wife found him unresponsive. While hospitalized, he was diagnosed with a [pheochromocytoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pheochromocytoma "Pheochromocytoma"), a rare and usually benign neuroendocrine tumor. Further diagnostics revealed Gilbert's tumor was malignant and on December 13, 2021 he had surgery to have the malignancy removed. On August 15, 2022, he reported that he had been diagnosed with cancer again and would undergo surgery the next day after a paraganglioma, another type of neuroendocrine tumor, was discovered in his spine. He announced on February 11, 2023, that he would begin intense [chemotherapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemotherapy "Chemotherapy") after doctors found evidence of metastasis to his lungs.

On March 23, 2026, Gilbert said he had undergone emergency brain surgery after three tumors were discovered. In April 2026, he reported that his diagnosis had changed from metastatic pheochromocytoma to stage 4 [adrenocortical carcinoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrenocortical_carcinoma "Adrenocortical carcinoma"). Gilbert died in his sleep surrounded by his family on September 20, 2026, aged 45.
