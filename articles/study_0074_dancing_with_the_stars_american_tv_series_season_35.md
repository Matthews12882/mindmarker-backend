# Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 35

**Season thirty-five** of *[Dancing with the Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series) "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series)")* premiered on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") and [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%2B "Disney+") on September 15, 2026, and is scheduled to conclude on November 24, 2026. This season was the fourth to air live on both networks simultaneously. [Season nineteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_19 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 19") champion [Alfonso Ribeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Ribeiro "Alfonso Ribeiro") returned to host the installment, while former professional dancer and judge [Julianne Hough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Hough "Julianne Hough") returned as co-host.

## Cast

### Couples

On April 22, 2026, during an [upfront](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upfront_(advertising) "Upfront (advertising)") for [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu")'s unscripted programming, [Ciara Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciara_Miller "Ciara Miller") and [Maura Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maura_Higgins "Maura Higgins") were announced as the first celebrity participants for the thirty-fifth season. [Jackson Olson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Olson "Jackson Olson") joined the cast three weeks later during an upfront for [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company "The Walt Disney Company")'s original programming. On June 17, [TMZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMZ "TMZ") reported that [Guillermo Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Rodriguez_(comedian) "Guillermo Rodriguez (comedian)") joined the cast; [Jimmy Kimmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel "Jimmy Kimmel") confirmed Rodriguez's appearance later that day. Throughout August, [Tyler Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_Cameron "Tyler Cameron"), [Jenna Dewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Dewan "Jenna Dewan"), [Julia Stiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Stiles "Julia Stiles"), [Tatyana Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatyana_Ali "Tatyana Ali"), [Sarah Jane Nader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Thy_Nader "Love Thy Nader") and [Conner Leavitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Secret_Lives_of_Mormon_Wives "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives") were named to the cast. Nader is the younger sister of [season thirty-three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_33 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 33") contestant [Brooks Nader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooks_Nader "Brooks Nader"), and Leavitt is the husband of [season thirty-four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_34 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 34") semi-finalist [Whitney Leavitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Leavitt "Whitney Leavitt"). [Amber Glenn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Glenn "Amber Glenn") and [Ezra Frech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezra_Frech "Ezra Frech") became the first [Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Olympic_Games "Winter Olympic Games") and [Paralympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paralympic_Games "Paralympic Games") champions to compete during the same season.

The first celebrity-professional dancer pairing, Rodriguez and the reigning champion [Witney Carson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witney_Carson "Witney Carson"), was announced on *[Good Morning America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Morning_America "Good Morning America")* on August 17, 2026. At the time of the announcement, Carson was four months pregnant with her third child. She became the first pro to enter an installment of *Dancing with the Stars* while pregnant, and the first to compete that far into her second trimester. [Gleb Savchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gleb_Savchenko "Gleb Savchenko") and [Sasha Farber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasha_Farber "Sasha Farber") did not return as pros this season, although Farber was still involved in other capacities. After a five-year hiatus, [Sharna Burgess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharna_Burgess "Sharna Burgess") returned to the lineup on August 24. [Adele Zaikman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele_Zaikman "Adele Zaikman"), winner of *[Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars:_The_Next_Pro "Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro")*, joined the series as a first-time pro on August 31. She is the first [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis "Israelis") professional dancer to be featured on the series. Season thirty-four troupe member [Hailey Bills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailey_Bills "Hailey Bills") was promoted to a first-time pro. Her pairing with Nader marked the third same-sex partnership in the series' history, following [JoJo Siwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JoJo_Siwa "JoJo Siwa") & [Jenna Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Johnson_(dancer) "Jenna Johnson (dancer)") from [season thirty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_30 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 30") and [Shangela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangela "Shangela") & Savchenko from [season thirty-one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_31 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 31").

The full roster of celebrity participants and their partnerships was revealed on *Good Morning America* on September 2. With sixteen couples, this season is tied with [season nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_9 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 9") and season thirty-one as having the largest cast in the show's history.

### Hosts and judges

The hosts and judges for the season were announced on *Good Morning America* on September 1, 2026, one day before the cast reveal. [Alfonso Ribeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Ribeiro "Alfonso Ribeiro") and [Julianne Hough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Hough "Julianne Hough") returned as hosts, while [Derek Hough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_Hough "Derek Hough"), [Carrie Ann Inaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Ann_Inaba "Carrie Ann Inaba"), and [Bruno Tonioli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Tonioli "Bruno Tonioli") returned as judges. [Joe Buck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Buck "Joe Buck") served as the guest judge for Super Bowl Night. [Kym Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kym_Johnson "Kym Johnson") will serve as the guest judge for Dedication Night.

The second season of the *Dancing with the Stars* podcast, hosted by season thirty-four quarter-finalist [Andy Richter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Richter "Andy Richter"), launched on September 10. An official *Dancing with the Stars* [aftershow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftershow "Aftershow"), hosted by season thirty-four champion [Robert Irwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Irwin "Robert Irwin"), aired live immediately after the second night premiere episode on September 16.

Ritcher revealed the majority of the season's themes during the second episode of the *Dancing with the Stars* podcast. He did not not reveal the theme for the fifth week of competition; Super Bowl Night was announced on *Good Morning America* on September 30. Throughout the season, Richter shared the songs and dance styles for the upcoming week. The songs and dance styles for Dedication Night were exclusively revealed by *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* magazine on October 14.

### Dance troupe

[Kamri Peterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamri_Peterson "Kamri Peterson"), a former pro on *[Dancing with the Stars: Juniors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars:_Juniors "Dancing with the Stars: Juniors")*, was announced as a first-time troupe member on August 2, 2026, during the series' inaugural [fan convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)#Fan_convention "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series)"). Jaxon Willard, Carter Williams, and Onye Stevenson were announced as second-year troupe members on September 4.

### Week 1: Premiere

For the two-night season premiere, each couple performed one unlearned dance to a song that topped one of *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")* magazine's [music charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_charts "Billboard charts") during the year each celebrity was born. The male celebrities performed on the first night and the female celebrities performed on the second night. One couple was eliminated at the end of each night.

Night 1 (Men)

The opening number, choreographed by [Jamal Sims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Sims "Jamal Sims"), was set to "[Dancing On My Own](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_On_My_Own "Dancing On My Own")" by [Robyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robyn "Robyn"). The troupe performed a [bumper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumper_(broadcasting) "Bumper (broadcasting)") to "[Bangaranga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangaranga "Bangaranga")" by [Dara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_(Bulgarian_singer) "Dara (Bulgarian singer)"). Due to technical difficulties, online voting for the night was thrown out and only votes cast via [text messaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_messaging "Text messaging") were accepted. The female celebrities and their professional partners performed a routine to "[Cha Cha Cha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Romantic_(album) "The Romantic (album)")" by [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars "Bruno Mars"), choreographed by [Brandon Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Armstrong_(dancer) "Brandon Armstrong (dancer)") and [Jan Ravnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Ravnik "Jan Ravnik"). [Adele Zaikman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele_Zaikman "Adele Zaikman"), Allen Genkin, Erik Linder, and Selena Hamilton from *[Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars:_The_Next_Pro "Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro")* performed a [salsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salsa_(dance) "Salsa (dance)") bumper to "[Dai Dai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dai_Dai "Dai Dai")" by [Shakira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakira "Shakira") and [Burna Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burna_Boy "Burna Boy"). Inspired by [Paris Baguette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Baguette "Paris Baguette"), one of the season's sponsors, co-host [Julianne Hough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Hough "Julianne Hough") and Tessandra Chavez choreographed a number to "[I Love Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Love_Paris "I Love Paris")" by [Tatiana Eva-Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatiana_Eva-Marie "Tatiana Eva-Marie") and the Avalon Band; Hough additionally performed the routine with the pros.

### Week 5: Super Bowl Night

*Individual judges' scores in the charts below (given in parentheses) are listed in this order from left to right: Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough, [Joe Buck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Buck "Joe Buck"), Bruno Tonioli.*

To commemorate [Super Bowl LXI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LXI "Super Bowl LXI"), each couple performed one unlearned dance to songs that were performed in various [Super Bowl halftime shows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Super_Bowl_halftime_shows "List of Super Bowl halftime shows"). *[Monday Night Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monday_Night_Football "Monday Night Football")* play-by-play commentator [Joe Buck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Buck "Joe Buck") appeared as a guest judge. The opening number, choreographed by [Jamal Sims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Sims "Jamal Sims"), was set to "[Nuevayol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuevayol "Nuevayol")" by [Bad Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Bunny "Bad Bunny"), "[Rude Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rude_Boy_(Rihanna_song) "Rude Boy (Rihanna song)")" by [Rihanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rihanna "Rihanna"), and "[Humble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humble_(song) "Humble (song)")" by [Kendrick Lamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendrick_Lamar "Kendrick Lamar"). ### Week 6: Dedication Night

*Individual judges' scores in the charts below (given in parentheses) are listed in this order from left to right: Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough, [Kym Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kym_Johnson "Kym Johnson"), Bruno Tonioli.*

Each couple will perform one unlearned dance to honor the most influential figures and institutions in the celebrities' lives. In a continuation from the [previous season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_34 "Dancing with the Stars (American TV series) season 34"), the figures selected by each celebrity will be incorporated into their routines. Two-time mirrorball champion [Kym Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kym_Johnson "Kym Johnson") will return as a guest judge for the second consecutive year.
