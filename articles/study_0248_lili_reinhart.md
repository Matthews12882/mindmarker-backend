# Lili Reinhart

**Lili Pauline Reinhart** (born September 13, 1996) is an American actress. She portrayed [Betty Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Cooper "Betty Cooper") on [the CW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW "The CW") teen drama series *[Riverdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverdale_(American_TV_series) "Riverdale (American TV series)")* (2017–2023) earning 7 [Teen Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Choice_Awards "Teen Choice Awards") as well as nominations for a [Critics' Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Award "Critics' Choice Award") and three [People's Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Choice_Awards "People's Choice Awards").

Reinhart early film work includes supporting roles in *[The Kings of Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kings_of_Summer "The Kings of Summer")* (2013), *[Miss Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Stevens "Miss Stevens")* (2016), and *[Hustlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hustlers_(film) "Hustlers (film)")* (2019). She has since taken leading roles in the romance films *[Chemical Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Hearts "Chemical Hearts")* (2020), *[Look Both Ways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_Both_Ways_(2022_film) "Look Both Ways (2022 film)")* (2022), and *[The Love Hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Love_Hypothesis_(film) "The Love Hypothesis (film)")* (2026) as well as the crime drama *[American Sweatshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Sweatshop "American Sweatshop")* (2025) and the comedy-drama miniseries *[Hal & Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hal_%26_Harper "Hal & Harper")* (2025), all of which she also served as an executive producer.

## Early life

Reinhart was born in [Cleveland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland "Cleveland"), Ohio, to Amy and Daniel Reinhart, and raised in the nearby town of [Bay Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_Village,_Ohio "Bay Village, Ohio"). She is of German and French descent and has stated [her surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinhard "Reinhard") is of German origin. Reinhart is the middle of three sisters. She developed a love for singing, acting, and dancing at the age of 10, and asked her mother to drive her to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") for auditions. Reinhart moved to [Charlotte, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte,_North_Carolina "Charlotte, North Carolina"), when she was 16 years old. Reinhart moved to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") when she was 18 years old to pursue acting and after five months, she returned home to [Charlotte, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte,_North_Carolina "Charlotte, North Carolina"), and worked with a therapist.

## Career

### 2010–2016: Early roles

Reinhart starred in the television pilot for *[Scientastic!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientastic! "Scientastic!")* (2010) and guest starred on the television series *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit")* (2011). She also starred in the films *Lilith* (2011), *[Not Waving But Drowning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_Waving_But_Drowning_(2012_film) "Not Waving But Drowning (2012 film)")* (2012), *[The Kings of Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kings_of_Summer "The Kings of Summer")* (2013), *Gibsonburg* (2013), *[Forever's End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forever%27s_End "Forever's End")* (2013), *[Miss Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Stevens "Miss Stevens")* (2016), and *[The Good Neighbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Neighbor_(2016_film) "The Good Neighbor (2016 film)")* (2016).

### 2017–2023: Breakthrough with *Riverdale*

On February 9, 2016, Reinhart was cast as [Betty Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Cooper "Betty Cooper") in [the CW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW "The CW")'s [teen drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_drama "Teen drama") television series *[Riverdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverdale_(2017_TV_series) "Riverdale (2017 TV series)")*, based on the characters of [Archie Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie_Comics "Archie Comics"). Series creator [Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Aguirre-Sacasa "Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa") originally sought out [Kiernan Shipka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiernan_Shipka "Kiernan Shipka") for the role after seeing her work in *[Mad Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Men "Mad Men")*. Reinhart auditioned twice for the role, first sending in an audition tape from her North Carolina home before appearing in person a month later. The series premiered on January 26, 2017, and in February 2021, it was renewed for a sixth season. Previously, Reinhart worked on the [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company "Fox Broadcasting Company") sitcom *[Surviving Jack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surviving_Jack "Surviving Jack")*. In 2017, Reinhart was cast in *[Galveston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galveston_(film) "Galveston (film)")*, based on [Nic Pizzolatto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Pizzolatto "Nic Pizzolatto")'s 2010 novel of the same name. In the film, directed by [Mélanie Laurent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9lanie_Laurent "Mélanie Laurent"), Reinhart plays Tiffany, who was placed for adoption as a child. The film premiered at [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest "South by Southwest") on March 10, 2018, and received a [limited theatrical release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_theatrical_release "Limited theatrical release") through [RLJE Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RLJE_Films "RLJE Films") on October 19, 2018. *Galveston* received mixed critical reviews and grossed only $190,320 at the box office.

Reinhart joined the cast of [Lorene Scafaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorene_Scafaria "Lorene Scafaria")'s *[Hustlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hustlers_(film) "Hustlers (film)")* in March 2019. Reinhart played Annabelle, "the baby of the group," a former [stripper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripper "Stripper") who joins Ramona and her crew in drugging and robbing their [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street "Wall Street") clientele. Reinhart described her character's aesthetic as "slutty [Hannah Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Montana "Hannah Montana")." A running gag shows Annabelle vomiting in times of stress, and the fake vomit was made of [animal crackers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_cracker "Animal cracker") and [Sprite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprite_(drink) "Sprite (drink)"). *Hustlers* premiered at the [Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival "Toronto International Film Festival") on September 7, 2019, and was theatrically released in the United States the next week. The film received positive critical reviews and grossed $157.6 million worldwide. The same year, Reinhart made a [cameo appearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameo_appearance "Cameo appearance") during the [mid-credits scene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-credits_scene "Post-credits scene") of [Elizabeth Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks "Elizabeth Banks")' *[Charlie's Angels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie%27s_Angels_(2019_film) "Charlie's Angels (2019 film)")* (2019).

In June 2019, it was announced that Reinhart would star as the female lead of [Amazon Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_MGM_Studios "Amazon MGM Studios")' romantic drama film *[Chemical Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Hearts "Chemical Hearts")*, written and directed by [Richard Tanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Tanne "Richard Tanne") and based on the 2016 [Krystal Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krystal_Sutherland "Krystal Sutherland") novel *[Our Chemical Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Chemical_Hearts "Our Chemical Hearts")*. Reinhart played Grace Town, a physically disabled high school senior who falls in love with school newspaper writer Henry Page. In addition to starring, *Chemical Hearts* was Reinhart's first [executive producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_producer "Executive producer") credit. The film was released on August 21, 2020, on [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") and received mixed reviews.

In May 2020, Reinhart voiced Bella-Ella, "a ... snooty rich [girl]" in the *[Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons "The Simpsons")* episode "[The Hateful Eight-Year-Olds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hateful_Eight-Year-Olds "The Hateful Eight-Year-Olds")". In March 2021, it was announced that Reinhart would star in [Wanuri Kahiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanuri_Kahiu "Wanuri Kahiu")'s *[Look Both Ways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_Both_Ways_(2022_film) "Look Both Ways (2022 film)")* as Natalie, a college student whose life diverges into two parallel realities on the eve of her college graduation. In one reality, she becomes pregnant and must navigate motherhood as a young adult; in the other, she moves to LA to pursue her career. In addition to starring in the film, *Look Both Ways* was Reinhart's second executive producer credit.

In June 2021, Reinhart's production company, Small Victory Productions, signed a deal with Amazon Studios. Small Victory Productions will develop original and adapted content for film and television with a focus on modern, young adult content that celebrates diversity and inclusivity, as well as supporting new voices.

### 2024–present

In 2025, she acted in and executive produced the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") thriller *[American Sweatshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Sweatshop "American Sweatshop")* which debuted on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). The film received positive reviews with critics praising her performance including Brian Tallerico of *[Roger Ebert.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert "Roger Ebert")* who wrote that he was "impressed by the choices made by Reinhart" in a dramatic role. That same year, she also executive produced and starred opposite [Cooper Raiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_Raiff "Cooper Raiff") and [Mark Ruffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ruffalo "Mark Ruffalo") in the independent dramedy miniseries *[Hal & Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hal_%26_Harper "Hal & Harper")* (2025) which premiered at the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival") and debuted on [Mubi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mubi_(streaming_service) "Mubi (streaming service)"). Ben Travers, television critic for *[IndieWire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndieWire "IndieWire")* highlighted Reinhart's performance among the cast as being "stellar". She received a nomination for the [Gotham Award for Outstanding Lead Performance in a Limited or Anthology Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Gotham_TV_Awards "3rd Gotham TV Awards").

## Other ventures

In October 2019, Reinhart was announced as an ambassador for [American Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Express "American Express") for the relaunch of its AmexGreen Card. That same month, Reinhart became a spokesperson and ambassador for [CoverGirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoverGirl "CoverGirl").

In September 2020, Reinhart released a book of poetry titled *Swimming Lessons: Poems*. The book explores themes such as "young love, anxiety, depression, fame, and heartbreak". For the week of October 18, 2020, *Swimming Lessons: Poems* debuted at No. 2 on [*The New York Times* Best Seller list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Best_Seller_list "The New York Times Best Seller list") for paperback trade fiction. She launched a skincare line called Personal Day in 2024.

## Personal life

Reinhart practices [Transcendental Meditation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendental_Meditation "Transcendental Meditation") and is training to be a [reiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reiki "Reiki") healer. In June 2020, she [came out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_out "Coming out") as bisexual. In an [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") story, she stated that she would be attending an [LGBTQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ "LGBTQ") for [Black Lives Matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Lives_Matter "Black Lives Matter") protest in Los Angeles.

Reinhart was diagnosed with [depression at age 14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depression_in_childhood_and_adolescence "Depression in childhood and adolescence"). She has also opened up about her experiences with anxiety and [body dysmorphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_dysmorphia "Body dysmorphia"). In January 2024, she was diagnosed with [alopecia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alopecia "Alopecia"). In 2025, she revealed that she was on [antidepressants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antidepressant "Antidepressant") and struggling with [attention deficit hyperactivity disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder "Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder"), severe [premenstrual dysphoric disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premenstrual_dysphoric_disorder "Premenstrual dysphoric disorder"), and [endometriosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endometriosis "Endometriosis").

In 2026, Reinhart sparked online debate and viral reactions when she attended a Q&A event for the film *[Forbidden Fruits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_Fruits "Forbidden Fruits")* wearing a white [tank top](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeveless_shirt "Sleeveless shirt") emblazoned with the phrase "I support a man's right to shut the fuck up". The fashion magazine *[Marie Claire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Claire "Marie Claire")* defended "her right to wear this tank top whenever she wants", and the fashion magazine *[L'Officiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Officiel "L'Officiel")* praised the clarity and consistency of the outfit.
