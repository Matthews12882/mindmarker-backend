# Avengers: Doomsday

***Avengers: Doomsday*** is an upcoming American [superhero film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhero_film "Superhero film") based on the [Marvel Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Comics "Marvel Comics") superhero team the [Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(comics) "Avengers (comics)"). Produced by [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios "Marvel Studios") and distributed by [Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Studios_Motion_Pictures "Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures"), it is intended to be the sequel to *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")* (2019) and [the 39th film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films "List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films") in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") (MCU). Directed by [Anthony and Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_and_Joe_Russo "Anthony and Joe Russo"), and written by [Michael Waldron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Waldron "Michael Waldron"), [Stephen McFeely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_McFeely "Stephen McFeely"), and the writing team of [Chris McKenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_McKenna_(writer) "Chris McKenna (writer)") and [Erik Sommers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Sommers "Erik Sommers"), the film features an [ensemble cast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ensemble_cast "Ensemble cast") led by [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr. "Robert Downey Jr.") as [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_von_Doom_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Victor von Doom (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"). In the film, the [Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), [Wakandans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakanda_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Wakanda (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), [New Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "New Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), [Fantastic Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Four_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Fantastic Four (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), and the [X-Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "X-Men (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") converge from different universes to face Doom amidst the collapse of the [multiverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiverse_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Multiverse (Marvel Cinematic Universe)").

Two new *Avengers* films, *The Kang Dynasty* and *[Secret Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Secret_Wars "Avengers: Secret Wars")*, were announced in July 2022 as the conclusion of the MCU's [Phase Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Six "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Six") and "[The Multiverse Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Multiverse_Saga "The Multiverse Saga")". [Jonathan Majors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Majors "Jonathan Majors") was set to reprise his MCU role as the villain [Kang the Conqueror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kang_the_Conqueror_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Kang the Conqueror (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), with [Destin Daniel Cretton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destin_Daniel_Cretton "Destin Daniel Cretton") and [Jeff Loveness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Loveness "Jeff Loveness") hired to direct and write *The Kang Dynasty*, respectively. In November 2023, Cretton departed, Waldron replaced Loveness as writer, and Marvel was considering moving away from the Kang storyline, in part due to [Majors's legal issues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Majors#Legal_issues "Jonathan Majors"); Majors was fired the following month. The return of the Russo brothers as directors and McFeely as co-writer, the casting of Downey as new villain Doctor Doom, and the new subtitle *Doomsday* were all announced in July 2024. The large cast features actors from previous MCU media and [20th Century Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Century_Fox "20th Century Fox")'s [*X-Men* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_(film_series) "X-Men (film series)"), using the idea of colliding universes to bring together actors from different franchises. Filming took place from April to September 2025 at [Pinewood Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinewood_Studios "Pinewood Studios") in England, with location filming in England and [Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain "Bahrain").

*Avengers: Doomsday* is scheduled to be released in the United States on December 18, 2026, as part of Phase Six of the MCU. The sequel, *Avengers: Secret Wars*, is set to be released on December 17, 2027.

## Premise

Fourteen months after the events of *[Thunderbolts\*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunderbolts* "Thunderbolts*")* (2025), amidst the collapse of the [multiverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiverse_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Multiverse (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), groups of heroes from three different universes—the [Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), [Wakandans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakanda_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Wakanda (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), and [New Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "New Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") from [Earth-616](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-616_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Earth-616 (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"); the [Fantastic Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Four_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Fantastic Four (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") from [Earth-828](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-828 "Earth-828"); and the [X-Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "X-Men (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") from [Earth-10005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-10005 "Earth-10005")—converge to face the existential threat of [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_von_Doom_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Victor von Doom (Marvel Cinematic Universe)").

## Cast

* [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr. "Robert Downey Jr.") as [Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_von_Doom_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Victor von Doom (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"):
  Directors [Anthony and Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_and_Joe_Russo "Anthony and Joe Russo") said Doom was one of the most complex comic book characters, and they thought Downey—who previously starred as [Tony Stark / Iron Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Tony Stark (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") (MCU)—was the one person who could take on the role. They found Doom challenging because of his "immense" power, but they also felt free to explore "the complexities and the vulnerabilities"; Joe said Doom hits the "sweet spot" of balancing original elements for their story with "the most awesome things about Doom in the comics". Downey developed his own backstory for Doom as reference for his performance, and also contributed costume ideas. He speaks in a "heavily accented" voice.
* [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor) "Chris Evans (actor)") as [Steve Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Rogers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Steve Rogers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"):
  The former leader of the [Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), a [super soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_soldier "Super soldier"), and [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II") veteran who was the first person to carry the [Captain America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_America "Captain America") mantle. After his "definitive ending" in that film, Evans said he would only return to the MCU if there was a good reason. He believed that the story for *Doomsday* had a "very real reason that these heroes need Steve Rogers ‌now". Evans and Mackie indicated that Rogers would take on the [Nomad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomad_(Marvel_Comics) "Nomad (Marvel Comics)") mantle, similar to the comics.

Actors reported to appear include [Benedict Cumberbatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benedict_Cumberbatch "Benedict Cumberbatch") as Dr. [Stephen Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Strange_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Stephen Strange (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), and [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland "Tom Holland") as [Peter Parker / Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Peter Parker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)").[*[additional citation(s) needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Following conflicting reports on whether [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds "Ryan Reynolds") would appear in the film, he and [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman "Hugh Jackman") made promotional appearances for *Doomsday* which some saw as confirmation that they would be in it. Reynolds and Jackman portrayed [Wade Wilson / Deadpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade_Wilson_(film_character) "Wade Wilson (film character)") and [Logan / Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_(film_character) "Logan (film character)"), respectively, in the *X-Men* films and *Deadpool & Wolverine*.

## Production

After directing the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") (MCU) [crossover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossover_(fiction) "Crossover (fiction)") films *[Avengers: Infinity War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Infinity_War "Avengers: Infinity War")* (2018) and *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame "Avengers: Endgame")* (2019), [Anthony and Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_and_Joe_Russo "Anthony and Joe Russo") expressed interest in directing a *Secret Wars* film based on the [1984–85 comic book crossover event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_Wars "Secret Wars") written by [Jim Shooter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Shooter "Jim Shooter") and the [2015–16 comic book storyline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_Wars_(2015_comic_book) "Secret Wars (2015 comic book)") written by [Jonathan Hickman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Hickman "Jonathan Hickman"). Both follow various Marvel characters who converge on the planet [Battleworld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battleworld "Battleworld"). *Infinity War* and *Endgame* writers [Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Markus_and_Stephen_McFeely "Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely") said they would return to write such a film if the Russo brothers were directing it.

At [San Diego Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con "San Diego Comic-Con") (SDCC) in July 2022, [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios "Marvel Studios") president [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige "Kevin Feige") announced the films *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty* and *Avengers: Secret Wars*, to be released on May 2, 2025, and November 7, 2025, respectively. They were set to conclude "[The Multiverse Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Multiverse_Saga "The Multiverse Saga")", which covers [Phases Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Four "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Four"), [Five](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Five "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Five"), and [Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Six "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Six") of the MCU. *The Kang Dynasty* was inspired by a [2001–02 comic book storyline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kang_Dynasty "Kang Dynasty") written by [Kurt Busiek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Busiek "Kurt Busiek") in which [Kang the Conqueror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kang_the_Conqueror "Kang the Conqueror") travels through time to enslave humanity. [Jonathan Majors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Majors "Jonathan Majors") was cast as [Kang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kang_the_Conqueror_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Kang the Conqueror (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") for several Phase Four projects, and was confirmed to be starring in both new *Avengers* films. [Destin Daniel Cretton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destin_Daniel_Cretton "Destin Daniel Cretton") was hired to direct *The Kang Dynasty*, with [Jeff Loveness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Loveness "Jeff Loveness") writing the script, and [Michael Waldron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Waldron "Michael Waldron") writing *Secret Wars*. Following [Majors's arrest in March 2023 and subsequent assault allegations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Majors#2023_assault "Jonathan Majors"), reports and speculation emerged about his future in the MCU. Loveness was no longer involved by the start of the [2023 Writers Guild of America strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Writers_Guild_of_America_strike "2023 Writers Guild of America strike") in May, reportedly because Marvel was "moving away" from the Kang storyline. In June, the release dates for *The Kang Dynasty* and *Secret Wars* were pushed back to May 1, 2026, and May 7, 2027, respectively, in part due to the scripts not being ready before the strike. Cretton stepped down as director of *The Kang Dynasty* in November 2023, and Waldron was set to rewrite the film. The next month, Majors was fired by Disney and Marvel Studios after he was found guilty of harassment and reckless assault in the third degree. The studio had started referring to *The Kang Dynasty* internally as *Avengers 5*. By February 2024, both *Avengers 5* and *Secret Wars* were being rewritten to minimize Kang's role or remove him entirely.

[Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy "Shawn Levy") became the studio's top choice to direct *Avengers 5* by mid-March 2024, after he directed the MCU film *[Deadpool & Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool_%26_Wolverine "Deadpool & Wolverine")* (2024), but there were potential scheduling issues with his other projects. By that May, Marvel Studios had approached [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr. "Robert Downey Jr.")—who starred as [Tony Stark / Iron Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Tony Stark (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in "[The Infinity Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Infinity_Saga "The Infinity Saga")", covering the MCU's [Phases One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_One "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase One"), [Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Two "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Two"), and [Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Three "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Three")—about returning to the MCU to take on the new character of [Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_von_Doom_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Victor von Doom (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"). Downey said he would only return if the Russo brothers also returned as directors. After McFeely approached the Russos with an idea for the films that excited them, they entered early talks to direct both *Avengers 5* and *Secret Wars* by mid-July. At SDCC at the end of the month, the Russos were confirmed to be directing the two films. They revealed that *Avengers 5* was now titled *Avengers: Doomsday*, and both films would feature Downey as Doctor Doom. McFeely had joined as writer for both films, working with Waldron. [Chris McKenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_McKenna_(writer) "Chris McKenna (writer)") and [Erik Sommers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Sommers "Erik Sommers"), writers on the [MCU *Spider-Man* films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_(2017_film_series) "Spider-Man (2017 film series)"), also worked on the scripts for *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*. Marvel Studios co-president Louis D'Esposito and executive Jonathan Schwartz also produce *Doomsday* alongside Feige. On casting Downey as Doom, Feige said, "He played the most iconic hero. Let's have him play the most iconic villain." Feige stated that "[Time Runs Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Runs_Out "Time Runs Out")", the prelude storyline for the 2015–16 "Secret Wars", also served as inspiration for the two films, and said one of their goals was to give closure to Marvel storylines that predated the MCU, including those of characters from [20th Century Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Century_Fox "20th Century Fox")'s [*X-Men* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_(film_series) "X-Men (film series)").

In March 2025, Marvel announced 26 cast members for the film alongside Downey. Many of those actors returned from previous MCU media and some were reprising their roles from Fox's *X-Men* films. Downey, Marvel Studios, and Feige teased that there were more cast members to reveal, including the return of [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor) "Chris Evans (actor)") as [Steve Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Rogers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Steve Rogers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"); the Russos felt the correct way for them to return to the MCU would be to continue his story, and Joe Russo said the story they came up with for the films had integral roles for both Downey and Evans. By September 2026, some additional cast members had been revealed, with the full cast still yet to be announced. *Doomsday* features groups of heroes from three different universes—the [Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), [Wakandans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakanda_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Wakanda (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"), and [New Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Avengers_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "New Avengers (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") from [Earth-616](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-616_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Earth-616 (Marvel Cinematic Universe)"); the [Fantastic Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Four_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Fantastic Four (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") from [Earth-828](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-828 "Earth-828"); and the "original" [X-Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "X-Men (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") from [Earth-10005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth-10005 "Earth-10005")—converging to face Doom. Feige said they were using the *Secret Wars* idea of different universes colliding to unite cast members from different film franchises, and that *Doomsday* was picking up where *Endgame* left off.

Production designer Gavin Bocquet and cinematographer [Newton Thomas Sigel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton_Thomas_Sigel "Newton Thomas Sigel") reteamed with the Russo brothers after working on their television series *[Citadel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citadel_(TV_series) "Citadel (TV series)")* (2023–present); Sigel also worked on the Russos' film *[Cherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_(2021_film) "Cherry (2021 film)")* (2021) and a selection of Fox's *X-Men* films. Costume designer [Judianna Makovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judianna_Makovsky "Judianna Makovsky") returned from previous MCU films. The Russos said *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars* were being produced together in a similar way to *Infinity War* and *Endgame*, but they would not be filmed [back-to-back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back-to-back_film_production "Back-to-back film production") like those films were. [Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") for *Doomsday* began on April 28, 2025, at [Pinewood Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinewood_Studios "Pinewood Studios") in Buckinghamshire, England, under the [working title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_title "Working title") *Apple Pie 1*. In May 2025, Disney delayed the release date to December 18, 2026, to allow more time for production to be completed. Location filming was taking place in [Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain "Bahrain") by the end of the month, and was also set to occur at [Windsor Great Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor_Great_Park "Windsor Great Park") in June and August. Filming also took place at [Shinfield Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinfield_Studios "Shinfield Studios") in [Berkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkshire "Berkshire"), England. Filming [wrapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrap_(filmmaking) "Wrap (filmmaking)") on September 19. [Jeffrey Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Ford_(film_editor) "Jeffrey Ford (film editor)") and [Matthew Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Schmidt "Matthew Schmidt") are editing the film, having worked on previous MCU media.[*[independent source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Independent_sources "Wikipedia:Independent sources")*][*[independent source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Independent_sources "Wikipedia:Independent sources")*] *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")* reported that there would be a "healthy" amount of additional photography, which was expected to take place in early 2026.[*[needs update](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Dates_and_numbers#Chronological_items "Wikipedia:Manual of Style/Dates and numbers")*]

## Music

In April 2024, [Alan Silvestri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Silvestri "Alan Silvestri") indicated that he could be composing the music for an upcoming MCU project after previously doing so for *Infinity War*, *Endgame*, and other MCU films. That July, he was confirmed to be returning as the composer for both *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*.

## Marketing

Following the announcement of Downey's casting as Doom and other details at SDCC in July 2024, several unique marketing stunts were used for *Doomsday*. Many cast members were announced as part of a nearly five-and-a-half-hour-long [livestream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestream "Livestream") in March 2025, which became the most-viewed cross-platform livestream stunt. Four short teasers premiered in theaters ahead of showings for the film *[Avatar: Fire and Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash "Avatar: Fire and Ash")* (2025), including one that revealed Evans's return as Rogers. These teasers were leaked upon their debuts, which Marvel had expected, and were officially released online soon after. The first full trailer debuted at [CinemaCon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaCon "CinemaCon") in April 2026, and was met with mixed responses when it was released online in July. A "special look" focusing on Doom received more positive responses when it was released in August, after being shown at SDCC 2026 and Disney's [D23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D23_(Disney)#2026 "D23 (Disney)") convention. A common element of the teasers and trailers is a clock counting down to the film's release. A re-release of *Endgame*, *[Avengers Endgame: Encore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_Endgame:_Encore "Avengers Endgame: Encore")*, was released in September 2026, with new footage that acts as a narrative bridge between the two films, enhancing the story of *Doomsday*. The additional footage was shot during production of *Doomsday*.

The *Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday* official podcast, hosted by [Iman Vellani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iman_Vellani "Iman Vellani")—who stars as [Kamala Khan / Ms. Marvel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamala_Khan_(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) "Kamala Khan (Marvel Cinematic Universe)") in the MCU—and podcaster Frank Alvarez, premiered on August 27, 2026. The five-episode weekly podcast recaps key MCU moments leading up to *Doomsday*, respectively covering the films *[The Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Avengers_(2012_film) "The Avengers (2012 film)")* (2012), *[Avengers: Age of Ultron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Age_of_Ultron "Avengers: Age of Ultron")* (2015), *[Captain America: Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_America:_Civil_War "Captain America: Civil War")* (2016), *Infinity War*, and *Endgame*, with the final episode coinciding with the re-release of *Endgame* in September, under the name *[Avengers Endgame: Encore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_Endgame:_Encore "Avengers Endgame: Encore")*. Six additional podcast episodes will begin releasing weekly on November 5, 2026, covering films from the Multiverse Saga, with one episode covering the original Fox *X-Men* film trilogy.

## Release

*Avengers: Doomsday* is scheduled to be released in the United Kingdom on December 16, 2026, and in the United States in over 4,000 theaters on December 18. It was originally scheduled for May 2, 2025, but was delayed to May 1, 2026, in part due to the 2023 Writers Guild of America strike, followed by another delay to the December 2026 date to allow more time for production to be completed. It will be part of Phase Six of the MCU.

In early 2026, *Doomsday* was revealed to not have access to [IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX "IMAX") theaters in the United States for at least three weeks because the film *[Dune: Part Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune:_Part_Three "Dune: Part Three")*, which is also scheduled for release on December 18, has exclusivity in that time frame. *Doomsday* is expected to be released in IMAX in select international markets, and will also be available in other [premium large formats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premium_large_format "Premium large format") (PLFs) such as [Dolby Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Vision "Dolby Vision"), [ScreenX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ScreenX "ScreenX"), [D-Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D-Box "D-Box"), and [4DX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4DX "4DX"). *[TheWrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TheWrap "TheWrap")* reported in March that Disney was planning to push hard to gain access to those other formats and to get into as many of the 322 chain-specific theater formats around the world as possible. The next month, Disney announced that *Doomsday* would receive the company's [Infinity Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_Vision "Infinity Vision") certification for PLFs, which is intended to inform audiences which theaters offer "the biggest, brightest, and most immersive cinematic experiences" and meet certain technical standards. Many commentators felt Infinity Vision was created in response to *Doomsday* not being able to open in IMAX in the United States.

## Box office projections

Rebecca Rubin at *Variety* reported in March 2026 that early box office tracking was "strong" and there were predictions that *Doomsday* would be 2026's highest-grossing film. Rubin and her colleague Jordan Moreau elaborated that Marvel Studios' recent box office track record had been "rocky", but the studio had success with the Avengers-style crossover films *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home "Spider-Man: No Way Home")* (2021) and *Deadpool & Wolverine*. Theatrical exhibitors were excited for *Doomsday* considering *Endgame*'s record-breaking run, and there was potential for the combined release of *Doomsday* and *Dune: Part Three* to result in one of the biggest box office weekends ever.

The film generated $16.5 million in advance premium format ticket sales in the first 24 hours of sales in July 2026, more than double the pre-sales *Deadpool & Wolverine* had. Rubin called the sales "notable" since they did not include IMAX. Many of the roughly 1,000 PLF theaters had sold out screenings on December 17 and 18, with theaters looking to add more screenings on Christmas Day and beyond. By the end of August, the film had grossed $35 million in advance ticket sales, with Global Box Office reporting that it could earn $260–300 million in its opening weekend. Pre-sales had reached $50 million by early September 2026, 100 days before its release, becoming the first film to achieve that in that time frame. Disney said 70 percent of advance tickets sold were for Infinity Vision showings.

## Sequel

A sequel, *Avengers: Secret Wars*, is scheduled to be released on December 17, 2027. It is also being written by Waldron, McFeely, McKenna, and Sommers, and directed by the Russo brothers. The film will conclude Phase Six and the Multiverse Saga of the MCU.
