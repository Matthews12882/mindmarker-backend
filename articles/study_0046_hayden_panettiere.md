# Hayden Panettiere

**Hayden Lesley Panettiere** ([/ˌpænətiˈɛər/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [ⓘ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-Panettiere.wav "File:En-us-Panettiere.wav") [*PAN-ə-tee-AIR*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); August 21, 1989 – August 16, 2026) was an American actress and singer. She first appeared on screen when she was 11 months old, in a 1990 commercial; by age five, she had appeared in more than 50 commercials. Her first major television roles were as [Sarah Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Roberts_(One_Life_to_Live) "Sarah Roberts (One Life to Live)") in the soap opera *[One Life to Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Life_to_Live "One Life to Live")* (1994–1997) and [Lizzie Spaulding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzie_Spaulding "Lizzie Spaulding") in the soap opera *[Guiding Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiding_Light "Guiding Light")* (1996–2000). She then earned a [Grammy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Spoken_Word_Album_for_Children "Grammy Award for Best Spoken Word Album for Children") nomination for the spoken word album for *[A Bug's Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Bug%27s_Life "A Bug's Life") Read-Along* (1998), making her [the sixth-youngest nominee for the award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_milestones#Youngest_nominees "Grammy Award milestones").

Panettiere continued to develop her career with roles in the live-action animated film *[Dinosaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur_(2000_film) "Dinosaur (2000 film)")* (2000), the [biographical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biographical_film "Biographical film") sports film *[Remember the Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remember_the_Titans "Remember the Titans")* (2000), the legal drama series *[Ally McBeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ally_McBeal "Ally McBeal")* (2002), the comedy film *[Racing Stripes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Stripes "Racing Stripes")* (2005), and the drama film *[Ice Princess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_Princess "Ice Princess")* (2005). She gained wide recognition for portraying [Claire Bennet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Bennet "Claire Bennet") in the [superhero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhero "Superhero") series *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series) "Heroes (American TV series)")* (2006–2010), winning the [Saturn Award for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_on_Television "Saturn Award for Best Supporting Actress on Television"). Further fame came with her portrayals of [Kirby Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirby_Reed "Kirby Reed") in two films in the [slasher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slasher_film "Slasher film") franchise *[Scream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_(franchise) "Scream (franchise)")* (2011, 2023), and [Juliette Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliette_Barnes "Juliette Barnes") in the [musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_film "Musical film") [drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_(film_and_television) "Drama (film and television)") series *[Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_(2012_TV_series) "Nashville (2012 TV series)")* (2012–2018), earning nominations for two [Golden Globes for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_%E2%80%93_Series,_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Series, Miniseries or Television Film").

Outside of big-screen projects, Panettiere voiced [Kairi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kairi_(Kingdom_Hearts) "Kairi (Kingdom Hearts)") and [Xion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xion_(Kingdom_Hearts) "Xion (Kingdom Hearts)") in the video game franchise *[Kingdom Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts "Kingdom Hearts")* (2002–2017) and [Samantha "Sam" Giddings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Until_Dawn#Plot "Until Dawn") in the horror game *[Until Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Until_Dawn "Until Dawn")* (2015). Towards the end of her career, she starred in a number of [direct-to-video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-video "Direct-to-video") films.

## Early life

Hayden Lesley Panettiere was born on August 21, 1989, in [Palisades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palisades,_New_York "Palisades, New York"), an unincorporated [hamlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_(place) "Hamlet (place)") in [Rockland County, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockland_County,_New_York "Rockland County, New York"). She was the eldest child of Lesley R. Vogel, a former daytime television [soap opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap_opera "Soap opera") actress, and Alan Lee "Skip" Panettiere, a [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_captain "Fire captain") in the [New York City Fire Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Fire_Department "New York City Fire Department") (FDNY). She had a younger brother, [Jansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jansen_Panettiere "Jansen Panettiere"). Panettiere was of Italian, German, and English descent. Her surname is of Italian origin and is associated with the Italian word for "baker". In her 2026 memoir, *This Is Me: A Reckoning*, she discussed the family's history and the pronunciation of the surname, which her family pronounced "pan-a-teery". According to her, the surname was inherited through her paternal family after her father was adopted by his stepfather.

Panettiere grew up in the Palisades area of Rockland County and maintained close ties with her extended family. Her maternal grandparents lived in [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana "Indiana"), and she spent extended periods there during her childhood. She attended [South Orangetown Middle School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Orangetown_Central_School_District "South Orangetown Central School District") in [Blauvelt, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blauvelt,_New_York "Blauvelt, New York"). Because of performing and travel, maintaining a conventional school schedule became difficult. She completed eighth grade and continued her education through [homeschooling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeschooling "Homeschooling") programs arranged around her work and travel schedule. Transitioning to homeschooling was a result of the workload she had during adolescence. Contemporary biographical accounts say she continued to study while balancing an irregular schedule.

## Career

### 1990s: Beginnings

Panettiere first appeared on-screen at 11 months old in a 1990 [Playskool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playskool "Playskool") toy train commercial. By five, she had appeared in more than 50 commercials. She told [Gayle King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayle_King "Gayle King") on *[CBS Mornings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Mornings "CBS Mornings")* on May 18, 2026, that she first appeared in a soap opera when she was four. She landed a role as [Sarah Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Roberts_(One_Life_to_Live) "Sarah Roberts (One Life to Live)") on the ABC [soap opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap_opera "Soap opera") *[One Life to Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Life_to_Live "One Life to Live")* from 1994 to 1997, followed by [Lizzie Spaulding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzie_Spaulding "Lizzie Spaulding") on the CBS soap opera *[Guiding Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiding_Light "Guiding Light")* in 1996 and again from 1997 to 2000. While on *Guiding Light*, Panettiere's character Lizzie battled [leukemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leukemia "Leukemia"). The [Leukemia & Lymphoma Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leukemia_%26_Lymphoma_Society "Leukemia & Lymphoma Society") gave her its Special Recognition Award for drawing the attention of daytime viewers to the disease and for improving national awareness. She made her feature film debut at age nine, as the voice of Princess Dot in *[A Bug's Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Bug%27s_Life "A Bug's Life")* (1998). Her first released film was *[The Object of My Affection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Object_of_My_Affection "The Object of My Affection")* (1998) at eight, in which she appears briefly as a mermaid in the school play in the opening scene. In 1999, she played the girl on a sinking sailboat in *[Message in a Bottle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Message_in_a_Bottle_(film) "Message in a Bottle (film)")*.

### 2000s: Breakthrough with *Heroes* and music

Panettiere provided the voice for Suri in [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Pictures "Walt Disney Pictures")'s [CGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery")-animated movie *[Dinosaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur_(2000_film) "Dinosaur (2000 film)")* (2000) and portrayed Sheryl Yoast in *[Remember the Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remember_the_Titans "Remember the Titans")* (2000), a role that won her a [Young Artist Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Artist_Award "Young Artist Award") for Best Supporting Young Actress. For her performance in [Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_channel) "Lifetime (TV channel)")'s *[If You Believe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_You_Believe_(film) "If You Believe (film)")*, she was nominated for the [Young Artist Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Artist_Award "Young Artist Award") for Young Actress Age Ten or Under in the category of Best Performance in a TV Movie or Pilot. Panettiere appeared on [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company "Fox Broadcasting Company")'s *[Ally McBeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ally_McBeal "Ally McBeal")* as the title character's daughter, played the daughter of the main character Ruth Applewood in [HBO Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Films "HBO Films")' *[Normal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_(2003_film) "Normal (2003 film)")*, had a recurring guest role on *[Malcolm in the Middle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malcolm_in_the_Middle "Malcolm in the Middle")*, and guest starred on *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit")* in two different roles. In 2004, [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") gave Panettiere her first starring role as the daughter of [Bill Pullman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Pullman "Bill Pullman")'s character in *[Tiger Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Cruise_(film) "Tiger Cruise (film)")*.

Panettiere voiced [Kairi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kairi_(Kingdom_Hearts) "Kairi (Kingdom Hearts)") in *[Kingdom Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_(video_game) "Kingdom Hearts (video game)")* (2002). In 2004, she appeared as a teenage girl caught in a fantasy transitional realm alongside fellow teen actor [Ryan Kelley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Kelley "Ryan Kelley") in *[The Dust Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dust_Factory "The Dust Factory")* and the role of adolescent niece to [Kate Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Hudson "Kate Hudson")'s title character in *[Raising Helen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raising_Helen "Raising Helen")*, where she portrayed an older sister of real-life siblings [Abigail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abigail_Breslin "Abigail Breslin") and [Spencer Breslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Breslin "Spencer Breslin"). In 2005, Panettiere starred alongside [Michelle Trachtenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Trachtenberg "Michelle Trachtenberg") as an ice-skating rival in Disney's *[Ice Princess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_Princess "Ice Princess")*. Also in 2005, she played a jockey with a rising career, Channing Walsh, in *[Racing Stripes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Stripes "Racing Stripes")*. She later reprised the role of Kairi in *[Kingdom Hearts II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_II "Kingdom Hearts II")* (2005) and *[Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_Birth_by_Sleep "Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep")* (2010).

Panettiere often sang the soundtracks for the films in which she also starred. She was nominated for a [Grammy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Spoken_Word_Album_for_Children "Grammy Award for Best Spoken Word Album for Children") in 1999 for *A Bug's Life Read-Along*. In 2004, she recorded "My Hero Is You" for *Tiger Cruise* and "Someone Like You", a duet with Watt White for *The Dust Factory* (both also with accompanying videos). The following year, she recorded "I Fly" for *Ice Princess*. She recorded a song for the [Hollywood Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Records "Hollywood Records") compilation *Girl Next* (2006) and another song titled "Go to Girl" for *Girl Next 2* (2007). Also in 2007, she sang the [U.S. National Anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._National_Anthem "U.S. National Anthem") at [A Capitol Fourth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Capitol_Fourth "A Capitol Fourth") and recorded a cover for "[Cruella De Vil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Hundred_and_One_Dalmatians "One Hundred and One Dalmatians")" for *[Disneymania 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disneymania_5 "Disneymania 5")*, "Try" for the *[Bridge to Terabithia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_to_Terabithia_(2007_film) "Bridge to Terabithia (2007 film)")* soundtrack, and a ballad titled "[I Still Believe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Still_Believe_(Hayden_Panettiere_song) "I Still Believe (Hayden Panettiere song)")" for *[Cinderella III: A Twist in Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinderella_III:_A_Twist_in_Time "Cinderella III: A Twist in Time")*.

Panettiere gained greater fame as [Claire Bennet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Bennet "Claire Bennet") in the NBC series *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series) "Heroes (American TV series)")*, created by [Tim Kring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Kring "Tim Kring"), as a high school cheerleader with healing powers. Thanks to her role on *Heroes*, she became a regular on the [science fiction convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_convention "Science fiction convention") circuit, invited to conventions around the world in 2007, including [Gen Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gen_Con "Gen Con"), [New York Comic Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Comic_Con "New York Comic Con"), and [Fan Expo Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_Expo_Canada "Fan Expo Canada"). Panettiere said her acting options were sometimes limited because "people look at [her] as either the popular cheerleader type or just the blonde". In early 2007, Panettiere appeared on the [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV") show *[Punk'd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk%27d "Punk'd")*. The appearance was engineered by her mother and involved a male "fan" discussing her work with her, instigating a jealous reaction from the man's spouse. In April 2012, she hosted an episode of the new series. In 2006, Panettiere played a cheerleader in *[Bring It On: All or Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bring_It_On:_All_or_Nothing "Bring It On: All or Nothing")*, which was released [direct-to-video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-video "Direct-to-video"). She also had a supporting role as Adelaide Bourbon in the independent film *[Shanghai Kiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Kiss "Shanghai Kiss")* (2007). In June 2007, she signed with the [William Morris Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morris_Agency "William Morris Agency"), after having being represented by the [United Talent Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Talent_Agency "United Talent Agency"). *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* estimated that she earned $2 million in 2007.

In 2008, Panettiere appeared in the drama *[Fireflies in the Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fireflies_in_the_Garden "Fireflies in the Garden")* as a younger version of [Emily Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Watson "Emily Watson")'s character, Jane Lawrence. In September 2008, she appeared in a satirical video, a mock-[PSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_service_announcement "Public service announcement") for [Funny or Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funny_or_Die "Funny or Die") titled "Hayden Panettiere PSA: Your Vote, Your Choice". In October, Panettiere appeared in another satirical Funny or Die PSA titled "Vote for [McCain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCain "John McCain"): He's just like [George Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush "George W. Bush"), except older and with a worse temper". Panettiere's first single not associated with an acting role, the [reggae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reggae "Reggae")-infused "Wake Up Call", was digitally released on August 5, 2008. The clothing brand [Candie's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candie%27s "Candie's") announced that it was premiering an ad campaign for the single in late July. Candie's provided additional promotion with a television advertisement and a music video. [Sebastian Stan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Stan "Sebastian Stan"), who portrayed the brother of Panettiere's character in *The Architect*, played her boyfriend in the video. In July 2009, Panettiere starred in the teen comedy *[I Love You, Beth Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Love_You,_Beth_Cooper_(film) "I Love You, Beth Cooper (film)")*.

### 2010s: Acclaim with *Scream* and *Nashville*

Panettiere voiced Kate in the animated film *[Alpha and Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_and_Omega_(film) "Alpha and Omega (film)")* (2010). In September 2010, she was cast as [Amanda Knox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Knox "Amanda Knox") in the film *[Amanda Knox: Murder on Trial in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Knox:_Murder_on_Trial_in_Italy "Amanda Knox: Murder on Trial in Italy")*. In April 2011, she appeared in *[Scream 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_4 "Scream 4")*, a sequel to [*Scream*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_(1996_film) "Scream (1996 film)") (1996), playing Kirby Reed. The film received mixed reviews, but she received acclaim for her role and a positive for the film. Also in 2011, she replaced [Anne Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Hathaway "Anne Hathaway") as the voice of [Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Red_Riding_Hood "Little Red Riding Hood") for the animated sequel *[Hoodwinked Too! Hood vs. Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoodwinked_Too!_Hood_vs._Evil "Hoodwinked Too! Hood vs. Evil")*.

In March 2012, it was announced Panettiere had been cast opposite [Connie Britton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connie_Britton "Connie Britton") on the ABC [musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_film "Musical film") drama series *[Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_(2012_TV_series) "Nashville (2012 TV series)")*, where she portrayed [Juliette Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliette_Barnes "Juliette Barnes"). The show reunited Panettiere with [Burgess Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgess_Jenkins "Burgess Jenkins") (who appears in the first few episodes) from *[Remember the Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remember_the_Titans "Remember the Titans")*. Panettiere recorded several songs for *Nashville*, which were released as singles and included on the show's soundtrack albums. She made numerous concert appearances associated with the show's touring promotion. In 2013, Panettiere recorded a version of "The Fabric of My Life" for a [Cotton Incorporated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_Incorporated "Cotton Incorporated") campaign.

In 2012, *[The Forger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Forger_(2011_film) "The Forger (2011 film)")*, in which she played the role of Amber, was released direct-to video (both this and *Hoodwinked Too! Hood vs. Evil* were made in 2009). She also voiced [Xion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xion_(Kingdom_Hearts) "Xion (Kingdom Hearts)") in *[Kingdom Hearts 3D: Dream Drop Distance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_3D:_Dream_Drop_Distance "Kingdom Hearts 3D: Dream Drop Distance")* (2012). She was replaced by [Alyson Stoner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alyson_Stoner "Alyson Stoner") for the later sequel games of the [series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts "Kingdom Hearts"). In April 2015, Panettiere joined the cast of *[Custody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Custody_(2016_film) "Custody (2016 film)")* (2016), alongside [Viola Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola_Davis "Viola Davis"), with whom she appeared in 2006's *[The Architect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Architect_(2006_film) "The Architect (2006 film)")*. Also in 2015, Panettiere acted in *[Until Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Until_Dawn "Until Dawn")*, a [PlayStation 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_4 "PlayStation 4") game, playing the role of Samantha "Sam" Giddings. She reprised the role for a new post-credits scene in the remake version of the game, released on [PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5 "PlayStation 5") and [Microsoft Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows "Microsoft Windows").

### 2020s: Final work

In January 2022, Panettiere briefly reprised her role as Kirby Reed in the *Scream 4* sequel *[Scream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_(2022_film) "Scream (2022 film)")* in a photograph used in the film, revealing the character had survived the events of the fourth film in the series. She is acknowledged with a mention of "Special Thanks" for the photograph's use and she made a voice [cameo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameo_appearance "Cameo appearance"). In May 2022, it was announced that Panettiere would be returning to the *Scream* franchise with *[Scream VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_VI "Scream VI")* (2023), in what was her first on-screen film appearance since 2018 after a career hiatus. She did not appear in the franchise's next iteration, *[Scream 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_7 "Scream 7")*. Her final film role was in the psychological thriller film *[Sleepwalker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleepwalker_(2026_film) "Sleepwalker (2026 film)")* (2026), in which she played the lead role of Sarah Pangborn. She also served as the film's executive producer.

Before her death, Panettiere said in May 2026 that she was "trying her hand" at writing a television series and hoped to expand her work behind the camera by directing and producing, particularly to create safer working environments for actors and child performers. She also expressed interest in establishing a production company through which she could develop projects and provide opportunities for overlooked talent. In January 2026, she stated that she would also be willing to return if either *[Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_(2012_TV_series) "Nashville (2012 TV series)")* or *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series) "Heroes (American TV series)")* were revived.

## Other ventures

### Modeling

In late 2006, [Neutrogena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrogena "Neutrogena") made Panettiere the cover girl for its new worldwide ad campaign. In September 2007, she appeared in a *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series) "Heroes (American TV series)")*-themed [Got Milk?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Got_Milk%3F "Got Milk?") ad photographed by [Annie Leibovitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Leibovitz "Annie Leibovitz").

In February 2008, [Kohl's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohl%27s "Kohl's") announced that Panettiere would be Candie's next spokesperson. From 2007 to 2008, Panettiere designed limited-edition handbags for Dooney & Bourke and was photographed with the bags in magazine ads. In 2009, she was one of the celebrities featured in the coffee-table book *Room 23*, produced by [Sanela Diana Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanela_Diana_Jenkins "Sanela Diana Jenkins") and photographed by [Deborah Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_Anderson "Deborah Anderson").

### Activism

In 2007, Panettiere became an official supporter of [Ronald McDonald House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_McDonald_House "Ronald McDonald House") charities and was a member of its celebrity board, the Friends of RMHC. In September 2008, she launched her own line of [calfskin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calfskin "Calfskin") leather bags for the company [Dooney & Bourke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dooney_%26_Bourke "Dooney & Bourke"). In October, she began to make her views known about the [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_United_States_presidential_election "2008 United States presidential election"), releasing a satirical [public service announcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_service_announcement "Public service announcement") (PSA) through [Funny or Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funny_or_Die "Funny or Die"). In the video, Panettiere mocked [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States) "Republican Party (United States)") candidate [John McCain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCain "John McCain") for his age and temper. She later made clear her intention to vote for [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama "Barack Obama") and urged other young people to vote. Panettiere had long supported the [District of Columbia statehood movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_of_Columbia_statehood_movement "District of Columbia statehood movement"). In 2008, she appeared in a public service announcement with D.C.'s [shadow senator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_Senator "Shadow Senator"), [Paul Strauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Strauss "Paul Strauss"), endorsing voting rights for [Washington, D.C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C. "Washington, D.C."). In 2011, D.C. [Mayor Vincent Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_C._Gray "Vincent C. Gray") acknowledged Panettiere's advocacy for securing full representation in the [U.S. Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Congress "U.S. Congress") for District of Columbia residents by naming a day in her honor. At the ceremony, Panettiere said it is an "unfathomable fact... it's [taxation without representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxation_without_representation "Taxation without representation") in D.C., and that there's no democracy in our democracy, at the heart of it". She participated in Strauss' "51 Stars" campaign, which aimed to get 51 celebrities to endorse making Washington, D.C., the 51st state.

She was also a teen ambassador for the [Candie's Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candie%27s_Foundation "Candie's Foundation"), whose mission is to prevent [teen pregnancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_pregnancy "Teenage pregnancy"). On May 6, 2009, she participated in a [town hall meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_hall_meeting "Town hall meeting") in [Midtown Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midtown_Manhattan "Midtown Manhattan") alongside [Bristol Palin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Palin "Bristol Palin") and [Tampa Bay Rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Rays "Tampa Bay Rays") pitcher [Matt Garza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Garza "Matt Garza") of [MLB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Baseball "Major League Baseball") to raise awareness of teen pregnancy. On December 6, 2013, Panettiere and [Wladimir Klitschko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wladimir_Klitschko "Wladimir Klitschko"), her fiancé at the time, visited the [Euromaidan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euromaidan "Euromaidan") protests in [Kyiv, Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv "Kyiv"). Wladimir's brother, [Vitali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Klitschko "Vitali Klitschko"), was a leading figure of the protests. In July 2020, Panettiere advocated for victims of [domestic violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_violence "Domestic violence") to come forward and share their stories, specifically after she herself had experienced abuse.

#### Marine-life advocacy

On October 31, 2007, Panettiere partnered with the [Whaleman Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whaleman_Foundation "Whaleman Foundation") to disrupt the annual [dolphin hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiji_dolphin_drive_hunt "Taiji dolphin drive hunt") in [Taiji, Wakayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiji,_Wakayama "Taiji, Wakayama"), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan "Japan"). She was involved in a confrontation between Japanese fishermen and five other activists on surfboards from Australia and the United States, including former *[Home and Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_and_Away "Home and Away")* actress [Isabel Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabel_Lucas "Isabel Lucas"). The confrontation lasted more than 10 minutes before the surfers were forced to return to the beach, after which they left the country. Parts of the confrontation appear in the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival") documentary *[The Cove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cove_(film) "The Cove (film)")* (2009). The fishermen consider the condemnation an attack on their culture.

At a 2007 [Greenpeace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenpeace "Greenpeace") event in [Anchorage, Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchorage,_Alaska "Anchorage, Alaska"), Panettiere defended [aboriginal whaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aboriginal_whaling "Aboriginal whaling"), saying that there is a difference between commercial [whaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whaling "Whaling") and the whaling practiced by [aboriginal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples "Indigenous peoples") tribes in the U.S. In January 2008, she delivered letters to the Japanese, Icelandic, and Norwegian embassies in Washington, D.C., and met with diplomats, calling for an end to commercial whaling. In May 2008, she took part in an [eBay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBay "EBay") auction to benefit *[Save the Whales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Save_the_Whales "Save the Whales") Again*, a campaign of the Whaleman Foundation. The auction included tickets to a fundraising dinner hosted at the [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles "Hollywood, Los Angeles") restaurant Beso, owned by [Eva Longoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Longoria "Eva Longoria"), and a [whale-watching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whale_watching "Whale watching") tour with Panettiere off the coast of [Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California "Santa Barbara, California"). Also in May 2008, during an interview with *[Teen Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Vogue "Teen Vogue")*, she explained how her fame gave her a platform for activism: "The show [*Heroes*] put me in a place to speak for things that I'm passionate about."

About two weeks before her death in 2026, she participated in a [PETA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_for_the_Ethical_Treatment_of_Animals "People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals") advertising campaign protesting against marine mammals being held in captivity in marine parks including [SeaWorld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SeaWorld "SeaWorld").

## Personal life

### Relationships

Panettiere began dating her *[Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series) "Heroes (American TV series)")* co-star [Milo Ventimiglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milo_Ventimiglia "Milo Ventimiglia") in December 2007, when she was 18 and he was 30. They separated in February 2009.

In 2009, Panettiere met then-world heavyweight boxing champion [Wladimir Klitschko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wladimir_Klitschko "Wladimir Klitschko") at the book launch party for mutual acquaintance [Diana Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanela_Diana_Jenkins "Sanela Diana Jenkins")'s *Room 23*, in which both Panettiere and Klitschko were featured. They soon began dating. Panettiere was ringside for Klitschko's [knockout victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wladimir_Klitschko_vs._Samuel_Peter_II "Wladimir Klitschko vs. Samuel Peter II") over [Samuel Peter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Peter "Samuel Peter") on September 11, 2010. In May 2011, she announced that they had separated. Both cited the long-distance nature of their relationship as the reason, and said they would remain close friends.

Panettiere and [Scotty McKnight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotty_McKnight "Scotty McKnight"), an [NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League "National Football League") player with the [New York Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Jets "New York Jets"), began dating in June 2011, and separated in December 2012.

Panettiere confirmed reports that she and Klitschko had resumed their relationship in an April 2013 interview. In October 2013, she announced her engagement to Klitschko. In December 2014, Panettiere gave birth to their daughter.

In August 2018, Panettiere's mother confirmed Panettiere and Klitschko had ended their relationship, but remained on friendly terms. Panettiere relinquished full custody of her daughter to Klitschko in 2018, amid struggles with [postpartum depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postpartum_depression "Postpartum depression") and addiction. She called the decision a "living nightmare" and "heartbreaking" but necessary for her recovery. She maintained a good co-parenting relationship with Klitschko and frequently visited her daughter.

Panettiere had been in an on‑and‑off relationship with Brian Hickerson starting in 2018. He was arrested in 2019, after an alleged confrontation with her, but the case was later dismissed. In 2020, he was arrested twice on [domestic violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_violence "Domestic violence")‑related charges. In 2021, he was sentenced to 45 days in jail after pleading [no contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_contest "No contest") to two felony counts of injuring a wife or girlfriend.

In May 2026, Panettiere released her memoir *This Is Me: A Reckoning*, in which she came out as [bisexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisexuality "Bisexuality") and revealed that she had dated women.

### Substance abuse and mental health problems

Panetierre began misusing drugs as a teenager. At age 16, she was given "happy pills" by personal assistants before awards shows and interviews. In her 20s, she turned to alcohol to manage the stress of her work schedule. She completed an alcohol rehabilitation program in 2015, but eventually relapsed. In 2020, she entered an eight-month drug and alcohol rehabilitation program after developing [jaundice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaundice "Jaundice") from alcohol-induced liver damage. Before entering rehab, doctors told her she would die within five years if she did not become sober.

In 2015, Panettiere said she had experienced [postpartum depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postpartum_depression "Postpartum depression") (PPD) after her daughter's birth. In an appearance on *[Live with Kelly and Michael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_with_Kelly_and_Mark "Live with Kelly and Mark")* in September 2015, she said the disorder was "scary and needs to be talked about". Her depression also worsened her alcoholism and dependence upon [clonazepam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clonazepam "Clonazepam"). She also had [body dysmorphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_dysmorphia "Body dysmorphia") as a result of years of [body shaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_shaming "Body shaming") by [tabloid media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabloid_media "Tabloid media") and [predatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_predator "Sexual predator") behavior by several men in Hollywood.

### Family

Panettiere's mother managed her career until she was 19. Panettiere later described their relationship as a difficult one in which she felt she had to succeed professionally to receive her mother's love; during an appearance on [Jay Shetty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Shetty "Jay Shetty")'s [podcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast "Podcast") *On Purpose* in May 2026, she said, "Over the years, I've tried to leave that door open, hoping that the relationship would evolve into a healthy mother-daughter relationship, and it's obvious that that's just not going to be the case." In response, Vogel told *[Page Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Page_Six "Page Six")* that she had chosen to "go the no contact route" with her daughter on "the advice of professionals". They were estranged at the time of Panettiere's death.

Panettiere's younger brother [Jansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jansen_Panettiere "Jansen Panettiere") died in February 2023, at age 28 of [aortic valve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aortic_valve "Aortic valve") complications arising from [cardiomegaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiomegaly "Cardiomegaly").

## Death

Panettiere died in an apartment in [Greenville, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenville,_South_Carolina "Greenville, South Carolina"), on August 16, 2026, at age 36. She was found unresponsive and in cardiac arrest around 1:51 pm. [EMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_medical_services "Emergency medical services") arrived and, after further efforts, pronounced her dead at 2:32 pm. Following an [autopsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autopsy "Autopsy"), the Greenville County Coroner's Office announced on September 22 that the [manner of death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manner_of_death "Manner of death") was accidental, and that the [cause of death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cause_of_death "Cause of death") was [toxic effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_drug_intoxication "Combined drug intoxication") of [fentanyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fentanyl "Fentanyl"), [4-ANPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-ANPP "4-ANPP"), [alprazolam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alprazolam "Alprazolam"), [methocarbamol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methocarbamol "Methocarbamol"), and [quetiapine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetiapine "Quetiapine"). Police are conducting a criminal investigation into dealers who might have provided Panettiere with lethal pills.

### Tributes

Panettiere's former co-star, [Viola Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola_Davis "Viola Davis"), called her "an amazing talent... so gifted", saying she was an "old, wise soul with such a deep heart". [Bryan Fuller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Fuller "Bryan Fuller"), who was a writer on *Heroes*' first season, said that she was "more than an actress, she was a [muse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_(person) "Muse (person)")". Panettiere's former fiancé, Wladimir Klitschko, said she had "built an incredible career through immense talent". Others in the entertainment industry including former co-stars posted tributes on social media.
