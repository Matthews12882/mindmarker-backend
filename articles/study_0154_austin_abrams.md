# Austin Abrams

**Austin Noah Abrams** (born September 2, 1996) is an American actor. He is best known for his performances in the [Zach Cregger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Cregger "Zach Cregger") films *[Weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_(2025_film) "Weapons (2025 film)")* (2025) and *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2026_film) "Resident Evil (2026 film)")* (2026). He has also had lead roles in the films *[Brad's Status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad%27s_Status "Brad's Status")* (2017), *[Do Revenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_Revenge "Do Revenge")* (2022), and *[Whalefall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whalefall_(film) "Whalefall (film)")* (2026), and supporting roles in *[The Kings of Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kings_of_Summer "The Kings of Summer")* (2013), *[Paper Towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_Towns_(film) "Paper Towns (film)")* (2015), *[Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scary_Stories_to_Tell_in_the_Dark_(film) "Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark (film)")* (2019), and *[Wolfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfs_(film) "Wolfs (film)")* (2024).

In television, he played Todd Cooper on the [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV") sitcom *[The Inbetweeners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Inbetweeners_(American_TV_series) "The Inbetweeners (American TV series)")* (2012), [Ron Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series)_characters#Alexandria_Safe-Zone "List of The Walking Dead (TV series) characters") in the post-apocalyptic horror drama series *[The Walking Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series) "The Walking Dead (TV series)")* (2015–2016), young Marc McKeon in the comedy drama series *[This Is Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Is_Us "This Is Us")* (2019–2021), and Dash in the romantic comedy series *[Dash & Lily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dash_%26_Lily "Dash & Lily")* (2020). He starred as [Ethan Daley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Euphoria_characters#Ethan_Daley "List of Euphoria characters") in the psychological teen drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")* (2019–2022).

For his performance as Gettys O'Brien in the coming-of-age drama film *[The Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Line_(2023_film) "The Line (2023 film)")* (2023), Abrams won the Rising Star Award at the [Sarasota Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarasota_Film_Festival "Sarasota Film Festival").

## Early life

Abrams was raised in [Sarasota, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarasota,_Florida "Sarasota, Florida"), and is the son of Lori and Bradley Abrams, who are doctors. He has a younger sister who is also an actress. He is of Jewish heritage. Abrams began taking acting lessons when he was five years old, and started appearing in theater productions at the age of nine.

## Career

### 2010s

Abrams made his feature film debut in the mystery action thriller film *[Ticking Clock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticking_Clock "Ticking Clock")* in 2011. He made his television debut in [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV "MTV")'s *[The Inbetweeners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Inbetweeners_(American_TV_series) "The Inbetweeners (American TV series)")* portraying Todd Cooper. In the same year, Abrams played the role of young version of [Joel David Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_David_Moore "Joel David Moore")'s character Adam Lipschitz in the comedy movie *[Jewtopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewtopia_(film) "Jewtopia (film)")*, which was released on April 26, 2012.

In 2013, Abrams appeared in the action thriller movie *[Gangster Squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangster_Squad_(film) "Gangster Squad (film)")* directed by [Ruben Fleischer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruben_Fleischer "Ruben Fleischer"), based on a non-fiction book by Paul Lieberman. In the same year, he appeared in the coming-of-age comedy-drama film *[The Kings of Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kings_of_Summer "The Kings of Summer")*, where he played the role of Aaron. In 2014, Abrams starred in the thriller drama film *[Sacrifice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacrifice_(2014_film) "Sacrifice (2014 film)")*, which premiered at the [Woodstock Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodstock_Film_Festival "Woodstock Film Festival") in October 2014. He had a recurring role as Ron Anderson in the [AMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMC_(TV_channel) "AMC (TV channel)") television series *[The Walking Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series) "The Walking Dead (TV series)")* during its fifth and sixth seasons. In 2015, Abrams appeared in a supporting role in [Jake Schreier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Schreier "Jake Schreier")'s romantic comedy-drama film *[Paper Towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_Towns_(film) "Paper Towns (film)")*, based on the 2008 [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_Towns_(novel) "Paper Towns (novel)") by [John Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Green "John Green").

In 2017, Abrams co-starred in Kyle Wilamowski's film *[All Summers End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Summers_End "All Summers End")*, playing Hunter Gorski, a scruffy 16-year-old who pays little attention to his best friend Conrad's first love. On the same year, he starred in *[Brad's Status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad%27s_Status "Brad's Status")*, a comedy-drama movie by [Mike White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_White_(filmmaker) "Mike White (filmmaker)"), playing Troy Sloan. He took on a supporting roles in the films *[Tragedy Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tragedy_Girls "Tragedy Girls")* and *[We Don't Belong Here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Don%27t_Belong_Here_(film) "We Don't Belong Here (film)")*. In 2018, Abrams appeared as James in *[Dude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dude_(2018_film) "Dude (2018 film)")*, a [coming-of-age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming-of-age "Coming-of-age") comedy-drama film by Olivia Milch (2018), released on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). In the same year, he appeared in the drama film *[Puzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzle_(2018_film) "Puzzle (2018 film)")* directed by [Marc Turtletaub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Turtletaub "Marc Turtletaub"), based on the 2010 Argentine [film of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzle_(2010_film) "Puzzle (2010 film)"). The film had its world premiere at the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival") on January 23, 2018, and was released in the United States on July 27, 2018.

In 2019, Abrams was cast as [Ethan Daley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Euphoria_characters#Ethan_Daley "List of Euphoria characters") in the teen drama series *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_(American_TV_series) "Euphoria (American TV series)")*, created by [Sam Levinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Levinson "Sam Levinson"). Abrams had a major role in the 2019 horror book adaptation *[Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scary_Stories_to_Tell_in_the_Dark_(film) "Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark (film)")*. The film, directed by [André Øvredal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_%C3%98vredal "André Øvredal") and based on the book series [of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scary_Stories_to_Tell_in_the_Dark "Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark") by [Alvin Schwartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvin_Schwartz_(children%27s_author) "Alvin Schwartz (children's author)"), was theatrically released on August 9, 2019, by [Lionsgate Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate_Films "Lionsgate Films"). Abrams had a recurring role in the TV series *[This Is Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Is_Us "This Is Us")*, playing Marc McKeon, Kate's abusive ex-boyfriend.

### 2020s

In early 2020, Abrams starred as Henry Page in the teen romance film *[Chemical Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Hearts "Chemical Hearts")* directed by [Richard Tanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Tanne "Richard Tanne"), based on the novel *[Our Chemical Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Chemical_Hearts "Our Chemical Hearts")* by [Krystal Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krystal_Sutherland "Krystal Sutherland"). The film was released on August 21, 2020, by [Amazon Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Studios "Amazon Studios"). Abrams was cast to play a lead role in the Netflix romantic comedy series *[Dash & Lily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dash_%26_Lily "Dash & Lily")*. The series, based on the young adult novel series *Dash & Lily's Book of Dares* by [David Levithan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Levithan "David Levithan") and [Rachel Cohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Cohn "Rachel Cohn"), premiered on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") on November 10, 2020.

In 2022, Abrams played the role of Max Broussard in the teen comedy film *[Do Revenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_Revenge "Do Revenge")* directed by [Jennifer Kaytin Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Kaytin_Robinson "Jennifer Kaytin Robinson"). It was released on Netflix on September 16, 2022. In 2023, Abrams portrayed Ben Taylor in the coming-of-age drama film *[The Starling Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Starling_Girl "The Starling Girl")*, directed by [Laurel Parmet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurel_Parmet "Laurel Parmet"). The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival on January 21, 2023, and was released theatrically on May 12, 2023. In the same year, Abrams appeared in the drama film *[The Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Line_(2023_film) "The Line (2023 film)"),* directed by Ethan Berger, which was released on June 9, 2023, at the [Tribeca Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribeca_Festival "Tribeca Festival").

In 2024, Abrams starred in the action comedy film *[Wolfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfs_(film) "Wolfs (film)")*, directed by [Jon Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Watts "Jon Watts").

In 2025, Abrams appeared in [Zach Cregger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Cregger "Zach Cregger")'s horror film *[Weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_(2025_film) "Weapons (2025 film)")* as James, a thieving drug addict who gets involved in the mystery of a group of disappeared schoolchildren. He was initially cast in 2023 alongside other actors, but production was interrupted by the [2023 Hollywood labor disputes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hollywood_labor_disputes "2023 Hollywood labor disputes"); Abrams was the only original cast member to remain attached to the film.

After being impressed by Abrams on the set of *Weapons*, Cregger cast him as the lead for his next film, *[Resident Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(2026_film) "Resident Evil (2026 film)")*. Abrams plays Bryan, a medical courier who must fight for survival against a viral outbreak of infected monsters. That same year Abrams stars as Jay Gardiner in *[Whalefall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whalefall_(film) "Whalefall (film)")* (2026), an epic and emotional survival thriller about one young man’s encounter with a massive sperm whale.
