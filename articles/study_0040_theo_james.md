# Theo James

**Theodore Peter James Kinnaird Taptiklis** (born 16 December 1984) is a British actor and producer. He is of Greek and Scottish descent. He gained recognition for playing [Tobias Eaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Divergent_characters#Tobias_"Four"_Eaton "List of Divergent characters") in *[The Divergent Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Divergent_Series "The Divergent Series")* (2014–2016). He has starred in the horror films *[Underworld: Awakening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underworld:_Awakening "Underworld: Awakening")* (2012) and *[Underworld: Blood Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underworld:_Blood_Wars "Underworld: Blood Wars")* (2016), the science fiction film *[Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive_(film) "Archive (film)")* (2020), and the dark comedy *[The Monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monkey_(film) "The Monkey (film)")* (2025) in a dual role as a pair of twin brothers.

In television, James appeared in the short-lived [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") crime drama series *[Golden Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Boy_(American_TV_series) "Golden Boy (American TV series)")* (2013), the romance series *[The Time Traveler's Wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Time_Traveler%27s_Wife_(TV_series) "The Time Traveler's Wife (TV series)")* (2022), and the dark comedy series *[The White Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_Lotus "The White Lotus")* (2022), which earned him a nomination for a [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award"). Since 2024, he has played the lead role in [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie")'s action comedy series *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2024_TV_series) "The Gentlemen (2024 TV series)")*.

## Early life

Theodore Peter James Kinnaird Taptiklis was born on 16 December 1984 in [High Wycombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Wycombe "High Wycombe"), England, the son of Philip Taptiklis, a business consultant from [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand "New Zealand"), and Jane ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9e "Née") Martin), who worked for the [National Health Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Service "National Health Service"). His paternal grandfather was a Greek refugee who fled [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece "Greece") for [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria "Syria") during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II"). He is also of part Scottish descent. He has two older brothers and two older sisters.

James was raised in [Askett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askett "Askett"). He attended [Aylesbury Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aylesbury_Grammar_School "Aylesbury Grammar School"), and earned his undergraduate degree in philosophy from the [University of Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham "University of Nottingham"). He trained at the [Bristol Old Vic Theatre School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Old_Vic_Theatre_School "Bristol Old Vic Theatre School").

## Career

In 2010, James made his television debut in two episodes of *[A Passionate Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Passionate_Woman "A Passionate Woman")*. The same year he played Turkish diplomat [Kemal Pamuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Downton_Abbey_characters#Kemal_Pamuk "List of Downton Abbey characters") in an episode of the first season of *[Downton Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downton_Abbey "Downton Abbey")*. His film credits include *[You Will Meet a Tall Dark Stranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Will_Meet_a_Tall_Dark_Stranger "You Will Meet a Tall Dark Stranger")* (2010). James was cast in *Stranger* while still in his final year at drama school. He was named a "Star of Tomorrow" in 2009 by *[Screen International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_International "Screen International")*.

In 2011, he starred in the short-lived horror series *[Bedlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedlam_(2011_TV_series) "Bedlam (2011 TV series)")*, playing the lead role of Jed Harper. That same year, he played James, an obnoxious night club rep, in the British comedy *[The Inbetweeners Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Inbetweeners_Movie "The Inbetweeners Movie")* (2011). In 2012, James appeared in the miniseries *[Room at the Top](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Room_at_the_Top_(novel) "Room at the Top (novel)")* as Jack Wales and [ITV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITV_(TV_network) "ITV (TV network)")'s *Case Sensitive* as Aiden Harper. He was in the films *The Domino Effect* (2012) and *[Underworld: Awakening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underworld:_Awakening "Underworld: Awakening")* (2012), playing the role of David in the latter opposite [Kate Beckinsale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Beckinsale "Kate Beckinsale"). He reprised David in [the fifth *Underworld* film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underworld:_Blood_Wars "Underworld: Blood Wars") as the film's male lead. James starred as Detective Walter William Clark Jr. in the American crime-drama series *[Golden Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Boy_(American_TV_series) "Golden Boy (American TV series)")* (2013), which aired for one season.

James' breakthrough role was his portrayal of [Tobias "Four" Eaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Divergent_characters#Tobias_"Four"_Eaton "List of Divergent characters") in *[Divergent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divergent_(film) "Divergent (film)")* (2014), a film adaptation of [Veronica Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veronica_Roth "Veronica Roth")'s [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divergent_(novel) "Divergent (novel)"). He reprised the character in the sequel, *[The Divergent Series: Insurgent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Divergent_Series:_Insurgent "The Divergent Series: Insurgent")* (2015). He returned in one more *Divergent* film, *[The Divergent Series: Allegiant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Divergent_Series:_Allegiant "The Divergent Series: Allegiant")* (2016). James performed his own stunts in the film series.

He starred alongside [Amber Heard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Heard "Amber Heard"), [Billy Bob Thornton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Bob_Thornton "Billy Bob Thornton"), and [Jim Sturgess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Sturgess "Jim Sturgess") in the film adaptation of *[London Fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Fields_(film) "London Fields (film)")*, and in the indie drama *[The Benefactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franny_(film) "Franny (film)")* (2015), with [Richard Gere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Gere "Richard Gere") and [Dakota Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakota_Fanning "Dakota Fanning"). James executive produced and starred as Michael in *[Backstabbing for Beginners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backstabbing_for_Beginners "Backstabbing for Beginners")* (2018). He starred as Will Younger in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") film *[How It Ends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_It_Ends_(2018_film) "How It Ends (2018 film)")* (2018). In 2019, James executive produced and played Sidney Parker in the British series *[Sanditon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanditon_(TV_series) "Sanditon (TV series)")* as a male lead. It is an eight-part period-drama adaptation of [Jane Austen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Austen "Jane Austen")'s final novel, written only months before her death in 1817. James did not reprise his role for the series' second and third seasons. He then executive produced and starred in the film *[Lying and Stealing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lying_and_Stealing "Lying and Stealing")*. James launched his own film and TV production company, named Untapped, that same year.

James produced and appeared in the 2020 science fiction film *[Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive_(film) "Archive (film)")*, playing a robotics scientist working on a secret project involving building a prototype based on his dead wife. He starred in the romance series *[The Time Traveler's Wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Time_Traveler%27s_Wife_(TV_series) "The Time Traveler's Wife (TV series)")*, which premiered in May 2022. *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")*'s Angie Han described James's portrayal of the titular time traveller as "dashing and a little sardonic" and highlighted his and co-star [Rose Leslie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Leslie "Rose Leslie")'s "inability to generate any real sparks between them". James played a series regular role in the second season of the [satire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satire "Satire") series *[The White Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_Lotus "The White Lotus")*.

In 2024, he starred as Edward "Eddie" Horniman, Duke of Halstead, alongside [Kaya Scodelario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaya_Scodelario "Kaya Scodelario"), [Daniel Ings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ings "Daniel Ings"), and [Ray Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Winstone "Ray Winstone"), in the [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie") television series *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2024_TV_series) "The Gentlemen (2024 TV series)")*, released on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). He reprised his role as the Duke of Halstead in 2026 .

In 2025, James starred in a dual lead role as the twin brothers Hal and Bill Shelburn in [Oz Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oz_Perkins "Oz Perkins")'s comedy horror *[The Monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Monkey_(film) "The Monkey (film)")*.

## Other ventures

Outside his acting career, James was the singer and guitarist of the London-based band Shere Khan, which disbanded in 2012. In 2015, he became the brand ambassador for [Hugo Boss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Boss "Hugo Boss") fragrances for men, appearing in print ads and commercials, and has also appeared in the 2024 and 2025 [Range Rover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Range_Rover "Range Rover") advertisements. James has supported UNHCR, the UN High Commissioner for Refugees, since 2016, and was appointed a Goodwill Ambassador in 2024.

## Personal life

Since 2018, James has been married to actress [Ruth Kearney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Kearney "Ruth Kearney"). They met at the [Bristol Old Vic Theatre School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Old_Vic_Theatre_School "Bristol Old Vic Theatre School"). The couple has two children. The family divides their time between [Venice Beach, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Beach,_California "Venice Beach, California"), and [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London").
