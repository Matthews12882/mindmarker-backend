# List of S&P 500 companies

The [S&P 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%26P_500 "S&P 500") is a [stock market index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_market_index "Stock market index") maintained by [S&P Dow Jones Indices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%26P_Dow_Jones_Indices "S&P Dow Jones Indices"). It comprises 503 [common stocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_stock "Common stock") which are issued by 500 companies with large [market capitalizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_capitalization "Market capitalization"), including two share classes of stock from three of its component companies: [Alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet_Inc. "Alphabet Inc."), [Fox Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Corporation "Fox Corporation") and [News Corp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_Corp "News Corp"). The index includes about 80% of the American stock market by capitalization. It is weighted by [free-float](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalization-weighted_index#Free-float_weighting "Capitalization-weighted index") market capitalization. An updated weighting of each component is available [online](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy).
