# Aaron Pierre (actor)

**Aaron Stone Pierre** (born 7 June 1994) is a British actor and model. He is best known for his roles as [John Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stewart_(character) "John Stewart (character)") / [Green Lantern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Lantern "Green Lantern") in the [DC Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Universe_(franchise) "DC Universe (franchise)") (DCU), Dev-Em in *[Krypton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krypton_(TV_series) "Krypton (TV series)")* (2018–2019), Caesar Garner in *[The Underground Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Underground_Railroad_(miniseries) "The Underground Railroad (miniseries)")* (2021), Rapper "Mid-Sized Sedan" in *[Old](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_(film) "Old (film)")* (2021), Terry Richmond in *[Rebel Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Ridge "Rebel Ridge")* (2024), [Malcolm X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malcolm_X "Malcolm X") in *[Genius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genius_(American_TV_series) "Genius (American TV series)")* (2024), and the voice of [Mufasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mufasa "Mufasa") in *[Mufasa: The Lion King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mufasa:_The_Lion_King "Mufasa: The Lion King")* (2024).

Pierre portrays Stewart in the DCU beginning with [*Lanterns*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanterns_(TV_series) "Lanterns (TV series)") (2026) and later in the upcoming [*Man of Tomorrow*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_Tomorrow_(film) "Man of Tomorrow (film)") (2027).

## Early life

Aaron Stone Pierre was born in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London") on 7 June 1994. His mother is from Jamaica, and he has Curaçaoan and Sierra Leonean heritage on his father’s side. He participated in athletics and sprinting as a child, having idolised American gold medalist sprinter [Maurice Greene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Greene_(sprinter) "Maurice Greene (sprinter)"), and developed an interest in acting as a teenager. He attended [The John Fisher School in Purley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_John_Fisher_School "The John Fisher School") and later joined the [Croydon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croydon "Croydon") Young People's Theatre (CRYPT). He studied Performing Arts at [Lewisham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewisham_College "Lewisham College") and trained in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto") before joining the [London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Academy_of_Music_and_Dramatic_Art "London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art"), where he graduated in 2016.

## Career

### Acting

Pierre appeared in two episodes of the [BBC One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_One "BBC One") series *[The A Word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_A_Word "The A Word")* and played a Roman soldier Antonius in series 1 of the [Sky Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Atlantic "Sky Atlantic") series *[Britannia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britannia_(TV_series) "Britannia (TV series)")*. In 2018, he began starring as Dev-Em in the [Syfy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syfy "Syfy") series *[Krypton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krypton_(TV_series) "Krypton (TV series)")*. That same year, Pierre starred as [Cassio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Cassio "Michael Cassio") in *[Othello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Othello "Othello")* at [Shakespeare's Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespeare%27s_Globe "Shakespeare's Globe"). For his performance, he received an [Ian Charleson Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Charleson_Award "Ian Charleson Award") commendation. He played the King opposite [Lenny Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenny_Henry "Lenny Henry") in the 2019 production of *[King Hedley II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Hedley_II "King Hedley II")* at the [Theatre Royal Stratford East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_Royal_Stratford_East "Theatre Royal Stratford East").

American director [Barry Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Jenkins "Barry Jenkins") saw Pierre in *Othello* and sent him a message afterwards, inviting him to audition for his new series. Pierre landed the role of Caesar in *[The Underground Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Underground_Railroad_(miniseries) "The Underground Railroad (miniseries)")*, released on [Amazon Prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime "Amazon Prime") in May 2021. In July 2021, Pierre appeared as Mid-Sized Sedan / Brendan in [M. Night Shyamalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Night_Shyamalan "M. Night Shyamalan")'s film *[Old](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_(film) "Old (film)")*.

In August 2021, it was reported that Pierre would reteam with Barry Jenkins as a young [Mufasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Lion_King_(franchise)_characters#Mufasa "List of The Lion King (franchise) characters") in a *[Lion King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lion_King_(2019_film)#Follow-up_film "The Lion King (2019 film)")* film. Pierre provided the voice of young Mufasa in *[Mufasa: The Lion King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mufasa:_The_Lion_King "Mufasa: The Lion King")*, released in December 2024.

In October 2021, after [John Boyega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Boyega "John Boyega") stepped down for family reasons, Pierre starred in [Jeremy Saulnier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Saulnier "Jeremy Saulnier")'s film *[Rebel Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Ridge "Rebel Ridge")*, streamed on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") in 2024.

In February 2022, Pierre joined the cast of the then-planned superhero film *[Blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_(upcoming_film) "Blade (upcoming film)")*, which was to be set in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe "Marvel Cinematic Universe") and was scheduled to premiere on November 7, 2025. He was released from the project in March 2024, as a result of script rewrites.

Pierre won the [Best Supporting Performance in a Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Screen_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Performance_in_a_Film "Canadian Screen Award for Best Supporting Performance in a Film") at the [11th Canadian Screen Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11th_Canadian_Screen_Awards "11th Canadian Screen Awards") in 2023, for his performance as Francis in the film *[Brother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brother_(2022_film) "Brother (2022 film)")*. In 2023, he starred in *[Foe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foe_(film) "Foe (film)")*, an adaptation of the [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foe_(Reid_novel) "Foe (Reid novel)") by [Iain Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iain_Reid "Iain Reid").

In October 2024, Pierre was cast as [John Stewart / Green Lantern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stewart_(character) "John Stewart (character)") in [DC Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Studios "DC Studios")'s [*Lanterns*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanterns_(TV_series) "Lanterns (TV series)") television series. According to *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")* magazine, 2024 was "a busy breakout year that included portraying Malcolm X in the *Genius: MLK/X* series, flexing his best Rambo in Netflix’s hit fall action thriller *Rebel Ridge*, and signing on to play DC Universe space cop John Stewart in the upcoming Green Lantern streaming series *Lanterns*. These days, Pierre’s rising star is everywhere".

In June 2025, it was announced that Pierre would be starring alongside [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley "Margaret Qualley") and [Patrick Schwarzenegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Schwarzenegger "Patrick Schwarzenegger") in [*Love of Your Life*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_of_Your_Life_(film) "Love of Your Life (film)") for [Amazon MGM Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_MGM_Studios "Amazon MGM Studios"). In July 2025, it was announced that Pierre would be joining [Jennifer Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hudson "Jennifer Hudson") and [Ayesha Curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayesha_Curry "Ayesha Curry") in [Stephen Curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Curry "Stephen Curry") and [Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures "Sony Pictures") animated film [*Goat*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goat_(2026_film) "Goat (2026 film)"). In August 2025, It was announced that Pierre would be joining the cast in *[Star Wars: Starfighter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Starfighter "Star Wars: Starfighter")* alongside [Amy Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Adams "Amy Adams"), and [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling "Ryan Gosling"), in the [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy "Shawn Levy")-directed *Star Wars* movie.

### Music

Pierre is also a rapper, although he rarely makes music. In 2019, Pierre released a rap EP titled *Ante Meridiem* under the name **Ilan Stone**. In 2021, he recorded the song "More Dough for the Cookin", which was featured on the soundtrack for the motion picture *[Old](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_(film) "Old (film)")*. In 2026, his track "Staying" began gaining more traction online due to being used in *Lanterns-*based memes.

## Personal life

Pierre briefly dated American singer [Teyana Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teyana_Taylor "Teyana Taylor") in 2025. He has a son from a previous relationship.

Pierre took up boxing in 2016 and [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu "Brazilian jiu-jitsu") in 2019, winning his first jiu-jitsu tournament in 2023.

Pierre revealed his [OCD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obsessive%E2%80%93compulsive_disorder "Obsessive–compulsive disorder") diagnosis during a press event for HBO’s “[Lanterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanterns_(TV_series) "Lanterns (TV series)")” series in 2026.
