# Opinion polling for the next United Kingdom general election

Various organisations continually conduct opinion polls to gauge voter intention in anticipation of the [next United Kingdom general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_United_Kingdom_general_election "Next United Kingdom general election"). The next general election must be held no later than 15 August 2029 under the [Dissolution and Calling of Parliament Act 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_and_Calling_of_Parliament_Act_2022 "Dissolution and Calling of Parliament Act 2022"), which mandates that any Parliament automatically dissolves five years after it first met – unless it is dissolved earlier at the request of the prime minister – and polling day occurs no more than 25 working days later.

Most of the polling companies listed are members of the [British Polling Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Polling_Council "British Polling Council") (BPC) and abide by its disclosure rules. The dates of the polls range from the [2024 United Kingdom general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election "2024 United Kingdom general election"), held on 4 July, to the present.

## National poll results

Most opinion polls do not cover [Northern Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ireland "Northern Ireland"), which has [different major political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_Northern_Ireland "List of political parties in Northern Ireland") from the rest of the United Kingdom. In the 'area' column of the tables below, "GB" ([Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain "Great Britain")) denotes polls that do not include Northern Ireland, whereas "UK" ([United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom")) denotes polls that do. [Plaid Cymru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaid_Cymru "Plaid Cymru") only stands candidates in [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales "Wales"), the [Scottish National Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_National_Party "Scottish National Party") only stands candidates in [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland "Scotland"), and the [Conservative Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_(UK) "Conservative Party (UK)") is the only party with its own column below that [stands candidates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ireland_Conservatives "Northern Ireland Conservatives") in Northern Ireland.

Due to rounding, total figures may not add up to 100%. The lead is calculated by subtracting the polling percentage of the first-placed party by that of the second-placed party. The first-placed party is shaded with its party colour, and in boldface; the second-placed party is shaded in grey. The table can be sorted by all features; to sort by party vote share, click on the coloured ribbons. Where polls specify parties within 'other', this may be expandable by pressing the [show] button in the 'other' cell.
