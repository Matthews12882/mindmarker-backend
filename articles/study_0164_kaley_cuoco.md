# Kaley Cuoco

**Kaley Christine Cuoco** ([/ˈkwoʊkoʊ/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*KWOH-koh*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born November 30, 1985) is an American actress. She starred as Bridget Hennessy on the [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") sitcom *[8 Simple Rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_Simple_Rules "8 Simple Rules")* (2002–2005), [Penny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_(The_Big_Bang_Theory) "Penny (The Big Bang Theory)") on the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") sitcom *[The Big Bang Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory "The Big Bang Theory")* (2007–2019), and as the title character in the [HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") comic thriller *[The Flight Attendant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flight_Attendant "The Flight Attendant")* (2020–2022).

Cuoco has also played [Billie Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Jenkins "Billie Jenkins") in the fantasy series *[Charmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charmed "Charmed")* (2005–2006), voiced the [title role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harley_Quinn "Harley Quinn") in the animated series *[Harley Quinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harley_Quinn_(TV_series) "Harley Quinn (TV series)")* since 2019, and played the lead role in the comedy thriller series *[Based on a True Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Based_on_a_True_Story_(TV_series) "Based on a True Story (TV series)")* (2023). Her film work includes *[Quicksand: No Escape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quicksand:_No_Escape "Quicksand: No Escape")* (1992) and *[Growing Up Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Growing_Up_Brady_(film) "Growing Up Brady (film)")* (2000), as well as *[Virtuosity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtuosity "Virtuosity")* (1995), *[Hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hop_(film) "Hop (film)")* (2011), *[The Wedding Ringer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Ringer "The Wedding Ringer")* (2015), *[The Man from Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Man_from_Toronto_(2022_film) "The Man from Toronto (2022 film)")*, *[Meet Cute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meet_Cute_(film) "Meet Cute (film)")* (both 2022), and *[Role Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role_Play "Role Play")* (2024). She received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame") in 2014, and in 2017 founded [Yes, Norman Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yes,_Norman_Productions "Yes, Norman Productions").

## Early life

Kaley Christine Cuoco was born on November 30, 1985, in [Camarillo, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camarillo,_California "Camarillo, California"). She is the elder daughter of Layne Ann ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9e "Née") Wingate), a homemaker, and Gary Carmine Cuoco, a real estate agent. Her father is of Italian descent while her mother is of English and German ancestry. Her sister, [Briana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Briana_Cuoco "Briana Cuoco"), is an actress and singer who competed on [the fifth season of *The Voice*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(American_season_5) "The Voice (American season 5)") and co-starred with her on the HBO Max series *[The Flight Attendant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flight_Attendant "The Flight Attendant")* and on *[Harley Quinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harley_Quinn_(TV_series) "Harley Quinn (TV series)")* as Barbara Gordon/Batgirl. As a child, Cuoco was a ranked amateur tennis player, a sport she took up when she was three years old. She stopped playing at the age of 16 to pursue a career in acting.

## Career

### 1995–2006: Early work and *8 Simple Rules*

Cuoco made her first feature film appearance as the young Karin Carter in the 1995 action thriller *[Virtuosity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtuosity "Virtuosity")*. In 2000, she portrayed former *[The Brady Bunch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Brady_Bunch "The Brady Bunch")* star [Maureen McCormick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maureen_McCormick "Maureen McCormick") in the TV film *[Growing Up Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Growing_Up_Brady_(film) "Growing Up Brady (film)")* as well as starring in the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") [original film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Disney_Channel_original_films "List of Disney Channel original films") *[Alley Cats Strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alley_Cats_Strike "Alley Cats Strike")*, and then in 2001, she appeared on the [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "CBS") sitcom *[Ladies Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladies_Man_(1999_TV_series) "Ladies Man (1999 TV series)")*. In September 2002, she began a starring role as Bridget Hennessy on the [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") sitcom *[8 Simple Rules for Dating My Teenage Daughter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_Simple_Rules_for_Dating_My_Teenage_Daughter "8 Simple Rules for Dating My Teenage Daughter")* (afterwards *8 Simple Rules*). Bridget was the oldest child, despite Cuoco being six years younger than [Amy Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Davidson "Amy Davidson"), who played her younger sister.

In addition to the final season of *8 Simple Rules*, Cuoco had starring roles on the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") miniseries *[10.5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10.5_(miniseries) "10.5 (miniseries)")*, the [ABC Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeform_(TV_channel) "Freeform (TV channel)") original film *[Crimes of Fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimes_of_Fashion "Crimes of Fashion")*, the independent film *Debating Robert Lee*, and in the film *[The Hollow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollow_(2004_film) "The Hollow (2004 film)")* at the same time. She also voiced the character of Brandy Harrington, a 14-year-old anthropomorphic mixed-breed dog, on the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel") animated series *[Brandy & Mr. Whiskers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandy_%26_Mr._Whiskers "Brandy & Mr. Whiskers")*. She voiced the character of Kirstee Smith in *[Bratz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bratz "Bratz")* from 2004 to 2005. In the eighth season of the TV series *[Charmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charmed "Charmed")*, she appeared as [Billie Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Jenkins "Billie Jenkins"), a powerful young [witch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witch "Witch") with the powers of [telekinesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telekinesis "Telekinesis") and projection. Executive producer [Brad Kern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Kern "Brad Kern") stated that Cuoco's character was initially brought in as a possible spin-off.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

### 2007–2016: *The Big Bang Theory* and other roles

In September 2007, Cuoco began starring in the CBS sitcom *[The Big Bang Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory "The Big Bang Theory")*, playing [Penny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_(The_Big_Bang_Theory) "Penny (The Big Bang Theory)"), a [Cheesecake Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheesecake_Factory "Cheesecake Factory") employee and aspiring actress who lives across the hall from [physicists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist "Physicist") [Dr. Leonard Hofstadter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Hofstadter "Leonard Hofstadter") and [Dr. Sheldon Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon_Cooper "Sheldon Cooper"). Prior to the 2010–2011 season, she earned US$60,000 an episode for the series, the same as her co-stars. In 2010, the cast negotiated a raise for each actor to make US$200,000 per episode. As of August 2014, Cuoco and her co-stars from *The Big Bang Theory* [Johnny Galecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Galecki "Johnny Galecki") and [Jim Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Parsons "Jim Parsons") each earned an estimated US$1 million per episode.

In late October 2012, Cuoco enlisted the help of her sister Briana to organize a [flash mob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash_mob "Flash mob") on the set of the show, in which she and the cast and crew surprised the studio audience by suddenly [lip syncing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lip_sync "Lip sync") and dancing to [Carly Rae Jepsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carly_Rae_Jepsen "Carly Rae Jepsen")'s song "[Call Me Maybe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_Me_Maybe "Call Me Maybe")". A video of the event was posted on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") and became a [viral success](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_video "Viral video"). A clip from the performance was also played during Cuoco's January 8, 2013, appearance on the late-night talk show *[Conan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_(talk_show) "Conan (talk show)")*.

Cuoco starred in the [Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network) "Lifetime (TV network)") film *[To Be Fat like Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Be_Fat_like_Me "To Be Fat like Me")*, which premiered on January 8, 2007. She played a minor role in the *[Prison Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prison_Break "Prison Break")* episodes "[The Message](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Message_(Prison_Break) "The Message (Prison Break)")" and "[Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_(Prison_Break) "Chicago (Prison Break)")", as well as starring in the comedy horror spoof *[Killer Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killer_Movie "Killer Movie")* in 2008. She also appeared in the 2010 film *[The Penthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Penthouse_(2010_film) "The Penthouse (2010 film)")*. In 2011, she posed nude in [*Allure* magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allure_(magazine) "Allure (magazine)")'s annual "Naked Truth" feature. That same year, she appeared in the films *[Hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hop_(film) "Hop (film)")* and *[The Last Ride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Ride_(2011_film) "The Last Ride (2011 film)")*. She was also chosen to host the [Teen Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Choice_Awards "Teen Choice Awards") in 2011, as well as the [People's Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Choice_Awards "People's Choice Awards") in 2012 and 2013. She is a regular on [Priceline.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priceline.com "Priceline.com") commercials as the daughter of [William Shatner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shatner "William Shatner")'s character.

### 2017–present: *Harley Quinn* animated series and *The Flight Attendant*

In October 2017, Cuoco founded [Yes, Norman Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yes,_Norman_Productions "Yes, Norman Productions"), a television [production company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_company "Production company") which entered an exclusive multi-year [first-look](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-look "First-look") production deal with [Warner Bros. Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Television "Warner Bros. Television"). Her first project through Yes, Norman was an adaptation of the book *[The Flight Attendant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flight_Attendant_(novel) "The Flight Attendant (novel)")*, written by author [Chris Bohjalian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Bohjalian "Chris Bohjalian"), which was developed into a comedy-drama series [of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flight_Attendant "The Flight Attendant"). Cuoco starred in the series as Cassie Bowden, and also served as executive producer. *[The Flight Attendant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flight_Attendant "The Flight Attendant")* premiered on November 26, 2020, on [WarnerMedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WarnerMedia "WarnerMedia")'s streaming platform [HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") to widespread critical acclaim, and was renewed for a second season on December 18, 2020.

In October 2018, it was announced that Cuoco would voice the [title character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harley_Quinn "Harley Quinn") of the [DC Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Universe_(streaming_service) "DC Universe (streaming service)") adult animation series *[Harley Quinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harley_Quinn_(TV_series) "Harley Quinn (TV series)")*, and also serve as executive producer. The series premiered on November 29, 2019, to critical acclaim. In September 2020, it was announced that the series was renewed for a third season and would move to HBO Max.

In April 2020, Cuoco joined the cast of the action comedy film *[The Man from Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Man_from_Toronto_(2021_film) "The Man from Toronto (2021 film)")* as the female lead alongside [Kevin Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Hart "Kevin Hart") and [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson "Woody Harrelson"). During May 2021, her production company expanded its [Warner Bros. Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Television "Warner Bros. Television") overall deal. In 2023, Cuoco launched an online [pet product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pet "Pet") business called "Oh Norman!".

## Personal life

While working on *The Big Bang Theory*, Cuoco privately dated co-star Johnny Galecki for roughly two years, until December 2009. She later told *[CBS Watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Watch "CBS Watch")* in September 2010 that they have remained on good terms since ending their relationship.

On September 13, 2010, Cuoco broke her leg in a horse-riding accident and missed the filming of two episodes of *The Big Bang Theory*.

In October 2011, she became engaged to [addiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addiction "Addiction") specialist Josh Resnik, but ended their engagement by March 2012. She became engaged to professional tennis player [Ryan Sweeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Sweeting "Ryan Sweeting") in September 2013, after three months of dating. They married on December 31, 2013, in [Santa Susana, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Susana,_California "Santa Susana, California"). On September 25, 2015, it was announced that she and Sweeting had decided to divorce. The divorce was finalized on May 9, 2016.

Cuoco began dating [equestrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_equestrian_sports "List of equestrian sports") [Karl Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Cook "Karl Cook") in late 2016. They became engaged on November 30, 2017, Cuoco's 32nd birthday, and were married on June 30, 2018. In September 2021, the couple announced their separation, stating "we have realized that our current paths have taken us in opposite directions". In June 2022, their divorce was finalized.

Cuoco confirmed her relationship with actor [Tom Pelphrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Pelphrey "Tom Pelphrey") in May 2022. Five months later, she announced on Instagram that they were expecting their first child together. Cuoco gave birth to their daughter Matilda on March 30, 2023. They announced their engagement on August 14, 2024. The couple's second daughter, Philomena, was born on October 12, 2026.
