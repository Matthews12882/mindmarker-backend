# Neem Karoli Baba

**Neem Karoli Baba** (born **Lakshman Narayan Sharma**; c. 1900 - 11 September 1973) ([Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi_language "Hindi language"): नीम करौली बाबा, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hindi "Romanization of Hindi"): *neem karoli bābā*), was an Indian spiritual master, widely considered to be one of the most revered and influential in 20th century India. His devotees called him "maharaj-ji". He was an ardent devotee of lord Hanuman. His name "Neem Karoli" Baba comes from Neem Karoli (or Neeb Karori), a village in the Farrukhabad district of Uttar Pradesh, where he spent significant time during his early years and perform several documented miracles. He passed in September 11, 1973 because of diabetic coma. His last words are "Jaya Jagadish Hare" meaning "Victory to you, O Lord of the World, O Hari."

## Biography

Lakshman Narayan Sharma was born around 1900 in the village Akbarpur in the [Firozabad district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firozabad_district "Firozabad district") of [Uttar Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Pradesh "Uttar Pradesh"), India, in a wealthy [Brahmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmin "Brahmin") family. After being married by his parents at the age of 11, he left home to become a wandering [sadhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadhu "Sadhu").
Neem Karoli Baba, known at the time as Baba Lakshman Das (also spelled "Laxman Das"), left his home in c. 1911 and wandered through Northern India. He later returned home, at his father's request, to live a settled married life. He fathered two sons Anek Singh and Dhrma Narayan Sharma and a daughter Girija. During his life two main [ashrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashram "Ashram") were built, at Kainchi and at Vrindavan. The [Kainchi Dham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainchi_Dham "Kainchi Dham"), where he stayed in the last decade of his life, was built in 1964 with a Hanuman ji temple. In time, over 100 temples were constructed in his name.

Neem Karoli Baba died at about 1:15 a.m. on 11 September 1973 in a hospital at [Vrindavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrindavan "Vrindavan"), India, after slipping into a diabetic coma. He had been returning by night train to [Kainchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainchi "Kainchi") near [Nainital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nainital "Nainital"), from [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra "Agra") where he had visited a heart specialist due to experiencing pains in his chest. He and his traveling companions had disembarked at [Mathura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathura "Mathura") railway station where he began convulsing and was taken to a hospital where he died. His last words, repeated several times, were "[Jaya Jagadish Hare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Jagdish_Hare "Jai Jagdish Hare")" ("Hail to the Lord of the Universe"). His [samadhi (shrine)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samadhi_(shrine) "Samadhi (shrine)") was built in the complex of the Vrindavan ashram.

## Legacy

American businessman and investor [Steve Jobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Jobs "Steve Jobs") idolized Baba, traveling to India with his friend and eventual [Apple Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Inc. "Apple Inc.") employee [Daniel Kottke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Kottke "Daniel Kottke") in mid-1974 to visit him, not realizing he had died the previous year.

After returning to the United States, [Ram Dass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Dass "Ram Dass") and [Larry Brilliant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Brilliant "Larry Brilliant") founded the [Seva Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seva_Foundation "Seva Foundation"), an international health organization based in Berkeley, California. In the late 2000s the 'Love Serve Remember Foundation' was established to promote the teachings of Neem Karoli Baba. In 2024 his follower [Rahul Verma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahul_Verma "Rahul Verma") founded the NKB Divine Meditation Foundation, offering free guided meditation sessions based on the teachings of Neem Karoli Baba.

Each year on June 15, to commemorate the foundation day of Kainchi Dham in 1964, a large [mela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mela "Mela") (fair) known as *Kainchi Dham Mela*, is organised, in which thousands of devotees and pilgrims take part.

## In popular culture

### Films and documentaries

*Windfall of Grace* is a documentary film directed by Japna Tulsi and Sandip Pokhrel (Nepal) about Neem Karoli Baba and his devotees. The film explores Baba's life and legacy through accounts by devotees, kirtan and visits to places associated with him.

*Moved by Grace* is a documentary about devotees of Neem Karoli Baba and their experiences following their association with him. The film was released on 1 December 2022, the anniversary of Baba's birth.

*[One Track Heart: The Story of Krishna Das](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Track_Heart:_The_Story_of_Krishna_Das "One Track Heart: The Story of Krishna Das")* is a documentary film about [Krishna Das](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_Das_(singer) "Krishna Das (singer)") in 2012. It follows his journey to India in search of Neem Karoli Baba and his subsequent spiritual relationship with Baba.

*American Yogi* is a documentary film directed by Steven Newmark and based on the life of Neem Karoli Baba. The film follows Newmark's journey and his efforts to present Indian spirituality to Western audiences.

*Brilliant Disguise: The Samadhi of K.C. Tewari* is a 2022 documentary directed by David Silver about K.C. Tewari, a devotee of Neem Karoli Baba. The film follows Tewari's life and his association with Baba and Western spiritual seekers who travelled to India.

*Shree Baba Neeb Karori Maharaj* is a 2026 Hindi-language biographical drama directed by Sharad Singh Thakur, with Subodh Bhave portraying Neem Karoli Baba. The film was released on 29 May 2026.

*[Hanuman Ansh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanuman_Ansh "Hanuman Ansh")* is a 2026 Hindi-language devotional drama written and directed by Vishal Chaturvedi. The film is based on real-life incidents associated with Neem Karoli Baba and stars Shobhinaw Satyaa as Baba. It was released in theatres on 7 August 2026.
