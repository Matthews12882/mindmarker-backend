# Ed Sheeran

**Edward Christopher Sheeran** ([/ˈʃɪərən/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*SHEER-ən*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born 17 February 1991) is an English singer-songwriter. Born in [Halifax, West Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_West_Yorkshire "Halifax, West Yorkshire"), and raised in [Framlingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham "Framlingham"), Suffolk, he began writing songs around the age of eleven. In early 2011, Sheeran independently released the extended play *[No.5 Collaborations Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No.5_Collaborations_Project "No.5 Collaborations Project")*. He signed with [Asylum Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asylum_Records "Asylum Records") the same year.

Sheeran's debut album, [*+* ("*Plus*")](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%2B_(album) "+ (album)") (2011), topped the [UK Albums Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Albums_Chart "UK Albums Chart") and featured his breakout hit, "[The A Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_A_Team_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "The A Team (Ed Sheeran song)")". His second studio album, [*×* ("*Multiply*")](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(Ed_Sheeran_album) "X (Ed Sheeran album)") (2014), became the second-best-selling album worldwide of 2015 and won the [Brit Award for British Album of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award_for_British_Album_of_the_Year "Brit Award for British Album of the Year"). Its third single, "[Thinking Out Loud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Out_Loud "Thinking Out Loud")", won two [Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Awards "Grammy Awards"), including [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year "Grammy Award for Song of the Year"). That same year, he established his own record label, [Gingerbread Man Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gingerbread_Man_Records "Gingerbread Man Records").

Sheeran's third album, [*÷* ("*Divide*")](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%B7_(album) "÷ (album)") (2017), became the [best-selling album worldwide of 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_albums#Best-selling_album_by_year_worldwide "List of best-selling albums") and charted ten top-10 singles on the [UK singles chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_singles_chart "UK singles chart"). Its joint-lead singles, "[Shape of You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_of_You "Shape of You")" and "[Castle on the Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_on_the_Hill "Castle on the Hill")", broke records in several countries by debuting in the top two positions on the charts, while its fourth single, "[Perfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Perfect (Ed Sheeran song)")", reached number one in the US, Australia, and the UK. The world's best-selling artist of 2017, he was named the [Global Recording Artist of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Recording_Artist_of_the_Year "Global Recording Artist of the Year"). His next albums, *[No.6 Collaborations Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No.6_Collaborations_Project "No.6 Collaborations Project")* (2019), [*=* ("*Equals*")](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%3D_(album) "= (album)") (2021) and [*−* ("*Subtract*")](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E2%88%92_(album) "− (album)") (2023) each topped the charts in most major markets. During this period, he accumulated seven UK number-one singles: "[I Don't Care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Care_(Ed_Sheeran_and_Justin_Bieber_song) "I Don't Care (Ed Sheeran and Justin Bieber song)")", "[Beautiful People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_People_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Beautiful People (Ed Sheeran song)")", "[Take Me Back to London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_Me_Back_to_London "Take Me Back to London")", "[Bad Habits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Habits_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Bad Habits (Ed Sheeran song)")", "[Shivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivers_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Shivers (Ed Sheeran song)")", "[Merry Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merry_Christmas_(song) "Merry Christmas (song)")" and "[Eyes Closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyes_Closed_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Eyes Closed (Ed Sheeran song)")". He has since released the albums *[Autumn Variations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autumn_Variations "Autumn Variations")* (2023) and  *[Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play_(Ed_Sheeran_album) "Play (Ed Sheeran album)")* (2025) to varying critical and commercial success.

Sheeran has sold 200 million records worldwide, making him one of the [world's best-selling music artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World%27s_best-selling_music_artists "World's best-selling music artists"). He has 119 million [RIAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIAA "RIAA")-certified units in the US, and two of his albums are in the list of the [best-selling albums in UK chart history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_albums_in_the_United_Kingdom "List of best-selling albums in the United Kingdom"). In December 2019, the [Official Charts Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Charts_Company "Official Charts Company") named him its artist of the decade, with the most combined success in the UK album and singles charts in the 2010s. As of September 2026, he is the fourth-most-followed artist on [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify "Spotify"). Several of his tours are amongst the [most-attended concert tours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-attended_concert_tours "List of most-attended concert tours"), and his [÷ Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%B7_Tour "÷ Tour") became the [highest-grossing of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_concert_tours "List of highest-grossing concert tours") in August 2019. An alumnus of the [National Youth Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Youth_Theatre "National Youth Theatre") in London, Sheeran's acting roles include appearing in the 2019 film *[Yesterday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yesterday_(2019_film) "Yesterday (2019 film)")*, playing himself. In 2025, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine listed him as one of [the world's 100 most influential people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100").

## Early life and education

Edward Christopher Sheeran was born on 17 February 1991 in [Halifax, West Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_West_Yorkshire "Halifax, West Yorkshire"). His early childhood home was on Birchcliffe Road in nearby [Hebden Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebden_Bridge "Hebden Bridge"). His father, John, was a curator at [Cartwright Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartwright_Hall "Cartwright Hall") in [Bradford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford "Bradford"), and his mother, Imogen, worked at [Manchester City Art Gallery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_City_Art_Gallery "Manchester City Art Gallery"). In December 1995, at the age of four, he moved with his family from Hebden Bridge to [Framlingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham "Framlingham") in [Suffolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffolk "Suffolk"), where he attended the independent [Brandeston Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeston_Hall "Brandeston Hall") [preparatory school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preparatory_school_(United_Kingdom) "Preparatory school (United Kingdom)") (now [Framlingham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham_College "Framlingham College") Prep School), and later [Thomas Mills High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Mills_High_School "Thomas Mills High School"), also in Framlingham. He has an older brother named Matthew, who works as a composer.

Sheeran's parents, John and Imogen, are from London. His father is Irish, and Sheeran has stated that his father is from a "very large" [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Catholic "Irish Catholic") family. John is an art curator and lecturer, and Imogen is a culture publicist turned jewellery designer. His parents ran Sheeran Lock, an independent art consultancy, from 1990 to 2010. Sheeran's grandfather was a [Belfast Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulster_Protestants "Ulster Protestants") who married a Catholic from the [Republic of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland "Republic of Ireland") during a time of [sectarian intolerance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sectarian_violence "Sectarian violence").

From a young age, Sheeran showed a strong inclination toward music. He sang at a local church choir at the age of four, learned how to play the guitar at age eleven, and began writing songs while at Thomas Mills High School in Framlingham. He also played the cello when he was younger. A 2004 school report described him as a "natural performer", and his classmates also voted him "most likely to be famous".

As a teenager, he was accepted into the [National Youth Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Youth_Theatre "National Youth Theatre") in London. He successfully auditioned for [Youth Music Theatre UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_Music_Theatre_UK "Youth Music Theatre UK") in 2007 and joined their production of *[Frankenstein – A New Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankenstein_%E2%80%93_A_New_Musical "Frankenstein – A New Musical")* in [Plymouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth "Plymouth"). He later became a patron of Youth Music Theatre UK, now renamed British Youth Music Theatre, and of [Access to Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_to_Music "Access to Music"), where he studied Artist Development. Sheeran is a [second cousin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_cousin "Second cousin") of Northern Irish broadcaster [Gordon Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Burns "Gordon Burns"), who hosted the British game show *[The Krypton Factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Krypton_Factor "The Krypton Factor")*.

## Career

### 2004–2010: career beginnings

Sheeran began recording music in 2004, and at the age of thirteen independently released his first collection of work, titled *Spinning Man*. He has been friends with fellow English singer [Passenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passenger_(singer) "Passenger (singer)") since he was 15, with the two playing the same gig in Cambridge. He moved to London in 2008 and began playing in small venues. In 2008, he auditioned for the ITV series *[Britannia High](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britannia_High "Britannia High")*. He also opened for [Nizlopi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizlopi "Nizlopi") in Norwich in April 2008, after being one of their guitar technicians. In the autumn of 2009, Sheeran began studying music at the [Academy of Contemporary Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Contemporary_Music "Academy of Contemporary Music") (ACM) in [Guildford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guildford "Guildford"), Surrey, as an undergraduate at the age of 18, but left without permission in the same year to support hip-hop artist [Just Jack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_Jack "Just Jack").
He released another EP in 2009, *You Need Me*, and also collaborated several times with Essex singer [Leddra Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leddra_Chapman "Leddra Chapman"), including [CeeLo Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CeeLo_Green "CeeLo Green")'s "[Fuck You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuck_You_(CeeLo_Green_song) "Fuck You (CeeLo Green song)")". In February 2010, Sheeran posted a video through [SB.TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SB.TV "SB.TV")—a channel launched by British entrepreneur [Jamal Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Edwards "Jamal Edwards")—and rapper [Example](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Example_(musician) "Example (musician)") invited Sheeran to tour with him. In the same month, he also released his critically acclaimed *[Loose Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_Change_(EP) "Loose Change (EP)")* EP, which featured his future debut single, "[The A Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_A_Team_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "The A Team (Ed Sheeran song)")".

Sheeran began to be seen by more people over the internet through YouTube and his fan base expanded, with him also receiving praise from *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")* newspaper and [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John "Elton John"). He played a *Station Session* in [St. Pancras International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Pancras_International "St. Pancras International") in June 2010. Some of the episode is on their Facebook page. Sheeran also self-released two other EPs in 2010, *Ed Sheeran: Live at the Bedford* and *[Songs I Wrote with Amy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_I_Wrote_with_Amy "Songs I Wrote with Amy")*, which is a collection of love songs he wrote in Wales with [Amy Wadge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Wadge "Amy Wadge"). When in Los Angeles in 2010, he was invited to perform at The Foxxhole, a club run by actor [Jamie Foxx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Foxx "Jamie Foxx"), which ended with an invitation to stay at Foxx's home.

On 8 January 2011, Sheeran released another independent EP, *[No.5 Collaborations Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No.5_Collaborations_Project "No.5 Collaborations Project")*, featuring grime artists such as [Wiley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiley_(rapper) "Wiley (rapper)"), [Jme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jme "Jme"), [Devlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devlin_(rapper) "Devlin (rapper)"), [Sway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sway_(British_musician) "Sway (British musician)") and [Ghetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghetts "Ghetts"). With this EP, Sheeran gained mainstream attention for having reached number 2 in the [iTunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITunes "ITunes") chart without any promotion or label, selling over 7,000 copies in the first week. Three months later, Sheeran put on a free show to fans at the Barfly in [Camden Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden_Town "Camden Town"). Over 1,000 fans turned up, so Sheeran played four different shows to ensure everyone saw a gig, including a gig outside on the street after the venue had closed. Later that month, Sheeran was signed to [Asylum Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asylum_Records "Asylum Records").

### 2011–2013: *+* ("*Plus*")

On 26 April 2011, Sheeran appeared on the BBC music show *[Later... with Jools Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Later..._with_Jools_Holland "Later... with Jools Holland")*, where he performed his debut single, "The A Team". Six weeks later, "The A Team" was released as a digital download in the UK. The release served as the lead single from Sheeran's debut studio album, *[+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%2B_(album) "+ (album)")* ("Plus"). "The A Team" entered the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart") at number three, selling over 58,000 copies in the first week. It was the best-selling debut single and the overall eighth-best selling single of 2011, selling 801,000 copies. The lead single also became a top ten hit in Australia, Germany, Ireland, Japan, Luxembourg, New Zealand, Norway and the Netherlands. During a headline set in the [BBC Introducing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Introducing "BBC Introducing") tent at [Glastonbury Festival 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury_Festival_2011 "Glastonbury Festival 2011"), Sheeran announced that "[You Need Me, I Don't Need You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Need_Me,_I_Don%27t_Need_You "You Need Me, I Don't Need You")" would be released on 26 August as the second single from the album. The second single peaked at number four on the UK Singles Chart. "[Lego House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_House "Lego House")" was released as the third single, reaching the top ten on the Australian, Irish and New Zealand Singles Charts. The music video for "Lego House" features actor [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint "Rupert Grint"), as a play on their similar appearance. "[Drunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drunk_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Drunk (Ed Sheeran song)")", released on 19 February 2012, became Sheeran's fourth consecutive top-ten single in the UK, peaking at number nine.

Sheeran released *+* on 12 September 2011. The album received generally favourable reviews from music critics and debuted at number one on the [UK Albums Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Albums_Chart "UK Albums Chart") for sales of 102,000 copies. By the end of 2011, sales of the album in the UK stood at 791,000; it became the second-best-selling debut album and the ninth-biggest-selling album there. The album has been certified platinum six times by the [British Phonographic Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Phonographic_Industry "British Phonographic Industry"), denoting shipments of 1.8 million copies. As of March 2012, the album had sold 1,021,072 copies in the UK. The album also reached the top five in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand and the US.

The song "[Moments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moments_(One_Direction_song) "Moments (One Direction song)")" on the [debut album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Up_All_Night_(One_Direction_album) "Up All Night (One Direction album)") by boy band [One Direction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Direction "One Direction"), released in November 2011, was co-written by Sheeran. At the [2012 Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Brit_Awards "2012 Brit Awards") on 21 February, Sheeran won the Brit Awards for [Best British Male Solo Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award_for_British_Male_Solo_Artist "Brit Award for British Male Solo Artist"), and [British Breakthrough Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award_for_British_Breakthrough_Act "Brit Award for British Breakthrough Act") of the Year. On 10 January 2012, it was announced that Sheeran would support [Snow Patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_Patrol "Snow Patrol") on their US tour from late March until May. His song "[Give Me Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Give_Me_Love_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Give Me Love (Ed Sheeran song)")" was featured in the episode "[Dangerous Liaisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dangerous_Liaisons_(The_Vampire_Diaries) "Dangerous Liaisons (The Vampire Diaries)")" of *[The Vampire Diaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Vampire_Diaries "The Vampire Diaries")*. At the [Ivor Novello Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivor_Novello_Awards "Ivor Novello Awards") in May 2012, Sheeran's "The A Team" bested [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele "Adele")'s "[Rolling in the Deep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_in_the_Deep "Rolling in the Deep")" and [Florence and the Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_and_the_Machine "Florence and the Machine")'s "[Shake It Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shake_It_Out "Shake It Out")" for Best Song Musically and Lyrically. Sheeran performed "The A Team" at the [Diamond Jubilee of Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Jubilee_of_Queen_Elizabeth_II "Diamond Jubilee of Queen Elizabeth II") [concert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Jubilee_Concert "Diamond Jubilee Concert") held on [The Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mall,_London "The Mall, London") outside [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace "Buckingham Palace") on 4 June 2012 and a cover of [Pink Floyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Floyd "Pink Floyd")'s "[Wish You Were Here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wish_You_Were_Here_(Pink_Floyd_song) "Wish You Were Here (Pink Floyd song)")" at the [closing ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Summer_Olympics_closing_ceremony "2012 Summer Olympics closing ceremony") of the [2012 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Summer_Olympics "2012 Summer Olympics") in London on 12 August 2012.

[Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift") contacted Sheeran after hearing his music while touring Australia in March 2012. He later co-wrote and provided vocals for "[Everything Has Changed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_Has_Changed "Everything Has Changed")", a single featured on Swift's fourth studio album, *[Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_(Taylor_Swift_album) "Red (Taylor Swift album)")*. Sheeran also contributed two songs to One Direction's second studio album, *[Take Me Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_Me_Home_(One_Direction_album) "Take Me Home (One Direction album)")*, released in November 2012; the single "[Little Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Things_(One_Direction_song) "Little Things (One Direction song)")" became the group's second number-one in the UK. Sheeran's album peaked at No. 5 on the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200"), while "The A Team" reached No. 16 on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"). In late 2012 and early 2013, he headlined a US tour of 6,000–9,000-capacity venues. "The A Team" received a nomination for Song of the Year at the [2013 Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Grammy_Awards "2013 Grammy Awards"). [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John "Elton John"), who runs Sheeran's management company, canvassed the award organisers to get Sheeran a performance slot at the ceremony but was told that Sheeran alone was not high-profile enough. John decided to appear with Sheeran to circumvent this problem. Sheeran was also featured on some tracks from Irish singer [Foy Vance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foy_Vance "Foy Vance")'s fourth album, *[Joy of Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joy_of_Nothing "Joy of Nothing")*.

From March to September 2013, Sheeran played at arenas and stadiums across North America as the opening act for Swift's [The Red Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Tour "The Red Tour"). According to Sheeran, it was then his biggest tour, and he added a scarlet RED tattoo to commemorate it. In October 2013, Sheeran headlined three sold-out shows at New York's [Madison Square Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Square_Garden "Madison Square Garden"). At the concert, Sheeran debuted new songs, including "[Tenerife Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenerife_Sea "Tenerife Sea")", a future track on his second studio album. Sheeran released "[I See Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_See_Fire "I See Fire")" on 5 November 2013. The song is featured in the [end credits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_credits "End credits") of the film *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug "The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug")*, [the film's soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_The_Hobbit_series "Music of The Hobbit series"), and on the deluxe version of his second album. Sheeran was nominated for [Best New Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_New_Artist "Best New Artist") at the [2014 Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Grammy_Awards "2014 Grammy Awards").

### 2014–2015: *×* ("*Multiply*")

On 24 March 2014, Sheeran performed at the [Teenage Cancer Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_Cancer_Trust "Teenage Cancer Trust") charity concert at the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall "Royal Albert Hall") in London where he unveiled "Take It Back", a track that would appear on the deluxe version of the second album. "[Sing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Sing (Ed Sheeran song)")", the lead single, was released on 7 April 2014. Sonically, the song is a departure from Sheeran's previous recordings. "Sing" was intended to create hype over the album release, but from concern that this might alienate Sheeran's fan base, "[One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "One (Ed Sheeran song)")", an acoustic ballad, was released on 16 May 2014; "One" also marked the first of several promotional singles released leading to the album release. By early June 2014, "Sing" had earned Sheeran his first number-one single in the UK.

Sheeran's second studio album, *[×](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(Ed_Sheeran_album) "X (Ed Sheeran album)")* ("Multiply"), was released worldwide on 23 June 2014. Spanning three years, Sheeran wrote more than 120 songs for the album. The album features tracks produced by [Rick Rubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Rubin "Rick Rubin"), [Pharrell Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharrell_Williams "Pharrell Williams"), and [Benny Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Blanco "Benny Blanco"), as well as that of Gosling's. *×* peaked at number one in both the UK Albums Chart and the US *Billboard* 200. To support the album, Sheeran embarked on [a world tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_Tour_(Ed_Sheeran_tour) "X Tour (Ed Sheeran tour)") starting on 6 August 2014 at [Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka "Osaka"), Japan. On 27 September 2014, Sheeran was one of the headline acts at the [Melbourne Cricket Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Cricket_Ground "Melbourne Cricket Ground") prior to the [2014 AFL Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_AFL_Grand_Final "2014 AFL Grand Final").
Following "[Don't](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Don't (Ed Sheeran song)")", "[Thinking Out Loud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Out_Loud "Thinking Out Loud")" was released on 24 September 2014 as the album's third single. Unlike his previous music videos, Sheeran took the lead role in the single's accompaniment, where he performed a ballroom dance. It became his second single to reach number one in the UK, and it also spent eight weeks at number two on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") (with only "[Uptown Funk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uptown_Funk "Uptown Funk")" by [Mark Ronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ronson "Mark Ronson") featuring [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars "Bruno Mars") keeping it from top spot). In 2014, combined [streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streaming_media "Streaming media") on Sheeran's catalogue in [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify "Spotify") reached 860 million; Spotify named him the most-streamed artist and *×* the most-streamed album. In the same year, the album made Sheeran [iTunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITunes "ITunes")' best-selling artist in the UK, Ireland and New Zealand.

*×* was nominated for Album of the Year at the [57th Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/57th_Grammy_Awards "57th Grammy Awards"). On 25 February, Sheeran won [British Male Solo Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award_for_British_Male_Solo_Artist "Brit Award for British Male Solo Artist") and British Album of the Year for *×* at the [2015 Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Brit_Awards "2015 Brit Awards"). On 21 May he received the [Ivor Novello Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivor_Novello_Award "Ivor Novello Award") for Songwriter of the Year. On 21 June, Sheeran co-hosted the [2015 Much Music Video Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Much_Music_Video_Awards "2015 Much Music Video Awards") in Toronto, where he performed the singles "Thinking Out Loud" and "[Photograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photograph_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Photograph (Ed Sheeran song)")"; he also won two awards, Best International Artist and Most Buzzworthy International Artist or Group. On 27 June, Sheeran performed as the opening act for [The Rolling Stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rolling_Stones "The Rolling Stones") in their [Zip Code Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zip_Code_Tour "Zip Code Tour") date in Kansas City's [Arrowhead Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrowhead_Stadium "Arrowhead Stadium"). On 10–12 July 2015, Sheeran performed sold-out shows at London's [Wembley Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wembley_Stadium "Wembley Stadium"). The shows, which were announced in November 2014, were part of his world tour. ### 2016–2018: *÷* ("*Divide*")

On 13 December 2016, after a year long hiatus and social media break, Sheeran tweeted a picture and changed his Twitter, Facebook and Instagram to a light blue, implying the release of a new album—each of Sheeran's previous albums were a single-coloured background with a solid mathematical symbol. On 2 January, he posted a 10-second video on Twitter and other social media platforms, revealing the track list and cover art of his fourth studio album, *[÷](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%B7_(album) "÷ (album)")* ("Divide"), which was released on 3 March 2017. The album debuted at number one in the UK, the US, Germany, Australia, Canada and other major markets. With first week sales of 672,000 it is the fastest-selling album by a male solo artist in the UK, and [third fastest in UK chart history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_fastest-selling_albums "Lists of fastest-selling albums") behind *[25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/25_(Adele_album) "25 (Adele album)")* by [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele "Adele") and *[Be Here Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be_Here_Now_(album) "Be Here Now (album)")* by [Oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oasis_(band) "Oasis (band)"). It had the biggest first-week sales of 2017 in the US, until it was surpassed by Taylor Swift's *[Reputation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reputation_(Taylor_Swift_album) "Reputation (Taylor Swift album)")*.

On 6 January, Sheeran released two singles, "[Shape of You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_of_You "Shape of You")" and "[Castle on the Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_on_the_Hill "Castle on the Hill")"; the theme of the latter single Sheeran's upbringing in his home town of [Framlingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham "Framlingham") in Suffolk, with the [castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle "Castle") referring to [Framlingham Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham_Castle "Framlingham Castle"). Following the release of these singles, Sheeran co-hosted the [BBC Radio 1 Breakfast Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_1_Breakfast_Show "BBC Radio 1 Breakfast Show") with Scott Mills where it was implied that he would possibly make an appearance at the [Glastonbury Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury_Festival "Glastonbury Festival") in 2017. It was also during this show that Sheeran used a new [Martin guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_guitar "Martin guitar") that featured the ÷ logo (of his new album) on both the headstock and body of the acoustic guitar. Both singles went on to break the [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify "Spotify") day one streaming record, with a combined total of over 13 million streams in 24 hours.

On 13 January, "Shape of You" and "Castle on the Hill" entered the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart") at number one and number two, the first time in history an artist has taken the top two UK chart positions with new songs. The same day he also became the first artist to debut at number one and number two on the [German Single Charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Single_Charts "German Single Charts"). On 15 January, the songs debuted at number one and number two on the [ARIA Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARIA_Singles_Chart "ARIA Singles Chart"), the first time this has been achieved in the history of the Australian chart. On 17 January, "Shape of You" debuted at number one on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), while "Castle on the Hill" entered at number six; this made Sheeran the first artist ever to have two songs simultaneously debut in the US top 10. The team behind [TLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TLC_(group) "TLC (group)")'s song "[No Scrubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Scrubs "No Scrubs")" were given writing credits on "Shape of You" after fans and critics found similarities between elements of the two songs.

On 26 January, Sheeran announced dates for the beginning of the Divide Tour with shows in Europe, South America and North America from 17 March until 14 June 2017. On 17 February, Sheeran released "[How Would You Feel (Paean)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_Would_You_Feel_(Paean) "How Would You Feel (Paean)")". Though not an official single, the song peaked at number two in the UK. By 11 March 2017, Sheeran had accumulated ten top-10 singles from *÷* on the UK Singles Chart, breaking Scottish DJ [Calvin Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Harris "Calvin Harris")'s record of nine top-10 singles from one album. On 25 June, Sheeran headlined the final night of [Glastonbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury_Festival "Glastonbury Festival"), performing in front of 135,000 people. At the [2017 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_MTV_Video_Music_Awards "2017 MTV Video Music Awards") Sheeran was named Artist of the Year. The fourth single from *÷*, "[Perfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Perfect (Ed Sheeran song)")", reached number one in the UK and Australia, and a stripped-down acoustic version of the song titled "Perfect Duet", a collaboration with [Beyoncé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyonc%C3%A9 "Beyoncé"), reached number one in the US and the UK, becoming the year's [UK Christmas number one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UK_Singles_Chart_Christmas_number_ones "List of UK Singles Chart Christmas number ones"). On 7 November, Taylor Swift revealed that Sheeran collaborated on the song "[End Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_Game_(Taylor_Swift_song) "End Game (Taylor Swift song)")" for her sixth studio album, *[Reputation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reputation_(Taylor_Swift_album) "Reputation (Taylor Swift album)")*. The song, which also features rapper [Future](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_(rapper) "Future (rapper)"), was released on 10 November.

On 4 December, Sheeran was named Spotify's most-streamed artist of 2017 with 6.3 billion streams. He has Spotify's biggest album of the year with *÷* streamed 3.1 billion times, and the top song with "Shape of You" with 1.4 billion streams. On 5 December 2017, hip-hop artist [Eminem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eminem "Eminem") announced that Sheeran had collaborated on the song "[River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_(Eminem_song) "River (Eminem song)")" for his ninth studio album, *[Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revival_(Eminem_album) "Revival (Eminem album)")*. On working with Eminem, Sheeran stated, "He is one of the reasons I started writing songs, and was such a pleasure to work with him". In December 2017, Sheeran appeared on [BBC Radio 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_1 "BBC Radio 1")'s [Live Lounge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Lounge "Live Lounge"), performing his song "Perfect" and a duet of [The Pogues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pogues "The Pogues")' festive classic "[Fairytale of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairytale_of_New_York "Fairytale of New York")" with [Anne-Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne-Marie "Anne-Marie").

On 3 January 2018, "Shape of You" was named the [best-selling single of 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UK_top-ten_singles_in_2017 "List of UK top-ten singles in 2017") in the UK, and the [best-selling single of 2017 on the *Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Billboard_Hot_100_number_ones_of_2017 "List of Billboard Hot 100 number ones of 2017") in the US. The same day, *÷* was named the best-selling album of 2017 in the UK, and the US. As the best-selling artist worldwide for 2017, the [International Federation of the Phonographic Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_the_Phonographic_Industry "International Federation of the Phonographic Industry") (IFPI) named him the [Global Recording Artist of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Recording_Artist_of_the_Year "Global Recording Artist of the Year"). At the [2018 Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Brit_Awards "2018 Brit Awards") held at [the O2 Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_O2_Arena "The O2 Arena") in London on 21 February, Sheeran performed "[Supermarket Flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermarket_Flowers "Supermarket Flowers")", and received the Global Success Award from Elton John and Rolling Stones guitarist [Ronnie Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronnie_Wood "Ronnie Wood"). Sheeran played to over 950,000 people in Australia and New Zealand in March and April, making it the biggest concert tour in Australasian music history, overtaking the previous record set by [Dire Straits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dire_Straits "Dire Straits") in 1986. In April, the IFPI named *÷* the [best-selling album worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_albums#Best-selling_album_by_year_worldwide "List of best-selling albums") of 2017. At the [2018 *Billboard* Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Billboard_Music_Awards "2018 Billboard Music Awards") on 20 May, Sheeran performed "[Galway Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_Girl_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Galway Girl (Ed Sheeran song)")" from [Phoenix Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Park "Phoenix Park") in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin "Dublin"), Ireland, and picked up six awards, including [Top Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Music_Award_for_Top_Artist "Billboard Music Award for Top Artist") and [Top Hot 100 Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Music_Award_for_Top_Hot_100_Artist "Billboard Music Award for Top Hot 100 Artist"). In 2018 Sheeran wrote songs for boy bands. "[Trust Fund Baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trust_Fund_Baby "Trust Fund Baby")", by [Why Don't We](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Why_Don%27t_We "Why Don't We"), was released on 1 February 2018, and "Summer On You", by [PrettyMuch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PrettyMuch "PrettyMuch"), was released on 21 June 2018.

### 2019–2022: *No.6 Collaborations Project* and *=* ("*Equals*")

On 10 May 2019, Sheeran released the single "[I Don't Care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Care_(Ed_Sheeran_and_Justin_Bieber_song) "I Don't Care (Ed Sheeran and Justin Bieber song)")", a duet with [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber "Justin Bieber"), from his fourth studio album, *[No.6 Collaborations Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No.6_Collaborations_Project "No.6 Collaborations Project")*. On [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify "Spotify"), "I Don't Care" debuted with 10.977 million daily global streams, breaking the platform's [single-day streaming record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-streamed_songs_on_Spotify#Most-streamed_songs_in_a_single_day "List of most-streamed songs on Spotify"). The song debuted at number one in the UK, Australia and other markets, and number two in the US. On 31 May, "[Cross Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_Me "Cross Me")" featuring [Chance the Rapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chance_the_Rapper "Chance the Rapper") and [PnB Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PnB_Rock "PnB Rock"), debuted at number 9 in the UK. Released on 28 June 2019, "[Beautiful People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_People_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Beautiful People (Ed Sheeran song)")" featuring [Khalid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalid_(American_singer) "Khalid (American singer)") debuted at number 3 in the UK and number 4 in Australia. On 5 July, Sheeran released two new songs, "[Best Part of Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Part_of_Me "Best Part of Me")" featuring [Yebba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yebba "Yebba"), and "[Blow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blow_(Ed_Sheeran,_Chris_Stapleton_and_Bruno_Mars_song) "Blow (Ed Sheeran, Chris Stapleton and Bruno Mars song)")" with [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars "Bruno Mars") and [Chris Stapleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Stapleton "Chris Stapleton"). On 12 July, he released the album, along with "[Antisocial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisocial_(Ed_Sheeran_and_Travis_Scott_song) "Antisocial (Ed Sheeran and Travis Scott song)")" with [Travis Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Scott "Travis Scott"). The album debuted at number one in the UK, the US, Australia and other markets. On 26 August, Sheeran wrapped up the 260-show Divide Tour with the last of four homecoming gigs in [Ipswich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich "Ipswich"), Suffolk; the tour included, for example, his performance for over 100,000 people at [Malmi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malmi_Airport "Malmi Airport") in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki "Helsinki"), Finland, on 24 July. On 30 August, the seventh single from the album, "[Take Me Back to London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_Me_Back_to_London "Take Me Back to London")", featuring [Stormzy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stormzy "Stormzy"), reached number one in the UK.

In 2019, Sheeran co-wrote country music singer [Kenny Chesney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Chesney "Kenny Chesney")'s single "[Tip of My Tongue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tip_of_My_Tongue_(Kenny_Chesney_song) "Tip of My Tongue (Kenny Chesney song)")". In December 2019, Sheeran was named artist of the decade by the [Official Charts Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Charts_Company "Official Charts Company") for being the most successful performer in the UK album and singles charts of the 2010s. Eight of his songs featured in the Official Chart Company's chart of the decade with three songs inside the top 5—"[Shape of You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_of_You "Shape of You")" was named number one. His four albums spent a combined 41 weeks at number one in the UK, the [most weeks at number one in the 2010s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UK_Albums_Chart_number_ones_of_the_2010s#Artists_with_the_most_weeks_at_number_one "List of UK Albums Chart number ones of the 2010s"), and five weeks more than Adele in second. He had the third and fourth [best-selling album of the 2010s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_albums_of_the_2010s_in_the_United_Kingdom "List of best-selling albums of the 2010s in the United Kingdom") in the UK, with only Adele's two albums selling more. Globally, [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify "Spotify") named him the second [most streamed artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-streamed_artists_on_Spotify#By_decade "List of most-streamed artists on Spotify") of the decade behind [Drake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drake_(musician) "Drake (musician)"). On 21 December 2020, after being on hiatus after having his first child with Cherry Seaborn in August, he released the surprise single, "[Afterglow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afterglow_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Afterglow (Ed Sheeran song)")".

On 25 June 2021, Sheeran released "[Bad Habits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Habits_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Bad Habits (Ed Sheeran song)")", the lead single from his upcoming fifth studio album. His 10th UK number one single, the song spent eleven consecutive weeks at the top of the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart "UK Singles Chart") and [Irish Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Singles_Chart "Irish Singles Chart"), topped the charts in Australia, Canada and Germany among others, and peaked at number two on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") in the US. His eleventh week at number one in the UK saw him become the first British solo artist to notch up 52 weeks at Number 1 across his catalogue, with only Elvis Presley (80 weeks) and The Beatles (69 weeks) achieving more weeks at the summit. On 19 August, Sheeran announced that his fifth studio album, *[=](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%3D_(album) "= (album)")* ("Equals"), would be released on 29 October 2021. The promotional single "[Visiting Hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_Hours_(song) "Visiting Hours (song)")" was released alongside the announcement. On 9 September, he was part of the 2021 Kickoff Experience ahead of the American NFL season opening game. Released on 10 September, "[Shivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivers_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Shivers (Ed Sheeran song)")" dethroned "Bad Habits" at the top of the UK and Irish singles charts. ### 2025–present: *Play* and label changes

On 14 February 2025, Sheeran announced his eighth studio album, *[Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play_(Ed_Sheeran_album) "Play (Ed Sheeran album)")*, through a social media comment. *Play* was released through his own label, [Gingerbread Man Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gingerbread_Man_Records "Gingerbread Man Records"), making it his second album released by the label. The release of *Play* officially marked the beginning of his new era of music, the first of his 5 part album series with *Play, Pause, Fast Forward, Rewind, and Stop*. *Play* is also Sheeran's first pop album since [=](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%3D_(album) "= (album)") ("Equals"), with [–](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E2%88%92_(album) "− (album)") ("Subtract") and *[Autumn Variations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autumn_Variations "Autumn Variations")* being mostly acoustic albums. The title had previously been hinted at in late 2023, when Sheeran left a comment on social media, writing “See you sometime next year when we press Play again on pop, and have a good holiday season x.” In early 2025, Sheeran changed his profile picture on [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") and fans started speculating which mathematical symbol would be featured in his next release. In his comments as responses to fans, he hinted at Play again, writing: “Irony is if you ask me anything maths related I would fail the question, I never finished high school. Play coming soon though."

"[Azizam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azizam_(song) "Azizam (song)")" was released on 4 April 2025 as the lead single of the album. It was written as a tribute for his wife Cherry Seaborn. Sheeran collaborated with [Ilya Salmanzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Salmanzadeh "Ilya Salmanzadeh") on this Persian-inspired song, which features [Farsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language "Persian language") lyrics. "Azizam" debuted at number three on the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_singles_chart "UK singles chart"), becoming Sheeran's 42nd top 10 hit in his home country. It was also nominated for Song of the Year at the 2026 [Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Awards "Brit Awards").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] To promote this single, Sheeran commissioned 34 giant inflatable hearts as part of a global campaign, since *Azizam* is Persian for ‘dear' or ‘beloved'. The inflatable hearts were rigged simultaneously in twelve different locations all over the world including [Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai "Dubai"), [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin "Berlin"), [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo "Tokyo") and [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi "New Delhi"). A second single, "[Old Phone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Phone "Old Phone")", was released on 1 May 2025. The third single "[Sapphire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapphire_(song) "Sapphire (song)")" was released on 5 June 2025, which was a collaboration with [Arijit Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arijit_Singh "Arijit Singh"), when he was on the tour of India. "[A Little More](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Little_More_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "A Little More (Ed Sheeran song)")" was released as the fourth single on 7 August 2025. A fifth single, "[Camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Camera (Ed Sheeran song)")" was released with the Album.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Most recently, "[Symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_(song) "Symmetry (song)")" (featuring [Karan Aujla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karan_Aujla "Karan Aujla")), was released as the sixth single on 17 October 2025. *Play* won Album of the Year at the [Los 40 Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_40_Music_Awards "Los 40 Music Awards"). To support it, Sheeran embarked on the [Loop Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_Tour "Loop Tour") on 1 December 2025 in [Auckland, New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland,_New_Zealand "Auckland, New Zealand").

While promoting *Play*, Sheeran said during an interview with [Zane Lowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Lowe "Zane Lowe") that he was creating an album titled *Eject* for release after his death, containing songs he recorded from age 18, with Seaborn intended to select the final track listing. He also revealed that there would be a series of albums following *Play*, which includes *Pause*, *Fast Forward*, *Rewind*, and *Stop*.

In May 2026, Sheeran announced his departure from Warner Music, citing his desire for a change in his professional career. Two months later, it was announced he had signed with [Universal Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Music "Universal Music")'s [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records "Interscope Records").

## Musical style and influences

Sheeran's musical style has been described as [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music"), [folk-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk-pop "Folk-pop"), [soft rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_rock "Soft rock"), and [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop"). Sheeran's earliest memories include listening to the records of [Joni Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joni_Mitchell "Joni Mitchell"), [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan "Bob Dylan") and [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John "Elton John")'s *[Greatest Hits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greatest_Hits_(Elton_John_album) "Greatest Hits (Elton John album)")*. According to Sheeran, the album that introduced him to music was [Van Morrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Morrison "Van Morrison")'s *[Irish Heartbeat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Heartbeat "Irish Heartbeat")*. During his childhood his father took him to live concerts that would inspire his musical creations. These included seeing [Eric Clapton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Clapton "Eric Clapton") at the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall "Royal Albert Hall"), [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney "Paul McCartney") in [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham "Birmingham"), and Bob Dylan. On the influence of Clapton, Sheeran states, "He's the reason I started playing guitar". He singled out Clapton's performance at the [Party at the Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_at_the_Palace "Party at the Palace") in the [grounds of Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace_Garden "Buckingham Palace Garden"), "I was eleven when I saw Eric Clapton play at the [Queen's Golden Jubilee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Jubilee_of_Queen_Elizabeth_II "Golden Jubilee of Queen Elizabeth II") concert in June 2002. I remember him walking on stage with this rainbow-coloured [Stratocaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratocaster "Stratocaster") and playing the first riff of '[Layla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Layla "Layla")'. I was hooked. Two days later I bought a black Stratocaster copy for £30 that came with an [amp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amplifier "Amplifier"). All I did for the next month was try to play that 'Layla' riff".

> In a complicated world, Sheeran's musical *[modus operandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modus_operandi "Modus operandi")* is straightforward; to create well-crafted, expertly vague songs that unite people. His songs are vessels broad enough to soundtrack both a first dance and a funeral procession, a gut-punch break-up and a trawl around a harshly lit shopping centre. They're for life's big moments, with all the cinematic edge of a [Richard Curtis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Curtis "Richard Curtis") film. He's also malleable – when he's done with [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop"), for example, he doesn't need an image overhaul to then revert to balladry. If one genre isn't to your tastes, then fear not, another will be along soon.

— Michael Cragg in *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* on Sheeran's style of music and mass appeal.

Sheeran has also cited [the Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles "The Beatles"), [Nizlopi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizlopi "Nizlopi") and [Eminem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eminem "Eminem") as his biggest musical influences. He is also a fan of heavier music and cites bands such as [Cradle of Filth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cradle_of_Filth "Cradle of Filth"), [Slipknot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slipknot_(band) "Slipknot (band)"), [Korn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korn "Korn"), [Marilyn Manson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marilyn_Manson_(band) "Marilyn Manson (band)") and [Bring Me the Horizon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bring_Me_the_Horizon "Bring Me the Horizon") as other influences. As a teenager, he also had a *[Kerrang!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerrang! "Kerrang!")* subscription. According to Sheeran, he had a stutter in his speech when he was younger, and he credited rapping along to Eminem's *[The Marshall Mathers LP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Marshall_Mathers_LP "The Marshall Mathers LP")* for helping him stammer less. He was also inspired by "[Cannonball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannonball_(Damien_Rice_song) "Cannonball (Damien Rice song)")" singer-songwriter [Damien Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Rice "Damien Rice") in 2002, with Sheeran stating, "seeing him play this small club in Ireland, I was able to meet him, and he was unbelievably cool. I went straight home and started writing songs. I would not be doing what I'm doing now if he'd been a jerk". He also played the guitar to [Westlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westlife "Westlife")'s *[Greatest Hits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unbreakable_%E2%80%93_The_Greatest_Hits_Volume_1 "Unbreakable – The Greatest Hits Volume 1")* album when he was ten, citing them as one of his influences. Sheeran collaborated with his idol Eric Clapton in April 2016, with Sheeran stating to *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* magazine, "I sang on Eric Clapton's album *[I Still Do](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Still_Do "I Still Do")*. It's one thing having him on mine, but being on his, that's an honour that you can't ever pinpoint on how great that is. I did something for his record, and I was credited as 'Angelo Mysterioso', appearing as a guest on Clapton's 'I Will Be There', in addition to performing the song with Clapton on stage, and he did something for my record performing a guitar solo on '[Dive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dive_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Dive (Ed Sheeran song)")' on Sheeran's album *÷* and was credited as 'Angelo Mysterioso'". Sheeran also cited [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift") as one of his influences, suggesting in 2015 their respective success drives each other on.

In 2024, Ed Sheeran also confirmed punk band [the Offspring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Offspring "The Offspring") as an influence. His aunt gave him ten pounds to buy a CD and the first CD he purchased was *[Conspiracy of One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_of_One "Conspiracy of One")* and he used to play their song "[Million Miles Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Million_Miles_Away_(The_Offspring_song) "Million Miles Away (The Offspring song)")" in front of a mirror. Sheeran later performed the song with the band.

More recent [releases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Sheeran_discography "Ed Sheeran discography") have seen increased [non-western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-western "Non-western") influence in his music: his 2025 single "[Azizam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azizam_(song) "Azizam (song)")", which debuted at No. 3 in the UK, included [Persian musical influences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_traditional_music "Persian traditional music") and "[Sapphire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapphire_(song) "Sapphire (song)")" was noted for its [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabis "Punjabis") undertones.

## Other ventures

### Gingerbread Man Records

In March 2015, Sheeran announced he was setting up a record label, [Gingerbread Man Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gingerbread_Man_Records "Gingerbread Man Records"), which is a deal with [Warner Music Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Music_Group "Warner Music Group"). The label was launched in August 2015 alongside its accompanying YouTube channel. [Jamie Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Lawson_(musician) "Jamie Lawson (musician)"), the label's first signee, met Sheeran while they were both in London's folk circuit. Lawson released his [self-titled debut album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Lawson_(album) "Jamie Lawson (album)") on 9 October 2015, which has earned him a number one in the UK Albums Chart. Sheeran signed his second artist, [Foy Vance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foy_Vance "Foy Vance"), in November 2015. [Maisie Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maisie_Peters "Maisie Peters") also signed with the label in 2021, and released her first debut studio album *[You Signed Up for This](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Signed_Up_for_This "You Signed Up for This")* in August 2021. Gingerbread Man Records has now released 14 albums by these 3 artists and Sheeran himself.

### Bertie Blossoms

On 29 September 2019, Sheeran announced he was teaming up with his manager Stuart Camp to open a bar located on Portobello Road in [Notting Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notting_Hill "Notting Hill"). The bar is called "Bertie Blossoms", and named after his wife Cherry Seaborn. Their restaurant is described as "blending Spanish flair, British comfort, and a little bit of everything else we love from around the world. Think crispy bites, bold flavours and dishes made for sharing, because let's be honest, it's more fun that way."

### Charity work

Sheeran performed a gig in Bristol, which raised £40,000 for a charity that reaches out to [street sex workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostitution "Prostitution"). "It's good to show insight that these people are real people with real emotions and they deserve the same charity work as anyone else," Sheeran said. "There's a lot more popular charities that get a lot of attention. And with certain subjects like this they're often washed over and people don't necessarily give them the attention they deserve". Tickets were available to those taking part in the charity's Give it up for One25 campaign by giving something up for 125 hours and hitting the £40,000 fundraising mark.

Sheeran frequently gives away his clothes to charity shops around Suffolk, his home county. An ambassador for [East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Anglia "East Anglia")'s Children's Hospice, he has donated clothes to the St Elizabeth Hospice charity shop in his home town Framlingham, including eight bags of clothes to the shop in February 2014. In 2016, he donated 13 bags of clothes to the shop. The tartan shirt worn by Sheeran when he met Renee Zellweger's character, Bridget, in *[Bridget Jones's Baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridget_Jones%27s_Baby "Bridget Jones's Baby")*, was auctioned online to raise further funds for the hospice.

On 15 November 2014, Sheeran joined the charity supergroup [Band Aid 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_Aid_30 "Band Aid 30") along with other British and Irish pop acts, recording the latest version of the track "[Do They Know It's Christmas?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_They_Know_It%27s_Christmas%3F "Do They Know It's Christmas?")" at [Sarm West Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarm_West_Studios "Sarm West Studios") in [Notting Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notting_Hill "Notting Hill"), London, to raise money for the [West African Ebola virus epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_African_Ebola_virus_epidemic "West African Ebola virus epidemic").

In November 2015, Sheeran supported the No Cold Homes campaign by the UK charity, [Turn2us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turn2us "Turn2us"). Sheeran was one of nearly thirty celebrities, which included [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren "Helen Mirren"), [Jeremy Irons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Irons "Jeremy Irons") and [Hugh Laurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Laurie "Hugh Laurie"), to donate items of winter clothing to the campaign, with the proceeds used to help people in the country struggling to keep their home warm in winter.

Sheeran teamed up with the cast of the BBC3 mockumentary sitcom *[People Just Do Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_Just_Do_Nothing "People Just Do Nothing")* to perform a charity single for the BBC's biennial telethon [Comic Relief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Relief "Comic Relief") which aired in March 2017. He appeared in a November 2017 episode of *[Gogglebox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogglebox "Gogglebox")* along with other UK celebrities such as [Ozzy Osbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozzy_Osbourne "Ozzy Osbourne"), former [Oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oasis_(band) "Oasis (band)") frontman [Liam Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Gallagher "Liam Gallagher"), and Labour Party leader [Jeremy Corbyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Corbyn "Jeremy Corbyn") as part of Channel 4 and [Cancer Research UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer_Research_UK "Cancer Research UK")'s *[Stand Up to Cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand_Up_to_Cancer_UK "Stand Up to Cancer UK")* fundraising campaign.

In the statement, Sheeran stated it will help artists aged under 18 with "small but hopefully useful grants". ## Impact

Music journalist [Alexis Petridis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexis_Petridis "Alexis Petridis") has stated that Ed Sheeran "brought pop back down to earth" with his music style, introducing himself "touting an even more austere version of [Coldplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coldplay "Coldplay")'s dressed-down authenticity: one unassuming man, his guitar and a [loop pedal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_pedal "Loop pedal")". Noting him as "one of the most influential" artists of his generation, as he spawned "endless imitators", Petridis remarked that the music charts were "packed with Sheeran-alikes" after *[×](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(Ed_Sheeran_album) "X (Ed Sheeran album)")*, describing it as "the wave of earnest, dressed-down, boy-next-door [troubadours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troubadour "Troubadour")" that reached critical mass. Similarly, *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* writer Laura Snapes cited him as "the godfather of the current crop of singer-songwriters" in 2019, stating that Sheeran inspired "troubadours" to enter the music charts, and marked "the calcification of the everyman male pop star", and the end of record labels marketing them "exclusively to teenage girls and their mums". *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")* writer Jason Lipshutz noticed that his appeal and performance style had influenced up-and-coming soloists, impacting "a significant number of pop artists who crave his type of singular success". The *[Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times "Financial Times")* considered that the scale of Sheeran's commercial accomplishments has "broken through to such a wide and lucrative extent, far beyond the achievements of any of the other singer-songwriters in the market" in the UK.

[iHeartMedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHeartMedia "IHeartMedia") senior executive Sharon Dastur declared that Sheeran's success has allowed newer artists to be given an opportunity in the mainstream scene with quieter material at the forefront, instead of dance music. *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")*'s George Chesterton deemed Sheeran "the de facto voice of a generation" as a consequence of his music reflecting his personality and "the defining characteristics of his audience", with such recurring qualities of his discography corresponding "with those that his own generation, the [millennials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennials "Millennials"), most value: authenticity, realness, earnestness, sincerity". [BBC Radio 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Radio_1 "BBC Radio 1") executive George Ergatoudis has stated that his "lyrical candour" and his "professional hunger" resonated with younger listeners, giving him a "very clear edge" to breakthrough in a music industry that is "saturated with singer-songwriters", while Sheeran's "niche combination" has made him able to perform at hip-hop, grime and underground events and "convince the urban crowd that he was authentic". According to *[Vox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_(website) "Vox (website)")*, Sheeran has reached "global pop culture ubiquity". In 2017, the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") named him the second-best performing artist of the decade, after [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele "Adele"), while in 2021, *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent "The Independent")* said he had "one of the biggest pop careers of this generation". *Billboard*, *[The Financial Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Financial_Express_(India) "The Financial Express (India)")*, *[The Brandon Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Brandon_Sun "The Brandon Sun")* and *[Arab News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_News "Arab News")* have cited him as a "British music icon". In a 2023 poll conducted by the [O2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O2_(UK) "O2 (UK)") Priority Tickets platform on what musical acts, past and present, music fans would most want to see live, Sheeran ranked third, after [Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_(band) "Queen (band)") and the Beatles. Sheeran and his work have influenced various recording artists, including [Shawn Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Mendes "Shawn Mendes"), [Louis Tomlinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Tomlinson "Louis Tomlinson"), [Camila Cabello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camila_Cabello "Camila Cabello") and [Cody Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Simpson "Cody Simpson").

## Accolades

On 19 October 2015, Sheeran received an honorary degree from the [University of Suffolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Suffolk "University of Suffolk") in Ipswich for his "outstanding contribution to music". Sheeran commented: "Suffolk is very much where I call home. Receiving this recognition is a real privilege". He was appointed [Member of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire "Member of the Order of the British Empire") (MBE) in the [2017 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Birthday_Honours "2017 Birthday Honours") for "services to music and charity". Sheeran received the award from [Prince Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Charles "Prince Charles") at [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace "Buckingham Palace") on 7 December 2017. In 2012, he was named a [baron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baron "Baron") of [Sealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sealand "Sealand").

In addition to having the [highest-grossing concert tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_concert_tours "List of highest-grossing concert tours") between 2017 and 2019, and being one of the [world's best-selling music artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World%27s_best-selling_music_artists "World's best-selling music artists") with 200 million records sold, Sheeran has received a number of awards. As of 2019, he has received four [Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Awards "Grammy Awards") (including [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_of_the_Year "Song of the Year") in 2016 for "Thinking Out Loud"), five [Brit Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Awards "Brit Awards") (including [British Male Solo Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Award_for_British_Male_Solo_Artist "Brit Award for British Male Solo Artist") in 2015), and six [*Billboard* Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Music_Award "Billboard Music Award") (including [Top Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Music_Award_for_Top_Artist "Billboard Music Award for Top Artist") in 2018). In 2015 and 2018, he received the [Ivor Novello Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivor_Novello_Award "Ivor Novello Award") for Songwriter of the Year from the [British Academy of Songwriters, Composers, and Authors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Songwriters,_Composers,_and_Authors "British Academy of Songwriters, Composers, and Authors").

Although he regards Suffolk as home having moved to the county as a young child, Sheeran was recognised by his county of birth in a 2018 poll when he was ranked the fourth-greatest Yorkshireman ever behind [Monty Python](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monty_Python "Monty Python") comedian [Michael Palin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Palin "Michael Palin"), and actors [Sean Bean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Bean "Sean Bean") and [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart "Patrick Stewart").

In 2024 he won a [Primetime Creative Arts Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Creative_Arts_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards") for [Outstanding Original Music and Lyrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Original_Music_and_Lyrics "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Original Music and Lyrics") for "A Beautiful Game" from *[Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso "Ted Lasso")*.

## Personal life

In early 2011, after securing recording and publishing deals, Sheeran purchased and renovated a farm near [Framlingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framlingham "Framlingham"), Suffolk, where he was raised. He said he hoped to raise a family there. This estate, which he has added buildings to over the years, has been nicknamed "Sheeranville" in the press. In 2013, he lived between [Hendersonville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendersonville,_Tennessee "Hendersonville, Tennessee") and Los Angeles, California. In 2014, he bought a house in [South London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_London "South London").

Sheeran was in a relationship with Scottish singer-songwriter [Nina Nesbitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Nesbitt "Nina Nesbitt") (who was in his music video for "[Drunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drunk_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Drunk (Ed Sheeran song)")") in 2012, before breaking up. Nesbitt is the subject of Sheeran's songs "Nina" and "[Photograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photograph_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Photograph (Ed Sheeran song)")", while most of Nesbitt's album *[Peroxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peroxide_(Nina_Nesbitt_album) "Peroxide (Nina Nesbitt album)")* is about Sheeran. In 2014, Sheeran was in a relationship with Athina Andrelos, who works for chef [Jamie Oliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Oliver "Jamie Oliver"). She is the inspiration of Sheeran's song "[Thinking Out Loud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Out_Loud "Thinking Out Loud")". They broke up in February 2015.

In July 2015, Sheeran began a relationship with childhood friend and former secondary school classmate Cherry Seaborn. They announced their engagement in January 2018 and were married a year later. She is the inspiration of the song "[Perfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_(Ed_Sheeran_song) "Perfect (Ed Sheeran song)")". They have two daughters, born in 2020 and 2022.

He is close friends with singer-songwriter [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift"); the pair collaborated on her albums *[Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_(Taylor_Swift_album) "Red (Taylor Swift album)")* (original and [re-recorded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_(Taylor%27s_Version) "Red (Taylor's Version)") versions) and *[Reputation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reputation_(Taylor_Swift_album) "Reputation (Taylor Swift album)")*, as well as on a remix of the song "[The Joker and the Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Joker_and_the_Queen "The Joker and the Queen")" from Sheeran's album *[=](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%3D_(album) "= (album)")*.

Sheeran is a supporter of his local football club [Ipswich Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipswich_Town "Ipswich Town") and of [FC Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Barcelona "FC Barcelona").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] His [+−=÷× Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%2B%E2%88%92%3D%C3%B7%C3%97_Tour "+−=÷× Tour") kit sponsored Ipswitch Town's [2021–22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Ipswich_Town_F.C._season "2021–22 Ipswich Town F.C. season") until [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Ipswich_Town_F.C._season "2024–25 Ipswich Town F.C. season"). Additionally, Sheeran was named in the club's squad list and given the squad number of 17. In August 2024, Sheeran acquired a minority stake in the club, amounting to 1.4%.

In June 2015, *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* listed his earnings at $57 million for the previous 12 months, and ranked him the 27th-highest-earning celebrity in the world. In July 2018, *Forbes* named Sheeran ninth on their list of the highest-paid celebrities. One of the [wealthiest musicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_music_artists_by_net_worth "List of music artists by net worth"), according to *[The Sunday Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times "The Sunday Times")* [Rich List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunday_Times_Rich_List "Sunday Times Rich List") of 2019, Sheeran was worth £160 million (US$207 million) as the 17th-richest musician in the UK. ## Legal issues

Sheeran has faced accusations of [plagiarism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plagiarism "Plagiarism") with regard to [chord progression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chord_progression "Chord progression") and other elements of his music. However, [tort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tort "Tort") actions which have reached a jury have all been decided in Sheeran's favour.

In 2017, Sheeran settled out of court over claims his song "Photograph" was a "note-for-note" copy of the chorus in the song "[Amazing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazing_(Matt_Cardle_song) "Amazing (Matt Cardle song)")" by *[The X Factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_X_Factor_(British_TV_series)_series_7 "The X Factor (British TV series) series 7")* winner [Matt Cardle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Cardle "Matt Cardle"). Sheeran later regretted the decision to settle, saying that it was done on the advice of his lawyers who thought the case was "more trouble than it was worth". He said he regretted settling the claim not because of the money involved, but because it changed his relationship with the song. He said: "I didn't play 'Photograph' for ages after that. I just stopped playing it. I felt weird about it, it kind of made me feel dirty". He also thought that settling the case opened a floodgate of claims, including the "Shape of You" lawsuit.

In 2018, legal action was brought against Sheeran, [Sony/ATV Music Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony/ATV_Music_Publishing "Sony/ATV Music Publishing") and [Atlantic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Records "Atlantic Records") by the estate and heirs of the late producer [Ed Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Townsend "Ed Townsend"), who co-wrote the song "[Let's Get It On](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Let%27s_Get_It_On_(song) "Let's Get It On (song)")" with [Marvin Gaye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvin_Gaye "Marvin Gaye"). [US District Judge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_district_court "United States district court") Louis Stanton rejected Sheeran's call in 2019 for dismissal of a legal case accusing him of copying parts of the song in "[Thinking Out Loud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_Out_Loud "Thinking Out Loud")". Stanton said that a jury should decide but that he found "substantial similarities between several of the two works' musical elements". A previous case by Townsend's estate was dismissed [without prejudice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Without_prejudice "Without prejudice") in February 2017.

The case, closely followed by those in music and legal circles, went to trial in New York in April 2023. Sheeran's lawyers argued that while the two songs have similar "building blocks" and a specific [chord progression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chord_progression "Chord progression"), such features are true for many pop songs, and the jury found in favour of Sheeran with a unanimous verdict. After winning the trial, Sheeran said: "These chords are common building blocks which were used to create music long before 'Let's Get It On' was written and will be used to create music long after we are all gone". The legal battle continued for years, with appeals attempting to revive the case. However, in June 2025, the [US Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Supreme_Court "US Supreme Court") declined to hear an appeal, effectively ending the dispute and upholding the previous rulings in Sheeran's favour.

Sheeran was taken also to court in March 2022 for a copyright lawsuit over "Shape of You". Musicians Sami Chokri and [Ross O'Donoghue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rory_O%27Donoghue "Rory O'Donoghue") alleged that the song infringed "particular lines and phrases" of their 2015 composition "Oh Why". Sheeran won the case, with [Mr Justice Zacaroli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Zacaroli "Antony Zacaroli") ruling he "neither deliberately nor subconsciously" copied a phrase from "Oh Why" when writing "Shape of You".

In August 2026, Sheeran pleaded guilty to failing to insure his 1966 [Aston Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin "Aston Martin") while it was being restored and converted into an electric car. He was ordered to pay £1,032.

## Political views

In July 2017, when he was asked how he had voted in the [June 2016 EU membership referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_Kingdom_European_Union_membership_referendum "2016 United Kingdom European Union membership referendum"), Sheeran said, "I don't get involved in politics. But I will say one thing: I was born a European and I fucking love being a European. You can probably guess my answer from that." After the British public voted for [Brexit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexit "Brexit"), Sheeran was among a group of British musicians, which included [Sting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sting_(musician) "Sting (musician)"), [Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_(band) "Queen (band)")'s drummer [Roger Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Taylor_(Queen_drummer) "Roger Taylor (Queen drummer)"), [Pink Floyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Floyd "Pink Floyd")'s drummer [Nick Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Mason "Nick Mason"), [Damon Albarn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damon_Albarn "Damon Albarn") of [Blur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blur_(band) "Blur (band)"), [Johnny Marr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Marr "Johnny Marr"), [Gorillaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorillaz "Gorillaz"), and others, who signed a letter to then-Prime Minister [Theresa May](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theresa_May "Theresa May"), drafted by [Bob Geldof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Geldof "Bob Geldof") in October 2018, calling for "[a second vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proposed_referendum_on_the_Brexit_withdrawal_agreement "Proposed referendum on the Brexit withdrawal agreement")". The letter stated that Brexit would "impact every aspect of the music industry. From touring to sales, to copyright legislation to royalty collation", adding: "We dominate the market and our bands, singers, musicians, writers, producers and engineers work all over Europe and the world and in turn, Europe and the world come to us. Why? Because we are brilliant at it ... [Our music] reaches out, all inclusive, and embraces anyone and everyone. And that truly is what Britain is".

In June 2017, Sheeran publicly endorsed the [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_(UK) "Labour Party (UK)") and then-leader [Jeremy Corbyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Corbyn "Jeremy Corbyn") during the [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_United_Kingdom_general_election "2017 United Kingdom general election"), while adding that "I'm not Mr. Political. I vote the way I feel I should, but won't tell somebody else what to do". Prior to the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election "2024 United Kingdom general election"), Sheeran criticised the [Conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_(UK) "Conservative Party (UK)") government for their lack of support for the arts, saying they believed the arts did not matter.

In 2021, Sheeran was one of over 300 other artists who signed an open letter, started by [Jack Antonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Antonoff "Jack Antonoff"), urging the [United States Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Congress "United States Congress") to pass the proposed [Equality Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equality_Act_(United_States) "Equality Act (United States)"), which would expand the [Civil Rights Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Rights_Act_of_1964 "Civil Rights Act of 1964") in order to outlaw [discrimination against LGBT people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrimination_against_LGBT_people_in_the_United_States "Discrimination against LGBT people in the United States"), which said the Act was "an essential part in protecting trans youth and all LGBTQ people by providing full protections to the most marginalized communities from discrimination".

In 2022, during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine "Russian invasion of Ukraine"), Sheeran expressed support for Ukraine and participated in the fundraising event [Concert for Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concert_for_Ukraine "Concert for Ukraine"), held at the [Resorts World Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resorts_World_Arena "Resorts World Arena") in [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham "Birmingham"), England in March of that year.

In March 2025, Sheeran wrote an open letter urging the [UK government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_government "UK government") to protect and expand music education in schools, warning that decades of under-funding have made music opportunities increasingly unequal and at risk of disappearing from state education. They argued that music is vital for students' creativity, well-being, and future careers, and should not be treated as a luxury. In response, the UK government announced updates to the national curriculum, including reducing the [EBacc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBacc "EBacc") performance measure to encourage a broader range of [GCSE subjects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCSE "GCSE") such as music and the arts, as well as diversifying music education. Government officials and education leaders credited Sheeran's letter as influential in pushing these reforms and reaffirmed a commitment to making arts education accessible to all students.

### Macklemore controversy

In September 2026, [Macklemore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macklemore "Macklemore") was removed as an opener from the US leg of Sheeran's [Loop Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_Tour "Loop Tour") after he made "[Free Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Palestine "Free Palestine")" statements on stage at the first two shows. Macklemore said Sheeran told him that [Robert Kraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Kraft "Robert Kraft") – who in 2019 was called Israel's most "loyal friend" by Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu "Benjamin Netanyahu") – had called to say he would not be allowed to perform at [Gillette Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillette_Stadium "Gillette Stadium"), which is owned by the Kraft Group. According to Macklemore, Sheeran said "Kraft had rallied some of the other stadium owners and collectively they gave him an ultimatum: If Macklemore stays on the tour, you will not be allowed to play in our venues." Sheeran had performed at Kraft's wedding in October 2022.

In a post on Instagram, Sheeran said, "I am appalled by the conflict between Israel and Palestine. The suffering and pain caused on both sides is a human tragedy. There are too many wars across the world causing immeasurable pain, none of which should be tolerated." He added that Macklemore's removal was "the promoter's decision, it was not mine," referring to [Messina Touring Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Messina "Louis Messina").

On 19 September, in his first concert after the Loop tour was canceled, Sheeran called the [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict "Israeli–Palestinian conflict") "the most complex political issue on the planet." He also condemned [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas "Hamas")'s [October 7 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_7_attacks "October 7 attacks") on Israel and the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war")'s impact on the [Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Strip "Gaza Strip"). "What happened in Israel and at the Nova music festival on October 7 was horrific," Sheeran said, also describing the war in Gaza as "catastrophic and unjustifiable and disproportionate." He also said he never intended to be an activist.
