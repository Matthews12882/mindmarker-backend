# 2026 Men's European Volleyball Championship

The **2026 Men's European Volleyball Championship**, commonly referred to as **EuroVolley Men 2026** (and the **CEV Enel EuroVolley 2026** for sponsorship reasons), was the 34th edition of the [Men's European Volleyball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "Men's European Volleyball Championship"), a biennial international volleyball tournament organised by [European Volleyball Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Volleyball_Confederation "European Volleyball Confederation") (CEV). The tournament took place from 9 to 26 September 2026 across Bulgaria, Finland, Italy, and Romania, marking the fourth consecutive time EuroVolley has been hosted by four nations since the multi-country hosting system was first used in 2019. Italy hosted the final in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan "Milan").

24 teams participated for the fourth time. Qualification took place in August 2024 and 2025 to decide the final 12 spots. The four co-hosts qualified automatically.

After [FIVB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIVB "FIVB")'s calendar changes starting 2025, this would be the first European Championship since [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_Women%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1958 Women's European Volleyball Championship") to be held in an even-numbered year.

The winner qualified for the [2028 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleyball_at_the_2028_Summer_Olympics "Volleyball at the 2028 Summer Olympics") while the top three finishers qualified for the [2027 FIVB World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIVB_Men%27s_Volleyball_World_Cup "2027 FIVB Men's Volleyball World Cup").

[Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Poland men's national volleyball team") were the defending champions, having beaten [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Italy men's national volleyball team") 3–0 in the final of the [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2023 Men's European Volleyball Championship") tournament in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome "Rome"). They retained the title after defeating [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "France men's national volleyball team") 3–1 in the final.

[Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Finland men's national volleyball team") claimed their first-ever medal at the European Volleyball Championship by defeating [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Slovenia men's national volleyball team") 3–0 in the bronze medal match.

## Year change

On 22 June 2023, the *[Fédération Internationale de Volleyball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_Internationale_de_Volleyball "Fédération Internationale de Volleyball")* (FIVB) announced a comprehensive restructuring of the international calendar for the 2025–2028 cycle. As part of this overhaul, continental championships shifted from odd-numbered years to even-numbered years, beginning in 2026. The structural change was implemented to prioritize athlete health and recovery by reducing calendar congestion, as well as to streamline the qualification pathways for major global tournaments such as the [FIVB World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIVB_Men%27s_Volleyball_World_Cup "FIVB Men's Volleyball World Cup") and the [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleyball_at_the_Summer_Olympics "Volleyball at the Summer Olympics").

## Host selection

* [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania "Romania") – On 5 December 2022, Romania was announced as the first host. This is Romania's first time hosting since [1963](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1963 Men's European Volleyball Championship") and third in total after [1955](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1955 Men's European Volleyball Championship") and 1963.

* [Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria "Bulgaria") – Bulgaria was confirmed as the second host on 26 February 2024, becoming the first ever country to host back to back European Championships. This is Bulgaria's fifth organisation of EuroVolley, after [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1950 Men's European Volleyball Championship"), [1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1981 Men's European Volleyball Championship"), [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2015 Men's European Volleyball Championship") and [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2023 Men's European Volleyball Championship").

* [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland "Finland") – Finnish city, [Tampere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampere "Tampere"), was given the hosting rights on 14 March 2024 and will host a group. This is the Finns fourth time holding the event, with Finland previously hosting in [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1977 Men's European Volleyball Championship"), [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1993 Men's European Volleyball Championship") and [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2021 Men's European Volleyball Championship").

* [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy "Italy") – On 25 March 2024 Italy was announced as the fourth and final host. Alongside Bulgaria, Italy becomes the second country to host consecutive championships. The tournament has previously taken place in Italy in [1948](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1948 Men's European Volleyball Championship"), [1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1971 Men's European Volleyball Championship"), [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2005 Men's European Volleyball Championship"), [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2015 Men's European Volleyball Championship") and [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2023 Men's European Volleyball Championship").

### Rejected countries

The following countries had expressed interest but didn't make the cut:

* [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany "Germany") – Germany had stated their interest in November 2022 and by May 2023, Germany's interest was approved by [CEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Volleyball_Confederation "European Volleyball Confederation") with the [SAP Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAP_Garden "SAP Garden") in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich "Munich") being proposed as a possible venue. However, in February 2024, Germany said to [CEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Volleyball_Confederation "European Volleyball Confederation") that they were not ready to host the event and they would focus on the [2028](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2028_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2028 Men's European Volleyball Championship") edition. The Germans were in line to become the main hosts before their withdrawal. The Italians then took their hosting place as they wrote to [CEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Volleyball_Confederation "European Volleyball Confederation") saying they wanted to host, which would later be approved.
* [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia "Serbia") – Serbia was briefly mentioned as a possible host in February 2023, but this never materialised.

## Qualification

24 teams qualify for the championship. The four co-hosts and the eight best teams from the [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2023 Men's European Volleyball Championship") edition automatically progress (in the event that one of the best eight teams is also a co-host, the next best team will inherit their place).

21 teams took part in qualification, with 12 spots on the line. The 21 teams were divided into seven groups of three, with the seven group winners plus the five best second place teams qualifying. The games were played in August 2024 and 2025. The groups were divided by the [Serpentine system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serpentine_system "Serpentine system") based on each teams' ranking.

Of the 24 teams who qualified, 21 of them were present at the previous tournament. After not even entering qualification for the 2023 edition, [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Sweden men's national volleyball team") qualified for the first time since [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "1993 Men's European Volleyball Championship"). [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvia_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Latvia men's national volleyball team") and [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Slovakia men's national volleyball team") returned after a one edition absence.

[Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Israel men's national volleyball team") qualified on merit for the first time. [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Israel men's national volleyball team") and [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Switzerland men's national volleyball team") qualified for a second consecutive edition for the very first time. [North Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Macedonia_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "North Macedonia men's national volleyball team") and [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Portugal men's national volleyball team") continued their record appearance streaks with four.

Of the non-qualifiers, [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Spain men's national volleyball team") failed to qualify after qualifying for the previous four championships. [Montenegro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montenegro_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Montenegro men's national volleyball team") failed to advance after qualifying every time since their debut in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2019 Men's European Volleyball Championship"). Despite narrowly missing out on qualifying automatically, [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia_men%27s_national_volleyball_team "Croatia men's national volleyball team") was winless in qualification and didn't manage to successfully qualify.

## Venues

Seven venues in seven cities, will host the tournament. After meetings in March 2025, the same city and venue as in 2023, the [Palace of Culture and Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace_of_Culture_and_Sports "Palace of Culture and Sports") in [Varna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varna,_Bulgaria "Varna, Bulgaria"), was chosen as Bulgaria's venue. In the same month, the [BTarena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BTarena "BTarena") in [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca "Cluj-Napoca") was announced as the venue for the group in Romania. On 6 June 2025, the [Nokia Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia_Arena_(Tampere) "Nokia Arena (Tampere)") was announced as Finland's venue. On 19 August 2025, [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples "Naples"), [Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modena "Modena"), [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin "Turin") and [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan "Milan") were announced as Italy's venues. On 14 October it was announced that Bulgaria's capital, [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia "Sofia"), would replace Varna as the country's host city with [Arena 8888](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Sofia "Arena Sofia") as the selected venue. This is due to the country's increased interest in volleyball after their second place finish at the [2025 FIVB Men's Volleyball World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FIVB_Men%27s_Volleyball_World_Championship "2025 FIVB Men's Volleyball World Championship"). [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia "Sofia") is allocating 500,000 [Bulgarian lev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_lev "Bulgarian lev") for the tournament. Prior to the venue change, [Varna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varna,_Bulgaria "Varna, Bulgaria") MPs [Desislav Taskov](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Desislav_Taskov&action=edit&redlink=1 "Desislav Taskov (page does not exist)") and [Kristian Ganchev](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kristian_Ganchev&action=edit&redlink=1 "Kristian Ganchev (page does not exist)") sent a letter asking for reassurance that Varna would keep the hosting rights after reports of Varna being replaced by Sofia came out. The director of Sports in Varna, [Kristian Dimitrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristian_Dimitrov "Kristian Dimitrov"), was reportedly angry with the venue swap. Varna had previous allocated 420,000 [Bulgarian lev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_lev "Bulgarian lev") for the tournament before the change of venue.

### Overview of venues

#### Italy

* Four Italian venues will be used during the tournament. The [Piazza del Plebiscito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_del_Plebiscito "Piazza del Plebiscito") in [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples "Naples") will host the opening match at the temporary venue at the square. The rest of the group phase will take place in [Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modena "Modena") at the [PalaPanini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PalaPanini "PalaPanini"), the home for numerous Modena-based clubs.

* Italy's round of 16 and quarterfinal matches will be held at [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin "Turin")'s [Palavela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torino_Palavela "Torino Palavela"). The venue was built in 1961 but renovated in 2004 for the [2006 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Winter_Olympics "2006 Winter Olympics"), where it held [figure skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_at_the_2006_Winter_Olympics "Figure skating at the 2006 Winter Olympics") and [short track speed skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_track_speed_skating_events_at_the_2006_Winter_Olympics "Short track speed skating events at the 2006 Winter Olympics") events. It has since organised championships in both aforementioned sports, plus [Gymnastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnastics "Gymnastics") and the [2015 Men's European Volleyball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2015 Men's European Volleyball Championship").

* The semifinals and final will take place at the [Unipol Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_di_Milano "Forum di Milano") in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan "Milan").

#### Bulgaria

* In Bulgaria, the nation's biggest indoor facility, [Arena Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Sofia "Arena Sofia"), would be chosen, replacing the originally chosen [Palace of Culture and Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace_of_Culture_and_Sports "Palace of Culture and Sports") in [Varna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varna,_Bulgaria "Varna, Bulgaria"). The established venue has hosted various competitions, with this being their third major volleyball tournament after the [2015 Men's European Volleyball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Men%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2015 Men's European Volleyball Championship") and [2018 FIVB Men's Volleyball World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIVB_Men%27s_Volleyball_World_Championship "2018 FIVB Men's Volleyball World Championship"). The venue has also hosted [Junior Eurovision Song Contest 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_Eurovision_Song_Contest_2015 "Junior Eurovision Song Contest 2015").

#### Finland

* In Finland, the [Nokia Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia_Arena_(Tampere) "Nokia Arena (Tampere)") would host games. Constructed in time for the [2022 IIHF World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_IIHF_World_Championship "2022 IIHF World Championship"), it has since hosted the [2023 IIHF World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_IIHF_World_Championship "2023 IIHF World Championship") and [EuroBasket 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_2025 "EuroBasket 2025"), plus numerous high-profile concerts.

#### Romania

* In Romania, the [BTarena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BTarena "BTarena") will organise matches. The venue was built in 2014 but expanded in preparation for [EuroBasket 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_2017 "EuroBasket 2017"). Romania's largest arena would later host the [2021 Women's European Volleyball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Women%27s_European_Volleyball_Championship "2021 Women's European Volleyball Championship"). Basketball team, [U-BT Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-BT_Cluj-Napoca "U-BT Cluj-Napoca"), plays their home games here.

## Final draw

The final draw took place at 20:45 [CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time "Central European Time") in [Bari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bari "Bari"), Italy on 4 October 2025 at the [Castello Normanno-Svevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castello_Normanno-Svevo_(Bari) "Castello Normanno-Svevo (Bari)"). Italian journalist, [Simona Rolandi](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simona_Rolandi "it:Simona Rolandi") and [CEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Volleyball_Confederation "European Volleyball Confederation") press officer, [Federico Ferraro](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Federico_Ferraro&action=edit&redlink=1 "Federico Ferraro (page does not exist)"), were the hosts of the final draw. Bulgarian libero [Damyan Kolev](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Damyan_Kolev&action=edit&redlink=1 "Damyan Kolev (page does not exist)"), Finnish outside hitter [Luka Marttila](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Luka_Marttila&action=edit&redlink=1 "Luka Marttila (page does not exist)"), Italian setter [Fabio Vullo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Vullo "Fabio Vullo") and Romanian middle blocker [Bela Bartha](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bela_Bartha&action=edit&redlink=1 "Bela Bartha (page does not exist)") were the guests for the final draw. The final draw started with the co-hosts and chosen teams being placed into their respective groups and continued with, in order, pots 1, 2, 3 and 4 being drawn, with each team selected then allocated into the first available group alphabetically.

## Preliminary round

* All times are local.
* The top four teams in each pool qualify for the final round.
* Match won 3–0 or 3–1: 3 match points for the winner, 0 match points for the loser
* Match won 3–2: 2 match points for the winner, 1 match point for the loser

### Tiebreakers

1. Number of matches won
2. Match points
3. Sets ratio
4. Points ratio
5. If the tie continues as per the point ratio between two teams, the priority is given to the team which won the match between them. When the tie in points ratio is between three or more teams, a new classification of these teams in the terms of points 1, 2, 3 and 4 is made taking into consideration only the matches in which they were opposed to each other.
