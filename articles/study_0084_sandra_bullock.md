# Sandra Bullock

**Sandra Annette Bullock** (born July 26, 1964) is an American actress and film producer. The [world's highest-paid actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-paid_film_actors "List of highest-paid film actors") of 2010 and 2014, [Bullock's filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock_filmography "Sandra Bullock filmography") spans both comedy and drama, and [her accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Sandra_Bullock "List of awards and nominations received by Sandra Bullock") include an [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award"), a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award"), two [Screen Actors Guild Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Awards "Actor Awards"), and three [Critics' Choice Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Awards "Critics' Choice Movie Awards"). She was named one of [*Time*'s 100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") in 2010.

After making her acting debut with a minor role in the thriller *[Hangmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangmen_(film) "Hangmen (film)")* (1987), Bullock received early attention for her supporting role in the action film *[Demolition Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demolition_Man_(film) "Demolition Man (film)")* (1993). Her breakthrough in the action thriller *[Speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_(1994_film) "Speed (1994 film)")* (1994) led to leading roles in the romantic comedies *[While You Were Sleeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/While_You_Were_Sleeping_(film) "While You Were Sleeping (film)")* (1995) and *[Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic "Practical Magic")* (1998), and the dramas *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(1996_film) "A Time to Kill (1996 film)")* (1996) and *[Hope Floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hope_Floats "Hope Floats")* (1998). She achieved further success in the following decades with the comedies *[Miss Congeniality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Congeniality_(film) "Miss Congeniality (film)")* (2000), *[Two Weeks Notice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Weeks_Notice "Two Weeks Notice")* (2002), *[The Proposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Proposal_(2009_film) "The Proposal (2009 film)")* (2009), *[The Heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heat_(film) "The Heat (film)")* (2013), *[Ocean's 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean%27s_8 "Ocean's 8")* (2018), and *[The Lost City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_City_(2022_film) "The Lost City (2022 film)")* (2022); the dramas *[Crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_(2004_film) "Crash (2004 film)")* (2004) and *[The Unforgivable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Unforgivable "The Unforgivable")* (2021); and the thrillers *[Premonition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premonition_(2007_film) "Premonition (2007 film)")* (2007) and *[Bird Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_Box_(film) "Bird Box (film)")* (2018). For her portrayal of [Leigh Anne Tuohy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leigh_Anne_Tuohy "Leigh Anne Tuohy") in the biographical drama *[The Blind Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Side_(film) "The Blind Side (film)")* (2009), Bullock won the [Academy Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress "Academy Award for Best Actress"). She was nominated for the same award for playing an astronaut stranded in space in the science fiction thriller *[Gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_(2013_film) "Gravity (2013 film)")* (2013), which is her highest-grossing live-action film.

In addition to acting, Bullock is the founder of the production company [Fortis Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortis_Films "Fortis Films"). She has produced some of the films in which she has starred, including *[Miss Congeniality 2: Armed and Fabulous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Congeniality_2:_Armed_and_Fabulous "Miss Congeniality 2: Armed and Fabulous")* (2005) and *[All About Steve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_About_Steve "All About Steve")* (2009), and served as an executive producer on the [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company "American Broadcasting Company") sitcom *[George Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lopez_(TV_series) "George Lopez (TV series)")* (2002–2007), on which she made numerous appearances. Dubbed "America's sweetheart" by the media, Bullock was also named the [Most Beautiful Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine)#People_Magazine's_100_Most_Beautiful_People "People (magazine)") by *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* magazine in 2015.

## Early life and education

Bullock was born on July 26, 1964, in [Arlington County, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_County,_Virginia "Arlington County, Virginia"), to Helga Mathilde ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9e "Née") Meyer; 1942–2000), an opera singer and voice teacher from [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany "Germany"); and John Wilson Bullock (1925–2018), an [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army "United States Army") employee and part-time voice coach from [Birmingham, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham,_Alabama "Birmingham, Alabama"). Her father, who was in charge of the [Army's Military Postal Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_mail#U.S._Military_Postal_Service_(MPS) "Military mail") in Europe, was stationed in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg "Nuremberg") when he met her mother. Her parents married in Germany. Bullock's maternal grandfather was a German rocket scientist from Nuremberg. The family returned to Arlington, where her father worked with the [Army Materiel Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Materiel_Command "United States Army Materiel Command") before becoming a contractor for [The Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon "The Pentagon"). Bullock has a younger sister, [Gesine Bullock-Prado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesine_Bullock-Prado "Gesine Bullock-Prado"), who served as president of Bullock's production company Fortis Films.

For 12 years, Bullock lived in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg "Nuremberg"), West Germany, and [Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna "Vienna") and [Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salzburg "Salzburg"), Austria, and grew up speaking German. She had a [Waldorf education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waldorf_education "Waldorf education") in Nuremberg. As a child, while her mother went on European opera tours, Bullock usually stayed with her aunt Christl and cousin Susanne, the latter of whom later married politician [Peter Ramsauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Ramsauer "Peter Ramsauer"). Bullock studied [ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet "Ballet") and vocal arts as a child and frequently accompanied her mother, taking small parts in her opera productions. In Nuremberg, she sang in the opera's children's choir. Bullock has a scar above her left eye which was caused by a fall into a creek when she was a child. While she maintains her American citizenship, she applied for German citizenship in 2009.

Bullock attended [Washington-Lee High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington-Lee_High_School "Washington-Lee High School"), where she was a cheerleader and performed in school theater productions. After graduating in 1982, she attended [East Carolina University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Carolina_University "East Carolina University") in [Greenville, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenville,_North_Carolina "Greenville, North Carolina"), where she received a [BFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Fine_Arts "Bachelor of Fine Arts") in Drama in 1987. While at East Carolina, she performed in theater productions, including *[Peter Pan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Pan_(play) "Peter Pan (play)")* and *[Three Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Sisters_(play) "Three Sisters (play)")*. She then moved to [Manhattan, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan,_New_York "Manhattan, New York"), where she supported herself as a bartender, cocktail waitress, and coat checker while auditioning for roles.

## Career

### Early roles and breakthrough (1987–1995)

While in New York, Bullock took acting classes with [Sanford Meisner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanford_Meisner "Sanford Meisner"). She appeared in several student films and later landed a role in an Off-Broadway play *No Time Flat*. Director [Alan J. Levi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_J._Levi "Alan J. Levi") was impressed by Bullock's performance and offered her a part in the made-for-television film *[Bionic Showdown: The Six Million Dollar Man and the Bionic Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bionic_Showdown:_The_Six_Million_Dollar_Man_and_the_Bionic_Woman "Bionic Showdown: The Six Million Dollar Man and the Bionic Woman")* (1989). This led to her being cast in a series of small roles in several [independent films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film "Independent film"), and in the lead role of the short-lived [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") television version of the film *[Working Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_Girl_(TV_series) "Working Girl (TV series)")* (1990). She went on to appear in several films, such as *[Love Potion No. 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Potion_No._9_(film) "Love Potion No. 9 (film)")* (1992), *[The Thing Called Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thing_Called_Love "The Thing Called Love")* (1993) and *[Fire on the Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_on_the_Amazon "Fire on the Amazon")* (1993), before her supporting role in the sci-fi action film *[Demolition Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demolition_Man_(film) "Demolition Man (film)")* (1993).

In 1994, she played Annie Porter, a passenger eventually driving a bus that was rigged by a terrorist, in the smash-hit blockbuster *[Speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_(1994_film) "Speed (1994 film)")* alongside [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves "Keanu Reeves"). She was required to read for *Speed* to ensure that there was the right chemistry between her and Reeves. She recalls they had to do "all these really physical scenes together, rolling around on the floor and stuff." *Speed* received critical acclaim, with [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") calling it a "terrific popcorn thriller [with] outstanding performances from Keanu Reeves, [Dennis Hopper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Hopper "Dennis Hopper"), and Sandra Bullock", and it grossed $350 million worldwide, and helped establish Bullock as a [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_States "Cinema of the United States") actress. She won [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Award_for_Best_Actress "Saturn Award for Best Actress") at the [21st Saturn Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_Saturn_Awards "21st Saturn Awards"), and [Best Female Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Movie "MTV Movie Award for Best Actor in a Movie") and [Most Desirable Female](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award_for_Most_Desirable_Female "MTV Movie Award for Most Desirable Female") at the [1995 MTV Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_MTV_Movie_Awards "1995 MTV Movie Awards").

Bullock headlined the romantic comedy *[While You Were Sleeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/While_You_Were_Sleeping_(film) "While You Were Sleeping (film)")* (1995) as a lonely [Chicago Transit Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Transit_Authority "Chicago Transit Authority") token collector who saves the life of a man. The film had a positive reception from critics, who felt that it was "assembled with such skill—and with such a charming performance from Sandra Bullock—that it gives formula a good name." She received her first nomination for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Motion Picture Comedy or Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture_Comedy_or_Musical "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Motion Picture Comedy or Musical"). The thriller *[The Net](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Net_(1995_film) "The Net (1995 film)")* (also 1995), starred Bullock as a computer programmer who stumbles upon a conspiracy that puts her life and the lives of those around her in great danger. [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman"), writing for *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")*, complimented her performance, saying, "Bullock pulls you into the movie. Her overripe smile and clear, imploring eyes are sometimes evocative of [Julia Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Roberts "Julia Roberts")". *While You Were Sleeping* and *The Net* made $182 million and $110.6 million, respectively.

### Worldwide recognition (1996–2007)

In *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(1996_film) "A Time to Kill (1996 film)")* (1996), a legal drama based on [John Grisham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Grisham "John Grisham")'s 1989 [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(Grisham_novel) "A Time to Kill (Grisham novel)"), Bullock portrayed a member of the defense team in the trial for the murder of two men who raped a young girl, opposite [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson "Samuel L. Jackson"), [Matthew McConaughey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_McConaughey "Matthew McConaughey") and [Kevin Spacey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Spacey "Kevin Spacey"). She received an [MTV Movie Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award "MTV Movie Award") nomination for Best Female Performance. She subsequently received $11 million for *[Speed 2: Cruise Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_2:_Cruise_Control "Speed 2: Cruise Control")* (1997), which she agreed to star in for financial backing for her next project, *[Hope Floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hope_Floats "Hope Floats")* (1998). Unlike the original film, *Speed 2* was a critical and commercial flop that she later disparaged. In *Hope Floats*, she starred as an unassuming housewife whose life is disrupted when her husband (played by [Michael Paré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Par%C3%A9 "Michael Paré")) reveals his infidelity to her on a talk show. Critic [James Berardinelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Berardinelli "James Berardinelli") remarked that her "undisputed strength lies in a blend of light drama and comedy". That film was a commercial success, grossing $81.4 million worldwide.

Bullock starred in the comedy *[Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic "Practical Magic")* (1998) alongside [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman "Nicole Kidman") as two witch sisters who face a curse which threatens to prevent them ever finding lasting love. While the film opened atop the chart on its North American opening weekend, it flopped at the box office. That year, she voiced Miriam in the [DreamWorks Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DreamWorks_Animation "DreamWorks Animation") film *[The Prince of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prince_of_Egypt "The Prince of Egypt")* and wrote, produced, and directed the short film *[Making Sandwiches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Making_Sandwiches "Making Sandwiches")*. She next played a free-spirited drifter who begins to talk to a writer ([Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck "Ben Affleck")) in the romantic comedy *[Forces of Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forces_of_Nature_(1999_film) "Forces of Nature (1999 film)")* (1999), on which *Boxoffice Magazine* remarked: "The combination of Affleck's deadpan by-the-book persona with the spontaneity of Bullock's character sparks with convincing chemistry." *Forces of Nature* made $93 million worldwide. Bullock took on the role of an [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI "FBI") agent who must go undercover as a beauty pageant contestant in the comedy *[Miss Congeniality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Congeniality_(film) "Miss Congeniality (film)")* (2000), which became another financial success, with a global gross of $212 million. It earned her a second nomination for the Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Motion Picture Comedy or Musical. In *[28 Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/28_Days_(film) "28 Days (film)")* (also 2000), a [dramedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_drama "Comedy drama") directed by [Betty Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Thomas "Betty Thomas"), Bullock starred as a newspaper columnist obliged to enter a rehabilitation program for alcoholism.

Bullock starred in the psychological thriller *[Murder by Numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_by_Numbers "Murder by Numbers")* (2002) as a seasoned homicide detective. [Roger Ebert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert "Roger Ebert") awarded the film three stars out of a possible four, stating: "Bullock does a good job here of working against her natural likability, creating a character you'd like to like, and could like, if she weren't so sad, strange and turned in upon herself." She teamed up with [Hugh Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Grant "Hugh Grant") for the romantic comedy *[Two Weeks Notice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Weeks_Notice "Two Weeks Notice")* (also 2002), in which she starred as a lawyer who walks out on her boss. Liz Braun, of *Jam! Movies*, found Bullock and Grant to be "perfectly paired", stating: "The script allows the two actors to be at their comedic best, even though the film as a whole is amateurish in many ways". *Two Weeks Notice* made $199 million globally. That year, she was presented with the [Raul Julia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raul_Julia "Raul Julia") Award for Excellence for helping expand career openings for Hispanic talent in the media and entertainment industry as the executive producer of the sitcom *[George Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lopez_(TV_series) "George Lopez (TV series)")* (2002–2007). She also made several appearances on the show as [Accident Amy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_George_Lopez_characters#Amy "List of George Lopez characters"), an accident-prone employee at the factory Lopez's character manages.

As part of a large ensemble cast, Bullock played the wife of a district attorney in the drama *[Crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_(2004_film) "Crash (2004 film)")* (2004), which won the [Academy Award for Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture "Academy Award for Best Picture"). She received positive reviews for her performance, with some critics suggesting that it was the best performance of her career to that point. For *Crash*, she received the [Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Cast_in_a_Motion_Picture "Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture") along with the rest of the cast. She next received a $17.5 million salary for *[Miss Congeniality 2: Armed and Fabulous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Congeniality_2:_Armed_and_Fabulous "Miss Congeniality 2: Armed and Fabulous")*, which Roger Ebert called a "doubly unnecessary" sequel. She was a co-recipient of the 2005 [Women in Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_Film_Crystal_%2B_Lucy_Awards "Women in Film Crystal + Lucy Awards") [Crystal Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_Film_Crystal_%2B_Lucy_Awards#THE_CRYSTAL_AWARD "Women in Film Crystal + Lucy Awards"). In 2006, Bullock reunited with [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves "Keanu Reeves") for the romantic drama *[The Lake House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lake_House_(film) "The Lake House (film)")*, although their characters were separated throughout the film and they were only on set together for two weeks during filming, and played [Harper Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harper_Lee "Harper Lee") in *[Infamous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infamous_(2006_film) "Infamous (2006 film)")*, a drama based on [George Plimpton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Plimpton "George Plimpton")'s 1997 book *Truman Capote*, alongside [Toby Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Jones "Toby Jones") and [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig "Daniel Craig"). *The Lake House* was a financial success, while *Infamous* received generally positive reviews. Bullock headlined the supernatural thriller *[Premonition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premonition_(2007_film) "Premonition (2007 film)")* (2007) as a housewife who experiences the days surrounding her husband's death in non-chronological order. Despite negative reviews, the film grossed $84.1 million worldwide.

### Established career (2008–2014)

In 2009, Bullock starred as a pushy editor-in-chief in the romantic comedy *[The Proposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Proposal_(2009_film) "The Proposal (2009 film)")*, opposite [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds "Ryan Reynolds"), which grossed $317 million at the worldwide box office, making it her fourth-most successful picture to date. She received her third nomination for the Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Motion Picture Comedy or Musical. That same year, the drama *[The Blind Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Side_(film) "The Blind Side (film)")* opened at number two behind *[New Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twilight_Saga:_New_Moon "The Twilight Saga: New Moon")* with $34.2 million, making it Bullock's second-highest opening weekend ever; it went on to gross over $309 million, making it her highest-grossing domestic film, her fourth-highest-grossing film worldwide, and the first one in history to pass the $200 million mark with only one top-billed female star. Bullock portrayed [Leigh Anne Tuohy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leigh_Anne_Tuohy "Leigh Anne Tuohy"), the adoptive mother of [Michael Oher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Oher "Michael Oher"), a role she had initially turned down three times due to discomfort in portraying a devout Christian. She was awarded the [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award "Academy Award"), [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award"), [Actor Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award "Actor Award") and [Critics' Choice Movie Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award "Critics' Choice Movie Award") for Best Actress. *The Blind Side* also received a nomination for the Academy Award for Best Picture. Winning the Oscar also gave Bullock another unique distinction: the prior night, she won the [Razzie Award for Worst Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Award_for_Worst_Actress "Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress") for her performance as an eccentric [crossword puzzle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossword_puzzle "Crossword puzzle") writer in *[All About Steve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_About_Steve "All About Steve")* (2009), becoming the only actor in history to win both awards for the same year.

By 2010, Bullock was said to be "courted for virtually every female starring role Hollywood has to offer", according to *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")*. [Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck "Ben Affleck"), her co-star in the romantic comedy *Forces of Nature* (1999), stated: "Every movie you hear about and every script I see, they say, 'We're going after Sandra Bullock for the woman'." She decided to eschew "the torrent of offers" in favour of "quietly [putting] acting on hold" to focus on her personal life. She also became "more selective" with the projects that she accepted, on which she said: "As I've gotten older, I say 'No' a lot more. I've become a better fighter for my work. I didn't have the guts to do that before". In 2010, she was the [world's highest-paid actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-paid_film_actors "List of highest-paid film actors"). She would next star alongside [Tom Hanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hanks "Tom Hanks") as a widow of the [September 11 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11_attacks "September 11 attacks") in the drama *[Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_Loud_%26_Incredibly_Close_(film) "Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close (film)")* (2011), a film adaptation based on the [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_Loud_%26_Incredibly_Close "Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close"). Despite mixed reviews, the film was nominated for numerous awards, including an Academy Award for Best Picture. Bullock was nominated for Favorite Actress Drama by [Teen Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Choice_Awards "Teen Choice Awards").

In 2013, Bullock starred alongside [Melissa McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_McCarthy "Melissa McCarthy") in the comedy *[The Heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heat_(film) "The Heat (film)")* as an [FBI Special Agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI_Special_Agent "FBI Special Agent") who, along with a city detective, must take down a mobster in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "Boston"). It received positive reviews from critics, and took in $230 million at the worldwide box office. Bullock subsequently played an astronaut stranded in space in the sci-fi thriller *[Gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_(2013_film) "Gravity (2013 film)")*, opposite [George Clooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Clooney "George Clooney"), which premiered at the [70th Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_Venice_International_Film_Festival "70th Venice International Film Festival") and was released on October 4, 2013, to coincide with the beginning of [World Space Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Space_Week "World Space Week"). *Gravity* received universal critical acclaim and a standing ovation in Venice. The film was called "the most realistic and beautifully choreographed film ever set in space" and certain critics considered Bullock's performance to be the best of her career. *Gravity* grossed $716 million worldwide to become Bullock's second-most successful film. For her role as Dr. Ryan Stone, Bullock was nominated for the Academy Award, Golden Globe Award, [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Leading_Role "BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Leading Role"), [Actor Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Female_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role "Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role"), and [Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award_for_Best_Actress "Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Actress"). On her performance, *Variety* wrote: Bullock inhabits the role with grave dignity and hints at Stone's past scars with sensitivity and tact, and she holds the screen effortlessly once *Gravity* becomes a veritable one-woman show [...] the actress remains fully present emotionally, projecting a very appealing combo of vulnerability, intelligence and determination that not only wins us over immediately, but sustains attention all the way through the cathartic closing reels. In 2014, she was Hollywood's highest-paid actress.

### Success with limited work (2015–present)

Bullock provided her voice for Scarlet Overkill, the villainous character, in the animated film *[Minions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minions_(film) "Minions (film)")* (2015), which became her highest-grossing film to date with a worldwide gross of over $1.1 billion. In 2015, she served as an executive producer and starred, as a political consultant hired to help win a Bolivian presidential election, in the drama *[Our Brand Is Crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Brand_Is_Crisis_(2015_film) "Our Brand Is Crisis (2015 film)")*, based on the [2005 documentary film of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Brand_Is_Crisis_(2005_film) "Our Brand Is Crisis (2005 film)") by Rachel Boynton. Peter Debruge of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* found Bullock's portrayal to be "easily one of the best female roles of the last 10 years", but film had the worst [wide release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide_release "Wide release") opening of her career.

In *[Ocean's 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean%27s_8 "Ocean's 8")* (2018), an all-female spin-off of the *Ocean's Eleven* franchise directed by [Gary Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Ross "Gary Ross"), Bullock played Debbie Ocean, the sister of [Danny Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Ocean_(character) "Danny Ocean (character)"), who helps plan a sophisticated heist of the annual [Met Gala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Met_Gala "Met Gala") in New York City. Writing for *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")*, Josh Spieger called the film a "welcome return to the big screen for Bullock" and observed: "She has reached a point in her career where she chooses her roles with care; before *Gravity*, she'd only co-starred in a handful of films over the previous five years, including her Oscar-winning turn in *The Blind Side*. *Ocean's 8* is Bullock's first true franchise film in decades [...] and it suggests that her decision to be more selective in which projects she picks can pay off in dividends". *Ocean's 8* had the best debut for the franchise, and ultimately made $297 million globally.

Her next role was that of Malorie, a woman who must find a way to guide herself and her children to safety despite the potential threat from an unseen adversary, in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") post-apocalyptic horror film *[Bird Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_Box_(film) "Bird Box (film)")* (2018), based on the [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_Box_(novel) "Bird Box (novel)"). She received acclaim for her performance, with *Variety* and *The Wrap* describing it as "wonderfully self-reliant", and "fascinating and terrifying to watch," respectively. *Bird Box* was the most-watched film on Netflix within 28 days of its release until 2021. In another production for Netflix, [Nora Fingscheidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nora_Fingscheidt "Nora Fingscheidt")'s drama *[The Unforgivable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Unforgivable "The Unforgivable")* (2021), Bullock played a woman who is released from prison after serving a sentence for a violent crime. Writing for *[Salon.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salon.com "Salon.com")*, Gary Kramer thought Bullock gave a "steely performance" despite the film being "forgettable". *The Unforgivable* became the fifth most-streamed on the platform at the time of its release.

She took on the role of a successful yet depressed best-selling romance novelist in [Paramount Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Pictures "Paramount Pictures")' *[The Lost City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_City_(2022_film) "The Lost City (2022 film)")* (2022), a *[Romancing the Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romancing_the_Stone "Romancing the Stone")*-style romantic comedy–adventure film, directed by [Adam and Aaron Nee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_and_Aaron_Nee "Adam and Aaron Nee"), opposite [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum "Channing Tatum") and [Daniel Radcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Radcliffe "Daniel Radcliffe"). The film was favorably received by critics, who praised the chemistry between Bullock and Tatum, and grossed $190.8 million globally, which made Bullock "the first actress to have $100 million earners in live-action star vehicles over four different decades", according to *Forbes*. She also featured in the [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt "Brad Pitt")-starring action thriller *[Bullet Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullet_Train_(film) "Bullet Train (film)")* (2022), in a mostly vocal performance.

She will reprise her role as Sally Owens in *[Practical Magic 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic_2 "Practical Magic 2")*, set for release on September 18, 2026.

## Other endeavors

### Business ventures

Bullock owns the production company [Fortis Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortis_Films "Fortis Films"), through which she has served as a producer for several of her [star vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_vehicles "Star vehicles"), including *Hope Floats* (1998), *Miss Congeniality* (2000), *Two Weeks Notice* (2002) and *Miss Congeniality 2: Armed and Fabulous* (2005), as well as for the sitcom *[George Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lopez_(TV_series) "George Lopez (TV series)")* (2002–2007). Her father, John Bullock, was the company's CEO and her sister, Gesine Bullock-Prado, is the former president. The company is headquartered in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"), and [West Hollywood, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Hollywood,_California "West Hollywood, California").

In November 2006, Bullock founded an Austin, Texas, restaurant named Bess Bistro which was located on West 6th Street. She later opened another business, Walton's Fancy and Staple, across the street in a building she extensively renovated. Walton's is a bakery, upscale restaurant, and floral shop that also offers services including event planning. After almost nine years in business, Bess Bistro closed on September 20, 2015.

### Philanthropy

Bullock has been a public supporter of the [American Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Red_Cross "American Red Cross") and has donated $1 million to the organization at least five times. Her first public donation of that amount was to the [Liberty Disaster Relief Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_assistance_following_the_September_11_attacks "Financial assistance following the September 11 attacks"). Three years later, she sent money in [response to the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunamis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanitarian_response_to_the_2004_Indian_Ocean_earthquake "Humanitarian response to the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake"). In 2010, she donated $1 million to relief efforts in Haiti following the [Haiti earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Haiti_earthquake "2010 Haiti earthquake") and again donated the same amount following the [2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami "2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami"). She donated $1 million in 2017 to support relief efforts for [Hurricane Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Harvey "Hurricane Harvey") in Texas.

In 2010, along with other public figures, Bullock made a public service announcement urging people to sign a petition for clean-up efforts of the [Deepwater Horizon oil spill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepwater_Horizon_oil_spill "Deepwater Horizon oil spill") in the Gulf of Mexico. Bullock was inducted into the Warren Easton Hall of Fame in 2012, for her donations to charities, and was honored with the [Favorite Humanitarian Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Choice_Awards#Special "People's Choice Awards") at the [39th People's Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/39th_People%27s_Choice_Awards "39th People's Choice Awards"), for her contributions to New Orleans's [Warren Easton High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Easton_High_School "Warren Easton High School"), which was severely damaged by [Hurricane Katrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina "Hurricane Katrina").

In 2014, Bullock designed a space-themed [Paddington Bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddington_Bear "Paddington Bear") statue, dubbed "Gravity Bear" (a reference to her 2013 film *Gravity*). The statue, number 43 out of 58 placed around London as part of the Paddington Trail, was located at the [Royal Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Observatory,_Greenwich "Royal Observatory, Greenwich") in [Greenwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenwich "Greenwich"), and was subsequently auctioned off to raise funds for the [National Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Society_for_the_Prevention_of_Cruelty_to_Children "National Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children") (NSPCC).

## Public image

Bullock has been dubbed "America's sweetheart" by the media in reference to her "friendly and direct and so unpretentious" nature. She was selected as one of *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* magazine's 50 Most Beautiful People in the world in 1996, 1999, and 2011. The publication named her as its Woman of the Year in 2010 as well as the [Most Beautiful Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine)#People_Magazine's_100_Most_Beautiful_People "People (magazine)") in 2015.

In 2010, *Time* magazine included Bullock in its annual [*Time* 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") as one of the most influential people in the world, and *Entertainment Weekly* named her The Most Powerful Actress in Hollywood. In 2013, she joined other Hollywood legends at the [TCL Chinese Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grauman%27s_Chinese_Theatre "Grauman's Chinese Theatre") on Hollywood Boulevard in making imprints of her hands and feet in cement of the theater's forecourt. That year, she was named among The Most Powerful Women in Entertainment by *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")*, and Entertainer of the Year, due to her success with *The Heat* and *Gravity*, by *[Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")*. Bullock ranked #2 on the 2014 *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")*' list of most powerful actresses and was honored with the Decade of Hotness Award by [Spike Guys' Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guys_Choice "Guys Choice").

## Personal life

Bullock owns properties in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), [Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas") and [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans").

On December 20, 2000, she was in a private jet crash on a runway from which she and the two crew escaped uninjured. Pilot error and blizzard conditions were responsible. The crew was unable to [activate the runway lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot-controlled_lighting "Pilot-controlled lighting") during a night landing at [Jackson Hole Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Hole_Airport "Jackson Hole Airport").

Bullock won a multimillion-dollar judgment against Benny Daneshjou, the builder of her Lake Austin, Texas, home in October 2004. The jury ruled that the house was uninhabitable. It has since been torn down and rebuilt. Daneshjou and his insurer later settled with Bullock for roughly half the awarded verdict.

While Bullock was in Massachusetts on April 18, 2008, shooting *The Proposal*, she and her then-husband [Jesse James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_James_(television_personality) "Jesse James (television personality)") were in a vehicle that was hit head-on by a drunk driver. They were uninjured.

### Relationships and family

Bullock was once engaged to actor [Tate Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_Donovan "Tate Donovan"), whom she met while filming *[Love Potion No. 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Potion_No._9_(film) "Love Potion No. 9 (film)")*. Their relationship lasted from 1990 to 1994. She dated football player [Troy Aikman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy_Aikman "Troy Aikman") and actors [Matthew McConaughey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_McConaughey "Matthew McConaughey") and [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling "Ryan Gosling").

Bullock married motorcycle builder and *[Monster Garage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Garage "Monster Garage")* host [Jesse James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_James_(television_personality) "Jesse James (television personality)") on July 16, 2005. They first met when Bullock arranged for her ten-year-old godson to meet James as a Christmas present. In November 2009, Bullock and James entered into a custody battle with James's second ex-wife, former adult film actress [Janine Lindemulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janine_Lindemulder "Janine Lindemulder"), with whom James had a child. Bullock and James subsequently won full legal custody of James's five-year-old daughter.

A scandal arose in March 2010 when several women claimed to have had affairs with James during his marriage to Bullock. Bullock canceled European promotional appearances for *The Blind Side* citing "unforeseen personal reasons". On March 18, James responded to the rumors of infidelity by issuing a public apology to Bullock. He stated, "The vast majority of the allegations reported are untrue and unfounded [...] beyond that, I will not dignify these private matters with any further public comment." James declared, "There is only one person to blame for this whole situation, and that is me." He asked that Bullock and their children one day "find it in their hearts to forgive me" for their "pain and embarrassment". James's publicist subsequently announced on March 30 that James had checked into a rehabilitation facility to "deal with personal issues" and save his relationship to Bullock. On April 23, Bullock filed for divorce in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas "Austin, Texas"). Their divorce was finalized on June 28, with "conflict of personalities" given as the reason.

Bullock announced in 2010 that she had proceeded with plans to adopt a son born in January 2010 in New Orleans, Louisiana. Bullock and James had begun an initial adoption process four months earlier. Bullock's son began living with them in January 2010 but they chose to keep the news private until after the Oscars ceremony that March. Given the pair's split and subsequent divorce, Bullock continued the adoption of her son as a single parent. Bullock announced in December 2015 that she had adopted a second child and appeared on the cover of *People* magazine with her then three-year-old new daughter.

Bullock was in a relationship with photographer Bryan Randall from mid-2015 until Randall's death due to [ALS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALS "ALS") on August 5, 2023.

### Stalking incidents

Beginning in 2002, Bullock was stalked across several states by a man named Thomas James Weldon. She obtained a restraining order against him in 2003, which was renewed in 2006. After the restraining order expired and Weldon was released from a mental institution, he again traveled through several states to find Bullock; she then obtained another restraining order.

On April 22, 2007, a woman named Marcia Diana Valentine was found lying outside James and Bullock's home in [Orange County, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County,_California "Orange County, California"). When James confronted the woman, she ran to her car, got behind the wheel and tried to run him over. She was said to be an obsessed fan of Bullock. Valentine was charged with one felony count each of aggravated assault and stalking. Bullock obtained a restraining order to bar Valentine from "contacting or coming near her home, family or work for three years". Valentine pleaded not guilty to charges of aggravated assault and stalking. She was subsequently convicted of stalking and sentenced to three years' probation.

Joshua James Corbett broke into Bullock's Los Angeles home in June 2014. Bullock locked herself in a room and dialed 911. Corbett pleaded no contest in 2017 and was sentenced to five years' probation for stalking Bullock and breaking into her residence. He was then subject to a ten-year protective order that required him to stay away from Bullock. After Corbett missed a court date the previous month, police officers went to his parents' residence on May 2, 2018, where he lived in a guest house, to arrest him. He refused to leave and threatened to shoot officers. A SWAT team was called and, after a five-hour standoff, they deployed gas canisters and entered the house where they found Corbett had killed himself. Corbett's death was the result of "multiple incised wounds" according to the Los Angeles County coroner.

## Filmography and accolades

Bullock has starred in [over 50 films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock_filmography "Sandra Bullock filmography"), and has helped produce over 15 works in film and television. Her highest-grossing releases are *[Speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_(1994_film) "Speed (1994 film)")* (1994), *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_(1996_film) "A Time to Kill (1996 film)")* (1996), *[The Prince of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prince_of_Egypt "The Prince of Egypt")* (1998), *[Miss Congeniality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Congeniality_(film) "Miss Congeniality (film)")* (2000), *[The Proposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Proposal_(2009_film) "The Proposal (2009 film)")* (2009), *[The Blind Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Side_(film) "The Blind Side (film)")* (2009), *[The Heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heat_(film) "The Heat (film)")* (2013), *[Gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_(2013_film) "Gravity (2013 film)")* (2013), *[Minions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minions_(film) "Minions (film)")* (2015), and *[Ocean's 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean%27s_8 "Ocean's 8")* (2018).

On March 24, 2005, Bullock received a [motion picture star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_actors_with_Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame_motion_picture_stars "List of actors with Hollywood Walk of Fame motion picture stars") on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame") at 6801 Hollywood Boulevard in Hollywood. Her performance in the biographical drama film *The Blind Side* (2009) garnered her the [Academy Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress "Academy Award for Best Actress"), the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Drama "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama") and the [Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Female_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role "Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role"). Bullock was nominated again in these categories for her performance in the film *Gravity* (2013).
