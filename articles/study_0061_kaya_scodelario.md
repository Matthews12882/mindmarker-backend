# Kaya Scodelario

**Kaya Rose Scodelario** (born 13 March 1992) is a British actress and model. She first came to prominence co-starring on [E4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E4_(TV_channel) "E4 (TV channel)")'s *[Skins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skins_(British_TV_series) "Skins (British TV series)")* (2007–2010, 2013), receiving two [Golden Nymph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Nymph_Awards "Golden Nymph Awards") nominations for her portrayal of [Effy Stonem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effy_Stonem "Effy Stonem"). She then had leading roles in films including *[Wuthering Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights_(2011_film) "Wuthering Heights (2011 film)")* (2011), the [*Maze Runner* series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maze_Runner_(film_series) "Maze Runner (film series)") (2014–2018), *[Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean:_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales "Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales")* (2017), *[Crawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawl_(2019_film) "Crawl (2019 film)")* (2019), and *[Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Welcome_to_Raccoon_City "Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City")* (2021). In 2024, she starred in the action-comedy series *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2024_TV_series) "The Gentlemen (2024 TV series)")* and limited biographical drama series *[Senna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senna_(miniseries) "Senna (miniseries)")*.

## Early life

Scodelario was born Kaya Rose Humphrey on 13 March 1992, in [Haywards Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haywards_Heath "Haywards Heath"), West Sussex. Her mother, Katia Scodelario, is a Brazilian who worked as an accountant in [Itu, São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itu,_S%C3%A3o_Paulo "Itu, São Paulo"), and moved to England in 1990; Scodelario's surname comes from her mother's Italian grandfather. Her father, Roger Humphrey, was Polish-English and died in November 2010.

Her parents divorced when Scodelario was one year old, and her mother took her to London when she was four. They spent their first night on the street before finding a [council flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_flat "Council flat") on [Holloway Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holloway_Road "Holloway Road"). Scodelario took her mother's surname and spoke Portuguese at home. She attended St Joseph's Catholic Primary School, Highgate Hill and she attended [Bishop Douglass Catholic School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_Douglass_Catholic_School "Bishop Douglass Catholic School") and then [Islington Arts and Media School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islington_Arts_and_Media_School "Islington Arts and Media School"). She participated in school plays and found drama an escape from bullying. Her mother took on multiple jobs to make ends meet and had clinical depression that worsened during Scodelario's teen years. Scodelario said she wanted to help, but did not know how, and moved to a flat in [Camden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Borough_of_Camden "London Borough of Camden").

## Career

In 2006, at age 14 and with no acting experience, Scodelario was cast in the first series of *[Skins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skins_(British_TV_series) "Skins (British TV series)")* as [Effy Stonem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effy_Stonem "Effy Stonem"). At the auditions, she became discouraged as she felt she was too young, but a producer told her to stay and she was asked to read for the part. While Scodelario's role in the first series had minimal speaking lines, her character developed considerably during the second series. She eventually became the central character in the third and fourth series after the cast had been replaced by a new generation of characters. This made Effy one of the only characters to appear from series 1 all the way through to series 4. Filming began in July and Scodelario said that 18 November 2009 was her last day of filming the series and that she would miss being on the show.[*[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")*]

Critics praised her performance, and she was nominated twice for Best Actress at the *[TV Quick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Quick "TV Quick")* Awards, in 2009 and 2010. In the episode "[Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skins_Fire "Skins Fire")", one of three parts of the seventh (and final) season of *Skins*, Scodelario reprised the role of Effy. It is a two-part depiction of her life as an adult, lasting two hours. She claims "Fire" is "more like a movie" and that she could relate to Effy's struggle to change from a teenager into an adult.

Scodelario made her film debut in the science fiction-[thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_(genre) "Thriller (genre)") film *[Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon_(2009_film) "Moon (2009 film)")*, which premiered at the 2009 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival"), receiving positive reviews. In her second film, *[Shank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shank_(2010_film) "Shank (2010 film)")*, released on 26 March 2010, she played teenager Tasha. In addition, she appeared in the 2010 remake of *[Clash of the Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clash_of_the_Titans_(2010_film) "Clash of the Titans (2010 film)")* as Peshet.

In April 2010, Scodelario confirmed her casting as Cathy in an adaptation of *[Wuthering Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights_(2011_film) "Wuthering Heights (2011 film)")* directed by [Andrea Arnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Arnold "Andrea Arnold"). The film premiered at the [Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Film_Festival "Venice Film Festival") in 2011 to generally positive reviews, and Scodelario was hailed as a "heart-wrenching revelation". *Wuthering Heights* also played at several other international film festivals, including the 2011 [Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival "Toronto International Film Festival"), the [London Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Film_Festival "London Film Festival"), and the 2012 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival").

In 2011, she signed on to play Sally Weaver in a British thriller, *[Twenty8k](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty8k "Twenty8k")*. The film was released in September 2012. In late June, Scodelario announced that she had signed on to star in *[Now Is Good](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_Is_Good "Now Is Good")* with [Dakota Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakota_Fanning "Dakota Fanning").[*[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")*] The film is based on [Jenny Downham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Downham "Jenny Downham")'s novel *[Before I Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_I_Die "Before I Die")*, about a teenage girl with leukaemia who makes a list of things to do before she dies. She portrays Zoey, the protagonist's best friend. In September 2011, Scodelario filmed a television serial for [BBC One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_One "BBC One"), *[True Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Love_(TV_series) "True Love (TV series)")*, an improvised series comprising five stand-alone episodes, each covering a different love-related dilemma. She is cast in the third storyline as Karen, the love interest of her teacher, portrayed by [Billie Piper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Piper "Billie Piper").

In January 2012, Scodelario made her first American film, *[The Truth About Emanuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Truth_About_Emanuel "The Truth About Emanuel")*, in Los Angeles. She stars as the title character, Emanuel, a troubled 17-year-old girl who babysits her new neighbour Linda's "baby", which is actually a very lifelike doll. It had a limited release on 10 January 2014. In April 2012, Scodelario shot a cameo appearance for the film *Spike Island*. She also participated in an advertising campaign for Korean jewelry brand J. Estina. In 2013, she starred in the four-part [Channel 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_4 "Channel 4") drama series *[Southcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southcliffe "Southcliffe")*, which tells the story of a fictional English town devastated by a spate of shootings, exploring the tragedy through the eyes of a journalist and those close to the victims.

She signed on to the film franchise *[The Maze Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Maze_Runner_(film) "The Maze Runner (film)")* in April 2013 as the lead female character, Teresa, in the first film, based on the [novel of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Maze_Runner "The Maze Runner") by [James Dashner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Dashner "James Dashner"). It was released in September 2014, and its sequel, *[Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maze_Runner:_The_Scorch_Trials "Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials")*, in September 2015. She filmed the lead role in the film adaptation of *[The Moon and the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moon_and_the_Sun "The Moon and the Sun")* in 2014, but the film was not released until 2022, under the title *[The King's Daughter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King%27s_Daughter_(2022_film) "The King's Daughter (2022 film)")*. She starred in the home-invasion thriller *[Tiger House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_House "Tiger House")*, released in 2015, and also had a starring role in the sequel *[Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean:_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales "Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales")*, which was released on 26 May 2017.

Scodelario reprised the role of Teresa in *[Maze Runner: The Death Cure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maze_Runner:_The_Death_Cure "Maze Runner: The Death Cure").* The film was set for release on 17 February 2017, but after her co-star [Dylan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_O%27Brien "Dylan O'Brien") was injured on set, it was pushed back to 26 January 2018.[*[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")*]

The year 2019 brought new projects for Scodelario. The first, released in May, was *[Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_Wicked,_Shockingly_Evil_and_Vile "Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile")*, about serial killer [Ted Bundy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Bundy "Ted Bundy"); she portrays his wife, Carole Ann Boone. She played the lead in [Alexandre Aja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Aja "Alexandre Aja")'s horror film *[Crawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawl_(2019_film) "Crawl (2019 film)")*, which was released in July to positive reviews. Her next film, 2021's *[Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil:_Welcome_to_Raccoon_City "Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City")*, saw Scodelario lead another horror film, this time portraying video game character [Claire Redfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Redfield "Claire Redfield") as a main protagonist.

Scodelario then signed up to lead a new [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") series, an edgy [figure skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating "Figure skating") drama called *[Spinning Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinning_Out "Spinning Out")* in 2019. Debuting in early 2020, the series explored multi-person mental illness within a family and its interaction with high-level competitive sports.

In spring 2019, Scodelario became the face of [Cartier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartier_(jeweler) "Cartier (jeweler)")'s new jewelry line, Clash de Cartier, and filmed the latest [Agatha Christie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agatha_Christie "Agatha Christie") miniseries adaptation, *[The Pale Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pale_Horse_(2020_miniseries) "The Pale Horse (2020 miniseries)")*, for Amazon and BBC. In August 2021, Scodelario joined dramedy *[Don't Make Me Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Make_Me_Go_(film) "Don't Make Me Go (film)")* being developed under [Amazon Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Studios "Amazon Studios"), and filmed *[This Is Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Is_Christmas_(2022_film) "This Is Christmas (2022 film)")* the next summer. Scodelario filmed two series for [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") in 2023: *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2024_TV_series) "The Gentlemen (2024 TV series)")*, inspired by the [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie") [film of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2019_film) "The Gentlemen (2019 film)"), and *Senna*, a biographical drama about the Brazilian [Formula One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One "Formula One") driver [Ayrton Senna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrton_Senna "Ayrton Senna"), where she played a fictional, though important character of a reporter called Laura Harrison. In 2025, she starred in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") thriller film *[The Woman in Cabin 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Woman_in_Cabin_10_(film) "The Woman in Cabin 10 (film)")*.

## Personal life

Scodelario dated her *Skins* co-star [Jack O'Connell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_O%27Connell_(actor) "Jack O'Connell (actor)") from 2008 to 2009, they remain good friends. She was in a relationship with [Elliott Tittensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Tittensor "Elliott Tittensor") from late 2009 to early 2014. During their relationship, she supported Tittensor after his arrest for hitting someone while driving an uninsured car.

Scodelario and [Benjamin Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Walker_(actor) "Benjamin Walker (actor)") began dating during the filming of *[The King's Daughter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King%27s_Daughter_(2022_film) "The King's Daughter (2022 film)")* in early 2014, becoming engaged in December 2014 and marrying in December 2015. Their first child, a son, was born in November 2016. Scodelario's *Skins* co-star [Daniel Kaluuya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Kaluuya "Daniel Kaluuya"), who grew up in the same neighbourhood as her, is her son's godfather. On 18 September 2021, Scodelario announced she was pregnant with her second child. In December 2021, she gave birth to a daughter. In February 2024, the couple announced they had separated.

She was voted 13th in the UK edition of [FHM's 100 World's Sexiest Women 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FHM%27s_100_Sexiest_Women_(UK) "FHM's 100 Sexiest Women (UK)").

In January 2018, Scodelario alleged that she had been sexually assaulted when she was 12 years old.
