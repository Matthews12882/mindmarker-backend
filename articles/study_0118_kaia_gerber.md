# Kaia Gerber

**Kaia Jordan Gerber** (born September 3, 2001) is an American model and actress. After starring in a series of ad campaigns for fashion brands since debuting at [Fashion Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Fashion_Week "New York Fashion Week") in 2017, Gerber won Model of the Year at the [British Fashion Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fashion_Awards "The Fashion Awards") in 2018. As an actress, she has appeared in supporting roles in the films *[Bottoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottoms_(film) "Bottoms (film)")* (2023) and *[Saturday Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_(2024_film) "Saturday Night (2024 film)")* (2024), and starred in a lead role in the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") series [*The Shards.*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shards_(TV_series) "The Shards (TV series)") She is the second child and only daughter of supermodel [Cindy Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cindy_Crawford "Cindy Crawford") and businessman [Rande Gerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rande_Gerber "Rande Gerber").

## Early life

Kaia Jordan Gerber was born on September 3, 2001, in Los Angeles, California. She is the younger of two children born to the supermodel [Cindy Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cindy_Crawford "Cindy Crawford") and the businessman [Rande Gerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rande_Gerber "Rande Gerber"). She had an elder brother, [Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presley_Gerber "Presley Gerber").

Her mother, who is of German, English, French, and Danish ancestry, is a [Congregationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congregationalist "Congregationalist") Christian, and her father is an American Jew of Ashkenazi Jewish descent. In a 2018 interview, she stated "my dad is Jewish and my mom is Christian so we grew up doing both; we call it [Chrismukah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrismukkah "Chrismukkah"). I love learning about both of those sides of my life. We'd do [Passover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passover "Passover"), [Passover dinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passover_Seder "Passover Seder") and [Yom Kippur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yom_Kippur "Yom Kippur"). I love learning all of that, and learning the cooking." Through her mother, she is a descendant of English [Puritan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puritan "Puritan") settler Thomas Trowbridge, who helped establish the [Congregational Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congregational_Church "Congregational Church") in [New Haven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Haven "New Haven").

## Career

### 2012–2018: Early work

When she was 10, Gerber landed her first modeling gig with [Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace "Versace")'s junior line, Young Versace. She took online classes at [Malibu High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_High_School "Malibu High School"). She was part of the Young Versace S/S 12 campaign when she was only 10 years old. Gerber made her acting debut at 15 in *[Sister Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_Cities_(film) "Sister Cities (film)")* as the young version of Carolina. In July 2015, she signed under IMG models.

Gerber appeared on the covers of *[Vogue Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Paris "Vogue Paris")*, *[Vogue Netherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Netherland "Vogue Netherland")*, and *[Vogue India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_India "Vogue India")* along with her mother in 2016. In 2016, Gerber was also part of the [Marc Jacobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Jacobs "Marc Jacobs") "Daisy" fragrance advertisement and beauty fall campaign. Gerber was also part of the [Alexander Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Wang_(designer) "Alexander Wang (designer)")'s S/S 16 campaign, and Chrome Hearts S/S 16 campaign. In 2017, Gerber appeared on the *[Teen Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Vogue "Teen Vogue")* Fall 2017 edition. In February 2018, Gerber appeared on the cover of *[Vogue Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Paris "Vogue Paris")*, in July 2018 on the cover of *[Vogue Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Italia "Vogue Italia")* and on October in the cover of *Vogue Paris*.

Gerber made her runway debut for [Raf Simons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raf_Simons "Raf Simons")' [Calvin Klein Collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Klein_Collection "Calvin Klein Collection") at 16 years old and walked for fashion houses such as [Marc Jacobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Jacobs "Marc Jacobs"), [Burberry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burberry "Burberry"), [Alexander Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Wang_(designer) "Alexander Wang (designer)"), [Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapestry,_Inc. "Tapestry, Inc."), [Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prada "Prada"), [Chanel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanel "Chanel"), [Fendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fendi "Fendi"), [Miu Miu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miu_Miu "Miu Miu"), [Max Mara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Mara "Max Mara"), [Givenchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Givenchy "Givenchy"), [Bottega Veneta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottega_Veneta "Bottega Veneta"), [Stella McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_McCartney "Stella McCartney"), [Isabel Marant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabel_Marant "Isabel Marant"), [Alberta Ferretti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Ferretti "Alberta Ferretti"), [Loewe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loewe_(fashion_brand) "Loewe (fashion brand)"), [Chloé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9 "Chloé"), [Sacai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacai "Sacai"), [Michael Kors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kors "Michael Kors"), [Moncler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moncler "Moncler"), [JW. Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JW_Anderson "JW Anderson"), [Longchamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longchamp_(company) "Longchamp (company)"), [Tom Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Ford "Tom Ford"), [Salvatore Ferragamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvatore_Ferragamo_S.p.A. "Salvatore Ferragamo S.p.A."), [Ports1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ports_International "Ports International"), [Lanvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanvin_(company) "Lanvin (company)"), [Anna Sui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Sui "Anna Sui"), [Moschino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moschino "Moschino"), [Saint Laurent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves_Saint_Laurent_(brand) "Yves Saint Laurent (brand)"), [Alexander McQueen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_McQueen "Alexander McQueen"), [Valentino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentino_(fashion_designer) "Valentino (fashion designer)"), and [Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace "Versace"), alongside her mother, during 2018 Spring Fashion Week. Gerber rose to fame after opening Alexander Wang's S/S 17 show at the age of 16.

Gerber has made editorials and has been on the cover of fashion magazines, including *[The Love Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_(magazine) "Love (magazine)"), [i-D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I-D "I-D")*, *[Teen Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Vogue "Teen Vogue")* and *[Pop Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Magazine "Pop Magazine")*, as well as different covers and international editions of [*Vogue*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(magazine) "Vogue (magazine)"), including *Vogue Paris*, *[Vogue Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Japan "Vogue Japan")*, *Vogue Italia*, [*Vogue British*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Vogue "British Vogue"), *Vogue India*, *[Vogue China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_China "Vogue China")*, [*Vogue Magazine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(magazine) "Vogue (magazine)"), and "The Book edition" for *[Vogue Netherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Netherland "Vogue Netherland")* with her mother in Winter 2016.

Gerber appeared in the [Miu Miu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miu_Miu "Miu Miu") eyewear campaign in 2016 and the [Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_SA "Omega SA") campaign in 2017 with her mother photographed by [Peter Lindbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Lindbergh "Peter Lindbergh"). In Spring 2018, she appeared in campaigns for [Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace "Versace"), [Calvin Klein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_Klein_(company) "Calvin Klein (company)"), and [Saint Laurent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves_Saint_Laurent_(brand) "Yves Saint Laurent (brand)") fall/winter. In 2018, she and [Sofia Mechetner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Mechetner "Sofia Mechetner") led the campaign for [Marc Jacobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Jacobs "Marc Jacobs")' fragrance Daisy. In the same year, she was the star of the [Chanel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanel "Chanel") spring handbags campaign photographed by [Karl Lagerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Lagerfeld "Karl Lagerfeld"), as well as [Valentino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentino_(fashion_designer) "Valentino (fashion designer)"). In 2018, Gerber collaborated with Karl Lagerfeld to create the KarlxKaia collection, her first collection. Also in 2018, Gerber won Model of the Year at the Fashion Awards.

### 2019–present: Rise to prominence

In 2019, Gerber starred in [Jimmy Choo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Choo_(fashion_house) "Jimmy Choo (fashion house)")'s spring/summer and fall/winter campaigns, the [Stella McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_McCartney "Stella McCartney") spring campaign and the [Fendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fendi "Fendi") spring campaign. She also debuted as the new face of advertising campaigns and brand ambassador for [YSL beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves_Saint_Laurent_(fashion_house) "Yves Saint Laurent (fashion house)"). Gerber also appeared on the cover of the *British Vogue* for the October Issue. For the F/W 2019 season Gerber walked 26 fashion shows, opening 4 and closing 4.

In 2020, Gerber starred in the [Louis Vuitton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Vuitton "Louis Vuitton") "Twist bag" spring summer advertising campaign photographed by [Craig McDean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_McDean "Craig McDean"). In 2020 Gerber appeared on her first American *Vogue* cover for the April issue along with supermodel [Adut Akech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adut_Akech "Adut Akech") and also appeared on the cover of *Vogue Italia* for the May and September Issue.

On August 14, 2020, *[Daily Front Row](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Front_Row "Daily Front Row")* listed Gerber as one of a group of high-profile investors who purchased *[W magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_magazine "W magazine")*, a fashion magazine. Gerber appeared in the [tenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Double_Feature "American Horror Story: Double Feature") of the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") horror anthology *[American Horror Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story "American Horror Story")*, as well as its spinoff, *[American Horror Stories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Stories "American Horror Stories")*. In 2021, she appeared on the American *[Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(magazine) "Vogue (magazine)")* cover, featuring on their June/July issue, and again on their September issue. She next appeared in [Damien Chazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Chazelle "Damien Chazelle")'s period drama *[Babylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon_(2022_film) "Babylon (2022 film)"),* which was her feature film debut.

In 2023, Gerber covered the American and [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_France "Elle France") editions of *[Elle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_(magazine) "Elle (magazine)")* and starred in an advertising campaign for [Alaïa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ala%C3%AFa "Alaïa"). She also appeared in the teen sex comedy film *[Bottoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottoms_(film) "Bottoms (film)")* alongside [Rachel Sennott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Sennott "Rachel Sennott") and [Ayo Edebiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayo_Edebiri "Ayo Edebiri"). It premiered at [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest "South by Southwest") on March 11, 2023 to positive reviews. It was released in the United States on August 25, 2023. In 2024, Gerber appeared on the British Vogue for the March issue which featured 40 icons in the fashion industry.

On March 20, 2024, *[Palm Royale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_Royale "Palm Royale")* debuted on [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+"), with Gerber taking on the role of Mitzi, an aspiring model in 1969 Palm Beach. She appeared in both seasons of the show. On April 6, 2024, Gerber appeared in a cameo role in the *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* sketch "Pilates". In December 2025, Gerber was named Global Brand Ambassador for [NARS Cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NARS_Cosmetics "NARS Cosmetics"). She fronts the brand's Afterglow Lip Balm campaign, which was photographed by NARS founder and creative director François Nars.

## Personal life

Gerber resides in New York City and [Malibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu,_California "Malibu, California"). She dated comedian [Pete Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Davidson "Pete Davidson") from late 2019 until February 2020. Gerber then dated [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi "Jacob Elordi") from September 2020 to November 2021. In her longest relationship to date, Gerber dated actor [Austin Butler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Butler "Austin Butler") from December 2021 to January 2025. As of January 2025, Gerber is in a relationship with [Lewis Pullman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Pullman "Lewis Pullman").
