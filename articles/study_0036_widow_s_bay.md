# Widow's Bay

***Widow's Bay*** is an American [comedy horror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_horror "Comedy horror") television series created by [Katie Dippold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Dippold "Katie Dippold") for [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_(streaming_service) "Apple TV (streaming service)"), and starring [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys "Matthew Rhys"), [Kate O'Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_O%27Flynn "Kate O'Flynn"), [Kevin Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Carroll_(actor) "Kevin Carroll (actor)"), [Dale Dickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Dickey "Dale Dickey"), [Kingston Rumi Southwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_Rumi_Southwick "Kingston Rumi Southwick"), and [Stephen Root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Root "Stephen Root"). The [supernatural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernatural_fiction "Supernatural fiction") series is set in the fictional [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England "New England") island town of Widow's Bay, which happens to be afflicted with a centuries-old curse that brings various supernatural evils upon the island. Dippold serves as showrunner and executive producer, while [Hiro Murai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiro_Murai "Hiro Murai") serves as director and executive producer.

The series premiered on April 29, 2026, to critical acclaim. For its first season, it received a record 14 wins at the [78th Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/78th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards "78th Primetime Emmy Awards"), including for [Outstanding Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series"), acting wins for Rhys, O'Flynn, Root, and [Betty Gilpin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Gilpin "Betty Gilpin"), as well as wins for directing and writing. In June 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

## Production

### Development

The series was created by [Katie Dippold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Dippold "Katie Dippold"), who is an executive producer and showrunner. It was ordered by [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_(streaming_service) "Apple TV (streaming service)") in September 2024, with [Hiro Murai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiro_Murai "Hiro Murai") directing and executive-producing, alongside Claudia Shin and Carver Karaszewski. In February 2025, [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys "Matthew Rhys") joined the project as an executive producer; he leads the cast as Mayor Tom Loftis. Murai directed five episodes of the first season; [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West "Ti West"), Sam Donovan, and [Andrew DeYoung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_DeYoung_(director) "Andrew DeYoung (director)") served as directors of the remaining episodes. Alongside Rhys, the cast includes [Kate O'Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_O%27Flynn "Kate O'Flynn"), [Stephen Root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Root "Stephen Root"), [Kingston Rumi Southwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_Rumi_Southwick "Kingston Rumi Southwick"), [Kevin Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Carroll_(actor) "Kevin Carroll (actor)"), and [Dale Dickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Dickey "Dale Dickey").

On June 11, 2026, Apple TV renewed the series for a second season. In August 2026, [Jeff Hiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Hiller "Jeff Hiller") was promoted to a series regular for the second season.

### Filming

Filming took place in [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts "Massachusetts") in 2025, with locations including [Ayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayer,_Massachusetts "Ayer, Massachusetts") and [Worcester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts "Worcester, Massachusetts") in May, and [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex,_Massachusetts "Essex, Massachusetts"), [Gloucester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloucester,_Massachusetts "Gloucester, Massachusetts") and [Rockport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockport,_Massachusetts "Rockport, Massachusetts") in June. Interiors were constructed and shot at [New England Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_Studios "New England Studios") in [Devens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devens,_Massachusetts "Devens, Massachusetts"). The [Berlin Town Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Town_Hall_(Berlin,_Massachusetts) "Berlin Town Hall (Berlin, Massachusetts)") stood in for the town hall of Widow's Bay while [Eastern Point Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Point_Light "Eastern Point Light") was used as the town's lighthouse. The [Rebecca Nurse Homestead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Nurse_Homestead "Rebecca Nurse Homestead") in [Danvers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danvers,_Massachusetts "Danvers, Massachusetts"), the [Martha Mary Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Mary_Chapel "Martha Mary Chapel") in [Sudbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudbury,_Massachusetts "Sudbury, Massachusetts"), and the Lorenzo Maynard House in [Maynard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maynard,_Massachusetts "Maynard, Massachusetts") were also used as filming locations. [Christian Sprenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Sprenger_(cinematographer) "Christian Sprenger (cinematographer)") served as [cinematographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinematographer "Cinematographer") for six of the ten episodes of *Widow's Bay*'s first season while Cody Jacobs shot the remaining four episodes. Visually, Sprenger wanted to "reference this feeling of nostalgia of cinema past" as nothing about the town of Widow's Bay is modern, citing *[Jaws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaws_(film) "Jaws (film)")* and *[The Shining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shining_(film) "The Shining (film)")* as references used. He shot the series using the [Arri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arri "Arri") [Alexa 35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arri_Alexa "Arri Alexa") [large format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_format "Large format") digital camera. The episode "[Our History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_History_(Widow%27s_Bay) "Our History (Widow's Bay)")" was shot with the Arri Alexa 265 in a 1.43:1 [aspect ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_(image) "Aspect ratio (image)") to make the episode stand out with its flashbacks to 1702. Despite shooting digitally, the series wanted to emulate the look of [35mm film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/35_mm_movie_film "35 mm movie film") with Sprenger photographing material on Kodak Vision3 500T 5219 [film stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_stock "Film stock") during camera tests as a side-by-side comparison to emulate. It is from these test images that the show's [LUT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lookup_table "Lookup table"), including [grain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_grain "Film grain") and highlight behavior.

### Music

After having filmed the first two episodes, Dippold contacted [David Fleming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fleming_(composer) "David Fleming (composer)") to score *Widow's Bay*. Neither of the two wanted to use [comedy music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_music "Comedy music"), with Fleming suggesting to use elements from the fictional town to create the soundscape. This included the use of [bass flutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_flute "Bass flute") and other woodwinds, as well as [conches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conch_(instrument) "Conch (instrument)"). For the sixth episode, set in 1702, instruments such as the [Baroque cello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroque_violin "Baroque violin") and [viola da gamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viol "Viol") were used. For the eighth episode, inspiration was drawn from [John Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Carpenter "John Carpenter")'s [film score to the 1978 film *Halloween*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween_(1978_soundtrack) "Halloween (1978 soundtrack)") and its use of the [analog synthesizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_synthesizer "Analog synthesizer"). The score was recorded in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"), and the soundtrack album was released on May 4, 2026, via [Milan Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Records "Milan Records").

## Release

The first two episodes were released on Apple TV on April 29, 2026, with further episodes being made available weekly.

## Reception

### Critical response

On the review aggregator website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), the first season holds an approval rating of 98%, based on 108 reviews, with an average rating of 8.5/10. The website's critics consensus states: "Katie Dippold successfully continues to invest in eccentricity with this outlandish horror-comedy that stokes the genre's well-worn tropes to winning effect, bringing scares, laughs, and a game cast." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 78 out of 100, based on 26 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.

[Lucy Mangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Mangan "Lucy Mangan") of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* gave the series 5 out of 5 stars, calling it "an absolute blast [...] rich and wonderful. Grownup, funny, scary, true—*[Mare of Easttown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mare_of_Easttown "Mare of Easttown")* meets *[Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt%27s_Creek "Schitt's Creek")*, but with something else that makes it singular". *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")*'s Alison Herman wrote, "*Widow's Bay* may not make the best tourist destination, but I'd happily make a return trip."
