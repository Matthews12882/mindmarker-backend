# Katseye

**Katseye** (stylized in [all caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps "All caps")) is a [girl group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_group "Girl group") formed in 2024, based in Los Angeles. The group consists of six members: [Daniela Avanzini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Avanzini "Daniela Avanzini"), [Lara Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Raj "Lara Raj"), [Manon Bannerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manon_Bannerman "Manon Bannerman"), [Megan Skiendiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Skiendiel "Megan Skiendiel"), [Sophia Laforteza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_Laforteza "Sophia Laforteza"), and Yoonchae Jeung. With members from the Philippines, South Korea, Switzerland, and the United States, Katseye is often called a "global girl group".

The group was formed through the 2023 reality competition series *[Dream Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_Academy "Dream Academy")*, a collaboration between [Hybe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybe "Hybe") and [Geffen Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geffen_Records "Geffen Records"). Its formation is chronicled in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") [docuseries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docuseries "Docuseries") *[Pop Star Academy: Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Star_Academy:_Katseye "Pop Star Academy: Katseye")*, which recounts the audition process, intensive training, and formation of the group. Katseye debuted in June 2024 with the single "[Debut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debut_(song) "Debut (song)")", followed by their breakthrough single, "[Touch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch_(Katseye_song) "Touch (Katseye song)")". They released their first [extended play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") (EP), *[SIS (Soft Is Strong)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIS_(Soft_Is_Strong) "SIS (Soft Is Strong)")*, later that year. In April 2025, they released "[Gnarly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnarly_(song) "Gnarly (song)")", the lead single for their second EP, *[Beautiful Chaos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_Chaos_(EP) "Beautiful Chaos (EP)")* (2025). Despite receiving mixed reactions on release, the single garnered significant attention and marked the group's first entry on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), while *Beautiful Chaos* debuted at number four on the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200"). The EP's second single, "[Gabriela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_(song) "Gabriela (song)")", peaked within the top 40 of the Hot 100 and was nominated for the [Grammy Award for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Duo/Group_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance"), with the group also nominated for [Best New Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_New_Artist "Grammy Award for Best New Artist") at the [68th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/68th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "68th Annual Grammy Awards"). Their third EP, *[Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_(Katseye_EP) "Wild (Katseye EP)")* (2026), became their first [number one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Billboard_200_number-one_albums_of_2026 "List of Billboard 200 number-one albums of 2026") release on the *Billboard* 200.

Katseye is recognized for their distinctive fashion identity, prominently drawing influence from [Y2K fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y2K_fashion "Y2K fashion"). Their growing brand image has led to multiple endorsements and campaigns tying into the style, including a campaign with [Fendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fendi "Fendi")'s [Baguette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baguette_(bag) "Baguette (bag)") bag and [Gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gap_Inc. "Gap Inc.")'s low-rise denim. Katseye has also worked with British cosmetics retailer [Lush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lush_(company) "Lush (company)"), American fashion house [Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coach_New_York "Coach New York"), and Filipino fast food chain [Jollibee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jollibee "Jollibee"). [Humberto Leon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humberto_Leon "Humberto Leon") is the group's creative director, taking inspiration from broad influences and the group's diverse backgrounds.

## Name

In an interview with *[InStyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InStyle "InStyle")*, group member Sophia explained that the name Katseye derives from the gemstone [chrysoberyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysoberyl "Chrysoberyl"), specifically the [cymophane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cymophane "Cymophane") variety, commonly known as "cat's eye"; the gemstone's distinctive optical effect, where light appears to shift and reflect differently when viewed from various angles, corresponds to the composition of the group's members' diverse personalities, talents, and backgrounds. The group was initially planned to be named "Catseye", but during an interview with LA radio station [KIIS-FM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KIIS-FM "KIIS-FM"), it was revealed that Lara suggested the spelling "Katseye", which the members felt gave the name a more distinctive and stylized appearance. Katseye's logo and branding were designed by Seoul-based graphic design studio Husky Fox, incorporating elements of the gemstone alongside the six members' unique backgrounds.

## History

### 2007–2024: Pre-debut activities and formation through *Dream Academy*

Several members of Katseye had experience in professional dance, television, and the entertainment industry before debuting with the group. [Sophia Laforteza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_Laforteza "Sophia Laforteza"), born to Filipino musical theater actress [Carla Guevara Laforteza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Guevara_Laforteza "Carla Guevara Laforteza"), began singing at age three, and in 2022 appeared on the [game show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_show "Game show") *[Family Feud Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Feud_(Philippine_game_show) "Family Feud (Philippine game show)")* as a contestant. Atlanta-born [Daniela Avanzini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Avanzini "Daniela Avanzini") began competitive [dancesport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancesport "Dancesport") at age three, and appeared on [*America's Got Talent* season 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America%27s_Got_Talent_season_8 "America's Got Talent season 8") in 2013, and was a top 10 finalist on *[So You Think You Can Dance: The Next Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/So_You_Think_You_Can_Dance:_The_Next_Generation_(American_TV_series) "So You Think You Can Dance: The Next Generation (American TV series)")* in 2016. [Megan Skiendiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Skiendiel "Megan Skiendiel") was also trained in professional dance from a young age. [Lara Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Raj "Lara Raj") began acting and singing at an early age, featuring in a campaign video for [Michelle Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Obama "Michelle Obama")'s Global Girls Alliance, an initiative for the [Obama Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obama_Foundation "Obama Foundation") in 2018. [Manon Bannerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manon_Bannerman "Manon Bannerman") gained popularity through short-form video content on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok") and [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram"), where she posted fashion and lifestyle clips beginning in 2021. She also pursued modeling work in Switzerland. Yoonchae Jeung, the group's youngest member and a native of South Korea, became a [K-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-pop "K-pop") trainee in 2020 before joining the lineup of *Dream Academy*.

Formal preparations for the group began in November 2021, when Hybe and Geffen Records launched a worldwide audition process for a girl group with "contestants from around the world", as part of the joint venture Hybe x Geffen Records. With auditions in South Korea, the United States, Japan, and the United Kingdom in 2022, the partnership aimed to create a group that would "transcend national, cultural, and artistic boundaries". In an interview with the *[Korea JoongAng Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea_JoongAng_Daily "Korea JoongAng Daily")*, Hybe founder [Bang Si-hyuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Si-hyuk "Bang Si-hyuk") expressed his intention to promote the resultant group to the US market. From more than 120,000 applicants, 20 were selected to participate in the reality competition series *[The Debut: Dream Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Debut:_Dream_Academy "The Debut: Dream Academy")*, which determined the final lineup through a series of performance-based missions. Before the series premiere, the contestants were trained for a year in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles") using methods from the Korean idol trainee system. The competition concluded on November 18, 2023, with Sophia Laforteza, Lara Raj, Yoonchae Jeung, Megan Skiendiel, Daniela Avanzini, and Manon Bannerman chosen as the members of Katseye.

Katseye's formation and preparation for their debut EP were serialized in the documentary *[Pop Star Academy: Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Star_Academy:_Katseye "Pop Star Academy: Katseye")*, which premiered on August 21, 2024. Distributed by Netflix, the documentary was directed by [Nadia Hallgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadia_Hallgren "Nadia Hallgren").

### 2024–2025: *SIS (Soft Is Strong)* and *Beautiful Chaos*

In June 2024, Hybe confirmed that the release of the group's first EP was set for August that year. For the EP, Katseye collaborated with several songwriters and producers, including [Ryan Tedder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Tedder "Ryan Tedder") and [Omer Fedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omer_Fedi "Omer Fedi"). Two singles preceded its release. The first, "[Debut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debut_(song) "Debut (song)")", was released on June 28, with a video filmed in [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn "Medellín"), Colombia, directed by Gregory Ohrel. The single is an upbeat pop track that explores themes of confidence and sisterhood in reference to the members' bond as a group. The second single, "[Touch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch_(Katseye_song) "Touch (Katseye song)")", was released on July 26. The [melodic drum and bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_funk "Liquid funk") [R&B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_R%26B "Contemporary R&B") track discusses the "complexity of love" and deviates from the sound of "Debut", emphasizing the members' vocals. The single ranked 22nd on the *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")* [Bubbling Under Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubbling_Under_Hot_100 "Bubbling Under Hot 100") chart and entered the *[Billboard Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Philippines "Billboard Philippines")* Hot 100 chart. "Touch" attained further success after its choreography went viral as a dance challenge on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok") in September 2024.

The full EP, *[SIS (Soft Is Strong)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIS_(Soft_Is_Strong) "SIS (Soft Is Strong)")*, was released on August 16. With five tracks, the EP debuted at number 119 on the *[Billboard 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200")* as Katseye landed atop the [Emerging Artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_Artists "Emerging Artists") chart. In support of the EP, the group embarked on an Asian tour, which began in South Korea on September 12. Megan stopped touring on September 21, owing to a back injury. On November 14, Katseye released "Flame", the theme song for the Netflix animated series *[Jentry Chau vs. the Underworld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jentry_Chau_vs._the_Underworld "Jentry Chau vs. the Underworld")*.

On March 7, 2025, before any new music was announced, Katseye was added to the lineup of [Wango Tango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wango_Tango "Wango Tango"), scheduled for May 10. They were then added to the lineup of [Lollapalooza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza "Lollapalooza") in Chicago, scheduled for August 3. Katseye released a new single, "[Gnarly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnarly_(song) "Gnarly (song)")", on April 30, which became their first entry on the *Billboard* [Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_100 "Hot 100"). The song was released as the first single from their second EP, *[Beautiful Chaos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_Chaos_(EP) "Beautiful Chaos (EP)")*. On June 6, Katseye released a remix of "Gnarly" featuring rapper [Ice Spice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_Spice "Ice Spice"). On June 20, they released "[Gabriela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_(song) "Gabriela (song)")", the EP's second single. The track was accompanied by a music video styled as a [telenovela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telenovela "Telenovela") and featured a cameo from actress [Jessica Alba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Alba "Jessica Alba"). Co-written by [Charli XCX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charli_XCX "Charli XCX"), "Gabriela" was described as a contemporary twist on Dolly Parton's "[Jolene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jolene "Jolene")".

On June 27, *Beautiful Chaos* was released, alongside a music video for the EP's third single, "[Gameboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gameboy_(song) "Gameboy (song)")". The EP debuted at number four on the *Billboard 200*, their first entry in the top ten of the chart. On November 7, Katseye earned nominations for [Best New Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_New_Artist "Grammy Award for Best New Artist") and [Best Pop/Duo Group Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Duo/Group_Performance "Grammy Award for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance") (for "Gabriela") at the [68th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/68th_Annual_Grammy_Awards "68th Annual Grammy Awards"). That same month, they embarked on [the Beautiful Chaos Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beautiful_Chaos_Tour "The Beautiful Chaos Tour"), their first concert tour. On the tour, they debuted a new song titled "[Internet Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Girl_(song) "Internet Girl (song)")", which was officially released on January 2, 2026. The single received polarized reactions, with some critics praising its "campy" and satirical approach to internet culture, while others criticized its heavy use of stylized production and lyrics. Commentary surrounding the track highlighted concerns that the group's songs prioritize shock value and [viral marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_marketing "Viral marketing") over artistic showcase or cohesion, with some questioning whether their music reflects artistic intent or label-driven decisions.

### 2026–present: Manon's and Sophia's hiatuses and *Wild*

On February 20, 2026, it was announced that Manon would take a hiatus from the group to focus on "personal health and wellbeing". The statement resulted in significant media coverage and public response, with outlets framing the hiatus within broader discussions about [work–life balance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Work%E2%80%93life_balance "Work–life balance") and health in high-intensity performance careers. Some online commentators noted that Bannerman, the group's only Black member, engaged with content highlighting mistreatment of Black women in the industry. In interviews, Katseye's members expressed their support for Bannerman during her hiatus.

On March 13, Katseye began a string of South American festival appearances with a performance at [Lollapalooza Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza_Argentina "Lollapalooza Argentina"), with approximately 100,000 in audience. The group performed at [Lollapalooza Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza_Chile "Lollapalooza Chile") the next day, and [Festival Estéreo Picnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festival_Est%C3%A9reo_Picnic "Festival Estéreo Picnic") in Colombia and [Lollapalooza Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lollapalooza_Brazil "Lollapalooza Brazil") later in the month. To coincide with a performance at [Coachella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella "Coachella"), Katseye released the single "[Pinky Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinky_Up "Pinky Up")" on April 9. Before their performance, the festival showcased a video of the active members announcing their new EP, *[Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_(Katseye_EP) "Wild (Katseye EP)")*, which released on August 14. On May 13, the group announced their second tour, [The Wildworld Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wildworld_Tour "The Wildworld Tour"), in support of the EP, which began on September 1 at the [3Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3Arena_(Dublin) "3Arena (Dublin)") in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin "Dublin"), Ireland, and will span 31 shows across 27 cities. On May 25, Katseye won all three awards they were nominated for at the [American Music Awards of 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Music_Awards_of_2026 "American Music Awards of 2026"), and performed "Pinky Up" at the ceremony. On June 12, Katseye released "[Iconic by Mistake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iconic_by_Mistake "Iconic by Mistake")" in collaboration with Hybe label mates [Le Sserafim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Sserafim "Le Sserafim") and [Illit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illit "Illit"), the groups' first collaboration.

On August 7, it was announced that Sophia was also going on a hiatus for "personal health and wellness". She wrote, "making this decision wasn't easy, but I'm learning that health has to come first." She is set to resume group activites in January 2027, missing the entirety of the Wildworld Tour. Katseye was initially announced as part of the lineup for [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo "Olivia Rodrigo")'s [Daisy Chain Fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Chain_Fields "Daisy Chain Fields") music festival on August 29, though canceled their set two days before the festival after Megan sprained her ankle. They later made a surprise appearance before [Devon Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon_Again "Devon Again")'s set.

On August 12, Hybe and Geffen, with the collaboration of [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records "Interscope Records") and [Boardwalk Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boardwalk_Pictures "Boardwalk Pictures"), released a documentary film, *[Katseye: Wild Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katseye:_Wild_Hearts "Katseye: Wild Hearts")*. The film was made available for streaming on [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") in the United States and [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%2B "Disney+") internationally on October 16. The next month they made their *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* debut as musical guests, with [Jalen Brunson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalen_Brunson "Jalen Brunson") hosting the season premiere. On October 13, they released "Unloveu" as a single, which was originally exclusive to the [Target](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Target_Corporation "Target Corporation") edition of *Wild*. They are set to headline the [Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria%27s_Secret_Fashion_Show_2026 "Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026"), scheduled for October 18, 2026.

Subsequently, Katseye were featured in Coach's collaboration campaign with the South Korean streetwear brand Matin Kim, which launched a line of [ready-to-wear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready-to-wear "Ready-to-wear") clothing for Japan, South Korea, and Hong Kong.

In April 2025, Katseye was featured in [Fendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fendi "Fendi")'s Fendi For Yourself campaign, launched in celebration of the brand's 100th anniversary. In May, Katseye partnered with [Glossier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossier "Glossier") to launch the brand's first lip oil product line titled 'Lip Glaze', with new products themed after the group's members. Additionally, the group released an edition of their EP *Beautiful Chaos* for pre-order through Urban Outfitters. In June 2025, Katseye partnered with jewelry brand [Pandora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandora_(jewelry) "Pandora (jewelry)") to release various charms selected by the members. In August, they launched a merchandise collaboration with Philippine fast food chain [Jollibee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jollibee "Jollibee"), which also released menu items themed after the group in its stores in the United States and Canada.

In August 2025, Katseye starred in [Gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gap_Inc. "Gap Inc.")'s Fall 2025 "[Better in Denim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_in_Denim "Better in Denim")" campaign, dancing to a re-recorded version of [Kelis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelis "Kelis")' "[Milkshake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milkshake_(song) "Milkshake (song)")". Gap CEO Richard Dickson described the campaign as "a huge success" for the brand. In November 2025, Katseye recorded public service announcements for [MTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Transportation_Authority "Metropolitan Transportation Authority") [New York City Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Transit_Authority "New York City Transit Authority")'s [subway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Subway "New York City Subway") system. In January 2026, Katseye partnered with [Laneige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laneige "Laneige") to promote their JuicePop Box lip tints. In February 2026, Katseye appeared on [State Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Farm "State Farm")'s [Super Bowl commercial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_commercial "Super Bowl commercial") for [Super Bowl LX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LX "Super Bowl LX"). At the same time, they were named Global Ambassadors for [L'Oréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Or%C3%A9al "L'Oréal")'s Matrix Essentials, promoting their new "Matrix Moves" campaign.

In October 2026, Katseye teamed up with [The Walt Disney Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company "The Walt Disney Company") and [Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_Networks#The_Complex_Shop "Complex Networks") to release [Mickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Mouse "Mickey Mouse") and [Minnie Mouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnie_Mouse "Minnie Mouse")-inspired merchandise ahead of the *Katseye: Wild Hearts* release on Disney+ and Hulu on October 16.

## Public image

Katseye is widely labeled as a "global girl group", blending stylistic traits of Western pop with the presentation attributed to K-pop. As Daniela described them in an interview with *[i-D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I-D "I-D")*, they are "the first American girl group to make American pop music but trained to do the crazy choreography of K‑pop." The group includes the first Indian, Filipina, Latina, and Black artists to be signed under Hybe: Lara, Sophia, Daniela, and Manon, respectively. Before the group's debut, *[Teen Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Vogue "Teen Vogue")* named Katseye one of 12 "Girl Groups to Watch in 2024". Musically, Katseye has cited [the Pussycat Dolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pussycat_Dolls "The Pussycat Dolls"), [Spice Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice_Girls "Spice Girls"), and [Le Sserafim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Sserafim "Le Sserafim") as influences.

Katseye is noted for their distinctive fashion styling, often cited as a defining feature of the group alongside their music. With [low-rise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-rise_(fashion) "Low-rise (fashion)") jeans, crop tops, and archival pieces, the group draws sartorial influence from late 1990s and [early 2000s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000s_in_fashion "2000s in fashion") pop culture, often referred to as Y2K fashion. Katseye's long-term creative director, [Humberto Leon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humberto_Leon "Humberto Leon"), states drawing inspiration from art, film, and history rather than solely musical influences for the group. In particular, the style of celebrities prominent in the early 2000s, such as [Devon Aoki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon_Aoki "Devon Aoki") for Megan, and [Shakira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakira "Shakira") for Daniela, served as key references for the image and appearance of the group's members. "Without [tokenizing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokenism "Tokenism") them", Leon cites the appreciation for the group's diverse backgrounds as a forerunner for their image, often incorporating items such as the [bindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bindi "Bindi") for Lara and [waist beads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waist_beads "Waist beads") for Manon. Members have highlighted the group's identity and openness to these influences, with Manon stating, "I have [locs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreadlocks "Dreadlocks") sometimes. Humberto is always like, 'Yeah, let's do it.' He's always asking if I have new ideas".

In December 2025, Katseye placed second in Google's U.S. "Trending Musicians" ranking, according to their annual [Year in Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Year_in_Search_top_searches "List of Year in Search top searches") review. Additionally, TikTok named Katseye the "Global Artist of the Year", citing over 30 billion views for the group on the platform.

## Members
