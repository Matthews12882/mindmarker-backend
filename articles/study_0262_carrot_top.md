# Carrot Top

**Scott Christopher Thompson** (born February 25, 1965), known professionally as **Carrot Top**, is an American [stand-up comedian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand-up_comedy "Stand-up comedy") and actor. He is known for his use of [prop comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prop_comedy "Prop comedy").

## Early life, family and education

Scott Christopher Thompson was born in [Rockledge, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockledge,_Florida "Rockledge, Florida"), and was raised in [Cocoa, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa,_Florida "Cocoa, Florida"). He has an older brother, and their father was a [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA "NASA") engineer. Thompson and his brother were raised [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic "Catholic") and attended St. Mary’s Catholic School as children. As a student at [Cocoa High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_High_School "Cocoa High School"), Scott played drums in the marching and concert band, and he graduated in 1983.

A local swimming coach gave him the nickname "Carrot Top", a reference to his [red hair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_hair "Red hair"), which would become his trademark in later life.

After high school he enrolled at [Florida Atlantic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Atlantic_University "Florida Atlantic University") in [Boca Raton, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Raton,_Florida "Boca Raton, Florida"). While a college freshman, Thompson performed his first standup comedy routine.

## Career

In the late 1980s, Thompson worked as a courier for a mortgage company.

His [big break](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/big_break "wikt:big break") was in 1991 when he performed on *[Comic Strip Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Strip_Live "Comic Strip Live")*. Around this time, he also performed on the competition television show *[Star Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Search "Star Search")*. He has appeared in *[Larry the Cable Guy's Christmas Spectacular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_the_Cable_Guy "Larry the Cable Guy")*, *[Gene Simmons Family Jewels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Simmons_Family_Jewels "Gene Simmons Family Jewels")*, *[Space Ghost Coast to Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Ghost_Coast_to_Coast "Space Ghost Coast to Coast")*, *[Criss Angel Mindfreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criss_Angel_Mindfreak "Criss Angel Mindfreak")*, *[Scrubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(2001_TV_series) "Scrubs (2001 TV series)")* (2001), *[George Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lopez_(TV_series) "George Lopez (TV series)")*, and *[Tugger: The Jeep 4x4 Who Wanted to Fly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugger:_The_Jeep_4x4_Who_Wanted_to_Fly "Tugger: The Jeep 4x4 Who Wanted to Fly")* (2005). His film roles include *[Chairman of the Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Board_(film) "Chairman of the Board (film)")*, and he also served as a spokesman in commercials for [1-800-CALL-ATT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1-800-COLLECT#Competition "1-800-COLLECT"). In 2002, he recorded a commentary track for *[The Rules of Attraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rules_of_Attraction_(film) "The Rules of Attraction (film)").* In 2006, Carrot Top appeared in the *[Reno 911!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reno_911! "Reno 911!")* episode "Weigel's Pregnant" as an enraged version of himself who trashes his hotel room and steals a police car. In 2008, he was a guest judge for *[Last Comic Standing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Comic_Standing "Last Comic Standing")*.

From 1995 to 1999, Carrot Top was the [continuity announcer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_announcer "Continuity announcer") for [Cartoon Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartoon_Network "Cartoon Network"), for which he also produced and starred in an early morning show, *Carrot Top's AM Mayhem*, from 1994 to 1996.

### Live comedy

Since 2005, Carrot Top has headlined at [MGM Resorts International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGM_Resorts_International "MGM Resorts International") properties in Las Vegas, Nevada. He has had a residency comedy show at the [Luxor Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxor_Las_Vegas "Luxor Las Vegas") in Las Vegas since November 22, 2005, and he performs various comedy engagements when his show is not playing. His comedy routine typically incorporates dozens of props stored in large trunks on stage and his prop jokes commonly consist of his pulling out a prop, describing it in a one-liner, and tossing it away. He typically closes his show with a lip-synced musical montage.

### Television appearances

On January 16, 2010, Carrot Top appeared on *[Don't Forget the Lyrics!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Forget_the_Lyrics!_(American_game_show) "Don't Forget the Lyrics! (American game show)")*, where he assisted illusionists [Penn & Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_%26_Teller "Penn & Teller") in their quest for the million-dollar grand prize. He was one of the [roasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roast_(comedy) "Roast (comedy)") at the *Comedy Central Roast of [Flavor Flav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavor_Flav "Flavor Flav")* and *[Gene Simmons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Simmons "Gene Simmons") Roast*. He appeared on a second-season episode of the television series *Criss Angel Mindfreak* and continued to be a regular guest. Carrot Top also appeared in an episode of the reality series *[The Bad Girls Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bad_Girls_Club "The Bad Girls Club")*. He appeared as himself in episodes of *[CSI: Crime Scene Investigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation "CSI: Crime Scene Investigation")* ("Man Up"), *[Family Guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Guy "Family Guy")* ("[Petergeist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petergeist "Petergeist")"), *Scrubs* ("My Balancing Act"), and *[Robot Chicken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robot_Chicken "Robot Chicken")* ("[The Unnamed One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robot_Chicken_(season_8) "Robot Chicken (season 8)")").

Carrot Top appeared briefly in both *[Bad Girls Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Girls_Club "Bad Girls Club")*'s [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Girls_Club_(season_3) "Bad Girls Club (season 3)") (2008–09) and *[Holly's World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holly%27s_World "Holly's World")* (2010–11). He appeared alongside country artist [Toby Keith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Keith "Toby Keith") in the music video for "[Red Solo Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Solo_Cup "Red Solo Cup")" and also appeared alongside Keith as he performed the song in the audience as a member of Keith's entourage at the 2011 [ACM Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Country_Music_Awards "Academy of Country Music Awards").

Carrot Top has appeared on numerous nighttime television [talk shows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk_show "Talk show"). In 1996, he appeared on the animated/live action Cartoon Network talk show *[Space Ghost Coast to Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Ghost_Coast_to_Coast "Space Ghost Coast to Coast")*. On February 8, 2010, he appeared on an episode of *[The Jay Leno Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jay_Leno_Show "The Jay Leno Show")*, wherein he had pies thrown at him by Leno and guest [Emma Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Roberts "Emma Roberts"). He appeared on *[The Late Late Show with Craig Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Late_Late_Show_with_Craig_Ferguson "The Late Late Show with Craig Ferguson")* alongside [Courteney Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courteney_Cox "Courteney Cox") on May 24, 2011.

Carrot Top appeared on an episode of *[Tosh.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosh.0 "Tosh.0")*, receiving a full-body massage from [Daniel Tosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Tosh "Daniel Tosh"). He was also a special guest performer in Tosh.0's [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez "Selena Gomez") tribute video of "[Good for You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_for_You_(Selena_Gomez_song) "Good for You (Selena Gomez song)")".

On October 22, 2011, Carrot Top appeared as a first-time guest panelist on *[Red Eye w/Greg Gutfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Eye_w/Greg_Gutfeld "Red Eye w/Greg Gutfeld")*. On April 13, 2016, he appeared as a panelist on the *[@midnight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/@midnight "@midnight")* [game show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panel_game_show "Panel game show").

### Internet series roles

Carrot Top appeared as himself on the [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper "Bradley Cooper") episode of *[Between Two Ferns with Zach Galifianakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Between_Two_Ferns_with_Zach_Galifianakis "Between Two Ferns with Zach Galifianakis")*. He also appeared as himself on the show *[Tom Green's House Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Green%27s_House_Tonight "Tom Green's House Tonight")* to discuss his props and career. In 2022, he appeared on *[The Joe Rogan Experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Joe_Rogan_Experience "The Joe Rogan Experience")* on Spotify episode #1758. On October 26, 2023, he appeared as himself in the ‘Guess How Much This Weighs (Challenge)’ episode of YouTube variety show *[Good Mythical Morning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Mythical_Morning "Good Mythical Morning")* with [Rhett and Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhett_%26_Link "Rhett & Link"). On May 26, 2025, he appeared on *[Kill Tony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Tony "Kill Tony")*, and again on September 15.

## Personal life

Carrot Top had a long-term relationship in the early 2000s with Amanda Hogan, according to the *[Detroit Free Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Free_Press "Detroit Free Press")*.

In August 2026, singer Brian Evans filed a $15 million lawsuit against Carrot Top, which claims the comedian sent an unsolicited sexually explicit video and subsequently tried to compel Evans into a legal settlement. Later, Zachary Defazio filed a supporting statement, and alleged Carrot Top [catfished](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catfishing "Catfishing") him on the [Grindr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grindr "Grindr") app by pretending to be younger than his actual age when making sexual advances, and also supplied alcohol when Defazio was 19 years old, under the legal drinking age.

On September 18, 2026, Carrot Top's scheduled appearances were canceled after he attempted suicide.
