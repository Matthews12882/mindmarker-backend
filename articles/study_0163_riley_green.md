# Riley Green

**Johnathan Riley Green** (born October 18, 1988) is an American [country music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music") singer-songwriter. He has been with [Nashville Harbor Records & Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Harbor_Records_%26_Entertainment "Nashville Harbor Records & Entertainment") formerly BMLG (Big Machine Label Group) since 2018 and has released four albums: *[Different 'Round Here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Different_%27Round_Here "Different 'Round Here")* in 2019, *[Ain't My Last Rodeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain%27t_My_Last_Rodeo "Ain't My Last Rodeo")* in 2023, *[Don't Mind If I Do](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Mind_If_I_Do_(Riley_Green_album) "Don't Mind If I Do (Riley Green album)")* in 2024, and *[That's Just Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/That%27s_Just_Me "That's Just Me")* in 2026. His debut and third albums have been certified Gold by the [Recording Industry Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America "Recording Industry Association of America"). During his career, he has had multiple Top 20 hits on the *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_(magazine) "Billboard (magazine)")* [Hot Country Songs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Country_Songs "Hot Country Songs") and [Country Airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Airplay "Country Airplay") charts.

## Background

Johnathan Riley Green was born on October 18, 1988 in [Jacksonville, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville,_Alabama "Jacksonville, Alabama"). He learned to play music from his grandfather, Buford Green, who ran a venue (The Golden Saw). Riley performed weekly at the music hall, a converted [sawmill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawmill "Sawmill"), at the time. While at [Jacksonville State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville_State_University "Jacksonville State University") in Jacksonville, he was a quarterback for the Gamecocks [NCAA Division I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I "NCAA Division I") football team. He was also a contestant on the [CMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CMT_(American_TV_channel) "CMT (American TV channel)") reality show *Redneck Island*.

## Career

### 2013–2018: Beginning of his career

Riley Green released a self-titled EP in 2013, followed by EPs in 2015, 2016, and 2017. In 2018, he signed to the [Big Machine Label Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Machine_Records "Big Machine Records"). His debut single, "[There Was This Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_Was_This_Girl "There Was This Girl")", was released on August 6, 2018. Billy Dukes of *[Taste of Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taste_of_Country "Taste of Country")* reviewed "There Was This Girl" favorably, comparing its theme to "[Chicks Dig It](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicks_Dig_It "Chicks Dig It")" by [Chris Cagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Cagle "Chris Cagle") and noting a more [neotraditional country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neotraditional_country "Neotraditional country") influence in the music compared to Green's peers. The song achieved commercial success and was on several country music charts including No. 1 on [Country Airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Airplay "Country Airplay") in April 2019.

### 2019–2023: *Different 'Round Here* and *Ain't My Last Rodeo*

In April 2019, Green announced the release of his second single, "In Love by Now", co-written with [Rhett Akins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhett_Akins "Rhett Akins"), [Ben Hayslip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Hayslip "Ben Hayslip"), and [Marv Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marv_Green "Marv Green"); Riley released an accompanying music video shot in [Belize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belize "Belize"). "[I Wish Grandpas Never Died](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Wish_Grandpas_Never_Died "I Wish Grandpas Never Died")" was released in August 2019 as the third single from Riley Green's debut album, *[Different 'Round Here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Different_%27Round_Here "Different 'Round Here")*, which came out on September 20, 2019. The [Academy of Country Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Country_Music "Academy of Country Music") presented Green with the New Male Artist of the Year Award for 2019. In 2022 and 2023, he served as direct support (opening act) on major stadium tours for [Luke Combs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Combs "Luke Combs") and [Morgan Wallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Wallen "Morgan Wallen"). He collaborated on a song with [Justin Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Moore "Justin Moore") for Moore's 2023 album *[Stray Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stray_Dog_(Justin_Moore_album) "Stray Dog (Justin Moore album)")*. On October 13, 2023, Green released his second studio album, *[Ain't My Last Rodeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain%27t_My_Last_Rodeo "Ain't My Last Rodeo")*. The album featured a re-recorded version of "[Different 'Round Here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Different_%27Round_Here_(song) "Different 'Round Here (song)")" as a duet with Combs.

### 2024–present: *Don't Mind If I Do*, *That's Just Me*, and television roles

Green released his third studio album, *[Don't Mind If I Do](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Mind_If_I_Do_(Riley_Green_album) "Don't Mind If I Do (Riley Green album)")*, on October 18, 2024. The album's [title track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Mind_If_I_Do_(song) "Don't Mind If I Do (song)") is a duet with [Ella Langley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Langley "Ella Langley"). In April 2026, it was reported that he would portray Garrett Garvey, Cal's and Kayce's former Navy SEAL teammate in a 4-episode arc on [*Marshals*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshals_(TV_series) "Marshals (TV series)"), making his acting debut on April 19, 2026. Green's *Cowboy As It Gets Tour* began in April 2026 and concluded in August 2026. In May 2026, he was nominated for Best Male Country Artist at the [2026 American Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Music_Awards_of_2026 "American Music Awards of 2026"). His fourth studio album, *[That's Just Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/That%27s_Just_Me "That's Just Me")*, was released on September 18, 2026. Two of the singles from the album, "[My Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Way_(Riley_Green_song) "My Way (Riley Green song)")" and "[Think as You Drunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Think_as_You_Drunk "Think as You Drunk")", were released in April and May of 2026, respectively. In late 2026, he debuted as a coach on the [30th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(American_TV_series)_season_30 "The Voice (American TV series) season 30") of the reality television series *[The Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_(American_TV_series) "The Voice (American TV series)")*.

## Personal life

Green lives near his hometown of Jacksonville, Alabama and his hobbies include hunting and fishing. He is noted for having a passion for [duck hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfowl_hunting "Waterfowl hunting") and was branded "Riley Duckman" by friends in Alabama. Green has since embraced the nickname in multiple aspects of his career.

## Controversies

In late 2019, Green drew controversy for a line in his song "Bury Me in Dixie" which referenced [Confederate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_Army "Confederate States Army") general [Robert E. Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_E._Lee "Robert E. Lee"). Green removed the song from streaming services in October 2019. He denied any racial motive for referencing Lee, saying the lyric was an expression of "pride and heritage in what I grew up around". He said that avoiding controversy was not the motive for removing the song from streaming services, citing concerns about poor audio quality instead. The song returned to streaming services a few months after the controversy.
