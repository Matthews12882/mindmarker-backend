# The Early Spring

***The Early Spring*** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters "Simplified Chinese characters"): 早春晴朗; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin "Pinyin"): *Zǎo Chūn Qíng Lǎng*) is a 2026 [Chinese television series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_television_series "Chinese television series") starring [Jing Boran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jing_Boran "Jing Boran") and [Sun Qian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Qian_(actress) "Sun Qian (actress)"). It is adapted from the web novel of the same name by Gu Niang Bie Ku, directed by Jiang Ji Zheng and written by Gao Xiao Xian. The story is about a young professional navigating a competitive advertising agency while entangled in a complex romance with her demanding superior. On 26 August 2026, it was released on [Youku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youku "Youku") and [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix").

The series exceeded 10,000 'Popularity Heat Index' points on [Youku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youku "Youku") throughout its broadcast period, and achieved a historic first for Chinese series by entering the 'Top 3' on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"): ranking 4th in 'Top TV Shows Worldwide' and 2nd in the 'Global Non-English TV List'. It was listed as S+ Tier i.e. 'Top Performing Shows' on Yunhe Chart. Upon its release, Jing Boran and Sun Qian were ranked as 'Top C-drama Artists in the International Market', while their portrayals topped the 'Character Index List'.

## Synopsis

Shang Zhitao ([Sun Qian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Qian_(actress) "Sun Qian (actress)")), a determined young woman from a modest background, unexpectedly lands a job at a top advertising agency. There she encounters an aloof and demanding industry veteran Luan Nian ([Jing Boran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jing_Boran "Jing Boran")). Navigating intense office politics and a complex romance with Luan Nian, she gradually transforms from a naive newcomer into a seasoned professional.

## Production

On 10 September 2024, the project was first announced for [Youku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youku "Youku") during autumn press conference, and the official poster was released. In March 2025, it was listed in the "Network Audiovisual Program Excellence Creation and Dissemination Project" organized by the [National Radio and Television Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Radio_and_Television_Administration "National Radio and Television Administration") (NRTA).

On 5 September 2025, [Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") officially began in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing "Beijing"). The popularity of the novel sparked heated discussions regarding its adaptation tone. In November 2025, early production took placed in Beijing and [Harbin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbin "Harbin"), the locations included historic landmarks like [Central Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Street_(Harbin) "Central Street (Harbin)"), [Saint Sophia Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Sophia_Cathedral,_Harbin "Saint Sophia Cathedral, Harbin"), and the East Hechang 1917 cultural complex. On 26 November 2025, the filming wrapped at [Jinlong Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinping_Mountains "Jinping Mountains").

[Tsai Chia-yin](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%94%A1%E5%98%89%E8%8C%B5 "zh:蔡嘉茵") was hired as an intimacy coordinator to establish boundaries and direct movement for sensitive scenes, representing the first formal use of intimacy coaching in a Chinese television series.

## Reception

*[Hindustan Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Times "Hindustan Times")* rated 3.5/5 and stated that "*The Early Spring* refuses to hand viewers a sugar-coated fairy tale, and that refusal is its greatest triumph. It is a messy, emotionally intense, and highly addictive exploration of flawed people trying to navigate love and ambition. However, if you crave a mature, fast-paced romance with sizzling chemistry, complex character growth, and a beautiful cinematic style, this drama is an absolute must-watch." *[WION](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WION "WION")* stated that "*The Early Spring* is presented as a workplace romance and drama, with considerable emphasis on Sun Qian's characters' professional growth rather than simply their romantic relationship."

*[Daily Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Tribune_(Philippines) "Daily Tribune (Philippines)")* states that "The drama has quickly become one of the titles viewers are talking about, thanks to its intense workplace romance, charged boss-and-rookie dynamic, and sizzling chemistry between Jing Boran and Sun Qian." *[The Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hindu "The Hindu")* states that "Moving away from the vanilla, saccharine sweet tropes one has come to associate with romances in many East Asian shows, *The Early Spring* embraces the chaos and messiness of an urban workplace relationship; one that must contend with pride, ego and, most importantly, a power imbalance." As per *Papo de Dorama* review "*The Early Spring* does not reinvent the office romance, but it finds its own personality in the way Jing Boran and Sun Qian play two people who spend far too long avoiding saying plainly what they feel."
