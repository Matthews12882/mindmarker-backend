# Sex

**Sex** most commonly refers to:

* [Biological sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_sex "Biological sex"), the trait of certain reproducing organisms producing either male or female gametes
  + [Sexual reproduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_reproduction "Sexual reproduction"), reproduction that combines multiple gametes
* [Human sexual activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sexual_activity "Human sexual activity"), a broad range of reproductive or erotic human practices
  + [Human sexuality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sexuality "Human sexuality"), the way people express themselves in terms of, or experience, the above activities
  + [Sexual intercourse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_intercourse "Sexual intercourse"), the activity of using reproductive organs penetratively
* [Animal sexual behaviour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_sexual_behaviour "Animal sexual behaviour"), the reproductive or erotic activities of non-human animals
  + [Copulation (zoology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copulation_(zoology) "Copulation (zoology)"), in which a male introduces sperm to a female's body
* [Legal sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_sex "Legal sex") or legal gender, a legal status
