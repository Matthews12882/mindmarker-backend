# Reacher season 4

The fourth season of the American [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_television "Action television") [crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_fiction "Crime fiction") television series *[Reacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reacher_(TV_series) "Reacher (TV series)")*, based on the [*Jack Reacher* book series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Reacher_(book_series) "Jack Reacher (book series)") by [Lee Child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Child "Lee Child"), premiered on [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") on August 12, 2026.

The season, based on *[Gone Tomorrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gone_Tomorrow "Gone Tomorrow")* (2009), stars [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson "Alan Ritchson"), [Sydelle Noel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydelle_Noel "Sydelle Noel"), [Agnez Mo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnez_Mo "Agnez Mo"), [Anggun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anggun "Anggun"), [Christopher Marquette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Marquette "Christopher Marquette"), and [Kevin Corrigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Corrigan "Kevin Corrigan"). The story follows Reacher, a former major in the [United States Army Military Police Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Police_Corps_(United_States) "Military Police Corps (United States)"), who has an encounter with a troubled woman named Anna Merrick on a train that takes a dangerous turn, and finds himself caught up in a complex and deadly conspiracy involving a missing USB drive, and enemies at the highest ranks of power.

In May 2026, the series was renewed for a fifth season.

## Production

### Development

During the [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reacher_season_3 "Reacher season 3") press tour, author [Lee Child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Child "Lee Child") announced the book the fourth season would be based on was decided and its script was done.

### Casting

On June 13, 2025, [Jay Baruchel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Baruchel "Jay Baruchel"), [Sydelle Noel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydelle_Noel "Sydelle Noel"), [Agnez Mo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnez_Mo "Agnez Mo"), [Anggun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anggun "Anggun"), and [Kevin Corrigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Corrigan "Kevin Corrigan") were cast as series regulars for the fourth season while [Kevin Weisman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Weisman "Kevin Weisman"), [Marc Blucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Blucas "Marc Blucas"), and [Kathleen Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Robertson "Kathleen Robertson") were cast in recurring roles. Later that month, Baruchel was replaced by [Christopher Marquette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Marquette "Christopher Marquette") after exiting the series due to a personal matter.

### Filming

Principal photography for the fourth season began on June 16, 2025, with former US President [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden "Joe Biden") and his son [Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Biden "Hunter Biden") visiting the set in [Rittenhouse Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rittenhouse_Square "Rittenhouse Square") in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia "Philadelphia"). Filming wrapped by November 18, 2025.

## Release

The season premiered on August 12, 2026, with its first three episodes. The remaining five episodes were released weekly until September 16.

## Reception

The fourth season has a 94% approval rating on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), based on 35 critic reviews, with an average rating of 7.6/10. The website's critics consensus states, "*Reacher* maintains its formula for maximum entertainment, serving more brawn, enough narrative brains, and always the right amount of Alan Ritchson to keep the operation up and running for seasons to come." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") assigned a score of 77 out of 100 based on 11 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".
