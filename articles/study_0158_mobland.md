# MobLand

***MobLand*** is a [crime drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_drama "Crime drama") television series created by [Ronan Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Bennett "Ronan Bennett") for [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+"). The series stars [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy "Tom Hardy") as Harry Da Souza, [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan "Pierce Brosnan") as Conrad Harrigan and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren "Helen Mirren") as Maeve Harrigan. Additional main characters include Kevin Harrigan ([Paddy Considine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_Considine "Paddy Considine")), Jan Da Souza ([Joanne Froggatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_Froggatt "Joanne Froggatt")), Isabella Harrigan ([Lara Pulver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Pulver "Lara Pulver")), Eddie Harrigan ([Anson Boon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anson_Boon "Anson Boon")), and Seraphina Harrigan ([Mandeep Dhillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeep_Dhillon "Mandeep Dhillon")). [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie"), Anthony Byrne, [Daniel Syrkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Syrkin "Daniel Syrkin"), and Lawrence Gough serve as directors. [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth "Jez Butterworth") and Bennett are credited with writing all ten episodes of the first season.

The series premiered on 30 March 2025, and it ranked second in viewership on Paramount+. In June 2025, the series was renewed for a second season, which premiered on 18 September 2026. In September 2026, the series was renewed for a third season.

## Premise

Harry Da Souza is a street-smart and formidable [fixer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixer_(person) "Fixer (person)") employed by the Harrigan family, a [North London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_London "North London") [crime family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_family "Crime family"), to clean up catastrophic messes and prevent an escalating gang war from tearing the reigning crime empire apart while shielding it from aggressive police scrutiny, enemy threats, and relentless internal betrayal.

The first season follows the explosive escalation of a bloody gang war between two dominant [crime syndicates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organized_crime "Organized crime"): the Harrigans and the Stevensons. As tensions between the families intensify, Harry is tasked with burying evidence, managing international criminal brokers, dealing with a high-level mole within the enterprise and preventing an all-out gang war

The second season follows the eruption of a bitter [conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intragroup_conflict "Intragroup conflict") as family members scheme to seize control of the North London empire. Caught in the crossfire, central fixer Harry must navigate a minefield of ruthless internal rivals, dangerous new cartels, and a corrupt detective hell-bent on dismantling the syndicate. To prevent the Harrigan dynasty from collapsing, he is forced to choose between family loyalty and his own survival.

## Production

### Development

The series was first announced in February 2024 as *The Donovans*, a loosely based [spinoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinoff_(media) "Spinoff (media)") of the [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network) "Showtime (TV network)") series *[Ray Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Donovan "Ray Donovan")* produced by [MTV Entertainment Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Entertainment_Studios "MTV Entertainment Studios") and 101 Studios, with Showtime/MTV executives Keith Cox and [Nina L. Diaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_L._Diaz "Nina L. Diaz") and 101 Studios' [David C. Glasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_C._Glasser "David C. Glasser") attached to the project. Ronan Bennett was hired to write the series and executive produce with Guy Ritchie, Ivan Atkinson, Glasser, Ron Burkle, [Bob Yari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Yari "Bob Yari") and David Hutkin.

In October 2024, the series was announced to have been reworked into a series with the [working title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_title "Working title") *The Associate*, with [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy "Tom Hardy"), [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan "Pierce Brosnan"), and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren "Helen Mirren") in final negotiations to star. Production began in November 2024 with Hardy, Brosnan and Mirren in London and the working title was removed. [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth "Jez Butterworth"), Tom Hardy, Dean Baker and Kris Thykier joined as executive producers. In February 2025, the final title of the series, *MobLand*, was revealed.

On 23 June 2025, the series was renewed for a second season. In May 2026, a third season was confirmed to be in development. Hardy was initially reported to have departed the series after completing his work on the second season due to "friction" between him and showrunner Jez Butterworth. Later reports confirmed that Hardy "was not fired" from the series and that there were discussions about his return for a potential third season. The following month, Hardy was confirmed to return for the third season, alongside Brosnan and Mirren. On 3 September 2026, the series was renewed for a third season.

In February 2025, [Geoff Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoff_Bell_(actor) "Geoff Bell (actor)"), [Daniel Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Betts "Daniel Betts"), [Lisa Dwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Dwan "Lisa Dwan"), and [Emily Barber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Barber "Emily Barber") joined the cast. In March 2025, [Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer "Janet McTeer") joined the cast.

In December 2025, [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn "Johnny Flynn") and [Ophelia Lovibond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophelia_Lovibond "Ophelia Lovibond") joined the cast as series regulars for the second season.

### Filming

Production on the series took place between November 2024 and March 2025 in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London"). At least six of the episodes in the series were written during production, and filming wrapped only four days before the premiere.

While filming, a bag of camera equipment was stolen from a set in East London. The thieves later returned and successfully stole more equipment, leading to the firing and replacement of the security team on set. During production, the set construction company, Helix 3D, went into [bankruptcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankruptcy "Bankruptcy"), leaving several crew members with unpaid wages leading into the holiday season. Hardy initially offered to make up the difference before the production companies paid the workers instead.

The first and second episodes feature songs by [the Prodigy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prodigy "The Prodigy"), "[Firestarter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firestarter_(The_Prodigy_song) "Firestarter (The Prodigy song)")" and "[Breathe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breathe_(The_Prodigy_song) "Breathe (The Prodigy song)")" respectively.

## Release

*MobLand* premiered on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount%2B "Paramount+") on 30 March 2025, with episodes released weekly through 1 June 2025. The premiere broke the streamer's record for its largest global series launch ever, with 2.2 million viewers on 30 March. *MobLand* went on to accumulate over 26 million views in its first season, ranking it second on Paramount+ during that time behind only *[Landman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landman_(TV_series) "Landman (TV series)")*. It also ranked in Nielsen's top 10 SVOD list, in the original series category, for six consecutive weeks.

The second season premiered on 18 September 2026.

## Reception

On the [review aggregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), the first season has an approval rating of 76% based on 45 critical reviews. The website's critical consensus reads, "Tom Hardy's gruff charisma is put to good use in *MobLand*, a gangster saga that hardly reinvents the genre but nails its conventions with crunchy style and memorable performances." On [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_average "Weighted average"), the first season received a score of 60 out of 100, based on 18 critics, indicating "mixed or average" reviews.

On Rotten Tomatoes, the second season has an approval rating of 90% based on 21 critical reviews. The website's critical consensus reads, "*MobLand* proves to be Guy Ritchie at his utmost sinister, funny, and intriguing best, featuring a truly game A-list cast that only elevates the proceedings in this charming second season." On Metacritic, the second season received a score of 73 out of 100, based on 12 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.
