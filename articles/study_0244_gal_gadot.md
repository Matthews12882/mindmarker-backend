# Gal Gadot

**Gal Gadot** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language "Hebrew language"): גל גדות, pronounced [[ˈɡal ɡaˈdot]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Hebrew "Help:IPA/Hebrew"); born 30 April 1985) is an Israeli actress. She gained recognition for portraying [Wonder Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_Prince_(DC_Extended_Universe) "Diana Prince (DC Extended Universe)") in the [DC Extended Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Extended_Universe "DC Extended Universe") films (2016–2023). In 2018, Gadot was named one of *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")*'s [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") and ranked by *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* as the tenth–highest-paid actress, later rising to third-highest in 2020. In 2025, she became the first Israeli actor to receive a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame").

Gadot grew up in [Rosh HaAyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosh_HaAyin "Rosh HaAyin") and first gained attention locally after winning [Miss Israel 2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Israel "Miss Israel"), followed by two years of [mandatory service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscription_in_Israel "Conscription in Israel") in the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces "Israel Defense Forces") (IDF) as a combat trainer. During this time, she participated in a *[Maxim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine) "Maxim (magazine)")* photo shoot that boosted her public profile, leading her to begin modeling and endorsing various brands. Shortly after, she made her television debut in the Israeli drama *Bubot* in 2008, followed by her film debut in a supporting role as [Gisele Yashar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gisele_Yashar "Gisele Yashar") in *[Fast & Furious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious_(2009_film) "Fast & Furious (2009 film)")* (2009), reprising it in multiple [sequels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious "Fast & Furious") (2011–2023). She also appeared on [Israeli television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_in_Israel "Television in Israel") in the second season of *[Asfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asfur "Asfur")* (2010–2012) and later in *[Kathmandu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_(TV_series) "Kathmandu (TV series)")* (2012).

Gadot first portrayed Wonder Woman in *[Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_v_Superman:_Dawn_of_Justice "Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice")* (2016). The success of the film led to her starring in *[Wonder Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman_(2017_film) "Wonder Woman (2017 film)")* (2017), which became a major [box office hit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_in_film#Highest-grossing_films "2017 in film"). She reprised the role again in *[Justice League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_League_(film) "Justice League (film)")* (2017) and *[Wonder Woman 1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman_1984 "Wonder Woman 1984")* (2020), the latter of which she co-produced. Since then, she has starred in *[Red Notice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Notice_(film) "Red Notice (film)")* (2021), *[Death on the Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_on_the_Nile_(2022_film) "Death on the Nile (2022 film)")* (2022), and *[Heart of Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_Stone_(2023_film) "Heart of Stone (2023 film)")* (2023), which she co-produced. In 2025 she portrayed the [Evil Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evil_Queen_(Disney) "Evil Queen (Disney)") in *[Snow White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_White_(2025_film) "Snow White (2025 film)")* and [Gemma Donati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Donati "Gemma Donati") in *[In the Hand of Dante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Hand_of_Dante_(film) "In the Hand of Dante (film)")*.

Beyond acting, Gadot and her husband have co-founded a film production company, launched a food brand, and invested in Israeli startups. Gadot is widely celebrated in Israel as a [national icon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_symbol "National symbol") and ambassador and has been honored by various Jewish and [Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionist "Zionist") organizations for her effect on Israel's image, including the [Israeli-American Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli-American_Council "Israeli-American Council") (IAC), [Anti-Defamation League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Defamation_League "Anti-Defamation League") (ADL), [Jerusalem Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Film_Festival "Jerusalem Film Festival"), and [Hadassah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadassah_(organization) "Hadassah (organization)"). She has also been awarded a [Genesis Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Prize "Genesis Prize"). Her support for the Israeli military and for [hostages held in the Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war_hostage_crisis "Gaza war hostage crisis") has drawn criticism from [Palestinian nationalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_nationalism "Palestinian nationalism"), as well as from some pro-[Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis "Israelis") nationalists who argue she has not shown strong support.

## Early life

Gadot was born on 30 April 1985 in [Petah Tikva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petah_Tikva "Petah Tikva"), where she initially lived at Stampfer street. She was born to Jewish parents of [Ashkenazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jews "Ashkenazi Jews") descent; her mother's roots are [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Jew "Czech Jew") and [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Poland "History of the Jews in Poland"), while her father's are [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Austria "History of the Jews in Austria"), [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Russia "History of the Jews in Russia"), and [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Germany "History of the Jews in Germany"). Her parents, Michael, an engineer, and Irit, a physical education teacher, [Hebraized their surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebraization_of_surnames "Hebraization of surnames") from "Greenstein" to "Gadot" before her birth. In Hebrew, her first name means "wave", and her Hebrew surname 'Gadot' translates to "riverbanks". She has a younger sister, Dana.

Her father was the sixth-generation of the [Land of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_of_Israel "Land of Israel")/[Palestine region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_(region) "Palestine (region)") and is an [Israeli-born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabra_(person) "Sabra (person)") citizen, and one of his ancestors was the first [chemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemist "Chemist") at [Shaare Zedek Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaare_Zedek_Medical_Center "Shaare Zedek Medical Center") in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem "Jerusalem"), while her mother is a first-generation Israeli-born citizen and the daughter of [Holocaust survivors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_survivors "Holocaust survivors"). Her maternal grandfather, Abraham Weiss, was born as Adolf Weiss, in the village of [Munkács](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukachevo "Mukachevo"), [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia "Czechoslovakia") in 1928. At thirteen, when [Nazi Germany invaded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Czechoslovakia_(1938%E2%80%931945) "Occupation of Czechoslovakia (1938–1945)"), his father (her great-grandfather) died in the army and Abraham, his mother, and his brother were sent to [Auschwitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp "Auschwitz concentration camp"), where the latter two perished in the [gas chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_chamber "Gas chamber"). Gadot's maternal grandmother, however, managed to escape Europe before [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II "World War II"). Abraham immigrated alone to [Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffa "Jaffa"), where he built a life. He later served in the [Golani Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golani_Brigade "Golani Brigade"); in his later years, Abraham joined [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces "Israel Defense Forces") (IDF) expeditions to give first-hand [testimony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence_and_documentation_for_the_Holocaust "Evidence and documentation for the Holocaust") about his experiences in Auschwitz.

In 1993, at age eight, Gadot and her family moved to the Givat Tal neighborhood of [Rosh HaAyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosh_HaAyin "Rosh HaAyin"), where she grew up, in what she described as a "very Jewish, Israeli family environment". As a child, she struggled with a weak voice and attended [speech therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech%E2%80%93language_pathology "Speech–language pathology"), where she learned proper [breathing techniques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscious_breathing "Conscious breathing"). Encouraged by her mother to be active from a young age, Gadot took up swimming at four and later played tennis. She joined the local volleyball and basketball teams, soccer-team cheerleader, and trained in ballet, hip-hop, and jazz for twelve years; at one point, she considered a career as a choreographer.

At eleven, Gadot visited [Holocaust memorial sites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Holocaust_memorials_and_museums "List of Holocaust memorials and museums") in Poland to learn about Auschwitz and [Majdanek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majdanek_concentration_camp "Majdanek concentration camp"). Reflecting on the experience, she recalled: "I, an entitled child, felt the suffering the [Muselmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muselmann "Muselmann") experienced back then." At twelve, she was invited to enter the [Girl of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=%D7%A0%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%91_%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A8&action=edit&redlink=1 "נערת השנה של מעריב לנוער (page does not exist)") [[he](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%A0%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%91_%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%A8 "he:נערת השנה של מעריב לנוער")] ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language "Hebrew language"): נערת השנה) competition but declined, initially dismissed modeling as a career, but later realized it is a fun job that makes great money and offers exciting experiences.

Although Gadot's native language is Hebrew, she began taking English lessons in [third grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Israel "Education in Israel"); she became fluent after starting to watch the sitcoms *[Seinfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinfeld "Seinfeld")* and *[Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends "Friends")*. She later attended Begin High School, where she majored in biology. After graduating, she had a few months before beginning her mandatory two-year service in the IDF. During this time, she received modeling offers but instead chose to work at [Burger King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burger_King "Burger King"), saying, "I was like, posing for money? Ugh, it's not for me." Meanwhile, her mother and a friend submitted an application on her behalf for the Miss Israel pageant. Before enlisting, she also worked as a [caregiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caregiver "Caregiver") for eight-month-old twins.

## Career

### 2004–2009: Miss Israel, IDF service and early work

In 2004, Gadot's army enlistment was postponed six months, so her mother encouraged her to enter a [beauty pageant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beauty_pageant "Beauty pageant"). Gadot saw it as a fun opportunity before beginning her service. To her surprise, she won the 54th [Miss Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Israel "Miss Israel") beauty pageant, which was held at the [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa "Haifa") Congress Center. She was crowned Miss Israel and received a [Citroën C3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citro%C3%ABn_C3 "Citroën C3") supermini car. Following her win, Gadot represented Israel in the [Miss Universe 2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2004 "Miss Universe 2004") pageant in Ecuador but admitted she intentionally "rebelled" to avoid winning, arriving late to events and refusing to wear an evening gown. During an interview with *[Ynet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ynet "Ynet")*, and before giving up her pageant title, Gadot highlighted the value of military service, viewing it as both a duty and a chance for personal growth. In 2005, a week after passing on the crown, Gadot was conscripted into the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces "Israel Defense Forces"). After excelling in a three-month boot camp, she went on to serve as a combat trainer, teaching soldiers gymnastics and [calisthenics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calisthenics "Calisthenics"). Reporting daily at 5 a.m., she led soldiers through a form of boot camp. She became so passionate about the techniques she learned that she eventually trained [sergeants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergeant "Sergeant") and high-ranking [commanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commanders_for_Israel%27s_Security "Commanders for Israel's Security"). Gadot also trained in [Krav Maga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krav_Maga "Krav Maga"), and gained proficiency in handling weapons and preparing for combat. She completed her [mandatory two-year service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_the_Israel_Defense_Forces "Women in the Israel Defense Forces"), of which only 20 days were in uniform. Reflecting on her military experience in a 2016 interview with *[Fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fashion_(magazine) "Fashion (magazine)")*, Gadot said, "It was demanding because you give up your freedom for two years, but there is something special in giving back to your community."

In 2007, while still serving, Gadot appeared in *[Maxim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine) "Maxim (magazine)")*'s "Women of the Israel Defense Forces" photo shoot, which was later featured on the cover of the *[New York Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Post "New York Post")*. The shoot was part of a campaign by [Israel's consulate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Israel "List of diplomatic missions of Israel") in New York, *Maxim*, and the [Ministry of Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Tourism_(Israel) "Ministry of Tourism (Israel)") to "promote Israel, attract young American men, and reshape its public image". The campaign faced criticism, particularly from [Knesset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knesset "Knesset") members, over the portrayal of female soldiers in bikinis. Following the criticism, Gadot defended the images, stating that she did not consider them pornographic and dismissed the political criticism, saying she was "not involved in politics". After the photo was featured on the July 2007 cover of the *[New York Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Post "New York Post")*, the shoot significantly boosted Gadot's public profile; as a result, she landed an international campaign for "Prada Ellis" perfume. That same year, she began pursuing law and [international studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_studies "International studies") at IDC Herzliya (now [Reichman University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichman_University "Reichman University")). She attributed this decision to being "so deep", and because she "loved" the legal comedy-drama television series *[Ally McBeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ally_McBeal "Ally McBeal")*. She eventually dropped out three times—the first time because of her first acting role.

After her first year at university, a casting director from London contacted Gadot's agent after seeing her [modeling card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comp_card "Comp card") on the agency's wall, which led to an unsuccessful audition for the role of [Camille Montes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camille_Montes "Camille Montes") in the James Bond film *[Quantum of Solace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_of_Solace "Quantum of Solace")*. In a 2017 interview with [*W Magazine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_(magazine) "W (magazine)"), Gadot said she initially felt "too serious and smart to be an actress" due to her studies and was "uncomfortable" speaking English. Her agent urged her to go "out of respect", and the casting director supported her through several callbacks. Although she lost the Bond role to [Olga Kurylenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Kurylenko "Olga Kurylenko"), Gadot found the experience "interesting" and told her agents to let her know if any other opportunities came up that might be a better fit for her, and two months later, made her acting debut in the Israeli drama *Bubot*. She recalled that on the first day of university classes, she received a call informing her that she had landed the lead role of Miriam. She later performed in *[Festigal Underwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festigal "Festigal")*, an annual Israeli musical show for kids during [Hanukkah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanukkah "Hanukkah"), where she performed as a [mermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mermaid "Mermaid"), acting, singing, and dancing.

Three months after her unsuccessful audition for *Quantum of Solace*, the same casting director recommended Gadot to [Universal Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Studios_Hollywood "Universal Studios Hollywood") and contacted her about a role in *[Fast & Furious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious "Fast & Furious")* franchise. She was asked to "urgently" send a filmed audition to Los Angeles, which was well received. As a result, the studio flew Gadot and her agent, the CEO of the Look Agency, to LA for a personal meeting with [Vin Diesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vin_Diesel "Vin Diesel"), who would decide if she got the role. Gadot was ultimately chosen over six other actresses for the [supporting role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supporting_role "Supporting role") of [Gisele Yashar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gisele_Yashar "Gisele Yashar") in the [fourth film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious_(2009_film) "Fast & Furious (2009 film)") of the franchise.

Reflecting on the audition process, Gadot shared that she worked closely with Israeli coach Orit Shar, preparing thoroughly and building a backstory that impressed director [Justin Lin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Lin "Justin Lin"). Filming took about a month. After filming wrapped in 2008, Gadot signed with [Endeavor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endeavor_(company) "Endeavor (company)"), becoming the only Israeli represented by the agency. Later that year, she signed with [ICM agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICM_Partners "ICM Partners") and One management. After *Fast & Furious* was released in 2009, Gadot reflected on her success saying, "I can't believe this happened to me..I have more luck than intelligence." The role is considered her [big break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_role "Breakthrough role"), earning rave reviews and cementing her status as a "Hollywood mainstay." That same year, she made a brief appearance in the TV series *[Entourage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entourage_(American_TV_series) "Entourage (American TV series)")* in the episode titled "[Amongst Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amongst_Friends_(Entourage) "Amongst Friends (Entourage)")", portraying Lisa, a date of the main character, [Vincent Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Chase "Vincent Chase"). She left due to unwanted and "inappropriate behavior." She later appeared on the American teen drama, *[The Beautiful Life: TBL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beautiful_Life "The Beautiful Life")*.

### 2010–2016: Breakthrough as Wonder Woman

In 2010, Gadot had small roles in the action-adventure comedy *[Knight and Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_and_Day "Knight and Day"),* playing Naomi, the date of [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise "Tom Cruise")’s character, in which she also speaks Hebrew. This was followed by the action comedy *[Date Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_Night "Date Night")*, where she appears briefly as Natanya, the Israeli bedfellow of [Mark Wahlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Wahlberg "Mark Wahlberg")’s character, Holbrooke; her scenes with Wahlberg were filmed over five days. In 2011, Gadot reprised her role as Gisele in *[Fast Five](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_Five "Fast Five")*. Gadot explained that giving Gisele a [Mossad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad "Mossad") background allowed for [character development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_arc "Character arc") across multiple films. She believes director Lin valued her Israeli military experience and aimed to use her weapons knowledge in the role. Later that year, she appeared in the second season of the Israeli series *[Asfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asfur "Asfur")*, playing Kiki, a coffee shop owner in Amsterdam. In 2012, she starred the Israeli drama, *[Kathmandu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_(TV_series) "Kathmandu (TV series)")*. Sources in the industry estimate that Gadot earned over [NIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_new_shekel "Israeli new shekel")3,000 (approximately US$800) per day of filming. In 2013, she returned to the *Fast & Furious* franchise, reprising her character in *[Fast & Furious 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious_6 "Fast & Furious 6")*, where she performed some of her own [stunt work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stunt "Stunt") in the film.
In November 2013, *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* reported that Gadot, along with other actresses, had tested for the role of Wonder Woman. In December 2013, Gadot was confirmed for the role in the untitled *[Batman v Superman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_v_Superman:_Dawn_of_Justice "Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice")* movie alongside [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill "Henry Cavill") and [Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck "Ben Affleck"), directed by [Zack Snyder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zack_Snyder "Zack Snyder"). The contract for her portrayal of Wonder Woman was managed by her then-agent, ADD agency, which represented Gadot both in Israel and internationally. She left the agency in 2019 on good terms. Reflecting on her audition for the role in a 2016 *Variety* interview, Gadot described the process as "super intensive, nerve-racking, a long roller coaster". While in Los Angeles, [Warner Bros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros. "Warner Bros."). invited her to audition for a then undisclosed role with Snyder. Her agent later informed her that she would [screen-test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_test "Screen test") opposite Affleck, and she ultimately secured the role, which was revealed to be in *Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice*.

Before landing the role, Gadot shared that she had almost quit acting due to frustration with the constant rejection she faced while auditioning in Los Angeles. After several *Fast & Furious* films and coming close to major parts, including [Imperator Furiosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperator_Furiosa "Imperator Furiosa") in *[Mad Max: Fury Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Max:_Fury_Road "Mad Max: Fury Road")* (2015), which ultimately went to [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron "Charlize Theron"), she considered leaving the profession. She also noted that acting was not a childhood dream but a field she grew curious about and eventually fell in love with. In June 2014, she signed a three-picture deal with Warner Bros. to appear as Wonder Woman, reportedly earning $300,000 per film.

In July 2014, Gadot's portrayal of Wonder Woman was unveiled at [Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic-Con "Comic-Con") during the *Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice* [panel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panel_show "Panel show"). Some critics argued Gadot's Israeli background and slim frame made her unfit to play the part. Gadot dismissed the backlash. Despite prior criticism of her physique, she stated that she had trained to build muscle for the role, moving beyond her previous Fast & Furious image. To prepare, she took lessons in [swordsmanship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swordsmanship "Swordsmanship"), [Kung Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kung_Fu "Kung Fu"), [kickboxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kickboxing "Kickboxing"), [capoeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capoeira "Capoeira") and [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu "Brazilian jiu-jitsu"), gaining 17 pounds of muscle. She also explained that she "trained six hours a day for six months—two hours of gym work, two hours of fight choreography, and up to two hours of horseback riding. It was much more intense than the army." That same year, Gadot landed her first Israeli film role in the comedy *[Kicking Out Shoshana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kicking_Out_Shoshana "Kicking Out Shoshana")*, playing lead character Mirit Ben-Harush. Later, in 2016, she was offered the lead role in the [Hollywood remake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_remake "Film remake") of *[Ben-Hur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Hur_(2016_film) "Ben-Hur (2016 film)")* (2016) but had to decline due to scheduling conflicts with *Batman v Superman*.

Prior to her debut as Wonder Woman, Gadot revealed she had previously been offered the role of [Faora-Ul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faora-Ul "Faora-Ul") in *[Man of Steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_Steel_(film) "Man of Steel (film)")* (2013)*.* Gadot turned it down due to her pregnancy; this led to [Antje Traue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antje_Traue "Antje Traue") being cast for the role instead. In 2016, Gadot officially debuted as Wonder Woman in the superhero film *Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice*. Gadot's performance as the superhero, the character's first film appearance, was singled out by *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* as one of the film's best parts. Her portrayal of Wonder Woman quickly became a defining role in her career, with critics praising her for bringing strength, grace, and a refreshing depth to the character. Critics particularly noted how she brought a strong sense of empowerment and integrity to the role, setting a new standard for female superheroes in mainstream cinema.

She then had a small role in [John Hillcoat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hillcoat "John Hillcoat")'s crime-thriller *[Triple 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_9 "Triple 9")*, where she starred along with [Kate Winslet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Winslet "Kate Winslet") and [Aaron Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Paul "Aaron Paul"). Later that year, she co-starred in the action crime thriller film *[Criminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_(2016_film) "Criminal (2016 film)")*, as the wife of [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds "Ryan Reynolds")' character, alongside [Kevin Costner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Costner "Kevin Costner"), [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman "Gary Oldman"), and [Tommy Lee Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee_Jones "Tommy Lee Jones"). She described the film as the most challenging project she had done to date. Her final film of the year was the action comedy *[Keeping Up with the Joneses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keeping_Up_with_the_Joneses_(film) "Keeping Up with the Joneses (film)")*, in which she played a secret agent, alongside [Zach Galifianakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Galifianakis "Zach Galifianakis"), [Jon Hamm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Hamm "Jon Hamm"), and [Isla Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_Fisher "Isla Fisher"). Critic Alex Welch wrote on *[IGN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IGN "IGN")* about Gadot's performance: "the script unfortunately banks mostly on her looks and deadly skills as a spy for her jokes, but Gadot manages to make it work."

### 2017–2020: *Wonder Woman* films

In 2017, Gadot starred in a solo film for her character, *[Wonder Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman_(2017_film) "Wonder Woman (2017 film)")*, which was paid $300,000. This figure sparked some controversy, as it was significantly lower than the salaries commanded by many male action stars in Hollywood. However, the film received a positive response from critics, with some calling it the best installment in the [DC Extended Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Extended_Universe "DC Extended Universe"). Praise was directed at [Patty Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patty_Jenkins "Patty Jenkins")'s direction, the chemistry between Gadot and [Chris Pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Pine "Chris Pine"), the musical score, and the thrilling action sequences. Due to her former service in the IDF, the film has been banned in [Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon "Lebanon"), [Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait "Kuwait"), and [Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan "Jordan"). She then reprised the role in the [ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ensemble_cast "Ensemble cast") film *[Justice League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_League_(film) "Justice League (film)")*, which was released in November 2017, and which was her third DC Extended Universe installment. During the film's production under director [Joss Whedon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joss_Whedon "Joss Whedon"), she alleged that Whedon verbally abused her and "threatened" her career. She stated in an interview that she took the matter to the heads of Warner Bros., who "took care of it".

That same year, Gadot was invited to join the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences "Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences"). Gadot then voiced the character Shank, who was modeled after her, in the [Walt Disney Animation Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Animation_Studios "Walt Disney Animation Studios") film, *[Ralph Breaks the Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet "Ralph Breaks the Internet")* (2018). The film's co-director, [Phil Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Johnston_(filmmaker) "Phil Johnston (filmmaker)"), praised Gadot's performance. She then appeared in the music video for [Maroon 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maroon_5 "Maroon 5")'s song "[Girls Like You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_Like_You "Girls Like You")" featuring [Cardi B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardi_B "Cardi B"). In January 2020, [National Geographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic "National Geographic") announced *Impact with Gal Gadot*. The six-part [documentary series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_documentary "Television documentary"), executive produced by Gadot and her husband, highlights young women overcoming poverty, violence, and discrimination to create positive change in their communities, premiered in April 2021 across National Geographic's digital and social platforms.

Gadot returned as Wonder Woman in *[Wonder Woman 1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman_1984 "Wonder Woman 1984")* (2020), for which she reportedly earned $10 million. The [sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequel "Sequel") to her 2017 solo film was released on 25 December 2020. It was also released on the [HBO Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Max "HBO Max") streaming platform due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic"). *[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post "The Washington Post")* reported that the response changed from "early praise to precipitous decline". The film received praise for Gadot's performance and the visual effects; however, it garnered mixed reviews from critics, with some finding the plot overly complex and less impactful than the first film. Although a third *Wonder Woman* film was [greenlit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenlight "Greenlight") on 27 December 2020, it was ultimately [cancelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unfinished_films "Unfinished films") in December 2022, despite Gadot teasing [development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_(film) "Development (film)") plans through 2023.

### 2021–present: Streaming projects

In November 2021, Gadot starred alongside [Dwayne Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwayne_Johnson "Dwayne Johnson") and [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds "Ryan Reynolds") in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") action comedy film *[Red Notice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Notice_(film) "Red Notice (film)")*, written and directed by [Rawson Marshall Thurber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawson_Marshall_Thurber "Rawson Marshall Thurber"). *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")*, in its review, opined that Gadot was "sleepwalking through the motions" and was "adept only when fighting". The film became Netflix’s most-watched title ever at the time, according to *[Bloomberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_L.P. "Bloomberg L.P.")*. The film has over 328 million hours streamed in its first three weeks. This equates to 164 million accounts watching it in full, making it the most-watched new movie of 2021.

The following year, she co-starred with [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh "Kenneth Branagh"), [Tom Bateman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Bateman_(actor) "Tom Bateman (actor)"), and [Annette Bening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annette_Bening "Annette Bening") in the 2022 mystery film *[Death on the Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_on_the_Nile_(2022_film) "Death on the Nile (2022 film)")*, which was also directed by Branagh. *[Los Angeles Times'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* chief film critic [Justin Chang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Chang "Justin Chang") deemed her "underwhelming" in the film, while *[The Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Week "The Week")*'s Jesse Hassenger defended Gadot's performance addressing the widespread criticism she received for her portrayal of Linnet Doyle, particularly for a meme-worthy line delivery. The film was banned in Tunisia following protests over Gadot's Israeli military service and her support for [Israel's 2014 Gaza offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Gaza_War "2014 Gaza War"). This decision, followed by bans in Kuwait and Lebanon, reflects longstanding support for the Palestinian cause.

In 2023, Gadot made uncredited cameo appearances, her two final outings, as Wonder Woman in *[Shazam! Fury of the Gods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shazam!_Fury_of_the_Gods "Shazam! Fury of the Gods")* and *[The Flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flash_(film) "The Flash (film)")*. She also returned to the *[Fast & Furious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious "Fast & Furious")* franchise with a cameo appearance in *[Fast X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_X "Fast X")*. Later that year, she was offered the role of Barbie in *[Barbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbie_(film) "Barbie (film)")*, but she was unavailable. Later that year, she played the lead role of a [double agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_agent "Double agent") in Netflix's action film *[Heart of Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_Stone_(2023_film) "Heart of Stone (2023 film)")*. This film is Gadot and her husband's first project developed and produced through their company, Pilot Wave, which they began developing in 2018. She called it an "extremely empowering and educational experience". The film however, received mostly negative reviews, with [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman") stating: "Gadot is game, but there's a slightly downbeat earnestness about her that doesn't mesh with the reflexive quippiness of the dialogue." Despite negative reception, the film emerged as Netflix's second-most-watched film in late 2023, with nearly 110 million views..

Gadot next starred as the [Evil Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evil_Queen_(Disney) "Evil Queen (Disney)") in a [live-action adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_White_(2025_film) "Snow White (2025 film)") of Disney's 1937 animated film *[Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_White_and_the_Seven_Dwarfs_(1937_film) "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937 film)")*. In an interview with *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")*, she said Disney needed to confirm she could sing, so she spent a month preparing before auditioning and landing the role. She also had her own personal [dialect coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialect_coach "Dialect coach") for the role. In August 2024, calls from [pro-Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_nationalism "Palestinian nationalism") activists to boycott Disney's *Snow White* remake emerged due to Gadot's support for Israel. In March 2025, it was reported that Disney assigned bodyguards to Gadot after she received death threats linked to co-star [Rachel Zegler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zegler "Rachel Zegler")'s "Free Palestine" post during *Snow White*'s promotional campaign. Gadot's performance in the film was polarizing; some felt it showcased her potential as a villain, while others argued she lacked the charisma and menace needed for the role. Her rendition of the song "All Is Fair" drew particular criticism for its awkward delivery and lack of vocal strength, which undercut the intended impact of the character. In response to the criticism she received for the film, Gadot stated in an interview that audiences primarily saw her as an Israeli rather than as an actress. She further added that the film’s lack of success was due to the "pressure on celebrities to speak out against Israel."

Also in 2025, Gadot appeared in [Julian Schnabel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Schnabel "Julian Schnabel")’s film *[In the Hand of Dante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Hand_of_Dante_(film) "In the Hand of Dante (film)")* as [Gemma Donati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Donati "Gemma Donati"), alongside [Oscar Isaac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Isaac "Oscar Isaac") as [Nick Tosches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Tosches "Nick Tosches") / [Dante Alighieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dante_Alighieri "Dante Alighieri") and other lead actors. Based on Tosches’ novel, the film moves between 14th- and 21st-century Italy and follows the discovery of a handwritten manuscript of [Dante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dante_Alighieri "Dante Alighieri")’s [The Divine Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divine_Comedy "Divine Comedy") that travels from the Vatican to the New York underworld. The film first premiered on 3 September 2025, at the [Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Film_Festival "Venice Film Festival"). Gadot’s appearance in the film sparked calls for a boycott, in response, Schnabel stated at that he chose the actors for their merits and that they did "an extraordinary job," adding that he sees no reason to boycott artists for their pro-Israel stances. When asked about casting Gadot, the director stated that he spoke to her on the phone and conducted screen tests, after which he believed she could perform the role. He also commented that in the film, Gadot’s character reminded him of [Ingrid Bergman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingrid_Bergman "Ingrid Bergman") and described her interactions with co-star Isaac as "warm and embracing".

She then starred as the lead alongside Damian Lewis in the thriller, *[The Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Runner_(2026_film) "The Runner (2026 film)")*. directed by [Kevin Macdonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Macdonald_(director) "Kevin Macdonald (director)"). Production on the film was disrupted when pro-Palestine protesters stormed the London set. Gadot and the production company brought a case against them based on a [little-used trade union law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_law "Labour law"). Gadot is set to star alongside [Matthias Schoenaerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_Schoenaerts "Matthias Schoenaerts") in *Ruin*, a post-WWII revenge thriller. The story follows a recently released [concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration_camp "Concentration camp") prisoner (Gadot) who forms an unlikely alliance with a former [German soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Army "German Army") (Schoenaerts) as they seek vengeance on a Nazi SS squad. The film is directed by [Niki Caro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niki_Caro "Niki Caro").

## Other endeavors

### Endorsements and modeling

Before enlisting in the Israeli army, she worked briefly as a model in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan "Milan"). She then returned to Israel and continued modeling while serving in the military. In 2007, following the *Maxim* "Women of the Israel Defense Forces" photoshoot, Gadot's public image gained significant attention, leading her to promote various products, appear in numerous advertisements, and was featured in several magazines. She first landed an international campaign for Prada Ellis perfume. She then became a [spokesmodel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotional_model "Promotional model") for the Israeli clothing brand [Castro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castro_(clothing) "Castro (clothing)"), a role she held for over 11 seasons, reportedly earning about $100,000 per season. In 2010, Gadot walked the runway for Castro's collection and continued modeling for the brand regularly through 2015.

In 2013, Castro CEO Gabi Rotter noted that Gadot was not widely known when she was chosen but was the "perfect fit to represent Israeli beauty". He cited her background as both an IDF soldier and a beauty pageant contestant as key factors in her selection. In 2013, Gadot signed a two-year deal with skincare brand Carline. According to Ofer Yaar from the advertising agency Bruckner Neta Yaar, Gadot was selected as the [spokesmodel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotional_model "Promotional model") because she embodies the brand's values, being "local and international, she exudes simplicity and accessibility, and she is your best friend, even when she is Wonder Woman." In 2015 she became the face of [Gucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gucci "Gucci") Bamboo. In 2018, [Reebok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reebok "Reebok") announced an [endorsement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Testimonial "Testimonial") deal with Gadot, reported to be worth $10 million (NIS 34 million). She also became a brand ambassador for [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei "Huawei"), which led to controversy after she [tweeted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tweet_(social_media) "Tweet (social media)") a promotional video using an [Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Inc. "Apple Inc.") device. Gadot explained that the mistake was made by a member of her publicity team, who incorrectly posted the video from the wrong phone.

In 2018, [Revlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revlon "Revlon") named Gadot its global ambassador for its "Live Boldly" campaign. The brand executives praised Gadot as a symbol of "strength, beauty, and empowerment," with Fabian Garcia, Revlon's president & CEO, emphasized that Gadot represents "the beauty, determination and attitude that reflect what it is for women to live boldly in today's world". However, her modeling contract for Revlon drew criticism, notably from Muslim blogger [Amani Al-Khatahtbeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amani_al-Khatahtbeh "Amani al-Khatahtbeh"), who rejected a Revlon award because of Gadot's support for the IDF.

In 2019, Gadot starred in commercials alongside [Hot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_(Israel) "Hot (Israel)")'s popular stars, promoting the brand's "Israeli pride" campaign. Hot CEO Tal Granot-Goldstein described her as the perfect fit, embodying the essence of "Israeliness" for the new campaign. In 2021, she became the brand ambassador for [Smartwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaceau_Smartwater "Glaceau Smartwater"), a division of [Coca-Cola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coca-Cola "Coca-Cola"). In April 2023, Gadot became the new [ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassador "Ambassador") of [Tiffany & Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_%26_Co. "Tiffany & Co.")., and was honored to cut the ribbon at the brand's upgraded and remodeled [Fifth Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_Avenue "Fifth Avenue") store in New York.

### Production ventures

Gadot’s husband and his brother owned a [boutique hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boutique_hotel "Boutique hotel") in [Neve Tzedek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neve_Tzedek "Neve Tzedek"), Tel Aviv, called the Varsano Hotel. In 2015, it was sold to Russian [oligarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oligarchy "Oligarchy") and politician [Roman Abramovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Abramovich "Roman Abramovich") for [INS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_new_shekel "Israeli new shekel") 100 million (approximately $26 million). In October 2019, Gadot formed a film and television production company, Pilot Wave, with her husband Jaron "Yaron" Varsano. Reflecting on the decision, Gadot stated in a 2023 interview with *[L'Oréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Or%C3%A9al "L'Oréal")* that it was to have greater control over her career, noting that she does not prefer to wait for opportunities to come to her. Since then, she has shared teasers for different projects—some got mixed reactions, but none have been released. In November 2021, the couple launched Goodles, a mac and cheese brand co-founded with "experienced partners" like former [Kraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraft_Foods "Kraft Foods") brand executive Paul Earle, with the goal of offering a healthier alternative to traditional boxed mac and cheese. In 2022, the couple invested in Safe School, an Israeli [startup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Startup_company "Startup company") founded by Doron Herman, which provides educational content focused on social issues for children and teenagers. This investment is part of their ongoing support for Israeli startups, following a previous investment in [K Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Health "K Health").

## Reception

### Media reception

Gadot faced early criticism for her slim physique during her pageant years in 2004, with some speculating about [eating disorders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eating_disorder "Eating disorder"), claims she dismissed as rooted in unrealistic beauty standards. *[Ynet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ynet "Ynet")* noted Gadot was once seen as a "shelf product", struggling to break into local fashion. Photographer Alon Shefransky called her transformation from a "baby-faced look" to a more defined presence a "wow moment," comparing it to discovering "the next [Natalie Portman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Portman "Natalie Portman")". By 2012, Gadot ranked second on *[Forbes Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_Israel "Forbes Israel")*'s list of top-earning models, with earnings of NIS 1.3 million (approximately US$350,000), and in 2013, her earnings rose to NIS2.4 million (approximately US$650,000), driven largely by brand endorsements and her role in *[Fast & Furious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious "Fast & Furious")*. In 2015, *Forbes Israel* ranked her 49th on their list of "The Most Powerful Women in Israel" and 38th on their list of "Influential Women".

Ahead of *Wonder Woman*'s 2017 release, Gadot received 95% positive online comments, with [Taykey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innovid "Innovid") analytics ranking her as the most beloved Israeli globally. The company CEO, Amit Avner, noted that Gadot had already been the most talked-about personality during [Super Bowl commercials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_commercials "Super Bowl commercials"), one of which she appeared in earlier that year. However, the release of *Wonder Woman* would elevated her popularity further. That same year *Forbes Israel* ranked her 35th on their list of "Influential Women", and ranked 53rd on *Forbes* "30 Under 30 list". Additionally, she topped *[FHM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FHM "FHM")*'s "[100 Sexiest Women in the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FHM%27s_100_Sexiest_Women_(UK) "FHM's 100 Sexiest Women (UK)")" list. That same year, she became the third highest-grossing actress of 2017.

In her January 2018 piece, *[The Forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Forward "The Forward")* staff writer Jenny Singer critiques how Gadot is portrayed in the media, particularly on the cover of *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")*. Despite Gadot's "success as an actress, activist, and athlete", Singer argues that Gadot is often reduced to a [sex object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_object "Sex object"), with her talents overshadowed by her physical appearance. Singer suggests that Gadot's background, including her modeling past and accent, may contribute to the media's focus on her looks rather than her accomplishments. That year, *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")* magazine named Gadot one of the [100 most influential people in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100"), and *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* ranked her as the tenth highest-paid actress in the world, with annual earnings of $10 million. In 2020, *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")* ranked Gadot as the third highest-paid actress in the world, with annual earnings of $31.5 million. In 2022, *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* reported that Gadot earned $5 million for her role in *[Death on the Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_on_the_Nile_(2022_film) "Death on the Nile (2022 film)"),* making her the film’s highest-paid star. In June 2023, it was announced that Gadot would become the first Israeli actor to receive a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. This was met with criticism, and by 18 March 2025, she officially received it. The ceremony was disrupted by both pro-Palestinian and pro-Israeli protesters, with signs reading "Snow White supports [genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_genocide "Gaza genocide")" and "standing with Israel". The star was reportedly vandalized.

### Cultural reception

In her honor, the [Azrieli Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azrieli_Center "Azrieli Center") in Tel Aviv was illuminated on 1 June 2017, ahead of the Israeli premiere of *Wonder Woman*. The towers displayed the message: "We are proud of you, Gal Gadot, our Wonder Woman."
As reported by [CBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_News "CBC News"), Gadot's portrayal of Wonder Woman has made her "the world's most famous Israeli and the country's most high-profile [ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassador "Ambassador")". She has been compared to Israeli model [Bar Refaeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Refaeli "Bar Refaeli"), who faced backlash for not serving in the military, while her own service has endeared her to Israelis. Gadot image as a "good Israeli" and her presence in *Wonder Woman* have made her a source of "national Israeli pride". In a 2017 *[Mako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mako_(website) "Mako (website)")* article, Neta Hotar noted that Gadot had become one of Israel’s most successful exports, surpassing [Jonah Lotan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonah_Lotan "Jonah Lotan")'s recognition from *[CSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation "CSI: Crime Scene Investigation")* (2000–2015). Hotar described her as "Gal from the block" and credited her success to her accessibility, her experience as a [media trainee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_training "Media training"), and her association with a major brand like Wonder Woman, a character strongly associated with American symbols and [patriotism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriotism "Patriotism"), which Hotar also sees as reflecting Israeli pride, showing "how nationalism can reflect female empowerment."

In 2017, Isabel Feinstein of *[The Jerusalem Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jerusalem_Post "The Jerusalem Post")* praised Gadot as a powerful example of "[soft power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_power "Soft power")" for Israel, highlighting how her rise to global fame offers the "[Jewish state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_state "Jewish state")" a unique opportunity for positive international exposure. Feinstein highlighted that Gadot's [Google search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Search "Google Search") results (20.7 million) surpass those of Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu "Benjamin Netanyahu") (16.1 million) emphasizing her global influence. She also noted that Gadot's appearances on Western [talk shows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk_show "Talk show") like *[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_Starring_Jimmy_Fallon "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon")* and *[The Ellen DeGeneres Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ellen_DeGeneres_Show "The Ellen DeGeneres Show")* have helped humanize the stereotype of the "brutal Israeli soldier", making her relatable to a worldwide audience. Feinstein went on to describe Gadot as "a living contradiction", claiming that "the association of an Israeli actress with a progressive agenda helps those who unfairly condemn all citizens of a nation for the actions of their government see that Israelis are complex and open-minded humans with a wide range of opinions". In a 2019 article by *[The Australian Jewish News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Australian_Jewish_News "The Australian Jewish News")*, it is highlighted that Gadot is often seen as a more effective ambassador for Israel than its diplomats, given her global fame. Ari Ingel of the [Creative Community for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Community_for_Peace "Creative Community for Peace") believes her interactions with prominent Hollywood figures can positively influence Israel's image. However, Shayna Weiss of [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University "Brandeis University") views this as a "soft power" issue—using culture to sway opinions—and questions whether such influence has measurable or lasting effects, saying, "I don't think Gal Gadot harms Israel, but do I think Gal Gadot helps Israel much? Not so sure."

The release of the film sparked a debate about Gadot's [Jewish identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_identity "Jewish identity") and her portrayal as a [woman of color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Person_of_color "Person of color"). Some argued that as an [Israeli Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Jews "Israeli Jews"), Gadot should not be considered straightforwardly white in the context that Hollywood has historically limited representation of diverse racial identities. Others, including some [Jews of color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews_of_color "Jews of color"), argued that Gadot is white, and thus *Wonder Woman* was a breakthrough for [white women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_feminism "White feminism") but lacked representation for women of color. In contrast, others, including [Tamar Herman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamar_Hermann "Tamar Hermann"), viewed Gadot's role as a positive step toward broader representation, especially for [Jewish women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_women "Jewish women"), who are often underrepresented or stereotyped in Hollywood, and further highlighting Gadot's [Israeli heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Heritage_Classroom "Israel Heritage Classroom"), distinct accent, and strength as a heroic figure. Arielle Iniko Newton, writing for *[Essence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essence_(magazine) "Essence (magazine)")*, argued that Black women's support for the film ignored Gadot's ties to the [IDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDF "IDF") and her stance on [Palestinian oppression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_genocide_accusation "Palestinian genocide accusation"). Salam Al-Mahadin, writing for the [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University "Duke University") [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalism "Journalism"), objected not to Gadot's whiteness but to "her status as an enthusiastic Israeli soldier and citizen and the film's [valorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valorisation "Valorisation") of US [imperial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperialism "Imperialism") militarism."

During his visit to Los Angeles in 2017 for the General Assembly of the [Jewish Federations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Federation "Jewish Federation"), then-President [Reuven Rivlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuven_Rivlin "Reuven Rivlin") was invited by Gadot to the premiere of *Justice League*, a film in which she stars. Rivlin embraced Gadot, expressing his pride in her as a "true ambassador [of Israel], who has managed to place us [Israeli] at the pinnacles of global filmmaking with grace, values, and an Israeli smile."

In June 2018, amid an [Arab boycott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_League_boycott_of_Israel "Arab League boycott of Israel") of Gadot, a cinema in the [northern Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_District_(Israel) "Northern District (Israel)") town of [Nazareth Illit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazareth_Illit "Nazareth Illit") was named "Gal Cinema" in her honor. The two-screen cinema opened with the film *Shelter* by Israeli director [Eran Riklis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eran_Riklis "Eran Riklis"). The spokeswoman Orna Yosef stated the decision aimed to inspire young people, highlighting Gadot as a symbol of success and the fulfillment of dreams. On 7 November 2022, Gadot was honored at the [Israeli Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Culture "Israeli Culture") & Arts (ICA) Awards by the [America-Israel Cultural Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America-Israel_Cultural_Foundation "America-Israel Cultural Foundation") (AICF). The award recognized her significant contributions to film, alongside other Israeli artists.

In May 2023, Gadot received an award for "extraordinary achievements" at an event celebrating [Israel's 75th Independence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Independence_Day "Israel Independence Day") in Los Angeles, organized by the Israeli-American Community (IAC) and the [Israeli Consulate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Consulate "Israeli Consulate"). In her speech, Gadot expressed pride in her Israeli heritage and emphasized the importance of strengthening ties between [Israelis and Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93United_States_relations "Israel–United States relations"). In January 2025 Dudi Caspi, Israel's representative to the [Golden Globes Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Gloves "Golden Gloves"), spoke with Iris Cole on 103FM radio about the ceremony. He praised Gadot, calling her "a good ambassador for Israel". Caspi noted that although Gadot did not wear a [hostages pin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostages_in_Gaza "Hostages in Gaza") at the event, she remains an important figure for Israel in Hollywood. In March 2025, the [Anti-Defamation League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Defamation_League "Anti-Defamation League") (ADL) awarded Gadot a "Certificate of Appreciation" for her strong stance against anti-Semitism and hatred, and her unwavering support for Israel and Jewish communities worldwide.

In July 2025, at the [Jerusalem Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Film_Festival "Jerusalem Film Festival"), Gadot received two awards: one from the Festival for her contributions to [international cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_cinema "World cinema") and her success over two decades of work, and another from [Hadassah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadassah_Women%27s_Zionist_Organization_of_America "Hadassah Women's Zionist Organization of America") for "her unwavering and courageous support of Israel and for using her global platform to promote truth and justice on behalf of Israel." Festival director Roni Mahadev-Levin credited Gadot as "an actress who turned the Israeli dream into a global reality," adding that she is "a true movie star who turns films into blockbusters, a leading figure in world cinema who opened doors for Israeli creators around the globe and raised international awareness of Israeli culture." Hadassah president Carol Ann Schwartz and chairwoman [Dalia Itzik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalia_Itzik "Dalia Itzik") stated that Gadot was an obvious choice for the award, which had previously been presented to actress [Gwyneth Paltrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwyneth_Paltrow "Gwyneth Paltrow"), with Schwartz saying, "Choosing Gadot was natural — she is a trailblazing woman who serves as an ambassador of Israel and the Jewish people wherever she goes." Gadot has one of [the most followed Israeli Twitter accounts](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=The_most_followed_Israeli_Twitter_accounts&action=edit&redlink=1 "The most followed Israeli Twitter accounts (page does not exist)") [[he](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%98%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%98%D7%A8_%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%A2%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A8 "he:חשבונות הטוויטר הישראליים הנעקבים ביותר")]. In September 2025, *[The Jerusalem Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jerusalem_Post "The Jerusalem Post")* included her on their list of "50 Most Influential Jews", ranked 45th in a group entry of "seven [pro-Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_advocacy "Israel advocacy") [influencers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_media_in_the_Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict "Social media in the Israeli–Palestinian conflict")". In 2026, she received the [2026 Genesis Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Genesis_Prize "2026 Genesis Prize") for her wartime support and stated that she would donate her $1 million prize to organizations that "help Israelis heal, rebuild, and recover after October 7".

## Advocacy

Gadot identifies as a feminist and promotes women's empowerment. In July 2016, Gadot joined a campaign by Israeli female master's students in communications to rebrand Israel by highlighting its strong women. Titled "Israel Creates Wonder Women," it spotlighted real "Israeli heroines" in different fields to promote female empowerment. Gadot supported the initiative after the students reached out to her.

Later, in 2016, Gadot, fellow *[Wonder Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman_(TV_series) "Wonder Woman (TV series)")* actress [Lynda Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynda_Carter "Lynda Carter"), [DC Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Entertainment "DC Entertainment") President [Diane Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Nelson_(businesswoman) "Diane Nelson (businesswoman)"), director [Patty Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patty_Jenkins "Patty Jenkins") and U.N. Under-Secretary-General [Cristina Gallach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristina_Gallach "Cristina Gallach") appeared at the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations") on 21 October 2016, the 75th anniversary of the first appearance of Wonder Woman, to mark the character's designation by the United Nations as its "Honorary Ambassador for the Empowerment of Women and Girls". The gesture was intended to raise awareness of UN [Sustainable Development Goal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_Development_Goal "Sustainable Development Goal") No. 5, which seeks to achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls by 2030. The decision was met with protests from UN staff members who stated in their petition to UN Secretary-General [Ban Ki-moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Ki-moon "Ban Ki-moon") that the character is "not culturally encompassing or sensitive" and served to objectify women. As a result, the character was stripped of the designation, and the project ended on 16 December.

### Views on Israel

Gadot has been described by the media as outspoken in her support for Israel. *[Israel Hayom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Hayom "Israel Hayom")* characterized her transition from being "face of a brand or movie to being the face of a country at war" as a personal battle, placing her at the center of [public diplomacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_diplomacy "Public diplomacy") as the country’s *[de facto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_facto "De facto")* "ambassador", noting that in Israel, she has been criticized for "political softness", while in Hollywood she has been accused of excessive [nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalism "Nationalism").

During the [2014 Gaza War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Gaza_War "2014 Gaza War"), Gadot posted on Facebook a picture of herself and her daughter praying in front of [Shabbat candles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabbat_candles "Shabbat candles") for Israeli citizens and soldiers, and condemned Hamas as "cowards" who are "hiding behind women and children". In 2019, Gadot clashed with Israeli prime minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Netanyahu "Benjamin Netanyahu") over the status of [Israel's Arab minority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_citizens_of_Israel "Arab citizens of Israel") after Netanyahu's remark that Israel is a "[nation-state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nation_state "Nation state")" for Jews only. Responding to actress [Rotem Sela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotem_Sela "Rotem Sela")’s call for equality for all citizens, Gadot supported her, saying it's about "peace, equality, and tolerance."

During the [2021 Israel–Palestine crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Israel%E2%80%93Palestine_crisis "2021 Israel–Palestine crisis"), Gadot initially stayed silent, which led to criticism from Israeli and Jewish media. Gadot later called for peace in a statement that sparked widespread backlash. While expressing support for Israel, she referred to Palestinians as "neighbors" rather than naming them directly, which many saw as downplaying their suffering. [Israeli nationalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_nationalist "Israeli nationalist") also expressed disappointment, believing she should have used her global influence to defend Israel more strongly.

In 2022, following several attacks, including the [Bnei Brak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Bnei_Brak_shootings "2022 Bnei Brak shootings") and [Tel Aviv shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Tel_Aviv_shooting "2022 Tel Aviv shooting") attacks, Gadot faced criticism from Israeli media and fans for staying silent. In October 2023, during the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war"), Gadot posted a [story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram_story "Instagram story") on her [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") stating, "Killing innocent Palestinians is horrific", alongside condemning the killing of Israelis. This led to criticism from some in Israel, accusing her of equating both sides. In response, Gadot later emphasized, "Killing innocent Israelis is horrific," and deleted the mention of Palestinians following further criticism. Gadot, along with over 700 [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_States "Cinema of the United States") professionals, signed an open letter condemning the [Palestinian militant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_fedayeen "Palestinian fedayeen") group [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas "Hamas"), demanding the release of [hostages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war_hostage_crisis "Gaza war hostage crisis"), and expressing support for Israel. In November, Gadot organized a screening of *[Bearing Witness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_Witness_(2023_film) "Bearing Witness (2023 film)")*, a film showing Hamas violence, though she did not attend. The screening drew criticism from [Jewish Voice for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Voice_for_Peace "Jewish Voice for Peace"), which called the film "propaganda" and accused Gadot and director [Guy Nattiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Nattiv "Guy Nattiv") of overlooking the Palestinian perspective and promoting a pro-war agenda.

In May 2024, Gadot publicly supported [Eden Golan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eden_Golan "Eden Golan"), Israel's representative in [that year's Eurovision Song Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_2024 "Eurovision Song Contest 2024"), encouraging her followers to vote for Golan. As a result, Gadot lost a million Instagram followers. Despite the backlash, Gadot continued to share posts praising Golan and urging votes throughout the competition. In September 2024, Gadot joined Israeli president [Isaac Herzog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Herzog "Isaac Herzog") in launching the "Voice of the People" initiative, aimed at fostering global Jewish unity amidst rising [antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism "Antisemitism") and the "ongoing hostage crisis".

## Personal life

In 2020, Gadot purchased a [penthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penthouse_apartment "Penthouse apartment") in a gated community in [Malibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu,_California "Malibu, California"), from former Miss Universe [Bui Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bui_Simon "Bui Simon"). The property is reportedly worth around $5 million. Despite having stated that she [does not believe in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism "Atheism") [God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_in_Judaism "God in Judaism") and is not a "[religiously observant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_Observance "Religious Observance")" person, she performs the [Modeh Ani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modeh_Ani "Modeh Ani") prayer, and has described how attending a [synagogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synagogue "Synagogue") in Los Angeles soon after [October 7 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_7_attacks "October 7 attacks") helped her feel the embrace of the Jewish community.

### Relationships and family

In 2006, Gadot was briefly linked to Israeli swimmer [Eithan Urbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eithan_Urbach "Eithan Urbach"). That same year, Gadot met her future husband, Jaron "Yaron" Varsano, a real estate developer from [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam "Amsterdam") who grew up in a Jewish family in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands "Netherlands"), while she was still serving in the Israeli Army. The couple connected at a "[chakra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chakra "Chakra")/yoga retreat type of party" in the [Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negev "Negev").

In 2008, during a weekend off from filming *[Fast & Furious 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious_(2009_film) "Fast & Furious (2009 film)")*, the couple went to [Napa Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napa_Valley_AVA "Napa Valley AVA"), where Varsano proposed to Gadot. Later that year, on 28 September, they married at the [David Intercontinental Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InterContinental "InterContinental") in Israel. They reside in the [Neve Tzedek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neve_Tzedek "Neve Tzedek") neighborhood of [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv "Tel Aviv"), and have four daughters, Alma born 5 November 2011, Maya born 20 March 2017, Daniella born 29 June 2021, and Ori, born 5 February 2024.

### Health

In a 2023 interview with *[L'Officiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Officiel "L'Officiel")*, Gadot talked about her struggles with [impostor syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impostor_syndrome "Impostor syndrome"), often doubting how her work would be received. On 5 February 2024, during her eighth month of pregnancy with her fourth daughter, Ori, Gadot was diagnosed with [cerebral venous thrombosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerebral_venous_sinus_thrombosis "Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis"), a rare type of [stroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroke "Stroke") that accounts for less than 3% of all strokes, after suffering from an intense headache. At the hospital, her pregnancy was induced and she gave birth before undergoing emergency surgery within hours. A year after, Gadot told *Harper's Bazaar* that the [near-death experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near-death_experience "Near-death experience") made her more grateful and present in her life, reminding herself daily to slow down and go at her own pace. She also went public with the experience to help raise awareness.

### Influences

Gadot has expressed admiration for [Penélope Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen%C3%A9lope_Cruz "Penélope Cruz"), particularly for her courage, individuality, and confidence in embracing her identity. She appreciates how Cruz "stands up for herself and has something to say—she has her own spice, which I love. She is very courageous and also has an accent, but she just goes with it." She also recognizes [Yafa Yarkoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yafa_Yarkoni "Yafa Yarkoni") as her "[Israeli cultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Cultural_Excellence_Foundation "Israel Cultural Excellence Foundation") hero", referring to her as "the singer of wars". Additionally, when asked which historical event she would hypothetically like to witness, Gadot shared that she would have chosen the pro-[Oslo Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_Accords "Oslo Accords") rally [Yitzhak Rabin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yitzhak_Rabin "Yitzhak Rabin") attended right before [he was assassinated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Yitzhak_Rabin "Assassination of Yitzhak Rabin"), as it symbolized "the extreme rejection of democracy".
