# Hasan Piker

**Hasan Doğan Piker** (born July 25, 1991), known online as **HasanAbi**, is an American [left-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-wing_politics "Left-wing politics") political commentator, [Twitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_(service) "Twitch (service)") [streamer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_streamer "Online streamer"), and [influencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influencer "Influencer").

He began streaming on Twitch in March 2018, while working at *[The Young Turks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Young_Turks "The Young Turks")* (*TYT*), leaving the network in 2020 to focus full-time on his streaming career. His broadcasts combine current affairs commentary, political debates, and international news with gameplay and reaction content. As of 2026,[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hasan_Piker&action=edit) Piker's channel ranks among the platform's [most-subscribed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-subscribed_Twitch_channels "List of most-subscribed Twitch channels").

Piker has been called a prominent and controversial voice of the [American Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Left "American Left") in digital media, using his platform to comment on U.S. politics and foreign policy, particularly his [criticism of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_Israel "Criticism of Israel") and the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war"). He has also engaged in offline political mobilization, campaigning for progressive candidates.

## Early life and education

Hasan Doğan Piker was born on July 25, 1991 in [New Brunswick, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Brunswick,_New_Jersey "New Brunswick, New Jersey"), to [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people "Turkish people") Muslim parents. He has said that he grew up mostly in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul "Istanbul") and [Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara "Ankara"), adding that he was surrounded by Muslim culture. He describes himself today as [culturally Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Muslim "Cultural Muslim") but not an avid follower of the faith. In Turkey, Piker attended the private [TED Ankara Koleji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TED_Ankara_Koleji "TED Ankara Koleji"), then a public school, and finally [Bilkent Laboratory and International School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilkent_Laboratory_and_International_School "Bilkent Laboratory and International School").

According to Piker, his father's family emigrated to Turkey from [Thessaloniki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thessaloniki "Thessaloniki") and [Crete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crete "Crete"). His father, Mehmet Behçet Piker, is a [political scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_science "Political science") and economist who was a board member and vice president of the Turkish financial conglomerate [Sabancı Holding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabanc%C4%B1_Holding "Sabancı Holding") and is a founding member of Turkey's [conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatism_in_Turkey "Conservatism in Turkey") [Future Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_Party_(Turkey) "Future Party (Turkey)"). His mother, Ülker Sedef Piker ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names "Birth name") Uygur), is an [art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_history "Art history") and [architectural historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architectural_historian "Architectural historian") who teaches at the [New Jersey Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_Institute_of_Technology "New Jersey Institute of Technology"). His uncle, [Cenk Uygur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenk_Uygur "Cenk Uygur"), is a political commentator and co-founder of The Young Turks (TYT), a left-wing news network.

After graduating, Piker returned to the United States and attended the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami "University of Miami"), then transferred to [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University "Rutgers University"), where he joined the [Theta Delta Chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_Delta_Chi "Theta Delta Chi") fraternity. In 2013, he graduated *[cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors#United_States "Latin honors")* from Rutgers with a double major in [political science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_science "Political science") and [communication studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communication_studies "Communication studies").

## Career

### 2013–2019: *The Young Turks* and early Twitch career

During his senior year of college in 2013, Piker [interned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internship "Internship") for *The Young Turks.* The network's ad sales and business department hired him after he graduated. Piker said this would make him a "[nepo baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepo_baby "Nepo baby")" but believed "those types of advantages don't matter" in "a perfect society". He asked to host the show when a fill-in was needed and later became a host and producer.

In 2016, Piker created and hosted *The Breakdown*, a TYT Network video series that aired on [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook "Facebook") and presented left-leaning political analyses targeted, at the time, at [millennial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennials "Millennials") supporters of presidential candidate [Bernie Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Sanders "Bernie Sanders"). Piker also contributed political content to *[HuffPost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HuffPost "HuffPost")* from 2016 to 2018.

Piker started streaming on [Twitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_(service) "Twitch (service)") on March 23, 2018, while working at TYT. Piker said he shifted his attention from Facebook to Twitch to reach a younger audience and because of what he saw as a preponderance of [right-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-wing_politics "Right-wing politics") commentators on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube") and a lack of leftist representation among streamers. He became a popular [left-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-wing_politics "Left-wing politics") political commentator and was invited to appear on [KTTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTTV "KTTV")'s *The Issue Is* and the political podcast *[Chapo Trap House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapo_Trap_House "Chapo Trap House")*. Piker also streams gameplay and commentary on video games. Sometimes he [role-plays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role-playing "Role-playing") as "Hank Pecker", an ethno-political caricature of a "right-wing [redneck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redneck "Redneck")".

During a Twitch stream in August 2019, Piker criticized [American foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_policy_of_the_United_States "Foreign policy of the United States") and made controversial comments about the [September 11 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11_attacks "September 11 attacks"), including "America deserved 9/11." Piker called his comments [satirical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satire "Satire") and said U.S. foreign policy made an event like 9/11 possible but acknowledged that he should have used "more precise" language. Piker received a short-term ban for his comments.

### 2020–2025: Twitch and political commentary

In January 2020, Piker left TYT to focus on his career as a Twitch streamer, becoming further established on the platform. His livestreams usually include discussions of current affairs and lifestyle stories, playing video games, reacting to memes and media clips, and interacting with his viewers. Piker has mobilized his followers to '[ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter_usage#Ratio "Twitter usage")' opponents, including conservative influencers and Republican politicians. According to researchers, "Piker's streams provide many young people with a sense of community, acceptance, place" and "figures like Piker have a critical role to play in moving young people away from both the '[red pill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_pill_and_blue_pill "Red pill and blue pill")'—online right-wing and misogynist discourse—and the '[black pill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incel "Incel")'—online discourses of hopelessness and nihilism."

In October 2020, U.S. representative [Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria_Ocasio-Cortez "Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez") collaborated with Piker and fellow Twitch streamer [Pokimane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pokimane "Pokimane") to organize a stream of Ocasio-Cortez playing the popular multiplayer game *[Among Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Us "Among Us")* for the "[Get out the vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_out_the_vote "Get out the vote")" initiative for the then upcoming [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election "2020 United States presidential election"). The stream aired the next day, featuring both Ocasio-Cortez and U.S. Representative [Ilhan Omar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilhan_Omar "Ilhan Omar") playing the game with Piker and other popular Twitch streamers, reaching a total concurrent viewership of almost 700,000.

During the first [2020 United States presidential debate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_debates#September_29_presidential_debate_(Case_Western_Reserve_University) "2020 United States presidential debates") in September, over 125,000 viewers watched Piker's commentary on the stream, the highest viewership for the debate on Twitch. Piker's stream covering the results of the [2020 United States presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election "2020 United States presidential election") peaked at 230,000 concurrent viewers and was the sixth most-watched source of election coverage on YouTube or Twitch, comprising 4.9% of the market share. He was the most watched Twitch streamer during the election week; his 80 hours of streams were viewed for a cumulative 6.8 million hours by an average of 75,000 concurrent viewers. Piker's stream reached a new high of 231,000 viewers during the [January 6 United States Capitol attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_6_United_States_Capitol_attack "January 6 United States Capitol attack").

Since 2021, Piker has hosted the podcast *Fear&* (formerly *Fear&[Malding](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/malding "wikt:malding")*) alongside his friend and fellow Twitch streamer [Will Neff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Neff "Will Neff"). Streamers [QTCinderella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QTCinderella "QTCinderella") and [AustinShow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AustinShow "AustinShow") joined the podcast as co-hosts in 2022. From September 2021 to October 2023, Piker also co-hosted the left-leaning political podcast *[Leftovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leftovers_(podcast) "Leftovers (podcast)")* with [Ethan Klein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H3h3Productions "H3h3Productions").

In December 2021, Piker was banned from Twitch for a week after using the slur "[cracker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cracker_(term) "Cracker (term)")" in reference to white people, after two of his chat moderators were banned for using it. He argued the term should not be considered a slur since a person using it is "powerless" and would be "doing it as someone who has been historically oppressed blowing off steam." A paper in *[New Media & Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Media_%26_Society "New Media & Society")* criticized Twitch for punishing Piker while epithets aimed at minority groups were prevalent on the platform.

Piker's YouTube channel, which features highlights of his streams, reached over 1,000,000 subscribers in 2022.

Piker was given credentialed access and livestreamed at the [2024 Democratic National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Democratic_National_Convention "2024 Democratic National Convention"), and his [2024 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election "2024 United States presidential election") night coverage stream had 7.5 million viewers.

In February 2025, Piker said that if Republicans "cared about Medicare fraud or Medicaid fraud, [they] would kill [Rick Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Scott "Rick Scott")", in a reference to Scott's involvement in Medicare and Medicaid fraud when he was the CEO of Columbia/[HCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HCA_Healthcare "HCA Healthcare"). Twitch banned Piker for 24 hours in March for the comment. In May, Twitch banned Piker for 24 hours for "improper handling of terrorist propaganda" after he showed a manifesto allegedly by Elias Rodriguez, the perpetrator of the [2025 Capital Jewish Museum shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Capital_Jewish_Museum_shooting "2025 Capital Jewish Museum shooting").

In May 2025, Piker was detained for around two hours by [U.S. Customs and Border Protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Customs_and_Border_Protection "U.S. Customs and Border Protection") at [O'Hare International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O%27Hare_International_Airport "O'Hare International Airport") in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago") after returning from a trip to France. According to Piker, the officials who questioned him during his detention knew who he was and asked him detailed questions about his views on Donald Trump, Israel, [Houthi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthis "Houthis") rebels, [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas "Hamas"), and his Twitch bans. Piker believed he was detained due to his political views and that his detention was part of the Trump administration's intimidation tactics to silence political opposition. Assistant Secretary of the [United States Department of Homeland Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Homeland_Security "United States Department of Homeland Security") [Tricia McLaughlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricia_McLaughlin_(spokeswoman) "Tricia McLaughlin (spokeswoman)") accused Piker of "lying for likes" and said it was a routine inspection that any traveler could be subjected to.

#### Fundraisers

During the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine "Russian invasion of Ukraine"), Piker, in partnership with [CARE International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CARE_International "CARE International"), raised over $200,000 for Ukrainian [humanitarian aid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanitarian_aid "Humanitarian aid") while playing *[Elden Ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elden_Ring "Elden Ring")*, with an average of over 70,000 people watching his coverage of the conflict.

In the aftermath of the [2023 Turkey–Syria earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Turkey%E2%80%93Syria_earthquake "2023 Turkey–Syria earthquake"), Piker organized a fundraiser that was also contributed to and promoted by other streamers and content creators, including [Jacksepticeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksepticeye "Jacksepticeye"), [Valkyrae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valkyrae "Valkyrae"), [Ludwig Ahgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Ahgren "Ludwig Ahgren"), and [IShowSpeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IShowSpeed "IShowSpeed"). As of February 10[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Hasan_Piker&action=edit), the fundraiser had raised over $1,200,000 for charities such as CARE International's Turkish and Syrian branches, as well as two Turkish [NGOs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NGO "NGO"): the [AKUT Search and Rescue Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AKUT_Search_and_Rescue_Association "AKUT Search and Rescue Association"), and Ahbap, which was founded by Turkish musician [Haluk Levent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haluk_Levent "Haluk Levent").

During the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war"), Piker's charity drive on his channel had raised over $1,000,000 as of October 21, 2023, for the [Palestine Children's Relief Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Children%27s_Relief_Fund "Palestine Children's Relief Fund"), [American Near East Refugee Aid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Near_East_Refugee_Aid "American Near East Refugee Aid"), [Medical Aid for Palestinians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_Aid_for_Palestinians "Medical Aid for Palestinians"), and the [Palestine Red Crescent Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Red_Crescent_Society "Palestine Red Crescent Society").

### 2025–present: Campaign activity

In 2025, Piker began campaigning for political candidates. Piker has appeared with political candidates during campaigns that sought support from popular internet personalities to reach voters online. He has also supported candidates online. Piker said he was "very clear" that he was not "some socialist kingmaker", believing the media was not "that powerful".

In the [2025 New York City mayoral election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_York_City_mayoral_election "2025 New York City mayoral election"), Piker expressed support for [Zohran Mamdani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zohran_Mamdani "Zohran Mamdani"), and Mamdani appeared on Piker's stream. During the campaign, Mamdani's political opponents used Piker's past remarks, especially his 2019 comment that "America deserved [9/11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11_attacks "September 11 attacks")", in attacks against Mamdani, including in a televised advertisement aired by a pro-[Andrew Cuomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Cuomo "Andrew Cuomo") [super PAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_PAC "Super PAC"). Mamdani disavowed Piker's comments during a mayoral debate, calling them "objectionable and reprehensible".

During a March 2026 livestream, Piker helped raise more than $56,000 for congressional candidate Oliver Larkin. *Wired* called this an example of content creators using their online influence for direct fundraising.

Piker rallied in Philadelphia with state representative [Chris Rabb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Rabb "Chris Rabb") during the 2026 primary for [Pennsylvania's 3rd congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania%27s_3rd_congressional_district "Pennsylvania's 3rd congressional district"), which Rabb went on to win in May. Piker also campaigned for and hosted [Adam Hamawy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Hamawy "Adam Hamawy") on stream during his run in [New Jersey's 12th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey%27s_12th_congressional_district "New Jersey's 12th congressional district"). Hamany won his primary in June. Another congressional candidate Piker endorsed, hosted and campaigned for, [Saikat Chakrabarti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saikat_Chakrabarti "Saikat Chakrabarti"), finished third in the [nonpartisan primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonpartisan_primary "Nonpartisan primary") for [California's 11th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California%27s_11th_congressional_district "California's 11th congressional district"), failing to advance to the general election.

In June 2026, Piker was refused entry to the UK alongside Cenk Uygur, which he argued was "due to my criticism of Israel". The UK [Home Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Office "Home Office") said the decision was due to Piker's "potential risk" and "may not be conducive to the public good". Free speech activists criticized the decision. He had been invited to speak at the [Oxford Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Union "Oxford Union") and [SXSW London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SXSW_London "SXSW London"). The Oxford Union said Piker would deliver his speech via live-stream. Piker said the decision showed the "fascist" direction the "Western world" was taking.

Ahead of the [2026 Democratic primaries for the House of Representatives in New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_New_York "2026 United States House of Representatives elections in New York"), Piker campaigned at a June event in [Bushwick, Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushwick,_Brooklyn "Bushwick, Brooklyn"), for [Claire Valdez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Valdez "Claire Valdez") and [Darializa Avila Chevalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darializa_Avila_Chevalier "Darializa Avila Chevalier"), who went on to win the Democratic nominations in New York's [7th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York%27s_7th_congressional_district "New York's 7th congressional district") and [13th districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York%27s_13th_congressional_district "New York's 13th congressional district"), respectively. That same month Piker hosted [Melat Kiros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melat_Kiros "Melat Kiros") on his stream ahead of her primary challenge to [Diana DeGette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_DeGette "Diana DeGette") in [Colorado's 1st congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado%27s_1st_congressional_district "Colorado's 1st congressional district"). Kiros defeated DeGette in [her primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Colorado "2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Colorado").

During the [2026 Democratic Party primaries in Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_Senate_election_in_Michigan "2026 United States Senate election in Michigan"), Piker campaigned for Democratic candidate [Abdul El-Sayed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_El-Sayed "Abdul El-Sayed"). Two of El-Sayed's opponents, [Mallory McMorrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallory_McMorrow "Mallory McMorrow") and [Haley Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haley_Stevens "Haley Stevens"), criticized Piker's involvement based on past remarks, particularly about the [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict "Israeli–Palestinian conflict"). Democratic State Representative [Carrie Rheingans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Rheingans "Carrie Rheingans") chose not to participate in the event with Piker while continuing to support El-Sayed. Piker's involvement in the Michigan primary became a source of division within the Democratic Party. El-Sayed won the primary. Piker also campaigned with [Donavan McKinney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donavan_McKinney "Donavan McKinney"), who defeated incumbent [Shri Thanedar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shri_Thanedar "Shri Thanedar") in the August primary for [Michigan's 13th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan%27s_13th_congressional_district "Michigan's 13th congressional district"). That month, former Missouri congresswoman [Cori Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cori_Bush "Cori Bush"), who had campaigned with Piker at a [May Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_Day "May Day") rally in [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis "St. Louis"), lost her bid for the Democratic nomination in [Missouri's 1st congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri%27s_1st_congressional_district "Missouri's 1st congressional district") to her successor, [Wesley Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Bell "Wesley Bell").

## Views and political beliefs

Piker has been described as [socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist "Socialist"), [leftist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leftist "Leftist"), progressive, and [far-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far-left "Far-left"). He has been called a controversial political commentator. Piker describes himself as a socialist. Piker is a frequent critic of U.S. mainstream politics, Israel, and the [Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war "Gaza war").

In a 2025 *New York Times* interview, Piker described himself as a [Marxist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxist "Marxist"), socialist, and leftist. In a later interview with *The New York Times*, when asked whether his preferred alternative to liberalism was Marxist, Piker said he did not have a "perfect solution", but that moving toward socialism would be a "wonderful start". Piker has rejected characterizations of himself as a [Maoist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maoist "Maoist"), saying that both Maoists and [Marxist-Leninists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxist-Leninists "Marxist-Leninists") have criticized his political views.

Piker has cited his upbringing in Turkey under the [premiership of Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premiership_of_Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan "Premiership of Recep Tayyip Erdoğan") as an influence on both his left-wing views and willingness to speak out about them. He has mentioned [Amy Goodman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Goodman "Amy Goodman"), [Jon Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Stewart "Jon Stewart"), [Aaron McGruder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_McGruder "Aaron McGruder"), and [Bill Burr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Burr "Bill Burr") as influences on the content and style of his streams.

### American politics

[Andrew Marantz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Marantz "Andrew Marantz") of *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker "The New Yorker")* described Piker as [anti-Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Trump "Anti-Trump") but "hardly a loyal Democrat". Instead, Marantz classified Piker as an "old-school leftist", not a liberal, who is critical of the "American empire". Piker said his goal was to push the Democratic Party to be more progressive. *[Intelligencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligencer_(website) "Intelligencer (website)")* called Piker "the [AOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria_Ocasio-Cortez "Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez") of Twitch".

Piker has advocated for [workplace democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workplace_democracy "Workplace democracy"),[*[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")*] [universal health care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_health_care "Universal health care"), [LGBTQ+ rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_rights_in_the_United_States "LGBT rights in the United States"), and [gun control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_control "Gun control"). Piker has argued that many conservatives have been "socially conditioned" by pro-corporate and pro-imperial media and politics, and that they can be won over to the Democrats by offering generous social-welfare programs such as [Medicare for All](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicare_for_All "Medicare for All") without first challenging their socially conservative beliefs. He has spoken in favor of [sewer socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewer_socialism "Sewer socialism"), a brand of socialism that wins over the population by building munipical-owned infrastructure. Piker has cited the [Tennessee Valley Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Valley_Authority "Tennessee Valley Authority") and the provision of broadband internet by [EPB of Chattanooga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPB_of_Chattanooga "EPB of Chattanooga") as something that should be rolled out across the United States.

Piker supported the [presidential primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_presidential_primary "United States presidential primary") campaigns of [Bernie Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Sanders "Bernie Sanders") in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Sanders_2016_presidential_campaign "Bernie Sanders 2016 presidential campaign") and [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Sanders_2020_presidential_campaign "Bernie Sanders 2020 presidential campaign"), and has been an outspoken critic of both the [Democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") and [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States) "Republican Party (United States)") Parties. Piker opposed [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump")'s [travel ban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_travel_ban "Trump travel ban") in 2017. After [Luigi Mangione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Mangione "Luigi Mangione")—the suspect in the [killing of Brian Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Brian_Thompson "Killing of Brian Thompson"), the CEO of [UnitedHealthcare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UnitedHealthcare "UnitedHealthcare")—was arrested, Piker "celebrat[ed] Mangione as 'hotter than me' and [spoke] in generally exalted terms about 'the [propaganda of the deed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propaganda_of_the_deed "Propaganda of the deed")'" on his stream but avoided breaking Twitch's [terms of service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terms_of_service "Terms of service") rules about glorification of violence. Citing [Friedrich Engels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Engels "Friedrich Engels"), Piker later said that Thompson had committed [social murder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_murder "Social murder").

### Israel and Palestine

Piker has been described as [anti-Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Zionist "Anti-Zionist") and has criticized Israel and Zionism. Piker has called himself "an outspoken advocate for Palestinian emancipation".

In May 2024, Piker described allegations of Hamas carrying out [sexual violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_and_gender-based_violence_in_the_October_7_attacks "Sexual and gender-based violence in the October 7 attacks") during the [October 7 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_7_attacks "October 7 attacks") as "rape fantasies" and "rape hallucinations". Piker has also said, "It doesn't matter if rape happened on October 7th. It doesn't change the dynamic for me". In an April 2026 interview with *[The New Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic")*, Piker said of the quotation: "I think it's very clear what I was saying. I was debating someone at the time about there being sexual violence that occurred on October 7th. And I said sexual violence does take place in situations like this, and there was definitely testimony of sexual violence that had taken place amongst the hostages. But that doesn't change the dynamic of Israel committing a genocide against the Palestinians, and my opposing that genocide".

In November 2024, Congressman [Ritchie Torres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritchie_Torres "Ritchie Torres") criticized Twitch for alleged "[amplification of antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalization_of_antisemitism "Normalization of antisemitism")" and called for an investigation into Piker. A campaign by various commentators pressured advertisers to leave Twitch due to the alleged [platforming of antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism_on_social_media "Antisemitism on social media"), including Piker's political commentary on the conflict. Both Torres and the pressure campaign posted clips of Piker's channel they alleged were antisemitic. Piker said the clips were taken out of context and showed him criticising Israel, not Jewish people. Because of the controversy, some advertisers pulled advertisements from Twitch.

Piker called some far-right [Haredi Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haredi_Judaism "Haredi Judaism") in Israel "inbred". He later said the term was "a pejorative against ethno-religious and racial supremacists of all different varieties. It has nothing to do with Judaism". In another interview, he said he regretted using it in reference to [ultra-Orthodox Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra-Orthodox_Jews "Ultra-Orthodox Jews").

In January 2026, Piker said "Hamas is a thousand times better than a fascist settler colonial [apartheid state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_apartheid "Israeli apartheid")" (referring to Israel) and that he "would vote for Hamas over Israel every single time", statements he stood by in April 2026 during interviews with *[Pod Save America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pod_Save_America "Pod Save America")* and *The New Republic*. He told *The New Republic*, "Hamas didn't do a genocide. Israel did", referring to the [Gaza genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_genocide "Gaza genocide").

In August 2026, Piker warned that associating American Jews with support for Israel could lead to violent backlash against them. Piker claimed that pro-Israel Jewish leaders conflate Jewish identity with support for Israel, and that anger at Israel can fuel antisemitism and endanger American Jews. The comments caused some division within the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") amid the [2026 Michigan Senate election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Michigan_Senate_election "2026 Michigan Senate election"), with candidate [Abdul El-Sayed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_El-Sayed "Abdul El-Sayed") said to have "distanced himself" from Piker amid the fallout.

### Authoritarianism

In 2021, Piker said he became interested in politics after [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan "Recep Tayyip Erdoğan") sued a cartoonist for depicting him as a cat, which Piker considered "suppression of freedom". *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")* described Piker as "fighting back" against authoritarianism through livestreaming. In the profile, Piker said [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump "Donald Trump") could "take out or erode" constitutional protections, including "due process, birthright citizenship, and the First Amendment", before rebuilding the legal system to advance what Piker called a "white nativist agenda". In an October 2025 interview with *[Morning Edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morning_Edition "Morning Edition")*, Piker said he worried that if the Trump administration succeeded in suppressing speech it deemed unacceptable, the United States would "end up in an authoritarian nightmare" in which "one of the last bastions of liberalism is eroded". In April 2026, during a debate at the [Yale Political Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_Political_Union "Yale Political Union"), Piker positioned himself against the "dying American Empire". He said capitalism had built things worth keeping, but that those things had been built through labor and the benefits had not been evenly distributed. Piker concluded, "If a dictatorship is inevitable, I'd rather have it be a [dictatorship of the proletariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dictatorship_of_the_proletariat "Dictatorship of the proletariat")."

Regarding international affairs, writers in *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic_(magazine) "The Atlantic (magazine)")*, *[The Dispatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dispatch "The Dispatch")* and *[New Lines Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Lines_Magazine "New Lines Magazine")* have characterized Piker as sympathizing with left-wing authoritarian governments, saying that he expressed admiration for China as his preferred model of socialism, likened China's absorption of [Tibet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibet "Tibet") to the [Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_(American_Civil_War) "Union (American Civil War)")'s defeat of the [Confederacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America "Confederate States of America"), called [Uyghur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uyghurs "Uyghurs") [detention facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinjiang_internment_camps "Xinjiang internment camps") "re-education camps", called [Mao Zedong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Zedong "Mao Zedong") "one of the great leaders of this world", and called the [collapse of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapse_of_the_Soviet_Union "Collapse of the Soviet Union") "one of the greatest catastrophes of the 20th century". Both *The Atlantic* and *the Dispatch* have also said that Piker's worldview leans toward "broad sympathy for China, Cuba, Russia, and authoritarianism in general", while *[New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(magazine) "New York (magazine)")* has stated that Piker holds "romantic and credulous attitudes about repressive regimes abroad". *[Vox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_(website) "Vox (website)")* argued that the best label for Piker's international relations stance is [campism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campism "Campism") (a position that judges foreign movements and regimes more by their degree of hostility toward the West than by their values), and outlined a series of positions Piker holds that point to campism, such as his stated "lack of issue" with [Hezbollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah "Hezbollah") and support for Mao despite the [Great Leap Forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Leap_Forward "Great Leap Forward")'s death toll, something *Vox* set against Piker's stated position that regimes should be judged by how many civilians they kill.

### Russian invasion of Ukraine

During the February prelude to the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine "2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine"), Piker said Russia would not attack. Piker apologized, saying he "didn't think a regional power would act so irrationally. I've admitted my mistakes. I got things wrong." He has said that the U.S. paved the way to the invasion and compared Russia's invasion with the U.S.'s history of invasions.

### China

In November 2025, Piker visited China and appeared on the state-run [China Global Television Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Global_Television_Network "China Global Television Network"), saying he wanted to see what the U.S. could "adopt and emulate" from China. He called the trip a "dream come true". During a livestream at [Tiananmen Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiananmen_Square "Tiananmen Square"), police briefly questioned him after a ceremony, an interaction that attracted media attention. During the trip, Piker displayed a copy of [Mao Zedong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Zedong "Mao Zedong")'s *[Little Red Book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotations_from_Chairman_Mao_Tse-tung "Quotations from Chairman Mao Tse-tung")*, which he said he had received as a gift and called "really, really special".

In a December 2025 interview, Piker said, "Regardless of its flaws, and repressive attitudes towards certain groups and whatnot, they have done a phenomenal achievement in greatly improving the material conditions of the average Chinese person".

In a September 2026 interview with *Axios*, Piker said the country is the closest government model he would like to see adopted in the U.S. He said he was concerned that Chinese children could have better living conditions than U.S. children in the next decade or two.

## Reception

Piker has been called Twitch's "*de facto* political commentator" who uses an unabashedly ideological framing. With about 3 million Twitch followers, he has emerged as a prominent commentator among young American leftists since 2025, largely due to his critiques of the Trump administration. Outlets dedicated to [video game culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_culture "Video game culture") and [youth culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_culture "Youth culture") have covered Piker's streams favorably. In particular, journalists have noted his ability to "combine information and entertainment", and to approach left-wing political coverage in a way that is relatable and accessible to Twitch viewers, who may feel out of touch with [cable news](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cable_news "Cable news"). Some authors also cite Piker's vulgar, animated style of expression and physical appearance as notable factors in his popularity. Piker has called his style of broadcasting "[agitative propaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agitprop "Agitprop")" and "[Rush Limbaugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_Limbaugh "Rush Limbaugh") for [Zoomers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gen_Z "Gen Z")". Several publications have compared him to radio "[shock jocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_jock "Shock jock")". Some sources have called Piker a member of the [dirtbag left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirtbag_left "Dirtbag left"), a [left-wing populist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-wing_populist "Left-wing populist") style of politics known for vulgarity.

A 2022 chapter in *Cultures of Authenticity* discussed Piker and *The Young Turks* as case studies of alternative political commentary. The authors said Piker participates with his audience in an ambivalent "post-authentic" subculture that uses antagonism, jokes, and irony to communicate political views indirectly. Both [Andrew Marantz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Marantz "Andrew Marantz") of *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker "The New Yorker")* and [Michelle Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Goldberg "Michelle Goldberg") of the *[New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times "New York Times")* have called Piker "[jejune](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/jejune "wikt:jejune")". Marantz wrote that Piker was always "trying to reduce any societal problem to a one-word culprit, usually 'capitalism' or 'imperialism'", an assessment Simon van Zuylen-Wood of *[New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(magazine) "New York (magazine)")* called "true". Van Zuylen-Wood also wrote that Piker "refuses to accept that not every opinion he disagrees with is shaped by someone's false consciousness".

In July 2024, Alex Mahadevan of the [Poynter Institute for Media Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poynter_Institute "Poynter Institute") cited Piker as an example of the "online chattering class" who do not hold themselves to [journalistic ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalistic_ethics "Journalistic ethics"), in contrast to their peers in [old media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_media "Old media"). A September 2024 article in the academic journal *[Digital Journalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Journalism_(journal) "Digital Journalism (journal)")* discussed a similar theme, calling Piker emblematic of an emerging "newsfluencer" class ([influencers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influencers "Influencers") who mainly cover news) with a tendency toward "problematic behavior" that conflicts with journalistic objectivity. In April 2024, a contributor to the same journal wrote that Piker is familiar with journalistic mechanics such as sourcing, framing, and objectivity but rejects these as methods used for "manufacturing consent for elites". Instead, Piker mainly sources his information from social media, often supplied to him by his audience. *[The Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post "The Washington Post")* has also categorized Piker as a news-based influencer who does not feel bound to journalistic objectivity.

Marantz wrote that Piker is controversial on both the political left and right: "When Piker is criticized by the right, it's usually for soft-pedaling the brutality of Hamas, or the Houthis, or the [Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Party "Chinese Communist Party")... By the left, he is more likely to be dismissed as a limousine socialist who lives in a $2.7-million house." In *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")* magazine, [Kieran Press-Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Press-Reynolds "Kieran Press-Reynolds") wrote that Piker is also controversial among liberals and the Democratic Party, which Piker attributes to his criticism of Israel. Marantz also wrote that Piker's tolerance of controversy and appeal to young men made him an ideal "[Joe Rogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Rogan "Joe Rogan") of the left". Van Zuylen-Wood has written that Piker is not a "left-wing Rogan, a genial and curious interviewer with unpredictable leanings" but a "left-wing [Charlie Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Kirk "Charlie Kirk"), a nonstop polemicist engaged in winning actual power".

## Awards and nominations

Gaming website *[Kotaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotaku "Kotaku")* selected Piker as one of its "Gamers of the Year" in 2020, calling him a major figure in the mainstreaming of political commentary on Twitch, a platform that in the past was seen as discouraging political discussion. Piker won a [Streamy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streamy_Awards "Streamy Awards") in the News category at the [10th annual awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Streamy_Awards "10th Streamy Awards") in 2020 and was nominated again in the same category for the [2021 event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11th_Streamy_Awards "11th Streamy Awards"). In 2022, Piker was [nominated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Streamy_Awards "12th Streamy Awards") in the categories News, Just Chatting, and Streamer of the Year, winning the News award for the second time and being nominated for three consecutive years. Piker was one of the [Time 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100") creators for 2025. He was number 3 on *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")*'s list of the most influential creators of 2026.
