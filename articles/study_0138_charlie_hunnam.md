# Charlie Hunnam

**Charles Matthew Hunnam** ([/ˈhʌnəm/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English"); born 10 April 1980) is an English actor. He portrayed [Jax Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jax_Teller "Jax Teller") in the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") drama series *[Sons of Anarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sons_of_Anarchy "Sons of Anarchy")* (2008–2014), for which he was twice nominated for the [Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actor in a Drama Series").

Hunnam has been featured as the lead in numerous films, including *[Green Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Street_(film) "Green Street (film)")* (2005), *[Nicholas Nickleby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Nickleby_(2002_film) "Nicholas Nickleby (2002 film)")* (2002), *[Pacific Rim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Rim_(film) "Pacific Rim (film)")* (2013), *[Triple Frontier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_Frontier_(film) "Triple Frontier (film)")* (2019). He has also portrayed [Percy Fawcett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Fawcett "Percy Fawcett") in *[The Lost City of Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_City_of_Z_(film) "The Lost City of Z (film)")* (2016) and [Arthur Pendragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Pendragon "Arthur Pendragon") in *[King Arthur: Legend of the Sword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Arthur:_Legend_of_the_Sword "King Arthur: Legend of the Sword")* (2017).

Hunnam's notable television projects include the [Channel 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_4 "Channel 4") drama *[Queer as Folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queer_as_Folk_(British_TV_series) "Queer as Folk (British TV series)")* (1999–2000), the [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company "Fox Broadcasting Company") series *[Undeclared](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undeclared "Undeclared")* (2001–2002) and the [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+") series *[Shantaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantaram_(TV_series) "Shantaram (TV series)")* (2022). In 2025, he portrayed the [titular character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Gein "Ed Gein") in *[Monster: The Ed Gein Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster:_The_Ed_Gein_Story "Monster: The Ed Gein Story"),* and in 2026, he portrayed Andrew Borden in *[Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster:_The_Lizzie_Borden_Story "Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story"),* the third and fourth season of the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") [true crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_crime "True crime") [anthology series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology_series "Anthology series") [*Monster*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_(American_TV_series) "Monster (American TV series)"), for which he received nominations for a [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards"), a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award"), an [Actor Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Awards "Actor Awards"), and a [Critics' Choice Television Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Awards "Critics' Choice Television Awards").

## Early life

Charles Matthew Hunnam was born in [Newcastle upon Tyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne "Newcastle upon Tyne") on 10 April 1980, the son of Jane Bell, a ballet dancer and business owner, and William Hunnam. One of his grandmothers was a portrait artist. He has said that his mother "did a very good job" of being a single parent. He has an older brother named William and two younger half brothers on his mother's side named Oliver and Christian.[*[non-primary source needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:No_original_research#Primary,_secondary_and_tertiary_sources "Wikipedia:No original research")*]

At age 12, when his mother remarried, the family moved to [Melmerby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melmerby,_Cumbria "Melmerby, Cumbria") in [Cumbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbria "Cumbria"). He attended [Queen Elizabeth Grammar School, Penrith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_Grammar_School,_Penrith "Queen Elizabeth Grammar School, Penrith"), from which he was later expelled. During his adolescence, he played [rugby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_football "Rugby football") and fought with his classmates. After that, instead of going to university, he decided to go to the [Cumbria College of Art and Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbria_College_of_Art_and_Design "Cumbria College of Art and Design") in [Carlisle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlisle "Carlisle") (now part of the [University of Cumbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cumbria "University of Cumbria")) to study performing arts. He graduated with a dual degree in [film theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_theory "Film theory") and [film history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_film "History of film") with a side in [performing arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Performing_arts "Performing arts"). He planned to write and direct his own films.

## Career

### 1997–2007

Hunnam was discovered at the age of 17 in a branch of [JD Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JD_Sports "JD Sports") on [Christmas Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_Eve "Christmas Eve"), while playing around with his brother during a trip to buy shoes. A production manager for the Newcastle-based teen drama *[Byker Grove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byker_Grove "Byker Grove")* approached Hunnam and he was later cast in his first role as Jason for three episodes of the show. He also had a brief modelling career where he did a photo shoot for [Kangol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kangol "Kangol") Caps and then decided modelling was not for him. Hunnam's first major role came at age 18 when he was cast by [Russell T Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_T_Davies "Russell T Davies") as 15-year-old schoolboy Nathan Maloney in Davies' [Channel 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_4 "Channel 4") drama *[Queer as Folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queer_as_Folk_(British_TV_series) "Queer as Folk (British TV series)")*. He followed this up with his role as Daz in the film *[Whatever Happened to Harold Smith?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whatever_Happened_to_Harold_Smith%3F "Whatever Happened to Harold Smith?")* (1999) and then moved to the United States. Hunnam was heavily considered by [George Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas "George Lucas") for the role of [Anakin Skywalker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker "Anakin Skywalker") in [Star Wars: Attack of the Clones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_II_%E2%80%93_Attack_of_the_Clones "Star Wars: Episode II – Attack of the Clones"), but following an awkward in-person interview with George Lucas and producers, ultimately [Hayden Christensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden_Christensen "Hayden Christensen") was cast in the role instead.

His career expanded to include a recurring role as Gregor Ryder in the [WB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_WB "The WB") series *[Young Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Americans_(TV_series) "Young Americans (TV series)")*. He then appeared in the short-lived [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company "Fox Broadcasting Company") series *[Undeclared](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undeclared "Undeclared")* as a British drama student called Lloyd Haythe. Despite critical acclaim, the series was cancelled after one season. Hunnam then appeared on the large screen in *[Abandon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abandon_(film) "Abandon (film)")* (2002), *[Nicholas Nickleby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Nickleby_(2002_film) "Nicholas Nickleby (2002 film)")* (2002), and *[Cold Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Mountain_(film) "Cold Mountain (film)")* (2003). Hunnam stated he did not wish to simply take any role offered to him, saying, "I have 60 years to make the money, but the choices I make in the next five years are really going to define my career."

This decision resulted in his return to the UK to take the lead role of Pete Dunham in the film *[Green Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Street_(film) "Green Street (film)")* (2005); however, his attempt at delivering a [Cockney accent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockney "Cockney") resulted in his inclusion in many critics' "worst accents in movie history" lists. Hunnam said his role as Patric, a member of "The Fishes" in *[Children of Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children_of_Men "Children of Men")* (2006), was the final part in his "trilogy of mad men": "I played the psycho in *Cold Mountain*, my character in *Green Street Hooligans* is fairly psychotic and now I've got this role."

### 2008–2012

From 2008 to 2014, Hunnam starred in *[Sons of Anarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sons_of_Anarchy "Sons of Anarchy")* as [Jackson "Jax" Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_%22Jax%22_Teller "Jackson \"Jax\" Teller") leader of [SAMCRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAMCRO "SAMCRO"), a prominent [motorcycle club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorcycle_club "Motorcycle club") set in the fictional town of Charming, California. Hunnam was cast after [Kurt Sutter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Sutter "Kurt Sutter"), the creator of the show, saw him in *[Green Street Hooligans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Street_Hooligans "Green Street Hooligans")*. His portrayal as Jax Teller has led Hunnam to receive a [Critics' Choice Television Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award "Critics' Choice Television Award") nomination, two [EWwy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EWwy_Award "EWwy Award") nominations for Best Lead Actor in a Drama series, and a [PAAFTJ Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-American_Association_of_Film_%26_Television_Journalists "Pan-American Association of Film & Television Journalists") nomination for Best Cast in a Drama Series.

In 2011, Hunnam played the role of Gavin Nichols in the philosophical drama/thriller *[The Ledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ledge_(film) "The Ledge (film)")* by [Matthew Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Chapman_(author) "Matthew Chapman (author)"). In 2012, he starred as the title character in the indie comedy *[3,2,1... Frankie Go Boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3,2,1..._Frankie_Go_Boom "3,2,1... Frankie Go Boom")* alongside his *Sons of Anarchy* co-star [Ron Perlman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Perlman "Ron Perlman"). Hunnam said he considered the day he filmed scenes with Perlman the best and funniest day of filming he's had in his career. He also appeared as Jay, an ex-boxer, in [Stefan Ruzowitzky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Ruzowitzky "Stefan Ruzowitzky")'s crime drama *[Deadfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadfall_(2012_film) "Deadfall (2012 film)")* (2012).

### 2013–2019

Hunnam starred as Raleigh Becket in [Guillermo del Toro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro "Guillermo del Toro")'s sci-fi film *[Pacific Rim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Rim_(film) "Pacific Rim (film)")*, which opened in July 2013 and grossed $411 million worldwide. It was announced on 2 September 2013 that Hunnam would play the lead role of Christian Grey in the [film adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifty_Shades_of_Grey_(film) "Fifty Shades of Grey (film)") of [E. L. James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._L._James "E. L. James")' novel *[Fifty Shades of Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifty_Shades_of_Grey "Fifty Shades of Grey")*. However, on 12 October 2013, [Universal Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Pictures "Universal Pictures") announced that Hunnam had withdrawn from the film due to conflicts with the schedule of his series *Sons of Anarchy*.

On 2 June 2014, Hunnam was awarded a [Huading Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huading_Awards "Huading Awards") for Best Global Emerging Actor, for his role as Raleigh in *Pacific Rim* due to the film performing well in Asian markets. Hunnam reunited with del Toro in the horror film *[Crimson Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimson_Peak "Crimson Peak")*, alongside [Mia Wasikowska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Wasikowska "Mia Wasikowska"), [Tom Hiddleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hiddleston "Tom Hiddleston"), and [Jessica Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Chastain "Jessica Chastain"). The film began shooting in February 2014 and was released on 16 October 2015.

Hunnam starred as geographer [Percy Fawcett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Fawcett "Percy Fawcett") in [James Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gray_(film_director) "James Gray (film director)")'s adaptation of author [David Grann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Grann "David Grann")'s 2009 book*, [The Lost City of Z: A Tale of Deadly Obsession in the Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_City_of_Z:_A_Tale_of_Deadly_Obsession_in_the_Amazon "The Lost City of Z: A Tale of Deadly Obsession in the Amazon")*. Gray dubbed his adventure drama *[The Lost City of Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_City_of_Z_(film) "The Lost City of Z (film)")*, which was filmed from August to October 2015. The film had its world premiere 15 October 2016 as the closing night selection at the [New York Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Film_Festival "New York Film Festival"). It was released in the United States on 14 April 2017. Hunnam played the eponymous title role in [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie")'s action-adventure film *[King Arthur: Legend of the Sword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Arthur:_Legend_of_the_Sword "King Arthur: Legend of the Sword")*, which was filmed between March and July 2015. It was released in May 2017. He next starred in [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie "Guy Ritchie")'s 2019 film *[The Gentlemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gentlemen_(2019_film) "The Gentlemen (2019 film)")* alongside [Matthew McConaughey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_McConaughey "Matthew McConaughey").

### 2020–present

Hunnam portrayed Charlie Waldo in the 2022 mystery/thriller *[Last Looks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Looks "Last Looks")* which also featured [Mel Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Gibson "Mel Gibson"). His first television series for [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV%2B "Apple TV+"), *[Shantaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantaram_(TV_series) "Shantaram (TV series)")*, premiered 14 October 2022. Hunnam led the cast of characters as Dale Conti/Lindsay "Linbaba" Ford. The series was based on [Gregory David Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_David_Roberts "Gregory David Roberts")' 2003 autobiographical novel.

On 9 February 2023, *[Deadline Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood "Deadline Hollywood")* reported that Hunnam joined [Zack Snyder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zack_Snyder "Zack Snyder")'s [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") project *[Rebel Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Moon "Rebel Moon")*. The project reunited Hunnam with *[King Arthur: Legend of the Sword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Arthur:_Legend_of_the_Sword "King Arthur: Legend of the Sword")* co-star [Djimon Hounsou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djimon_Hounsou "Djimon Hounsou"). The [epic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_film "Epic film") [space opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_opera "Space opera"), *[Rebel Moon – Part One: A Child of Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Moon "Rebel Moon")* had a limited theatrical release on 15 December 2023. On 21 December 2023, the film was released worldwide via Netflix's streaming service. *[Rebel Moon – Part Two: The Scargiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Moon_%E2%80%93_Part_Two:_The_Scargiver "Rebel Moon – Part Two: The Scargiver")* was released worldwide 14 April 2024 on Netflix.

In May 2024, Hunnam was cast in the lead role of Leo, "a brilliant master thief", for Amazon Prime Video's [adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_(TV_series) "Criminal (TV series)") of the comic series by [Ed Brubaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Brubaker "Ed Brubaker") and [Sean Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Phillips "Sean Phillips"), *[Criminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_(comics) "Criminal (comics)")*.

In October 2025, Hunnam portrayed convicted murderer and grave robber [Ed Gein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Gein "Ed Gein") in *[Monster: The Ed Gein Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster:_The_Ed_Gein_Story "Monster: The Ed Gein Story")*, the third season of the biographical crime drama anthology series [*Monster*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_(American_TV_series) "Monster (American TV series)"), created by [Ryan Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Murphy_(producer) "Ryan Murphy (producer)") and [Ian Brennan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Brennan_(writer) "Ian Brennan (writer)") for [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). Hunnam also served as an executive producer for the season. Hunnam reappeared in the fourth season of the same series, this time playing Andrew Borden, father of Lizzie Borden, who was accused of killing her parents in [Fall River, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_River,_Massachusetts "Fall River, Massachusetts").

## Personal life

Hunnam met actress Katharine Towne, daughter of actress [Julie Payne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Payne_(actress,_born_1940) "Julie Payne (actress, born 1940)") and filmmaker [Robert Towne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Towne "Robert Towne"), in 1999 when they both auditioned for roles on *[Dawson's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawson%27s_Creek "Dawson's Creek")*. After dating for three weeks, they married in [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas "Las Vegas"); they divorced in 2002. He dated model [Sophie Dahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Dahl "Sophie Dahl"), actress Stella Parker and film producer Georgina Townsley. He has been in a relationship with artist Morgana McNelis since 2005. He referred to McNelis as his wife in an interview in January 2026 at the Critic’s Choice Awards.

In 2016, he began training in [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu "Brazilian jiu-jitsu") under [Rigan Machado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigan_Machado "Rigan Machado"), and received his [blue belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu_ranking_system#Blue_belt "Brazilian jiu-jitsu ranking system") in October 2018.
