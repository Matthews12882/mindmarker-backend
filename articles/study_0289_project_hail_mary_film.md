# Project Hail Mary (film)

***Project Hail Mary*** is a 2026 [science fiction film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_film "Science fiction film") produced and directed by [Phil Lord and Christopher Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Lord_and_Christopher_Miller "Phil Lord and Christopher Miller") and written by [Drew Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Goddard "Drew Goddard"), based on the [2021 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary "Project Hail Mary") by [Andy Weir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Weir "Andy Weir"). It stars [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling "Ryan Gosling"), who also produced the film, as Ryland Grace, with [Sandra Hüller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_H%C3%BCller "Sandra Hüller"), [James Ortiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ortiz "James Ortiz"), and [Lionel Boyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Boyce "Lionel Boyce") in supporting roles. The story follows Ryland Grace, a middle school teacher who awakens aboard an interstellar spacecraft with no memory of how he got there.

Pre-production began in 2020, before Weir's novel was published. Filming took place throughout 2024 in the United Kingdom, both on location and on sets. Scenes aboard the spacecraft *Hail Mary* were filmed on a practical set that was constructed for the film.

*Project Hail Mary* premiered in London on March 9, 2026, and was released in the United States by [Amazon MGM Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_MGM_Studios "Amazon MGM Studios") on March 20, with [Sony Pictures Releasing International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Motion_Picture_Group#International_arrangements "Sony Pictures Motion Picture Group") releasing in other territories. It received positive reviews and was a commercial success, grossing over $685 million worldwide and becoming the [seventh-highest-grossing film of 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_film#Highest-grossing_films "2026 in film").

## Plot

In the near future, middle school teacher and former [molecular biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_biologist "Molecular biologist") Dr. Ryland Grace wakes from an [induced coma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Induced_coma "Induced coma") aboard the [interstellar spacecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") *Hail Mary*, suffering from amnesia. He learns that he is the sole survivor of a three-person crew travelling to the [Tau Ceti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau_Ceti "Tau Ceti") system, 11.9 [light-years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-year "Light-year") from Earth.

In flashbacks, Grace recalls that scientists discovered a "Petrova line" of [infrared light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared "Infrared") between the [Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun "Sun") and [Venus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus "Venus"). A dark substance in the line, dubbed "astrophage", was proliferating on the Sun, dimming it at a rate that would cause catastrophic [global cooling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_cooling "Global cooling") within thirty years. Eva Stratt, head of the Petrova Taskforce, recruited Grace because of his expertise in speculative [astrobiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrobiology "Astrobiology"). Grace discovered that astrophage samples consisted of [unicellular organisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicellular_organism "Unicellular organism") capable of absorbing [electromagnetic radiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_radiation "Electromagnetic radiation") from the Sun and expelling it for [propulsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propulsion "Propulsion").

Grace joined Stratt's Project [Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail_Mary_pass#Metaphorical_usage_in_other_fields "Hail Mary pass"), an international "long-shot" mission to send a crewed spacecraft to Tau Ceti, using astrophage as fuel. Tau Ceti was the only nearby star not occluded by astrophage. The mission was a [suicide mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_mission "Suicide mission"), as the spacecraft could carry only enough fuel for a one-way journey; any findings would have to be returned by [space probes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_probe "Space probe").

In the present, Grace encounters an alien spacecraft that docks with the *Hail Mary*. Made from a solid form of [xenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenon "Xenon"), which Grace calls "xenonite", it carries a rock-like, five-legged alien from Erid, a planet in the [40 Eridani A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/40_Eridani "40 Eridani") system. Grace names the alien "Rocky", deduces that Eridians perceive their surroundings through [echolocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_echolocation "Animal echolocation"), and develops a [machine translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_translation "Machine translation") system to interpret Rocky's [musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chord_(music) "Chord (music)") language. Rocky is a [mechanical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering "Mechanical engineering"), and the sole survivor of the Eridians' mission to save their own star from astrophage. Rocky and Grace agree to collaborate, and soon become close friends. As neither can survive each other's atmosphere, Rocky and Grace construct pressurized xenonite chambers inside the *Hail Mary* for Rocky to work in.

Grace and Rocky investigate the Petrova line around [Tau Ceti e](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau_Ceti_e "Tau Ceti e"), which Grace names "Adrian" after the name he gave to Rocky's Eridian [mate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mating "Mating"). They discover a [microbial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microorganism "Microorganism") [biosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosphere#Extraterrestrial_biospheres "Biosphere") in the line; Rocky theorises that other organisms further down in Adrian's atmosphere keep the astrophage population in check. After learning that Grace cannot return home, Rocky offers to share some of his astrophage fuel with the *Hail Mary*. Grace then remembers that an astrophage explosion killed the original crew's science officers, and that Stratt recruited him when no replacements could be trained in time.

During a risky maneuver to collect the astrophage's predator, a fuel leak causes the *Hail Mary* to [spin uncontrollably](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifugal_force "Centrifugal force"), knocking Grace unconscious. Rocky enters Grace's atmosphere to save him, but is badly injured. While Rocky recovers, Grace studies the captured predator, names it "taumoeba" and [selectively breeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selective_breeding "Selective breeding") it to survive in the [nitrogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrogen "Nitrogen")-rich atmosphere of Venus. After Rocky recovers, the pair celebrate their success. Grace then remembers that he initially refused to join the mission, but Stratt had him [drugged and put aboard the ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghaiing "Shanghaiing"), believing he was the only person capable of completing it.

Grace and Rocky part ways to return home. Soon afterwards, Grace discovers that the taumoeba has escaped its xenonite breeding tanks and begun consuming the *Hail Mary*'s astrophage fuel. Although he contains the outbreak, he realises that Rocky's xenonite ship cannot contain the organism and will eventually lose all its fuel, stranding Rocky and dooming Erid. Grace chooses to sacrifice his remaining fuel to rescue Rocky and the Eridians, sending his video logs and taumoeba samples to Earth by probe.

Stratt and her team receive the probes, and use the taumoeba to cure the Sun's astrophage infection. On Erid, Grace lives in an Earth-like [biodome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_ecological_system "Closed ecological system") built for him by the Eridians. Rocky tells him that Eridian scientists can prepare the *Hail Mary* for a return voyage to Earth. Grace considers this before beginning another day teaching science to enthusiastic Eridian children.

## Production

### Development

In March 2020, [Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer "Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer") was nearing a deal to acquire adaptation rights to the then-upcoming [Andy Weir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Weir "Andy Weir") novel *[Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary "Project Hail Mary")* (2021), with [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling "Ryan Gosling") set to star in and produce the film. The rights for the novel were acquired for $3 million. In May 2020, [Phil Lord and Christopher Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Lord_and_Christopher_Miller "Phil Lord and Christopher Miller") were hired to direct and produce the film. Due to Lord and Miller being unable to work on the screenplay because of commitments to *[Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Across_the_Spider-Verse "Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse")* (2023), [Drew Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Goddard "Drew Goddard") (who previously adapted Weir's 2011 novel *[The Martian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Martian_(Weir_novel) "The Martian (Weir novel)")* into a [2015 film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Martian_(film) "The Martian (film)")) was hired the following month to write the screenplay. In April 2024, [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company) "Amazon (company)"), which acquired Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer in 2022, revealed a possible 2026 release window for the film. In May 2024, [Sandra Hüller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_H%C3%BCller "Sandra Hüller") joined the cast, with [Greig Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greig_Fraser "Greig Fraser") hired as [cinematographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinematographer "Cinematographer"). [Milana Vayntrub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milana_Vayntrub "Milana Vayntrub") joined the cast the following month.

### Filming

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") began on June 3, 2024, in the United Kingdom and [wrapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrap_(filmmaking) "Wrap (filmmaking)") on October 26, 2024. Filming took place at [Shepperton Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepperton_Studios "Shepperton Studios"). Location filming occurred at [The Hemel Hempstead School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hemel_Hempstead_School "The Hemel Hempstead School"), the [South Parade Pier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Parade_Pier "South Parade Pier") in [Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth "Portsmouth"), the [Mullard Radio Astronomy Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullard_Radio_Astronomy_Observatory "Mullard Radio Astronomy Observatory") in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge "Cambridge"), and [Durdle Door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durdle_Door "Durdle Door") and [Man o' War Cove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_o%27_War_Cove "Man o' War Cove") in [Dorset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorset "Dorset").

The film was "filmed for [IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX "IMAX")" by using large-sensor [Arri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arri "Arri") [Alexa 65](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arri_Alexa#Alexa_65 "Arri Alexa") cameras, which provide [6.5K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6K_resolution "6K resolution") (20 [MP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel "Pixel")) of resolution, with an [aspect ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_(image) "Aspect ratio (image)") of 2.1:1, utilizing the whole sensor with [anamorphic lenses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anamorphic_format "Anamorphic format") rotated by 90 degrees to achieve the IMAX standard [1.43:1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_(image)#1.43:1 "Aspect ratio (image)") aspect ratio. For regular presentations, the common 2:1 format ([Univisium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Univisium "Univisium")) was derived by [cropping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cropping_(image) "Cropping (image)") the top and bottom of the IMAX image. The film switches between taller and wider aspect ratios. When a scene takes place in the present, a taller aspect ratio is used—1.43:1 for [IMAX 15/70 mm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70_mm_film#IMAX "70 mm film") or [Dual Laser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX#Laser_projection "IMAX"), [1.85:1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_(image)#1.85:1 "Aspect ratio (image)") for [Xenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX#Digital_projection "IMAX") or [Single Laser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX#Laser_projection "IMAX"), and [2.00:1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_(image)#2.00:1 "Aspect ratio (image)") for non-IMAX presentations. For scenes in the past, an aspect ratio of [2.39:1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anamorphic_format#Aspect_ratio "Anamorphic format"), a standard ratio for anamorphic process cinematography, was achieved with the regular use of anamorphic lenses.

In an interview with *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter "The Hollywood Reporter")*, Miller mentioned that no [chroma keying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key "Chroma key") was used in the film, with the entire set being shot practically. Fraser had previously collaborated with [visual effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_effects "Visual effects") supervisor [Paul Lambert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Lambert_(special_effects_artist) "Paul Lambert (special effects artist)") on the [*Dune* films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_(franchise)#Films "Dune (franchise)"); their disdain for green and blue screens, which impacts lighting and visuals through [color spill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key#Lighting "Chroma key"), led them to use [blackouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackout_(fabric) "Blackout (fabric)") and grey screens on the film instead. Miller had previously stated that VFX was used to depict the spaceship from the outside, while shots of Gosling in space were shot against a black or varying color backdrop.

[NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA "NASA") provided support through both the input of scientific experts and astronaut [Kjell N. Lindgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kjell_N._Lindgren "Kjell N. Lindgren") visiting Gosling during filming to share insights on [human spaceflight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_spaceflight "Human spaceflight").

### Post-production

The visual effects were developed by [Framestore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framestore "Framestore") (which also collaborated on the film adaptation of *The Martian*), [Industrial Light & Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Light_%26_Magic "Industrial Light & Magic") (ILM), [Sony Pictures Imageworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Imageworks "Sony Pictures Imageworks"), [BUF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BUF_Compagnie "BUF Compagnie") and Wylie Co. VFX, with Mags Sarnowska serving as visual effects supervisor alongside Lambert.

While puppeteer Ortiz voiced Rocky during filming with Gosling, it was initially thought that a more high-profile actor would replace him. However, Lord and Miller felt that Ortiz's performance could not be improved upon during screenings of the film. Lord praised the collaboration between practical and visual effects in the creation of the character Rocky's performance:

> Rocky was built and designed by the legendary [Neal Scanlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neal_Scanlan "Neal Scanlan") and his creature shop and is performed by puppeteering legend James Ortiz and his team, who were on set with Ryan in every scene. Rocky's performance is a beautiful collaboration between James and the other Rockyteers on set and the wonderful animators at Framestore led by the funny soulful Arslan Elver.

Miller noted that by the end of the film shots with Rocky were about 50% VFX animation and 50% puppetry.

The digitally shot and produced film was transferred to [film stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_stock "Film stock") and then [re-digitized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digitization "Digitization") to achieve the "warmth" of [analog film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photographic_film "Photographic film"). The initial [test screening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_screening "Test screening") of the film was 3 hours and 45 minutes long, and was cut down to the final length due to feedback from the screenings. Joel Negron served as film editor.

## Music

It was announced at [San Diego Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con "San Diego Comic-Con") that [Daniel Pemberton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Pemberton "Daniel Pemberton") would score the film. Pemberton worked with Lord and Miller on *[Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Into_the_Spider-Verse "Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse")* (2018) and its [2023 sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Across_the_Spider-Verse "Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse"), which they produced. This is the first film directed by Lord and Miller to not be scored by [Mark Mothersbaugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Mothersbaugh "Mark Mothersbaugh").

The score, consisting of 38 tracks, was released digitally on March 20, 2026. The film also includes fourteen other songs, including "[Sign of the Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sign_of_the_Times_(Harry_Styles_song) "Sign of the Times (Harry Styles song)")" by [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles "Harry Styles"), "[Two of Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_of_Us_(Beatles_song) "Two of Us (Beatles song)")" by [the Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles "The Beatles"), "[Rainbows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbows_(Dennis_Wilson_song) "Rainbows (Dennis Wilson song)")" by [Dennis Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Wilson "Dennis Wilson") and "[Gracias a la vida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gracias_a_la_vida "Gracias a la vida")" sung by [Mercedes Sosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_Sosa "Mercedes Sosa").

## Marketing

The first trailer was released on June 30, 2025. It was the most-viewed trailer for any original film in the first week of its release, accumulating 400 million views globally. [Lego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego "Lego") released a model of the *Hail Mary* spacecraft on March 1, 2026, as part of the [Lego Icons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_Icons "Lego Icons") series.

## Release

### Theatrical

*Project Hail Mary* was released on March 20, 2026, in over 4,000 theaters by [Amazon MGM Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_MGM_Studios "Amazon MGM Studios") in the United States and Canada, and [Sony Pictures Releasing International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Releasing_International "Sony Pictures Releasing International") in all other territories. It was also released in select [IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX "IMAX") theaters, with a 1.43:1 [aspect ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_(image) "Aspect ratio (image)").

### Home media

The film was released on [digital streaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streaming_media "Streaming media") platforms on May 12, 2026. It began streaming on the [MGM+ subscription streaming service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGM%2B#Streaming "MGM+") and [Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") on June 18 and July 3, respectively.

It was also released on [4K Ultra HD Blu-ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra_HD_Blu-ray "Ultra HD Blu-ray"), [Blu-ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blu-ray "Blu-ray"), and [DVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD "DVD") on August 11, 2026, by Alliance Entertainment. Following the standard disc release will be an Amazon exclusive three-disc [SteelBook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_disc_packaging#SteelBook "Optical disc packaging") gift set, scheduled for October 13. It will include a third disc with the film in its full 1.43:1 IMAX ratio, plus additional bonus material and a collectible booklet.

## Reception

### Box office

As of September 11, 2026[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Project_Hail_Mary_(film)&action=edit) *Project Hail Mary* has grossed $344.1 million in the United States and Canada, and $341.2 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $685.3 million.

In the United States and Canada, *Project Hail Mary* was released on March 20, 2026, and was projected to gross $63–65 million in North America from 4,007 theaters in its opening weekend. Initially, publications estimated that the film would gross $45–55 million. After grossing $31 million on opening day, including $12 million in previews, the film debuted to $80.6 million in the United States and Canada, and $60.4 million overseas for a worldwide total of $141 million, becoming Amazon MGM Studios' biggest debut to date. In its second weekend, the film made $54.1 million (a drop of 32.8%), remaining in first. It was surpassed by *[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Super_Mario_Galaxy_Movie "The Super Mario Galaxy Movie")* in its third weekend, grossing $31.7 million. In Japan, the film became the highest-grossing foreign film of 2026 in terms of box office revenue attendance on its first day of release.

### Critical response

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 95% of 430 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 8.2/10. The website's consensus reads: "A visually dazzling space odyssey that's carried along effortlessly by the gravitational pull of Ryan Gosling at his most winning, *Project Hail Mary* is a near-miraculous fusion of smarts and heart." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean"), assigned the film a score of 77 out of 100, based on 60 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews. Audiences polled by [CinemaScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaScore "CinemaScore") gave the film an "A" on an A+ to F scale. The film received an average rating of 8 out of 10 from *[Cine21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cine21 "Cine21")*.

G. Allen Johnson of the *[San Francisco Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Chronicle "San Francisco Chronicle")* deemed it "a masterpiece of a family popcorn movie, with eye-popping hand-crafted production design and outstanding creature design and puppetry work." Randy Myers of *[The Mercury News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mercury_News "The Mercury News")* gave the film a 3.5/4 rating, writing: "While you can't help but marvel at the visuals and applaud the commitment of Gosling's endearing performance, what also makes *Project Hail Mary* so gosh darn lovable is that it imbues us with child-like wonder from scenes that pay homage to such classics as *[E.T.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.T._the_Extra-Terrestrial "E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial")*, *[Flight of the Navigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_of_the_Navigator "Flight of the Navigator")* and, yes, *[Rocky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky "Rocky")*."

[Robbie Collin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbie_Collin "Robbie Collin") of *[The Telegraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Telegraph "The Daily Telegraph")* concluded in his review: "Does it have many original ideas of its own? Perhaps not. But its greatest hits mixtape of other people's has been compiled with such flair – as well as a sound comprehension of why they worked so well the first time – that it's hard not to be swept up regardless." Barry Hertz of *[The Globe and Mail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Globe_and_Mail "The Globe and Mail")* shared a similar opinion, writing: "At almost every turn, *Project Hail Mary* attempts to convince you that it is groundbreaking, innovative filmmaking. But in actuality, the movie lands as a grand act of cinematic recycling – the fusing together of familiar, comforting bits and pieces into something determined to please crowds and warm hearts."

[Amy Nicholson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Nicholson "Amy Nicholson") of the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* wrote: "While [Stanley Kubrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Kubrick "Stanley Kubrick")'s unsentimental *[2001: A Space Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001:_A_Space_Odyssey "2001: A Space Odyssey")* inspired the [iPad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPad "IPad"), Lord and Miller want to inspire a better version of us." Brian Truitt of *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today "USA Today")* gave the film a four out of four rating, writing that "*Project Hail Mary* is one of those old-school popcorn movies with big ideas, the kind of film that [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg "Steven Spielberg") and [George Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas "George Lucas") would have made back in the day and kids of all ages would have obsessed over and bought the toys." Ryan Cooper of *[The American Prospect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_American_Prospect "The American Prospect")* wrote that the film portrayed a "shockingly realistic view of heroism" and was "one of the rare films that is quite a bit better than the book".

[Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw "Peter Bradshaw") of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* wrote: "Gosling is an effortlessly charming screen player, and he keeps it watchable, though the film itself has moments of dullness and a sort of puppyish silliness [...] Perhaps refreshingly, the film doesn't aim for the stunned awe and rapture of, say, [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan "Christopher Nolan")'s *[Interstellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film) "Interstellar (film)")* or even [Jon Spaihts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Spaihts "Jon Spaihts")' underrated *[Passengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passengers_(2016_film) "Passengers (2016 film)")*, but it does have the classic sci-fi spacecraft tropes: the huge, mysterious architecture with its vertiginous tunnels in which legacy pop music is played to soothe the inhabitants." [GVSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Valley_State_University "Grand Valley State University") anthropology professor [Deana L. Weibel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deana_L._Weibel "Deana L. Weibel") praised Grace's intellectual humility in the film.

Nicholas Barber of the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC "BBC") declared it one of the best films of 2026, writing:

> *Project Hail Mary* is an unusual science-fiction blockbuster in that it's mostly about people using their brains to solve problems [...] Does that approach sound a bit dry and academic? If so, rest assured that *Project Hail Mary* is touching and inspiring – and surprisingly fun [...] it's directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller, who take a complicated, potentially bleak narrative and make it as fast-paced and cheerful as their animated hit *[The Lego Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lego_Movie "The Lego Movie")*. Meanwhile, Ryan Gosling brings all of his goofball charm to the role of an amnesiac biologist who is trying to save the world.

Conversely, [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman") of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* called it "not a very good movie", adding that "it's baggy and incredibly derivative of movies you've seen before [...] More crucially, everything to do with the onboard alien is far too cute and formulaic".
