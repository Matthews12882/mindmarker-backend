# Bruce Willis

**Walter Bruce Willis** (born March 19, 1955) is a retired American actor. Widely recognized as a Hollywood icon of the action genre, he first achieved fame with a leading role on the comedy-drama series *[Moonlighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonlighting_(TV_series) "Moonlighting (TV series)")* (1985–1989) and has appeared in over one hundred films, gaining widespread recognition as an action hero for his portrayal of [John McClane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McClane "John McClane") in the [*Die Hard* franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard_(franchise) "Die Hard (franchise)") (1988–2013).

Willis's other credits include *[The Last Boy Scout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Boy_Scout "The Last Boy Scout")* (1991), *[Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction "Pulp Fiction")* (1994), *[12 Monkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12_Monkeys "12 Monkeys")* (1995), *[The Fifth Element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fifth_Element "The Fifth Element")* (1997), *[Armageddon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_(1998_film) "Armageddon (1998 film)")* (1998), *[The Sixth Sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sixth_Sense "The Sixth Sense")* (1999), *[Unbreakable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unbreakable_(film) "Unbreakable (film)")*, *[The Whole Nine Yards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Whole_Nine_Yards_(film) "The Whole Nine Yards (film)")* (both 2000), *[Tears of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears_of_the_Sun "Tears of the Sun")* (2003), *[Sin City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin_City_(film) "Sin City (film)")* (2005), *[The Expendables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Expendables_(2010_film) "The Expendables (2010 film)")*, *[Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_(2010_film) "Red (2010 film)")* (both 2010), *[Looper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looper_(film) "Looper (film)")* (2012) and *[Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_(2019_film) "Glass (2019 film)")* (2019). In the last years of his career, he starred in many low-budget [direct-to-video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-video "Direct-to-video") films, which were poorly received. Willis retired in 2022 due to [aphasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aphasia "Aphasia") and was diagnosed with [frontotemporal dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontotemporal_dementia "Frontotemporal dementia") in 2023.

Willis has received [various accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Bruce_Willis "List of awards and nominations received by Bruce Willis") throughout his career, including a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globes "Golden Globes"), two [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards") and two [People's Choice Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Choice_Awards "People's Choice Awards"). He received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame") in 2006. Films featuring Willis have grossed between US$2.64 billion and US$3.05 billion at North American box offices, and he was the eighth-highest-grossing [leading actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_actor "Leading actor") in 2010s.

## Early life and education

Walter Bruce Willis was born on March 19, 1955, in [Idar-Oberstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idar-Oberstein "Idar-Oberstein"), [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany "West Germany"); his mother, Marlene, was from [Kassel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassel "Kassel"), Germany, and his father, David Willis (1929–2009), was an American soldier. He has a younger sister, Florence, and two younger brothers, Robert (1959–2001) and David. After being discharged from the military in 1957, his father relocated the family to his hometown of [Carneys Point, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carneys_Point_Township,_New_Jersey "Carneys Point Township, New Jersey"). Willis has described his background as a "long line of [blue-collar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-collar_worker "Blue-collar worker") people". His mother worked in a bank and his father was a welder, master mechanic and factory worker.

Willis spoke with a [stutter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttering "Stuttering"). He attended [Penns Grove High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penns_Grove_High_School "Penns Grove High School") in Carneys Point Township, where his schoolmates nicknamed him "Buck-Buck". Willis joined the drama club, and found that acting on stage reduced his stutter. He was eventually elected student council president. He graduated from Penns Grove in 1973.

After graduating from high school, Willis worked as a security guard at the [Salem Nuclear Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Nuclear_Power_Plant "Salem Nuclear Power Plant") and transported crew members at the [DuPont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DuPont "DuPont") Chambers Works factory in [Deepwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepwater,_New_Jersey "Deepwater, New Jersey"). He turned to acting after working as a [private investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_investigator "Private investigator"), a role he would later play in the comedy-drama series *[Moonlighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonlighting_(TV_series) "Moonlighting (TV series)")* and the action-comedy film *[The Last Boy Scout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Boy_Scout "The Last Boy Scout")*.

Willis enrolled in the drama program at [Montclair State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montclair_State_University "Montclair State University"), where he was cast in a production of *[Cat on a Hot Tin Roof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_on_a_Hot_Tin_Roof "Cat on a Hot Tin Roof")*. He left the school in 1977, and moved to New York City where he was part of the [NBC's page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_page "NBC page") program and worked for *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live").* In the early 1980s he supported himself as a bartender at various nightspots in Manhattan including Kamikaze, Cafe Central & Chelsea Central, while living in the [Hell's Kitchen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hell%27s_Kitchen,_Manhattan "Hell's Kitchen, Manhattan") neighborhood.

## Career

### 1980s: *Moonlighting*, *Die Hard* and rise to fame

Willis was cast as David Addison Jr. in the television series *[Moonlighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonlighting_(TV_series) "Moonlighting (TV series)")* (1985–1989), competing against 3,000 other actors for the position. His starring role in *Moonlighting*, opposite [Cybill Shepherd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybill_Shepherd "Cybill Shepherd"), helped to establish him as a comedic actor. During the show's five seasons, he won an [Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series") and a [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Television Series Musical or Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_-_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy "Golden Globe Award for Best Actor - Television Series Musical or Comedy"). During the height of the show's success, beverage maker [Seagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seagram "Seagram") hired Willis as the pitchman for their Golden [Wine Cooler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wine_Cooler "Wine Cooler") products. The advertising campaign paid Willis US$5–7 million over two years. Willis chose not to renew his contract when he decided to stop drinking alcohol in 1988.

In 1987, Willis obtained his first lead role in the [Blake Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Edwards "Blake Edwards") film *[Blind Date](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_Date_(1987_film) "Blind Date (1987 film)")*, co-starring with [Kim Basinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Basinger "Kim Basinger") and [John Larroquette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Larroquette "John Larroquette"). Edwards cast him again to play the real-life cowboy actor [Tom Mix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Mix "Tom Mix") in *[Sunset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_(1988_film) "Sunset (1988 film)")* (1988). The same year, he starred in an action role in *[Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard "Die Hard")* (1988) as [John McClane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McClane "John McClane"). He performed most of his own [stunts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stunt "Stunt") in the film, and the film grossed $138,708,852 worldwide. Following his success with *Die Hard*, Willis had a leading role in the drama *[In Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Country "In Country")* as Vietnam veteran Emmett Smith and also provided the voice for a talking baby in *[Look Who's Talking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_Who%27s_Talking "Look Who's Talking")* (1989) and the sequel *[Look Who's Talking Too](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look_Who%27s_Talking_Too "Look Who's Talking Too")* (1990).

In the late 1980s, Willis enjoyed moderate success as a [recording artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_artist "Recording artist"), recording an album of pop-blues, *[The Return of Bruno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Return_of_Bruno_(album) "The Return of Bruno (album)")*, credited to Willis's fictional [alter ego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alter_ego "Alter ego"), Bruno Radolini, supposedly an influential blues musician. The album included the hit single "[Respect Yourself](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respect_Yourself "Respect Yourself")" using [the Pointer Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pointer_Sisters "The Pointer Sisters") as guest vocalists. The LP was promoted by a [mockumentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mockumentary "Mockumentary") featuring scenes of "Bruno" performing at famous events including [Woodstock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodstock "Woodstock"). Willis released a version of [the Drifters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Drifters "The Drifters") song "[Under the Boardwalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Boardwalk "Under the Boardwalk")" as a second single; it reached No. 2 on the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_singles_chart "UK singles chart"), but was less successful in the US. Willis returned to the recording studio several times.

### 1990s: *Die Hard* sequels, *Pulp Fiction* and dramatic roles

Having acquired major personal success and pop culture influence playing John McClane in *Die Hard*, Willis reprised his role in the sequels *[Die Hard 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard_2 "Die Hard 2")* (1990) and *[Die Hard with a Vengeance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard_with_a_Vengeance "Die Hard with a Vengeance")* (1995). These first three installments in the *[Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard_(franchise) "Die Hard (franchise)")* series grossed over US$700 million internationally and propelled Willis to the first rank of Hollywood action stars.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] At one point, *Die Hard 2* and *[Ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_(1990_film) "Ghost (1990 film)")*, starring Willis's then wife [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore "Demi Moore"), would occupy the number one and number two spots at the box office, a feat that would not be accomplished again for a married Hollywood couple until 2024.

In the early 1990s, Willis's career suffered a moderate slump, as he starred in [flops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_office_bomb "Box office bomb") such as *[The Bonfire of the Vanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bonfire_of_the_Vanities_(film) "The Bonfire of the Vanities (film)")* (1990) and *[Hudson Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Hawk "Hudson Hawk")* (1991), although he did find box office success with *[The Last Boy Scout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Boy_Scout "The Last Boy Scout")* (1991). He gained more success with *[Striking Distance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striking_Distance "Striking Distance")* (1993) but flopped again with *[Color of Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_of_Night "Color of Night")* (1994): it was savaged by critics but did well in the [home video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_video "Home video") market and became one of the Top 20 most-rented films in the United States in 1995. [*Maxim*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine) "Maxim (magazine)") also ranked his sex scene in the film as the best in film history.

In 1994, Willis also had a leading role in one part of [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino "Quentin Tarantino")'s acclaimed *[Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction_(film) "Pulp Fiction (film)")*; the film's success gave a boost to his career, and he starred alongside his *Look Who's Talking* co-star [John Travolta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Travolta "John Travolta"). In 1996, he was the executive producer and star of the cartoon *[Bruno the Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_the_Kid "Bruno the Kid")*, which featured a [CGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery") representation of himself. That same year, he starred in [Mike Judge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Judge "Mike Judge")'s animated film *[Beavis and Butt-head Do America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beavis_and_Butt-head_Do_America "Beavis and Butt-head Do America")* with his then-wife [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore "Demi Moore"). In the movie, he plays a drunken criminal named "Muddy Grimes", who mistakenly sends Judge's titular characters to kill his wife, Dallas (voiced by Moore). He then played the lead roles in *[12 Monkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12_Monkeys "12 Monkeys")* (1995), *[The Fifth Element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fifth_Element "The Fifth Element")* (1997) and *[The Jackal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jackal_(1997_film) "The Jackal (1997 film)")* (1997), the latter of which was a box-office success despite negative reviews. These were followed by *[Mercury Rising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_Rising "Mercury Rising")* (1998) and *[Breakfast of Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakfast_of_Champions_(film) "Breakfast of Champions (film)")* (1999), which were also poorly reviewed. In 1998, his voice and likeness were featured in the [PlayStation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_(console) "PlayStation (console)") video game *[Apocalypse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocalypse_(1998_video_game) "Apocalypse (1998 video game)")*.

Willis was involved in *[Broadway Brawler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_Brawler "Broadway Brawler")* as both producer and lead actor. That production failed, and as compensation he was contracted for three films: *[Armageddon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_(1998_film) "Armageddon (1998 film)")*, *[The Sixth Sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sixth_Sense "The Sixth Sense")* and *[Disney's The Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%27s_The_Kid "Disney's The Kid")*. Both *Armageddon* and *The Sixth Sense* were critical and commercial successes, with the former being the highest-grossing film of 1998 worldwide.

### 2000s

In 2000, Willis won an [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Awards "Emmy Awards") for [Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series") for his work on *[Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends "Friends")* (in which he played the father of [Ross Geller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Geller "Ross Geller")'s much-younger girlfriend). He was also nominated for a 2001 American Comedy Award (in the Funniest Male Guest Appearance in a TV Series category) for his work on *Friends*. Also in 2000, Willis played Jimmy "The Tulip" Tudeski in *[The Whole Nine Yards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Whole_Nine_Yards_(film) "The Whole Nine Yards (film)")* alongside *Friends* star [Matthew Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Perry "Matthew Perry"), and Russ Duritz in *[Disney's The Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney%27s_The_Kid "Disney's The Kid")* opposite [Emily Mortimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Mortimer "Emily Mortimer"). Willis was originally cast as Terry Benedict in *[Ocean's Eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean%27s_Eleven "Ocean's Eleven")* (2001) but dropped out to work on recording an album. In the sequel, *[Ocean's Twelve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean%27s_Twelve "Ocean's Twelve")* (2004), he makes a [cameo appearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameo_appearance "Cameo appearance") as himself. In 2005, he appeared in the film adaptation of *[Sin City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin_City_(film) "Sin City (film)")*. In 2006, he lent his voice as RJ the Raccoon in *[Over the Hedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_the_Hedge "Over the Hedge")*. In 2007, he appeared in the *[Planet Terror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_Terror "Planet Terror")* half of the double feature *[Grindhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grindhouse_(film) "Grindhouse (film)")* as the villain, a mutant soldier. This marked Willis's second collaboration with the director [Robert Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Rodriguez "Robert Rodriguez"), following *[Sin City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin_City_(film) "Sin City (film)")*.

Willis appeared on the *[Late Show with David Letterman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_Show_with_David_Letterman "Late Show with David Letterman")* several times throughout his career. He filled in for an ill [David Letterman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Letterman "David Letterman") on his show on February 26, 2003, when he was supposed to be a guest. On many of his appearances on the show, Willis staged elaborate jokes, such as wearing a day-glo orange suit in honor of the [Central Park gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gates "The Gates"), having one side of his face made up with simulated [birdshot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birdshot "Birdshot") wounds after the [Harry Whittington shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Cheney_hunting_incident "Dick Cheney hunting incident"), or trying to break a record (a parody of [David Blaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Blaine "David Blaine")) of staying underwater for only twenty seconds.

On April 12, 2007, he appeared again, this time wearing a [Sanjaya Malakar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjaya_Malakar "Sanjaya Malakar") wig. On his June 25, 2007 appearance, he wore a mini-wind turbine on his head to accompany a joke about his own fictional documentary titled *An Unappealing Hunch* (a wordplay on *[An Inconvenient Truth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Inconvenient_Truth "An Inconvenient Truth")*). Willis also appeared in Japanese [Subaru Legacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subaru_Legacy "Subaru Legacy") television commercials. Tying in with this, Subaru did a limited run of Legacys, badged "Subaru Legacy Touring Bruce", in honor of Willis.

Willis has appeared in five films with [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson "Samuel L. Jackson") (1993's National Lampoon's *[Loaded Weapon 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loaded_Weapon_1 "Loaded Weapon 1")*, 1994's *[Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction "Pulp Fiction")*, 1995's *[Die Hard with a Vengeance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard_with_a_Vengeance "Die Hard with a Vengeance")*, 2000's *[Unbreakable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unbreakable_(film) "Unbreakable (film)")*, and 2019's *[Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_(2019_film) "Glass (2019 film)")*) and both actors were slated to work together in *[Black Water Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Water_Transit "Black Water Transit")*, before dropping out. Willis also worked with his eldest daughter, [Rumer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumer_Willis "Rumer Willis"), in the 2005 film *[Hostage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostage_(2005_film) "Hostage (2005 film)")*. In 2006, he appeared in the crime/drama film *[Alpha Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_Dog "Alpha Dog")*, opposite [Sharon Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_Stone "Sharon Stone"). In 2007, he appeared in the [thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_film "Thriller film") *[Perfect Stranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Stranger_(film) "Perfect Stranger (film)")*, opposite [Halle Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Berry "Halle Berry"), and reprised his role as [John McClane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McClane "John McClane") in *[Live Free or Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Free_or_Die_Hard "Live Free or Die Hard")*. Subsequently, he appeared in the films *[What Just Happened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_Just_Happened_(2008_film) "What Just Happened (2008 film)")* (2008) and *[Surrogates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrogates "Surrogates")* (2009), based on the [comic book of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Surrogates "The Surrogates").

Willis was slated to play U.S. Army general [William R. Peers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_R._Peers "William R. Peers") in director [Oliver Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Stone "Oliver Stone")'s *Pinkville*, a drama about the investigation of the 1968 [My Lai massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Lai_massacre "My Lai massacre"). However, due to the [2007 Writers Guild of America strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Writers_Guild_of_America_strike "2007 Writers Guild of America strike"), the film was canceled. Willis appeared on the 2008 [Blues Traveler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues_Traveler "Blues Traveler") album *[North Hollywood Shootout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Hollywood_Shootout_(album) "North Hollywood Shootout (album)")*, giving a [spoken word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spoken_word "Spoken word") performance over an instrumental [blues rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues_rock "Blues rock") jam on the track "Free Willis (Ruminations from Behind Uncle Bob's Machine Shop)". In early 2009, he appeared in an advertising campaign to publicize the insurance company [Norwich Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_Union "Norwich Union")'s change of name to [Aviva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviva "Aviva").

### 2010s

As of 2010, Willis was the eighth highest-grossing actor in a leading role and 12th-highest including supporting roles. Willis starred with [Tracy Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Morgan "Tracy Morgan") in the 2010 comedy *[Cop Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cop_Out_(2010_film) "Cop Out (2010 film)")*, directed by [Kevin Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Smith "Kevin Smith"), about two police detectives investigating the theft of a baseball card. Willis appeared in the music video for the song "[Stylo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stylo_(song) "Stylo (song)")" by [Gorillaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorillaz "Gorillaz"). Also in 2010, he appeared in a cameo with the former [Planet Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_Hollywood "Planet Hollywood") co-owners and 80s action stars [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone "Sylvester Stallone") and [Arnold Schwarzenegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Schwarzenegger "Arnold Schwarzenegger") in the film *[The Expendables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Expendables_(2010_film) "The Expendables (2010 film)")*. Willis played the role of CIA agent "[Mr. Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Expendables_characters#Mr._Church "List of The Expendables characters")". It was the first time the three action stars had appeared on screen together. Although the scene featuring the three was short, it was one of the most highly anticipated scenes in the film. The trio filmed their scene in an empty church on October 24, 2009. Willis next starred in *[Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_(2010_film) "Red (2010 film)")*, an adaptation of the comic book mini-series [of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_(WildStorm) "Red (WildStorm)"), in which he portrayed Frank Moses. The film was released on October 15, 2010.

Willis starred alongside [Bill Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Murray "Bill Murray"), [Edward Norton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Norton "Edward Norton"), and [Frances McDormand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_McDormand "Frances McDormand") in *[Moonrise Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonrise_Kingdom "Moonrise Kingdom")* (2012). Filming took place in [Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhode_Island "Rhode Island") under the direction of [Wes Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Anderson "Wes Anderson"), in 2011. Willis returned, in an expanded role, in *[The Expendables 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Expendables_2 "The Expendables 2")* (2012). He appeared alongside [Joseph Gordon-Levitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gordon-Levitt "Joseph Gordon-Levitt") in the sci-fi action film *[Looper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looper_(film) "Looper (film)")* (2012), as the older version of Gordon-Levitt's character, Joe.

Willis teamed up with [50 Cent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/50_Cent "50 Cent") in a film directed by [David Barrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Barrett_(director) "David Barrett (director)") called *[Fire with Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_with_Fire_(2012_film) "Fire with Fire (2012 film)")*, starring opposite [Josh Duhamel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Duhamel "Josh Duhamel") and [Rosario Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosario_Dawson "Rosario Dawson"), about a fireman who must save the love of his life. Willis also joined [Vince Vaughn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_Vaughn "Vince Vaughn") and [Catherine Zeta-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Zeta-Jones "Catherine Zeta-Jones") in *[Lay the Favorite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lay_the_Favorite "Lay the Favorite")*, directed by [Stephen Frears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Frears "Stephen Frears"), about a Las Vegas cocktail waitress who becomes an elite professional gambler. The two films were distributed by [Lionsgate Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate_Studios "Lionsgate Studios").

Willis reprised his most famous role, John McClane, for a fifth time, starring in *[A Good Day to Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Good_Day_to_Die_Hard "A Good Day to Die Hard")*, which was released on February 14, 2013. In an interview, Willis said: "I have a warm spot in my heart for *Die Hard*..... it's just the sheer novelty of being able to play the same character over 25 years and still be asked back is fun. It's much more challenging to have to do a film again and try to compete with myself, which is what I do in *Die Hard*. I try to improve my work every time." That same year, Willis reprised his role as Frank Moses in *[Red 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_2_(film) "Red 2 (film)")*.

On October 12, 2013, Willis hosted *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* with [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry "Katy Perry") as a musical guest. In 2015, Willis made his [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_theatre "Broadway theatre") debut in [William Goldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Goldman "William Goldman")'s adaptation of [Stephen King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_King "Stephen King")'s novel *[Misery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misery_(play) "Misery (play)")* opposite [Laurie Metcalf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurie_Metcalf "Laurie Metcalf") at the [Broadhurst Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadhurst_Theatre "Broadhurst Theatre"). His performance was generally panned by critics, who called it "vacant" and "inert". Willis was the subject of a [roast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roast_(comedy) "Roast (comedy)") by [Comedy Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_Central_Roast "Comedy Central Roast") in a program broadcast on July 29, 2018. Willis played himself in a cameo in the 2019 film *[The Lego Movie 2: The Second Part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lego_Movie_2:_The_Second_Part "The Lego Movie 2: The Second Part")*.

### 2020s: Critical decline, health problems and retirement

In the final years of his career, Willis starred in many low-budget [independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film "Independent film") thrillers and science fiction films. He worked primarily with the production companies [Emmett/Furla Oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmett/Furla_Oasis "Emmett/Furla Oasis") (which produced 20 films starring Willis) and 308 Entertainment. Most of the films were released [direct-to-video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-video "Direct-to-video") and were widely panned. [Chris Nashawaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Nashawaty "Chris Nashawaty") of *[Esquire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_(magazine) "Esquire (magazine)")* described the direct-to-video films as "a profitable safe harbor" for older actors, similar to *[The Expendables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Expendables_(franchise) "The Expendables (franchise)")*. Willis would often earn US$2 million for two days' work, with an average of 15 minutes' screentime per film. He nonetheless featured heavily in the films' promotional materials, earning them the derogatory nickname "[geezer teasers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geezer_teaser "Geezer teaser")". The [Golden Raspberry Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Awards "Golden Raspberry Awards"), an annual award for the year's worst films and performances, created a dedicated category, the [Worst Bruce Willis Performance in a 2021 Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/42nd_Golden_Raspberry_Awards "42nd Golden Raspberry Awards"), for Willis's roles in eight films released that year.

Those working on the films later said Willis appeared confused, did not understand why he was there and had to be fed lines through an [earpiece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-ear_monitor "In-ear monitor"). Days before Willis was scheduled to arrive on set for *[Out of Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_Death "Out of Death")* (2021), the screenwriter, Bill Lawrence, was instructed to reduce his role and abbreviate his dialogue. The director, [Mike Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Burns_(producer) "Mike Burns (producer)"), was told to complete all of Willis's scenes in a single day of filming.

On March 30, 2022, Willis's family announced that he was retiring because he had been diagnosed with [aphasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aphasia "Aphasia"), a disorder typically caused by damage to the area of the brain that controls language expression and comprehension. The Golden Raspberry Awards retracted its Willis category, deeming it inappropriate to give a Razzie to someone whose performance was affected by a medical condition. At the time of his retirement, Willis had completed eleven films awaiting release.

## Business activities

Willis owns houses in Los Angeles and [Penns Grove, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penns_Grove,_New_Jersey "Penns Grove, New Jersey"). He also rents apartments at [Trump Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Tower "Trump Tower") and in [Riverside South, Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside_South,_Manhattan "Riverside South, Manhattan"). In 2000, Willis and his business partner [Arnold Rifkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Rifkin "Arnold Rifkin") started a motion picture production company called [Cheyenne Enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheyenne_Enterprises "Cheyenne Enterprises"). He left the company to be run solely by Rifkin in 2007 after *Live Free or Die Hard*. He also owns several small businesses in [Hailey, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailey,_Idaho "Hailey, Idaho"), including The Mint Bar and The Liberty Theater and was one of the first promoters of [Planet Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_Hollywood "Planet Hollywood"), with actors [Arnold Schwarzenegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Schwarzenegger "Arnold Schwarzenegger") and [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone "Sylvester Stallone"). Willis and the other actors were paid for their appearances and endorsements through an [employee stock ownership plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employee_stock_ownership_plan "Employee stock ownership plan").

In 2009, Willis signed a contract to become the international face of [Belvedere SA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belv%C3%A9d%C3%A8re_(company) "Belvédère (company)")'s [Sobieski Vodka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobieski_Vodka "Sobieski Vodka") in exchange for 3.3% ownership in the company. In 2018, Willis became the brand ambassador of the Hungarian brand [Hell Energy Drink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hell_Energy_Drink "Hell Energy Drink").

## Personal life

Willis's acting role models are [Gary Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Cooper "Gary Cooper"), [Robert De Niro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_De_Niro "Robert De Niro"), [Steve McQueen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_McQueen "Steve McQueen") and [John Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wayne "John Wayne"). He is left handed. He resides in the [Brentwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentwood,_Los_Angeles "Brentwood, Los Angeles") neighborhood of Los Angeles with his family.

On November 21, 1987, Willis married [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore "Demi Moore") at the [Golden Nugget Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Nugget_Las_Vegas "Golden Nugget Las Vegas"). On December 20, the couple held a second reception for about 450 guests on a soundstage at [Warner Bros. Studios Burbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Studios_Burbank "Warner Bros. Studios Burbank"). They have three daughters: [Rumer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumer_Willis "Rumer Willis") (b. 1988), Scout (b. 1991), and Tallulah (b. 1994). Willis and Moore announced their separation on June 24, 1998. They filed for divorce on October 18, 2000, and the divorce was finalized later that day. Regarding the divorce, Willis stated: "I felt I had failed as a father and a husband by not being able to make it work." He credited actor [Will Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Smith "Will Smith") for helping him cope with the situation. He has maintained a close friendship with both Moore and her subsequent husband, actor [Ashton Kutcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashton_Kutcher "Ashton Kutcher"), and attended their wedding.

Willis was engaged to actress [Brooke Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooke_Burns "Brooke Burns") until they broke up in 2004 after ten months together. He married model [Emma Heming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Heming_Willis "Emma Heming Willis") in [Turks and Caicos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turks_and_Caicos_Islands "Turks and Caicos Islands") on March 21, 2009; guests included his three daughters, as well as Moore and Kutcher. The ceremony was not legally binding, so the couple wed again in a civil ceremony in [Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills,_California "Beverly Hills, California") six days later. The couple has two daughters, one born in 2012 and another born in 2014.

In August 2025, Emma said that she and Willis were now living in separate homes. Emma stated that this was due to Willis's declining health, with his new, separate one-story home being better suited to accommodate his progressing [frontotemporal dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontotemporal_dementia "Frontotemporal dementia").

### Health

On February 16, 2023, Willis's family announced that he had been diagnosed with [frontotemporal dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontotemporal_dementia "Frontotemporal dementia"). According to Gregg Day, a neurologist at the [Mayo Clinic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Clinic "Mayo Clinic")'s [Florida campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_Clinic_Florida "Mayo Clinic Florida"), the symptoms include difficulties with language and comprehension, and misinterpretation of instructions. In a statement, the family said that Willis's condition had progressed and that "challenges with communication are just one symptom of the disease". They expressed hope that media attention on Willis would raise awareness about the disease. Emma Willis's memoir *The Unexpected Journey*, which focuses on her experience as her husband's caregiver, was published in September 2025.

As his condition progressed, Willis was moved to a separate, one-story home containing an on-site team providing full time support. In an August 2025 interview, Emma said the decision was made to prioritize the needs of their two daughters, and added that she believed Willis would have made the choice himself if he could. She said the family visits Willis "a lot" for meals and to spend time together.

### Military interests

Throughout his film career, Willis has depicted several military characters in films such as *[In Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Country "In Country")*, *[The Siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Siege "The Siege")*, *[Hart's War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hart%27s_War "Hart's War")*, *[Tears of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears_of_the_Sun "Tears of the Sun")*, *[Grindhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grindhouse_(film) "Grindhouse (film)")*, and *[G.I. Joe: Retaliation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.I._Joe:_Retaliation "G.I. Joe: Retaliation")*. Willis grew up in a [military family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_brat "Military brat"). Willis has donated [Girl Scout cookies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_Scout_cookies "Girl Scout cookies") to the United States armed forces. In 2002, Willis's then eight-year-old daughter, Tallulah, suggested that he purchase Girl Scout cookies to send to troops. Willis purchased 12,000 boxes of cookies, and they were distributed to sailors aboard [USS *John F. Kennedy*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_John_F._Kennedy_(CV-67) "USS John F. Kennedy (CV-67)") and other troops stationed throughout the Middle East at the time.

In 2003, Willis visited [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq "Iraq") as part of the [USO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Service_Organizations "United Service Organizations") tour, singing to the troops with his band, The Accelerators. Willis considered joining the military to help fight the second [Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_War "Iraq War"), but was deterred by his age. It was believed he offered US$1 million to any noncombatant who turned in terrorist leaders [Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden "Osama bin Laden"), [Ayman al-Zawahiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayman_al-Zawahiri "Ayman al-Zawahiri"), or [Abu Musab al-Zarqawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Musab_al-Zarqawi "Abu Musab al-Zarqawi"); in the June 2007 issue of *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")*, however, he clarified that the statement was made hypothetically and not meant to be taken literally. Willis has also criticized the media for its coverage of the war, complaining that the press was more likely to focus on the negative aspects of the war:

> I went to Iraq because what I saw when I was over there was soldiers—young kids for the most part—helping people in Iraq; helping getting the power turned back on, helping get hospitals open, helping get the water turned back on and you don't hear any of that on the news. You hear, "X number of people were killed today," which I think does a huge disservice. It's like spitting on these young men and women who are over there fighting to help this country.

### Religious beliefs

Willis was a [Lutheran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheranism "Lutheranism"), but says he no longer practices. In a July 1998 interview with *[George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_(magazine) "George (magazine)")* magazine, he said: "Organized religions in general, in my opinion, are dying forms. They were all very important when we didn't know why the sun moved, why weather changed, why hurricanes occurred, or volcanoes happened. Modern religion is the end trail of modern mythology. But there are people who interpret the Bible literally. Literally! I choose not to believe that's the way. And that's what makes America cool, you know?"

During the *Tears of the Sun* premiere in March 2003, when asked by a *Hollywood.tv* reporter how he stays grounded in [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles "Hollywood, Los Angeles"), Willis said: "I just thank God every day for [...] everything great that's come my way."

### Political views

In 1988, Willis and then-wife [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore "Demi Moore") campaigned for then [Democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States) "Democratic Party (United States)") [Massachusetts governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Massachusetts "Governor of Massachusetts") [Michael Dukakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dukakis "Michael Dukakis")'s presidential campaign in the [1988 presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_United_States_presidential_election "1988 United States presidential election"). In the [1992 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_United_States_presidential_election "1992 United States presidential election"), however, he supported President [George H. W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush "George H. W. Bush") for re-election and was an outspoken critic of [Bill Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Clinton "Bill Clinton"). In 1996, he declined to endorse Clinton's [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States) "Republican Party (United States)") opponent [Bob Dole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dole "Bob Dole") because Dole criticized Demi Moore for her role in the film *[Striptease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striptease_(film) "Striptease (film)")*. Willis was an invited speaker at the [2000 Republican National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Republican_National_Convention "2000 Republican National Convention"), and supported [George W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush "George W. Bush") that year.

In 2006, Willis said that the United States should intervene more extensively in [Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia "Colombia") to end drug trafficking from that nation. In several interviews, Willis has said that he supports increased salaries for teachers and police officers, and that he is disappointed in the United States [foster care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_care "Foster care") system and its treatment of [Native Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas "Indigenous peoples of the Americas"). Willis has supported [gun rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_to_keep_and_bear_arms_in_the_United_States "Right to keep and bear arms in the United States"), saying: "Everyone has a right to bear arms. If you take guns away from legal gun owners, then the only people who have guns are the bad guys."

In February 2006, when Willis was in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan "Manhattan") to promote his film *[16 Blocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16_Blocks "16 Blocks")*, he was asked his opinion on the [Bush administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_George_W._Bush "Presidency of George W. Bush"). Willis responded: "I'm sick of answering this fucking question. I'm a Republican only as far as I want a [smaller government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_government "Limited government"), I want less government intrusion. I want them to stop shitting on my money and your money and tax dollars that we give 50 percent of every year. I want them to be fiscally responsible and I want these goddamn lobbyists out of Washington. Do that and I'll say I'm a Republican. I hate the government, OK? I'm [apolitical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apoliticism "Apoliticism"). Write that down. I'm not a Republican". Willis did not make any contributions or public endorsements in the [2008 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_United_States_presidential_election "2008 United States presidential election"). In several June 2007 interviews, he said that he maintains some beliefs aligned with Republican ideas.

On August 17, 2006, Willis was named in a *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")* advertisement that condemned [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas "Hamas") and [Hezbollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah "Hezbollah") and supported [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel "Israel") in the [2006 Israel-Lebanon war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War "2006 Lebanon War"). In the [2012 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_United_States_presidential_election "2012 United States presidential election"), Willis said that he had a negative opinion of [Mitt Romney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitt_Romney "Mitt Romney").
