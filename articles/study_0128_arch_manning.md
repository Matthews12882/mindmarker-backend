# Arch Manning

**Archibald Charles Manning** (born April 27, 2004) is an American [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football "College football") [quarterback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterback "Quarterback") for the [Texas Longhorns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Longhorns_football "Texas Longhorns football"). He is a member of the [Manning family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manning_family "Manning family") of football players.

## Early life

Archibald Charles Manning was born on April 27, 2004 in [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans"). Arch is [Cooper Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_Manning "Cooper Manning") and Ellen Heidingsfelder's oldest son, grandson to [Archie Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie_Manning "Archie Manning"), and nephew of [Peyton Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peyton_Manning "Peyton Manning") and [Eli Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Manning "Eli Manning"), all three former NFL quarterbacks. His younger brother, Heid, was the [center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_(American_football) "Center (American football)") for the football team and played lacrosse at [Isidore Newman School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isidore_Newman_School "Isidore Newman School").

## High school career

### 2019 season

Manning played [high school football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school_football "High school football") at [Isidore Newman School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isidore_Newman_School "Isidore Newman School") in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans"). In his freshman year, Manning was the varsity starter at quarterback, the first freshman quarterback to start Newman's season opener in at least 40 years. He shone in his debut, leading Newman to a 26-point victory. In the final game of the season against [Dunham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dunham_School "The Dunham School"), Manning threw for only 71 yards on 20 attempts and 10 completions. They would lose, 22–20. Manning completed his freshman season with 2,509 yards and 34 touchdowns, earning [MaxPreps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MaxPreps "MaxPreps") National Freshman of the Year honors.

### 2020 season

Manning opened his sophomore season by scoring six touchdowns against [East Jefferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Jefferson_High_School "East Jefferson High School"). On October 15, 2020, he made his national television debut in a game against [Booker T. Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T._Washington_High_School_(New_Orleans,_Louisiana) "Booker T. Washington High School (New Orleans, Louisiana)"), throwing for two touchdowns and rushing for two more. In a game where Newman beat [Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohen_College_Prep_High_School "Cohen College Prep High School"), 76–0, Manning passed for 169 yards and five touchdowns in the first quarter of action before being pulled from the game. In the first round of the Division III playoffs, Newman received a bye and played [Catholic – N.I.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_High_School_(New_Iberia,_Louisiana) "Catholic High School (New Iberia, Louisiana)") in the second round. Manning would throw for 127 yards and a touchdown in the 14–7 win. In the final game of the season in the semifinals, Manning completed less than 50 percent of his passes and threw an interception to lose to [Lafayette Christian Academy](https://www.lafayettechristianacademy.com/), 21–7.

### 2021 season

Manning was again named the starting quarterback for Newman for his junior season in 2021. In Newman's first game against [Vandebilt Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandebilt_Catholic_High_School "Vandebilt Catholic High School"), Manning passed for 258 yards and accounted for all four touchdowns in the 28-point shutout victory. Weeks later, Manning went a perfect 11-of-11 for 179 yards and five total touchdowns in the first half of their 70–0 win over [Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher_Middle-High_School "Fisher Middle-High School"). Manning had another perfect night against Cohen, going 9-of-9 for 153 yards and five passing touchdowns in the 34-point shutout win. In the second round of the playoffs, Manning played [Episcopal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episcopal_School_of_Baton_Rouge "Episcopal School of Baton Rouge") and threw for 164 yards and accounted for three touchdowns in the 37—6 win. The following week, Lafayette Christian would blow out Newman, 49–7. In 2021, Manning threw for 1,841 yards, 26 touchdowns, and four interceptions while having six rushing touchdowns.

### 2022 season

Manning was the captain of the Newman football team for his senior season in 2022. In their matchup against [Pearl River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_River_High_School_(Louisiana) "Pearl River High School (Louisiana)"), Manning threw for 356 yards and seven touchdowns in a 59–7 victory, breaking his uncle Eli's Newman record of 7,268 career passing yards and his uncle Peyton's Newman record of 93 career touchdowns.

## College career

### 2023 season

After playing in the [spring game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exhibition_game#College_and_high_school_football "Exhibition game") for Team Orange, Manning was named the team's third-string quarterback behind [Quinn Ewers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinn_Ewers "Quinn Ewers") and [Maalik Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maalik_Murphy "Maalik Murphy"). Manning made his collegiate debut on November 24, during the third quarter of a blowout win against [Texas Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Texas_Tech_Red_Raiders_football_team "2023 Texas Tech Red Raiders football team"), completing 2-of-5 passing attempts for 30 yards. After the season, Manning was [redshirted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redshirted "Redshirted").

### 2024 season

Manning made his [2024 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Texas_Longhorns_football_team "2024 Texas Longhorns football team") debut against [Colorado State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Colorado_State_Rams_football_team "2024 Colorado State Rams football team") when he entered the game in relief of Ewers during the third quarter while the Longhorns had a 38–0 lead. Manning finished with five completions for 95 yards and a touchdown. He also added a rushing touchdown. Against [UTSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UTSA_Roadrunners_football_team "2024 UTSA Roadrunners football team"), Ewers left with an abdominal injury in the second quarter forcing Manning to come into the game with a 14–0 lead. On his first play of the game, Manning threw a 19-yard touchdown to DeAndre Moore. Two plays later, he rushed for a 67-yard touchdown to put the Longhorns ahead 28–7. After Manning threw a 75-yard strike to freshman [wide receiver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide_receiver "Wide receiver") [Ryan Wingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Wingo "Ryan Wingo") to go up 42–7, he added another passing touchdown in the fourth quarter to go up 49–7 before being relieved by freshman Trey Owens. Manning finished the game with 223 passing yards, 67 rushing yards, and five total touchdowns (four passing, one rushing) as Texas blew out UTSA, 56–7. On September 19, head coach [Steve Sarkisian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Sarkisian "Steve Sarkisian") announced that Manning would be the starting quarterback the following week against [Louisiana–Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Louisiana%E2%80%93Monroe_Warhawks_football_team "2024 Louisiana–Monroe Warhawks football team"). This announcement came after Manning shared the title of [SEC Freshman of the Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Southeastern_Conference_football_season#Players_of_the_week "2024 Southeastern Conference football season") with [Texas A&M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Texas_A%26M_Aggies_football_team "2024 Texas A&M Aggies football team") quarterback [Marcel Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Reed "Marcel Reed") for Week 3. He was also named [Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Campbell_Tyler_Rose_Award "Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award") National Player of the Week and was tabbed to the [Davey O'Brien Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_O%27Brien_Award "Davey O'Brien Award") Great 8 list.

### 2025 season

Manning was named the starting quarterback for the preseason #1 Texas Longhorns heading into the 2025 season. In the first game against the defending national champion #3 [Ohio State Buckeyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ohio_State_Buckeyes_football_team "2025 Ohio State Buckeyes football team"), Manning struggled as he completed 17 of 30 passes for 170 yards, a touchdown, and an interception as the Longhorns lost 14–7 on the road. In the Week 4 win against [Sam Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Sam_Houston_Bearkats_football_team "2025 Sam Houston Bearkats football team"), Manning completed 18 of 21 passes for 309 yards, three passing touchdowns, and two rushing touchdowns, becoming the fourth Texas quarterback with at least two rushing touchdowns and two passing touchdowns in a game. In Week 9 against [Mississippi State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mississippi_State_Bulldogs_football_team "2025 Mississippi State Bulldogs football team"), Manning completed 29 of 46 passes for 346 yards, three passing touchdowns, and one rushing touchdown. For his performance, he was named one of eight [Manning Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manning_Award "Manning Award") "Stars of the Week". In Week 10 against [Vanderbilt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Vanderbilt_Commodores_football_team "2025 Vanderbilt Commodores football team"), Manning completed 25 of 33 passes for 328 yards and three passing touchdowns, earning him SEC Offensive Player of the Week, Manning Award "Stars of the Week", and [Davey O'Brien Award's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_O%27Brien_Award "Davey O'Brien Award") Great 8. On November 22 against the [Arkansas Razorbacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Arkansas_Razorbacks_football_team "2025 Arkansas Razorbacks football team"), Manning became the first player in program history to record a passing, receiving, and rushing touchdown in the same game, scoring six total times and logging 389 passing yards.

### 2026 season

Manning is currently the starting quarterback for the Texas Longhorns. In their second game of the season, Manning led the #4 ranked Longhorns to a win against the #1 ranked Ohio State Buckeyes where they won 24-23. In Week 6, Manning threw three touchdowns and two interceptions in the [Red River Shootout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_River_Rivalry "Red River Rivalry") against the unranked Oklahoma Sooners, losing 24-27.
