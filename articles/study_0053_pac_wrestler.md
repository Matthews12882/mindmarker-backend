# Pac (wrestler)

**Benjamin Satterley** (22 August 1986 – 27 September 2026), better known by his [ring name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_name "Ring name") **Pac** (often stylised in [all caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps "All caps")), was an English [professional wrestler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestler "Professional wrestler"). He was best known for his tenure in [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling") (AEW) and [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE").

Satterley began his wrestling career under the ring name **Pac** in his native [north east England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_east_England "North east England") with the Independent Wrestling Federation (IWF). After establishing himself throughout the country with promotions such as Real Quality Wrestling (RQW) and [One Pro Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Pro_Wrestling "One Pro Wrestling") (1PW), he began to perform overseas, most notably for American promotions such as [Pro Wrestling Guerrilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Wrestling_Guerrilla "Pro Wrestling Guerrilla") (PWG), [Ring of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Honor "Ring of Honor") (ROH), and [Chikara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikara_(professional_wrestling) "Chikara (professional wrestling)"), as well as the Japanese promotion [Dragongate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_(wrestling) "Dragon Gate (wrestling)"), where he won the [Open the Brave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_the_Brave_Gate_Championship "Open the Brave Gate Championship"), [Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_the_Triangle_Gate_Championship "Open the Triangle Gate Championship"), and [Twin Gate Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Gate_Championship "Twin Gate Championship"). He was a former [PWG World Tag Team Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PWG_World_Tag_Team_Champion "PWG World Tag Team Champion") and winner of PWG's inaugural [Dynamite Duumvirate Tag Team Title Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamite_Duumvirate_Tag_Team_Title_Tournament "Dynamite Duumvirate Tag Team Title Tournament").

From 2012 to 2015, Satterley wrestled under the ring name **Adrian Neville** in [NXT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_(WWE_brand) "NXT (WWE brand)"), the [developmental territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developmental_territory "Developmental territory") of [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE"). He won the [NXT Tag Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Tag_Team_Championship "NXT Tag Team Championship") [twice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NXT_Tag_Team_Champions "List of NXT Tag Team Champions"), with his first reign coming as one half of the inaugural champions with [Oliver Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Grey "Oliver Grey") and his second reign being with [Corey Graves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Graves "Corey Graves"). He was also a [one-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NXT_Champions "List of NXT Champions") [NXT Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Champion "NXT Champion"), making him the first man to have held both the NXT Championship and the NXT Tag Team Championship. He was promoted to the main roster under the shortened ring name **Neville** in 2015, and began competing in the cruiserweight division in 2016, becoming a record [two-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NXT_Cruiserweight_Champions "List of NXT Cruiserweight Champions") [WWE Cruiserweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Cruiserweight_Championship "NXT Cruiserweight Championship").

After leaving WWE in 2018, Satterley reverted to his previous ring name of Pac and returned to Dragongate, holding the [Open the Dream Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_the_Dream_Gate_Championship "Open the Dream Gate Championship") for much of his time there and joining the villainous [R.E.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.D._(professional_wrestling) "R.E.D. (professional wrestling)") stable. He assumed the moniker of "The Bastard", which he continued to use until his death. In 2019, he left Dragon Gate and signed with AEW, where he became a member of the [Death Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Triangle "Death Triangle") and [Death Riders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Riders "Death Riders"). He was also a [one-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AEW_International_Champions "List of AEW International Champions") and the inaugural [AEW International Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_International_Champion "AEW International Champion") and a [two-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AEW_World_Trios_Champions "List of AEW World Trios Champions") [AEW World Trios Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_World_Trios_Champion "AEW World Trios Champion"), at one point holding both the International and Trios titles simultaneously.

## Early life

Benjamin Satterley was born on 22 August 1986 in [Newcastle upon Tyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne "Newcastle upon Tyne"), the son of Jill and Stephen Satterley.

## Professional wrestling career

### Early career and training (2002–2004)

By the age of 16, Satterley had wrestled with [backyard wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_wrestling "Backyard wrestling") promotions TWXW in [Gateshead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gateshead "Gateshead") and FXW in [Middlesbrough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlesbrough "Middlesbrough"), as well as the promotions Scarborough Wrestling Alliance in [Scarborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough,_North_Yorkshire "Scarborough, North Yorkshire") and Essex Championship Wrestling in [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex "Essex"). Even as one of the younger wrestlers in the group, his technical ability and athleticism ensured he was always in the most entertaining matches. Many of the FXW roster went on to form 3Count Wrestling (3CW).[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] He started training as a wrestler on a more permanent basis at the age of 18 at the Indpendent Wrestling Federation training school at St Joseph's Hall in Gateshead, which he later described as "pretty much the only wrestling school in the area". He received professional training at the [Frontier Wrestling Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_Wrestling_Alliance "Frontier Wrestling Alliance") Academy under Mark Sloan.

### British independent circuit (2004–2012)

Satterley made his debut under the ring name **Pac** at Independent Wrestling Federation on 27 March 2004, losing to Assassin. For the first two years of his career, he wrestled mainly for IWF and 3 Count Wrestling. He won his first championship on 26 August 2005 when he teamed up with Harry Pain to defeat Chris Prime. In 2006, he began to wrestle all across Britain and Europe. He later performed in 3 Count Wrestling, where he debuted on 14 October 2005 and defeated EdEn and Falcon in the opening match. Pac went on to capture the 3CW North East Championship on 24 February 2006. He then captured the 3CW Triple Crown Championship at an [ICW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insane_Championship_Wrestling "Insane Championship Wrestling") event, which he held for 110 days. Pac was defeated in his last 3CW match by Liam Thomson on 30 April 2010.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

Pac made his debut for [International Pro Wrestling: United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Pro_Wrestling:_United_Kingdom "International Pro Wrestling: United Kingdom") in April 2006, wrestling for the IPW:UK Title on his debut, losing in a triple threat against Leroy Kincaide and champion and match winner Martin Stone. He later had two more opportunities at the top title in IPW:UK, against Stone and then against Andy Boy Simmonz, but lost both. After a September 2006 triple threat against Kevin Steen and El Generico, the two Canadian wrestlers recommended Satterley to PWG head-booker Super Dragon. He made his PWG debut two months later as a result.[*[paragraph citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

After joining [One Pro Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Pro_Wrestling "One Pro Wrestling") in May 2006, Pac became the first-ever [Openweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1PW_Openweight_Championship "1PW Openweight Championship") on 26 November after winning a [battle royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_royal_(professional_wrestling) "Battle royal (professional wrestling)"). He successfully retained the title on several occasions before losing it to James Wallace on 30 June 2007 in a match that started immediately after Pac retained the title in a four-way match. On 29 December, Pac failed to reclaim the title after losing to then-champion Darren Burridge.

In late April, Pac represented [International Pro Wrestling: United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Pro_Wrestling:_United_Kingdom "International Pro Wrestling: United Kingdom") in the prestigious [King of Europe Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Europe_Cup "King of Europe Cup"). He defeated [Trent Acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trent_Acid "Trent Acid") in the first round before falling to [Nigel McGuinness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigel_McGuinness "Nigel McGuinness") in the quarter finals.

On 29 September 2006, Pac teamed with [Sheamus O'Shaunessy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheamus "Sheamus") to defeat Micky L and Tony Spitfire for [British Championship Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Championship_Wrestling "British Championship Wrestling")'s *Retribution* event. He returned for *BCW No Blood, No Sympathy VI* in 2008 to score a singles victory over CJ Hunter. Pac made his as yet final appearance for BCW at *BCW Pac Is Back* on 12 April 2019 where he unsuccessfully challenged [BT Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._T._Gunn "B. T. Gunn") for the BCW Heavyweight Championship in a triple threat with [Jody Fleisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jody_Fleisch "Jody Fleisch").

On 17 November 2009, Pac debuted in American Wrestling Rampage during its European Invasion tour, and lost to Dunkan Disorderly in a three-way match also involving Shawn Maxer for the promotion's No Limits Championship. Two days later, he wrestled against Disorderly in a singles match for the title, but was defeated. After defeating Disorderly in tag-team matches over the next two days, Pac and Maxer wrestled against Disorderly for the No Limits Title in another three-way match on 22 November, but were again defeated. After trading wins with [El Generico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sami_Zayn "Sami Zayn") over the next few days, Pac finally won the title after defeating Generico and new champion Michael Knight on 29 November. After successfully retaining the title against Generico on 4 and 6 December, Pac lost the title to Dunkan Disorderly on 8 December. The next day, Pac and Generico defeated Disorderly and Shawn Maxer in a tag-team match.

### Pro Wrestling Guerrilla (2006–2008)

On 17 November 2006, Pac made his debut in [Pro Wrestling Guerrilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Wrestling_Guerrilla "Pro Wrestling Guerrilla"). He competed against [A.J. Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AJ_Styles "AJ Styles") in a losing effort of the first night of All Star Weekend IV. The next night, he faced [El Generico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sami_Zayn "Sami Zayn") in a match that was considered by those in attendance a serious Match of the Year contender. Pac returned to PWG on 24 February 2007, defeating [Kevin Steen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Owens "Kevin Owens") to pick up his first win in the promotion. He faced Generico again on 7 April, this time in the main event of the first night of All Star Weekend V with the [PWG World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PWG_World_Championship "PWG World Championship") on the line. Generico once again came out on top. The next night, Pac faced [All Japan Pro Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Japan_Pro_Wrestling "All Japan Pro Wrestling") star [Kaz Hayashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaz_Hayashi "Kaz Hayashi") in a losing effort.[*[paragraph citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

Pac was originally scheduled to compete in singles matches during the first PWG [Dynamite Duumvirate Tag Team Title Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Pro_Wrestling_Guerrilla_tournaments#DDT4_(2007) "List of Pro Wrestling Guerrilla tournaments") on 20 and 21 May. However, [Roderick Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roderick_Strong "Roderick Strong")'s original partner, [Jack Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Evans_(wrestler) "Jack Evans (wrestler)"), had been called back to [Dragon Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_(wrestling) "Dragon Gate (wrestling)") at the last moment and decided to have Pac fill the vacant spot. After defeating [Super Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Dragon "Super Dragon") and [Davey Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_Richards "Davey Richards") in the first round, [Naruki Doi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naruki_Doi "Naruki Doi") and [Masato Yoshino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masato_Yoshino "Masato Yoshino") in the second round and then The [Briscoe Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Briscoe_Brothers "Briscoe Brothers") in the finals, Pac and Strong won the tournament and as a result, became the new [World Tag Team Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PWG_World_Tag_Team_Championship "PWG World Tag Team Championship"). In their first title defence, Pac and Strong defeated [The Havana Pitbulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Havana_Pitbulls "The Havana Pitbulls") ([Ricky Reyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Reyes "Ricky Reyes") and [Rocky Romero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Romero "Rocky Romero")) at PWG's event Roger Dorn Night on 10 June. From 15 to 22 July, Pac took part and competed for Dragon Gate's biggest tour of the year, Wrestle Jam, where he and El Generico represented PWG alongside [Ring of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Honor "Ring of Honor") representatives [Austin Aries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Aries "Austin Aries"), [Delirious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delirious_(wrestler) "Delirious (wrestler)"), Jack Evans, [Matt Sydal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Sydal "Matt Sydal") and [Jimmy Rave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Rave "Jimmy Rave").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

On 29 July, Pac and Strong were scheduled to defend their World Tag Team Championship against the Briscoe Brothers in a 2 out of 3 falls match. However, they were replaced by [Kevin Steen and El Generico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Owens_and_Sami_Zayn "Kevin Owens and Sami Zayn"), who ended up winning the World Tag Team Championship in a one fall match. Pac was among the 24 participants in the [2007 Battle of Los Angeles Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Los_Angeles_(professional_wrestling)#2007 "Battle of Los Angeles (professional wrestling)") in Burbank, California, that ran from 31 August to 2 September. He defeated Jack Evans in a first round match then Claudio Castagnoli in a second round match before losing to eventual tournament winner [Cima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cima_(wrestler) "Cima (wrestler)") in a semi-final round match. After almost a year-long absence from the company, Pac made his return to PWG at All Star Weekend VII on 30 August 2008, where he picked up the win for the team of himself and [The Young Bucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Young_Bucks "The Young Bucks") by pinning Kevin Steen, who teamed with El Generico and [Susumu Yokosuka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susumu_Mochizuki "Susumu Mochizuki"). The next night, Pac was unsuccessful in his attempt to win the World Championship from then-champion [Chris Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hero "Chris Hero"). Pac returned to PWG on 23 July 2011, facing Kevin Steen in a losing effort. On 29 January 2012, at *Kurt Russellreunion 3*, Pac teamed with El Generico and Masato Yoshino to defeat [Akira Tozawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Tozawa "Akira Tozawa"), Kevin Steen and Super Dragon in a six-man tag team main event.

### Various international promotions (2007–2012)

In January 2007, Pac wrestled as part of [Total Nonstop Action Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_Nonstop_Action_Wrestling "Total Nonstop Action Wrestling")'s "[ImPact!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNA_Impact! "TNA Impact!") Total" tour in [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal "Portugal") along with fellow British wrestlers [Spud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drake_Maverick "Drake Maverick"), [Jonny Storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonny_Storm "Jonny Storm") and Jody Fleisch. Soon after the tour, Pac debuted in [Ring of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Honor "Ring of Honor") at their 3 and 4 March shows at the [Liverpool Olympia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_Olympia "Liverpool Olympia") in [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool "Liverpool"), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England "England"). On the first night, he lost to [Roderick Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roderick_Strong "Roderick Strong") in a match for the [FIP World Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIP_World_Heavyweight_Championship "FIP World Heavyweight Championship"). On the second, he was defeated by [Matt Sydal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Sydal "Matt Sydal"). On 24 August, he returned to ROH in a losing effort against [Bryan Danielson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Danielson "Bryan Danielson"). At ROH's Manhattan Mayhem, he lost to [Davey Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_Richards "Davey Richards").

On 21 April 2007, Pac debuted in [Chikara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikara_(professional_wrestling) "Chikara (professional wrestling)") at their show Rey De Voladores in a four-way elimination match against [Chuck Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Taylor_(wrestler) "Chuck Taylor (wrestler)"), [Ricochet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_(wrestler) "Ricochet (wrestler)") and Retail Dragon, which was won by Taylor. In August 2007, Pac returned to Chikara at their International Invaders weekend. On the first night, he was defeated by [Ricochet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_(wrestler) "Ricochet (wrestler)"). The next night, he was defeated by [Claudio Castagnoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Castagnoli "Claudio Castagnoli"). On 23 August, he participated at Chikara's eighth Ballpark Brawl, where he lost a six-way elimination match that featured [Sterling James Keenan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_James_Keenan "Sterling James Keenan"), Trent Acid, El Generico, Xtremo and John McChesney, which was won by Keenan.

On 27 May 2012, Pac made his [New Japan Pro-Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Japan_Pro-Wrestling "New Japan Pro-Wrestling") debut, when he entered the [2012 Best of the Super Juniors tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_of_the_Super_Juniors#2012 "Best of the Super Juniors"), defeating [Jushin Thunder Liger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jushin_Thunder_Liger "Jushin Thunder Liger") in his first match. When the [round-robin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Round-robin_tournament "Round-robin tournament") stage of the tournament concluded on 9 June, Pac finished with five wins out of his eight matches, winning his block and advancing to the semifinals of the tournament. He lost to [Ryusuke Taguchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryusuke_Taguchi "Ryusuke Taguchi") the following day, and was eliminated from the tournament.

### Dragon Gate and affiliates (2007–2012)

Shortly after participating in Pro Wrestling Guerrilla's Battle of Los Angeles tournament, Pac was invited to [Dragon Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragongate "Dragongate") for its Dragon Storm 2007 tour beginning on 8 September. The tour was the last for [World Wrestling Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wrestling_Entertainment "World Wrestling Entertainment")-bound Matt Sydal, who on 14 September endorsed Pac to be his replacement in the Dragon Gate [faction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_professional_wrestling_terms#Stable "Glossary of professional wrestling terms") Typhoon. On 15 September, Pac defeated [Dragon Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Kid "Dragon Kid") for the [Westside Xtreme Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westside_Xtreme_Wrestling "Westside Xtreme Wrestling") World Lightweight Championship. On 25 April 2008, Pac lost to [Gamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_(wrestler) "Gamma (wrestler)") in the first round of a tournament for the [Open the Brave Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_Open_the_Brave_Gate_Championship "Dragon Gate Open the Brave Gate Championship"). On 31 May, Pac and Dragon Kid wrestled against fellow Typhoon members [Ryo Saito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryo_Saito_(wrestler) "Ryo Saito (wrestler)") and [Susumu Yokosuka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susumu_Yokosuka "Susumu Yokosuka") for the [Open the Twin Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_Open_the_Twin_Gate_Championship "Dragon Gate Open the Twin Gate Championship"), but were unable to win. On 27 July, Pac, Dragon Kid and [Anthony W. Mori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takayuki_Mori "Takayuki Mori") represented Typhoon in a four-way match for the [Open the Triangle Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_Open_the_Triangle_Gate_Championship "Dragon Gate Open the Triangle Gate Championship"), but were unable to defeat Gamma, [Yamato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamato_(wrestler) "Yamato (wrestler)") and [Yasushi Kanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasushi_Kanda "Yasushi Kanda") for the title. Typhoon disbanded later that year.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

In 2009, Pac joined the World-1 stable. On 22 March 2009, Pac and stablemates [BxB Hulk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BxB_Hulk "BxB Hulk") and [Naoki Tanizaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naoki_Tanizaki "Naoki Tanizaki") represented World-1 in a loss to the Open the Triangle Gate Champions Kamikaze (Dragon Kid, [Shingo Takagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shingo_Takagi "Shingo Takagi") and [Taku Iwasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taku_Iwasa "Taku Iwasa")) in a three-way match also involving Real Hazard ([Kenichiro Arai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenichiro_Arai "Kenichiro Arai"), Yamato and Yasushi Kanda). World-1 again challenged for the title on 24 May, but were defeated by the new champions Warriors-5 (Cima, Gamma and [Kagetora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumiyuki_Hashimoto "Fumiyuki Hashimoto")). On 7 June, World-1 finally won the Open the Triangle Gate Championship after defeating Warriors-5. World-1 made their first successful title defence on 19 July, where they defeated Kamikaze (Akira Tozawa, Dragon Kid and Taku Iwasa). On 7 February 2010, Pac lost to [K-ness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makoto_Sait%C5%8D_(wrestler) "Makoto Saitō (wrestler)") in a match for the [Open the Brave Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_Open_the_Brave_Gate_Championship "Dragon Gate Open the Brave Gate Championship"). On 5 July 2010, Pac made his debut for Dragon Gate's international expansion [Dragon Gate USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_USA "Dragon Gate USA"), wrestling in a match where he and BxB Hulk were defeated by Yamato and Shingo. At the following day's tapings of the Uprising [pay-per-view](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay-per-view "Pay-per-view"), Pac and Naruki Doi defeated [Jigsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigsaw_(wrestler) "Jigsaw (wrestler)") and [Mike Quackenbush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Quackenbush "Mike Quackenbush"). On 14 October, World-1 lost the Open the Triangle Gate Title to Akebono, Fujii and Mochizuki in a rematch. On 20 December 2010, Dragon Gate USA announced that they had signed Pac to a contract that made him a regular member of the promotion's roster. On 30 January 2011, Pac and Masato Yoshino defeated [Chuck Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Taylor_(wrestler) "Chuck Taylor (wrestler)") and [Johnny Gargano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Gargano "Johnny Gargano") to become Dragon Gate USA's first ever [Open the United Gate Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DGUSA_Open_the_United_Gate_Championship "DGUSA Open the United Gate Championship"). On 11 September, Pac and Yoshino lost the Open the United Gate Championship to Open the Twin Gate Champions, Cima and Ricochet, in a title vs. title match. On 29 August 2010, Pac defeated Susumu Yokosuka in a tournament final to win the vacant Open the Brave Gate Championship for the first time. On 14 April 2011, Pac, BxB Hulk and Susumu Yokosuka failed to win the Open the Triangle Gate Championship from Blood Warriors (Cima, Dragon Kid and Ricochet) and, as a result, World-1 was forced to disband.

On 24 April 2011, former World-1 members Pac, Hulk, Yokosuka and Yoshino agreed to form a new alliance with Masaaki Mochizuki to battle Blood Warriors. On 8 June, the new group was named Junction Three in reference to it being a union between the former members of World-1, Kamikaze and the Veteran-gun. On 19 June, Pac and Dragon Kid defeated the Blood Warriors team of Ryo Saito and Genki Horiguchi to win the Open the Twin Gate Championship. They lost the title to Blood Warriors representatives Cima and Ricochet on 17 July. On 19 November, Pac lost the Open the Brave Gate Championship to Ricochet, ending his record reign at 447 days. On 9 February 2012, Junction Three was forced to disband, after losing a fourteen-man elimination tag team match to Blood Warriors. On 4 March, Pac received a shot at Dragon Gate's top title, the [Open the Dream Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_Open_the_Dream_Gate_Championship "Dragon Gate Open the Dream Gate Championship"), but was defeated by defending champion, Cima. On 25 March 2012, Pac joined Masato Yoshino, Naruki Doi, Ricochet, and [Rich Swann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rich_Swann "Rich Swann") and formed World-1 International.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] On 6 May 2012, Pac, alongside World-1 International members Masato Yoshino and Naruki Doi defeated Genki Horiguchi H.A.Gee.Mee!, Jimmy Kanda, and Ryo "Jimmy" Saito at the 2012 Dead or Alive pay-per-view to become the 34th Open the Triangle Gate Champions.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] On 22 July, Pac made his final Dragon Gate appearance, during which he, Yoshino and Doi successfully defended the Open the Triangle Gate Championship in a three-way match.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

### WWE (2012–2018)

#### British Ambition (2012–2013)

In July 2012, it was reported that Satterley had signed a contract with WWE. He joined WWE's [developmental territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farm_team#Professional_wrestling "Farm team") [NXT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_(WWE_brand) "NXT (WWE brand)") and made his television debut under the ring name **Adrian Neville** on 16 January 2013 episode of *[NXT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_NXT_(TV_series) "WWE NXT (TV series)")*, defeating [Sakamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazma_Sakamoto "Kazma Sakamoto").

Neville formed the British Ambition tag team with [Oliver Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Grey "Oliver Grey"), and the English duo won a [tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Tag_Team_Championship#Inaugural_tournament "NXT Tag Team Championship") to crown the inaugural [NXT Tag Team Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Tag_Team_Championship "NXT Tag Team Championship") by defeating [The Wyatt Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wyatt_Family "The Wyatt Family") ([Luke Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Harper "Luke Harper") and [Erick Rowan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erick_Rowan "Erick Rowan")) in the finals on 13 February episode of *NXT*. British Ambition had earlier defeated 3MB ([Heath Slater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Slater "Heath Slater") and [Drew McIntyre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Galloway "Drew Galloway")) in the first round and [Kassius Ohno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hero "Chris Hero") and [Leo Kruger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Rose_(wrestler) "Adam Rose (wrestler)") in the semi-finals. With Grey out injured with a torn [ACL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anterior_cruciate_ligament "Anterior cruciate ligament") (in storyline inflicted by the Wyatt Family), Neville chose [Bo Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bo_Dallas "Bo Dallas") as his new partner in April. On 24 April on *NXT*, Neville lost to [Antonio Cesaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Cesaro "Antonio Cesaro") while challenging for his [United States Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_United_States_Championship "WWE United States Championship"). On 8 May episode of *NXT*, Neville and Dallas failed in their first defence of the tag title when they lost them to the Wyatt Family.

#### NXT Champion (2013–2015)

On 29 May episode of *NXT*, Neville competed in an [NXT Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Championship "NXT Championship") number one contender [battle royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_royal_(professional_wrestling) "Battle royal (professional wrestling)"), eliminating [Mason Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_Ryan "Mason Ryan") and [Bray Wyatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bray_Wyatt "Bray Wyatt") (who had eleven and two eliminations respectively) to make it to the final two participants, but his former tag partner Dallas eliminated him to win the match. Later, Dallas won his title match to become NXT Champion. Neville continued his feud with the Wyatt Family by losing to them in two six-man tag matches, once while teaming with [Corey Graves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Graves "Corey Graves") and Kassius Ohno, and once with Graves and [William Regal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Regal "William Regal"). On 20 June, Neville regained the NXT Tag Team Championship from the Wyatt Family with a new partner in Graves. On 6 August episode of *NXT*, Neville faced [Dean Ambrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Ambrose "Dean Ambrose") for his United States Championship, which Ambrose retained when the other members of [The Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shield_(professional_wrestling) "The Shield (professional wrestling)") caused a disqualification. Neville and Graves lost the title to [The Ascension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ascension_(professional_wrestling) "The Ascension (professional wrestling)") ([Conor O'Brian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konnor_(wrestler) "Konnor (wrestler)") and [Rick Victor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_(wrestler) "Viktor (wrestler)")) in September. Following this, the two feuded after failing to regain the title.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

Neville first became number one contender to the NXT Championship on 27 November episode of *NXT* by defeating [Sami Zayn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sami_Zayn "Sami Zayn"), after both men previously tied a beat the clock challenge. Neville faced former tag partner Bo Dallas for the title on the next episode and won by countout, which meant that the title did not change hands. Two episodes later, Neville faced Dallas again for the title in a [lumberjack match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_match_types#Lumberjack_match "Professional wrestling match types"), but lost when lumberjack [Tyler Breeze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_Breeze "Tyler Breeze") interfered. Neville once again became number one contender after Dallas failed to defeat Neville in a [beat-the-clock challenge match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_match_types#Beat_the_Clock_challenge "Professional wrestling match types") on 22 January episode of *NXT* that arose due to Dallas' boasting. At [NXT Arrival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Arrival "NXT Arrival"), Neville won the NXT Championship from Dallas in a [ladder match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladder_match "Ladder match"). A rematch was scheduled for the 20 March episode of *NXT*, which he won after debuting a new finisher, the [imploding 450° splash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_aerial_techniques#Imploding_450°_splash "Professional wrestling aerial techniques").[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

Neville's first feud as champion began following a non-title match against [Brodus Clay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brodus_Clay "Brodus Clay"), where Neville won by countout. The feud ended with Neville scoring a No Disqualification match victory over Clay to successfully defend the NXT Championship. At [NXT TakeOver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_TakeOver "NXT TakeOver"), Neville successfully defended the NXT Championship against [Tyson Kidd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyson_Kidd "Tyson Kidd"). Two weeks later, Neville retained his championship against Kidd again after [Natalya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalya_Neidhart "Natalya Neidhart") prevented Kidd from using a chair. On 31 July episode of *NXT*, he successfully defended his title against Tyler Breeze by disqualification as Tyson Kidd attacked Neville after being super kicked by Neville. On 8 September, Neville made his first appearance on *[Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Raw "WWE Raw")* as part of an NXT showcase match to promote [NXT TakeOver: Fatal 4-Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_TakeOver:_Fatal_4-Way "NXT TakeOver: Fatal 4-Way"), he teamed with Sami Zayn to defeat Kidd and Breeze in a tag team match. On 11 September at the NXT TakeOver: Fatal 4-Way event, Neville successfully defended his NXT Championship against Sami Zayn, Tyler Breeze and Tyson Kidd in a fatal four-way match. On 4 December, Neville became the longest reigning NXT Champion of all time, surpassing the record set by Bo Dallas (which was surpassed by [Finn Bálor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_B%C3%A1lor "Finn Bálor") in April 2016). Later, on 11 December at the [NXT TakeOver: R Evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_TakeOver:_R_Evolution "NXT TakeOver: R Evolution") event, Neville lost the championship to Zayn, ending his reign at 287 days. When Zayn offered a handshake, Neville kicked it away and instead hugged Zayn in respect.

The following week on 18 December episode of *NXT*, Neville faced [Kevin Owens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Owens "Kevin Owens"), but the match ended in a double countout. Owens powerbombed Neville into the apron after the match. Neville received a rematch on 15 January 2015 episode of *NXT*, but failed to regain the title. Neville went on to compete in a NXT Championship number one contender's tournament, defeating Tyson Kidd in the quarterfinals on 28 January and [Baron Corbin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baron_Corbin "Baron Corbin") in the semifinals on 4 February, but lost in the finals to [Finn Bálor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_B%C3%A1lor "Finn Bálor") at [NXT TakeOver: Rival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_TakeOver:_Rival "NXT TakeOver: Rival"), which marked his final NXT appearance.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

#### Various feuds (2015–2016)

On 30 March 2015 episode of *[Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Raw "WWE Raw")*, Neville made his main roster debut, now under the mononym "Neville", defeating [Curtis Axel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Axel "Curtis Axel"). He then defeated [Bad News Barrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade_Barrett "Wade Barrett") at the [Extreme Rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_Rules_(2015) "Extreme Rules (2015)") pay-per-view the following month. Following this, Neville competed in the [2015 King of the Ring tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_the_Ring_(2015) "King of the Ring (2015)"), where he was defeated by Barrett in the finals. Following this, Neville began a feud with Bo Dallas, setting up a match at [Elimination Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elimination_Chamber_(2015) "Elimination Chamber (2015)"), which Neville won.

Neville then entered a feud with [Stardust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Rhodes "Cody Rhodes") in a [comic book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_book "Comic book") inspired rivalry. At [SummerSlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(2015) "SummerSlam (2015)") on 23 August, Neville teamed up with actor [Stephen Amell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Amell "Stephen Amell"), defeating the team of Stardust and Barrett. At [Night of Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_of_Champions_(2015) "Night of Champions (2015)") in September, Neville teamed with [The Lucha Dragons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lucha_Dragons "The Lucha Dragons") ([Kalisto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalisto_(wrestler) "Kalisto (wrestler)") and [Sin Cara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinta_de_Oro "Cinta de Oro")) in a loss to Stardust and the Ascension. At the [Survivor Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_(2015) "Survivor Series (2015)") pre-show, Neville competed in a traditional [Survivor Series match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_match "Survivor Series match"), teaming with [The Dudley Boyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dudley_Boyz "The Dudley Boyz") ([Bubba Ray Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubba_Ray_Dudley "Bubba Ray Dudley") and [D-Von Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D-Von_Dudley "D-Von Dudley")), [Goldust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldust "Goldust") and [Titus O'Neil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titus_O%27Neil "Titus O'Neil") in a victory over Stardust, The Ascension, [The Miz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Miz "The Miz") and Bo Dallas. On 21 December episode of *Raw*, Neville won the [Slammy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slammy_Award "Slammy Award") for "Breakout Star of the Year".

At the [Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_(2016) "Royal Rumble (2016)") on 24 January 2016, Neville competed in his first [Royal Rumble match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_match "Royal Rumble match") as the number 16 entrant, lasting over 10 minutes before being eliminated by [Luke Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Harper "Luke Harper"). In March 2016, Neville injured his ankle during a match with [Chris Jericho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Jericho "Chris Jericho") on *Raw*, rendering him out of action for an estimated two to three months and forcing him to miss [WrestleMania 32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_32 "WrestleMania 32"), at which he was scheduled to compete.

On 19 July, at the [2016 WWE draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_WWE_draft "2016 WWE draft"), Neville was drafted to [Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_(WWE_brand) "Raw (WWE brand)"). On 25 July episode of *Raw*, Neville made his televised return, defeating Curtis Axel. Neville teamed with Sami Zayn to defeat [The Dudley Boyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dudley_Boyz "The Dudley Boyz") ([Bubba Ray Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubba_Ray_Dudley "Bubba Ray Dudley") and [D-Von Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D-Von_Dudley "D-Von Dudley")) at [SummerSlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(2016) "SummerSlam (2016)"). The following night on *Raw*, Neville failed to defeat Kevin Owens to qualify for a fatal four-way elimination match for the vacant [WWE Universal Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Universal_Championship "WWE Universal Championship") after the interference of Chris Jericho. On 31 October episode of *Raw*, Neville participated in a battle royal for a spot on Team Raw at [Survivor Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_(2016) "Survivor Series (2016)"), but was eliminated by eventual winner [Braun Strowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braun_Strowman "Braun Strowman").

#### Cruiserweight Champion (2016–2017)

On 18 December at [Roadblock: End of the Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadblock:_End_of_the_Line "Roadblock: End of the Line"), Neville returned to seemingly congratulate [Cruiserweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Cruiserweight_Championship "NXT Cruiserweight Championship") [Rich Swann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rich_Swann "Rich Swann") after his match only to attack Swann and his opponent [T. J. Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T._J._Perkins "T. J. Perkins"), turning [heel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heel_(professional_wrestling) "Heel (professional wrestling)") and joining the Cruiserweight division. The following night on *Raw*, Neville cemented his villainous turn after claiming that the fans only cheered him because he was "vulnerable or small" and proceeded to attack Swann along with [Brian Kendrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Kendrick "Brian Kendrick") after he interrupted him, followed by Perkins after he attempted to save Swann. Over the coming weeks, Neville defeated multiple Cruiserweights in singles competition including Perkins, [Cedric Alexander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedric_Alexander "Cedric Alexander"), [Lince Dorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lince_Dorado "Lince Dorado") and Swann in a non-title match.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] On 29 January 2017 at the [Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_(2017) "Royal Rumble (2017)"), Neville defeated Swann to capture the Cruiserweight Championship for the first time, marking his first title win on the main roster. Neville then started a feud with [Jack Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Gallagher_(wrestler) "Jack Gallagher (wrestler)"), and successfully defended his title at [Fastlane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fastlane_(2017) "Fastlane (2017)") against Gallagher.

The following night on *Raw*, Neville defended his title against Swann successfully. After the match, [Austin Aries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Aries "Austin Aries") seemingly appeared to interview Neville only to attack him and start a feud between the two. At [WrestleMania 33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_33 "WrestleMania 33"), Neville defeated Aries in his WrestleMania in-ring debut. Aries won a fatal-four-way match two days later on *[205 Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_205_Live "WWE 205 Live")*, to earn a rematch against Neville at [Payback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payback_(2017) "Payback (2017)"), which Neville lost by disqualification, retaining the title as a result. Around this time, Neville formed an alliance with T. J. Perkins, who assisted him in his rivalry with Aries, in exchange for a promised championship opportunity.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Neville defeated Aries again in a submission match at [Extreme Rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_Rules_(2017) "Extreme Rules (2017)") to retain the Cruiserweight Championship, concluding their rivalry in the process. He ended his alliance with Perkins the following night, attacking him and later going on to successfully defend his championship against him on 6 June episode of *205 Live*.

Neville then began feuding with [Akira Tozawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Tozawa "Akira Tozawa") who was granted a championship match for [Great Balls of Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Great_Balls_of_Fire "WWE Great Balls of Fire") by [Titus O'Neil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titus_O%27Neil "Titus O'Neil"), in which Neville retained the title. He was also set to defend his title against Tozawa for the second time at [SummerSlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(2017) "SummerSlam (2017)"), until the 14 August episode of *Raw*, where Neville lost the championship to Tozawa, ending his record setting reign at 197 days. However, Neville won the title back from Tozawa on the SummerSlam pre-show, making him the first-ever two-time WWE Cruiserweight Champion with the current belt.

Afterwards, he was confronted by [Enzo Amore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzo_Amore "Enzo Amore"), the latest member of the Cruiserweight division. On 24 September at [No Mercy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Mercy_(2017) "No Mercy (2017)"), Neville dropped the title to Amore after being hit with a low blow while the referee was distracted. The following night on *Raw*, a double turn took place; Amore turned heel by disparaging the cruiserweight division, while Neville turned [face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_(professional_wrestling) "Face (professional wrestling)") when he spoke on behalf of the cruiserweights and attempted to attack Amore. Amore revealed he had a no-contact clause, which prevents any of the cruiserweights of receiving a future title shot should they attack him, but Neville attacked him nonetheless after the cruiserweights prevented Amore from leaving. The next night on *205 Live*, after Neville defeated [Ariya Daivari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariya_Daivari "Ariya Daivari"), he was attacked by Amore with a crutch.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

#### Contract dispute and departure (2017–2018)

On 9 October 2017, Neville walked out on *Raw*, on which he was scheduled to lose to Enzo Amore. According to multiple sources Neville appeared to have quit the promotion, with one report stating that he had been unhappy for a while. WWE denied that Neville had left the promotion. By 13 November, there had reportedly been "positive discussions" between Neville and WWE with some in the company believing he could be on his way back. In January 2018, WWE froze Neville's contract, keeping him under contract indefinitely or until a release is agreed upon. On 24 August, it was reported that Neville was no longer under contract.

### Return to Dragongate (2018–2019)

On 2 October 2018, two months after his WWE contract expired and almost exactly one year after he initially walked out on WWE, Satterley returned to [Dragongate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_(wrestling) "Dragon Gate (wrestling)"). Using his former ring name, Pac, he aligned himself with [Eita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eita_Kobayashi "Eita Kobayashi") and the villainous [R.E.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.D._(professional_wrestling) "R.E.D. (professional wrestling)") stable. In his return match, he pinned [Shingo Takagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shingo_Takagi "Shingo Takagi") at [Korakuen Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korakuen_Hall "Korakuen Hall"). On 4 December, he defeated [Masato Yoshino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masato_Yoshino "Masato Yoshino") to win the [Open the Dream Gate Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_the_Dream_Gate_Championship "Open the Dream Gate Championship"), the first [world championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_championship#World_championships "Professional wrestling championship") win in his career, at Dragon Gate's "Fantastic Gate 2018" from Korakuen Hall. He held the championship until 21 July 2019, when he lost to [Ben-K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-K "Ben-K"), ending his reign at 229 days.

### All Elite Wrestling (2019–2026)

#### Various feuds (2019–2020)

On 8 January 2019, Pac was announced as one of the first signees to [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling") (AEW). At AEW's inaugural rally in [Jacksonville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville,_Florida "Jacksonville, Florida"), Pac interrupted [Adam Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Page "Adam Page"), setting up a feud between the two wrestlers. At AEW's second rally in Las Vegas, a match between Page and Pac was officially announced for the company's inaugural event, [Double or Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_or_Nothing_(2019) "Double or Nothing (2019)"). However, in late May the match was cancelled due to "creative differences". They wrestled a match for independent promotion Wrestle Gate Pro on 17 May a week before Double or Nothing. Page won the match by disqualification and after the match, Pac attacked Page's knee and said that his goal all along was to injure Page and with that done, he had no reason to show up at Double or Nothing, thus establishing himself as a heel in the process. This was done to write the match off the card. On 23 August, Pac was revealed as [Jon Moxley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Moxley "Jon Moxley")'s replacement at [All Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Out_(2019) "All Out (2019)") against [Kenny Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Omega "Kenny Omega") after Moxley was forced to pull out of the event due to injury. At All Out on 31 August, Pac defeated Omega by making him pass out in a crucifix hold (commonly known as the Rings of Saturn or the Brutaliser). On the premiere episode of *[Dynamite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Dynamite "AEW Dynamite")* on 2 October, Pac defeated Page, thus rekindling their feud. At [Full Gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Gear_(2019) "Full Gear (2019)") on 9 November, Pac was defeated by Page, thereby suffering his first singles loss in AEW. Four days later on 13 November episode of *Dynamite*, Pac defeated Page to end the feud.

By the end of 2019 and going into 2020, Pac began a prolonged feud with Kenny Omega. After losing to him in a rematch on the [Thanksgiving Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving_Eve_Dynamite_(2019) "Thanksgiving Eve Dynamite (2019)") special episode of *Dynamite* on 27 November, Pac began relentlessly pursuing a [rubber match](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/rubber_match "wikt:rubber match"). After attacking Omega's friend [Michael Nakazawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Nakazawa "Michael Nakazawa"), Omega agreed to the match on 5 February 2020. The two wrestled in a 30-minute [Iron Man match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_match "Iron Man match") on 26 February episode of *Dynamite*, which Pac lost after the match went to [sudden death overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudden_death_(sport) "Sudden death (sport)"). After the loss, Pac's post-match interview was interrupted by [Orange Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Cassidy "Orange Cassidy"), leading to a match between the two being arranged for the [Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(2020) "Revolution (2020)") event on 29 February, in which Pac was victorious.

#### Death Triangle (2020–2024)

On 4 March 2020 episode of *Dynamite*, Pac formed an alliance with the [Lucha Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucha_Brothers "Lucha Brothers") ([Penta El Zero Miedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penta_El_Zero_Miedo "Penta El Zero Miedo") and [Rey Fenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9nix_(wrestler) "Fénix (wrestler)")) called the [Death Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Triangle "Death Triangle"), attacking [Orange Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Cassidy "Orange Cassidy") and [Best Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Friends_(professional_wrestling) "Best Friends (professional wrestling)") ([Chuck Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Taylor_(wrestler) "Chuck Taylor (wrestler)") and [Trent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trent_Beretta "Trent Beretta")). Afterwards, Pac was unable to travel due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic") and became absent from AEW television for eight months, returning to AEW as a face on 11 November episode of *Dynamite* where he confronted [Eddie Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Kingston "Eddie Kingston") On the following episode of *Dynamite*, Death Triangle was reformed after Fénix and Pentagón Jr. saved Pac from an attack by Kingston, [The Butcher and The Blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Butcher_and_The_Blade "The Butcher and The Blade").

At [New Year's Smash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_New_Year%27s_Smash "AEW New Year's Smash") on 13 January 2021, Pac defeated Kingston. At [Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(2021) "Revolution (2021)") on 7 March, Pac and Fénix won a [Casino Tag Team Battle Royale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_royal_(professional_wrestling)#Casino_Battle_Royale "Battle royal (professional wrestling)") to determine the next challengers for the [AEW World Tag Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_World_Tag_Team_Championship "AEW World Tag Team Championship"). They challenged [The Young Bucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Young_Bucks "The Young Bucks") (Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson) for the tag team championship on 14 April episode of *Dynamite*, but were defeated. Ranked as the #2 contender for the [AEW World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_World_Championship "AEW World Championship"), Pac and [Orange Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Cassidy "Orange Cassidy") faced off in a rematch to decide the #1 contender for [Kenny Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Omega "Kenny Omega") at [Double or Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_or_Nothing_(2021) "Double or Nothing (2021)"). The match ended in a double count-out, due to interference from Omega and [Don Callis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Callis "Don Callis"). However, Tony Khan signed a three-way match for Double or Nothing, in which Omega retained his championship.

After Double or Nothing, Pac started a feud with [Andrade El Idolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrade_El_Idolo "Andrade El Idolo"), which led to a match on 9 September episode of *Rampage*, where Pac was defeated. Pac and Andrade had a rematch on 22 October episode of *Rampage*, where Pac emerged victorious. At [Full Gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Gear_(2021) "Full Gear (2021)"), Pac teamed with [Cody Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Rhodes "Cody Rhodes") against Andrade and [Malakai Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakai_Black "Malakai Black") in a winning effort. On 23 February edition of *Dynamite*, Pac again teamed with Penta El Zero Miedo, who was now using the name 'Penta Oscuro', to defeat Kings Of The Black Throne ([Brody King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brody_King "Brody King") and Malakai Black). At [Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(2022) "Revolution (2022)")'s [buy-in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(2022)#The_Buy-In "Revolution (2022)") show, Pac and Oscuro teamed with [Erick Redbeard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erick_Redbeard "Erick Redbeard") in a trios match against the House of Black (now completed with [Buddy Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Murphy "Buddy Murphy")), which they lost.

In June, Pac participated in a tournament for the [AEW All-Atlantic Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_All-Atlantic_Championship "AEW All-Atlantic Championship"), defeating [Buddy Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Matthews "Buddy Matthews") on 8 June edition of *Dynamite*, advancing to the finals.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] On 26 June at [Forbidden Door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_Door "Forbidden Door"), Pac defeated [Miro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miro_(wrestler) "Miro (wrestler)"), [Malakai Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakai_Black "Malakai Black") and [Clark Connors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Connors "Clark Connors") in a fatal four-way match to become the inaugural champion.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Death Triangle won the then-vacated AEW World Trios Championship on 7 September episode of *Dynamite*.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] He lost his All-Atlantic Championship to Orange Cassidy on 12 October episode of Dynamite, ending his reign at 108 days. On 11 January 2023 edition of *Dynamite*, Death Triangle lost their Trios titles to [the Elite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elite_(professional_wrestling) "The Elite (professional wrestling)") (Kenny Omega and [the Young Bucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Young_Bucks "The Young Bucks")) in the finale of a [Best of Seven Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_match_types#Best_of_Series_match "Professional wrestling match types"). During that series of matches, Pac suffered a major nose injury during a ladder match at [Full Gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Gear_(2022) "Full Gear (2022)"). Following the conclusion of the series with the Elite, Pac became inactive in order to heal his injury.

On 12 July 2023, episode of *Dynamite*, Pac returned from the nose injury and was announced as the fifth member of the [Blackpool Combat Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool_Combat_Club "Blackpool Combat Club")'s team for the [Blood and Guts match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_and_Guts_(2023) "Blood and Guts (2023)") the following week, alongside [Jon Moxley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Moxley "Jon Moxley"), [Claudio Castagnoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Castagnoli "Claudio Castagnoli"), [Wheeler Yuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheeler_Yuta "Wheeler Yuta") and [Konosuke Takeshita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konosuke_Takeshita "Konosuke Takeshita"). Pac entered as the BCC's second member, but the team lost against [The Golden Elite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Golden_Elite "The Golden Elite"). Castagnoli, frustrated with how Pac left the match, challenged him to a match for the [Ring of Honor World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ROH_World_Championship "ROH World Championship") at [Death Before Dishonor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Before_Dishonor_(2023) "Death Before Dishonor (2023)") the Friday after Blood and Guts, which Pac lost. On 26 July episode of *Dynamite*, he faced the luchador [Gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_(wrestler) "Gravity (wrestler)"), with Pac winning via submission. During the match, Pac was reinjured. The exact injury was not publicly disclosed, but it was described as serious in nature and took Pac out of action for several months.

On 6 March 2024 edition of *Collision*, Pac made his return, as a face, to save Eddie Kingston and Penta from an attack from The Elite (who are now heels and including [Kazuchika Okada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazuchika_Okada "Kazuchika Okada")). On 21 April at [Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Dynasty "AEW Dynasty"), Pac unsuccessfully challenged Okada for the [AEW Continental Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Continental_Championship "AEW Continental Championship"). On 10 July episode of *Dynamite*, Pac defeated Claudio Castagnoli, [Kyle Fletcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_Fletcher "Kyle Fletcher"), and [Tomohiro Ishii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomohiro_Ishii "Tomohiro Ishii") to earn the right to challenge for the AEW International Championship (formerly known as the All-Atlantic Championship). On 26 May at [Double or Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_or_Nothing_(2024) "Double or Nothing (2024)"), Death Triangle unsuccessfully challenged the [Bang Bang Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Bang_Gang "Bang Bang Gang") ([Jay White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_White "Jay White") and the [Gunns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gunns "The Gunns")) for the AEW World Trios Championships.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

#### Death Riders (2024–2026)

On 25 August 2024 at [All In](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_In_(2024) "All In (2024)"), Pac teamed with Claudio Castagnoli and Wheeler Yuta in the [Four-way London Ladder match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladder_match "Ladder match") for the [AEW World Trios Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_World_Trios_Championship "AEW World Trios Championship"), which they won. On 7 September at [All Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Out_(2024) "All Out (2024)"), Pac failed to capture the AEW International Championship from [Will Ospreay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Ospreay "Will Ospreay"). Later in the show, Pac turned heel again when he held Yuta back and prevented him from interfering in a violent attack on Yuta's [Blackpool Combat Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool_Combat_Club "Blackpool Combat Club") (BCC) teammate [Bryan Danielson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Danielson "Bryan Danielson") at the hands of his other BCC teammates Castagnoli and [Jon Moxley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Moxley "Jon Moxley"), officially joining the BCC in the process. In November 2024, the BCC rebranded to the "[Death Riders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Riders "Death Riders")" a nod to Moxley's nickname in [New Japan Pro-Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Japan_Pro-Wrestling "New Japan Pro-Wrestling"). During his time in the Death Riders, Pac and the other members constantly interfered in Moxley's title defences to help him win. On 6 April 2025 at [Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Dynasty_(2025) "AEW Dynasty (2025)"), Pac, Castagnoli, and Yuta successfully defended their titles against [Rated FTR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rated_FTR "Rated FTR") ([Cash Wheeler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_Wheeler "Cash Wheeler"), [Cope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_(wrestler) "Edge (wrestler)"), and [Dax Harwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dax_Harwood "Dax Harwood")). On the following episode of *Dynamite*. Pac suffered a foot injury during his match with [Swerve Strickland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swerve_Strickland "Swerve Strickland"). On 16 April at [*Dynamite*: Spring BreakThru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEW_Spring_BreakThru "AEW Spring BreakThru"), Castagnoli and Yuta with Moxley filling in for Pac, lost the trios titles to [Samoa Joe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoa_Joe "Samoa Joe"), [Katsuyori Shibata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsuyori_Shibata "Katsuyori Shibata"), and [Powerhouse Hobbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powerhouse_Hobbs "Powerhouse Hobbs"), ending their reign at 234 days.
At [All Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Out_(2025) "All Out (2025)") on 20 September, Pac made his return, assisting Moxley to defeat [Darby Allin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darby_Allin "Darby Allin") in a [Coffin match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffin_match "Coffin match"). The Death Riders were defeated by Allin, [Roderick Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roderick_Strong "Roderick Strong"), and The Conglomeration ([Mark Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Briscoe "Mark Briscoe"), [Orange Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Cassidy "Orange Cassidy"), and [Kyle O'Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_O%27Reilly "Kyle O'Reilly")) in [Blood and Guts match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_and_Guts_match "Blood and Guts match") on 12 November at the [namesake event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_%26_Guts_(2025) "Blood & Guts (2025)"). On 22 November at [Full Gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Gear_(2025) "Full Gear (2025)"), Pac defeated Darby Allin. On 24 November, Pac was announced as a participant in the [2025 Continental Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Continental_Classic "2025 Continental Classic"), where he was placed in the Gold League. On 14 January 2026 at [*Dynamite*: Maximum Carnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AEW_Dynamite_special_episodes#2026 "List of AEW Dynamite special episodes"), Pac was defeated by Allin in a Full Gear rematch, ending their feud. ## Professional wrestling style and persona

Pac utilized a [corkscrew shooting star press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_aerial_techniques#Corkscrew_shooting_star_press "Professional wrestling aerial techniques") as a [finishing maneuver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finisher_(wrestling) "Finisher (wrestling)"); previously known as the *Red Arrow*, it is now referred to as the *Black Arrow*. After turning into a villain in December 2016, he started using a [double underhook crossface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_holds#Scissored_armbar_crossface "Professional wrestling holds") submission maneuver called the *Rings of Saturn* as a finisher in tribute to [Perry Saturn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Saturn "Perry Saturn"). After leaving WWE, he changed the move's name to the *Brutalizer*. During his independent career and the first part of his WWE run, he was known as "the man that gravity forgot" due to his high-flying style and aerial attacks.[*[unreliable source?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources "Wikipedia:Reliable sources")*]

After his heel turn and move to [205 Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/205_Live "205 Live"), he became known as the "King of the Cruiserweights" and adopted the gimmick of a deluded man who referred to himself as a king and viewed the [Cruiserweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Cruiserweight_Championship "NXT Cruiserweight Championship") as his crown. [Mark Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Henry "Mark Henry") assisted him in creating his villainous character, including methods of agitating the crowd such as climbing up to the top rope to tease his *Red Arrow* move, only to jump down without performing the move and lock in a submission. Upon leaving WWE, Pac tweaked his villainous character by dropping the "king" aspect and presenting himself as a self-proclaimed "bastard" with no morals or empathy.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

## Personal life

Satterley married his girlfriend Natalie in June 2015. Satterley was a lifelong supporter of his hometown football club [Newcastle United F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C. "Newcastle United F.C."), and a fan of British [ska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ska "Ska") band [The Specials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Specials "The Specials"); he chose "Neville" (shortened from "Adrian Neville") as his WWE [ring name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_name "Ring name") in part as a reference to former member [Neville Staple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neville_Staple "Neville Staple"). He was a close friend of fellow professional wrestlers [Bryan Danielson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Danielson "Bryan Danielson"), [Finn Bálor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_B%C3%A1lor "Finn Bálor"), [Kenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenta_Kobayashi "Kenta Kobayashi"), [Kevin Owens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Owens "Kevin Owens"), [Sami Zayn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sami_Zayn "Sami Zayn"), [Shingo Takagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shingo_Takagi "Shingo Takagi"), and [Zack Sabre Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zack_Sabre_Jr "Zack Sabre Jr"). Satterley's ring name, Pac — which he used on the independents and latterly in AEW — came from the nickname given to him by his father.

## Death

On 27 September 2026 at about 4:00 p.m. [CST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone "Central Time Zone"), roughly 21 hours after his match at [All Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Out_(2026) "All Out (2026)"), a passerby called [911](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/911_(emergency_telephone_number) "911 (emergency telephone number)") after finding Satterley slumped over and unresponsive in a rental car in the parking lot of a [Cava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cava_(restaurant) "Cava (restaurant)") restaurant in [Des Plaines, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Des_Plaines,_Illinois "Des Plaines, Illinois"). Satterley was pronounced dead at the scene by paramedics at the age of 40. The following day, a spokesperson for Des Plaines confirmed that there were no signs of foul play or trauma and Satterley's death "didn't appear suspicious". His cause of death was later determined to be [bronchial asthma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchial_asthma "Bronchial asthma"), and the death was ruled [natural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_causes "Natural causes") by the Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office. His death was announced later that night by [All Elite Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Elite_Wrestling "All Elite Wrestling") on their [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(social_network) "X (social network)") account.

Following the announcement of his death, tributes came from across the professional wrestling industry, including [Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwayne_%22The_Rock%22_Johnson "Dwayne \"The Rock\" Johnson"), [The Young Bucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Young_Bucks "The Young Bucks"), [John Cena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cena "John Cena"), [Tony Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Khan "Tony Khan"), and [Paul "Triple H" Levesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_%22Triple_H%22_Levesque "Paul \"Triple H\" Levesque"), amongst others.[*[excessive citations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources#Bundling_citations "Wikipedia:Citing sources")*]

In that same night's episode of [Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consejo_Mundial_de_Lucha_Libre "Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre") (CMLL) *Lunes Clásico*, KeMalito paid tribute to Satterley during his entrance and after his match. [Bayley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayley_(wrestler) "Bayley (wrestler)"), [Penta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentag%C3%B3n_Jr. "Pentagón Jr."), [Becky Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Becky_Lynch "Becky Lynch"), and [Kevin Owens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Owens "Kevin Owens") also wore armbands with "Ben" and "Pac" written on them. [Total Nonstop Action Wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_Nonstop_Action_Wrestling "Total Nonstop Action Wrestling") (TNA) dedicated their 1 October episode of *[Impact!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNA_Impact!
