# Tom Bateman (actor)

**Thomas Jonathan Bateman** (born 15 March 1989) is a British actor best known for his roles as [Giuliano de' Medici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuliano_de%27_Medici "Giuliano de' Medici") in the [Starz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starz "Starz") historical fantasy drama series *[Da Vinci's Demons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da_Vinci%27s_Demons "Da Vinci's Demons")* (2013–2015), as Bouc in the mystery films *[Murder on the Orient Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_on_the_Orient_Express_(2017_film) "Murder on the Orient Express (2017 film)")* (2017) and *[Death on the Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_on_the_Nile_(2022_film) "Death on the Nile (2022 film)")* (2022), and as Matt Pierce in the [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_(streaming_service) "Peacock (streaming service)") comedy thriller *[Based on a True Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Based_on_a_True_Story_(TV_series) "Based on a True Story (TV series)")* (2023–2024).

## Early life

Thomas Jonathan Bateman was born in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford "Oxford"), Oxfordshire, to a music teacher father and primary-school teacher mother. He has 13 siblings, including a twin brother named Merlin.

Bateman studied drama at the [London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Academy_of_Music_and_Dramatic_Art "London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art") (LAMDA), where he appeared in a production of *[Much Ado About Nothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Much_Ado_About_Nothing "Much Ado About Nothing")* with [Catherine Tate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Tate "Catherine Tate") and [David Tennant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Tennant "David Tennant"). He later joined [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh "Kenneth Branagh")'s company at the [Garrick Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrick_Theatre "Garrick Theatre"), where he acted alongside [Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench "Judi Dench") in *[The Winter's Tale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Winter%27s_Tale "The Winter's Tale")*.

## Personal life

In 2017, Bateman started a relationship with actress [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley "Daisy Ridley") after meeting on the set of *[Murder on the Orient Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_on_the_Orient_Express_(2017_film) "Murder on the Orient Express (2017 film)")*. During Ridley's promotion of *[Sometimes I Think About Dying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sometimes_I_Think_About_Dying "Sometimes I Think About Dying")* at the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival") in January 2023, she confirmed that she and Bateman had married.
