# Neagley

***Neagley*** ([/ˈniːli/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English")) is an American [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_television "Action television") [crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_fiction "Crime fiction") television series created by [Nick Santora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Santora "Nick Santora") and [Nicholas Wootton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Wootton "Nicholas Wootton") for [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video"). A spin-off of the television series *[Reacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reacher_(TV_series) "Reacher (TV series)"),* it stars [Maria Sten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sten "Maria Sten") as Frances Neagley, a former military police officer who becomes a private investigator. The series follows Neagley as she investigates the suspicious death of a childhood friend.

*Neagley* premiered its first season in its entirety on September 16, 2026. In October 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

## Premise

Frances Neagley, a [private investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_investigator "Private investigator") based in [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois "Chicago, Illinois"), seeks the truth after her childhood friend dies in a suspicious accident that leads her to Echo Canyon Farm, a peaceful [cult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cult "Cult")-like commune that [fronts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_organization "Front organization") an illegal [human experimentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_subject_research "Human subject research") operation run by Lawrence Cole and Invection Pharmaceuticals CEO Pierce Woodrow.

## Production

### Development

The series was ordered by [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") in October 2024. Nick Santora and Nicholas Wootton developed, executive produced, and are co-showrunners of the series, which was produced by [Amazon MGM Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_MGM_Studios "Amazon MGM Studios") and [Skydance Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skydance_Television "Skydance Television"), along with [CBS Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Studios "CBS Studios"). The series is also executive-produced by Adam Higgs, [Sam Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Hill_(director) "Sam Hill (director)"), Lisa Kussner, [Paula Wagner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paula_Wagner "Paula Wagner"), [Lee Child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Child "Lee Child"), [Don Granger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Granger "Don Granger"), [David Ellison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ellison "David Ellison"), [Dana Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana_Goldberg_(producer) "Dana Goldberg (producer)"), and [Matt Thunell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Thunell "Matt Thunell"). By August 2025, [Paramount Television Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Television_Studios "Paramount Television Studios") had taken over production of the series following the merger of Skydance Television's parent company [Skydance Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skydance_Media "Skydance Media") with CBS Studios' parent [Paramount Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Global "Paramount Global") into [Paramount Skydance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Skydance "Paramount Skydance"). In October 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

### Writing

The series' writing team is composed of [Nick Santora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Santora "Nick Santora") & [Nicholas Wootton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Wootton "Nicholas Wootton"), Adam Higgs, Kyle Long, and Lacey Herbert. *Neagley* explores the title character's life in greater detail than in *[Reacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reacher_(TV_series) "Reacher (TV series)")*. Sten said: "Neagley has a lot going on in her life. She has a history, and she has a life lived. She has some quite severe personal issues of her own that she's grappling with… I'm just thrilled to get going and sink my teeth in".

### Casting

Sten reprises her role of Frances Neagley from *Reacher* with [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson "Alan Ritchson") appearing as [Jack Reacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Reacher "Jack Reacher") in a guest starring role. In February 2025, [Greyston Holt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greyston_Holt "Greyston Holt"), [Adeline Rudolph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adeline_Rudolph "Adeline Rudolph"), Jasper Jones, [Matthew Del Negro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Del_Negro "Matthew Del Negro") and [Damon Herriman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damon_Herriman "Damon Herriman") were cast as series regulars.

### Filming

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") began in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto "Toronto"), Ontario, Canada, on February 18, 2025, and [wrapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrap_(filmmaking) "Wrap (filmmaking)") on June 7, 2025.

## Release

All eight episodes of *Neagley* were released on September 16, 2026.

## Reception

The [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") reported a 94% approval rating, based on 31 critic reviews, with an average rating of 7.2/10. [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 67 out of 100 based on 15 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".
