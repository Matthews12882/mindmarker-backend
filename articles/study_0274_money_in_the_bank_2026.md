# Money in the Bank (2026)

The 2026 **Money in the Bank**, also promoted as **Money in the Bank: New Orleans**, was a [professional wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling "Professional wrestling") [premium live event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premium_live_event "Premium live event") (PLE) produced by [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE"). It was held for wrestlers from the promotion's [main roster brands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_brand_extension "WWE brand extension"), [Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_(WWE_brand) "Raw (WWE brand)") and [SmackDown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SmackDown_(WWE_brand) "SmackDown (WWE brand)"). It was the 17th annual [Money in the Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Money_in_the_Bank "WWE Money in the Bank") event and took place on Saturday, October 10, 2026, at the [Smoothie King Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoothie_King_Center "Smoothie King Center") in [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans,_Louisiana "New Orleans, Louisiana").

The event is based around the [Money in the Bank ladder match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_in_the_Bank_ladder_match "Money in the Bank ladder match") in which multiple wrestlers compete to retrieve a briefcase hanging above the ring that contains a contract that guarantees a world championship match of the winner's choosing at any time within the next year. The men's match was won by Raw's [Penta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentag%C3%B3n_Jr. "Pentagón Jr."), while the women's match was won by SmackDown's [Tiffany Stratton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_Stratton "Tiffany Stratton").

Three other matches were contested at the event. [Liv Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liv_Morgan "Liv Morgan") defeated defending champion [Stephanie Vaquer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Vaquer "Stephanie Vaquer") and [Becky Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Becky_Lynch "Becky Lynch") in a [triple threat match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_threat_match "Triple threat match") to win Raw's [Women's World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_World_Championship_(WWE) "Women's World Championship (WWE)"), [Oba Femi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oba_Femi "Oba Femi") defeated [Bronson Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronson_Reed "Bronson Reed"), and in the opening bout, [Roman Reigns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Reigns "Roman Reigns") defeated [LA Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Knight "LA Knight") to retain Raw's [World Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heavyweight_Championship_(WWE) "World Heavyweight Championship (WWE)").

Critics gave the event mostly positive grades, but fans gave the event negative reviews with criticisms geared towards the opening match, the women’s Money in the Bank ladder match, and the Women’s World Championship match mainly due to the finishes and the winners of such.

## Production

### Background

[Money in the Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Money_in_the_Bank "WWE Money in the Bank") is an annual [premium live event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premium_live_event "Premium live event") (PLE) produced by [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE "WWE") since 2010, generally held between May and July. It was originally broadcast exclusively via [pay-per-view](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay-per-view "Pay-per-view") (PPV) before it began [simulcasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulcasting "Simulcasting") on WWE's [livestreaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestreaming "Livestreaming") platforms in 2014. As of August 2021, it is considered one of the promotion's five biggest PLEs of the year, along with [WrestleMania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania "WrestleMania"), [Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble "Royal Rumble"), [SummerSlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam "SummerSlam"), and [Survivor Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series "Survivor Series"), referred to as the "Big Five". The [concept](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gimmick_(professional_wrestling) "Gimmick (professional wrestling)") of the event comes from WWE's established [Money in the Bank ladder match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_in_the_Bank_ladder_match "Money in the Bank ladder match").

[New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans,_Louisiana "New Orleans, Louisiana") was originally set to host [WrestleMania 42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_42 "WrestleMania 42") in April 2026, but on May 22, 2025, it was revealed that the city would no longer be hosting the event. This came as part of a larger agreement with New Orleans, in which the city would host a future WrestleMania as well as the 2026 Money in the Bank. The following day, WWE Chief Content Officer [Paul "Triple H" Levesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_%22Triple_H%22_Levesque "Paul \"Triple H\" Levesque") confirmed the date for the 17th Money in the Bank as Saturday, August 29, 2026. However, the event's date would be pushed back twice, as on October 22, 2025, WWE announced a new date of Sunday, September 6, 2026, with the venue confirmed as the [Smoothie King Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoothie_King_Center "Smoothie King Center"), but on June 8, 2026, WWE revealed that Money in the Bank would instead take place one month later on Saturday, October 10 at the same venue, with [Sunday Night's Main Event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunday_Night%27s_Main_Event "Sunday Night's Main Event") taking place on September 6, at the [State Farm Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Farm_Arena "State Farm Arena") in [Atlanta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta,_Georgia "Atlanta, Georgia"). This will be the first Money in the Bank to take place in October, also making it the latest calendar date for the event. *Fightful Select* reported that the change to October was due to a scheduling conflict with broadcast partners, while [Bryan Alvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Alvarez "Bryan Alvarez") of *[Wrestling Observer Figure Four Online](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrestling_Observer_Figure_Four_Online "Wrestling Observer Figure Four Online")* said it was more so due to conflicts with WWE's touring dates in September as production trucks could not get to New Orleans in time. As part of Money in the Bank weekend, WWE had a merchandise store at the Smoothie King Center open from Thursday, October 8 through the end of the PLE itself on Saturday. "[A Milli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Milli "A Milli")" by [Lil Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Wayne "Lil Wayne") was the official theme song for the event.

The Money in the Bank ladder match features multiple wrestlers using [ladders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladder_match "Ladder match") to retrieve a briefcase hanging above the ring. The briefcase contains a contract that guarantees the winner a match for a championship at any time within the next year. The 2026 event featured wrestlers from the [Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_(WWE_brand) "Raw (WWE brand)") and [SmackDown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SmackDown_(WWE_brand) "SmackDown (WWE brand)") [brand divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_brand_extension "WWE brand extension"); male wrestlers competed for a contract to grant them a match for a [championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_championship "Professional wrestling championship") of their choosing, while female wrestlers competed for a [WWE Women's Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Women%27s_Championship "WWE Women's Championship") or [Women's World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_World_Championship_(WWE) "Women's World Championship (WWE)") match contract.

### Broadcast outlets

In addition to airing on traditional PPV worldwide, the event was available to livestream on [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN "ESPN")'s [direct-to-consumer streaming service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_(streaming_service) "ESPN (streaming service)") in the United States, [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") in most international markets, and [SuperSport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperSport_(South_African_broadcaster) "SuperSport (South African broadcaster)") in Sub-Saharan Africa. This marked the first Money in the Bank to livestream on ESPN in the United States, as WWE's contract with [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_(streaming_service) "Peacock (streaming service)") to air main roster PLEs expired at the conclusion of [Clash in Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clash_in_Paris "Clash in Paris") in August 2025.

### Storylines

The event included matches that resulted from scripted storylines. Results were predetermined by WWE's writers on the Raw and SmackDown brands, while storylines were produced on WWE's weekly television programs, *[Monday Night Raw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monday_Night_Raw "Monday Night Raw")* and *[Friday Night SmackDown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_Night_SmackDown "Friday Night SmackDown")*.

Qualifying matches for the men's Money in the Bank ladder match consisted of six [triple threat matches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_threat_match "Triple threat match") held across episodes of Raw and SmackDown, with three spots for each brand. The first spot was determined on the September 7 episode of *Raw* where [World Tag Team Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Tag_Team_Championship_(WWE) "World Tag Team Championship (WWE)") [Bron Breakker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bron_Breakker "Bron Breakker") defeated [Ethan Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Page "Ethan Page") and [Rey Mysterio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rey_Mysterio "Rey Mysterio"). Qualifiers continued on the following episode where [Je'Von Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Je%27Von_Evans "Je'Von Evans") earned the second spot by defeating World Tag Team Champion [Austin Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory "Austin Theory") and [Big Cass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Cass "Big Cass"). The third spot was determined on that week's *SmackDown*, where [WWE United States Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_United_States_Champion "WWE United States Champion") [Trick Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trick_Williams "Trick Williams") defeated [Randy Orton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Orton "Randy Orton") and [AAA World Cruiserweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAA_World_Cruiserweight_Champion "AAA World Cruiserweight Champion") [Rey Fénix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rey_F%C3%A9nix "Rey Fénix"). The fourth spot was determined on the September 21 episode of *Raw*; originally, [Solo Sikoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Sikoa "Solo Sikoa") was set to participate in the match, however, the 946 ([Royce Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royce_Keys "Royce Keys"), [Bronco Nima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronco_Nima "Bronco Nima"), and [Lucien Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucien_Price_(wrestler) "Lucien Price (wrestler)")) attacked and abducted Sikoa. Raw General Manager [Adam Pearce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Pearce "Adam Pearce") announced [Penta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentag%C3%B3n_Jr. "Pentagón Jr.") as Sikoa's replacement, who went on to defeat [Dominik Mysterio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominik_Mysterio "Dominik Mysterio") and [Dragon Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Lee_(wrestler) "Dragon Lee (wrestler)") to qualify. The fifth spot was determined on that week's *SmackDown* where [CM Punk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CM_Punk "CM Punk") defeated [Gunther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunther_(wrestler) "Gunther (wrestler)") and [Finn Bálor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_B%C3%A1lor "Finn Bálor"). The final spot was determined on the following week's *SmackDown*, where [Kevin Owens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Owens "Kevin Owens") defeated [Cody Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Rhodes "Cody Rhodes") and [Johnny Gargano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Gargano "Johnny Gargano").

Qualifying matches for the women's Money in the Bank ladder match consisted of six triple threat matches held across episodes of Raw and SmackDown, with three spots for each brand. The first spot was determined on the September 7 episode of *Raw* where [Sol Ruca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sol_Ruca "Sol Ruca") defeated [Lyra Valkyria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyra_Valkyria "Lyra Valkyria") and [Maxxine Dupri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxxine_Dupri "Maxxine Dupri"). Qualifiers continued on the following episode where [Lola Vice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Vice "Lola Vice") earned the second spot by defeating [NXT Women's Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Women%27s_Champion "NXT Women's Champion") [Kelani Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelani_Jordan "Kelani Jordan") and [WWE Women's Intercontinental Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Women%27s_Intercontinental_Champion "WWE Women's Intercontinental Champion") [Raquel Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raquel_Rodriguez_(wrestler) "Raquel Rodriguez (wrestler)"). The third spot was determined on that week's *SmackDown*, where [WWE Women's United States Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Women%27s_United_States_Champion "WWE Women's United States Champion") [Jacy Jayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacy_Jayne "Jacy Jayne") defeated [Alexa Bliss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexa_Bliss "Alexa Bliss") and [Jade Cargill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Cargill "Jade Cargill"). The fourth spot was determined on the September 21 episode of *Raw* where [Roxanne Perez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roxanne_Perez "Roxanne Perez") defeated [Iyo Sky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iyo_Sky "Iyo Sky") and [AAA Reina de Reinas Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAA_Reina_de_Reinas_Champion "AAA Reina de Reinas Champion") [La Catalina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Catalina "La Catalina"). The fifth spot was determined on that week's *SmackDown* where [Lash Legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lash_Legend "Lash Legend") defeated [Charlotte Flair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Flair "Charlotte Flair") and [Giulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giulia_(wrestler) "Giulia (wrestler)"). The final spot was determined on the following week's *SmackDown* where [Tiffany Stratton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_Stratton "Tiffany Stratton") defeated [Candice LeRae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_LeRae "Candice LeRae") and [Tatum Paxley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatum_Paxley "Tatum Paxley").

On the August 3 episode of *Raw*, as [Roman Reigns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Reigns "Roman Reigns") celebrated his successful [World Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heavyweight_Championship_(WWE) "World Heavyweight Championship (WWE)") defense at [SummerSlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SummerSlam_(2026) "SummerSlam (2026)"), he was interrupted by [LA Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Knight "LA Knight") who wanted a title opportunity. Reigns denied Knight's challenge and stated that he is sick of giving opportunities to non-deserving people. Later that night, [Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucha_Libre_AAA_Worldwide "Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide") General Manager [Rey Mysterio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rey_Mysterio "Rey Mysterio") proposed something to Reigns, which was later announced as a tournament where the winner would face Reigns for the title on [the September 14 episode of *Raw* in Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Raw_in_Mexico_City_special "WWE Raw in Mexico City special"). Penta won the tournament, however at the show, Reigns retained the title. Following the match and after *Raw* went off air, Knight came to the ring and attacked Reigns with a BFT. Reigns subsequently accepted to face Knight for the title at Money in the Bank and the match was made official. On October 9, it was announced that the match would be the opener of the show.

After [Oba Femi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oba_Femi "Oba Femi") defeated [Brock Lesnar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brock_Lesnar "Brock Lesnar") at Night 1 of SummerSlam, Femi started being referred to as the future of WWE, which upset [The Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Vision_(professional_wrestling) "The Vision (professional wrestling)")'s [Bron Breakker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bron_Breakker "Bron Breakker"). After some confrontations, a match between both men was scheduled for [Sunday Night's Main Event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunday_Night%27s_Main_Event "Sunday Night's Main Event"), where Femi defeated Breakker by [disqualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disqualification_(professional_wrestling) "Disqualification (professional wrestling)") after interference from a returning [Bronson Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronson_Reed "Bronson Reed"), who viciously attacked Femi. On the following *Raw*, The Vision (Breakker, Reed, [Austin Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory "Austin Theory"), and [Logan Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_Paul "Logan Paul")) attacked Femi, sidelining him for a few weeks. On the September 21 episode, Femi returned and fended off The Vision. The following week, Femi revealed a contract for a match between him and Reed at Money in the Bank, with his signature already on the contract. Femi called out Reed, who accepted the challenge and signed the contract.

At Night 1 of [WrestleMania 42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_42 "WrestleMania 42"), [Liv Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liv_Morgan "Liv Morgan") defeated [Stephanie Vaquer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Vaquer "Stephanie Vaquer") to win the [Women's World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_World_Championship_(WWE) "Women's World Championship (WWE)"). Afterwards on the April 27 episode of *Raw*, Morgan's stablemates in the [The Judgment Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Judgment_Day "The Judgment Day") ([Raquel Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raquel_Rodriguez_(wrestler) "Raquel Rodriguez (wrestler)") and [Roxanne Perez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roxanne_Perez "Roxanne Perez")) attacked Vaquer, sidelining her for months. After interfering in Perez's match on the August 3 episode of *Raw*, Morgan was confronted by a returning [Becky Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Becky_Lynch "Becky Lynch"), who wanted a shot at the title, turning [face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_(professional_wrestling) "Face (professional wrestling)") in the process. Vaquer returned afterwards and attacked Morgan in the ring before having a staredown with Lynch, also signaling her intentions for the title. Vaquer was granted a rematch for the title on the September 7 episode where despite Lynch trying to help Vaquer, Morgan retained the title against Vaquer after inference from Rodriguez and Perez. However, five days later at Night 4 of [WWE's South America Live Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_show "House show"), Vaquer defeated Morgan to win the title in a match that featured Lynch as the [special guest referee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_guest_referee "Special guest referee"). On the October 5 episode of *Raw*, Lynch and Morgan were scheduled for a match to determine the #1 contender for the title at Money in the Bank, however, after Vaquer appeared to fend off Perez and Rodriguez, both Morgan and Lynch accidentally hit Vaquer, which prompted Vaquer to attack both women, with the match ending in a no-contest. Vaquer called out Raw General Manager [Adam Pearce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Pearce "Adam Pearce") and demanded to defend the title against Lynch and Morgan in a triple threat match at Money in the Bank, which Pearce agreed.

### Preliminary matches

The PLE began with [Roman Reigns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Reigns "Roman Reigns") defending the [World Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heavyweight_Championship_(WWE) "World Heavyweight Championship (WWE)") against [LA Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Knight "LA Knight"). During the match, Knight performed an Elbow Drop on Reigns for a nearfall. Reigns performed a Superman Punch on Knight for a nearfall. After Knight avoided a Spear attempt, he attempted a Superplex on Reigns, who countered. Knight eventually performed the Superplex and performed a Burning Hammer for a nearfall. After attempting a BFT, Knight performed a reverse DDT and went to the middle rope. Reigns made it to his feet and performed a Uranage slam for a nearfall. Outside the ring, Knight shoved Reigns into the ring steps. Back inside, Reigns performed a Superman Punch. A BFT and a Spear by Knight and a Spear by Reigns all resulted in nearfalls. Back outside, Knight sidestepped Reigns, sending him into the timekeeper's area. Knight performed a Powerbomb on Reigns through the announce table. In the end, Knight performed a top-rope Elbow Drop on Reigns and attempted another BFT. Reigns countered it, and performed two Superman Punches and a Spear on Knight to retain the title. After the match, The 946 ([Royce Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royce_Keys "Royce Keys"), [Bronco Nima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronco_Nima "Bronco Nima"), and [Lucien Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucien_Price_(wrestler) "Lucien Price (wrestler)")) showed up, followed by [Solo Sikoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Sikoa "Solo Sikoa"), [Jey Uso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jey_Uso "Jey Uso"), and [Jimmy Uso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Uso "Jimmy Uso"). The teams brawled until [Jaida Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaida_Parker "Jaida Parker") showed up in an SUV and took The 946 away.

The next match was the women's Money in the Bank ladder match, featuring Sol Ruca, Lola Vice, and Roxanne Perez from Raw and Tiffany Stratton, Jacy Jayne, and Lash Legend from SmackDown. During the match, Stratton performed a Swanton Bomb off a ladder onto the other opponents, where she hit her head on the back of the padded floor. The participants got close to retrieving the briefcase at various points but the others prevented it from happening. In the climax, Perez performed Pop Rox on Stratton and climbed the ladder before Ruca performed a Sol Snatcher on Perez. After Legend subdued Ruca, she climbed the ladder where Stratton showed up. She struck Legend with the briefcase and retrieved it to win the match.

In the third match, Oba Femi took on Bronson Reed. During the match, [Austin Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory "Austin Theory") and [Maxxine Dupri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxxine_Dupri "Maxxine Dupri") appeared to distract Femi, only for [Logan Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_Paul "Logan Paul") to make the save before being ejected from ringside. Theory handed brass knuckles to Reed, who used them to strike Femi twice before performing the Tsunami for a nearfall. In the end, Reed performed a Death Valley Driver on Femi and attempted a Moonsault, but Femi moved out of the way. Femi performed a Chokeslam on Theory onto the ring apron before performing a Fall from Grace on Reed to win the match.

### Main event

The main event was the men's Money in the Bank ladder match, featuring Bron Breakker, Je'Von Evans, and Penta from Raw and Trick Williams, Kevin Owens, and CM Punk from SmackDown.

## Reception

Jason Powell of *Pro Wrestling Dot Net* called the World Heavyweight Championship match "very good", stating that Roman Reigns and LA Knight "did a great job of creating suspense with some excellent nearfalls", with the key being that "Knight looked so competitive with Reigns that he will gain something in defeat if they frame it properly on television". For the women's Money in the Bank ladder match, Powell noted Stratton "wasn't the most popular choice to win in the building", and called the men's Money in the Bank ladder match a "mixed bag", as it "felt overbooked with the ambulance, including the big stunt of Evans taking a bump through a clearly gimmicked top", along with Penta's entertainment "erased by him winning the match". Powell concluded that the event was entertaining.

Brent Brookhouse of *[CBS Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Sports "CBS Sports")* graded the World Heavyweight Championship match B, stating that it "felt like it tried to do a little bit more than was needed". The women's Money in the Bank ladder match was graded C-, with Brookhouse calling it "a Money in the Bank match to forget". The Oba Femi vs. Bronson Reed match was graded B, with Brookhouse calling it a "solid enough big man match and the right man won". He also graded the Women's World Championship match B, stating that it was "another match where things got a bit clunky at times, but it was otherwise perfectly fine". The men's Money in the Bank ladder match was graded B-, as Brookhouse called it "good overall and had the right winner, but the ambulance bit was completely unnecessary and comes off as corny, given that it makes no sense for an ambulance's roof to shatter like thin plywood", but "WWE seems determined to lean on weird stunt stuff right now".

## Aftermath

### Raw

Penta began the following episode of *Raw* to talk about his Money in the Bank win while promising that he will cash in the contract and become a world champion. [Je'Von Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Je%27Von_Evans "Je'Von Evans") appeared to show respect to Penta before Breakker attacked Evans with a Spear.

Also on *Raw*, Oba Femi gave a promo about his win over Bronson Reed when Paul Heyman interrupted. Femi told Heyman that he was an "Oba Femi guy".

In the main event, the six-man tag team match between The 946 (Royce Keys, Bronco Nima, and Lucien Price) and The Bloodline (Jey Uso, Jimmy Uso, and Solo Sikoa) ended in a no-contest when Jaida Parker attacked the referee, leading to the teams brawling around the arena. Afterwards, [Carmelo Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmelo_Hayes "Carmelo Hayes") appeared and struck Sikoa with a crowbar, joining The 946 in the process.
