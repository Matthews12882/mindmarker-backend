# Qazi Touqeer

**Qazi Touqeer** is an Indian [singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singer "Singer"), [television personality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_personality "Television personality"), who won the show *[Fame Gurukul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fame_Gurukul "Fame Gurukul")* – along with [Ruprekha Banerjee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruprekha_Banerjee "Ruprekha Banerjee"). He sings in the [Kashmiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiri_language "Kashmiri language"), [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi "Hindi") and [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu "Urdu") languages. He is currently in the *[Bigg Boss 20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bigg_Boss_Hindi "Bigg Boss Hindi")* house.

## Early Life

Qazi Touqeer was born on 4 June 1986 in [Srinagar, Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srinagar,_Kashmir "Srinagar, Kashmir") to Mushtaq Ahmed Qazi, a lawyer who later embraced [Sufism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufism "Sufism"), and Syeda Rukhsana, a school teacher. His uncle is Qazi Rafi, a well-known Kashmiri singer. Touqeer also has a younger brother named Qazi Touseef, an entrepreneur.

## Career

In 2005, Qazi Touqeer won the first season of the show *[Fame Gurukul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fame_Gurukul "Fame Gurukul")*, India's version of *[Fame Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fame_Academy "Fame Academy"),* along with [Ruprekha Banerjee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruprekha_Banerjee "Ruprekha Banerjee"). During the finale, he received 2.5 [crore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crore "Crore") [paid SMS votes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Televoting "Televoting"), which was reported to be the highest number of votes received for a contestant on Indian Television.

The [President of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India "President of India"), in regards to Qazi Touqeer, declared him to be the hero of Kashmir. As a result of Qazi's success, a plethora of Kashmiri youth auditioned in *[Indian Idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Idol "Indian Idol")* tryouts, which were held in Srinagar, a city in the [Kashmir Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir "Kashmir").

Later that year, he released a new [album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album "Album") along with Ruprekha Banerjee titled "Yeh Pal". In 2008, He injured himself while rehearsing for his acting debut film, *Take Off*. Qazi was rehearsing at Ganesh Acharya Studios when he fell down and injured his neck.

In September 2026, Touqeer entered the twentieth season of the Hindi-language reality television show Bigg Boss as a contestant. His participation marked his return to mainstream television nearly two decades after he won the singing reality show Fame Gurukul in 2005. During his time on [Bigg Boss 20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bigg_Boss_(Hindi_TV_series)_season_20 "Bigg Boss (Hindi TV series) season 20"), Touqeer attracted attention for his interactions and activities in the house, including a prank that was later addressed by host [Salman Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salman_Khan "Salman Khan").
