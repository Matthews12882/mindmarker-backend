# Forgotten Island

***Forgotten Island*** is a 2026 American animated [fantasy comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_comedy "Fantasy comedy") film written and directed by [Joel Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Crawford_(director) "Joel Crawford (director)") and Januel Mercado. Produced by [DreamWorks Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DreamWorks_Animation "DreamWorks Animation"), the film features the voices of [H.E.R.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.E.R. "H.E.R."), [Liza Soberano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liza_Soberano "Liza Soberano"), [Dave Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Franco "Dave Franco"), [Jenny Slate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Slate "Jenny Slate"), [Manny Jacinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manny_Jacinto "Manny Jacinto"), [Dolly de Leon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_de_Leon "Dolly de Leon"), [Jo Koy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo_Koy "Jo Koy"), [Ronny Chieng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronny_Chieng "Ronny Chieng"), Kevin McCann, and [Lea Salonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lea_Salonga "Lea Salonga"). Set during the 1990s in the Philippines, the story follows two best friends, Jo and Raissa, who are trapped in the mystical world of Nakali, which they have grown up hearing stories of.

DreamWorks Animation officially announced the film in April 2025, with Crawford and Mercado attached to write and direct. They drew inspiration from their friendship and [Philippine mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_mythology "Philippine mythology"). Initial members of the cast were announced in October 2025, with further casting later confirmed the following March.

Following early "work-in-progress" screenings at the 2026 [CinemaCon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaCon "CinemaCon") and at the [2026 Annecy International Animation Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Annecy_International_Animation_Film_Festival "2026 Annecy International Animation Film Festival"), *Forgotten Island* was theatrically released in the United States on September 25, 2026, by [Universal Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Pictures "Universal Pictures"). The film received critical acclaim for its visuals and cultural representation, with some calling it one of DreamWorks Animation's best films. It has grossed $54 million worldwide against an $80 million budget.

## Plot

In the Philippines during the 1990s, Jo Yuson presents a story to her class about the island of Nakali (short for "[nakalimutan](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/nakalimutan "wikt:nakalimutan")", meaning "forgotten" in Tagalog) and the dreaded [manananggal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manananggal "Manananggal"). When her classmate Reynaldo mocks her, Jo attacks him and is sent to the principal's office, where she meets Raissa Molina, another classmate who had defended her. Jo invites Raissa to a sleepover with her grandmother, Fatima, who tells them about her travels to Nakali. The girls become best friends, making matching charm bracelets that represent significant moments in their friendship. Fatima later dies of old age, leaving Jo heartbroken.

After graduating high school, Raissa reveals that she is attending [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology "California Institute of Technology") in America, disappointing Jo, who is trying to make ends meet by working at a fast food restaurant. At Raissa's farewell party, Jo overhears Raissa's mother expressing disapproval over their friendship. Jo and Raissa sneak out to the beach, where Jo discovers a magical [sand dollar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sand_dollar "Sand dollar") that serves as a portal to Nakali. Despite Raissa's hesitation, they enter together, awakening the next morning with no memory of the previous night. Raissa discovers half of her head shaved and that she has grown wings belonging to Manang, a Manananggal who attacks them. They escape with help from Batiba, a [batibat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batibat "Batibat") who offers to send them home in exchange for their bracelets and the memories attached to them, as she perpetually stays in her cave and lives vicariously through the memories of others due to her [agoraphobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agoraphobia "Agoraphobia"). The girls refuse and flee with Raww, a were-[aspin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askal "Askal") who claims to have hung out with them the previous night and becomes their guide.

At a market, the trio meet Bungi, a [tiyanak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiyanak "Tiyanak") who tells them that the "Jewel of the Sea" they seek was stolen by a [duwende](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duende "Duende") king. Their search leads them to Mermahn, a [kataw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataw_(Philippine_mythology) "Kataw (Philippine mythology)") who claims the sand dollar is in his kingdom. Feeling excluded, Raww lashes out until Jo reassures him and shares her fear of being abandoned by Raissa. Following Raww's suggestion, Jo takes them to [Kapre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapre "Kapre") Village, where they learn that they had previously saved the villagers from Manang, after which Raissa stole her wings. Manang attacks again, separating Bungi from the group. After escaping, the group learns that Mermahn lied about the sand dollar, while Raww reveals that he works for Batiba, who is actually a demon. He also admits that he had lured Jo and Raissa to Batiba the previous night, after which they relinquished their memories of their time in Nakali.

Batiba recaptures them and restores their memories, revealing that Jo, afraid of losing Raissa, had hidden the sand dollar after Batiba gave it to them to prevent them from being able to return home. Furious, Raissa reveals that she herself disapproves of Jo, and leaves to retrieve the sand dollar, ending their friendship. Manang attacks Raissa and reclaims her wings, but Raissa informs her that Batiba has stolen from her. Jo then reveals that she offered Batiba her memories of their friendship in exchange for saving Raissa. Raissa is sent back to the Philippines, while Jo stays behind. Back on the beach, a remorseful Raissa adds a leftover firecracker to her bracelet, sending her back to Nakali. Reconciling with Raww, she enters Jo's fading memories and admits how much their friendship shaped who she is, reconciling with her. Manang arrives, and Jo and Raissa, aided by Raww, Bungi, and Mermahn, attempt to fight her, but Batiba, finally understanding the value of memories, willingly returns Manang's stolen memories, causing Manang to become docile. Jo finally accepts Raissa's decision to attend college overseas, and the girls head home while Batiba finally goes outside.

A few years later, Jo visits Raissa, who has become more laid back, at Caltech to promote a book she has published based on their adventures. Before returning home, the two use their bracelets to reopen the portal so they can visit Nakali once more.

## Production

### Development

In April 2025, [DreamWorks Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DreamWorks_Animation "DreamWorks Animation") announced that an original animated [adventure comedy film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_comedy_film "Adventure comedy film") inspired by [Philippine mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_mythology "Philippine mythology"), titled *Forgotten Island*, was in development, with [Joel Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Crawford_(director) "Joel Crawford (director)") and Januel Mercado signed to direct. Crawford and Mercado, who had worked together at the studio since *[Kung Fu Panda 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kung_Fu_Panda_2 "Kung Fu Panda 2")* (2011), drew inspiration from their friendship.

### Casting

*Forgotten Island* had a primarily Filipino and [Filipino-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_Americans "Filipino Americans") cast.
In October 2025, [H.E.R.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.E.R. "H.E.R."), [Liza Soberano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liza_Soberano "Liza Soberano"), [Dave Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Franco "Dave Franco"), [Manny Jacinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manny_Jacinto "Manny Jacinto"), [Jenny Slate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Slate "Jenny Slate"), and [Lea Salonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lea_Salonga "Lea Salonga") were announced as part of the cast. In March 2026, [Jo Koy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo_Koy "Jo Koy"), [Dolly de Leon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_de_Leon "Dolly de Leon"), [Ronny Chieng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronny_Chieng "Ronny Chieng"), and Amielynn Abellera joined the cast.

Crawford and Mercado had H.E.R. in mind from the beginning for the role of Jo noting her previous work on a video for [Vogue Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Philippines "Vogue Philippines"). The directors found her persona to be "cool" for her tendency to wear sunglasses while noting her bubbly demeanor when talking about Filipino food which led the duo to find H.E.R. suitable for the role of Jo. Meanwhile, Soberano, originally auditioned for the role of Jo but was invited back to try for the role of Raissa instead. Crawford and Mercado found Soberano was able to bring humor and emotion to Raissa's character. Some of Soberano's audition lines made to the final film. Salonga and de Leon were approached by the production team for their roles.

### Animation and design

The film's animation style is similar to that of DreamWorks' previous film *[Puss in Boots: The Last Wish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puss_in_Boots:_The_Last_Wish "Puss in Boots: The Last Wish")* (2022), also directed by Crawford. Mercado and Crawford stated that the visual style was inspired by Filipino art and [1990s graphic design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_graphic_design "History of graphic design"). The animation style varies throughout the film, periodically shifting from its primary contemporary [computer-generated animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_animation "Computer animation") to [anime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anime "Anime")-influenced [2D animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_animation "Traditional animation"). These stylized 2D sequences are used during flashbacks, referencing the style of anime series such as *[Dragon Ball Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Z "Dragon Ball Z")*, *[Street Fighter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_Fighter "Street Fighter")*, and [*Sailor Moon*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailor_Moon_(TV_series) "Sailor Moon (TV series)"), depending on which character recalls a memory. These sequences were animated by [Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Manila "Metro Manila")-based studio [Snipple Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snipple_Animation "Snipple Animation"), reflecting the [history of Philippine studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outsourcing_of_animation_to_the_Philippines "Outsourcing of animation to the Philippines") working on outsourced anime series. At the film's Annecy presentation, the filmmakers highlighted the contributions of these Filipino animators, noting that the local culture served as the project's foundation rather than a mere influence. [Sony Pictures Imageworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Imageworks "Sony Pictures Imageworks") also assisted on the animation in some shots throughout the film.

## Music

In October 2025, it was announced that [Nathan Matthew David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Matthew_David "Nathan Matthew David") would compose the score for the film. Composing the score for *Forgotten Island*, David travelled throughout the Philippines to research on different [indigenous music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_the_Philippines "Music of the Philippines"), including by spending time in [Cordillera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Administrative_Region "Cordillera Administrative Region") and [Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinga_(province) "Kalinga (province)"), and with the [T'boli people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tboli_people "Tboli people") in [Lake Sebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Sebu "Lake Sebu"). He also recorded the [Bamboo Organ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamboo_Organ "Bamboo Organ"), a 19th-century church [pipe organ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_organ "Pipe organ") made with bamboo, in [Las Piñas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Pi%C3%B1as "Las Piñas"), [Metro Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Manila "Metro Manila"). Musician Farid Guinomla travelled from [Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindanao "Mindanao") to play and record the [gong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gong "Gong") and [kulintang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulintang "Kulintang") instruments in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles "Los Angeles"). The recording was done in [Abbey Road Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbey_Road_Studios "Abbey Road Studios") in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London "London") with the score's vocals contributed by Salonga.

The soundtrack album was released digitally by Universal's [Back Lot Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_Lot_Music "Back Lot Music") label on September 18, 2026, ahead of the film's release date. It features performances by local Filipino acts [Bini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bini_(group) "Bini (group)"), Lea Salonga, [SB19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SB19 "SB19"), [Francis Magalona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Magalona "Francis Magalona"), [Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katseye "Katseye")'s [Sophia Laforteza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_Laforteza "Sophia Laforteza"), [Ruby Ibarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruby_Ibarra "Ruby Ibarra") and Carl Angelo, in addition to leads H.E.R. and Liza Soberano.

All tracks are written by [Nathan Matthew David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Matthew_David "Nathan Matthew David"), except where noted.

## Marketing

The first trailer was released on March 25, 2026. On July 24, members of the cast took part in the 2026 [San Diego Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con "San Diego Comic-Con") to promote the film. They were joined by [Bini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bini_(group) "Bini (group)"), who performed one of the film's songs, titled "A Parallel World".

[Jollibee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jollibee "Jollibee") collaborated with DreamWorks and released food offerings tied to *Forgotten Island* in the Philippines.

## Release

*Forgotten Island* was released in the United States on September 25, 2026. A work-in-progress cut was screened at [CinemaCon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaCon "CinemaCon") in [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas "Las Vegas") on April 15, 2026, and at the [2026 Annecy International Animation Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Annecy_International_Animation_Film_Festival "2026 Annecy International Animation Film Festival") in France on June 24, receiving a five-minute standing ovation at the latter festival.

The film was released with limited direct competition, with the animated film *[The Cat in the Hat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cat_in_the_Hat_(2026_film) "The Cat in the Hat (2026 film)")* set to premiere in November.

### Home media

*Forgotten Island* will be released on [digital download](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_distribution "Digital distribution") on October 27, 2026.

## Reception

### Box office

As of October 14, 2026[[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Forgotten_Island&action=edit), *Forgotten Island* has grossed $33 million in the United States and Canada, and $21 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $54 million.

In the United States and Canada, *Forgotten Island* was released alongside *[Primetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_(film) "Primetime (film)")*, *[Heart of the Beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_the_Beast "Heart of the Beast")*, and *[Avengers Endgame: Encore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_Endgame:_Encore "Avengers Endgame: Encore")*, and was projected to gross $15–17 million from 3,548 theaters in its opening weekend. The film made $4.3 million on its opening day, and went on to debut to $12.8 million, finishing in fifth place. The film finished in sixth place in the next two weekends, grossing $7.2 million and $4.3 million, respectively. The FranchiseRe newsletter using the $14.6 million as the reference point, noted that *Forgotten Island* had a weaker than average opening gross for an original animated film from a major studio.

In the Philippines, *Forgotten Island* started strong with a ₱97.2 million box office gross on its opening weekend which marked as the best performing non-franchise DreamWorks Animation film of all time in the country.

### Critical response

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 94% of 120 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 7.8/10. The website's consensus reads: "A candy-colored ode to friendship and Filipino folklore, *Forgotten Island* bursts at the seams with imagination while never losing sight of the compelling relationship at its core." [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean "Weighted arithmetic mean"), assigned the film a score of 72 out of 100, based on 27 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews. Audiences polled by [CinemaScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaScore "CinemaScore") gave the film an average grade of "A" on an A+ to F scale, while 83% of those surveyed by [PostTrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PostTrak "PostTrak") said they would definitely recommend it.

Matt Goldberg of *[TheWrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TheWrap "TheWrap")* gave the film a three-and-a-half out of five rating and wrote, "With influences ranging from *[The Hangover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hangover "The Hangover")* to *[Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Sunshine_of_the_Spotless_Mind "Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind")*, the DreamWorks animated feature is admirably ambitious." Matt Zoller Seitz of *[RogerEbert.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RogerEbert.com "RogerEbert.com")* gave the film three-and-a-half out of four rating and says: "The movie is so imaginative, energetic, and sincere that it should be fun for just about everyone."
