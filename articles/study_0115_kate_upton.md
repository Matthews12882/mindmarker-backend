# Kate Upton

**Katherine Elizabeth Upton** (born June 10, 1992) is an American model and actress. She first appeared in the [*Sports Illustrated* Swimsuit Issue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated_Swimsuit_Issue "Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issue") in 2011, and was the [cover model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Sports_Illustrated_Swimsuit_Issue_cover_models "List of Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issue cover models") for the 2012, 2013, and 2017 issues. In addition, she was the subject of the 100th-anniversary *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")* cover. Upton has also appeared in the films *[Tower Heist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_Heist "Tower Heist")* (2011), *[The Other Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Other_Woman_(2014_film) "The Other Woman (2014 film)")* (2014), and *[The Layover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Layover_(film) "The Layover (film)")* (2017).

## Early life

Katherine Elizabeth Upton was born in [St. Joseph, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph,_Michigan "St. Joseph, Michigan"). She is the daughter of Shelley (née Davis), a former [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas "Texas") state tennis champion, and Jeff Upton, a high school [athletic director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_director "Athletic director"). Her uncle is former U.S. Representative [Fred Upton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Upton "Fred Upton"). Upton's great-grandfather, [Frederick Upton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_and_Louis_Upton "Frederick and Louis Upton"), was co-founder of appliance manufacturer and marketer [Whirlpool Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whirlpool_Corporation "Whirlpool Corporation").

In 1999, Upton moved with her family to [Melbourne, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne,_Florida "Melbourne, Florida"), where she was a student at [Holy Trinity Episcopal Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Trinity_Episcopal_Academy "Holy Trinity Episcopal Academy"), a private [Christian school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_school "Christian school"), although she did not graduate.

As a young equestrian, she showed at the [American Paint Horse Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Paint_Horse_Association "American Paint Horse Association") (APHA) and competed at a national level. With her horse Roanie, she won three APHA Reserve World Championships – 13 and Under Western Riding, 13 and Under Horsemanship, and 14–18 Western Riding. She was named the 13 and Under Reserve All-Around Champion, giving her a total of four reserve championships (second place). In addition, Upton ended up third overall on the APHA youth Top 20. With a second horse, Colby, she won 14–18 Western Riding and was included in the top five in 14–18 Horsemanship and 14–18 Western Pleasure in 2009.

## Career

Upton attended a [casting call](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casting_call "Casting call") in [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami "Miami") for [Elite Model Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_Model_Management "Elite Model Management") in 2008 and was signed the same day. She eventually moved to New York City, where she then signed with [IMG Models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMG_Models "IMG Models"). Upton first modeled clothing for [Garage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garage_(store) "Garage (store)") and [Dooney & Bourke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dooney_%26_Bourke "Dooney & Bourke"). She was the 2010–11 face of [Guess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guess_(clothing) "Guess (clothing)"). In 2011, Upton appeared in the *Sports Illustrated* Swimsuit Issue. She was featured in the [body paint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_paint "Body paint") section and named Rookie of the Year for the issue. She has since modeled for Beach Bunny Swimwear, and has appeared in *[Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_(magazine) "Complex (magazine)")*, as well as *[Esquire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_(magazine) "Esquire (magazine)")* as "*The* Woman of the Summer". Upton wore one of the Beach Bunny suits in the 2014 *Sports Illustrated* Swimsuit Issue.

In April 2011, an Internet video of her doing the [dougie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dougie "Dougie") hip-hop dance at a [Los Angeles Clippers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Clippers "Los Angeles Clippers") game went viral and served to increase her popularity. Guy Trebay of *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")* observed that this served to illustrate the power of social media to bestow stardom upon a model, which had previously been the domain of top designer runway shows. In April 2012, Upton appeared in a video of herself performing the Cat Daddy dance to the [song of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_Daddy "Cat Daddy") by [The Rej3ctz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rej3ctz "The Rej3ctz"), which also went viral.

Upton made an appearance in a June 2011 episode of *[Tosh.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosh.0 "Tosh.0")* and participated in the 2011 [Taco Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Bell "Taco Bell") All-Star Legends and Celebrity Softball Game at [Chase Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Field "Chase Field") in [Phoenix, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix,_Arizona "Phoenix, Arizona"), in July 2011. Upton's acting debut was in the film, *[Tower Heist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_Heist "Tower Heist")*, as Mr. Hightower's mistress. It was released in November 2011. She also appeared in *[The Three Stooges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Three_Stooges_(2012_film) "The Three Stooges (2012 film)")* as Sister Bernice.

In April 2012, she faced criticism for her raunchy portrayal the kind-hearted Sister Bernice, one of the sisters in the Sisters of Mercy Orphanage, in *[The Three Stooges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Three_Stooges_(2012_film) "The Three Stooges (2012 film)")*, where according to *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")*, towards the end of the film she emerges from a pool "in a [nun habit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_habit "Religious habit"), a [rosary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosary "Rosary"), and little else". [The Catholic League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_League_(U.S.) "Catholic League (U.S.)") criticised Upton's performance as "crudity", claiming that it was symptomatic of a willingness to "[trash] Christianity, especially Catholicism". However, the scene of Upton emerging from the pool in the revealing [bikini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bikini "Bikini") only appears in the film's [trailer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_(promotion) "Trailer (promotion)"). In the final film, her swimsuit only appears briefly in a couple of scenes.

Upton appeared on the cover of the 2012 *Sports Illustrated* Swimsuit Issue, released in February 2012. Upton has also recounted the negative aftereffects of the 2012 *Sports Illustrated* cover, as she was subjected to criticism and felt she had been objectified.

Upton has appeared in editorials for American, Italian, British, Spanish, German, and Brazilian *[Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(magazine) "Vogue (magazine)")*, *[Harper's Bazaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harper%27s_Bazaar "Harper's Bazaar")*, *[V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_(American_magazine) "V (American magazine)")*, *[Mademoiselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mademoiselle_(magazine) "Mademoiselle (magazine)")*, *[LOVE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LOVE_(magazine) "LOVE (magazine)")*, Italian, German, and American *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")*, *[Cosmopolitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmopolitan_(magazine) "Cosmopolitan (magazine)")*, *[Glamour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glamour_(magazine) "Glamour (magazine)")*, and *[Esquire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_(magazine) "Esquire (magazine)")*.
She has appeared on the covers of Italian, American, and British *[Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(magazine) "Vogue (magazine)")*, French and American *[Elle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_(magazine) "Elle (magazine)")*, American, Italian, and German *[GQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ "GQ")*, *[Esquire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_(magazine) "Esquire (magazine)")*, and *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_(magazine) "Vanity Fair (magazine)")*. She has appeared in advertising campaigns for [David Yurman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Yurman "David Yurman"), [Sam Edelman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Edelman "Sam Edelman"), [Betsey Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betsey_Johnson "Betsey Johnson"), Guess, [Victoria's Secret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria%27s_Secret "Victoria's Secret"), [Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Express,_Inc. "Express, Inc."), and [Bare Necessities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bare_Necessities_(company) "Bare Necessities (company)"). She was named the "new face" of [Bobbi Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobbi_Brown "Bobbi Brown") cosmetics in 2014.

In 2012, Upton was ranked the fifth-sexiest model by Models.com. She ranked number three on the American publication of *[AskMen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AskMen "AskMen")'s*  *Top 99 Women* for 2013. In 2012, she was listed on *[Maxim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine) "Maxim (magazine)")*'s Hot 100 list, citing her photo shoots with *Sports Illustrated*. Upton was the cover model for the 2013 *Sports Illustrated* Swimsuit Issue for the second consecutive year. Parts of her 2013 *[Sports Illustrated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated "Sports Illustrated")* pictorial were filmed in [Antarctica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctica "Antarctica") and Upton suffered from failing hearing and eyesight due to the extreme cold. After getting the November 2012 *[Vogue Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_Italia "Vogue Italia")* cover photographed by [Steven Meisel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Meisel "Steven Meisel") and the January 2013 *[Vogue UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_UK "Vogue UK")* cover, Upton landed the June 2013 *Vogue* cover by [Mario Testino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Testino "Mario Testino"). She was then photographed by [Annie Leibovitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Leibovitz "Annie Leibovitz") for the October 2013 100th anniversary cover of *Vanity Fair*.

In September 2013, Upton was named Model of the Year at the 10th annual Style Awards at the Mercedes-Benz Fashion Week in New York City. Upton appeared on the flip-side cover of the 2014 50th-anniversary *Sports Illustrated* Swimsuit Issue, the first time that type of cover was used by that magazine. Part of her shoot took place at [Cape Canaveral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Canaveral "Cape Canaveral"), capturing her in [zero gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_gravity "Zero gravity"). In April 2014, she appeared with [Cameron Diaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Diaz "Cameron Diaz"), [Leslie Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Mann "Leslie Mann"), and [Nikolaj Coster-Waldau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolaj_Coster-Waldau "Nikolaj Coster-Waldau") in the comedy *[The Other Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Other_Woman_(2014_film) "The Other Woman (2014 film)")* as Amber, one of Coster-Waldau's character's mistresses.

In 2014, Upton starred with [Tony Hale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Hale "Tony Hale") in [Lady Antebellum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Antebellum "Lady Antebellum")'s music video "[Bartender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartender_(Lady_Antebellum_song) "Bartender (Lady Antebellum song)")". *[People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_(magazine) "People (magazine)")* named Upton its Sexiest Woman for 2014, the first time the title has been awarded. Also in 2014, Upton was the centerpiece of a $40 million advertising campaign for Machine Zone's mobile app *[Game of War: Fire Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_War:_Fire_Age "Game of War: Fire Age")*. In 2017, Upton starred with [Alexandra Daddario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Daddario "Alexandra Daddario") in *[The Layover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Layover_(film) "The Layover (film)")*, a [road trip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_trip "Road trip") [sex comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_comedy "Sex comedy") directed by [William H. Macy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_H._Macy "William H. Macy"). The film follows two best friends on vacation as they fight over the same man during a layover in [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis "St. Louis"). Also that year, Upton filmed scenes in [James Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Franco "James Franco")'s film adaptation *[The Disaster Artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Disaster_Artist_(film) "The Disaster Artist (film)")*. Upton hosts fashion reality television show *Dress My Tour*, which premiered on [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu "Hulu") in 2024.

## Personal life

Upton is a committed [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christians "Christians"), who has been outspoken in defending her faith from critics who have suggested that her modeling career is incompatible with [religious values](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purity_culture "Purity culture").

During an interview with *[Elle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_(magazine) "Elle (magazine)")* magazine in 2013, she reflected on the importance of Christian faith to her life and career. Upton recalled an occasion during a photoshoot when someone joked about her [cross necklace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_necklace "Cross necklace"), saying "Why are you wearing a [cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_cross "Christian cross")? Like you would be religious" and then removing the necklace from her for the shoot. She says the incident "really affected" her and galvanized her commitment to expressing her faith. As a result, she tattooed a cross on the inside of her finger so that she could have "a cross with me, at all times".

Upton started dating then-[Detroit Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Tigers "Detroit Tigers") pitcher [Justin Verlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Verlander "Justin Verlander") in early 2014, and they were engaged in 2016. In 2017, the couple married in [Tuscany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuscany "Tuscany"), Italy. They have a daughter, Genevieve, born in 2018, and a son, Bellamy Brooks, born in 2025.

In 2014, nude photographs of Upton and several other female celebrities [were illegally leaked to the Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_celebrity_nude_photo_leak "2014 celebrity nude photo leak").
