# Keri Russell

**Keri Lynn Russell** (born March 23, 1976) is an American actress. Working mainly in dramatic television since the 1990s, she has received eight nominations for the [Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actress in a Drama Series"). She won a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award "Golden Globe Award") [in 1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/56th_Golden_Globe_Awards "56th Golden Globe Awards") for her lead role in the drama series *[Felicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felicity_(TV_series) "Felicity (TV series)")*, and has received eight [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award "Primetime Emmy Award") nominations and four additional Golden Globe Award nominations for her roles in *[The Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Americans "The Americans")* and *[The Diplomat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Diplomat_(American_TV_series) "The Diplomat (American TV series)")*. In 2017, she was honored with a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame") for her contributions to television.

Russell first appeared on [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel")'s revival of *[The Mickey Mouse Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mickey_Mouse_Club "The Mickey Mouse Club")* when she was 15 and was a regular cast member on the show from 1991 to 1994. She made her first film appearance in *[Honey, I Blew Up the Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey,_I_Blew_Up_the_Kid "Honey, I Blew Up the Kid")* (1992). Russell's other film appearances include *[We Were Soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Were_Soldiers "We Were Soldiers")* (2002), *[Mission: Impossible III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission:_Impossible_III "Mission: Impossible III")* (2006), *[Waitress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waitress_(2007_film) "Waitress (2007 film)")* (2007), *[Dawn of the Planet of the Apes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_of_the_Planet_of_the_Apes "Dawn of the Planet of the Apes")* (2014), and *[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Rise_of_Skywalker "Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker")* (2019).

On stage, she acted in [Neil LaBute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_LaBute "Neil LaBute")'s [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") play *[Fat Pig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fat_Pig "Fat Pig")* (2004–2005) and made her [Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_(theater) "Broadway (theater)") debut in the [Lanford Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanford_Wilson "Lanford Wilson") revival *[Burn This](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_This "Burn This")* (2019).

## Early life

Russell was born on March 23, 1976, in [Fountain Valley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Valley,_California "Fountain Valley, California"), a suburb of [Orange County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County,_California "Orange County, California"). Her mother, Stephanie Stephens, is a homemaker, and her father, David Russell, is a [Nissan Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nissan_Motors "Nissan Motors") executive. She has an older brother and a younger sister. The family lived in [Coppell, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppell,_Texas "Coppell, Texas"); [Mesa, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesa,_Arizona "Mesa, Arizona"); and [Highlands Ranch, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highlands_Ranch,_Colorado "Highlands Ranch, Colorado"), moving frequently due to her father's work.

## Career

### 1990–2002: Early roles and rise to prominence with *Felicity*

Russell danced in middle school and high school, and her dancing during her audition had helped her get cast on *[The All New Mickey Mouse Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_All_New_Mickey_Mouse_Club "The All New Mickey Mouse Club")* television show on the [Disney Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney_Channel "Disney Channel"). Russell was on the show from 1991 to 1994, encompassing seasons four to six.

In 1992, she appeared in *[Honey, I Blew Up the Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey,_I_Blew_Up_the_Kid "Honey, I Blew Up the Kid")* and in 1993, had a minor role on the sitcom *[Boy Meets World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_Meets_World "Boy Meets World")* as Mr. Feeny's niece. She appeared on *[Married... with Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Married..._with_Children "Married... with Children")* in a 1995 episode ("Radio Free Trumaine", production 9.24). She subsequently had several film and television roles, including the 1996 made-for-television film *[The Babysitter's Seduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Babysitter%27s_Seduction_(1996_film) "The Babysitter's Seduction (1996 film)").* That year she also had a role on the short-lived soap opera series *[Malibu Shores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_Shores "Malibu Shores").*

In 1994, Russell appeared as the "other woman" in [Bon Jovi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_Jovi "Bon Jovi")'s music video "[Always](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Always_(Bon_Jovi_song) "Always (Bon Jovi song)")". In 1997, she appeared in two episodes of *[Roar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roar_(1997_TV_series) "Roar (1997 TV series)")*.

From 1998 to 2002, Russell starred as the title character on the successful [WB Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_WB_Television_Network "The WB Television Network") series *[Felicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felicity_(TV_series) "Felicity (TV series)")*, and in 1999 won a Golden Globe for the role.

During the show's run, Russell appeared in the films *[Eight Days a Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eight_Days_a_Week_(film) "Eight Days a Week (film)")*, *[The Curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Curve_(1998_film) "The Curve (1998 film)")*, and *[Mad About Mambo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_About_Mambo "Mad About Mambo")*, all of which received only limited releases in North America. Her next role was in the film *[We Were Soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Were_Soldiers "We Were Soldiers")* (2002), as the wife of a United States serviceman during the Vietnam War. The film was released two months before the end of *Felicity*'s run.

### 2003–2012: Theater and film roles

When *Felicity* ended, Russell moved to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") and made her [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") stage debut in 2004 in [Neil LaBute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_LaBute "Neil LaBute")'s *[Fat Pig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fat_Pig "Fat Pig")*. In 2005, she returned to television and film, beginning with an appearance in the [Hallmark Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallmark_Hall_of_Fame "Hallmark Hall of Fame") television movie *[The Magic of Ordinary Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Magic_of_Ordinary_Days "The Magic of Ordinary Days")*, the theatrical film *[The Upside of Anger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Upside_of_Anger "The Upside of Anger")*, and the television miniseries *[Into the West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_West_(miniseries) "Into the West (miniseries)")*. In 2005, director [J. J. Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Abrams "J. J. Abrams") asked Russell to join the cast of *[Mission: Impossible III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission:_Impossible_III "Mission: Impossible III")*, and she accepted. She was screen tested for the role of Lois Lane in *[Superman Returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_Returns "Superman Returns")* but lost the part to [Kate Bosworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Bosworth "Kate Bosworth").

In mid-2006, Russell was chosen to be a celebrity spokeswoman for [CoverGirl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoverGirl "CoverGirl") cosmetics. In the summer of 2007, Russell appeared in *The Keri Kronicles*, a [reality show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_show "Reality show") [sitcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitcom "Sitcom") sponsored by CoverGirl that aired on [MySpace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MySpace "MySpace"); the show was filmed at Russell's home in Manhattan and spotlighted her life. Also in 2007, she played Melody on the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC "NBC") show *[Scrubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(season_6) "Scrubs (season 6)")*.

Russell next starred in the film *[Waitress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waitress_(2007_film) "Waitress (2007 film)")*, which marked the fourth time she played a pregnant woman. Her performance was positively received by critics, with Michael Sragow of *[The Baltimore Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Baltimore_Sun "The Baltimore Sun")* writing that Russell's performance had "aesthetic character" and "wields tenderness and fierceness with quiet heat". In 2007, Russell also completed roles in *[Grimm Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grimm_Love "Grimm Love")* and *[The Girl in the Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_in_the_Park "The Girl in the Park")*.

Russell next appeared in *[August Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Rush "August Rush")*, released in November 2007. She later appeared in *[Bedtime Stories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedtime_Stories_(film) "Bedtime Stories (film)")*. In an appearance on *[The View](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_View_(U.S._TV_series) "The View (U.S. TV series)")* on December 15, 2008, Russell said she got the part because Adam Sandler's wife Jackie had seen her in *Waitress* and suggested her for the movie. Russell voiced [Wonder Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman "Wonder Woman") in a [direct-to-video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-video "Direct-to-video") [animated feature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Woman_(2009_film) "Wonder Woman (2009 film)") released on March 3, 2009.

Russell starred in the [Tom Vaughan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Vaughan_(director) "Tom Vaughan (director)")-helmed *[Extraordinary Measures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_Measures_(film) "Extraordinary Measures (film)")* for [CBS Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Films "CBS Films"). The drama, released on January 22, 2010, was the company's first film to go into production. Russell plays Aileen Crowley, a mother who tries to build a normal home life for her sick children while her husband and an unconventional scientist race against time to find a cure.

Russell starred in the [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company "Fox Broadcasting Company") series *[Running Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Running_Wilde "Running Wilde")* from 2010 to 2011.

### 2013–present: *The Americans* and *The Diplomat*

From 2013 to 2018, Russell starred in the [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") drama series *[The Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Americans "The Americans")*, playing [Elizabeth Jennings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Jennings_(The_Americans) "Elizabeth Jennings (The Americans)"), a deep-undercover Russian [KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB "KGB") spy living as an American in the 1980s [Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War "Cold War") era. She appeared opposite [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys "Matthew Rhys"), who portrays her character's husband and spy partner. Russell and Rhys became partners in real life during this time. The series ended after six seasons. For her performance, she was nominated for three consecutive [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series"), as well as two [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Drama "Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Drama"). Emily St. James of *[Vox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_(magazine) "Vox (magazine)")* called her performance "gorgeously restrained".

In 2013, Russell starred in the science-fiction horror film *[Dark Skies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Skies_(2013_film) "Dark Skies (2013 film)")* and the romantic comedy film *[Austenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austenland_(film) "Austenland (film)")*. In 2014, she starred in the science fiction action film *[Dawn of the Planet of the Apes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_of_the_Planet_of_the_Apes "Dawn of the Planet of the Apes")*, a sequel to 2011's *[Rise of the Planet of the Apes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rise_of_the_Planet_of_the_Apes "Rise of the Planet of the Apes")*. In 2016, she starred as Serena Knight in the historical U.S. Civil War film *[Free State of Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_of_Jones_(film) "Free State of Jones (film)")*.

In 2017, Russell received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame "Hollywood Walk of Fame"). In 2018, Russell was announced to have joined the cast of the film *[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Rise_of_Skywalker "Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker")*, which was released on December 20, 2019. The film reunited her with J.J. Abrams, with whom she had worked on *Felicity* and *Mission: Impossible III*.

In March 2019, she starred in the first Broadway revival of [Lanford Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanford_Wilson "Lanford Wilson")'s play *[Burn This](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_This "Burn This")* at the [Hudson Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Theatre "Hudson Theatre"). Frank Rizzo of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_(magazine) "Variety (magazine)")* wrote of her performance, "Russell, whose stage credits are slim but who's proven her chops onscreen in *The Americans*, creates a vivid, if less flashy, performance", adding, "Still, she's a force in her own right."

In 2023, Russell began starring in *[The Diplomat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Diplomat_(American_TV_series) "The Diplomat (American TV series)")*, a political series on [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") from writer and producer [Debora Cahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debora_Cahn "Debora Cahn"). In 2026, she received the [Actor Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Awards "Actor Awards") for [Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Female_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series "Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Drama Series") for her work on the show's third season.

## Personal life

In 2006, Russell became engaged to Shane Deary, a Brooklyn-based contractor she met through mutual friends. They married in New York on February 14, 2007. They have two children: a son born in 2007 and a daughter born in 2012. Russell and Deary separated in early 2013 and divorced in mid-2014. Since 2014, she has been in a relationship with actor [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys "Matthew Rhys"), her co-star in *The Americans*. They have a son, born in 2016. In interviews conducted in 2021, Russell and Rhys referred to each other as husband and wife, although in an interview in 2025, Rhys said, "We literally haven't got round to marriage yet".
