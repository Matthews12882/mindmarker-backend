# Ella Langley

**Elizabeth Camille Langley** (born May 3, 1999) is an American singer-songwriter. She is signed to [Sony Music Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Music_Nashville "Sony Music Nashville") and [Columbia Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Records "Columbia Records"). Her debut studio album, *[Hungover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungover_(album) "Hungover (album)")* (2024), produced breakthrough hits "[You Look Like You Love Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Look_Like_You_Love_Me "You Look Like You Love Me")" and "[Weren't for the Wind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weren%27t_for_the_Wind "Weren't for the Wind")", which both topped the [Country Airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Airplay "Country Airplay") chart.

Her second studio album, *[Dandelion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandelion_(album) "Dandelion (album)")* (2026), debuted atop the US [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200"). Its lead single, "[Choosin' Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choosin%27_Texas "Choosin' Texas")", became the [longest-running number-one song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Billboard_Hot_100_chart_achievements_and_milestones#Most_weeks_at_number_one "List of Billboard Hot 100 chart achievements and milestones") in US [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100") history and made Langley the first female artist to simultaneously top the US Hot 100, Hot Country Songs, and Country Airplay charts.

## Early life

Elizabeth Camille Langley was born in [Hope Hull, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hope_Hull,_Alabama "Hope Hull, Alabama"), on May 3, 1999, and grew up in a musically inclined family alongside her two brothers and sister. Langley was homeschooled in [Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery,_Alabama "Montgomery, Alabama") until seventh grade due to being a "distraction." Her early exposure to music came through singing in [Southern Baptist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptists "Baptists") churches and informal [jam sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jam_session "Jam session"). She loved sitting next to her grandfather at the piano, singing "[Frog Went a-Courting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frog_Went_a-Courting "Frog Went a-Courting")". After her grandfather's death, her father had his guitar restrung for her when she was 14. That same night, she looked up the chords to "[Three Little Birds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Little_Birds "Three Little Birds")" by [Bob Marley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Marley "Bob Marley") and taught herself to play the song. She initially practiced performing in front of her family's cow pasture.

In 2016, she gave her first public performance at Hooper Academy in Hope Hull, in the student talent show. In high school she worked at a trampoline park and also performed at local venues before graduating in 2017. After graduation, she attended [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University "Auburn University") to study [forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry "Forestry"). During college, she was a member of [Phi Mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Mu "Phi Mu") sorority.

## Career

### 2017–2023: Early career and *Excuse the Mess*

In 2017, Langley co-wrote her first song, "Clear the Clouds", with her aunt, starting from a melody she played on her porch at home. The song was recorded at Guest House Studios in [Eclectic, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eclectic,_Alabama "Eclectic, Alabama"), and released on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube"). Her debut single, "Perfect" was released on May 24, 2018, although it and much of her early catalog was later removed from streaming platforms. Over the next two years, she continued to perform across Alabama at various bars and festivals. In 2019, Langley moved to [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee") and immersed herself in the local songwriting community and participated in numerous writers' rounds. She gained support from independent country platforms, including "Raised Rowdy" and "65 South" during that time. During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic") in 2020, she shifted her focus to livestreaming and expanding her online presence.

In 2021, she signed her first publishing deal with [Sony Music Publishing Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Music_Publishing "Sony Music Publishing"). Also in 2021, she released a track, titled "If You Have To", after previewing the single on [TikTok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok "TikTok"). In early 2022, Langley joined [Randy Houser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Houser "Randy Houser") as a guest artist on his tour. On October 28, Langley released the promotional single, "Country Boy's Dream Girl". In 2023, she made her [Grand Ole Opry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Ole_Opry "Grand Ole Opry") debut on February 17.

The following week, she signed a record deal with [Sony Music Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Music_Nashville "Sony Music Nashville") and [Columbia Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Records "Columbia Records"). She collaborated with [Koe Wetzel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koe_Wetzel "Koe Wetzel") on the song "That's Why We Fight", released on April 21. Her debut [extended play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_play "Extended play") (EP), *Excuse the Mess*, came out on May 19. In addition to her solo work, Langley co-wrote multiple tracks for other artists, including five songs on [Elle King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_King "Elle King")'s *[Come Get Your Wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_Get_Your_Wife "Come Get Your Wife")* and a [Runaway June](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runaway_June "Runaway June") single, "Make Me Wanna Smoke". Langley also recorded a duet with [Kameron Marlowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kameron_Marlowe "Kameron Marlowe") titled "Strangers", which served as the lead single from his second studio album, *Keepin' the Lights On*. From 2023 through 2024, she opened for artists [Jon Pardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Pardi "Jon Pardi") and [Riley Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riley_Green "Riley Green").

### 2024–present: *Hungover* and *Dandelion*

Langley's full-length debut studio album, *[Hungover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungover_(album) "Hungover (album)")*, was released on August 2, 2024. It debuted at No. 77 on the [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200") and No. 11 on the *Billboard* [Top Country Albums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_Country_Albums "Top Country Albums") chart. After the release of the deluxe edition, *Still Hungover*, on November 1, 2024, the album re-entered the *Billboard* 200 at No. 49. Among several promotional singles, "[You Look Like You Love Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Look_Like_You_Love_Me "You Look Like You Love Me")", featuring Riley Green, gained significant traction after going viral on TikTok. It became her first entry on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100 "Billboard Hot 100"), debuting at number 53. Released to country radio on August 5, 2024, the song peaked at No. 30 on the *Billboard* [Country Airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Airplay "Country Airplay"), ultimately earning [Platinum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIAA_certification "RIAA certification") certification and going to No. 1—making her the only woman in 2024 to achieve this milestone.

In June 2024, Langley announced the Hungover Tour to support her debut album. A subsequent headlining run, the Still Hungover Tour, took place from January through April 2025. The second single from the deluxe album, "[Weren't for the Wind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weren%27t_for_the_Wind "Weren't for the Wind")", was released to country radio on January 2, 2025. It became her then-highest-charting single on the Hot 100, peaking at No. 18, and her second country radio No. 1. Her second duet with Riley Green "[Don't Mind If I Do](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Mind_If_I_Do_(song) "Don't Mind If I Do (song)")" was her third country radio number one song. She also collaborated with American rapper [BigXthaPlug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BigXthaPlug "BigXthaPlug") on his [country rap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_rap "Country rap") song "[Hell at Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hell_at_Night "Hell at Night")". Langley is one of a few entertainment celebrity [spokespeople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spokesperson "Spokesperson") for [Chase Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Bank "Chase Bank")'s 2025 [advertising campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advertising_campaign "Advertising campaign") promoting its Sapphire Reserve premium [credit card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_card "Credit card").

Langley confirmed that the release of her second album would be delayed until 2026, a decision she says is driven by her desire to take her time crafting the material rather than rushing a follow-up album. Prior to its release, she released the singles "[Never Met Anyone Like You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Never_Met_Anyone_Like_You "Never Met Anyone Like You")", a collaboration with [Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy_(singer) "Hardy (singer)") in June, and "[Choosin' Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choosin%27_Texas "Choosin' Texas")", co-written and co-produced with [Miranda Lambert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Lambert "Miranda Lambert"). "Choosin' Texas" went viral on TikTok and debuted at No. 39 on the *Billboard* Hot 100, her highest debut at the time. The song would go on to become the first by a female artist to reach number one on the Hot 100, Hot Country Songs, and Country Airplay charts all at once. It has spent 24 non-consecutive weeks on top of the *Billboard* Hot 100, the longest reign in the chart's history.

On January 27, 2026, Langley formally announced her second album, *[Dandelion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandelion_(album) "Dandelion (album)")*, which released on April 10, 2026. The album's [title track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandelion_(Ella_Langley_song) "Dandelion (Ella Langley song)"), "[Be Her](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be_Her "Be Her")", and "[Loving Life Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loving_Life_Again "Loving Life Again")" were released in promotion for the record. *Dandelion* debuted at number one on the US [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200 "Billboard 200"), making it Langley's first number-one album and the largest streaming debut for an album of new country material by a female country primary artist. The second single "Be Her" peaked at No. 2 on the *Billboard* Hot 100, being blocked from the top by her own "Choosin' Texas", making her the first female country primary artist in history to occupy the top 2 simultaneously. On September 22, 2026, "Choosin' Texas" broke *Billboard*'s Hot 100 record for number of weeks at number one. An all-female lineup of opening acts was booked for her *Dandelion* tour, which ran through August 2026, featuring artists like [Gabriella Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriella_Rose "Gabriella Rose"), [Kaitlin Butts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaitlin_Butts "Kaitlin Butts"), [Avery Anna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avery_Anna "Avery Anna"), [Laci Kaye Booth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laci_Kaye_Booth "Laci Kaye Booth"), and Madeline Edwards.

## Artistry

Langley's influences include rock, [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music"), and [folk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music "Folk music"). She admires artists including [Stevie Nicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Nicks "Stevie Nicks") and [Willie Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Nelson "Willie Nelson") for their raw, honest songwriting and she also appreciates 1970s and 1980s rock bands for their performances on stage, artistry, and music. She combines [Southern rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_rock "Southern rock"), folk, and country influences to create a raw sound shaped by her musical upbringing.

## Personal life

Langley has been outspoken about being a [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christians "Christians") and having [mental health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health "Mental health") struggles, including [depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depression_(mood) "Depression (mood)") and [impostor syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impostor_syndrome "Impostor syndrome"). She has lived in [Nashville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee "Nashville, Tennessee") since 2019.

## Other ventures

In 2025, Langley participated in [St. Jude Children's Research Hospital Music Gives to St. Jude Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Jude_Children%27s_Research_Hospital "St. Jude Children's Research Hospital") fundraising campaign, appearing in promotional materials for the charity's annual shirt initiative.

In February 2026, Langley was named [American Eagle Outfitters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Eagle_Outfitters "American Eagle Outfitters")' "Denim Darling", serving as the face of their "American Eagle Jeans Country" advertisement campaign.

Langley debuted her "Be Her" fragrance in April 2026, developed in partnership with NOYZ.
