# Julia Stiles

**Julia O'Hara Stiles** (born March 28, 1981) is an American actress and director. Stiles began acting at the age of 11 as part of New York's [La MaMa Experimental Theatre Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_MaMa_Experimental_Theatre_Club "La MaMa Experimental Theatre Club"). Her film debut was a small role at age 15 in *[I Love You, I Love You Not](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Love_You,_I_Love_You_Not "I Love You, I Love You Not")* (1996), followed by a lead role in [*Wicked*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wicked_(1998_film) "Wicked (1998 film)") (1998) for which she received the [Karlovy Vary Film Festival Award for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlovy_Vary_International_Film_Festival "Karlovy Vary International Film Festival"). Stiles co-starred in the made-for-TV mini-series *The '60s* (1999) as a teenage daughter in a middle-class American family from Chicago. She rose to prominence with leading roles in teen films such as *[10 Things I Hate About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Things_I_Hate_About_You "10 Things I Hate About You")* (1999), *[Down to You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_to_You "Down to You")* (2001), and *[Save the Last Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Save_the_Last_Dance "Save the Last Dance")* (2001). Her accolades include a [Teen Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Choice_Awards "Teen Choice Awards") and two [MTV Movie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_%26_TV_Awards "MTV Movie & TV Awards"), as well as nominations for a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards "Golden Globe Awards"), and [Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards "Primetime Emmy Awards").

Stiles added to her list of credits with films such as *[The Business of Strangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Business_of_Strangers "The Business of Strangers")* (2001), *[Mona Lisa Smile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Lisa_Smile "Mona Lisa Smile")* (2003), and *[The Omen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Omen_(2006_film) "The Omen (2006 film)")* (2006), and became known to audiences worldwide with her portrayal of Nicky Parsons in the [*Bourne* franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourne_(franchise) "Bourne (franchise)") (2002–2016). Her other notable film credits include *[Hamlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_(2000_film) "Hamlet (2000 film)")*, *[State and Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_and_Main "State and Main")* (both 2000), *[O](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O_(2001_film) "O (2001 film)")* (2001), *[A Guy Thing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Guy_Thing "A Guy Thing")* (2002), *[Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_(2003_film) "Carolina (2003 film)")* (2003), *[The Prince & Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prince_%26_Me "The Prince & Me")* (2004), *[Edmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmond_(film) "Edmond (film)")*, *[A Little Trip to Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Little_Trip_to_Heaven "A Little Trip to Heaven")* (both 2005), *[The Cry of the Owl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cry_of_the_Owl_(2009_film) "The Cry of the Owl (2009 film)")* (2009), *[Silver Linings Playbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Linings_Playbook "Silver Linings Playbook")* (2012), *[Out of the Dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_the_Dark_(2014_film) "Out of the Dark (2014 film)")* (2014), *[Blackway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackway "Blackway")* (2015), *[11:55](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11:55 "11:55")* (2016), *[Hustlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hustlers_(film) "Hustlers (film)")* (2019) and *[Orphan: First Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orphan:_First_Kill "Orphan: First Kill")* (2022).

Outside of film, Stiles played [Lumen Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumen_Pierce "Lumen Pierce") on the [fifth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter_(season_5) "Dexter (season 5)") of *[Dexter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter_(TV_series) "Dexter (TV series)")* (2010), earning nominations for the [Golden Globe for Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_%E2%80%93_Series,_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Series, Miniseries or Television Film") and the [Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Guest Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actress in a Drama Series"). From 2012 to 2014 she appeared as the titular character in the web series *[Blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_(web_series) "Blue (web series)")*, for which she earned two [IAWTV Awards for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_of_Web_Television "International Academy of Web Television"). From 2017 to 2020 she starred as Georgina Ryland on the [Sky Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Atlantic "Sky Atlantic") series *[Riviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riviera_(TV_series) "Riviera (TV series)")*. She starred in the [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video "Amazon Prime Video") series *[The Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lake_(TV_series) "The Lake (TV series)")* (2022–2023).

## Early life

Stiles was born in New York City to Judith Newcomb Stiles, a [Greenwich Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenwich_Village "Greenwich Village") artist, and John O'Hara, a businessman. She is the oldest of three children. Stiles is of English, Irish, and German descent. She started acting at age 11, performing with New York's [La MaMa Theatre Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_MaMa_Experimental_Theatre_Club "La MaMa Experimental Theatre Club").

Stiles grew up in [SoHo, Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoHo,_Manhattan "SoHo, Manhattan") with her siblings Jane and John.

## Career

### Film and television

After finding an agent, Stiles began auditioning for television in 1993 and films in 1996. She made her acting debut in 1993 on the mystery show *[Ghostwriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghostwriter_(1992_TV_series) "Ghostwriter (1992 TV series)")* as Erica Dansby.[*[self-published source?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability#Self-published_sources "Wikipedia:Verifiability")*]

Stiles's first film role was in *[I Love You, I Love You Not](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Love_You,_I_Love_You_Not "I Love You, I Love You Not")* (1996), with [Claire Danes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Danes "Claire Danes") and [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law "Jude Law"). She also had small roles as [Harrison Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Ford "Harrison Ford")'s character's daughter in [Alan J. Pakula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_J._Pakula "Alan J. Pakula")'s *[The Devil's Own](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil%27s_Own "The Devil's Own")* (1997) and in [M. Night Shyamalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Night_Shyamalan "M. Night Shyamalan")'s *[Wide Awake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide_Awake_(1998_film) "Wide Awake (1998 film)")* (1998). Her first lead was in *[Wicked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wicked_(1998_film) "Wicked (1998 film)")* (1998), in which she played a teenage girl who might have murdered her mother so she could have her father all to herself. Critic Joe Baltake wrote she was "the darling of the 1998 [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival "Sundance Film Festival")." She next starred in the TV miniseries *The '60s* in 1999.

Stiles was cast at the age of 17 for the role of Kat Stratford, opposite [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger "Heath Ledger") in [Gil Junger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Junger "Gil Junger")'s *[10 Things I Hate About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Things_I_Hate_About_You "10 Things I Hate About You")* (1999), an adaptation of [Shakespeare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare "William Shakespeare")'s *[The Taming of the Shrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Taming_of_the_Shrew "The Taming of the Shrew")* set in high school. She won an [MTV Movie Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award "MTV Movie Award") for Breakthrough Female Performance for the role. The [Chicago Film Critics Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Film_Critics_Association "Chicago Film Critics Association") voted her as the most promising new actress of 1999. Her next starring role was in *[Down to You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_to_You "Down to You")* (2000), which was panned by critics, but earned both her and her co-star [Freddie Prinze, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_Prinze,_Jr. "Freddie Prinze, Jr.") a [Teen Choice Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Choice_Award "Teen Choice Award") nomination for their on-screen chemistry. She subsequently appeared in two more [Shakespearean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespearean "Shakespearean") adaptations. The first was as [Ophelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophelia "Ophelia") in [Michael Almereyda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Almereyda "Michael Almereyda")'s *[Hamlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_(2000_film) "Hamlet (2000 film)")* (2000), with [Ethan Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Hawke "Ethan Hawke") in the lead. The second was in the [Desdemona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desdemona "Desdemona") role, opposite [Mekhi Phifer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekhi_Phifer "Mekhi Phifer"), in [Tim Blake Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Blake_Nelson "Tim Blake Nelson")'s *[O](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O_(2001_film) "O (2001 film)")* (2001), a version of *[Othello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Othello "Othello")* set at a boarding school. Neither film was a great success; *O* was subject to many delays and a change of distributors, and *Hamlet* was an [art house film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_house_film "Art house film") shot on a minimal budget.

Stiles's next commercial success was in *[Save the Last Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Save_the_Last_Dance "Save the Last Dance")* (2001) as an aspiring [ballerina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet "Ballet") forced to leave her small town in downstate Illinois to live with her struggling musician father in Chicago after her mother dies in a car accident. At her new, nearly all-black school, she falls in love with the character played by [Sean Patrick Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Patrick_Thomas "Sean Patrick Thomas") who teaches her [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip_hop_music "Hip hop music") dancing to help get her into the [Juilliard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juilliard_School "Juilliard School"). The role won her two more MTV awards for Best Kiss and Best Female Performance and a Teen Choice Award for best fight scene for her battle with [Bianca Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bianca_Lawson "Bianca Lawson"). *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* magazine named her "the coolest co-ed" and put her on the cover of its April 12, 2001, issue. She told *Rolling Stone* that she performed all her own dancing in the film, except for some closeups of the feet.

In [David Mamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Mamet "David Mamet")'s *[State and Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_and_Main "State and Main")* (2000), about a film shooting on location in a small town in [Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont "Vermont"), Stiles played a teenage girl who seduces a film actor ([Alec Baldwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Baldwin "Alec Baldwin")) with [a weakness for teen girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ephebophilia "Ephebophilia"). Stiles also appeared opposite [Stockard Channing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockard_Channing "Stockard Channing") in the dark art house film *[The Business of Strangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Business_of_Strangers "The Business of Strangers")* (2001) as a conniving, amoral secretary who exacts revenge on her boss. Channing was impressed by her co-star: "In addition to her talent, she has a quality that is almost feral, something that can make people uneasy. She has an effect on people." Stiles later described the [*Bourne* franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourne_franchise "Bourne franchise") as very important for her career, stating that it "reinvented the action genre, especially for female characters". Her small role as [Treadstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Treadstone "Operation Treadstone") operative Nicolette "Nicky" Parsons in *[The Bourne Identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bourne_Identity_(2002_film) "The Bourne Identity (2002 film)")* (2002) grew in *[The Bourne Supremacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bourne_Supremacy_(film) "The Bourne Supremacy (film)")* (2004), then greatly expanded in *[The Bourne Ultimatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bourne_Ultimatum_(film) "The Bourne Ultimatum (film)")* (2007).

Between the *Bourne* films, she appeared in *[Mona Lisa Smile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Lisa_Smile "Mona Lisa Smile")* (2003) as Joan, a student at [Wellesley College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellesley_College "Wellesley College") in 1953, whose art professor ([Julia Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Roberts "Julia Roberts")) encourages her to pursue a career in law rather than become a wife and mother. Critic [Stephen Holden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Holden "Stephen Holden") called her one of cinema's "brightest young stars", but the film met with generally unfavorable reviews. Stiles played a Wisconsin college student who is swept off her feet by a Danish prince, played by [Luke Mably](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Mably "Luke Mably"), in *[The Prince and Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prince_and_Me "The Prince and Me")* (2004), directed by [Martha Coolidge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Coolidge "Martha Coolidge"). Stiles told an interviewer that she was very similar to her character Paige Morgan. Critic Scott Foundas said she was "irrepressibly engaging" and the film was a "strange career choice for Stiles". This echoed criticism in reviews of *[A Guy Thing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Guy_Thing "A Guy Thing")* (2003), a romantic comedy with [Jason Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Lee_(actor) "Jason Lee (actor)") and [Selma Blair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selma_Blair "Selma Blair"). Critic Dennis Harvey wrote that Stiles was "wasted", and Holden called her "a serious actress from whom comedy does not seem to flow naturally". In 2006, Stiles starred opposite her *Hamlet* co-star [Liev Schreiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liev_Schreiber "Liev Schreiber") in *[The Omen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Omen_(2006_film) "The Omen (2006 film)"),* a remake of [the 1976 horror film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Omen "The Omen"). She returned to the *Bourne* series with a much larger role in *[The Bourne Ultimatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bourne_Ultimatum_(film) "The Bourne Ultimatum (film)")* (2007), her highest-grossing film to date.

Stiles acted in *[Between Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Between_Us_(2012_film) "Between Us (2012 film)")* (2012) with [Taye Diggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taye_Diggs "Taye Diggs"), [David Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harbour "David Harbour"), and [Melissa George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_George "Melissa George"). *Between Us* is the screen adaptation of the [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway "Off-Broadway") play of the same name by [Joe Hortua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Hortua "Joe Hortua"). Stiles starred alongside [David Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cross "David Cross") and [America Ferrera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America_Ferrera "America Ferrera") in the dark comedy *[It's a Disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_a_Disaster "It's a Disaster")*. The film premiered at the 2012 [Los Angeles Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Film_Festival "Los Angeles Film Festival") and was picked up by [Oscilloscope Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscilloscope_Laboratories "Oscilloscope Laboratories") and received a limited release the next year. Stiles had a small but pivotal role as a reporter in the 2013 British-American film *[Closed Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_Circuit_(2013_film) "Closed Circuit (2013 film)")*. Stiles starred in the indie supernatural thriller *[Out of the Dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_the_Dark_(2014_film) "Out of the Dark (2014 film)")* (2014) alongside [Scott Speedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Speedman "Scott Speedman") and [Stephen Rea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Rea "Stephen Rea").

In 2015, Stiles signed on to reprise her role as Nicky Parsons in *[Jason Bourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Bourne_(film) "Jason Bourne (film)")*, the fifth installment of the *Bourne* franchise. She also featured as Courtney, the wayward mother of [Sophie Nélisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_N%C3%A9lisse "Sophie Nélisse"), in *[The Great Gilly Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Gilly_Hopkins_(film) "The Great Gilly Hopkins (film)")* (2016). In 2019, Stiles appeared in the movie *[Hustlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hustlers_(film) "Hustlers (film)")* as the journalist, Elizabeth. The film was a box office success.

### Stage

While Stiles performed in a school play in fourth grade, [Bob McGrath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_McGrath "Bob McGrath") of Ridge Theater in Manhattan, a friend of her parents, needed an actor for a nonspeaking role. Stiles's first theatrical roles were in works by author/composer [John Moran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Moran_(composer) "John Moran (composer)") at Ridge Theater from 1993 to 1998. In the summer of 2002, she performed on stage in [Eve Ensler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve_Ensler "Eve Ensler")'s *[The Vagina Monologues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Vagina_Monologues "The Vagina Monologues")*, and appeared as Viola, the lead role in [Shakespeare in the Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespeare_in_the_Park "Shakespeare in the Park")'s production of *[Twelfth Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelfth_Night "Twelfth Night")* with [Jimmy Smits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Smits "Jimmy Smits").

In 2004, she made her [West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre "West End theatre") stage debut opposite [Aaron Eckhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Eckhart "Aaron Eckhart") in a revival of [David Mamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Mamet "David Mamet")'s play *[Oleanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleanna_(play) "Oleanna (play)")* at the [Garrick Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrick_Theatre "Garrick Theatre"). She reprised the role of Carol in a 2009 production of *Oleanna*, directed by [Doug Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Hughes "Doug Hughes") and co-starring [Bill Pullman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Pullman "Bill Pullman") at the [Mark Taper Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Taper_Forum "Mark Taper Forum"). The production moved to Broadway's [John Golden Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Golden_Theatre "John Golden Theatre").

Stiles was to play Jeannie in a production of [Neil LaBute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_LaBute "Neil LaBute")'s *[Fat Pig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fat_Pig "Fat Pig")* directed by the playwright beginning in spring 2011, but the show was postponed indefinitely.

### Other work

Stiles appeared in the video for [Cyndi Lauper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyndi_Lauper "Cyndi Lauper")'s single "[Sally's Pigeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally%27s_Pigeons "Sally's Pigeons")" in 1993. In 2001, she hosted *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live "Saturday Night Live")* and returned to parody as then-President [George W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Bush "George W. Bush")'s daughter [Jenna Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Bush "Jenna Bush") in a skit that poked fun at the two first daughters for being arrested for underage drinking. MTV profiled her in its *[Diary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diary_(American_TV_series) "Diary (American TV series)")* series in 2003, and she was *[Punk'd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk%27d "Punk'd")* by [Ashton Kutcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashton_Kutcher "Ashton Kutcher") at a Washington, D.C., museum in 2004.

In 2010, Stiles played a major role in 10 episodes of the [Showtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network) "Showtime (TV network)") series *[Dexter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dexter_(TV_series) "Dexter (TV series)")* For this role, she received a nomination for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Series, Miniseries or Television Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress_%E2%80%93_Series,_Miniseries_or_Television_Film "Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actress – Series, Miniseries or Television Film"), as well as a [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actress in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series "Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actress in a Drama Series").

In 2012, the web series *[Blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_(web_series) "Blue (web series)")* starred Stiles as a single mother with a 13-year-old son. She works at an office and also as a [call girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_girl "Call girl") to make ends meet on an otherwise meager income fighting to protect her son from the collision between her complicated past and tenuous present. For her work on *Blue*, Stiles won two [IAWTV Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAWTV_Awards "IAWTV Awards"), in 2013 and 2014. The actress during the recordings shared set with artists like [Michelle Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Forbes "Michelle Forbes"), [JC Gonzalez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JC_Gonzalez "JC Gonzalez"), and [Uriah Shelton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uriah_Shelton "Uriah Shelton").

Stiles played Maisy-May in the Canadian Amazon Prime series *[The Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lake_(TV_series) "The Lake (TV series)")*. Maisy-May is the "picture-perfect" stepdaughter/stepsister who was given the family cottage by her stepfather, to the dismay of her stepbrother Justin. Season 1 premiered in summer 2022.

### Film director

Stiles made her writing and directorial debut with *[Elle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_(magazine) "Elle (magazine)")* magazine's [short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_subject "Short subject") *[Raving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raving_(film) "Raving (film)")* starring [Zooey Deschanel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zooey_Deschanel "Zooey Deschanel"). It premiered at the 2007 [Tribeca Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival "Tribeca Film Festival").

Stiles' first feature film as a director, *[Wish You Were Here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wish_You_Were_Here_(2025_film) "Wish You Were Here (2025 film)")*, was released in January 2025.

## Personal life

Stiles graduated from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University "Columbia University") with a degree in [English literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_literature "English literature") in 2005. She almost turned down the first *Bourne* film because of college exams, and deferred a semester for the first two films. At Columbia she dated her *[10 Things I Hate About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Things_I_Hate_About_You "10 Things I Hate About You")* co-star [Joseph Gordon-Levitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gordon-Levitt "Joseph Gordon-Levitt") and the two lived in [John Jay Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Jay_Hall "John Jay Hall"). She and actor [David Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harbour "David Harbour") were in a relationship between 2011 and 2015. In 2010, she received a [John Jay Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Jay_Award "John Jay Award"), an honorary award given annually to five alumni by the Columbia College Alumni Association for professional achievements.

Stiles has also worked for [Habitat for Humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat_for_Humanity "Habitat for Humanity"), building housing in [Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica "Costa Rica"), and has worked with [Amnesty International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amnesty_International "Amnesty International") to raise awareness of the harsh conditions of immigration detention of unaccompanied juveniles. In January 2004, *[Marie Claire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Claire "Marie Claire")* featured Stiles's trip to witness conditions at the [Berks County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berks_County,_Pennsylvania "Berks County, Pennsylvania") Youth Center in [Leesport, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leesport,_Pennsylvania "Leesport, Pennsylvania").

Stiles is a former [vegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegan "Vegan"), occasionally eating red meat. She says she gave up veganism after she developed [anemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anemia "Anemia") and found it difficult to get proper nutrition while traveling.

She has described herself as a [feminist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feminist "Feminist") and wrote about the subject in *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")*.

She is a fan of baseball and the [New York Mets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Mets "New York Mets"). She threw the ceremonial first pitch before their May 29, 2006 game.

In September 2017 Stiles married camera assistant Preston J. Cook with whom she worked on *[Blackway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackway "Blackway")*. They have three children.
