# American Horror Story: 13

The thirteenth season of the American [horror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horror_fiction "Horror fiction") [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology_series "Anthology series") television series *[American Horror Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story "American Horror Story")*, subtitled ***13***, takes place primarily in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"), and follows Ben DeSoto (portrayed by [Joey Pollari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Pollari "Joey Pollari")), a hospice caretaker suffering from [triskaidekaphobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triskaidekaphobia "Triskaidekaphobia"), who takes a strange job at The Apollyon, an eerie residential building inhabited by characters from seasons past. The season also features the return of regular actors [Sarah Paulson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Paulson "Sarah Paulson"), [Evan Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Peters "Evan Peters"), [Emma Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Roberts "Emma Roberts"), [Billie Lourd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Lourd "Billie Lourd"), [Kathy Bates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_Bates "Kathy Bates"), [Angela Bassett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Bassett "Angela Bassett"), [Gabourey Sidibe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabourey_Sidibe "Gabourey Sidibe"), [Leslie Grossman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Grossman "Leslie Grossman"), [Frances Conroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Conroy "Frances Conroy"), [Jamie Brewer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Brewer "Jamie Brewer"), [John Carroll Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Carroll_Lynch "John Carroll Lynch"), [Mena Suvari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mena_Suvari "Mena Suvari"), [Mat Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat_Fraser_(actor) "Mat Fraser (actor)") and [Jessica Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lange "Jessica Lange").

Created by [Ryan Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Murphy_(producer) "Ryan Murphy (producer)") and [Brad Falchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Falchuk "Brad Falchuk") for the [cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cable_television_in_the_United_States "Cable television in the United States") network [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)"), the series is produced by [20th Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Television "20th Television"). The season began filming on April 6, 2026, and concluded on July 27; the subtitle was announced the day after filming finished. *13* premiered on September 24, 2026, and is expected to run until October 29, consisting of thirteen episodes released in mostly either pairs or trios.

## Production

### Background and theme

On January 9, 2020, *[American Horror Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story "American Horror Story")* was renewed for up to a thirteenth season. On December 5, 2018, [Ryan Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Murphy_(producer) "Ryan Murphy (producer)") said the witches from *[Coven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Coven "American Horror Story: Coven")* would return in a future season. On July 28, 2026, Murphy announced in an interview with *[Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight "Entertainment Tonight")* that the season, officially titled *13*, would consist of 13 episodes, and while the season would revisit *Coven*, it was not a direct sequel. He described it as an *[Avengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(film_series) "Avengers (film series)")*-style [crossover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossover_(fiction) "Crossover (fiction)") between "all the seasons", with characters coming together "to defeat the ultimate evil", and added that he spent years writing it. [Leslie Grossman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Grossman "Leslie Grossman") explained that the series was set in one universe with different timelines, which "intersect in a way that makes sense".

The theme of the [number 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_(number)#Luck "13 (number)") was announced alongside the title. The show's official [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_(TV_channel) "FX (TV channel)") page for the season reads: "FX's *American Horror Story* ... returns for an unrivaled 13th installment that will do justice to everyone's most feared number. ... Which iconic horrors will return to haunt the hallowed halls of 13 and what new terrors await? Light your candles, draw your pentagrams and prepare for a supreme surprise."

In August 2026, Murphy revealed he would direct an episode, for the first time since "[Checking In](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checking_In_(American_Horror_Story) "Checking In (American Horror Story)")" (2015). In September 2026, he said that the crossover with *[Scream Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_Queens_(2015_TV_series) "Scream Queens (2015 TV series)")* was planned to be included in the season, but it was scrapped after the departure of [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande "Ariana Grande"), who was supposed to reprise her role as Chanel #2.

### Casting

In February 2025, [Jessica Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lange "Jessica Lange"), who starred in the first four seasons and briefly appeared in *[Apocalypse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Apocalypse "American Horror Story: Apocalypse")* (2018), answered a question about her potential return for the thirteenth season: "Oh Christ, no. I mean, I haven't done it for more than 10 years, 12 years, so, no, I'm not doing it." Murphy revealed that Lange was the first person he turned to about the return and she agreed under the condition she would have a musical number. She revealed she was not interested in portraying new characters and considered coming back only for reprising her previous characters. Subsequently, Murphy turned to [Sarah Paulson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Paulson "Sarah Paulson") and [Evan Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Peters "Evan Peters"), who agreed instantly. They both last appeared in *[Double Feature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Double_Feature "American Horror Story: Double Feature")* (2021).

In October 2025, Lange, Paulson, Peters, [Emma Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Roberts "Emma Roberts"), [Billie Lourd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Lourd "Billie Lourd"), [Kathy Bates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_Bates "Kathy Bates"), [Angela Bassett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Bassett "Angela Bassett"), [Leslie Grossman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Grossman "Leslie Grossman"), [Jessica Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lange "Jessica Lange"), and [Gabourey Sidibe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabourey_Sidibe "Gabourey Sidibe") were all confirmed to return for the thirteenth season, along with the newcomer [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande "Ariana Grande"). Bates, Bassett and Sibide last appeared in *Apocalypse*. Bates revealed that she would have only one day of filming. In February 2026, [John Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Waters "John Waters") joined the cast, while [Joey Pollari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Pollari "Joey Pollari") joined in April, and [Alex Consani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Consani "Alex Consani") and [Paul Anthony Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Anthony_Kelly "Paul Anthony Kelly") joined in May. [John Carroll Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Carroll_Lynch "John Carroll Lynch") and [Matt Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat_Fraser_(actor) "Mat Fraser (actor)") were also confirmed to reprise their roles from *[Freak Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Freak_Show "American Horror Story: Freak Show")*, with [Mena Suvari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mena_Suvari "Mena Suvari") and Berto Colón also joining the cast. In July, [Frances Conroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Conroy "Frances Conroy") was confirmed to reprise her role from *Coven*, with [Avantika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avantika_Vandanapu "Avantika Vandanapu"), Grace Dumdaw, [Cara Delevingne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cara_Delevingne "Cara Delevingne"), and [Charlie Carver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Carver "Charlie Carver") also joining the cast. In July 2026, it was reported that Grande dropped out due to scheduling conflicts with the season's production schedule and her [Eternal Sunshine Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Eternal_Sunshine_Tour "The Eternal Sunshine Tour"). In August, [Jamie Brewer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Brewer "Jamie Brewer"), [Elle Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Chapman "Elle Chapman"), [Seth Gabel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Gabel "Seth Gabel"), [Orlando Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Jones "Orlando Jones"), [Tig Notaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tig_Notaro "Tig Notaro"), [Madelaine Petsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madelaine_Petsch "Madelaine Petsch"), Fedor Steer, and [Zach Villa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Villa "Zach Villa") were confirmed as part of the cast.

Murphy revealed that Paulson was set to play six to seven roles, while Peters was set to play five characters. Paulson revealed that a storyline with their *[Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Asylum "American Horror Story: Asylum")* characters, Lana Winters and Kit Walker, was planned, but it was scrapped because Peters did not want to do the accent again. Lange reprised all four of her characters from the first four seasons, and confirmed having a musical number as [Elsa Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Mars "Elsa Mars"). She also teased portraying a fifth undisclosed character, later revealed as [Margaret Thatcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Thatcher "Margaret Thatcher"), which was her idea to be included in a storyline. Moreover, she recorded a cover of [Peggy Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peggy_Lee "Peggy Lee")'s "[Is That All There Is?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Is_That_All_There_Is%3F "Is That All There Is?")" for the fourth episode.

## Reception

### Critical response

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), 75% critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 5.7/10, based on 8 reviews. [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 58 out of 100 based on 4 critics, indicating "mixed or average" reviews.

*[Slate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slate_(magazine) "Slate (magazine)")*'s J. Bryan Lowder deemed the season a "relieving return to form", adding "the writers have finally tuned back into the mix of camp and the grotesque that made the golden era so good". Michel Ghanem of *[TheWrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TheWrap "TheWrap")* called the season a "beautifully puzzling ode to superfans". He highlighted the performance of [Jessica Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lange "Jessica Lange"), calling her "as good as ever, scene-stealing through snippy lines and entertaining monologues, brightening up a slow start to the season". Writing for *[Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times "Radio Times")*, Louise Griffin deemed it "hellishly flawed but still fiendishly fun", feeling that the series "has reached its pinnacle – not, certainly, in quality, but in terms of pure spectacle". Similarly, she highlited Lange, commenting: "it's a delight to once again relish in the devilry of one of [the series'] best creations."

*[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_(magazine) "Vulture (magazine)")*'s Jen Chaney felt that watching the season "feels more than ever like being stuck in some sort of hell", arguing it delivered the "most incoherent debut in [the series'] decade-plus of existence". However, she praised Lange, opining she "lends some stability to this narratively shaky material through her performance". Ben Gibbons of *[Screen Rant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Rant "Screen Rant")* criticized the shortening of the episodes compared with previous seasons, arguing that the first three episodes have been "jumping from one narrative to another, trying to create loose connections between them all, but struggling to make one consistent thread that captivates the audience". Writing for *[Slant Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slant_Magazine "Slant Magazine")*, Michael Savio called the season a "nostalgia-ridden nightmare as mildly engrossing as it is exceedingly gross". He felt that Lange was "mesmerizing any time she's on screen", but when she "isn't holding our attention, these early episodes are borderline incoherent, if not just plain boring".

### Ratings

On FX, *American Horror Story: 13* averaged approximately 378,000 same-day viewers for its linear airing on September 24.

Streaming analytics firm FlixPatrol, which monitors daily updated VOD charts and streaming ratings worldwide, reported that in the United States, following the season's premiere, *American Horror Story* topped the streaming chart on Hulu among all programs and on Disney+ among the series. Internationally, it ranked among the top titles on Disney+ in multiple countries, while in Australia it was the most-streamed series on [Binge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binge_(streaming_service) "Binge (streaming service)"). FX announced that *American Horror Story: 13* drew approximately 4 million views worldwide on Hulu and Disney+ during the four days following its September 24 premiere. The figure represented a 50 percent increase over the twelfth season, *[Delicate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story:_Delicate "American Horror Story: Delicate")*, in 2024, and nearly double the viewership of the two preceding season premieres. The premiere was also the biggest launch to date for any show by *American Horror Story* co-creator and executive producer Ryan Murphy on Hulu and Disney+, excluding shows with next-day streaming releases, such as those he produces for ABC. [Nielsen Media Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_Media_Research "Nielsen Media Research"), which records streaming viewership on certain U.S. television screens, reported that *American Horror Story* was the second most-streamed acquired series between September 21–27, with 763 million minutes viewed, including *13* accounting for a third of that total.
